Notice to voters: If you are voting on Election Day, your ballot must be initialed by two (2) election inspectors. If you are voting absentee, your
ballot must be initialed by the municipal clerk or deputy clerk. Your ballot may not be counted without initials (see end of ballot for initials).

ausencia, la papeleta debe contener las iniciales del secretario municipal o del secretario suplente (vea al final de la papeleta para las iniciales).
