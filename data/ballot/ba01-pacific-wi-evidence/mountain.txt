Absentee Voting
Application for Absentee Ballot
can be completed and returned by mail or dropped off in person at the Town Office.
Online request –
www.myvote.wi.gov
Town of Mountain In-Person Absentee Early Voting:
Location:
Town Office, 13503 Weller Rd, Mountain, WI  54149
Voting hours:
In Person – by appointment starting Tuesday, October 20, 2026
Office hours with no appointment needed:
– Wednesday, October 21st  10 am – 2 pm
– Monday, October 26th from 10 am – 2 pm
– Wednesday, October 28th from 10 am – 2 pm
– Friday, October 30th from 10 am – 5 pm
or call for an appointment – (715) 276-6474 or (715) 850-0661
Final day of In Person Absentee Voting is Sunday, November 1, 2026
By Mail – final request postmarked by Thursday, October 29, 2026 (except for military electors and indefinitely confined electors)
Voter Registration
No In Person Voter Registration is allowed after 5:00 p.m. Friday, October 30, 2026.
Voters can register in person on Election Day (proof of residency is required showing your name and physical address within the Town of Mountain).
Voter Registration
form can be completed and mailed or dropped off at the Town Office; or brought with you on Election Day.  Proof of residency is required at time of registration.
Online registration –
www.myvote.wi.gov
