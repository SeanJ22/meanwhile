If you do not see your profile included here and you would like to, please submit a candidate portal application here and we will review as soon as possible. Also, lower level offices are shown by county and not necessarily the voter's specific local district. For example, Justice of the Peace or Constable candidates from different districts will all be organized by which county they fall within. Please refer to your official ballot for which local races apply to you.

                This page houses large amounts of data & profile photos, with more candidates utilizing these resources than ever before. It is recommended that you utilize the search by address feature, as that vastly reduces the amount of data you are requesting.

                However, if viewing all and you notice some, or many, of the profile photos not loading immediately please "refresh" the page. It may take a couple of refreshes, as your computer fetches every source image, but most candidates do have a statement, picture and contact information. Thank you for your understanding.
