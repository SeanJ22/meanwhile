  HOW DO I KNOW VOTING BY MAIL IS SECURE?
                 Voting by mail has been available in Arizona for almost
                 three decades. Approximately 80% of voters choose this
                 method. The infrastructure and security measures are
                 well in place to ensure ballots are safe and secure.

  The first step begins with voter registration. At the time of registering,
  voters must prove their identity and citizenship. A mail ballot will only
  be sent to someone who is a registered voter, who has specifically
  requested a mail ballot, and to the address on file.

  Every ballot that is returned to the county must have the voter’s ID
  verified before the ballot can be tabulated. ID verification occurs either
  through signature verification, a process in which trained elections
  workers review the signature on the early ballot and compare it to the
  signature on file, or through ID presented by the voter when dropping
  off their ballot at the polls.

  Voters can track their ballot status to receive alerts, including when the
  ballot is mailed and counted, at trackmyballot.azsos.gov/voter.
