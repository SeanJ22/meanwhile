L9: REGISTRATION DEADLINE – October 5, 2026
L10: Voter Registration closes at 9:00 P.M. at the Board of Elections. Voters may register online at VOTEOHIO.GOV until midnight.
L11: ABSENTEE VOTING BEGINS – October 6, 2026
L12: Ballots requested by application start being mailed out. In-person voting at our office begins.

L14: BALLOT RETURN DEADLINE – All absentee ballots must be received by the Board of Elections or placed in the office dropbox by 7:30 p.m. on Election Day (November 3, 2026) to be counted. Ballots that arrive after this time will NOT be counted.
