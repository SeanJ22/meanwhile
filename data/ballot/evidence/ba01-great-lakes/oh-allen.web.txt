L435:                 Amendment
L436: 
L437:                 TO REQUIRE VOTERS TO
L438:                 PRESENT PHOTO
L439:                 IDENTIFICATION IN ORDER TO
L440:                 VOTE
L441: 
L442:                 Proposed by Joint Resolution of the
L443:                 General Assembly
L444: 
L445:                 To enact Section 5 of Article V of the
L446:                 Constitution of the State of Ohio
L447: 
L448:                 A majority yes vote is required for the
L449:                 adoption of Section 5.
L450: 
L451:                 This proposed amendment would:
L452: 
L453:                 • Require voters to present an approved
L454:                 form of government-issued photo
L455:                 identification in order to vote.
L456: 
L457:                 • Allow state law to provide for an
L458:                 alternative method of verifying a voter’s
L459:                 identity for a voter who has a sincere
L460:                 religious objection to being photographed
L461:                 and, therefore, does not possess an
L462:                 approved form of government-issued
L463:                 photo identification.
L464: 
L465:                 • Require that voters who do not vote in
L466:                 person provide an approved form of
L467:                 government-issued photo identification,
L468:                 unless the law authorizes those persons to
L469:                 vote by providing a signature and at least
L470:                 one other specified unique identifier.
L471: 
L472:                 • Define approved forms of government-
L473:                 issued photo identification as an
L474:                 unexpired Ohio driver license or state
L475:                 identification card; a United States
L476:                 passport or passport card; or a United
L477:                 States military identification card, Ohio
L478:                 National Guard identification card, or
L479:                 United States Department of Veterans
L480:                 Affairs identification card.
L481: 
L482:                 If approved, the amendment shall take
L483:                 effect immediately.

L485:                      INSTRUCTIONS TO VOTER FOR INDICATING YOUR CHOICES ON A
L486:                             FEDERAL WRITE-IN ABSENTEE BALLOT (FWAB):
L487: To complete a Federal Write-In Absentee Ballot (FWAB), go to www.fvap.gov. You have the option of downloading a blank, hard copy
L488: FWAB to complete by hand, or proceeding through electronic completion of the FWAB using the website’s FWAB Wizard.
L489: The first page of the FWAB is a Voter Declaration/Affirmation you must complete in order for your ballot to count.
L490: After completing the Voter Declaration/Affirmation, use the initial election notice issued on the 100th day before the election and the
L491: updated election notice issued on the 46th day before the election as a guide to:
L492:            (1) write the name of each candidate or issue contest for which you are casting a vote and then
L493:            (2) write the name of your choice of candidate or choice for or against an issue.
L494: After you have completed the FWAB, you must PRINT the ballot and MAIL it to your county board of elections at this address:
L495: Allen County Board of Elections, 204 N. Main Street, Lima, OH 45801.
L496: A complete listing of all county board of elections mailing addresses and contact information is available at https://ohiosos.gov/SOS/
L497: elections/electionsofficials/boeDirectory.aspx#dir.
L498:             Do not send your ballot via e-mail or fax; Ohio law prohibits electronic transmission of a voted ballot.
