L352@P7: INSTRUCTIONS TO VOTER FOR INDICATING YOUR CHOICES ON A
L353@P7: FEDERAL WRITE-IN ABSENTEE BALLOT (FWAB):
L354@P7: To complete a Federal Write-In Absentee Ballot (FWAB), go to www.fvap.gov. You have the option of downloading a blank, hard
L355@P7: copy FWAB to complete by hand, or proceeding through electronic completion of the FWAB using the website’s FWAB Wizard.
L356@P7: The first page of the FWAB is a Voter Declaration/Affirmation you must complete in order for your ballot to count.
L357@P7: After completing the Voter Declaration/Affirmation, use the initial election notice issued on the 100th day before the election and the
L358@P7: updated election notice issued on the 46th day before the election as a guide to:
L359@P7: (1) write the name of each candidate or issue contest for which you are casting a vote and then
L360@P7: (2) write the name of your choice of candidate or choice for or against an issue.
L361@P7: After you have completed the FWAB, you must PRINT the ballot and MAIL it to your county board of elections at this
L362@P7: address: Belmont County Board of Elections, 52180 National Road, PO Box 994, St. Clairsville, OH 43950.
L363@P7: A complete listing of all county board of elections mailing addresses and contact information is available at
L364@P7: https://ohiosos.gov/SOS/elections/electionsofficials/boeDirectory.aspx#dir.
L365@P7: Do not send your ballot via e-mail or fax; Ohio law prohibits electronic transmission of a voted ballot.
