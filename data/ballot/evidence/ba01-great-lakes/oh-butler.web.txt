L5@P0: Board of Elections, therefore “(P) Not pulled” may appear by some of the listed candidates, otherwise the date the candidate pulled their petitions will be displayed
L6@P0: (ex. (P) 01-01-2026). If a candidate has not yet filed their petitions, “(F) Not filed” will appear by their name. Once a candidate files their petitions, the date will be
L7@P0: displayed (ex. (F) 01-02-2026). If a candidate’s petitions have not been certified to the ballot, “(C) Not certified” will appear by their name. Once a candidate is
L8@P0: certified by the Board, the date will be displayed (ex. (c) 01-03-2026). Candidates and issues for districts that overlap with surrounding counties may be required to
L9@P0: file their petitions with another county board of elections, in this case the name of the other county will appear by their name.
