L124@P1: State Issue 3
L125@P1: Proposed Constitutional Amendment
L126@P1: TO REQUIRE VOTERS TO PRESENT PHOTO IDENTIFICATION IN ORDER TO VOTE
L127@P1: Proposed by Joint Resolution of the General Assembly
L128@P1: To enact Section 5 of Article V of the Constitution of the State of Ohio
L129@P1: A majority yes vote is required for the adoption of Section 5.
L130@P1: This proposed amendment would:
L131@P1: • Require voters to present an approved form of government-issued photo identification in order to vote.
L132@P1: • Allow state law to provide for an alternative method of verifying a voter’s identity for a voter who has a sincere religious objection to being
L133@P1: photographed and, therefore, does not possess an approved form of government-issued photo identification.
L134@P1: • Require that voters who do not vote in person provide an approved form of government-issued photo identification, unless the law authorizes
L135@P1: those persons to vote by providing a signature and at least one other specified unique identifier.
L136@P1: • Define approved forms of government-issued photo identification as an unexpired Ohio driver license or state identification card; a United
L137@P1: States passport or passport card; or a United States military identification card, Ohio National Guard identification card, or United States
L138@P1: Department of Veterans Affairs identification card.
L139@P1: If approved, the amendment shall take effect immediately.
L140@P1: A “YES” vote means approval of the amendment.
L141@P1: A “NO” vote means disapproval of the amendment.
