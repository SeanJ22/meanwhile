L314:          INSTRUCTIONS TO VOTER FOR INDICATING YOUR CHOICES ON A
L315:                 FEDERAL WRITE-IN ABSENTEE BALLOT (FWAB):
L316: 
L317: 
L318: To complete a Federal Write-In Absentee Ballot (FWAB), go to www.fvap.gov. You have the option of
L319: downloading a blank, hard copy FWAB to complete by hand, or proceeding through electronic completion of
L320: the FWAB using the website’s FWAB Wizard.
L321: 
L322: The first page of the FWAB is a Voter Declaration/Affirmation you must complete in order for your ballot to
L323: count.
L324: 
L325: After completing the Voter Declaration/Affirmation, use the initial election notice issued on the 100th day before
L326: the election and the updated election notice issued on the 46th day before the election as a guide to:
L327: 
L328:          (1) write the name of each candidate or issue contest for which you are casting a vote and then
L329:          (2) write the name of your choice of candidate or choice for or against an issue.
L330: 
L331: After you have completed the FWAB, you must PRINT the ballot and MAIL it to your county board of
L332: elections at this address: 76 S. Riverside Dr., Batavia, Ohio 45103.
L333: 
L334: A complete listing of all county board of elections mailing addresses and contact information is available at:
L335: https://ohiosos.gov/SOS/elections/electionsofficials/boeDirectory.aspx#dir.
L336: 
L337:  Do not send your ballot via e-mail or fax. Ohio law prohibits electronic transmission of a voted ballot.
