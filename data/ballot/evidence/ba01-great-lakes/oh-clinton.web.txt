L136@P3: Proposed Constitutional Amendment
L137@P3: TO REQUIRE VOTERS TO PRESENT PHOTO IDENTIFICATION IN
L138@P3: ORDER TO VOTE
L139@P3: Proposed by Joint Resolution of the
L140@P3: General Assembly
L141@P3: To enact Section 5 of Article V of the
L142@P3: Constitution of the State of Ohio
L143@P3: A majority yes vote is required for the adoption of Section 5.
L144@P3: This proposed amendment would:
L145@P3: • Require voters to present an approved form of government-issued photo
L146@P3: identification in order to vote.
L147@P3: • Allow state law to provide for an alternative method of verifying a voter’s
L148@P3: identity for a voter who has a sincere religious objection to being
L149@P3: photographed and, therefore, does not possess an approved form of
L150@P3: government-issued photo identification.
L151@P3: • Require that voters who do not vote in person provide an approved form of
L152@P3: government-issued photo identification, unless the law authorizes those
L153@P3: persons to vote by providing a signature and at least one other specified
L154@P3: unique identifier.
L155@P3: • Define approved forms of government-issued photo identification as an
L156@P3: unexpired Ohio driver license or state identification card; a United States
L157@P3: passport or passport card; or a United States military identification card,
L158@P3: Ohio National Guard identification card, or United States Department of
L159@P3: Veterans Affairs identification card.
L160@P3: If approved, the amendment shall take effect immediately.
L161@P3: A “YES” vote means approval of the amendment.
L162@P3: A “NO” vote means disapproval of the amendment.

L414@P9: INSTRUCTIONS TO VOTER FOR INDICATING YOUR CHOICES ON A
L415@P9: FEDERAL WRITE-IN ABSENTEE BALLOT (FWAB):
L416@P9: To complete a Federal Write-In Absentee Ballot (FWAB), go to www.fvap.gov. You have the option of downloading a blank, hard
L417@P9: copy FWAB to complete by hand, or proceeding through electronic completion of the FWAB using the website’s FWAB Wizard.
L418@P9: The first page of the FWAB is a Voter Declaration/Affirmation you must complete in order for your ballot to count.
L419@P9: After completing the Voter Declaration/Affirmation, use the initial election notice issued on the 100th day before the election and the
L420@P9: updated election notice issued on the 46th day before the election as a guide to:
L421@P9: (1) write the name of each candidate or issue contest for which you are casting a vote and then
L422@P9: (2) write the name of your choice of candidate or choice for or against an issue.
L423@P9: After you have completed the FWAB, you must PRINT the ballot and MAIL it to your county board of elections at this
L424@P9: address: Clinton County Board of Elections, 111 S. Nelson Ave., Suite 4, Wilmington, OH 45177.
L425@P9: A complete listing of all county board of elections mailing addresses and contact information is available at
L426@P9: https://ohiosos.gov/SOS/elections/electionsofficials/boeDirectory.aspx#dir.
L427@P9: Do not send your ballot via e-mail or fax; Ohio law prohibits electronic transmission of a voted ballot.
