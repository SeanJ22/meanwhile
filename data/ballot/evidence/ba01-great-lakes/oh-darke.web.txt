L151@P3: Proposed Constitutional Amendment
L152@P3: TO REQUIRE VOTERS TO PRESENT PHOTO
L153@P3: IDENTIFICATION IN ORDER TO VOTE
L154@P3: Proposed by Joint Resolution of the General Assembly
L155@P3: To enact Section 5 of Article V of the Constitution of the State of Ohio
L156@P3: A majority yes vote is required for the adoption of Section 5. A majority
L157@P3: affirmative vote is necessary for passage.
L158@P3: This proposed amendment would:
L159@P3: • Require voters to present an approved form of government-issued photo
L160@P3: identification in order to vote.
L161@P3: • Allow state law to provide for an alternative method of verifying a voter's identity
L162@P3: for a voter who has a sincere religious objection to being photographed and,
L163@P3: therefore, does not possess an approved form of government-issued photo
L164@P3: identification.
L165@P3: • Require that voters who do not vote in person provide an approved form of
L166@P3: government-issued photo identification, unless the law authorizes those
L167@P3: persons to vote by providing a signature and at least one other specified unique
L168@P3: identifier.
L169@P3: • Define approved forms of government-issued photo identification as an
L170@P3: unexpired Ohio driver license or state identification card; a United States
L171@P3: passport or passport card; or a United States military identification card, Ohio
L172@P3: National Guard identification card, or United States Department of Veterans
L173@P3: Affairs identification card.
L174@P3: If approved, the amendment shall take effect immediately.
L175@P3: A "YES" vote means approval of the amendment.
L176@P3: A "NO" vote means disapproval of the amendment.

L231@P5: INSTRUCTIONS TO VOTER FOR INDICATING YOUR CHOICES ON A
L232@P5: FEDERAL WRITE-IN ABSENTEE BALLOT (FWAB):
L233@P5: To complete a Federal Write-In Absentee Ballot (FWAB), go to www.fvap.gov. You have the option of downloading a blank, hard
L234@P5: copy FWAB to complete by hand, or proceeding through electronic completion of the FWAB using the website’s FWAB Wizard.
L235@P5: The first page of the FWAB is a Voter Declaration/Affirmation you must complete in order for your ballot to count.
L236@P5: After completing the Voter Declaration/Affirmation, use the initial election notice issued on the 100th day before the election and the
L237@P5: updated election notice issued on the 46th day before the election as a guide to:
L238@P5: (1) write the name of each candidate or issue contest for which you are casting a vote and then
L239@P5: (2) write the name of your choice of candidate or choice for or against an issue.
L240@P5: After you have completed the FWAB, you must PRINT the ballot and MAIL it to your county board of elections at this
L241@P5: address: Darke County Board of Elections, 300 Garst Ave., Greenville, OH 45331.
L242@P5: A complete listing of all county board of elections mailing addresses and contact information is available at
L243@P5: https://ohiosos.gov/SOS/elections/electionsofficials/boeDirectory.aspx#dir.
L244@P5: Do not send your ballot via e-mail or fax; Ohio law prohibits electronic transmission of a voted ballot.
