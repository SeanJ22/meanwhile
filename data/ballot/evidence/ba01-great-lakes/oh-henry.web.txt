L204@P3-5: 11.4, 22.2INSTRUCTIONS TO VOTER FOR INDICATING YOUR CHOICES ON A
L205@P5: FEDERAL WRITE-IN ABSENTEE BALLOT (FWAB):
L206@P5: To complete a Federal Write-In Absentee Ballot (FWAB), go to www.fvap.gov. You have the option of
L207@P5: downloading a blank, hard copy FWAB to complete by hand, or proceeding through electronic completion of
L208@P5: the FWAB using the website’s FWAB Wizard.
L209@P5: The first page of the FWAB is a Voter Declaration/Affirmation you must complete in order for your ballot to
L210@P5: count.
L211@P5: After completing the Voter Declaration/Affirmation, use the initial election notice issued on the 100th day before
L212@P5: the election and the updated election notice issued on the 46
L213@P5: th day before the election as a guide to:
L214@P5: (1) write the name of each candidate or issue contest for which you are casting a vote and then
L215@P5: (2) write the name of your choice of candidate or choice for or against an issue.
L216@P5: After you have completed the FWAB, you must PRINT the ballot and MAIL it to your county board of
L217@P5: elections at this address: 1827 Oakwood Ave, Napoleon, OH 43545.
L218@P5: A complete listing of all county board of elections mailing addresses and contact information is available at:
L219@P5: https://ohiosos.gov/SOS/elections/electionsofficials/boeDirectory.aspx#dir.
