L139@P2: Proposed Constitutional Amendment
L140@P2: TO REQUIRE VOTERS TO PRESENT PHOTO IDENTIFICATION IN ORDER TO VOTE
L141@P2: Proposed by Joint Resolution of the General Assembly
L142@P2: To enact Section 5 of Article V of the Constitution of the State of Ohio
L143@P2: A majority yes vote is required for the adoption of Section 5.
L144@P2: This proposed amendment would:
L145@P2: • Require voters to present an approved form of government-issued photo identification in order to vote.
L146@P2: • Allow state law to provide for an alternative method of verifying a voter's identity for a voter who has a
L147@P2: sincere religious objection to being photographed and, therefore, does not possess an approved form of
L148@P2: government-issued photo identification.
L149@P2: • Require that voters who do not vote in person provide an approved form of government-issued photo
L150@P2: identification, unless the law authorizes those persons to vote by providing a signature and at least one
L151@P2: other specified unique identifier.
L152@P2: • Define approved forms of government-issued photo identification as an unexpired Ohio driver license or
L153@P2: state identification card; a United States passport or passport card; or a United States military
L154@P2: identification card, Ohio National Guard identification card, or United States Department of Veterans
L155@P2: Affairs identification card.
L156@P2: If approved, the amendment shall take effect immediately.
L157@P2: A "YES" vote means approval of the amendment.
L158@P2: A "NO" vote means disapproval of the amendment.

L389@P7: INSTRUCTIONS TO VOTER FOR INDICATING YOUR CHOICES ON A
L390@P7: FEDERAL WRITE-IN ABSENTEE BALLOT (FWAB):
L391@P7: To complete a Federal Write-In Absentee Ballot (FWAB), go to http://www.fvap.gov. You have the option of downloading a blank,
L392@P7: hard copy FWAB to complete by hand, or proceeding through electronic completion of the FWAB using the website’s FWAB
L393@P7: Wizard.
L394@P7: The first page of the FWAB is a Voter Declaration/Affirmation you must complete in order for your ballot to count.
L395@P7: After completing the Voter Declaration/Affirmation, use the initial election notice issued on the 100th day before the election and the
L396@P7: updated election notice issued on the 46th day before the election as a guide to:
L397@P7: (1) write the name of each candidate or issue contest for which you are casting a vote and then
L398@P7: (2) write the name of your choice of candidate or choice for or against an issue.
L399@P7: After you have completed the FWAB, you must PRINT the ballot and MAIL it to your county board of elections at this
L400@P7: address: Holmes County Board of Elections, 75 E. Clinton St., Suite 108, Millersburg, OH 44654. A complete listing of all
L401@P7: county board of elections mailing addresses and contact information is available at https://www.sos.state.oh.us/elections/electionsofficials/county-boards-of-elections-directory/.
L402@P7: Do not send your ballot via e-mail or fax; Ohio law prohibits electronic transmission of a voted ballot.
