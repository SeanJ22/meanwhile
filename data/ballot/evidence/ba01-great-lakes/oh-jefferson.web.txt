L217@P5: INSTRUCTIONS TO VOTER FOR INDICATING YOUR CHOICES ON A
L218@P5: FEDERAL WRITE-IN ABSENTEE BALLOT (FWAB):
L219@P5: To complete a Federal Write-In Absentee Ballot (FWAB), go to www.fvap.gov. You have the option of downloading a
L220@P5: blank, hard copy FWAB to complete by hand, or proceeding through electronic completion of the FWAB using the
L221@P5: website’s FWAB Wizard.
L222@P5: The first page of the FWAB is a Voter Declaration/Affirmation you must complete in order for your ballot to count.
L223@P5: After completing the Voter Declaration/Affirmation, use the initial election notice issued on the 100th day before the
L224@P5: election and the updated election notice issued on the 46
L225@P5: th day before the election as a guide to:
L226@P5: (1) write the name of each candidate or issue contest for which you are casting a vote and then
L227@P5: (2) write the name of your choice of candidate or choice for or against an issue.
L228@P5: After you have completed the FWAB, you must PRINT the ballot and MAIL it to your county board of elections at
L229@P5: this address: Jefferson County Board of Elections, 500 Market Street 1st Floor, Suite 1, Steubenville OH 43952.
L230@P5: A complete listing of all county board of elections mailing addresses and contact information is available at:
L231@P5: https://ohiosos.gov/SOS/elections/electionsofficials/boeDirectory.aspx#dir.
L232@P5: Do not send your ballot via e-mail or fax. Ohio law prohibits electronic transmission of a voted ballot.
