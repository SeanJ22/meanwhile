L322@P7: INSTRUCTIONS TO VOTER FOR INDICATING YOUR CHOICES ON A
L323@P7: FEDERAL WRITE-IN ABSENTEE BALLOT (FWAB):
L324@P7: To complete a Federal Write-In Absentee Ballot (FWAB), go to www.fvap.gov. You have the option of downloading a blank, hard
L325@P7: copy FWAB to complete by hand, or proceeding through electronic completion of the FWAB using the website’s FWAB Wizard.
L326@P7: The first page of the FWAB is a Voter Declaration/Affirmation you must complete in order for your ballot to count.
L327@P7: After completing the Voter Declaration/Affirmation, use the initial election notice issued on the 100th day before the election and the
L328@P7: updated election notice issued on the 46th day before the election as a guide to:
L329@P7: (1) write the name of each candidate or issue contest for which you are casting a vote and then
L330@P7: (2) write the name of your choice of candidate or choice for or against an issue.
L331@P7: After you have completed the FWAB, you must PRINT the ballot and MAIL it to your county board of elections at this
L332@P7: address: Knox County Board of Elections, 104 E. Sugar St, Mount Vernon, OH 43050.
L333@P7: A complete listing of all county board of elections mailing addresses and contact information is available at
L334@P7: https://ohiosos.gov/SOS/elections/electionsofficials/boeDirectory.aspx#dir.
L335@P7: Do not send your ballot via e-mail or fax; Ohio law prohibits electronic transmission of a voted ballot.
