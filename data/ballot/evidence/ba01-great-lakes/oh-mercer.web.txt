L138@P3: TO REQUIRE VOTERS TO PRESENT PHOTO IDENTIFICATION IN
L139@P3: ORDER TO VOTE
L140@P3: Proposed by Joint Resolution of the General Assembly
L141@P3: To enact Section 5 of Article V of the Constitution of the State of Ohio
L142@P3: A majority yes vote is required for the adoption of Section 5.
L143@P3: This proposed amendment would:
L144@P3: • Require voters to present an approved form of government-issued photo
L145@P3: identification in order to vote. • Allow state law to provide for an alternative
L146@P3: method of verifying a voter’s identity for a voter who has a sincere religious
L147@P3: objection to being photographed and, therefore, does not possess an approved
L148@P3: form of government-issued photo identification.
L149@P3: • Require that voters who do not vote in person provide an approved form of
L150@P3: government-issued photo identification, unless the law authorizes those persons
L151@P3: to vote by providing a signature and at least one other specified unique identifier.
L152@P3:  • Define approved forms of government-issued photo identification as an
L153@P3: unexpired Ohio driver license or state identification card; a United States
L154@P3: passport or passport card; or a United States military identification card, Ohio
L155@P3: National Guard identification card, or United States Department of Veterans
L156@P3: Affairs identification card.
L157@P3: If approved, the amendment shall take effect immediately.

L231@P4: INSTRUCTIONS TO VOTER FOR INDICATING YOUR CHOICES ON A
L232@P4: FEDERAL WRITE-IN ABSENTEE BALLOT (FWAB):
L233@P4: To complete a Federal Write-In Absentee Ballot (FWAB), go to www.fvap.gov. You have the option of downloading a
L234@P4: blank, hard copy FWAB to complete by hand, or proceeding through electronic completion of the FWAB using the
L235@P4: website’s FWAB Wizard.
L236@P4-5: The first page of the FWAB is a Voter Declaration/Affirmation you must complete in order for your ballot to count.After completing the Voter Declaration/Affirmation, use the initial election notice issued on the 100th day before the
L237@P5: election and the updated election notice issued on the 46th day before the election as a guide to:
L238@P5: (1) write the name of each candidate or issue contest for which you are casting a vote and then
L239@P5: (2) write the name of your choice of candidate or choice for or against an issue.
L240@P5: After you have completed the FWAB, you must PRINT the ballot and MAIL it to your county board of elections at
L241@P5: this address: Mercer County Ohio Board of Elections, 101 North Main Street Room 107, Celina, Ohio 45822
L242@P5: A complete listing of all county board of elections mailing addresses and contact information is available at:
L243@P5: https://ohiosos.gov/SOS/elections/electionsofficials/boeDirectory.aspx#dir
L244@P5: .Do not send your ballot via e-mail or fax; Ohio law prohibits electronic transmission of a voted ballot.
