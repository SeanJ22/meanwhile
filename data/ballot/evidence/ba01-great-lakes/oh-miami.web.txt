L179@P3: TO REQUIRE VOTERS TO PRESENT PHOTO IDENTIFICATION IN ORDER TO VOTE
L180@P3: Proposed by Joint Resolution of the General Assembly
L181@P3: To enact Section 5 of Article V of the Constitution of the State of Ohio
L182@P3: A majority yes vote is required for the adoption of Section 5.
L183@P3: This proposed amendment would:
L184@P3: • Require voters to present an approved form of government-issued photo identification in order
L185@P3: to vote.
L186@P3: • Allow state law to provide for an alternative method of verifying a voter’s identity for a voter who
L187@P3: has a sincere religious objection to being photographed and, therefore, does not possess an
L188@P3: approved form of government-issued photo identification.
L189@P3: • Require that voters who do not vote in person provide an approved form of government-issued
L190@P3: photo identification, unless the law authorizes those persons to vote by providing a signature and
L191@P3: at least one other specified unique identifier.
L192@P3: • Define approved forms of government-issued photo identification as an unexpired Ohio driver
L193@P3: license or state identification card; a United States passport or passport card; or a United States
L194@P3: military identification card, Ohio National Guard identification card, or United States Department
L195@P3: of Veterans Affairs identification card.
L196@P3: If approved, the amendment shall take effect immediately.
L197@P3: A "YES" vote means approval of the amendment.
L198@P3: A "NO" vote means disapproval of the amendment.

L334@P6: INSTRUCTIONS TO VOTER FOR INDICATING YOUR CHOICES ON A
L335@P6: FEDERAL WRITE-IN ABSENTEE BALLOT (FWAB):
L336@P6: To complete a Federal Write-In Absentee Ballot (FWAB), go to www.fvap.gov. You have the option of downloading a blank, hard
L337@P6: copy FWAB to complete by hand, or proceeding through electronic completion of the FWAB using the website’s FWAB Wizard.
L338@P6: The first page of the FWAB is a Voter Declaration/Affirmation you must complete in order for your ballot to count.
L339@P6: After completing the Voter Declaration/Affirmation, use the initial election notice issued on the 100th day before the election and the
L340@P6: updated election notice issued on the 46th day before the election as a guide to:
L341@P6: (1) write the name of each candidate or issue contest for which you are casting a vote and then
L342@P6: (2) write the name of your choice of candidate or choice for or against an issue.
L343@P6: After you have completed the FWAB, you must PRINT the ballot and MAIL it to your county board of elections at this
L344@P6: address: Miami County Board of Elections, 215 West Main Street, Troy, OH 45373.
L345@P6: A complete listing of all county board of elections mailing addresses and contact information is available at
L346@P6: https://ohiosos.gov/SOS/elections/electionsofficials/boeDirectory.aspx#dir.
L347@P6: Do not send your ballot via e-mail or fax; Ohio law prohibits electronic transmission of a voted ballot.
