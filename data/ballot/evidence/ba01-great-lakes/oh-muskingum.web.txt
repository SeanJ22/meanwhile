L292@P7: INSTRUCTIONS TO VOTER FOR INDICATING YOUR CHOICES ON A
L293@P7: FEDERAL WRITE-IN ABSENTEE BALLOT (FWAB):
L294@P7: To complete a Federal Write-In Absentee Ballot (FWAB), go to www.fvap.gov. You have the option of downloading a blank, hard
L295@P7: copy FWAB to complete by hand, or proceeding through electronic completion of the FWAB using the website’s FWAB Wizard.
L296@P7: The first page of the FWAB is a Voter Declaration/Affirmation you must complete in order for your ballot to count.
L297@P7: After completing the Voter Declaration/Affirmation, use the initial election notice issued on the 100th day before the election and the
L298@P7: updated election notice issued on the 46th day before the election as a guide to:
L299@P7: (1) write the name of each candidate or issue contest for which you are casting a vote and then
L300@P7: (2) write the name of your choice of candidate or choice for or against an issue.
L301@P7: After you have completed the FWAB, you must PRINT the ballot and MAIL it to your county board of elections at this
L302@P7: address: Muskingum County Board of Elections, 627 Market Street, Zanesville, OH 43701.
L303@P7: A complete listing of all county board of elections mailing addresses and contact information is available at
L304@P7: https://ohiosos.gov/SOS/elections/electionsofficials/boeDirectory.aspx#dir.
L305@P7: Do not send your ballot via e-mail or fax; Ohio law prohibits electronic transmission of a voted ballot.
