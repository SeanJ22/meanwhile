L381:          INSTRUCTIONS TO VOTER FOR INDICATING YOUR CHOICES ON A
L382:                 FEDERAL WRITE-IN ABSENTEE BALLOT (FWAB):
L383: To complete a Federal Write-In Absentee Ballot (FWAB), go to www.fvap.gov. You have the option of
L384: downloading a blank, hard copy FWAB to complete by hand, or proceeding through electronic completion of
L385: the FWAB using the website’s FWAB Wizard.
L386: 
L387: The first page of the FWAB is a Voter Declaration/Affirmation you must complete in order for your ballot to
L388: count.
L389: 
L390: After completing the Voter Declaration/Affirmation, use the initial election notice issued on the 100th day before
L391: the election and the updated election notice issued on the 46th day before the election as a guide to:
L392: 
L393:          (1) write the name of each candidate or issue contest for which you are casting a vote and then
L394:          (2) write the name of your choice of candidate or choice for or against an issue.
L395: 
L396: After you have completed the FWAB, you must PRINT the ballot and MAIL it to your county board of
L397: elections at this address: 449 S. Meridian, Ravenna, OH 44266. A complete listing of all county board of
L398: elections mailing addresses and contact information is available at
L399: http://www.sos.state.oh.us/SOS/elections/electionsofficials/boeDirectory.aspx#dir.
L400: 
L401:  Do not send your ballot via e-mail or fax; Ohio law prohibits electronic transmission of a voted ballot.
