L251@P5: INSTRUCTIONS TO VOTER FOR INDICATING YOUR CHOICES ON A
L252@P5: FEDERAL WRITE-IN ABSENTEE BALLOT (FWAB):
L253@P5: To complete a Federal Write-In Absentee Ballot (FWAB), go to http://www.fvap.gov. You have the option of
L254@P5: downloading a blank, hard copy FWAB to complete by hand, or proceeding through electronic completion of the
L255@P5: FWAB using the website’s FWAB Wizard.
L256@P5: The first page of the FWAB is a Voter Declaration/Affirmation you must complete in order for your ballot to count.
L257@P5: After completing the Voter Declaration/Affirmation, use the initial election notice issued on the 100th day before the
L258@P5: election and the updated election notice issued on the 46th day before the election as a guide to:
L259@P5: (1) write the name of each candidate or issue contest for which you are casting a vote and then
L260@P5: (2) write the name of your choice of candidate or choice for or against an issue.
L261@P5: After you have completed the FWAB, you must PRINT the ballot and MAIL it to your county board of elections
L262@P5: at this address: Scioto County Board of Elections, 602 7th St., Room 105, Portsmouth, OH 45662. A complete
L263@P5: listing of all county board of elections mailing addresses and contact information is available at
L264@P5: https://www.sos.state.oh.us/elections/elections-officials/county-boards-of-elections-directory/.
L265@P5: Do not send your ballot via e-mail or fax; Ohio law prohibits electronic transmission of a voted ballot.
