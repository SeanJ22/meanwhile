L131@P3: Constitution of the State of Ohio
L132@P3: A majority yes vote is required for the adoption of
L133@P3: Section 5.
L134@P3: This proposed amendment would:
L135@P3: Require voters to present an approved form of
L136@P3: government-issued photo identification in order to
L137@P3: vote.
L138@P3: Allow state law to provide for an alternative method
L139@P3: of verifying a voter’s identity for a voter who has a
L140@P3: sincere religious objection to being photographed
L141@P3: and, therefore, does not possess an approved form
L142@P3: of government-issued photo identification.
L143@P3: Require that voters who do not vote in person
L144@P3: provide an approved form of government-issued
L145@P3: photo identification, unless the law authorizes those
L146@P3: persons to vote by providing a signature and at
L147@P3: least one other specified unique identifier.
L148@P3: Define approved forms of government-issued photo
L149@P3: identification as an unexpired Ohio driver license or
L150@P3: state identification card; a United States passport or
L151@P3: passport card; or a United States military
L152@P3: identification card, Ohio National Guard
L153@P3: identification card, or United States Department of
L154@P3: Veterans Affairs identification card.
L155@P3: If approved, the amendment shall take effect
L156@P3: immediately.

L158@P3: amendment.
