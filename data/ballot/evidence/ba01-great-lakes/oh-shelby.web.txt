L207@P4: INSTRUCTIONS TO VOTER FOR INDICATING YOUR CHOICES ON A
L208@P4: FEDERAL WRITE-IN ABSENTEE BALLOT (FWAB):
L209@P4: To complete a Federal Write-In Absentee Ballot (FWAB), go to www.fvap.gov. You have the option of downloading a blank, hard
L210@P4: copy FWAB to complete by hand, or proceeding through electronic completion of the FWAB using the website’s FWAB Wizard.
L211@P4: The first page of the FWAB is a Voter Declaration/Affirmation you must complete in order for your ballot to count.
L212@P4: After completing the Voter Declaration/Affirmation, use the initial election notice issued on the 100th day before the election and the
L213@P4: updated election notice issued on the 46th day before the election as a guide to:
L214@P4: (1) write the name of each candidate or issue contest for which you are casting a vote and then
L215@P4: (2) write the name of your choice of candidate or choice for or against an issue.
L216@P4: After you have completed the FWAB, you must PRINT the ballot and MAIL it to your county board of elections at this
L217@P4: address: Shelby County Board of Elections, 230 E. Court St., Sidney, OH 45365.
L218@P4: A complete listing of all county board of elections mailing addresses and contact information is available at
L219@P4: https://ohiosos.gov/SOS/elections/electionsofficials/boeDirectory.aspx#dir.
L220@P4: Do not send your ballot via e-mail or fax; Ohio law prohibits electronic transmission of a voted ballot.
