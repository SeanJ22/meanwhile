L160@P4: • Require voters to present an approved form
L161@P4: of government-issued photo identification in
L162@P4: order to vote.
L163@P4: • Allow state law to provide for an alternative
L164@P4: method of verifying a voter’s identity for a voter

L166@P4: photographed and, therefore, does not possess
L167@P4: an approved form of government-issued photo
L168@P4: identification.
L169@P4: • Require that voters who do not vote in person
L170@P4: provide an approved form of governmentissued photo identification, unless the law
L171@P4: authorizes those persons to vote by providing a
L172@P4: signature and at least one other specified
L173@P4: unique identifier.
L174@P4: • Define approved forms of government-issued
L175@P4: photo identification as an unexpired Ohio driver
L176@P4: license or state identification card; a United
L177@P4: States passport or passport card; or a United

L179@P4: Guard identification card, or United States

L348@P7-8: NEWTON TWP DINSTRUCTIONS TO VOTER FOR INDICATING YOUR CHOICES ON A
L349@P8: FEDERAL WRITE-IN ABSENTEE BALLOT (FWAB):
L350@P8: To complete a Federal Write-In Absentee Ballot (FWAB), go to www.fvap.gov. You have the option of
L351@P8: downloading a blank, hard copy FWAB to complete by hand, or proceeding through electronic completion of
L352@P8: the FWAB using the website’s FWAB Wizard.
L353@P8: The first page of the FWAB is a Voter Declaration/Affirmation you must complete in order for your ballot to
L354@P8: count.
L355@P8: After completing the Voter Declaration/Affirmation, use the initial election notice issued on the 100th day before
L356@P8: the election and the updated election notice issued on the 46
L357@P8: th day before the election as a guide to:
L358@P8: (1) write the name of each candidate or issue contest for which you are casting a vote and then
L359@P8: (2) write the name of your choice of candidate or choice for or against an issue.
L360@P8: After you have completed the FWAB, you must PRINT the ballot and MAIL it to your county board of
L361@P8: elections at this address: 2947 Youngstown Rd SE Warren OH 44484.
L362@P8: A complete listing of all county board of elections mailing addresses and contact information is available at:
L363@P8: https://ohiosos.gov/SOS/elections/electionsofficials/boeDirectory.aspx#dir.
L364@P8: Do not send your ballot via e-mail or fax. Ohio law prohibits electronic transmission of a voted ballot.
