L132@P3: Proposed Constitutional Amendment
L133@P3: TO REQUIRE VOTERS TO PRESENT PHOTO IDENTIFICATION IN
L134@P3: ORDER TO VOTE
L135@P3: Proposed by Joint Resolution of the General Assembly
L136@P3: To enact Section 5 of Article V of the Constitution of the State of Ohio
L137@P3: A majority yes vote is required for the adoption of Section 5.
L138@P3: All Precincts
L139@P3: This proposed amendment would:
L140@P3: Require voters to present an approved form of government-issued photo identification in order to vote.
L141@P3: Allow state law to provide for an alternative method of verifying a voter’s identity for a voter who has a sincere religious
L142@P3: objection to being photographed and, therefore, does not possess an approved form of government-issued photo
L143@P3: identification.
L144@P3: Require that voters who do not vote in person provide an approved form of government-issued photo identification,
L145@P3: unless the law authorizes those persons to vote by providing a signature and at least one other specified unique identifier.
L146@P3: Define approved forms of government-issued photo identification as an unexpired Ohio driver license or state
L147@P3: identification card; a United States passport or passport card; or a United States military identification card, Ohio
L148@P3: National Guard identification card, or United States Department of Veterans Affairs identification card.
L149@P3: If approved, the amendment shall take effect immediately.
L150@P3: A “YES” vote means approval of the amendment.
L151@P3: A “NO” vote means disapproval of the amendment.

L235@P5: INSTRUCTIONS TO VOTER FOR INDICATING YOUR CHOICES ON A
L236@P5: FEDERAL WRITE-IN ABSENTEE BALLOT (FWAB):
L237@P5: To complete a Federal Write-In Absentee Ballot (FWAB), go to www.fvap.gov. You have the option of downloading a blank, hard
L238@P5: copy FWAB to complete by hand, or proceeding through electronic completion of the FWAB using the website’s FWAB Wizard.
L239@P5: The first page of the FWAB is a Voter Declaration/Affirmation you must complete in order for your ballot to count.
L240@P5: After completing the Voter Declaration/Affirmation, use the initial election notice issued on the 100th day before the election and the
L241@P5: updated election notice issued on the 46th day before the election as a guide to:
L242@P5: (1) write the name of each candidate or issue contest for which you are casting a vote and then
L243@P5: (2) write the name of your choice of candidate or choice for or against an issue.
L244@P5: After you have completed the FWAB, you must PRINT the ballot and MAIL it to your county board of elections at this
L245@P5: address: Union County Board of Elections, 835 E. 5th St, Suite A, Marysville, Ohio 43040.
L246@P5: A complete listing of all county board of elections mailing addresses and contact information is available at
L247@P5: https://ohiosos.gov/SOS/elections/electionsofficials/boeDirectory.aspx#dir.
L248@P5: Do not send your ballot via e-mail or fax; Ohio law prohibits electronic transmission of a voted ballot.
