L164@P3: Proposed Constitutional Amendment
L165@P3: TO REQUIRE VOTERS TO PRESENT PHOTO
L166@P3: IDENTIFICATION IN ORDER TO VOTE
L167@P3: Proposed by Joint Resolution of the General Assembly To
L168@P3: enact Section 5 of Article V of the Constitution of the State of
L169@P3: Ohio
L170@P3: A majority yes vote is required for the adoption of Section 5.
L171@P3: This proposed amendment would:
L172@P3: • Require voters to present an approved form of governmentissued photo identification in order to vote.
L173@P3: • Allow state law to provide for an alternative method of
L174@P3: verifying a voter’s identity for a voter who has a sincere
L175@P3: religious objection to being photographed and, therefore, does
L176@P3: not possess an approved form of government-issued photo
L177@P3: identification.
L178@P3: • Require that voters who do not vote in person provide an
L179@P3: approved form of government-issued photo identification,
L180@P3: unless the law authorizes those persons to vote by providing a
L181@P3: signature and at least one other specified unique identifier.
L182@P3: • Define approved forms of government-issued photo
L183@P3: identification as an unexpired Ohio driver license or state
L184@P3: identification card; a United States passport or passport card; or
L185@P3: a United States military identification card, Ohio National
L186@P3: Guard identification card, or United States Department of
L187@P3: Veterans Affairs identification card.
L188@P3: If approved, the amendment shall take effect immediately.
L189@P3: A “YES” vote means approval of the amendment.
L190@P3: A “NO” vote means disapproval of the amendment.
