L204@P6: FEDERAL WRITE-IN ABSENTEE BALLOT (FWAB):
L205@P6: To complete a Federal Write-In Absentee Ballot (FWAB), go to www.fvap.gov. You have the option of
L206@P6: downloading a blank, hard copy FWAB to complete by hand, or proceeding through electronic completion of
L207@P6: the FWAB using the website’s FWAB Wizard.
L208@P6: The first page of the FWAB is a Voter Declaration/Affirmation you must complete in order for your ballot to
L209@P6: count.
L210@P6: After completing the Voter Declaration/Affirmation, use the initial election notice issued on the 100th day before
L211@P6: the election and the updated election notice issued on the 46
L212@P6: th day before the election as a guide to:
L213@P6: (1) write the name of each candidate or issue contest for which you are casting a vote and then
L214@P6: (2) write the name of your choice of candidate or choice for or against an issue.
L215@P6: After you have completed the FWAB, you must PRINT the ballot and MAIL it to your county board of
L216@P6: elections at this address:
L217@P6: Wyandot County Board of Elections 350 N Warpole St. Upper Sandusky, OH 43351
L218@P6: A complete listing of all county board of elections mailing addresses and contact information is available at:
L219@P6: https://ohiosos.gov/SOS/elections/electionsofficials/boeDirectory.aspx#dir.
L220@P6: Do not send your ballot via e-mail or fax. Ohio law prohibits electronic transmission of a voted ballot.
