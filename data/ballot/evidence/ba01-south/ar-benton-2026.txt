approved may be combined into a single

to time. If the bonds for one or more of the

the net collections of which remaining after
deduction of the administrative charges of

approved. No bonds will be issued for any
purpose unless the Refunding Bonds are

accordance with Arkansas statutes.

net collections of which remaining after
deduction of the administrative charges

rebates will be used solely to retire or
pay obligations with respect to the

No. 62 to the Arkansas Constitution.

replace the City's existing 0.625%
sales and use tax levied in 2018 for the
purpose of retiring bonds to finance
water improvements (the "2018 Tax").
The effective date of the Bond Tax will
be the day following the date that the
2018 Tax expires. Proceeds of the
bonds may fund a debt service reserve
and pay expenses of issuing the
bonds. The bonds may be issued at
one time or in series from time to time.
