Election results will be reported at:
White County Extension Office2400 Old Landing Rd.Searcy, ARELECTION OFFICIALS
Election officials appointed to conduct early voting, Election Day voting, and all other required duties will be posted for public viewing in the White County Clerk’s Office on October 11, 2026 and will remain posted for not less than 10 days. Objections must be filed in writing with the White County Clerk before the following deadlines:
Objections that an official is the spouse of a county election commissioner or county party chair must be filed within 7 days of October 11, 2026.
Objections that an official is married to a candidate or related within two degrees of blood relation must be filed within 10 days of October 11, 2026.
