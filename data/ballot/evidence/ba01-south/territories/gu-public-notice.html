SECOND NOTICE - Guam Election Commission Regular Meeting

MEETING

  • Posted on: 08/12/2026 08:00 AM
  • Posted by: Ana Ramirez
  • Meeting Date: 08/15/2026 10:00 AM
  • Department(s): GUAM ELECTION COMMISSION
  • Division(s): GUAM ELECTION COMMISSION
  • Notice Topic(s): MEETING
  • Types of Notice: MEETING
  • For Audience(s): PUBLIC
  • Share this notice

Saturday, August 15, 2026, 10:00 a.m.
Guam Election Commission Regular Meeting
Westin Resort Guam – Mezzanine Level
Tamuning, Guam

For meeting livestream, visit GEC’s website at https://gec.guam.gov/board

 

Agenda

 

I.    Call to Order – Certification of a Quorum Present
II.   Certification – Public Notice Requirements – Guam Daily Post and Guam Public Notice Website (8/10/2026 & 8/12/2026)
III.   Approval of Agenda
IV.  Election of Independent Member
V.   Approval of Minutes

a.   June 24, 2026 Regular Meeting Minutes

b.   July 13, 2026 Reconvened Meeting Minutes

c.   July 30, 2026 Regular Meeting Minutes

d.   August 1, 2026 Regular Meeting Minutes

VI.   Correspondence

a.   August 3, 2026 – Dr. Ronald McNinch re: Request to Defer Initiative until GEC Rules are Clarified

VII.  Executive Director’s Report

a.     2026 General Election

i.   2026 General Election Candidate Filings

ii.  Approval of 2026 General Election Precinct Officials

iii. Approval of 2026 General Election Precinct Official Manual

iv. Approval of 2026 General Election Early Vote Center Operations

VIII. Old Business

a.  Fiscal Year 2027 Budget Request

b.  Public Release of Voter Registration Information

c.  Safeguard American Voter Eligibility Act

d.  “The Don’t Quit Your Day Jobs Act of 2026” Initiative

e.  Qualifications of Volunteer Voter Registrars

f.  Jordan Pauluhn Complaint

IX.  New Business

a.  Administrative Complaints

i.   26P-01 – Mr. Robert J.L. Barcinas re: Electioneering

ii.  26P-02 – Mr. Darryl A. Taggerty re: Electioneering

iii. 26P-03 – Mr. Darryl A. Taggerty re: Election Process

b.  Processing and Tabulation of Provisional Ballots

c.  Processing and Tabulation of Absentee Ballots

d.  Recount, if required

e.  Certification of 2026 Primary Election

f.   Ratification of 2026 General Election Candidates

g.  Approval of General Election Ballot Design

X.   Legal Counsel

a.     Executive Order 14248 “Preserving and Protecting the Integrity of American Elections”

b.     Ronald L. McNinch v. Guam, the Guam Election Commission, the Democratic Party of Guam and the Republican Party of Guam (Civil Action No. CV25-00034)

XI.    Public Comment
XII.   Next Meeting
XIII.  Adjournment

please contact Christiana Ramirez at (671) 477-9791 or send an email to vote@gec.guam.gov.
