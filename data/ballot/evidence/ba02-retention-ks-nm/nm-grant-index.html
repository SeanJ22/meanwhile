2026 General Election Mandatory Challengers, Watchers and Observers Training

Pursuant to §1-2-22(A)

Before accepting or entering into service as a challenger, or observer, a person shall attend a training session in advance of that election and pass the knowledge exam.

Any interested individuals can access the online training beginning September 28 through October 29. The registration link for the training is: https://olt.goasked.com/registerwithcode?code=NMSOSWOGEN26

A computer is available in the clerk’s office by appointment only for those who do not have a computer or internet access. To make an appointment call the Grant County Clerk’s Office (575) 574-0042.

Appointees to any position (Watcher, Challenger, or Observer) shall be required to bring the acknowledgement form and certificate of completion, along with their appointment letter, to the presiding judge when they appear in-person at the polling place.
