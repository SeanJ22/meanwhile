CLERK'S OFFICE

//www.sos.nm.gov/candidate-and-campaigns/ 


OUR MISSION

The Otero County Clerk's Office strives to provide informative, efficient, and courteous customer service.  We ensure that our office is accessible to those searching our public records while continuing to maintain fulfillment of our other duties to the public.  The Clerk's Office is dedicated to ensuring the highest level of integrity in the county's election process and strives to comply with state statutes and the Constitution of New Mexico.


OUR RESPONSIBILITIES

The Otero County Clerk is an elected county official.  The duties and responsibilities of the office include (but not limited to):

  • Recording, filing, and preserving all types of documents submitted for public and permanent record (i.e.: deeds, mortgages, agreements, etc.)
  • Issuance and recording of marriage licenses
  • Administration of elections, including maintaining voter registrations for Otero County
  • Serves as ex-officio clerk to the Otero County Probate Judge
  • Serves as ex-officio clerk to the Otero County Board of Commissioners
