14-8-9.1. Public records; inspection; exceptions.
Documents recorded in the office of the county clerk are public records, subject to inspection and disclosure.
Searches
Our office is not licensed or bonded to conduct detailed real estate searches. Please contact a local Abstract or Title Company. The San Miguel County Clerk’s Office will not assume liability for searches conducted by others.
