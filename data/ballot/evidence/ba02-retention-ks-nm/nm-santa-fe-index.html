Find Your Sample Ballot

We have Sample Ballots posted below!
If you know your Precinct, you can reference that in the chart below.
If you don't know your precinct yet, visit NMVote.gov and come back here!

1Visit the NMVOTE Voter Information Portal

Go to NMvote.gov

Click the button above to open NMVOTE in a new tab.

2Enter Your Voter Registration Information

On the NMVOTE page, enter your name, birth date, and the county where you are registered to vote to find your record.

3View and Print Your Sample Ballot

After your record appears, select "My Sample Ballot" to view and print.
Reviewing your ballot at home helps you plan and can shorten your time at the polling site.

Need Help?

If you have trouble finding your sample ballot on NMVOTE, contact the Santa Fe County Clerk's Office:
Phone: 505-986-6280
Email: elections@santafecountynm.gov
