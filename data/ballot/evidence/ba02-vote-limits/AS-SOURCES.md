# American Samoa voting instructions

The November 3, 2026 Election Office certified candidate list is saved as
`as-candidates.html`. Its exact delegate contest and seventeen numbered House
districts join to the governing voting instructions below. Candidate counts do
not establish how many choices a voter may make.

American Samoa Code Annotated §6.0602(b) explicitly requires the ballot to state
that the elector may not vote for more than the seats available (or fewer
listed candidates). The full code is saved from the International Labour
Organization's NATLEX repository as `as-elections-ilo.pdf`. The current ASBar
page contains the same provision; `as-code-60602.web.txt` is the connected web
reader's corroborating extract, clearly distinguished from an HTTP capture.
The PDF also includes §6.0805's single winning delegate rule.

`as-official-results-2022-house.pdf` is the American Samoa Government's
supplemental submission to the U.S. House, hosted by docs.house.gov. Its first
section contains the Governor's December 14, 2022 letter and the Revised
Constitution incorporating the **five voter-approved amendments**. PDF pages
18–20 (printed pages 7–9), Article II §2, give the seventeen district village
lists and seat allocation. Districts 1, 12, and 15 have two representatives;
each other district has one. The official January 16, 2025 House announcement
`as-current-amendments-house.html` confirms approval and implementation of
those amendments. The old ASBar constitutional text is retained for comparison.

The same submission later reproduces all eleven convention proposals. Do not
use that later draft (PDF pages 44–46): it includes rejected additional seats
and a rejected addition of Malaeimi to district 12. The five approved amendments
change district names but do not change their village or seat allocations.

`as-delegate-usc2024.html` is the actual GovInfo U.S. Code chapter, current
through January 6, 2025. Sections 1731 and 1732 establish one nonvoting delegate,
elected territory-wide by separate ballot and plurality vote.

The successful route combined the current certified list with the explicit
statutory ballot instruction and an official government constitutional text.
Current Election Office navigation exposed no candidate specimen. Guessed
sample/manual URLs returned 404. Archived official 2016 certification and
candidate manual had no vote limits; the archived 2020 certification was a
truncated PDF. The 2022 official sample located through Talanei was referendum
only. Current official Facebook redirected to login, and old public snapshots
did not produce a usable candidate specimen. No login was bypassed.

Raw ASBar §6.0602 requests returned 403 and then 503, while the connected web
reader succeeded; use the valid NATLEX PDF for reproducible raw extraction.
The Secretary's repository labels an original **1960** constitution as revised:
that file has obsolete allocations and must not supply current districts.
GovInfo's 2025 U.S. Code route returned a soft 404, and the House preliminary
Code reader returned maintenance HTML; the validated 2024 GovInfo edition works.
Failed bodies and unused research downloads were removed after these results
were recorded. Every retained raw source has its URL, retrieval time, and hash.
