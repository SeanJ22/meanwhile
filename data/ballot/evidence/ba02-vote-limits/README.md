# BA-02 voting instructions

`scripts/ba02-vote-limits.py --write` reproduces the SC and AS counts from these saved source bytes and their capture receipts. It checks each quoted instruction, joins it to the exact office or district, and attaches every supporting source to the contest's `voteForSources`. The normal ballot assembler consumes those state packages; both earlier roster builders also reapply the enrichment when rebuilding their source lists.

South Carolina's 298 published contests have 254 limits of one, 43 limits of two, and one limit of three. The current Georgetown County November 3, 2026 sample supplies the general single-selection instruction and the printed soil-and-water instruction. The April 2026 SCDNR memo explicitly assigns two regular positions to each conservation district in this election; none of the 43 published soil-and-water contests is an unexpired vacancy. State statutes or the constitution establish one seat for each legislative district and the singular county offices. Current county records establish the single at-large seats in Chester and Colleton. Hampton's official **2018** specimen prints “County Council At Large / Vote for up to Three”; the three current county term records ending in December 2026 establish its application to the current regular election. The Hampton specimen is not represented as a 2026 sample. Its scanned instruction is read from page 3 by a focused OCR crop.

American Samoa's 18 certified offices have 15 limits of one and three limits of two, for House districts 1, 12 and 15. The current Election Office certified list identifies the delegate and all 17 House districts. ASCA 6.0602(b) explicitly limits selections to the available seats or, if fewer, the listed candidates. The Governor's official constitutional enclosure filed with the U.S. House supplies the exact district seats; the January 2025 House announcement confirms the approved amendments took effect. Only the **first** constitutional table is used: the later eleven-proposal table contains rejected extra seats and the rejected Malaeimi reassignment. The code checks every published candidate against the exact certified office section before applying the statutory formula. The U.S. Code's single delegate, separate-ballot provision supplies the delegate limit. The ASCA PDF is published by the International Labour Organization NATLEX; its publisher is recorded honestly, while the current office list and seat table come directly from government sources.

SC's live candidate-search HTML, script, CSV export and print endpoint contain no seat-limit column. They remain useful for offices, names and parties, with ballot instructions and scoped statutory rules supplying the independent voting limit. Georgetown's government election index links its current full sample. For CivicPlus document centers, the normal public page's document-list request can locate older official specimens when the visible current sample page is empty. A county council roster's candidate count is never a voting limit: the ballot instruction and the expiring official terms must establish the rule together.

The AS office site exposes the current certification but no current House specimen in the routes inspected. Old official certification PDFs list names without voting limits, and a recovered 2022 specimen concerns only the referendum. Combining the exact current certified offices with the explicit statutory ballot rule and the official constitutional district table resolves that gap. Direct ASBar requests returned access errors; its statute text was also read through the web tool and agreed with the saved ILO reproduction. Failed-response bodies and unrelated research captures are excluded from the final evidence set.

`data/ballot/reports/ba02-sc-vote-limits.json` and `ba02-as-vote-limits.json` list every enriched contest and its sources. `ba02-missing-vote-counts.json` lists every remaining null count nationally by state, contest ID, title, election and division; `--inventory-only` refreshes it one state at a time. That report is coverage work and does not hold verified contests back from publication.
