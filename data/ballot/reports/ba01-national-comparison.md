# BA-01 national source comparison

This compares the acquired official sources with prepared ballot data. It is not
a live-delivery result or a claim that a state has complete local coverage.
Counts are contests, not seats. A multi-seat contest counts once; a joint ticket
counts as one ballot choice, with each named person retained. The regional
reports below contain the exact sources, saved bytes, exclusions and remaining
work. A statutory office count is not a certified-candidate denominator.
