Description:
Abstract:
The New Hampshire House of Representatives Floterial District Boundaries, 2022, contains "floterial" district boundaries drawn by the New Hampshire Legislature based on the 2020 US Census population count. Floterial districts overlay other NH House of Representatives districts ("base" districts), which are available from companion shape file NHHouseDists2022. Both shape files are required to depict all NH House of Representatives districts. District boundaries were derived by applying district codes retrieved from the NH Secretary of State web site to the geometry provided by the GRANIT town boundary layer (PBNH).
Purpose:
These data, in concert with NHHouseDists2022.shp, may be used to represent New Hampshire's State House of Representatives Districts.

Process_Step:
Process_Description:
Attribute was added to PBNH to capture the NH House of Representatives corresponding floterial district, if one existed, for each town. Areas with no floterial district were assigned no district code. For sub-municipal districts, ward boundary maps and descriptions were obtained from city offices. Boundaries were obtained from corresponding GRANIT base map layers (DOTRDS, NHHYDRO) and used to split municipalities into ward polygons. TOWNLISTINGS was used to populate the field. Results were "dissolved" to generate the House floterial district boundaries.
