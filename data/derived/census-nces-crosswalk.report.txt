CENSUS <-> NCES SCHOOL-DISTRICT CROSSWALK

identifier in the two files this repo holds; NCES publishes a Census ID on
the F-33 fiscal survey, which is a separate acquisition.

on either side is AMBIGUOUS and is counted as UNMATCHED, not resolved.
