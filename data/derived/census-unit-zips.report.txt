gap in our retrieval; it is a gap in what the Census publishes as geometry.

and this number caps Tier 1 nationally.

NEXT RUNGS FOR RESOLVING THEM, named and not walked in this session:
  1. County assessor / auditor special-district tax-levy layers, which
     carry district boundaries because they have to bill them.

     file its boundary with the secretary of state or a local-government
     commission.
  3. Per-state GIS open-data portals, which publish fire, water and library
     district layers county by county.
  4. The district's own published service-area map, per district.
