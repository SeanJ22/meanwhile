The official canvass of the July 21, 2026 Primary Election results has been completed in accordance with Arizona law. The Town of Carefree Town Council has also conducted its official canvass of the municipal election results, formally certifying the election results.
You can watch the Town Council meeting where the election results were canvassed and certified HERE.
Following a legal review of the election results, the applicable provisions of A.R.S. § 9-821.01, and the Town of Carefree Town Code governing majority-vote municipal elections, the Town certified:
John Crane as elected Mayor
Sheila Amoroso as elected to the Town Council
Les Hardie as elected to the Town Council
Because only two candidates received the statutory majority required for election to the three available Town Council seats, one council seat remains to be filled. Consistent with A.R.S. § 9-821.01 and the Town of Carefree Town Code, the remaining seat will be decided at the November General Election between the two eligible candidates receiving the next highest number of votes:
Sharon P. Smith
Ted Taylor
