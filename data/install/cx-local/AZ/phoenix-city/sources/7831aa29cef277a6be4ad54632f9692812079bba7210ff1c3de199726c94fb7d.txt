            Find My Council District
            
             The Phoenix City Council is made up of a mayor and eight council members. They are elected on a non-partisan ballot for four-year terms. This interactive page will help you locate your City Council district. You can view its boundaries and discover ways to get in touch with your council member.

            Find Your City Council District
            To find your Phoenix City Council District, enter your address in the search box below. The map will zoom to the address. You can then click on the parcel to see which Council District your address is in.
