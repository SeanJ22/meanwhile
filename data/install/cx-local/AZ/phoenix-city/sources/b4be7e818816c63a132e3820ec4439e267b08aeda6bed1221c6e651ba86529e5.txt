        Redistricting
        
        
        
        
        

        
    


        
    




    
    
        



    
    

    Every 10 years, the City is required by law to review and adjust its Council District boundaries to equalize population based on the most recent Census data.
The 2020 Census data showed that the population of the City grew from 1,445,632 to 1,608,139, making the new ideal equal population target for each Council District: 201,017.  To meet this ideal population, Council Districts 1, 3, 4 and 6 needed to increase in population while Council Districts 2, 5, 7, & 8 needed to decrease in population. To achieve the ideal equal population target, in 2023, the City used a redistricting process to redraw the Council District boundaries in accordance with the redistricting requirements and guidelines. 
The final recommended Redistricting Map was approved by the City Council at the September 26, 2023 Policy Meeting.
