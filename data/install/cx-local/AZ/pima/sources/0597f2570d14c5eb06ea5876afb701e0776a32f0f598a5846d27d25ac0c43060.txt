Sample Ballot Locator





Enter the home address of registered voter and click submit button to identify precinct.
 You will get at least two sample ballots. One is the federal only ballot for people who have not provided the County Registrar of Voters adequate identification required by state law. The other is the ballot for your precinct. About 60 precincts have voting districts that bisect the precinct, mostly school districts. For instance, a precinct may have voters who reside in Tucson Unified School District and voters who reside in Catalina Foothills School District. To pick the correct sample ballot for you, you will need to know which jurisdiction you reside in. If you are uncertain, you'll need to check your registration on the 
 County Recorder's website.
