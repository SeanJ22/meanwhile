2026 PIMA COUNTY SCHOOL DISTRICT GOVERNING BOARD ELECTION CANCELLATIONS

		

	NOVEMBER 3, 2026 | GENERAL ELECTION



	The following Pima County school districts and Community College will all have uncontested governing board positions for the General Election on November 3, 2026. The Pima County School Superintendent has determined that all the candidates listed below meet the nomination requirements to seek a position on a school district governing board. Therefore, pursuant to A.R.S. §15-424 and §15-1442, the Pima County School Superintendent requests that the Pima County Board of Supervisors cancel these uncontested governing board elections at their meeting on August 11, 2026. The school district governing board candidates shall be appointed to a four (4) year term through December 31, 2030, unless otherwise indicated on the listing below. Write-in candidates are notated below.
