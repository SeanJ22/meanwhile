Abstract
dist_jp displays Justice Precincts in Pima County.  Justice Precincts define areas served by Justices of the Peace and Constables. 



      

  



Known errors/qualifications
Population and demographic data have not been added to the newest version of dist_jp.

20220323 - Boundaries updated following Board of Supervisors action on 09/21/2021.
