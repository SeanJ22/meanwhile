Understanding San Leandro’s November 2026 Election Results
In June 2026, San Leandro voters overwhelmingly approved Measure F (87% Yes, 13% No), establishing district-based City Council elections beginning in November 2026.
What does this mean?
The Mayor continues to be elected citywide.
Councilmembers must live in the districts they represent.
Only votes cast by residents living within each respective district will count toward determining that Council District’s winner (Councilmember-elect).
How will winners be determined?
Following certification by the Alameda County Registrar of Voters (which is expected to be by December 2, 2026), the City will follow Measure F and Section 225(a) of the updated City Charter to determine the by-District election winners:
Use the voter-approved District map and County precinct boundaries to identify the votes cast for Districts 1, 2, 3, and 5.
Publicly present certified vote totals by precinct and candidate.
Calculate each candidate’s percentage of the votes cast within their respective districts.
The City Council will declare a candidate the winner if they receive at least 50% plus one of the votes cast in that district.
The Mayor’s election will continue to be determined by the citywide vote.
The City is committed to a transparent process that follows Measure F and the updated City Charter.

Candidate Statements
Candidates running for the office of Mayor or City Council may optionally submit a Candidate Statement of Qualifications. Original documents filed by candidates are available for public inspection in the City Clerk's Office during normal business hours (Monday - Friday, 8:30 am - 5:00 pm).

The City Clerk has issued nomination papers to the incumbent for the office of Mayor. The office of Mayor is elected at large and Measure F, which was passed at the June 2, 2026 Special Municipal Election, did not affect an incumbent’s eligibility to run for the open office of Mayor.
Due to the passage of Measure F at the June 2, 2026 Special Municipal Election, the offices of City Council Districts 1, 2, 3, and 5 transitioned from at large elections to district elections. Accordingly, these offices have no eligible incumbents for the purposes of the incumbent filing provisions.
Nomination papers may be obtained and filed at City Hall, City Clerk’s Office, 835 East 14th Street, San Leandro, between July 13, 2026 through August 7, 2026, between the hours of 8:30 a.m. and 5:00 p.m. If an incumbent eligible for re-election does not file nomination papers by the deadline, the filing deadline for that office is extended to Wednesday, August 12, 2026, for non-incumbent candidates only.

Special Municipal Election
On February 2, 2026, the City Council called for a special municipal election on Tuesday, June 2, 2026, for the purpose of considering an amendment to the City's Charter. Read more about Measure F on our ballot measure webpage.
Alameda County Registrar of Voters
The Registrar of Voters is responsible for registering voters and conducting Federal, State, County, special, and local elections. Contact the Registrar of Voters with questions about voting, voter registration, vote-by-mail ballots, and election results.
California Fair Political Practices Commission
The Fair Political Practices Commission (FPPC) enforces California laws related to campaign financing, financial conflicts of interest, and gifts given to public officials and candidates under the Political Reform Act. The FPPC also provides written advice to public agencies, candidates, and officials regarding Political Reform Act requirements. The FPPC's advice line is 1-866-ASK-FPPC (866-275-3772) or email Fair Political Practices Commission.
