Candidate Information
The City Clerk's Office is responsible for the conduct of municipal elections. We provide nomination packets and general guidelines to candidates interested in running for Mayor or City Council.
Notice of Nomination Papers Issued
This notice is posted in accordance with Elections Code § 10224.5:
Notice of Nomination Papers Issued
The City Clerk has issued nomination papers to the incumbent for the office of Mayor. The office of Mayor is elected at large and Measure F, which was passed at the June 2, 2026 Special Municipal Election, did not affect an incumbent’s eligibility to run for the open office of Mayor.
Due to the passage of Measure F at the June 2, 2026 Special Municipal Election, the offices of City Council Districts 1, 2, 3, and 5 transitioned from at large elections to district elections. Accordingly, these offices have no eligible incumbents for the purposes of the incumbent filing provisions.
Nomination papers may be obtained and filed at City Hall, City Clerk’s Office, 835 East 14th Street, San Leandro, between July 13, 2026 through August 7, 2026, between the hours of 8:30 a.m. and 5:00 p.m. If an incumbent eligible for re-election does not file nomination papers by the deadline, the filing deadline for that office is extended to Wednesday, August 12, 2026, for non-incumbent candidates only.
Appointments Required
Please contact us to schedule an appointment. Candidate filing is a multi-step process and may require more than one appointment. Below are common types of appointments and how long you can expect at each one:
Picking up paperwork to run for Mayor or City Council (60 minutes)
Dropping off paperwork to run for Mayor or City Council (60 minutes)
Meeting with the City Clerk or Deputy City Clerk (30 minutes)
Assistance with the City's electronic campaign filing system (30-45 minutes)
Nomination Period and Candidate Filing Information
During the Nomination Period, candidates can pick up and file paperwork to run for office. For the November 3, 2026 General Municipal Election, the nomination period opens on July 13, 2026, and closes at 5 p.m. on August 7, 2026 (unless extended).
If an eligible incumbent does not file nomination documents for re-election by the close of the nomination period on August 7, 2026, the nomination period for that office shall be extended to Wednesday, August 12, 2026, at 5:00 p.m. for non-incumbents only.
Filing Fee
There is no filing fee to run for office. A fee may apply if the candidate chooses to publish a Candidate Statement of Qualifications in the voter information guide.
Preparing to Run for Office
You can prepare to run for office by taking the following steps:
Verify that your voter registration is current
File a Form 501 (Candidate Intention Statement) before raising or spending any money
File a Declaration of Intent to Install Temporary Election Campaign Signs (at least 2 days before installing any sign)
Review the FPPC Candidate Toolkit to understand the rules for raising or spending money
Get candidate and treasurer training from the Fair Political Practices Commission
Review important reporting deadlines for campaign committees
Preparing for a Candidate Appointment
The candidate will meet with the City Clerk or Deputy City Clerk. The first appointment begins with verifying the candidate's voter registration. The following documents will be issued to the candidate and reviewed:
Candidate Information Guide
Nomination Paper
Declaration of Candidacy
Ballot Designation Worksheet
Candidate Statement of Qualifications
Code of Fair Campaign Practices
Declaration of Intent to Install Political Signs
FPPC Forms and Campaign Finance Deadlines
Form 700
Form 501
Form 410
Form 460
Form 470 + Supplement
Form 497
Campaign Disclosure Manual 2
Submittal Checklist
Council Member District Map (PDF)
The City Clerk's Office maintains a roster of candidate information that is available to the public, media, and others. This roster is updated during the Nomination Period as forms are filed. At the return appointment, candidates will meet with the City Clerk or Deputy City Clerk to file the completed paperwork and take the Oath of Office.
