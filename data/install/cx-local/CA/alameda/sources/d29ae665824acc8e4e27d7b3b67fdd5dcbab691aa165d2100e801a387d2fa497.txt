LARPD Board of Directors Candidate Forum
Monday, September 21, 2026 | 6–8 p.m.
Cresta Blanca Room at Robert Livermore Community Center
4444 East Ave., Livermore
The Livermore Vine and Pleasanton Weekly will host an in-person forum featuring candidates for the Livermore Area Recreation and Park District Board of Directors.
Three at-large seats on the LARPD Board of Directors are up for election on November 3, 2026. The forum will give community members a chance to hear from candidates and learn more about their perspectives on issues affecting LARPD and the community.
RSVP: Register to attend the Candidate Forum
A recording of the forum will be made available following the event.
Official Election Information
For official information regarding candidates, voting, voter registration, and the November 3, 2026 General Election, visit the Official Election Site of Alameda County.
View Official Election & Candidate Information
Notice
This forum is hosted by the Livermore Vine and Pleasanton Weekly. LARPD is providing information about the event as a public service and does not endorse or oppose any candidate, campaign, or position presented at the forum.
