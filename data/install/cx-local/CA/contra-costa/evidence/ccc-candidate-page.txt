# Run for Office

Qualifications 

The qualifications to run for office vary by office and jurisdiction. For further details on specific requirements, please call our office or check back at a later time.

Campaign Finance 

 All federal, state, and local candidates and their committees are required to report campaign contributions and expenditures. All Contra Costa county, school, and special district candidates are required to submit their [campaign filing reports(Opens in a new window)](https://netfile.com/filer) electronically.
For detailed information regarding campaign finances and deadlines visit the [California Fair Political Practices Commission (FPPC) website(Opens in a new window)](http://www.fppc.ca.gov/).

**Campaign Contributions**  
 Campaign contributions include monetary contributions, non-monetary contributions such as the donation of goods or services, and loans provided to candidates, committees, or committees supporting or opposing ballot measures. Most counties, states, and countries have laws that regulate who, and how much, someone can contribute to a campaign. In Contra Costa, all candidates and committees have campaign contribution filing requirements.

Use our [Public Access System(Opens in a new window)](https://public.netfile.com/pub2/?AID=CCC) to see who contributed money to campaigns, which campaigns received money from which donor, and how those funds were spent.

Campaign Resources 

As a candidate, you can purchase [voter data](https://www.contracostavote.gov/elections/candidates-campaigns-measures/voter-information-requests-2/) and [maps(Opens in a new window)](https://www.contracostavote.gov/elections/candidates-campaigns-measures/district-precinct-maps/) from the Elections Division. For more information, please email [data.map@vote.cccounty.us](mailto:data.map@vote.cccounty.us).

November 3, 2026, General Election - Candidate and Measure Filing 

The upcoming November 3, 2026, General Election is a state and countywide election.

Key Dates 

Candidate Nomination Period July 13 – August 7, 2026
Last day to place a measure on the ballot August 7, 2026
Measure Letter Assignment August 10, 2026
Randomized Alphabet Drawing August 13, 2026
Deadline for Primary Arguments For/Against August 19, 2026
Deadline for Rebuttal Arguments August 24, 2026
Write-In Declaration of Candidacy September 7* – October 20, 2026
Vote-By-Mail Packets Mailed Out October 5, 2026
Registration Deadline October 19, 2026
Election Day November 3, 2026
Certification Deadline December 3, 2026

*Date falls on a weekend or state holiday;it moves forward to the next business day.

Candidate Documents 

**Candidate Guide:**  
 The Contra Costa County Elections Division has created this guide to help candidates who are running for office. The guide provides answers to frequently asked questions about the nomination process and guidelines for those who are interested in running campaigns in the county. This guide is not intended to render legal advice or be a substitute for legal counsel. To view or download the Candidate Guide, please follow the link below.

[Candidate Guide(PDF document) (PDF document)](https://www.contracostavote.gov/wp-content/uploads/26Nov03_CandidateGuide.pdf)

**Authorization Form:**  
 If a candidate is unable to pick up their candidate application packet in person, they may authorize a person to act on their behalf. Please note that the nomination documents cannot be filed by fax or email as an original signature is mandatory. Moreover, the candidate’s designee is not authorized to make any changes to the nomination documents and can only receive and deliver papers. To access or download the Letter of Authorization, click on the link below.

[Letter of Authorization Form(PDF document) (PDF document)](https://www.contracostavote.gov/wp-content/uploads/26Nov03_Authorization-Form-1.pdf)

**Candidate Statement of Qualifications:**  
 Candidates for each elective office must complete a Candidate Statement of Qualifications Form and may prepare an optional candidate statement at their own expense. The statement is included in the county Voter Information Guide which is sent to all eligible registered voters and is printed in English, Spanish, and Traditional Chinese. To access or download the Statement of Qualifications, please use the click on the link provided below.

[Candidate Statement of Qualifications Form(PDF document)(Opens in a new window) (PDF document)](https://www.contracostavote.gov/wp-content/uploads/Candidate-Statement-of-Qualifications.pdf)
