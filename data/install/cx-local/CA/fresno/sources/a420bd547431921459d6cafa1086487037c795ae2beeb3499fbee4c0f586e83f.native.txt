FRESNO COUNTY ELECTIONS DEPARTMENT IDENTIFIES PRINTING ERROR ON LIMITED NUMBER OF BALLOTS 
PRINTED FROM BALLOT-MARKING DEVICE AT VOTE CENTERS 
FRESNO, CA –The Fresno County Elections Department identified a minor typographical printing error affecting 
a limited number of ballots printed by the County’s ballot-marking devices for the Fresno Unified School District 
Trustee Areas 1, 3, and 4 contests. 
The screen on the ballot-marking devices lists the correct contest title, Fresno Unified School District, when used 
by a voter residing within the jurisdiction. However, when ballots are printed from the ballot-marking devices, 
the contest title is incorrectly listed as Fowler Unified School District Trustee Areas 1, 3, or 4 rather than Fresno 
Unified School District Trustee Areas 1, 3, or 4.  
Fresno County Clerk/Registrar of Voters James A. Kus has confirmed that the ballot still records the voter’s 
choice correctly, and the vote is counted in the correct Fresno Unified School District Trustee contest.  No other 
contests on the ballot are affected. 
At this time, the Department cannot change how these ballots print from the ballot-marking devices.  Signs will 
be posted at all ballot-marking devices describing this typographical printing error. Voters eligible to vote in 
Fresno Unified School District Trustee Areas 1, 3, or 4 may request a ballot printed on demand at any Vote 
Center rather than using a ballot-marking device. Ballots printed on demand do not show the typographical 
error. 
Following Election Day, additional precincts in each of Fresno Unified School District Trustee Areas 1, 3, and 4 
will be drawn for auditing in the Manual Tally.  For future elections, the County will add another manual proofing
