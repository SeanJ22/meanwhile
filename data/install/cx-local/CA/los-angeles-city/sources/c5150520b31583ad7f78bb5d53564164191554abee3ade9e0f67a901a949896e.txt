BALLOT RESOLUTION
OF 26-1100-S19
Shall the Los Angeles City Charter be amended to clarify the campaign finance 
rules for Board of Education elections and allow for changes by ordinance?
Section 1. The Charter amendment attached to this Resolution shall be 
submitted to the qualified voters of the Los Angeles Unified School District at an election 
consolidated with the City’s General Municipal Election and the State General Election 
held on November 3, 2026.
Sec. 2. The vote requirement for the Charter amendment to pass is a majority of 
the votes cast.
RESOLUTION OF THE LOS ANGELES CITY COUNCIL 
SUBMITTING A CHARTER AMENDMENT TO THE VOTERS OF THE LOS ANGELES 
UNIFIED SCHOOL DISTRICT AND REQUESTING THE LOS ANGELES COUNTY

CHARTER AMENDMENT WITH THE STATE GENERAL ELECTION TO BE HELD ON 
NOVEMBER 3, 2026
Sec. 4. The City Clerk is hereby authorized to make technical and formatting 
adjustments to the attached Charter amendment to facilitate presentation in ballot 
materials.
WHEREAS, the City Council wishes to submit a Charter amendment to the 
qualified voters of the Los Angeles Unified School District at a Special Election to be 
consolidated with the City’s General Municipal Election and the State General Election 
held on November 3, 2026, regarding the following subject: Campaign Finance Rules 
related to LAUSD Elections; and
Sec. 5. The City Clerk is hereby authorized and directed to publish a notice 
containing the text of proposed City ballot measures and specifying the date of the 
election for the ballot measures. The notice shall be published once in a newspaper of

WHEREAS, the City Council wishes to request that the Board of Supervisors of

(1) Criminal Enforcement - Any person who knowingly or willfully
