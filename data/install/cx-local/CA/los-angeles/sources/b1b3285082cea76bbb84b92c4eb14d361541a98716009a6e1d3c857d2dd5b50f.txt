<h1>Interactive Sample Ballot</h1>
<div class="m_img_span">
<img src="/imgs/rrcc/images/ISB_Banner.jpg?v=2" alt="Interactive Sample Ballot" width="100%" />
</div>
<h2>Save time at the Vote Center</h2>
<p>The Interactive Sample Ballot is an optional tool that allows voters to access, review and mark their selections prior to going to a Vote Center. The Interactive Sample Ballot is not online voting and does not store any identifiable information, once you access your Sample Ballot all of your selections are saved on your phone, computer or personal device.</p>
<div class="align_c m_img_span">
<a class="no_margin vsap_button" href="https://isb.lavote.gov/" target="_blank"><strong>Get Started</strong></a>
</div>
<h2>How to Use the Interactive Sample Ballot</h2>
<div class="m_vid_span">
<iframe aria-label="How to Use the Interactive Sample Ballot" role="presentation" src="https://www.youtube.com/embed/O3iGe4Pj9Cw" allow="accelerometer; autoplay; encrypted-media; gyroscope; picture-in-picture" width="960" height="500" frameborder="0"></iframe>
</div>
<h2>Frequently Asked Questions</h2>
<ul>
    <li><span style="font-size: 16px;">What is the Interactive Sample Ballot?<br />
    The Interactive Sample Ballot is a digital version of your sample ballot booklet that will allow you to make selections and generate a Poll Pass.</span></li>
    <li><span style="font-size: 16px;">What is a Poll Pass?<br />
    A Poll Pass is a QR code containing your selections. You can transfer your selections from your Poll Pass to a Ballot Marking Device at any vote center.</span></li>
    <li><span style="font-size: 16px;">Is my personal information stored in the Poll Pass?<br />
    No. The Poll Pass saves your ballot type and selections. No information about you is stored.</span></li>
    <li><span style="font-size: 16px;">Are my selections private?<br />
    Yes. Your selections are only saved in your Poll Pass or on your personal device.</span></li>
    <li><span style="font-size: 16px;">Am I required to use the Interactive Sample Ballot and Poll Pass?<br />
    No. The Interactive Sample Ballot and Poll Pass are optional tools to expedite your voting experience at the vote center.</span></li>
    <li><span style="font-size: 16px;">Where can I find a Vote Center?<br />
    You can find a Vote Center by <a href="https://locator.lavote.net/locations/vc">clicking here</a>.</span></li>
</ul>
