Candidate Guide

Statement of Qualifications
Form for submitting a candidate statement of qualifications for publication in the San Joaquin County voter information guide.
Local Candidate Roster
This roster includes the names of local candidates who have completed and filed all required nomination documents for countywide offices.
The list is updated daily at 5:00 p.m. Candidates who file after 2:00 p.m. will be added on the next business day.
Please note:
We only post candidates for countywide offices.
For information regarding state or city candidates, please contact those offices directly.
Offices in Extension
