Staff Recommendation 
It is recommended that the Board of Directors adopt the attached Resolution calling for 
the County to conduct an election for three members of the Board of Directors during the 
November 3, 2026 General Election. 
 
Background and Discussion 
Pursuant to Elections Code section 10509, the Fire Protection District must formally call 
for an election and deliver notice to the County Elections Official no less than 125 days 
prior to each election at which the Fire Protection District will elect members to the 
Board of Directors.  As the current terms for President Burke, Director Hanna, and 
Director Sherrill are set to expire at the end of 2026, the Fire Protection District shall 
elect three members to four-year terms to begin upon the expiration of those terms at 
the November 3, 2026 Election.  The attached Resolution calls for the election and will 
provide the required notice to the County Elections Official upon its delivery by the 
Deputy Chief. 
 
The November 3, 2026 Election will be the third election at which the Fire Protection 
District will elect members to the Board of Directors through the by-district form of 
elections.  Registered voters from Districts C, D, and E, as identified on the District 
Boundary Map adopted by the Board in 2022, shall elect members from their respective 
districts during this election cycle.  Voters from Districts A and B last elected members 
from their respective districts to four-year terms in 2024 and shall next vote at the 2028 
election.

WHERAS, on April 13, 2022, the Coastside Fire Protection District formally 
switched from an at-large to a by-district form of electing members to the Board of 
Directors through the adoption of Ordinance No. 2022-01; and 
 
WHEREAS, Ordinance No. 2022-01 included as an attachment the District 
Boundaries Map defining the five districts from which members of the Board of Directors 
shall be elected; and   
 
WHEREAS, Ordinance No. 2022-01 dictated that the Coastside Fire Protection 
District would elect members to the Board of Directors through by-district elections 
beginning with the General Election in November 2022, at which time members from 
Districts C, D, and E were elected to four-year terms; and 
 
WHEREAS, Ordinance No. 2022-01 dictated that members from Districts A and 
B would be elected to four-year terms beginning with the November 2024 General 
Election;  
 
WHEREAS, the Coastside Fire Protection District has provided the District 
Boundaries Map and associated information describing the boundaries of the five 
electoral districts to the San Mateo County Elections Office; and  
 
WHEREAS, an election has been ordered to be held on November 3, 2026 in the 
Coastside Fire Protection District for the purpose of electing members of its Board of 
Directors (the “Election”); and 
 
WHEREAS, pursuant to Section 1001 of the Elections Code of the State of 
California, there is an established General Statewide election to be held on the same 
day as the Election; and 
 
WHEREAS, pursuant to Part 3, Consolidation of Elections, and commencing with 
Section 10400 of the Elections Code of the State of California, an election for members 
of the Board of Directors may be either completely or partially consolidated with another 
election held on the same day, if in the same territory, or in territory that is part the 
same; and 
 
WHEREAS, pursuant to Section 1002 of the Elections Code of the State of 
California, the Board of Directors desires to adopt this resolution to request that the 
Board of Supervisors of the County of San Mateo permit the San Mateo County Chief

Elections Officer & Assessor–County Clerk–Recorder (the “Chief Elections Officer”) to 
render specified services to the Coastside Fire Protection District relating to the conduct 
of the November 3, 2026 election. 
 
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by Board of Directors for the Coastside 
Fire Protection District as follows: 
 
1. 
That an election is hereby called to be held in and for the Coastside Fire 
Protection District on November 3, 2026, to elect three members to the Board of 
Directors for full terms of four years each.  Those three members shall be elected from 
sub district areas known as Districts C, D, and E as shown on the District Boundaries 
Map. 
 
2. 
That, pursuant to Elections Code 10002, the Board of Directors of the Coastside 
Fire Protection District hereby requests the Board of Supervisors of the County of San 
Mateo to make available the services of the San Mateo County Chief Elections Officer & 
Assessor – County Clerk – Recorder (the “Chief Elections Officer”) as the County 
Elections Official for the purpose of rendering services in the conduct of the Election to 
be held on November 3, 2026. 
 
3. 
That the Election to be held on November 3, 2026 will be conducted by-district.  
Each candidate for the Board of Directors shall reside in the sub district area where they 
seek to represent and shall be elected by the registered voters of that particular sub 
district area. 
 
4. 
That, pursuant to Elections Code section 10509, not less than 125 days prior to 
the date of the election, the Deputy Chief/District Secretary shall deliver this Resolution 
to the Chief Elections Officer as notice of the call for the Election on November 3, 2026.  
The Deputy Chief shall, prior to submitting the Resolution, affix the seal of the Coastside 
Fire Protection District with his signature on the Resolution. 
 
5. 
That the Deputy Chief is hereby authorized and directed to enter into an 
Elections Services Agreement with the Chief Elections Officer regarding the Election to 
be held on November 3, 2026 to outline the detailed services to be provided by both the 
Coastside Fire Protection District and the Chief Elections Officer. 
 
6. 
The Chief Elections Officer will send an itemized invoice to the Coastside Fire 
Protection District for all services provided pursuant to the Elections Service Agreement 
after the Election is conducted and all related costs are determined, and the Deputy 
Chief, or his/her designee, is hereby authorized and directed to submit payment on the 
full amount of the invoice to the County of San Mateo within forty-five (45) days of the 
date of the invoice 
 
7. 
That pursuant to Elections Code 13307, each candidate for the Board of 
Directors to be voted in the Election to be held on November 3, 2026 may prepare a 
candidate statement on the appropriate form provided by the Chief Elections Officer.
