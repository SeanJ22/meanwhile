2026 Elections		
	
		
			

		
				
	

	
	
	

	    


        November 3, 2026 General Municipal ElectionThe Town of Woodside holds by-district elections for its five Town Council seats in November of even numbered years. Councilmembers serve staggering four-year terms. The General Municipal Election on November 3, 2026 is being held for two Town Council seats for full four-year terms ending 2030 in the following Districts: District 2District 3Town Council CandidatesFor information on Town Council candidates or becoming a candidate, please see: November 3, 2026 Election - Town Council Candidates.Campaign Disclosure FormsPlease see: Statement of Economic Interests & Officeholder & Candidate Campaign Form Information.Additional ResourcesRegister to VoteIf you have moved, changed your name, or want to update your political party, you will need to re-register to vote. Visit the San Mateo County Elections Office Voter Registration website for more information. Fair Political Practices CommissionLeague of Women VotersCalifornia Election CodeSan Mateo County Elections
