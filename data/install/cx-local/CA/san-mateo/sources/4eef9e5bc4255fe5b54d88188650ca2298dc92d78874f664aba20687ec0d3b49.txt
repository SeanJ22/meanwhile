                    Board of Directors Elections        
        
            
At the February 11, 2020 Board of Director’s meeting, the Board voted to change the Coastside County Water District’s electoral system for members of the Board of Directors from at-large to zone elections beginning with the November 3, 2020 election. The District now uses a system based on five zones and has established zone boundaries and sequencing of the elections. Instead of voting for each available open seat on the Board of Directors, you will now vote only for the candidates running within your zone.
