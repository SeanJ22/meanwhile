
                        
                           Trustee Areas and Elections
                        
                        
                           
                           The San Mateo County Community College District Board of Trustees transitioned to
                                 a by-trustee-area election system in 2018, with the county divided into distinct areas,
                                 each electing one member to the Board of Trustees. 
                           
                           Interested in Running for the Board of Trustees?
                           
                           Individuals interested in seeking election to the San Mateo County Community College
                              District Board of Trustees can access candidate information and filing deadlines at
                              the San Mateo County Office of Elections at: https://www.smcacre.org/elections.
                           
                           To find out which Trustee Area you live in, click here. 
                           
                           About the San Mateo County Community College District
                           
                           The San Mateo County Community College District is a three-college district comprised
                              of Cañada College in Redwood City, College of San Mateo and Skyline College in San
                              Bruno.  Serving nearly 30,000 students each year, the District offers world-class
                              learning opportunities in more than 100 programs that prepare students for graduation,
                              transfer, careers and personal enrichment.
