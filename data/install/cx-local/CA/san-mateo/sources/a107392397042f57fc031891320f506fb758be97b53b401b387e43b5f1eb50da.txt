						Overview of the Redistricting Process
	
	
		
	

				
			The South San Francisco Unified School District transitioned to by-trustee area elections starting in November 2020. This change aimed to improve local representation and comply with the California Voting Rights Act.


	
	In October 2019, SSFUSD reviewed two proposed maps (Map #1 and Map #2) developed by a consulting firm.
	
	
	Residents also submitted two additional maps (Map #3 and Map #4), which were designed in accordance with district boundary guidelines, including population balance (with a maximum difference of 1,558 between the largest and smallest areas).
	
	
	After community feedback and public hearings, the SSFUSD Board of Trustees adopted Map #5 as the final map on October 31, 2019.

						Final Map & Trustee District Descriptions
	
	
		
	

				
			Content:
On November 14, 2019, the SSFUSD Board of Trustees approved the final voting district map. This map established five trustee districts, each with roughly equal population to ensure fair representation.

District Descriptions:


	
	District A: Northwest section of South San Francisco, from Skyline Blvd into Serra Highlands, north to Serramonte Blvd.
	
	
	District B: Below District A, includes the Westborough neighborhood, up to Interstate 280.
	
	
	District C: Covers Winston Manor through Brentwood neighborhoods.
	
	
	District D: Surrounds Orange Ave, including Sunshine Gardens and Sign Hill, north of Hillside Blvd.
	
	
	District E: Includes Orange Park, Lindenville, Downtown, extending to Paradise Valley and the bayfront.
	


Population Stats:


	
	Total residents: 77,916
	
	
	Residents of voting age: 55,827
	
	
	Registered voters: 39,364 (2016 figures)
