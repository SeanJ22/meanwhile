Voter Education and Outreach Toolkit

The Registration & Elections Division creates election materials for distribution to eligible voters. The materials available on this website are intended for community members and partners to print and distribute within their organizations, meetings, and community events throughout San Mateo County. Printed copies of these materials are also available in limited quantities at the Registration & Elections Division. Please email registrar@smcacre.gov, call or text (650) 312-5222 to confirm the availability of the item and quantity you would like to pick up.
