Markdown Content:
Congratulations on your decision to seek office during the November 3, 2026 General Election. Public service is one of the pillars of our democracy. By running for public office, you give voters more choices, making it easier for them to support candidates who embody their values and priorities. The Office of the Registrar of Voters is committed to providing excellent candidate services to promote ballot access to all segments of our community.

We recommend that you file as early as possible to ensure that your documents are complete and accurate. Filing deadlines are absolute. Waiting until the end of the filing period could result in your name not being on the ballot if your documents are incomplete or in error.

If you have any questions or need additional information, please contact the Candidate Services Division. For additional information and resources about candidate filing as well as the overall election process, please refer to the information below and visit our [Candidate and Campaign Services](https://vote.santaclaracounty.gov/candidates-and-measures/candidate-and-campaign-services) webpage.

MEASURE INFORMATION AND ARGUMENT DEADLINES[![Image 2: Ballot Measure Image](https://files.santaclaracounty.gov/exjcpb1296/styles/wide_832/s3/2024-04/img_0035.jpg.webp?VersionId=DDTlcjNmFKQxMa0KswrU0RuNSnDcgqrq&itok=Ed05gH7F)](https://vote.santaclaracounty.gov/node/8411)Information related to regional, county, city, school, and special district measures can be found on our [List of Local Measures](https://vote.santaclaracounty.gov/list-local-measures-4) webpage. The webpage also includes deadline dates for primary and rebuttal arguments. This webpage will be updated daily as resolutions are adopted and filed with the Registrar of Voters office calling for a measure election.

Arguments and their submission process must comply with all rules. The deadlines for filing arguments for a city, school district, or special district measure on the November 3, 2026 General Election ballot are as follows:

**Primary Arguments in Favor of or Against a Measure**  
August 11, 2026 at 5:00 p.m. (12:00 p.m. for City of San Jose)

**Rebuttal Arguments to Arguments in Favor of and Against a Measure, and Impartial Analyses**  
August 18, 2026 at 5:00 p.m. (12:00 p.m. for City of San Jose)
