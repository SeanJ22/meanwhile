**Important Information as Voting for the November 3rd Gubernatorial General Election Approaches the Last Day: NOW’S THE TIME TO VOTE!**

Voters are encouraged to take advantage of the last few days of early voting ahead of Election Day on Tuesday, November 3; and be aware of**new urgency legislation** in California that may impact initial results reporting on Election Night: **Pursuant to California Elections Code §§ 407 and 15152, election results cannot be released if voting hours have been extended at any polling place in California due to a disruption or court order.**[(**Read the latest press release here**).](https://clerkrecorder.venturacounty.gov/county-clerk/press-releases/important-information-as-voting-for-the-november-3rd-gubernatorial-general-election-approaches-the-last-day-nows-the-time-to-vote/)

*   Check your registration

## Military and Overseas Voters

Looking for information for uniformed service members, their families and/or overseas citizens from California? Go to the [Military & Overseas Voter page](https://clerkrecorder.venturacounty.gov/elections/elections/voter-information/military-overseas/) to find out about registration, vote by mail ballots, voter information and more. You can also call us at 805-654-2664 or email elections@venturacounty.gov.
