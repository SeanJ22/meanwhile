# Voter Information Guides: County and State

### **County Voter Information Guide (CVIG)**

All active registered voters in Ventura County are sent a County Voter Information Guide (CVIG). The CVIG contains information about local candidates and ballot measures, and a sample of your ballot. The CVIG can also be accessed electronically prior to and during the election.

In order to access your CVIG in English and Spanish (the guide specific to your voter registration residence), as well as find information about your current elected officials, please enter the following information:

Processing...

![Image 6: Spinner](https://clerkrecorder.venturacounty.gov/img/spinner.gif)

1. Enter complete **House Number** of your home address.  
2. Begin typing your **Street Name** of your home address and street name choices will display.   
Only enter the Street Name. Do NOT include name descriptors like Road, Street or Avenue and do NOT enter any directional information such as E, W, S, N.   
3. Select **Search** button.  
***Contact the Elections Division if you experience any issues (805) 654-2664.

### **State Voter Information Guide (SVIG)**

All active registered voter households in Ventura County are sent a State Voter Information Guide (SVIG). The SVIG contains information about federal and statewide candidates and statewide ballot measures.

The SVIG can be accessed here electronically in your preferred language as they become available :

[**English**](https://vig.cdn.sos.ca.gov/2026/general/pdf/complete-vig.pdf)[**Espanol/Spanish**](https://vig.cdn.sos.ca.gov/2026/general/es/pdf/complete-vig.pdf)[**Tiếng Việt/Vietnamese**](https://vig.cdn.sos.ca.gov/2026/general/vi/pdf/complete-vig.pdf)
[**中文/ Chinese**](https://vig.cdn.sos.ca.gov/2026/general/ch/pdf/complete-vig.pdf)[**한국어/ Korean**](https://vig.cdn.sos.ca.gov/2026/general/ko/pdf/complete-vig.pdf)[**Tagalog**](https://vig.cdn.sos.ca.gov/2026/general/tl/pdf/complete-vig.pdf)
**[American Sign Language (ASL)](https://voterguide.sos.ca.gov/asl/)**

### **Large Print / Audio Versions – State Voter Information Guide**

The California Secretary of State provides the **State Voter Information Guide in accessible formats, including large-print and audio versions** in English, Spanish, Chinese, Hindi, Japanese, Khmer, Korean, Tagalog, Thai, and Vietnamese.

**Interested in an accessible format?** Visit the California Secretary of State’s [Other Formats | Official Voter Information Guide | California Secretary of State](https://voterguide.sos.ca.gov/en/alt-versions/) webpage to request the Audio/Large Print guides.
