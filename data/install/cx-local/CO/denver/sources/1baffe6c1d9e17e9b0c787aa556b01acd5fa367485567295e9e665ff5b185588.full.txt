federal law, and, in connection therewith, establishing hunting and 
fishing as the preferred means of managing fish and wildlife 
populations; and preserving the right of the state to regulate 
hunting, fishing, and wildlife management if necessary for sound

all species?

Ballot questions referred by the general assembly or any political 
subdivision are listed by letter, and ballot questions initiated by the 
people are listed numerically. A ballot question listed as an 
"amendment" proposes a change to the Colorado constitution, 
and a ballot question listed as a "proposition" proposes a change 
to the Colorado Revised Statutes. A "yes/for" vote on any ballot 
question is a vote in favor of changing current law or existing 
circumstances, and a "no/against" vote on any ballot question is a 
vote against changing current law or existing circumstances. 
 
Su’aalaha codbixinta ee Gollaha Sharci-dejinta ama qayb-hoosaad 
siyaasadeed kasta soo gudbiyo waxaa lagu taxaa xarfo, halka su’aalaha 
codbixinta ee dadku bilaabaan lagu taxo tirooyin. Su’aasha codbixinta ee lagu 
magacaabo "wax ka beddel" waxay soo jeedinaysaa in wax laga beddelo 
Dastuurka Colorado, halka su’aasha codbixinta ee lagu magacaabo "soo-
jeedin" ay soo jeedinayso in wax laga beddelo Qaanuunnada Dib loo 
Habeeyey ee Colorado. Codka "haa/taageersan" ee su’aal kasta oo codbixin 
ah waa cod lagu taageerayo beddelidda sharciga hadda jira ama duruufaha 
jira, halka codka "maya/diidan" ee su’aal kasta oo codbixin ah uu yahay cod 
lagaga soo horjeedo beddelidda sharciga hadda jira ama duruufaha jira.

Referred Question 2I 
Shall the Charter of the City and County of Denver be 
amended to adjust the City and County’s budget and 
appropriation process, and in connection therewith, 
allowing the City Council to adopt a two-year budget 
planning and adoption process and modernizing or 
repealing certain charter sections? 
Cinwaanka Cod bixinta [ 2I ]:Ma in Axdiga Magaalada iyo 
Degmada Denver wax laga beddelaa si loo hagaajiyo habka 
miisaaniyadda iyo qoondaynta lacagaha ee Magaalada iyo 
Degmada, oo iyadoo taas la xiriirinayo, loo ogolaado Golaha 
Magaalada inuu qaato hab laba-sano ah oo ah qorshaynta iyo 
ansixinta miisaaniyadda, iyo in la casriyeeyo ama la tirtiro 
qodobada qaarkood ee Axdiga? 
YES/FOR - HAA/TAAGEERSAN 
  
NO/AGAINST - MAYA/DIIDAN 
 
Referred Question 2J 
Shall the Charter of the City and County of Denver be 
amended to modernize the Civil Service Commission 
and Department of Safety by removing outdated 
language, giving City Council the ability to add duties 
via code, and providing for an extension of probation 
when required for medical, military, or other approved 
leave? 
Cinwaanka Cod bixinta [ 2J ]: Ma in Axdiga Magaalada iyo 
Degmada Denver wax laga beddelaa si loo casriyeeyo 
Guddiga Adeegga Rayidka iyo Waaxda Badbaadada 
(Department of Safety), iyadoo laga saarayo ereyad duugga 
ah, oo markaas Golaha Magaalada la siiyo awoodda uu 
waajibaadyo dheeraad ah ugu dari karo xeer, iyo in la 
ogolaado kordhinta mudada tijaabada shaqada marka loo 
baahdo sababo la xiriira fasax caafimaad, fasax milatari, ama 
fasax kale oo la ansixiyey awgood? 
YES/FOR - HAA/TAAGEERSAN 
  
NO/AGAINST - MAYA/DIIDAN 
 
Referred Question 2K 
Shall the City and County of Denver approve the 
terms of a twenty-year franchise contract, starting 
January 1, 2027, with Public Service Company of 
Colorado (Xcel Energy) to continue its program for 
undergrounding overhead electrical lines, maintaining 
infrastructure safety, and partnering with the City for 
emergency management coordination, and to require 
that Xcel Energy continue to pay the City for 
reasonable use of public rights-of-way and other City 
property necessary to provide safe and reliable utility 
services for the benefit of the City, its residents, and 
its businesses? 
Cinwaanka Cod bixinta [ 2K ]:Ma in Magaalada iyo 
Degmada Denver ay ansixiyaan baa shuruudaha heshiiska 
rukhsad-isticmaalka soconaya labaatan sannadood, kaasoo 
bilaabanaya Janaayo 1, 2027, oo lala gelayo Public Service 
Company of Colorado (Xcel Energy), si ay u sii wataan 
barnaamijyadooda dhulka hoostiisa loogu gelinayo 
khadadka korontada ee dusha sare mara, u ilaaliyaan 
badbaadada kaabayaasha, oo ay Magaalada kala 
shaqeeyaan isku-dubbaridka maaraynta xaaladaha 
degdegga ah; iyo in Xcel Energy laga rabo inay sii waddo 
bixinta lacagta Magaalada loogu talagalay isticmaalka 
macquulka ah ee waddooyinka iyo dhulalka kale ee ay 
Magaaladu leedahay ee dadweynuhu isticmaalaan, 
kuwaasoo lagamamaarmaan u ah bixinta adeegyo koronto 
oo ammaan ah oo la isku halayn karo, si ay uga 
faa’iidaystaan Magaalada, dadka deggan, iyo ganacsiyada? 
YES/FOR - HAA/TAAGEERSAN 
  
NO/AGAINST - MAYA/DIIDAN 
 
Initiated Ordinance 311 
Shall the voters of the City and County of Denver 
adopt an ordinance prohibiting the force-feeding of 
birds and, in connection therewith, prohibiting the 
sale of products derived from force-feeding birds, 
including foie gras? 
Cinwaanka Cod bixinta [ 311 ]:Miyaanay cod bixiyayaasha 
Magaalada iyo Degmada Denver ansixin xeer mamnuucaya 
in shimbiraha si khasab ah loo quudiyo, islamarkaana 
mamnuucaya iibinta badeecooyinka laga soo saaro 
shimbiraha sida khasabka ah loo quudiyo, oo ay ku jirto 
beerka shimbiraha? 
YES/FOR - HAA/TAAGEERSAN 
  
NO/AGAINST - MAYA/DIIDAN
