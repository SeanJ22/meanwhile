Denver Clerk and Recorder / Denver Elections Division / Campaign & Candidate Resources / City Council Vacancy Election City Council Vacancy Election On August 5, 2026, Denver City Council declared a vacancy in City Council At-Large, Seat B. Denver Elections will hold a Special Election to fill that seat in conjunction with the General Election on November 3, 2026. Maximum Public Funding Available: Up to $298,365 per candidate Fair Elections Fund Payment Schedule First Disbursement: Within 10 days of the August Campaign Finance Report due date. Final Disbursement: After the September Campaign Finance Report and no later than October 20, 2026. Candidate Debates There is no debate requirement for candidates participating in the Fair Elections Fund during a special vacancy election. Already Running for a 2027 Municipal Office? Vacancy election contribution limits are separate from 2027 limits. Existing candidate committee funds may be transferred to a new committee. New FEF qualification is required, including a new application and 100 new qualifying contributions. FEF funds received for the 2027 election cannot be used in the vacancy election. Vacancy Election FAQs Which City Council seat is being vacated? In 2025, voters adopted a charter amendment that designated Denver's two at-large City Council offices as Councilmember at-large seat A and Councilmember at-large seat B. Councilmember Parady's resignation leaves a vacancy in Councilmember at-large seat B. What are the qualifications to run? Candidate must be a citizen of the United States, a resident of the City and County of Denver for the two (2) years immediately preceding the member’s election, a qualified elector of the City and County of Denver, at least twenty-five (25) years of age by the date of the election. No person who has been convicted of willful evasion of City or State taxes; or who has been convicted of malfeasance in office, bribery or other corrupt practices, shall be qualified for membership in the Council. Do I need to run for the vacancy if I am already running for the 2027 election? No. It is your choice whether to run for the vacant office. When can I file for the office? When City Council declares a vacancy election (tentatively August 5, 2026), filing for the vacant office will open. When can I pick up petitions? When City Council declares a vacancy election (tentatively August 5, 2026), petitions for the vacant office will open. How many signatures are needed to qualify for the ballot? Petitions for Councilmember-at-large need 300 valid signatures for ballot access. When is the deadline to turn in petitions? Should City Council declare a vacancy election on August 5, 2026, petitions would tentatively be due no later than August 20, 2026. For information on petition circulation requirements, please view Step 4 of the 2027 Municipal Candidate Guide. Will there be a runoff election? No. The candidate receiving the greatest number of votes at said election shall qualify and take such office immediately. How long will the winning candidate serve in office? The winner of the vacancy election fills the remainder of the vacating member’s term. Vacancy Election Sample Ballots This section is coming soon Need Help?
