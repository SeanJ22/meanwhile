        Learn About the Town's ElectionsTown elections are held in November of even-numbered years and are coordinated and administered by El Paso County. Coordinated elections may include the election of Town Council members, ballot questions and issues, and referred ordinances or resolutions. The Town Council may also call a special election, if necessary, by ordinance or resolution. Special elections may be held on certain Tuesdays outside the regular election cycle, subject to timing restrictions established by state law. The Town Clerk serves as the Designated Election Official for the Town of Monument.November 3, 2026 Regular Municipal ElectionAt the upcoming November 3, 2026, Regular Municipal Election, Monument voters will elect five officials: the mayor, two at-large councilmembers, one district 1 councilmember, and one district 2 councilmember. The mayor, district 1 councilmember, and district 2 councilmember will each be elected to four-year terms. For the two at-large councilmember seats, the candidate receiving the highest number of votes will be elected to a four-year term, and the candidate receiving the second-highest number of votes will be elected to a two-year term. Assigning one shorter at-large term preserves the Charter's staggered term structure and ensures that the two at-large seats do not expire at the same time in future elections.Candidate(s) for mayorLaura Kronick, Kenneth KimpleCandidate(s) for district 1 councilmembe​r Dan DeBooCandidate(s) for district 2 councilmemberMarco P.
