REFERENDUM ON PROPOSED CHARTER AMENDMENTS - LIMITATIONS ON SALE,
            LEASE, PURCHASE OF CITY-OWNED PROPERTY.

Amending Charter Article XIII, "Limitation on Sale, Lease or Purchase of City-Owned Real
Property," providing such leases of property for 20 years or longer, including renewals,
require a five-sevenths approval of City Commission; providing such sales or leases of 50
years or longer, including renewals, of City-owned parks, golf courses, recreational facilities,
designated historic properties, or properties east of the Intracoastal Waterway require
majority approval of City's electors.
