Assessment Information
TOLOMATO COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT DISTRICT
The schedules included herein are for general reference purposes only.
The schedules detail the annual assessments for the TOLOMATO COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT DISTRICT (CDD) for the Fiscal Year 2027. These assessments will be paid as part of the County Property Tax bill received in November 2026.
The
Total Assessment
is the amount that is shown on the County Property Tax bill for November 2026. This assessment is for the CDD fiscal year, beginning October 1, 2026, and ending September 30, 2027. This represents your entire CDD assessment for the fiscal year.  The component parts of the Total Assessment are Debt and O&M.
Debt
represents the amount of the annual debt payment (principal and interest) that was billed on the November 2026 County Property Tax Bill. These funds go directly to a bond Trustee to pay down bonds used to construct District infrastructure.
Operations & Maintenance (O&M)
is responsible for the day-to-day operations and maintenance of the CDD, including landscape maintenance and amenities.
The
Approximate Debt Payoff
represents the approximate amount remaining on the debt portion of the CDD assessment.  This is not intended to be an actual amount for purposes of paying off or paying down the debt portion of the CDD assessment or for any legal purposes.
If you require an exact number for purposes of paying off the debt, please request an estoppel letter from
joy@nocatee.com
.  There is a $250 fee for each estoppel letter.
Properties platted after January 2026 are not reflected on the November 2026 county property tax records and are not reflected here.  This information will be available next tax year.
This schedule does not reflect debt payoffs or paydowns that occurred after 8/1/26.
For any additional information, you may request an estoppel letter by emailing
joy@nocatee.com
There is a $250 charge for each estoppel letter issued.
The Tolomato CDD has made efforts to ensure the accuracy of this information.  However, the information is provided as-is, without warranty of any kind.
The Tolomato CDD does not accept any responsibility or liability for the accuracy, content, completeness, or reliability of this information.
If you require accurate and complete information, please request an estoppel letter from
joy@nocatee.com
.
