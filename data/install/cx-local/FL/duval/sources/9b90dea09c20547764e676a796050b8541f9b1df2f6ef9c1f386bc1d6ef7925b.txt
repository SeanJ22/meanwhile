AB MUNICIPAL ELECTION
General Election - November 3, 2026
The purpose of this upcoming election will be to fill the following Commission Seats:
Offices up for election:
Mayor/Commissioner- Seat 1
(2-year term) -
At Large
Currently held by Curtis Ford
Commissioner- Seat 4
(4-year term) -
District 1306
Currently held by Candace Kelly
Please Note: Jessica Ring (incumbent) qualified unopposed and is therefore considered (re)elected to the office of Commissioner - Seat 5
(4-year term) -
District 1312.  As a result, Seat 5 will not be on the ballot.
All Atlantic Beach Commission Offices are elected
at
-large
.  AB registered voters can vote for candidates
of all seats up for election
, regardless of their district.  A seat’s district assignment relates to the residency requirements for candidates.
Residency requirements
All candidates must have been full-time residents and electors of the city for at least four years immediately prior to qualifying, and must not hold any other elective office. Candidates for Seats 4 & 5 are required to reside within the district for which they are seeking.
The Mayor has no district requirement. The Mayor and Commissioners are all elected At-Large.
If you’d like to know which district you are in, please review the
district map
or enter your address on the
precinct finder page
on the Duval County Supervisor of Election’s website.
The City Clerk serves as the filing officer for AB municipal elections. For more information, please contact the City Clerk.
