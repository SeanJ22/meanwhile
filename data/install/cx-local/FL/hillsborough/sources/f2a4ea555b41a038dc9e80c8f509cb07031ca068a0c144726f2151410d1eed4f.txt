Registered voter in Hillsborough County. Candidates running for Districts 2 and 4 must reside within the district
for which the candidate qualifies at the time of taking office. Candidates for District 6 may live anywhere in
Hillsborough County.
                                               FORMS REQUIRED TO OPEN CAMPAIGN ACCOUNT

Form DS-DE9, Appointment of Campaign Treasurer and Designation of Campaign Depository, must be filed with the
qualifying officer before opening a campaign bank account or accepting or spending any funds. Within ten days
after filing Form DS-DE9, the candidate must file Form DS-DE84, Statement of Candidate, with the qualifying officer.
