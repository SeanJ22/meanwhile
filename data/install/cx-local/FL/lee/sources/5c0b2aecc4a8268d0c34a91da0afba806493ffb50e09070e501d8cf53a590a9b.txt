Lee County / Fort Myers Beach Mosquito Control District Referendum



					

				




	    



        
Posted September 8, 2026
Merger of the Lee County and Fort Myers Beach Mosquito Control Districts
Florida Legislature Special District Referendum
The Lee County Supervisor of Elections gives notice that on Tuesday, November 3, 2026, a Special Election will be conducted at which the voters of Lee County residing in the Lee County Mosquito Control and the Fort Myers Beach Mosquito Control Districts will be asked whether they approve or disapprove of amending the boundaries of the Lee County Mosquito Control District to include all of Lee County, abolishing the Fort Myers Beach Mosquito Control District, and transferring the assets and liabilities of the Fort Myers Beach Mosquito Control District to the Lee County Mosquito Control District.

Posted September 8, 2026
Merger of Fort Myers Beach and Iona-McGregor Fire Control and Rescue Service Districts
Florida Legislature Special District Referendum
The Lee County Supervisor of Elections gives notice that on Tuesday, November 3, 2026, a Special Election will be conducted at which the voters of Lee County residing in the Iona-McGregor Fire Protection and Rescue Service District and the Fort Myers Beach Fire Control District will be asked whether they approve or disapprove abolishing the Fort Myers Beach Fire Control District, providing for a transitional board, and transferring the assets and liabilities of the Fort Myers Beach Fire Control District to the Iona-McGregor Fire Protection and Rescue Service District.

Withdrawn Candidates



					

				




	    



        
Posted: September 15, 2026 | Updated: September 23, 2026
The following candidates withdrew their names from the November 3, 2026, General Election:
Town of Fort Myers Beach Town Council
John McLean - withdrew 9/15/2026
Gateway Services Community Development District - Seat 4
Paul LeoGrande - withdrew 9/23/2026
Important Note: Due to the late withdrawal, the candidate's name will still appear on the ballot. Any votes for balloted, withdrawn candidates will be tallied, but will not count. 
A vote cast in the Gateway Services Community Development District Seat 4 race will not change the outcome, as the remaining candidate is deemed by law to be unopposed and elected for that race. All votes cast in this race will be tallied but will not count.
