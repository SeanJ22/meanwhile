Deadline to Return Vote-by-Mail Ballot to the Lee County Supervisor of Elections
Before 7 pm on Election Day for any election. A Vote-by-Mail ballot received after 7 pm on November 
3
, 2026
, is ineligible to be counted.
By Mail
If you plan to return your ballot in the mail, we suggest you do so 
no later than Tuesday, October 20, 2026
.
Hand Delivery
If you would prefer that your ballot be hand-delivered, d
uring Early Voting, Vote-by-Mail ballots may be dropped off at an authorized, secure ballot intake station located at each early voting location. (

any Supervisor of Elections office during regular business hours.
