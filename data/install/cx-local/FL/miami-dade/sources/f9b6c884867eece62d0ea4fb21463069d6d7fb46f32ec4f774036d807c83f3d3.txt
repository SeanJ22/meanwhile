The official Sample Ballot for the November 3 General Election is now available
Will be available in print in several Miami-Dade newspapers in the coming days and
at government offices, libraries, Supervisor of Elections locations, during early
voting at all sites, on Election Day, and at other key distribution points
Miami-Dade County Supervisor of Elections Alina Garcia is encouraging voters to make
a plan for the November 3 General Election beginning with reviewing the official Sample
Ballot now available on our website.
<p>Miami-Dade County Supervisor of Elections Alina Garcia is encouraging voters to
make a plan for the November 3 General &nbsp;Election beginning with reviewing the
official Sample Ballot now available at <a href="https://www.miamidade.gov/elections/library/2026-11-03-general-election-sample-ballot.pdf">https://www.miamidade.gov/elections/library/2026-11-03-general-election-sample-ballot.pdf</a>.</p>
<p>The official Sample Ballot allows voters to study the contests, candidates, and
questions that will appear on the ballot before heading to the polls or completing
their Vote-by-Mail ballot. Reviewing a Sample Ballot in advance helps voters make
informed decisions and ensures a quicker, smoother, and more efficient voting experience.
Voters can also view their customized personal ballots at <a href="https://www.votemiamidade.gov/elections/voters/information.page">https://www.votemiamidade.gov/elections/voters/information.page</a>.</p>
<p>&ldquo;Our goal is to make voting as easy and accessible as possible for every
voter in Miami-Dade County,&rdquo; said <strong>Supervisor Garcia</strong>. &ldquo;By
reviewing the Sample Ballot and especially your customized personal ballot before
voting, you can feel prepared, confident, and ready to make your voice heard.&rdquo;</p>
<p>Voters are encouraged to visit <strong>votemiamidade.gov</strong> to view important
election information, including voting locations, deadlines, and other voter resources.
The 2026 General Election Master Ballot with all contests and questions can be viewed
at <a href="https://www.miamidade.gov/elections/library/2026-11-03-general-election-master-ballot.pdf">https://www.miamidade.gov/elections/library/2026-11-03-general-election-master-ballot.pdf</a>.</p>
<p>For more information, please call <strong>305-499-VOTE (8683)</strong>.</p>

Miami-Dade Supervisor of Elections
<p>We pledge to instill trust and confidence by conducting elections that are fair,
accurate, transparent and accessible for all voters of Miami-Dade County.</p>
<p>We handle all federal, state, county and municipal elections. We also provide election
services and assistance to municipalities, School Districts, Special Taxing Districts,
Community Council Districts, and Community Development Districts in Miami-Dade County.</p>
<p>We provide services to registered and potential voters, as County candidates and
political committees, third-party organizations, and the community at large.</p>
<p>Voting is one of our constitutional rights and key to our nation's democracy. Through
your vote, you exercise your right to be heard.</p>
<p>The Miami-Dade Supervisor of Elections is here to serve you! Please do not hesitate
to email us with your questions and suggestions, or call 311 for additional information.</p>

MIAMI-DADE ( September 22, 2026 )–
Miami-Dade County Supervisor of Elections Alina Garcia is encouraging voters to make a plan for the November 3 General Election beginning with reviewing the official Sample Ballot now available at https://www.miamidade.gov/elections/library/2026-11-03-general-election-sample-ballot.pdf.
The official Sample Ballot allows voters to study the contests, candidates, and questions that will appear on the ballot before heading to the polls or completing their Vote-by-Mail ballot. Reviewing a Sample Ballot in advance helps voters make informed decisions and ensures a quicker, smoother, and more efficient voting experience. Voters can also view their customized personal ballots at https://www.votemiamidade.gov/elections/voters/information.page.
“Our goal is to make voting as easy and accessible as possible for every voter in Miami-Dade County,” said Supervisor Garcia. “By reviewing the Sample Ballot and especially your customized personal ballot before voting, you can feel prepared, confident, and ready to make your voice heard.”
Voters are encouraged to visit votemiamidade.gov to view important election information, including voting locations, deadlines, and other voter resources. The 2026 General Election Master Ballot with all contests and questions can be viewed at https://www.miamidade.gov/elections/library/2026-11-03-general-election-master-ballot.pdf.
For more information, please call 305-499-VOTE (8683).
