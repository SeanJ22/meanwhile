Sample ballots
Sample ballots are a great way to prepare before you vote. They allow you to review the contests and candidates that may appear on your ballot, research issues in advance, and make informed decisions before heading to the polls. Orange County voters can view and download their personalized sample ballot once ballots become available for an upcoming election. Your sample ballot is based on your residential address, precinct, districts, and party affiliation, if applicable.
VIEW YOUR PERSONALIZED SAMPLE BALLOT
You can view your personalized sample ballot for the next upcoming election by:
Entering your information into the Check Your Voter Information tool to view your personalized sample ballot for the next upcoming election.
Use the What’s On My Ballot tool to view sample ballots for any precinct or residential address in Orange County.
