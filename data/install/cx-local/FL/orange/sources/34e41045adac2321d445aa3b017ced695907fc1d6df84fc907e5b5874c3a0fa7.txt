CANDIDATE LIST
This page serves as the official list of candidates and committees participating in upcoming elections in Orange County. Candidates are organized by election and office, making it easy to view who has filed and qualified to appear on the ballot. Click on a candidate’s name to view additional information, including contact information, party affiliation, and candidate status.
You can also use this page to review campaign finance activity filed with the Orange County Supervisor of Elections office. Available records may include campaign treasurer reports, contributions, expenditures, loans, transfers, and other financial filings submitted throughout the election cycle. Campaign finance information is updated as reports are filed.
