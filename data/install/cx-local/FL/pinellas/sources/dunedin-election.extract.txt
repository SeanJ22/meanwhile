Mail Ballot

Mail ballot requests expire after every General Election (December 31 in even numbered years). Voters must update their request electronically at the link below or by calling 727-464-VOTE (8683).
If you recently renewed your Florida Driver's License and the number changed, or if you have a protected address, then the request tool may not work for you. Call 727-464-VOTE (8683) instead.  
Las solicitudes de boletas por correo se vencen después de cada Elección General (el 31 de diciembre de los años pares). Los vontantes deben actualizar su solicitud electrónicamente a través del enlace que aparece a continuación o llamando al 727-464-VOTE (8683).
Si recientemente renovó su licencia de conducir de Florida y el número cambió, o si tiene una dirección protegida, es posible que la herramienta de solicitud no funcione para usted. En ese caso, llame al 727-464-VOTE (8683).

Everything You Need to Know

Early voting
Skip the Election Day lines and vote early at any early voting site in Pinellas County from Monday, October 19 to Sunday, November 1. The Dunedin Public Library is also an official ballot drop-off location. You may drop your ballot here between October 19 and October 31 from 10 a.m. to 5 p.m., Monday through Saturday. There is no drop-off on Sunday, November 1. Please place your ballot in the official ballot box inside the library. Do not put your ballot in the library book returns or hand to library staff members. Find other locations and hours at VotePinellas.gov.
Election Day
Election Day is Tuesday, November 3. Polls are open from 7 a.m. to 7 p.m. Look up your polling place at VotePinellas.gov.
What to bring
State law requires a voter to present valid picture and signature identification at the polling place. Florida accepts several forms of photo and signature ID, including a driver's license, state ID card, U.S. passport, or student ID. Voters without acceptable identification may vote using a provisional ballot, which will be evaluated later for eligibility by a canvassing board.
Votación anticipada
Evite las filas del día de las elecciones y vote por adelantado en cualquier centro de votación anticipada del condado de Pinellas, desde Lunes, Octubre 19 hasta Domingo, Noviembre 1. La Biblioteca Pública de Dunedin también es un lugar oficial para depositar las boletas. Puede entregar su boleta allí entre el 19 y el 31 de Octubre, de Lunes a Sábado, en horario de 10 a. m. a 5 p. m. No se aceptarán boletas el Domingo 1 de Noviembre. Por favor, deposite su boleta en la urna oficial ubicada dentro de la biblioteca. No entrege la boleta en el buzón de devolución de libros ni se la entregue al personal de la biblioteca. Consulte otras ubicaciones y horarios en VotePinellas.gov.
Día de las elecciones
El día de las elecciones es el Martes 3 de Noviembre. Los centros de votación estarán abiertos de 7 a. m. a 7 p. m. Busque su centro de votación en VotePinellas.gov.
Qué llevar
La ley estatal exige que el votante presente una identificación válida con fotografía y firma en el centro de votación. Florida acepta varios tipos de identificación con foto y firma, incluyendo la licencia de conducir, la tarjeta de identificación estatal, el pasaporte de los EE. UU. o la identificación de estudiante. Los votantes que no tienen una identificación aceptable pueden votar mediante una boleta provisional; la boleta será evaluada posteriormente por una junta de escrutinio para determinar su validez.

Election Schedule

Commission Seats to be elected in the City of Dunedin, Florida, on November 3, 2026: 
Commissioner Seat 2
Commissioner Seat 4
Per the City Charter, Section 5.03, Elections, the Commissioners will serve a four (4) year term, commencing the second (2nd) Monday following the election (Special Meeting of November 16, 2026).
Pursuant to the Dunedin City Charter, Section 5.01, Elections, "All persons qualified as an elector of this State, under the Constitution and Statutes of the State of Florida, who reside within the corporate boundaries of the City of Dunedin, and who are duly registered on the registration books of Pinellas County shall be qualified electors of the City of Dunedin in all elections except as otherwise provided by law."
Further, pursuant to the Dunedin City Code, Sec. 26-73, Method of qualifying, "As a condition precedent to qualifying, the candidates shall be required to file with their petition cards personal affidavits showing that they are residents of the city, having physically resided therein for a period of at least one year immediately prior to submitting the petition cards and are qualified electors of the City."
Further, pursuant to the Dunedin City Code, Sec. 26-74, Time for qualifying, "No person may qualify as a candidate prior to the 113th day before the next city election nor later than 12:00 pm, local time, on the 99th day before the said election, except that if the 99th day falls on a day that is a Saturday, Sunday or city holiday, the qualifying period shall be extended to the next city business day."
