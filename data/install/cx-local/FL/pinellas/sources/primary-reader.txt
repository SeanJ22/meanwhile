# August 18, 2026 Primary Election Information Page

1.   [Key Dates, Deadlines & Other Details](https://www.votepinellas.gov/375/August-18-2026-Primary-Election-Informat?x=1#tabad3c9b90-20fd-4f59-a7ec-e166e121c3c6_0)

## Voter Registration

Voter registration deadline is July 20. If voters want to change their party affiliation to participate in Florida's [Closed Primaries](https://www.votepinellas.gov/m/faq?cat=25#question-136), they must do so by this date.

## Early Voting

Early Voting will be held August 8-16. See the [Early Voting](https://www.votepinellas.gov/200) page for hours and locations.

## Mailing Dates for Mail Ballots

*   Military and Overseas Voters - July 2 (statutory deadline July 4)
*   Domestic Voters - July 14 (statutory deadline July 16)

## Request a Mail Ballot

[Complete the online form to request a mail ballot](https://www.votepinellas.gov/190/Request-a-Mail-Ballot) or call 727-464-VOTE (8683).

The deadline to request a ballot to be mailed is August 6 at 5 pm.

## Election Day Hours

Polls are open 7 am to 7 pm on August 18.

## Sample Ballots

*   Use [Find Your Precinct](https://www.votepinellas.gov/198/Find-Your-Precinct-Polling-Place) to view your precinct-specific sample ballot.
*   [ADA Accessible Sample Ballot](https://app.enhancedvoting.com/ebd/voter/pinellas-county-fl/sampleballots/lookup)

*   *** Note:**The contests for State Senator District 16, State Representative District 62, and County Commissioner Seat 4 are **Universal Primary Contests** where all qualified candidates are registered with the same political party. All qualified electors who reside in the applicable district are eligible to vote in those contests, regardless of party affiliation. The candidate who receives the most votes in each contest will be the winner, and the contest will not appear on the General Election ballot.  
*   For nonpartisan contests, if no candidate wins the majority of the vote in the contest (50% plus one vote) the two candidates receiving the most votes will be in a runoff on the November 3 General Election ballot.
*   Unopposed candidates will not appear on the ballot.
*   Information on this page is subject to change.
