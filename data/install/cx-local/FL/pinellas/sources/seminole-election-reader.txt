# Election Information

## Election Day: November 3, 2026

## Seats Open

The following three candidates have qualified for the two City Council Seats, each serving a three-year term, in the upcoming November 3, 2026, election.

When you receive your ballot, remember to vote for **two (2) candidates**.

For further election information, please view the [Pinellas County Supervisor of Elections website](https://www.votepinellas.gov/).

## Candidate Requirements

Must be a registered voter and resident of the City of Seminole for at least a one-year period immediately prior to qualifying, and meet all other qualifying requirements as outlined in the [City of Seminole Charter](https://www.myseminole.com/167/City-Charter-PDF) and [Code of Ordinances](https://library.municode.com/fl/seminole/codes/code_of_ordinances).

Council Position Salary - $10,168.34 per year

## Method of Qualifying

1.   Obtain a Qualifying Packet from the City Clerk (Packets available starting July 14, 2026).
2.   File the necessary documents during the qualifying period, including the following fees and/or petition cards.

### Petition Cards and Fees

**Option 1:**  
Submit 40 petition cards signed by qualified city electors, pay a qualifying fee of $15.00, and a State Election Assessment fee of $101.68 for a total of $116.68.

**Option 2:**  
Pay a qualifying fee of $100.00 and a State Election Assessment fee of $101.68 for a total of $201.68.

**Must be payable with a check from a campaign account.**

View additional [Election information (PDF)](https://www.myseminole.com/DocumentCenter/View/186/Election-Information-PDF)
