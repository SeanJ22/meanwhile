							Candidate Rules & Information
Election Dates

Primary Election: August 18, 2026
General Election: November 3, 2026

Please contact the Clerk's Office about future elections and more information on how to run for office

Email: city.clerk@stpete.org
Phone: 727-893-7448

Qualifying Period

Starts Monday, May 25, 2026, at noon
Ends on Friday, May 29, 2026, at noon

Qualifying papers may be accepted no earlier than Monday, May 11, 2026 – Monday, May 25, 2026, fourteen days prior to the start of the qualifying period.
*Please note that the dates will be updated once the City Council addresses the qualifying period during a future Committee of the Whole.
Qualifications

Must be a registered voter.
Candidates for Council Member must have been a resident of the declared district for at least the past 12 months, as of the date of the primary election.
Candidates for Mayor must have been a resident of the City for at least the past 12 months, as of the date of the primary election.
Candidates cannot have become and cannot be a candidate, a nominee, or representative of any political party or any committee or convention representing or acting for any political party.

Term of Office - 2026

The elections for Mayor and City Council Districts 2, 4, 6, and 8 are for a full term of approximately four 4 Years, beginning the first Thursday in January 2027 and running through the first Thursday in January 2031.
