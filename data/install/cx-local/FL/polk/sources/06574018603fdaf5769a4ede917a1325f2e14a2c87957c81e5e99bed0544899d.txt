Notice is hereby given that a referendum election will be held throughout Polk County on November 3, 2026, at the polling place designated within each voting precinct of the County for the purpose of determining the following question:
County School Board Referendum / Referéndum Junta Escolar del Condado
Millage for School Safety, Teachers, and Career Education
Shall the Polk County School District levy an additional 1.0 mill in ad valorem taxes annually to fund school operations for teacher recruitment and retention and education support staff, for career and technical education, school safety, arts, and early learning--with proceeds shared with charter schools as required by law and monitored by an independent citizens' oversight committee?
Tasa Impositiva para la Seguridad Escolar, los Maestros y la Educación Profesional
¿Debería el Distrito Escolar del Condado de Polk imponer anualmente una tasa adicional de 1.0 milésima en impuestos ad valorem para financiar las operaciones escolares destinadas a la contratación y retención de maestros y personal de apoyo educativo para la educación profesional y técnica, la seguridad escolar, las artes y la educación temprana, distribuyendo los fondos recaudados con las escuelas chárter según lo exige la ley y bajo la supervisión de un comité ciudadano independiente?
_______ Yes / Sí
_______ No / No
The polls will be open at the voting places on the date of such election from 7:00 a.m. to 7:00 p.m.  All qualified voters residing within Polk County may vote in said election.  Early voting and absentee voting will be permitted upon compliance with the applicable provisions of general law.
By:      Justin Sharpless, Board Chair
Attest:  Frederick R. Heid, Secretary
Publication dates:  September 28, 2026 and October 13, 2026

PRE-QUALIFICATION OF VENDORS FOR PRICED-BASED SOLICITATIONS
FOR CONSTRUCTION PROJECTS
The School Board of Polk County ("District") invites qualified vendors with current licenses in the State of Florida to register for the District's Pre Qualification Vendor Program. This Program applies only to price-based solicitations (Invitations to Bid (ITB)) or formal quotes for alterations, renovations, modifications, repairs and minor, new construction projects with an estimated budget less than $4,000,000.
The Pre-Qualification Vendor Program is an ongoing program of the School District. Once a firm has been qualified and approved by the District, it may participate and compete with other qualified vendors in Invitations to Bid for a three year period commencing January 1, 2026 – December 31, 2028.
