3 Easy Ways to Vote
Vote by Mail
:
Request a mail ballot
online. Voters have
until Thursday, October 22, 2026,
to request a mail ballot for this election. All voted mail ballots must be received by the Supervisor of Elections by 7 p.m. on Election Day. Return postage is paid for mail ballots. However, voters who prefer to return their voted mail ballots in person can drop them off at any of the listed Early Voting sites during Early Voting days and hours, or at Election Headquarters in Bartow or the Election Operations Center in Winter Haven on weekdays, 8 a.m. to 5 p.m.

Need a Ride to the Polls?
Citrus Connection is making it easier for Polk County residents to participate in the democratic process by offering free fixed-route bus service to voters during the 2026 General Election Early Voting period and on Election Day.
The initiative is designed to eliminate transportation barriers and encourage civic engagement by providing convenient, free transportation to polling locations throughout Polk Count.  Passengers simply need to present their voter registration card to the bus operator to ride free on any Citrus Connection fixed-route bus.
For more information and to plan your route, visit
