0143.1 -
RESIDENCE AREAS
As provided by law, the School Board may make any change that it deems necessary in the boundaries of any Board member residence area at any meeting of the Board, provided that such changes are not made in the 270 days before a general election and that no change would affect the residence qualifications of any incumbent members if it disqualifies such incumbent member during their elected term. Residence areas may not be drawn with the intent to favor or disfavor a candidate for Board member or an incumbent Board member based on the candidate's or incumbent's residential address.
Each elected candidate for the Board member must, by the date the candidate assumes office, be a resident of the Board member residence area from which the candidate was elected and shall reside through the member's term of office in the residency area in which election occurred.
Each qualified elector of the District is entitled to vote for one (1) candidate from each Board member residence area.
