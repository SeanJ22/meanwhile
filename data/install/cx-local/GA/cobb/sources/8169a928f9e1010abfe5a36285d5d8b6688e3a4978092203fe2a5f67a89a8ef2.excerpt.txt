A sample ballot gives you an opportunity to see the contests and questions you can expect to find on your ballot ahead of time. What Is a Sample Ballot? A sample ballot is a preview of the ballot for an upcoming election. Because ballots are based on your voter registration information and the districts associated with your residence, your ballot may be different from the ballot of a friend, family member or someone who lives elsewhere in Cobb County. Where Can I Find My Sample Ballot? Cobb voters have two resources for viewing sample ballots: Personalized Sample Ballot Available through the Georgia My Voter Page (MVP). Your personalized sample ballot shows the contests associated with your voter record and districts. View Your Personalized Sample Ballot on Georgia's My Voter Page Consolidated Sample Ballot Available on the Cobb Elections website. The consolidated sample ballot includes contests from across Cobb County, including contests that may not appear on your individual ballot. View the consolidated sample ballot For the best look at what will appear on your ballot, use your personalized sample ballot through MVP. Why Review Your Ballot Ahead of Time? Reviewing your sample ballot gives you time to: See the offices and contests on your ballot. Read ballot questions and proposed amendments. Research candidates and issues using sources you choose. Make notes for your own reference. Become familiar with ballot terminology. Coming Tomorrow: Tomorrow, we'll look at your voting options and the information you need to make a plan to vote. About National Voter Education Week National Voter Education Week is a nonpartisan civic education initiative focused on helping voters understand the voting process and find reliable election information. Throughout the week, Cobb Elections is sharing resources covering voter registration, sample ballots, voting options, election processes and trusted sources for election information.
