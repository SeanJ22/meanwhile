Cobb Elections Announces Isolated Districting Error Affecting Sewell Mill 01 Precinct

Share:

Monday, October 26, 2026

Cobb County Board of Elections and Registration recently discovered a districting error that affects a limited number of voters in the Sewell Mill 01 Precinct. This error is confined to a single precinct and involves only two unopposed races for the Georgia House of Representative, Ga HD 37 (Mary Frances Williams) and Ga HD 43 (Solomon Adesanya). Both races have a single candidate running unopposed, so this error cannot affect the outcome of either race. No other races are affected by the error.

 

All of Sewell Mill 01 Precinct, as shown on the attached maps, is districted to vote in Georgia House District 37 under the most recent district maps enacted by 2023 HB 1EX. However, due to an error in uploading ballot combinations for the 2026 election cycle, the voters of Sewell Mill 01 were inadvertently placed in House District 43.

 

Because the error was discovered after Advance-In-Person Voting had already begun for this election, it is not possible to make changes to the precincts and districts in the State’s voter information system, GARViS. That means that during this election voters who vote in Sewell Mill 01 Precinct will see the uncontested race for HD 43 (Solomon Adesanya) on their ballot, when they should see the uncontested race for HD 37 (Mary Frances William) on their ballot. A notice informing voters about this error will be posted at Sewell Mill 01 precinct and at all Advance Voting locations. The notice will also: request that voters vote their ballot normally; will note that any votes cast in the HD 43 race by voters in Sewell Mill 01 precinct will not be counted towards the vote totals for that race; and note that because the race is uncontested, this will not affect the election outcome.

 

Cobb Elections staff has performed an audit of precincts in the affected districts to verify that the error was isolated to this single precinct and only involves these two races. Cobb Elections staff can also confirm that this error was only present for the 2026 election cycle.1

 

Cobb County BOER is committed to ensuring fair, accurate, and transparent elections, and part of that duty of transparency is informing the public of any problems that occur at an early stage. Cobb Elections has informed both candidates of the issue, along with both parties, and the Secretary of State. Cobb BOER will also self-report this problem to the State Election Board. The Board will maintain that same level of transparency throughout the process of correcting the issue and finalizing the complete audit of all precinct ballot combinations.

 

__________________________

1 The candidate running unopposed in the General Election for House District 43, Solomon Adesanya, was also unopposed during the May 2026 Primary, so the additional 627 votes he received from voters in Sewell Mill 01 in that primary would not have affected the outcome of the primary race. The candidate running unopposed in the General Election for House District 37, Mary Frances Williams, won her May 2026 Primary by margin of 2,755 votes. Thus, even if the candidate opposing her in the primary, Graham Bowers, had received all 627 of the votes cast in the HD 43 race (Adesanya) from Sewell Mill 01 precinct, Williams’ margin would still have been 2,128, meaning those votes would not have changed the outcome of her primary.
