Terms & Qualifications
Council members are elected to four-year terms. The mayor is also elected to a four-year term, but elected at-large. To be eligible to run for a city council seat, a person must:
Be 21 years of age;
Have resided in the city and in the district the person seeks to represent for at least one year;
Be a qualified elector of the city;
Meet any other requirements as established by state law.
The Mayor and Council serve staggered terms. The following are the terms currently being served:
Mayor - 01/01/2024 to 12/31/2027
Councilmember, District 1 - 01/01/2022 to 12/31/2029
Councilmember, District 2 - 01/01/2024 to 12/31/2027
Councilmember, District 3 - 11/17/2022 to 12/31/2029
Councilmember, District 4 - 01/01/2024 to 12/31/2027
Councilmember, District 5 - 01/01/2022 to 12/31/2029

To register to vote, you must:
Be a citizen of the United States
Be a legal resident of Georgia
Be at least 17½ years of age (and at least 18 to vote)
Not be serving a sentence for a conviction of a felony involving moral turpitude
Have not been found mentally incompetent by a judge
