Mission
The mission of the DeKalb County Voter Registration and Elections (VRE) is to provide the residents with quality voter registration and election services, information and education that enable them to exercise their right to vote and have confidence that the elections are fair, impartial, and accurate.
Goals
DeKalb Voter Registration and Elections (VRE) is committed to strengthening public trust and operational excellence through a strategic focus on accuracy, accessibility, transparency and accountability. To that end, VRE works throughout the year to enable electors to be super voters by maintaining accurate and compliant voter registration records, delivering secure, accessible, and transparent elections, enhancing community engagement through proactive communication and education, and advancing organizational excellence through collaboration, innovation, and a skilled and accountable workforce.
Goal 1:
Strengthen Voter Registration Integrity
Goal 2:
Deliver Secure, Transparent,  Accessible and Accurate Elections
Goal 3:
Enhance Engagement and Public Trust
Goal 4:
Advance Organizational Excellence
