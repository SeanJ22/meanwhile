                           
Pursuant to O.C.G.A. § 21-2-540(b), the public is hereby notified 
that the City of Stonecrest, Georgia, will have a Special Election on 
November 3, 2026, for the purpose of electing one (1) City Council 
Member, for District 2 in the City of Stonecrest. 
 
Qualifying will be held Monday, August 10th – Friday, August 14, 
2026, at Stonecrest City Hall, 3120 Stonecrest Boulevard, 
Stonecrest, GA 30038 during the hours of 8:30am - 12:00pm and 
1:00pm - 4:30pm each day during the qualifying period.   
The qualifying fee is Four Hundred Fifty Dollars ($450.00) for City 
Council Member candidates. 
 
It is not a requirement to schedule an appointment for qualifying, 
however, if you would like to do so, please send your requested date 
and time to cityclerk@stonecrestga.gov.   
     For more information, go to: 
  https://stonecrestga.gov/Elections.aspx  
  or scan the QR code.
