Elections, Qualifying & Reports
The City Clerk coordinates all elections with DeKalb County Voter Registration and Elections and ensures that the City abides by the Georgia Election Code and Brookhaven City Charter.
General municipal elections are held on the Tuesday following the first Monday in November in each
odd-numbered year
(Georgia Election Code 21-2-9 (c). Special elections will be held as needed.
Per the City Charter, the Council has staggered four-year terms. The qualifying fees for Council districts are set at $936 and for Mayor at $1,404. The fees are 3% of the total gross salary of the preceding year (Georgia Election Code 21-2-131 (a). In accordance to the set time and dates, each candidate shall file a notice of candidacy in the office of the City Clerk of Brookhaven and must meet the qualifications of the Charter and Code of the City of Brookhaven, as well as all applicable state and constitutional laws.
The next City of Brookhaven election will be November 3, 2026. One City Council positions will be on the ballot: Council District 2.
PUBLIC NOTICE
CITY OF BROOKHAVEN
CALL FOR AND NOTICE OF SPECIAL ELECTION
TO THE QUALIFIED VOTERS OF THE CITY OF BROOKHAVEN, COUNTY OF DEKALB
Notice is hereby given that a Special Election for the City of Brookhaven will be held on Tuesday, November 3, 2026, in conjunction with the General Election for the purpose of filling the unexpired term of District 2 Brookhaven City Council member, Jennifer Owens.
The qualifying fee for the District 2 council seat is $936.00, which is 3% of the total gross salary of the preceding year (O.C.G.A. § 21-2-131(a)).  Each candidate shall file a notice of candidacy in the office of the City Clerk of Brookhaven, 4001 Peachtree Road, Georgia between Monday, August 17, 2026, and Wednesday, August 19, 2026, between the hours of 8:30 a.m. and 4:30 p.m. (O.C.G.A. § 21-2-132(c)(3)(A)).  Each candidate must meet the qualifications of the Charter and Code of the City of Brookhaven, as well as applicable State laws.
The last day a person may register and be eligible to vote in the Municipal Special Election and Runoff is Monday, October 5, 2026 (O.C.G.A. § 21-2-224(a)).  The polls will be open on Election Day, Tuesday, November 3, 2026, from 7:00 a.m. until 7:00 p.m.  The date of the Special Election Run-off, if necessary, is Tuesday, December 1, 2026.
