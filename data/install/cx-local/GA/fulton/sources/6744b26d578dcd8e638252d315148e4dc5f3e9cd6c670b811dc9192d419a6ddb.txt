Commitment to Voter Security
Fulton County is committed to ensuring every voter can vote safely, privately, and without interference. We will continue to follow all applicable federal and state laws and protect the integrity and security of the voting process. Voters can be confident that every eligible vote cast in Fulton County will be handled with care. For factual information about election-related topics, please visit
Election Facts
and/or email
elections.fulton@fultoncountyga.gov
with any questions or concerns.
×
Notice To Voters Listed as “Challenged”
If your voter registration status appears as
“Challenged”
on the My Voter Page (MVP) or at a polling location, you are still able to exercise your right to vote.  This status indicates that Fulton County needs to confirm your residential address to ensure you receive the correct precinct ballot, with the correct candidates representing that residential area. You are encouraged to contact the Fulton County Department of Registration & Election,
Elections.VoterRegistration@fultoncountyga.gov
before heading to the polls to resolve your status, but that is not required. Assistance is also available when you arrive at the polling location.  No voters are being removed from the voter roll as a result of this address review. If the issue cannot be resolved onsite, a provisional ballot will be offered in accordance with applicable procedures.
Your right to vote matters, and we are here to assist you.

MUNICIPAL ELECTIONS
The Department of Registration & Elections conducts elections for municipalities within Fulton County, pursuant to an intergovernmental agreement with the Municipality. However, Municipal Candidates and Municipal Elected Officials qualify and file campaign reports with their Municipal Clerk.
Please contact the municipality directly for candidate qualifying information.
WRITE-IN CANDIDATES
Write-in candidates must be qualified in accordance with the guidelines outlined in Election Code 21-2-133. Write-in candidates are not listed on the ballot but are voted for by voters who write in the candidate’s name on a ballot marking device (BMD) touchscreen or on a paper ballot.
A list of certified Write-In Candidates is posted at each Advance Voting and Election Day polling location for each election, allowing voters to review it. If applicable for the upcoming election, a list of qualified candidates will be posted on our
website along with the Sample Ballots
.
