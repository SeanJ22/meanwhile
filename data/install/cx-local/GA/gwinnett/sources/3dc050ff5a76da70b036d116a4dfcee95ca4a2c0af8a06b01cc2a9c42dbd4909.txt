City of Norcross Special Municipal Election Notice
Norcross voters are advised that a special election will be held on Tuesday, November 3, 2026, to fill the unexpired City Council term previously held by Marshall Cheek.
Qualified Candidates for 2026 Special Municipal Election
The City of Norcross has announced the qualified candidates for the 2026 Special Municipal Election.
The following candidates qualified during the official qualifying period held September 14–16, 2026:
Jeffrey Allen
Bucky Johnson
The election will be held Tuesday, November 3, 2026, to fill the remainder of the unexpired City Council term previously held by Marshall Cheek. ADVANCE VOTING will be held from Tuesday, October 13, 2026, through Friday, October 30, 2026, and Saturday, Oct. 17, 2026, and Oct. 24, 2026, from 9:00 a.m. – 5:00 p.m. at Norcross City Hall, 65 Lawrenceville Street Norcross, GA 30071.

Voter Registration Deadline
The voter registration deadline for this election will close on
Monday, October 5, 2026
. Only registered voters within the Norcross city limits are eligible to vote in this special election. All qualified residents of the City of Norcross, Georgia, are urged to participate. Those qualified to vote shall be determined in all respects in accordance with and in conformity with the laws of the State of Georgia. Information on voter registration may be obtained through the Chief Registrar of Gwinnett County, Georgia.
The election will be held on Tuesday, November 3, 2026, between 7:00 a.m. and 7:00 p.m. at Norcross City Hall, 65 Lawrenceville Street, 2nd Floor Community Room, Norcross, Georgia.
