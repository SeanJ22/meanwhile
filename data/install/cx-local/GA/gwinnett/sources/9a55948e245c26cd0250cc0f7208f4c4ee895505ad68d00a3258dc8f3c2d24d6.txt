Qualified Candidates for 2026 Special Municipal Election
The City of Norcross has announced the qualified candidates for the 2026 Special Municipal Election.
The following candidates qualified during the official qualifying period held September 14–16, 2026:
Jeffrey Allen
Bucky Johnson
The election will be held Tuesday, November 3, 2026, to fill the remainder of the unexpired City Council term previously held by Marshall Cheek. ADVANCE VOTING will be held from Tuesday, October 13, 2026, through Friday, October 30, 2026, and Saturday, Oct. 17, 2026, and Oct. 24, 2026, from 9:00 a.m. – 5:00 p.m. at Norcross City Hall, 65 Lawrenceville Street Norcross, GA 30071.
Candidate Requirements
There are several requirements for becoming a candidate in a city election. Once you have decided which seat you wish to seek election for, you may pick up a qualifying packet from the City Clerk's Office at City Hall or download it

Candidate Requirements
during the advertised qualifying dates,. The requirements include the following:
You must be eligible to vote in the election in which you are a candidate (must be a registered voter).
You must be at least 18 years of age.
You must have been a legal resident of Norcross for at least one year.
You must be a citizen of the United States.
You have never been convicted and sentenced in any court of competent jurisdiction for a fraudulent violation of primary or election laws, malfeasance in office, or felony involving moral turpitude or conviction of domestic violence in the laws of this state or any other state of the United States, or, if so convicted, that your civil rights have been restored, and at least ten years have elapsed from the date of the completion of the sentence.
You must not be a defaulter for any federal, state, county, municipal, or school system taxes.
You must pay the applicable filing fees at the time of qualifying.
