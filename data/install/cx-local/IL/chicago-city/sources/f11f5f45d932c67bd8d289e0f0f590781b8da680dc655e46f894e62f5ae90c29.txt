# Chicago School Board   
Voter Guide

For the first time, Chicagoans will elect the entire Board of Education — 20 members plus an at-large president. Find which district you vote in and compare candidates before Election day, Nov. 3, 2026.

## Board president

3 still running

Every Chicagoan can vote in this race. The president runs board meetings, helps set the agenda and represents the board publicly.
