October 28, 2026
With Election Day less than a week away, we strongly encourage DuPage citizens to consider voting at any of our
24 Early Voting locations
, or at any Polling Place within the county on
Election Day
.
Online voter registration for the upcoming election is now closed.
Voters may still register or update their existing registration in person at an early voting location or any Polling Place on Election Day (with 2 forms of ID, one with a home address)
The registration period for the online voter registration system will reopen 11/05/2026.
Upcoming Election(s) - Polling Places are open from 6 a.m. until 7 p.m. on Election Day.
