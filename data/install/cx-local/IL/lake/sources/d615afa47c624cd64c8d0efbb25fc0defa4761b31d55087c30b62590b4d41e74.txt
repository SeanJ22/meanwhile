November 03, 2026 GENERAL ELECTION ELECTION
REFERENDA as of August 27, 2026
LAKE COUNTY (Regional Board of Schools)
Proposition To Impose County School Sales Tax
Shall a retailers' occupation tax and a service occupation tax (commonly referred to as a sales tax) be imposed in The
County of Lake, Illinois, at a rate of 1% to be used exclusively for school facility purposes, school resource officers, and
mental health professionals?
VILLAGE OF LAKE ZURICH
Question To Issue $20,300,000 General Obligation Bonds
Shall bonds in the amount of $20,300,000 be issued by the Village of Lake Zurich, Lake County, Illinois, for the purpose of
building and equipping a new fire station and improving the site thereof to replace Station 1, bearing interest at the rate of
not to exceed 7.0%?
VILLAGE OF ROUND LAKE
Proposition To Determine Whether The Village Clerk Shall Be Appointed Rather Than Elected
Shall the Village Clerk in the Village of Round Lake, Lake County, Illinois be appointed by the Village President with the
advice and consent of the Village Board of Trustees, rather than elected, effective upon passage of this referendum?
VILLAGE OF WAUCONDA
Question Regarding Appointed Village Clerk
Shall the Village Clerk of the Village of Wauconda be appointed by the Village President, with the advice and consent of
the Board of Trustees, rather than be elected, effective after the current term of office of the Village Clerk expires?
CITY OF HIGHLAND PARK
Binding Public Question To Establish Term Limits For The Elected Offices Of Mayor And Councilmember
Shall the City of Highland Park, beginning with the terms of office that commence at the April 2027 consolidated election
and for each election thereafter, limit the terms of the office of Mayor and of the office of Councilmember so that no
person shall be eligible to hold that respective office if that person has been previously elected to no more than two full
four-year terms for that office, whether or not those terms were served consecutively, or if holding that office would cause
that person to serve in that office for more than a combined total of ten years? In accordance with Illinois law, terms of
office that commenced before the April 2027 consolidated election will not be considered in determining whether a person
has reached these limits.
ANTIOCH TOWNSHIP
Proposition To Increase The Limiting Rate
Shall the limiting rate under the Property Tax Extension Limitation Law for the Antioch Township be increased by an
additional amount equal to 0.049375% above the limiting rate for Township purposes for levy year 2025 and be equal to
0.160000% of the equalized assessed value of the taxable property therein for levy year 2026?
(1) The approximate amount of taxes extendable at the most recently extended limiting rate is $1,317,028, and the
approximate amount of taxes extendable if the proposition is approved is $1,894,595.
(2) For the 2026 levy year the approximate amount of the additional tax extendable against property containing a single-
family residence and having a fair market value at the time of the referendum of $100,000 is estimated to be $40.53.
(3) If the proposition is approved, the aggregate extension for levy year 2026 will be determined by the limiting rate set
forth in the proposition, rather than the otherwise applicable limiting rate calculated under the provisions of the Property
Tax Extension Limitation Law (commonly known as the Property Tax Cap Law).
VERNON AREA PUBLIC LIBRARY DISTRICT
Proposition To Issue $39,500,000 Library Bonds
Shall the Vernon Area Public Library District, Lake County, Illinois, repair, remodel, improve and build an addition to an
existing library building, including constructing a secure and accessible central entrance; constructing a community
makerspace; expanding spaces for library programs and community group use; improving spaces for early childhood and
teens; constructing individual study and collaborative workspaces and issue its bonds to the amount of $39,500,000 for
the purpose of paying the costs thereof?
FOX LAKE FIRE PROTECTION DISTRICT
Proposition To Create A New Tax Rate For Pension Fund Purposes
Shall the Fox Lake Fire Protection District, Lake and McHenry Counties, Illinois, be authorized to levy a new tax for
firefighters' pension purposes and have an additional tax of 0.06% of the equalized assessed value of the taxable property
therein extended for such purposes?
(1) The approximate amount of taxes extendable at the most recently extended limiting rate is $6,253,824, and the
approximate amount of taxes extendable if the proposition is approved is $7,036,134.
(2) For the 2026 levy year the approximate amount of the additional tax extendable against property containing a
single family residence and having a fair market value at the time of the referendum of $100,000 is estimated to be
$20.00.
