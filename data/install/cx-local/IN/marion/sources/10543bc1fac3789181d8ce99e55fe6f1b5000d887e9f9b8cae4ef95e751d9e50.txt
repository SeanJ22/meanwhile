Judge Mary A. DeBoer


Mary A. DeBoer
 grew up in Kalamazoo, Michigan and relocated permanently to Valparaiso, Indiana in 1990. She graduated with honors from Western Michigan University in 1989 and earned her J.D. from Valparaiso University School of Law in 1993. Judge DeBoer served as a deputy prosecutor and as a magistrate for Starke and Porter Counties. She was appointed as the Porter Circuit Court Judge in 2019 by Governor Eric J. Holcomb, who later appointed her to serve on the Court of Appeals of Indiana in 2024.

Hon. Paul A Felix


Paul A. Felix was appointed to the Court of Appeals by Governor Holcomb and began his service on July 28, 2023.


Judge Felix was born in Elkhart. He was raised in Greenwood and attended Whiteland schools. He earned his undergraduate and law degrees from Indiana University - Bloomington.


Following graduation, Judge Felix served as a Deputy Prosecuting Attorney in the Johnson County Prosecutor’s Office from 1995 to 2006. During his prosecuting career, he created the county’s first Domestic Violence Unit and led the prosecution of DV cases for three years.

Dana J. Kenworthy, a lifelong Hoosier raised in small town Amboy, has devoted her entire career to public service. Governor Eric Holcomb appointed her to the Court of Appeals in December 2022, resulting in the first female majority in the Court’s history.

Hon. Justin L. McAdam


Justin L. McAdam is the third judge to serve on the Indiana Tax Court since its creation in 1986, following Judge Thomas G. Fisher and Judge Martha Blood Wentworth. He was appointed by Governor Eric J. Holcomb and took the bench on September 1, 2023.


Judge McAdam was born in San Diego, California, and lived in Florida and Colorado before his family eventually settled in Terre Haute, Indiana, in 1992. After graduating from Terre Haute North Vigo High School, he began his college career studying mathematics at Purdue University. He later transferred to Indiana University, where he earned Bachelor of Arts degrees in economics and political science in 2005, graduating Phi Beta Kappa with highest distinction.
