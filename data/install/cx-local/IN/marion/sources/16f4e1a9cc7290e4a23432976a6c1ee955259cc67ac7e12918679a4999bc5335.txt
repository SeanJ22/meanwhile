			Indianapolis Public Schools Board Outlines Process to Fill District 4 Vacancy Seat



	



		

					

						
Jun
 
17
 
2026

					

		






		

			
Following the resignation of District 4 Commissioner Allissa Impink, the Indianapolis Public Schools (IPS) Board of School Commissioners has announced the process for filling the vacancy.



By law, the Board will appoint a representative to serve until the next regular election on November 3, 2026. State law requires specific eligibility criteria and fulfillment of the vacancy within 30 days.  



Candidate Eligibility




	
Residency: Candidates must currently live in District 4 and have resided within the IPS district for at least one year preceding the appointment.

	
Legal Status: Candidates must not have any felony convictions (unless pardoned, reversed, vacated, or set aside).





Selection Timeline & Requirements




	
June 16 – June 23: Applications and resumes accepted via the Board’s online application. The deadline is Tuesday, June 23, at 5 p.m.

	
June 23: The Board will hold an executive session to select three finalists.

	
June 24: Finalists will participate in public interviews in the IPS Boardroom at 6 p.m. Applicants must ensure they are available at this time before applying for this position.

	
June 25: The Board will vote to appoint the new commissioner during its regular Action Session at 6 p.m. in the IPS Boardroom. A minimum of four votes is required for appointment. Finalists must attend the Action Session, as the District 4 appointee will take the Oath of Office at the conclusion of the meeting.

	
The selected appointee must be available to participate in comprehensive Board member onboarding sessions starting the week of June 29th.





The eligibility requirements and application process for filling the District 4 vacancy are available 
HERE
 on the Board page.



For questions regarding eligibility or the application process, please contact Board Administrator Leslie-Ann James at boardofcommissioners@myips.org or 317.226.2056.
