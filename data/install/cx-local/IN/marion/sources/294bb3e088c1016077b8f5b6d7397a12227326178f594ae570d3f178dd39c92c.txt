What is On Your Ballot This Election
				

				

					
See What Contests Exist in Marion County
				

				

									
The precinct in which you are registered to vote determines what contests, candidates, and public questions appear on your ballot each election cycle.
Using the Sample Ballot Tool below, enter your Marion County, Indiana address to see what will appear on your ballot. You can use the populated filter to search for specific offices, or generate a downloadable PDF of the ballot specific to your precinct.

Get Ready for the Election
				

				

					
Know What Will Be on Your Ballot
				

				

									
Make a plan prior to Election Day, to know what will appear on the ballot for your specific precinct. Using the tool above to identify your voting districts, candidates for office, and public questions appearing on your ballot for the 2026 General Election.
								

		

		

				

															
															

				

					
BALLOT
				

				

		

				

									
Your sample ballot shows the offices, candidates and public questions you may vote on in the next election. Preview your ballot as part of your voting plan this election. 
								

				

				

		

		

				

															
															

				

					
DISTRICTS
				

				

		

				

									
Your Marion County voter registration address determines which local, state and federal districts you vote on. Because district boundaries can be determined by township lines, city borders, or even neighborhood streets, your district may not be the same as a voter around the corner. To find a collection of maps for all voting districts in the county, 
click here.

Using the 
Office Holder Tool
, you can review a list of your current representatives at all levels of government.  The Office Holder Tool lists office and contact information, where available for each elected official.
