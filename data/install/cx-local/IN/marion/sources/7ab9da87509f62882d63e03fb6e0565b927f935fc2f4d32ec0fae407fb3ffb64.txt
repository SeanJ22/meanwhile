PUBLIC QUESTION #1
Currently, under the Constitution of
the State of Indiana, a person
charged with murder or treason is
not entitled to be released on bail if
the proof is evident or the
presumption strong. Shall the
Constitution of the State of Indiana
be amended to provide that a
person charged with an offense
other than murder or treason is not
entitled to bail if: (1) the proof is
evident or the presumption strong;
and (2) the state proves by clear and
convincing evidence that no release
conditions will reasonably protect
the safety of any other person or the
community. (This question concerns
Article 1, Section 17 of the
Constitution of the State of Indiana.)
