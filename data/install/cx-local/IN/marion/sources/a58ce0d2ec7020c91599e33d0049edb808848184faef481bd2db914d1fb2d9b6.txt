BOARD MEMBER DISTRICT BOUNDARIES
 
The Metropolitan School District of Lawrence Township, Marion County, Indiana, is divided into three (3) Board Member districts. Descriptions of such districts were certified by the Indiana State Board of Education on December 5, 2001
