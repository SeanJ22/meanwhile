Offices on the Ballot in 2026 
 
Below is a comprehensive list of offices that will appear on Marion County ballots in 2026. Not all 
contests listed below will appear on every ballot in Marion County.  Offices are specific to an individual 
voter’s precinct, and based on the physical address of each registered voter. Voters may visit 
Indianavoters.IN.gov, log into your voter portal, and access the election districts specific to your precinct.
