2026 Judicial Retention

                
                                                    

                                                    

                                                                    

                                                                                                     
Courts

                                                            

                                                    

                                                                    

                                                                                                    
Judicial Selection

                                                            

                                                    

                                                                    

                                                                                                    
Marion County Judicial Selection Committee

                                                            

                                                    

                                                                    

                                                                                                     
2026-retention

                                                            

                                                                                            

                            

        

    

                                    
                
                                    
                                                                                                                                                
                                                                                                    
                    

                        


                                                                              
                                                                                                        
                            

                                                                

    

In 2026, the Marion County Judicial Selection Committee (MCJSC) will fulfill its statutory duty to make public recommendations to voters concerning the retention of Marion Superior Court judges. To stand for retention (approval or rejection by Marion County voters) in the 2026 General Election, incumbent judges in 
retention class B 
were asked to submit an application to the committee and invited to take part in public interviews. The committee will make recommendations after the interviews. See IC 33-33-49-13.7 for more information.
On March 4, Governor Mike Braun signed 
HEA 1033
, which created IC 33-33-49-13.8 and modified the MCJSC’s duties regarding retention. The committee will now interview each judge wishing to stand for retention and vote upon the judge’s suitability to serve another term. Each judge found suitable to remain in office by the committee will appear on the retention ballot in 2026 to be approved or rejected by Marion County voters. Judges who are not found suitable by the committee will not be retained beyond the judge’s current term.
