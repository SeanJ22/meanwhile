: BBBA - DISTRICT SCHOOL COMMITTEE MEMBER QUALIFICATIONS
In order to serve on the District School Committee, an individual must be a resident of one of the District´s member towns and a registered voter from the town in which he resides and must take an oath of office as required by law.
Any person who is a resident of a member town and is a registered voter in the town in which he resides shall be entitled to have his name printed on the ballot to be used in the district-wide election if he shall file with the district clerk nomination papers signed by the number of voters as provided in section six who are registered and qualified to vote in the regional school district in which he resides.
Committee members shall be elected by way of district-wide, at large elections, with a residency requirements which will result in one resident of each member town having a position on the Committee.  That is, the candidate who receives the highest number of district-wide, at-large votes when compared to all other candidates who reside in the same member town shall be seated on the Committee.
Each new member will present to the District Committee secretary official certification of having sworn the oath before an officer duly qualified to administer oaths prior to entering on his official duties as a member of the Committee.  From the Town Clerk, newly qualified Committee members, by law, receive, and sign a receipt for, a copy of the Massachusetts open meeting law governing the conduct of Committee meetings in general and executive sessions in particular.
Membership on the District School Committee is not limited race, color, age, gender, sexual orientation, disability, religious or political conviction. 
The District School Committee shall serve without compensation, except that  compensation may be authorized by majority vote of the voting member towns as authorized at their respective town meetings.
No member of the District School Committee shall be eligible to be employed in the position of teacher, administrator or superintendent of the District.
