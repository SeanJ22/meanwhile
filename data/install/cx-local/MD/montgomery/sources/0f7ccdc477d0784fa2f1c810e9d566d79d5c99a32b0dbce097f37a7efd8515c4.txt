Voting Process
The city election is conducted with mail-in and in-person voting options. Only voters registered with the City of Takoma Park may vote in the city election.
Review the information below to understand how to get a ballot in the mail, to fill out a ballot, and return a completed ballot to ensure that your vote is counted in the city election. Ballots must be returned in-person or postmarked by Election Day to be counted.

Election Day for the City Election 2026 is:
Tuesday, November 3, 2026
. The complete election calendar and filing deadlines for candidates is available on the election webpage.
Official ballots for the city election are mailed via the U.S. Postal Service to all voters registered with the City of Takoma Park. Ballots will be mailed by
October 19, 2026.
The recommended date to mail completed ballots:
Friday, October 30, 2026.

Returning Ballots
By Mail
Completed ballots can be returned by mail to the City Clerk using the U.S. Postal Service (USPS). A return envelope is provided with the ballot that was mailed to you. You do not need to add a stamp to this envelope.
To ensure that your ballot is postmarked by the USPS by Election Day, which is essential for your vote to be counted, we strongly reccommended that you mail your ballot by: Friday, October 30, 2026. Your vote will be counted if your ballot envelop is postmarked by Election Day: Tuesday, November 3, 2026.
The USPS recently made changes to when and where postmark date stamps are made on pieces of mail in the handling processes, and this can vary by location. Do not assume that your mail will be postmarked the same day that you place it in your mailbox for outgoing mail, or hand it to a postal carrier, or drop it off at the post office box. This is why we recommend mailing your ballot several days in advance. You can request that your mail is postmark with today’s date at a USPS Post Office during normal business hours when speaking to a clerk.
In-Person
You can return your completed ballot to the City Clerk on Election Day at the Takoma Park Community Center (7500 Maple Avenue, Takoma Park, MD 20912). The office will be open from
7:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m.
to return your ballots or to vote in-person for the city election.
A ballot drop box for Takoma Park election ballots is located outside of Takoma Park library's main entrance (101 Philadelphia Ave.) and at the Takoma Park Recreation Center (7315 New Hampshire Ave.) The ballot drop boxes are open 24 hour a day and will close at 8:00 PM on November 3, 2026.
For the voter's convenience, available parking at the Community Center will be signed as “30-Minute Voter Parking.”
Processing Ballots
The 2026 City of Takoma Park mail-in ballot process begins with two workers collecting ballots from the post office box and drop boxes each weekday. Ballots are logged, scanned as received, and grouped into batches of 50 or fewer. Two-person teams verify voter information, confirm wards, and separate ballots from their envelopes. Accepted ballots are de-identified, sealed in batches, and scanned, but votes are not tabulated until polls close.
Ballots with problems, unreadable marks, or unclear voter intent are flagged. The City Clerk contacts voters to correct eligible issues by 5 p.m. on Monday, November 9. The Board of Elections reviews unresolved or unreadable ballots on Tuesday, November 10, and election certification is scheduled for Thursday, November 12, at a special City Council meeting. All ballot storage boxes remain sealed between work sessions, with seal numbers and box movements documented.

Election Results
The City of Takoma Park issues vote totals promptly after counting all ballots received by Election Day. The official vote totals are posted on the election webpage: City Election 2026.
However, final vote totals may change as more ballots that were postmarked by Election Day are received by the City Clerk in the days following the election. Final election results and vote totals are typically complete about one week after Election Day.
See specific election webpages for the official candidate and vote totals.
