City Election 2026
The upcoming City of Takoma Park election for Mayor and City Councilmembers will take place on Tuesday, November 3, 2026. The city election is conducted with mail-in and in-person voting options. View the Voting Process webpage to learn more about how to get a ballot in the mail, fill out a ballot, and return a complete ballot to ensure that your vote is counted in the city election.
Track your Ballot
Takoma Park election ballots have been mailed to all registered voters. To track where your ballot is in the mailing process, please visit the
