About the Takoma Park City Council
The City Council is the elected governing body of the City. Six members are elected by ward and are called Councilmembers, and one is elected at large and is called the Mayor.
The regular term of the Councilmembers and the Mayor is two years or until their successors have been elected and duly sworn. The current City Council was elected on November 5, 2024.
