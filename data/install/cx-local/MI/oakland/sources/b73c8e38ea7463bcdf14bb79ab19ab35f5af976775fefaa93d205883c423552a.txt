45 E. Long Lake Road

### Absentee Voting

Request an absentee ballot to be mailed to you by Saturday, October 31, 2026  
Come in person to City Hall to request and obtain an absentee ballot by 4:00 p.m. Monday, November 2, 2026.  
Complete the [Application for Absentee Ballot (PDF)](https://www.bloomfieldhills.gov/DocumentCenter/View/1380) and [email it to the City Clerk](mailto:cityclerk@bloomfieldhills.gov).

**Drop Box for Returning Absentee Ballots**  
There is a secure, 24/7 monitored dropbox at City Hall in front of the Public Safety Department, for voters to return a voted absentee ballot to be counted on Election Day.

### Important Deadlines for Absentee Voting

**Absentee Ballot Request Deadlines**

*   The deadline to request that an absentee ballot be mailed to you is **5:00 p.m. on October 30, 2026.**
*   The deadline to request and receive an absentee ballot in person at the Clerk’s office is **4:00 p.m. on November 2, 2026.**

**Absentee Ballot Return Deadline**

*   Absentee ballots must be received **by 8:00 p.m. on Election Day,** whether returned by mail or placed in a drop box.

**Spoiling or Canceling a Returned Absentee Ballot**

*   If you have already returned your absentee ballot but wish to change your vote, you must request to spoil (cancel) your ballot and receive a new one by **5:00 p.m. on October 23, 2026.**

After the deadline, you will not be able to cancel your absentee ballot or vote in person.

**Signature Cure Deadlines**

*   If your signature is missing or does not match on your absentee voter ballot application, you must cure it by **4:00 p.m. on October 30, 2026.**
*   If your signature is missing or does not match on your absentee ballot, you must complete and return the Absent Voter Ballot Signature Cure Form to the Clerk’s office by **5:00 p.m. on November 6, 2026.** The form may be submitted by email, mail, or in-person.

For more information about absentee voter ballots, please visit the [Michigan Voter Information Center webpage](https://mvic.sos.state.mi.us/).

### Voter Eligibility

To register to vote, you must be all the following:

*   A U.S. citizen
*   At least 18 years old by Election Day
*   A resident of Michigan

A resident of the City of Bloomfield Hills for at least 30 days before Election Day

### Voter Identification

A voter must show valid photo identification or sign an affidavit swearing that they are not in possession of photo identification. Acceptable photo identification are:

*   Current or expired Michigan driver’s license.
*   Current or expired official Michigan personal identification card or enhanced official Michigan personal identification.
*   A current drivers license issued by another state.
*   A current personal identification card issued by another state.
*   A current state government issued photo identification card.
*   A current US passport or federal government-issued photo identification card.
*   A current military photo identification card.
*   A current tribal photo identification card.
*   A current photo identification card issued by a local government – including a Concealed Pistol License.
*   A current student photo identification card issued by an educational institution (previously must be accredited educational institution).
