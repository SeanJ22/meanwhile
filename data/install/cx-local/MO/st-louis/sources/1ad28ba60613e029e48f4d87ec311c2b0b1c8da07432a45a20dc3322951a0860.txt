to:

petition process by making it a fundamental
right;

initiative or referendum powers;

repealing laws enacted through the initiative
process, or passing laws similar to those
rejected by referendum, without approval
from at least 80% of both chambers; and
* preserve existing majority vote and
signature requirements for initiative and
referendum petitions?

estimate no costs or savings.
