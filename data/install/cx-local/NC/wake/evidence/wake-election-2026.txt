                  <h2><strong>
            Early Voting Has Ended
      </strong></h2>
        
        
            <p><strong>Election Day is Tuesday, November 3, 2026. Polling places will be open from 6:30 a.m. to 7:30 p.m. Registered voters must vote at their assigned polling place on Election Day.</strong></p>
      
                  <a class="
            cta
      " href="/departments-government/board-elections/election-information/election-day-voting">Find Your Election Day Polling Place</a>
              </div>
    </div>
      </div>

            <div class="paragraph paragraph--type--text paragraph--view-mode--default">
                  
            <h4 class="text-align-center"><a href="https://s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/wakegov.com.if-us-west-1/s3fs-public/documents/2026-10/2026EVunofficial_18.pdf" data-entity-type="media" data-entity-uuid="00f37fb0-db4b-4efa-9dfc-dae0d50e6774" data-entity-substitution="media"><strong>Early Voting Unofficial Turnout</strong></a></h4>
      
          </div>

          


        <div class="paragraph paragraph--type--image paragraph--view-mode--default clearfix">
                    <figure class="img-wrap center full">
                      <img src="https://s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/wakegov.com.if-us-west-1/s3fs-public/styles/max_1300x1300/public/images/2026-06/g26evwebsitemap.jpg?itok=qaSltEa-" alt="Map listing the 18 Early Voting Sites for the 2026 General Election by the Wake County Board of Elections" />
                              </figure>

            Early Voting FAQs
      </h2>
        <div class="accordion-container">
        
            
      <div class="accordion-item">
      <h3 class="title dark-blue">
            How do I find information regarding the photo ID requirement for voting?
       <i class="fas fa-sort-down"></i><i class="fas fa-sort-up"></i></h3>
        <div class="body">
          
              <div class="paragraph paragraph--type--text paragraph--view-mode--default">
                  
            <p>Voters will now be asked to present a valid photo identification when voting in person. If you do not have a valid photo ID card, you may obtain one from your county board of elections prior to the election, through the end of the early voting period.</p>

<p>If you do not have a valid photo ID card on Election Day, you may still vote and have your vote counted by signing an affidavit of reasonable impediment (or "Photo ID Exception Form") as to why you have not presented a valid photo ID. The Exception Form can also be used if you have a religious objection to being photographed or are a victim of a recently declared natural disaster.</p>

<p>As an alternative, if you don't have your ID when you vote, you can still vote and then bring your valid photo ID to your county board of elections by noon on the third business day after Election Day.</p>

<p>If you vote by mail, then you must include a photocopy of a valid photo ID when returning your ballot. You may also complete the Absentee Photo ID Exception Form that is provided with your absentee ballot materials.</p>

<p>A list of all types of ID that can be used for voting is available on the&nbsp;<a href="https://www.ncsbe.gov/voting/voter-id">State Board of Elections Voter ID</a>&nbsp;webpage. For information on how to get a free ID, see&nbsp;<a href="https://www.ncsbe.gov/voting/voter-id/get-free-voter-photo-id">Get a Free Voter Photo ID</a>.</p>
      
          </div>

      
        </div>
    </div>
  
          
      <div class="accordion-item">
      <h3 class="title dark-blue">
            I missed the voter registration deadline. May I register and vote at an Early Voting location?
       <i class="fas fa-sort-down"></i><i class="fas fa-sort-up"></i></h3>
        <div class="body">
          
              <div class="paragraph paragraph--type--text paragraph--view-mode--default">
                  
            <p><span>Yes.&nbsp;Eligible individuals may register and vote during the Early Voting period. Please note that same-day registration is not available during second primaries.</span></p><p><a href="https://s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/wakegov.com.if-us-west-1/s3fs-public/documents/2026-09/2026_NOTICE TO SAME DAY REGISTRANTS-General.pdf" data-entity-type="media" data-entity-uuid="20041d29-16af-4a5a-b9e9-c24fa352c38b" data-entity-substitution="media">Learn about same-day registration requirements for the 2026 November General Election.</a></p>
      
          </div>

      
        </div>
    </div>
  
          
      <div class="accordion-item">
      <h3 class="title dark-blue">
            Who may vote at Early Voting locations? 
       <i class="fas fa-sort-down"></i><i class="fas fa-sort-up"></i></h3>
        <div class="body">
          
              <div class="paragraph paragraph--type--text paragraph--view-mode--default">
                  
            <p>Any eligible Wake County voter may vote at any Wake County Early Voting location.</p>
      
          </div>

      
        </div>
    </div>
  
          
      <div class="accordion-item">
      <h3 class="title dark-blue">
            May I update my name or address at an Early Voting location? 
       <i class="fas fa-sort-down"></i><i class="fas fa-sort-up"></i></h3>
        <div class="body">
          
              <div class="paragraph paragraph--type--text paragraph--view-mode--default">
                  
            <p>Yes. You may update your name and address before voting.</p>

<p>If you moved 30 or more days before Election Day, you will receive the ballot style of your new address.<br>
If you moved fewer than 30 days before Election Day, you will receive the ballot style of your old address.</p>
      
          </div>

      
        </div>
    </div>
  
          
      <div class="accordion-item">
      <h3 class="title dark-blue">
            I am a college student. What are my options for registering and voting?
       <i class="fas fa-sort-down"></i><i class="fas fa-sort-up"></i></h3>
        <div class="body">
          
              <div class="paragraph paragraph--type--text paragraph--view-mode--default">
                  
            <p>College students may register to vote in the county/state of their home address or in the county/state where they are attending college. Learn about Election Day, absentee by mail, and Early Voting options for college students&nbsp;<a data-entity-substitution="canonical" data-entity-type="node" data-entity-uuid="0d89c5c2-3b80-43e5-8fb5-27fdc8f32f12" href="/departments-government/board-elections/voter-information/resources-teens-and-youth/information-college-students">here</a>.&nbsp;</p>
      
          </div>

      
        </div>
    </div>
  
          
      <div class="accordion-item">
      <h3 class="title dark-blue">
            What are the registration and voting requirements for persons in the criminal justice system?
       <i class="fas fa-sort-down"></i><i class="fas fa-sort-up"></i></h3>
        <div class="body">
          
              <div class="paragraph paragraph--type--text paragraph--view-mode--default">
                  
            <p>Eligible individuals may register and vote during the Early Voting period.&nbsp;</p>

<p>If you have been convicted of a misdemeanor you do not lose your right to vote.</p>

<p>To register to vote, you must not be serving a felony sentence, including any period of probation, post-release supervision, or parole.</p>

<p><a href="https://www.ncsbe.gov/registering/who-can-register/registering-person-nc-criminal-justice-system">View information on registering as a person in the NC Criminal Justice System</a></p>
      
          </div>

      
        </div>

            May I change my party affiliation at an Early Voting location? 
       <i class="fas fa-sort-down"></i><i class="fas fa-sort-up"></i></h3>
        <div class="body">

Look for the parking spaces near the site entrance labeled "Curbside Voting." Early Voting officials monitor the parking area throughout the day and will walk to your car when you park in one of the designated spaces. Click the <strong><em>Site Details&nbsp;</em></strong>links above to view the designated curbside voting area at each site.</p>
