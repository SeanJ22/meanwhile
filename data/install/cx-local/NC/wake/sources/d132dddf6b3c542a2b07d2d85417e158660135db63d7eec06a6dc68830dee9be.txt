programs to provide and rehabilitate

additional taxes may be levied in an
amount sufficient to pay the principal of
and interest on the bonds, be approved,
in light of the following:
     (1) The estimated cumulative cost
over the life of said bonds, using the

bonds, plus $66,385,618 of estimated
interest.

tax liability increase for each one
