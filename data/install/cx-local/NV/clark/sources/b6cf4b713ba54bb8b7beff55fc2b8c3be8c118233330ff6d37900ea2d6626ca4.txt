General Information
The Eighth Judicial District Court of Clark County encompasses all municipalities and outlying communities of Clark County. The court serves more than 2.2 million citizens as the trial court for Nevada and is part of the judicial branch of state government as set forth by the Nevada Constitution. The 58 elected judges preside over more than 90,000 criminal and civil cases filed each year in District Court.
Civil, Criminal, Probate and Family courts are in session Monday through Friday 8 a.m. to 5 p.m., exclusive of legal holidays.
