Definition of Terms 
Inspection 
Examine. 
Manual Recount 
A tabulation and totaling of cast ballot numbers by affected contest done 
by certified election officials 
Canvass 
Bi-partisan ballot envelope review and sign-off 
Audit 
A second tabulation of results through a different election device. 
Review 
Inspection. 
Recanvass 
A re-collection of results comparing those results to the initial collection 
of results. 
Scanning 
Opening and tabulation of canvassed (reviewed) absentee ballots 
Cure 
The option provided to correct an election related document. 
Sample Ballots 
Non-official images of the ballot face. 
Affidavit Ballots 
Provisional ballots subject to review.
