<h2><a class="item" href="/index.asp?SEC=33BD7D65-42BC-45CB-ABAB-A6E2B4FB1BFB&amp;DE=46BA0A04-5AB6-44CD-AEE7-C133F66C5956">Fall Hydrant Flushing</a>
</h2>
<div class="newsdatetime">
<!----></div>
<div class="body"><div class="jodit-content"><p>The&nbsp;Long Beach&nbsp;Water Transmission Department will conduct Fall hydrant flushing beginning&nbsp;Sunday, November 1 through November 15&nbsp;between the hours of 9pm and 5am.</p>
<p>This semi-annual flushing is done to remove sediments from the system and helps to ensure proper water pressure. During this time, you may experience some low pressure or water discoloration.&nbsp;The process will begin on Riverside Boulevard and Park Avenue and is expected to proceed both east and west, one to two blocks each night. We will be updating the approximate location of flushing daily.</p>
<p>For more information, please <a href="https://longbeachny.gov/hydrantflushing">click here</a>.</p></div></div>

<h2><a class="item" href="/index.asp?SEC=33BD7D65-42BC-45CB-ABAB-A6E2B4FB1BFB&amp;DE=9377759F-BAEB-4BD0-86F7-7789A3E88FCB">Drug Take Back Day</a>
</h2>
<div class="newsdatetime">
<!----></div>
<div class="body"><div class="jodit-content"><p>The City of Long Beach Police Department and Long Beach Aware are once again partnering with the DEA to hold A Prescription Drug Take Back Day on Saturday, October 24, 2026. The event will take place from 10:00am to 12:00pm at Long Beach City Hall (1 West Chester Street, Long Beach, NY).</p>
<p>DEA and its partners will collect tablets, capsules, patches, and other solid forms of prescription drugs. Liquids (including intravenous solutions), syringes, other sharps and illegal drugs will not be accepted. The DEA will accept vaping devices and cartridges at its drop off locations provided lithium batteries are removed.</p></div></div>

<h2><a class="item" href="/index.asp?SEC=33BD7D65-42BC-45CB-ABAB-A6E2B4FB1BFB&amp;DE=9EF238B8-D8A9-46BD-A2AA-27437895E1CF">&#x1F342; Important Update: City of Long Beach Fall Festival &#x1F342;</a>
</h2>
<div class="newsdatetime">
<!----></div>
<div class="body"><div class="jodit-content"><p>Due to the weather forecast, we regret to inform you that today, Sunday, October 11, the City of Long Beach Fall Festival will feature limited activities. All rides and vendors have been canceled for your safety. However, you can still enjoy some autumn fun! Pumpkins will be on sale behind City Hall starting at 10 AM&#8212;perfect for decorating or carving at home.</p><p>The Beer, Wine &amp; Food Experience will be OPEN from 12 PM to 5 PM in the tent behind City Hall. Indulge in wine tastings and wines by the glass offered by Beach Wine &amp; More, along with craft beers from Bright Eye Beer Co. and Barrier Brewing Co. Don&#8217;t miss live music by Wrong Sister at 1 PM to keep the festive spirit alive!</p><p>Additionally, free hay is available for pick up in the lot behind City Hall and on Park Avenue. We appreciate your understanding and hope you still enjoy the cozy autumn vibes. Thank you for your support! &#x1F341;</p></div></div>

<h2><a class="item" href="/index.asp?SEC=33BD7D65-42BC-45CB-ABAB-A6E2B4FB1BFB&amp;DE=5976EC03-957E-401E-8A7D-912263DCBFC4">Halloween Costume Parade</a>
</h2>
<div class="newsdatetime">
<!----></div>
<div class="body"><div class="jodit-content"><div class="xdj266r x11i5rnm xat24cr x1mh8g0r x1vvkbs x126k92a">


<div dir="auto"><div><div>The City of Long Beach Department of Parks and Recreation will hold its annual Halloween Costume Parade in conjunction with the Fall Festival on Saturday, Ocotber 10, 2026.</div>

<div>This popular long-standing tradition will begin at 12:00 pm from Veteran's Park located at the Recreation Center Campus on Magnolia Blvd and the bay. The Costume Parade is sponsored by the Long Beach CSEA, the Commanding Officers Association, the Long Beach PBA, the Community Police Relations Foundation and the Long Beach Professional Firefighters.</div><p>Children in grades 5 and<span>&nbsp;</span>under are invited to participate by wearing their favorite costumes. All children in costume at the Halloween Parade will receive a trick-or-treat bag filled with goodies.</p>
