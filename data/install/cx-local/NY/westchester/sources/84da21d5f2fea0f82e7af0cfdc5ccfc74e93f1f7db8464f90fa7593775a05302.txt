has relocated to:

2026 Elections
General Election – Tuesday, November 3, 2026
Polls open 6:00 a.m. – 9:00 p.m.
Early Voting: Saturday, October 24th – Sunday, November 1st
Our office will be open for Vote By Mail Department Extended Hours
Tuesday, October 20, 2026 until 8:00 PM
Thursday, October 22, 2026 until 8:00 PM
Saturday, October 24, 2026 from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM
Tuesday, October 27, 2026 until 8:00 PM
Thursday, October 29, 2026 until 8:00 PM
Saturday, October 31, 2026 from 9:00 AM until 6:00 PM
This is for the purpose of obtaining an absentee/early vote by mail ballot/application and dropping off completed absentee/early vote by mail ballots.
Last Day to Register to Vote for General Election is
Saturday, October 24, 2026
.
Mail Registration:
Applications must be received by the Westchester County Board of Elections no later than
October 24, 2026
to be eligible to vote in the General Election.
In Person Registration:
You may register at the Westchester County Board of Elections, any Early Voting sites by completing an Affidavit Ballot form, and any state agency participating in the National Voter Registration Act, but, to be eligible to vote in the General Election, your application must be received no later than
October 24, 2026
.

We are committed to keeping you aware of changes to the election law and how those changes affect voting in Westchester. Our office is here to assist you in any way we can.
Westchester County works hard to encourage and protect your right to vote as a United States citizen. From the county’s Board of Elections, you can
find your ballot and polling place
,
register to vote
and learn about
how to request an election ballot
.
You can learn how to
run for office
or obtain county, state and federal
financial disclosure statements
.
For additional voting information, visit
Vote411
, an interactive Web site created and maintained by the League of Women Voters. The site supplies personalized ballots and polling locations simply by entering your home address.
