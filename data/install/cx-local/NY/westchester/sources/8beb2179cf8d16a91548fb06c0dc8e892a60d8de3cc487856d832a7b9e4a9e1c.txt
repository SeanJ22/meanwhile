Voting by Early Mail or Absentee Ballot:
*Application request must be received by Board of Elections by October 24, 2026.
Voting by Early Mail or Absentee Ballot is a two-step process.
You may apply for an Absentee Ballot in any one of the following ways:
Request your Early Mail or Absentee
Ballot online
or download the paper application in English or Spanish with the New York State
Request a Ballot Portal
.
Or you can apply for a Ballot:
By sending an
email request to the Westchester Board of Elections
By sending a fax request to 914-995-7753 or 914-995-3190
By mailing a paper application to the Westchester County Board of Elections.
By going in person to the Westchester County Board of Elections.
The Westchester County Board of Elections has relocated to:
445 Hamilton Avenue, 8th Floor, White Plains, NY 10601.
Complete and return the Voting Ballot to the Westchester County Board of Elections:
Mail or Deliver in person to 445 Hamilton Avenue, 8th Floor, White Plains, NY 10601.
Dates and further mailing instructions are detailed on the New York State
Request a Ballot Portal
.
