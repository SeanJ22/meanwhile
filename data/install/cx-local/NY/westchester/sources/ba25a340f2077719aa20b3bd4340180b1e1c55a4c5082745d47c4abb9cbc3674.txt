                    The Bylaws of the Board of Trustees of the Mount Kisco Public Library
                                     Approved & Adopted December 17, 2019



Mission Statement
The Mount Kisco Public Library exists to promote a love of reading and a sense of wonder and discovery
among our patrons.

Preamble
The Mount Kisco Public Library Trustees (hereinafter designated as the “Board”) a corporation created under a
charter granted under Section 253 of the New York State Education Law by the Board of Regents (or Secretary
of State) of the State of New York, dated March 10, 1910, will be governed by the laws of New York State, the
regulations of the Commissioner of Education and by the following Bylaws.

Bylaws
   1. NAME OF ORGANIZATION
      a. The name of the organization is The Mount Kisco Public Library Trustees.
   2. PURPOSE
      The purpose of the organization is to provide superior library service to the residents of Mount Kisco,
      New York.
   3. FISCAL YEAR
      The fiscal year of the Library is June 1 through May 31 to coincide with the Mount Kisco Village fiscal
      year.
   4. BOARD OF TRUSTEES
      The Library is governed by a Board of Trustees. The Board consists of a minimum of (5) members and a
      maximum of (7) members elected at general town elections for terms of (3) years. In the case of the
      resignation, death or removal of a trustee who has six months or more left on his/her term, an Interim
      Trustee will be appointed by a majority of the Board. Interim Trustee(s) will serve until the next
      general election date at which a new trustee will be elected to a full three-year term. Newly elected
      members will take office after being sworn in by the Mayor. In governing the Library, which is a
      municipal library chartered by the New York State Board of Regents and primarily funded by the
      Village of Mount Kisco, the Board and Library Director will communicate, coordinate, and work closely
      with the Village Board and management to ensure effective management of the Library, the Library
      building, and the Library finances.
      a. Eligibility for office is limited to adults residing or owning property within the geographical limits of
           the Library district.
      b. Absence from (3) consecutive meetings could result in dismissal, at the discretion of the Board.
           The Board may also remove a Trustee for misconduct, incapacity, or neglect of duty by a majority
           vote.
      c. Any vacancies will be filled at the next general election, or, when there are vacancies lasting for at
           least six months until the end of the term, by appointment of a majority vote of the remaining
           members of the Board.

   d. Each Trustee has one vote, irrespective of office held.
   e. A Trustee must be present at a meeting to have his/her vote counted. Meetings via conference call
       are permitted, including voting. Voting via electronic means or other Board-approved means
       between meetings is permitted for matters not pertaining to taxpayer funds.
   f. All actions of the Board are of the Board as a unit. No Board member will act on behalf of the
       Board, on any matter, without prior approval of the Board. No Board member will exercise any
       administrative authority with respect to the Library, nor, as an individual, command the services of
       any Library employee.
   g. The Bylaws will be reviewed at the January meeting and stand until amended.
5. OFFICERS
   The officers of the Board are the President, Vice President, Secretary and Treasurer. These officers are
   elected annually by the Board at the Annual Meeting and serve for one year or until their successors
   are duly elected.
   a. The duties of such officers are as follows:
       i.       The President presides at all meetings of the Board, appoints all committees, executes all
                documents authorized by the Board, serves as an ex officio voting member of all
                committees, and generally performs all duties associated with the office.
       ii.      The Vice President, in the event of the absence of the President, or of a vacancy in that
                office, assumes and performs the duties and functions of the President, and performs all
                duties of the President when the President is not available to perform these duties.
       iii.     The Secretary keeps an accurate record of all meetings of the Board, including any official
                votes that take place outside of a meeting, and performs such other duties as are generally
                associated with the office.
       iv.      The Treasurer shall be the disbursing officer of the Board and shall perform such duties as
                generally devolve upon the office. In the absence or inability of the Treasurer, his/her
                duties shall be performed by the President and any Board member(s) to whom the
                President delegates.
6. LIBRARY DIRECTOR
   The Board appoints a Library Director who is the executive officer and implements the policies of the
   Board and is responsible for the administration of the Library under the direction and review of the
   Board.
       a. The Library Director is responsible for the care of the buildings and equipment, the
            employment and direction of the staff, the efficiency of the Library’s service to the public, and
            the operation of the Library under the financial conditions outlined in the annual budget.
       b. The Library Director submits to the Board reports and recommendations of such policies and
            procedures, which, in the opinion of the Library Director, will improve the efficiency and
            quality of Library service.
       c. The Library Director attends all Board meetings except the portion of the meeting at which the
            Library Director’s appointment or salary is discussed or decided.
7. COMMITTEES
   The President may appoint committees for specific purposes, including planning.
       a. Such committees will serve until the completion of the work for which they were appointed.
       b. All committees must make a progress report to the Board at each of its meetings.

8. MEETINGS
   Meetings will be held each month, the date and hour to be set by the Board. Written notice of all
   meetings will be mailed or emailed with a confirmation request by the Secretary to each member at
   least five days prior to the meeting.
       a. A special meeting of the Board may be called at any time by the President or upon the request
            of 3 members for a specific purpose. No business may be transacted at such special meetings
            except the stated business.
       b. The Annual Meeting will be held in January of each year. New trustees elected at the
            November general election will be sworn in by the Mayor at a date prior to the Annual
            Meeting. The business transacted at the Annual Meeting will include the election of new
            trustees if there is a vacancy to be filled.
       c. The operating and financial reports for the previous year will be presented at the first meeting
            that they are available, but no later than two months after the end of the fiscal year.
       d. The Library budget for the subsequent fiscal year will be presented and voted on at the regular
            meeting in January or at a special meeting for that purpose. The Board will continuously
            review the Library’s financial performance against the approved budget throughout the year.
       e. A simple majority of the existing Board will constitute a quorum for the conducting of all
            business. If a quorum is not present at a regular meeting, the attending members may set a
            date for another meeting to be held within two weeks, and the presiding officer will notify the
            absent members of this specially called meeting.
       f. The order of business for regular meetings will include, but not be limited to, the following
            items which should be covered in the sequence shown unless circumstances make an altered
            order more efficient:
                   i. Pledge of Allegiance
                  ii. Adoption of Agenda
                 iii. Approval of Prior Meeting Minutes
                 iv. President’s Report and Communications
                  v. Director’s and Department Heads’ Reports
                 vi. Business Manager’s Report
                vii. Treasurer’s Report
               viii. Committee Reports
                 ix. Old Business
                  x. New Business
                 xi. Dates of future Board meetings
                xii. Other Business
               xiii. Period for public expression (speakers limited to three minutes.)
               xiv. Adjournment
9. AMENDMENTS

    Amendments to these Bylaws may be proposed at any regular meeting and must be voted upon at the
    next regular meeting. Written notice of the proposed amendment or amendments will be sent to all
    absent members at least ten days prior to the voting session. A two-thirds majority (5/7) of the Board
    will be required for adoption of an amendment.
         a. Any rule or resolution of the Board, whether contained in these Bylaws or otherwise, may be
             suspended temporarily in connection with the business at hand, but such suspension, to be
             valid, may be taken only at a meeting at which a quorum is present and two-thirds of those
             present approve.
10. PROCEDURE
    All procedures not specified herein must be in accord with Robert’s Rules of Order, Revised.
