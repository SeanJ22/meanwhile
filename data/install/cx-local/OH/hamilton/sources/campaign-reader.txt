## Campaign & Media Tools

## Registered Voter Lists

Registered voter lists are a matter of public record. Use our database to export by district, precinct, and party.

[Go to registered voter lists](http://votehamiltoncountyohio.gov/campaign-media/voter-lists/)

## Absentee Voter Lists

Absentee voter lists are a matter of public record. Use our database to export by district, precinct, and party.

[Go to Absentee Voter Lists](https://votehamiltoncountyohio.gov/?page_id=3361&preview=true)

## Current Polling Locations List

See list of all current Hamilton County polling locations in an Excel document.

[Go to polling locations list](https://votehamiltoncountyohio.gov/campaign-media/current-polling-locations-list/)

## Precinct & District Maps

See PDF or interactive maps of Ohio state precincts and districts.

[Go to precinct and district maps](http://votehamiltoncountyohio.gov/campaign-media/maps/)

## Elected Officials List

See a list of all current Hamilton County officials.

[Go to elected officials list](https://votehamiltoncountyohio.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/EOD-09222026-1.pdf)
