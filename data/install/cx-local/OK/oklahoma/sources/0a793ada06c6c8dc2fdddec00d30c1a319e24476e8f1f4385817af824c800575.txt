PRESS RELEASE

FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE


Sample ballots are now available for voters who want to get a preview of

what will be at stake in the November 3, 2026, General Elections.



Voters can use the OK Voter Portal at oklahoma.gov/elections/ovp to view

their sample ballot and find their assigned polling location.



Sample ballots will also be posted at every precinct polling location on

election day so voters can review them before casting their votes.
