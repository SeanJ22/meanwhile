EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT
EXECUTIVE PROCLAMATION

I, J. Kevin Stitt, Governor of the State of Oklahoma, pursuant to the authority vested in me by 26 O.S.
§ 11-116, and pursuant to my constitutional obligation to cause the laws of the State to be faithfully executed,
hereby order that a Special Election be held for the office of Oklahoma County District Judge, due to the death
of judicial candidate Warren Plunk.

The candidate Filing Period for the Special Election shall be Monday, December 7, Tuesday,
December 8, and Wednesday, December 9, 2026; the Special Primary Election shall be held Tuesday,
February 9, 2027; and the Special General Election shall be held Tuesday, April 6, 2027.

The Secretary of the State Election Board and the Secretary of the Oklahoma County Election Board
are hereby directed to take all actions necessary to conduct this election. Copies of this Executive Proclamation
shall be distributed to them.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, | have hereunto set my hand and caused the Great Seal of the State of
Oklahoma to be affixed at Oklahoma City, Oklahoma, this 3" day of September, 2025.

BY THE GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF OKLAHOMA
