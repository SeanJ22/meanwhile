BERRYHILL FIRE PROTECTION DISTRICT CANDIDATE FILINGS SET TO BEGIN 
MONDAY, JULY 6 
 
Candidates for Board of Directors for the Berryhill Fire Protection District can file Declarations 
of Candidacy beginning at 8:00 a.m. Monday, July 6 
 
Gwen Freeman, Secretary of the Tulsa County Election Board, said the filing period ends at 5 p.m. Wednesday, July 8. 
 
Declarations of Candidacy will be accepted at the Tulsa County Election Board office at 12000 E. Skelly Drive for the 
following Berryhill Fire Protection District Board of Directors Office: 
 
Berryhill Fire Protection District Board of Directors Office 1 expired term (2026) 
Berryhill Fire Protection District Board of Directors Office 2 unexpired term (2030) 
Berryhill Fire Protection District Board of Directors Office 4 unexpired term (2028) 
Berryhill Fire Protection District Board of Directors Office 5 unexpired term (2029) 
 
 
 
 
 
The Office at stake will be filled in the General Election scheduled November 3, 2026.
