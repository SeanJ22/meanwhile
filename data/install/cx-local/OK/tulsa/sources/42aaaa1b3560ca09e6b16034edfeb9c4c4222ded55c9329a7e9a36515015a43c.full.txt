OK VOTER PORTAL

            
To access the OK Voter Portal, you will need your name

(as it appears on your registration) and your date of birth.


Additional verification is required to access some services.


Online voter registration requires voters to have a

Driver's License or State ID AND a digital signature on file with Service Oklahoma.
