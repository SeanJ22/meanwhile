2026 Candidate Filing Information




    







    
Candidate filing for the 2026 elections for federal, state, legislative, county, and judicial offices will be held 
April 1-3, 2026 (8 a.m. - 5 p.m.)





    







    
OFFICES ON THE BALLOT IN 2026





    






    

        
    


        
2026 Candidate Filing - List of Candidates - Federal, State, Legislative, and Judicial Offices Link

    






    

        
    


        
2026 Candidate List Book

    







    
2026 Candidate Withdrawals


2026 Contests of Candidacy





    







    
WHERE TO FILE 




    







    
Candidates filing for federal, state, legislative or judicial offices file with the Secretary of the State Election Board at the State Capitol. 




Candidates filing for county offices file with the secretary of the appropriate 
county election board
.  





    







    
WHEN TO FILE 




    







    
Candidates must submit a notarized Declaration of Candidacy, along with a filing fee or petition supporting their Declaration of Candidacy, Voter Registration Verification Form, and any other required documents 
April 1, 2, or 3 between 8 a.m. and 5 p.m.
  By state statute - early, late or incomplete submissions cannot be accepted. 





    







    
FILING FEES AND PETITIONS 




    







    
Candidates have the option to submit their notarized Declaration of Candidacy with a filing fee, in the form of a cashier’s check or certified check, 
OR 
 a petition. (See 
26 O.S. § 5-112
)

Petitions




    






    
    

        




    
    



    
A petition supporting a candidate’s filing must be signed by not fewer than two percent (2%) of the number of registered voters in the district, county or state, as appropriate for the office sought. (See 
26 O.S. § 5-112
)



The official number of signatures required for each office is based on the number of registered voters as of November 1 of the year preceding the candidate filing period. (See 
Okla. Admin. Code § 230:20-9-4
.) Signature requirements will be posted on this webpage when they become available after November 1, 2025.

CANDIDATE FILING PACKETS




    







    
All candidates, with the exception of federal candidates, will need to provide a 
Voter Registration Verification Form
 
with their Declaration of Candidacy. 
Voter Registration Verification Forms 
can be downloaded from the 
OK Voter Portal
 or requested in person from the 
State Election Board
 or the candidate's 
County Election Board
. 
The form must include the candidate's address.


Candidates who are 
Restricted Records
 voters will need to go to their 
County Election Board
 office to request a copy of the Voter Registration Verification Form. The County Election Board staff will print the form, write the candidate's address information on the form and initial it. The initialed form must be included with the Candidate's Declaration of Candidacy.


Candidate filing packets may be obtained from the County Election Board or State Election Board or downloaded by selecting the appropriate Candidate Filing Packet below:

2026 CANDIDATE WITHDRAWALS

            
        

        


    
        


            
            
                
    

                
List of Candidate Withdrawals

            

        

    
    
    




    


   
   



    






    
Primary Election




A candidate may withdraw from the Primary Election by filing a written notice with the Secretary of the State Election Board before 5:00 p.m. on the second business day following the close of the filing period – 
Tuesday, April 7, 2026
.  (See 
26 O.S. § 5-115
)  


Runoff Primary Election


A candidate may withdraw from the Runoff Primary Election by  filing a written notice before 5:00 p.m. on Friday following the Primary Election –
 Friday, June 19, 2026.
  (See 
26 O.S. § 5-116
) 


General Election 


A candidate may withdraw from the General Election by filing a written notice before 5:00 p.m. on the Friday following the Runoff Primary Election –
 Friday, August 28, 2026
. (See 
26 O.S. § 5-116.1
)

CONTESTS OF CANDIDACY 




    






   
    

        

            

        

        

            
2026 CONTESTS OF CANDIDACY

            
        

        


    
        


            
            
                
    

                
List of Candidate Contests

            

        

    
    
    




    


   
   



    






    
State, Federal, Legislative and Judicial Candidates 




Any candidate who files with the Secretary of the State Election Board may contest the candidacy of any other candidate for the same office by filing a written petition with the Secretary of the State Election Board before 5:00 p.m. on the second business day following the close of the filing period – 
Tuesday, April 7, 2026
.  (See 
26 O.S. § 5-118
) 


County Candidates
 


Any candidate who files with the secretary of a county election board may contest the candidacy of any other candidate for the same office by filing a written petition with the secretary of the county election board before 5:00 p.m. on the second business day following the close of the filing period – 
Tuesday, April 7, 2026
.  (See 
26 O.S. § 5-118
) 


All Candidates
 


In the event a candidate is unopposed, a contest of candidacy may be filed by any voter who is eligible to vote for the candidate. (See 
26 O.S. § 5-118
) 


 All petitions to contest a candidacy must be accompanied by a cashier’s check or certified check in the amount of $250.00. (See 
26 O.S. § 5-121
)
