SAMPLE BALLOTS AVAILABLE FOR NOVEMBER 3 ELECTION 
Sample ballots are now available for November 3, Federal and State General Election, State Questions 845 and 847, City 
of Tulsa General Runoff Election District 1; as well as the City of Tulsa Special Hotel Tax Proposition Election. Voters can 
download a sample ballot using the State Election Board’s OK Voter Portal at oklahoma.gov/elections/ovp or pick up a 
copy at the Tulsa County Election Board during normal business hours. 
Tulsa County Election Board Secretary, Gwen Freeman, reminds voters that sample ballots are specific to each person.  
“If the OK Voter Portal indicates that you have no sample ballots available, it means you are not eligible to vote in an 
upcoming election. We recommend checking the OK Voter Portal to make sure you have an election before heading to 
the polls.”  
Freeman said sample ballots will also be posted outside every precinct polling place on Election Day, so voters can 
review them before casting their votes. 
The Tulsa County Election Board is located at 12000 E. Skelly Dr., Tulsa and is open from 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m., Monday 
through Friday. For questions, please contact the Election Board at 918-596-5780  
Visit our website at www.tulsacounty.org.
