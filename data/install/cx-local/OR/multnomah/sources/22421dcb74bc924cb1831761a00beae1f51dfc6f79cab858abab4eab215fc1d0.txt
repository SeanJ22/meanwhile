Burlington Water District
Measure
26-269 
Five-Year Local Option Tax for Fire &
Ambulance Operations
 
Question: Shall the District create a new
five-year levy of $1.70 per $1000 of
assessed value for five years beginning
2027-2028? This measure creates new
local option taxes which may cause
property taxes to increase more than
three percent.
 
Summary: The taxes will be used to meet
the expenses Burlington Water District
pays for emergency services (Fire and
Ambulance) and to maintain a
contingency fund for the future increased
expenses.
 
An estimate of the total amount to be
raised each fiscal year is:
 
$84,420 in 2027-2028
$86,953 in 2028-2029
$89,561 in 2029-2030
$92,248 in 2030-2031
$95,016 in 2031-2032
 
The estimated tax cost for this measure is
an ESTIMATE ONLY based on the best
information available from the county
assessor at the time of the estimate and
may reflect the impact of early payment
discounts, compression and the collection
rate. A property with an assessed value of
$166,000.00, the average assessed value
for the district, will pay an estimated $24
per month in additional property taxes.
