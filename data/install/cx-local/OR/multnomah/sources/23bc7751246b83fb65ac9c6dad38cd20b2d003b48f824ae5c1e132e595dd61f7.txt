Notice of PUD Candidate Election - November 2026 General Election






  



  
    
      
    
  



      People's Utility District (PUD) board positions scheduled to be on the November 3, 2026 General Election ballot. 
  


  


  


      
    
    

      

        
          

    

  
    
      











      
  
      

      
            

        
      
              

        
          

  
              

      
              

            
NOTICE OF ELECTION - November 3, 2026 General Election
At the November 3, 2026 General Election, board members will be elected to the below positions and terms for People's Utility Districts (PUDs). Any qualified district voter who desires to be a candidate for one of these offices must file their declaration of candidacy with the Multnomah County Elections Division. The filing period opens on July 16, 2026, and candidates may file no later than 5:00 p.m. on their applicable candidate filing deadline: 
August 18, 2026
: The candidate filing deadline for 
elected incumbents 
(current officeholder who was elected to the office they are filing for)
August 25, 2026:
 The candidate filing deadline for 
non-incumbents 
(does not currently hold the office they are filing for) or 
appointed incumbents 
(current officeholder who was appointed to the office since it was last elected) 
The People's Utility District Candidate Guide provides information about office qualifications, filing processes and forms:
