City of Portland measures
The following measure was proposed by initiative petition for the November 3, 2026, General Election ballot. Visit the 
Portland measures webpage
 to view the ballot title, text of the legislation, and other information about this measure. 
Measure 26-267
Amends Charter: Portland residents decide 2% of city's annual discretionary budget.
Question:
 Should Portland create participatory budgeting program for residents to make binding decisions to spend 2% of city's discretionary budget?
Other items on your ballot
City of Portland voters will receive their November 2026 General Election ballots from their voting county (Multnomah, Clackamas, or Washington County). To find out more about what specific contests, candidates, and measures are on your ballot, contact your county elections office:

Where can I go to vote or return my ballot? 
Official drop box sites
Special drop boxes just for ballots are located around the state and are available 24 hours a day to collect ballots. Voters can deposit their ballots until 8:00 PM on Election Day.
 Voters who are already in line at 8:00 PM are allowed to drop off their ballot.
Multnomah County drop boxes
Clackamas County drop boxes
Washington County drop boxes
State of Oregon ballot drop box locator map
Vote by mail 
Oregonians can vote by taking their ballot to the post office or placing in a local mailbox. The Oregon Secretary of State recommends mailing your ballot by October 27 to ensure your vote is received in time to be counted.  
Use this map to find a Portland mailbox near you
Vote in person
Voting ends at 8:00 PM on Election Day.
 Voters who are already in line at 8:00 PM will still be allowed to vote.

How can I get information on the status of my ballot? 
Sign up for Track Your Ballot. You will receive 
an alert when your ballot is mailed out to you. After you vote and return your ballot, Track Your Ballot will notify you when your ballot has been received by the elections office, and again when it has been accepted for counting.
