Important dates for candidates:
Aug. 18, 2026 - Filing deadline for 
elected incumbent
 candidates
Aug. 25, 2026 - Filing deadline for 
non-incumbent (new)
 and 
appointed incumbent
 candidates
Aug. 25, 2026 - County Voters' pamphlet deadline for candidates
Oct, 27, 2026 - Certified write-in filing deadline (for County positions)
Other Information
Ranked Choice Voting (RCV)
Multnomah County voters will use RCV to elect:
County Chair 
Commissioner, District 2
City of Portland voters will use RCV to elect: 
City Auditor 
City Councilors, Districts 3 and 4
Learn about Ranked Choice Voting
Important Dates for Voters
Sep. 18, 2026 - Military/overseas ballots mailed
Sep. 21, 2026 - Absentee ballots available
Oct. 5, 2026 - Out of state ballots mailed
Oct. 8, 2026 - Gresham 
Voting Center Express
 opens
Oct. 13, 2026 - Voter registration deadline
Oct. 14, 2026 - Ballots mailed to voters
Nov. 3, 2026 - Election Day
Nov. 30, 2026 - Final Election Results Certified
