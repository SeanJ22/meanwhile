Shared Districts with Candidate Filing Office in Another County
Some voting districts are "shared" and have voters in more than one county. Candidates 
only 
file their candidacy with their position's designated filing officer (county where the district's administrative office is located; city elections official; Dept. of Agriculture, Oregon Secretary of State). After the filing period ends, filing officers certify the list of filed candidates to the county elections officials in counties that have district voters.  
Visit neighboring elections offices' websites to view information and filings for the shared districts managed by those counties: 
Filing Office: 
Columbia 
County
: Columbia River People's Utility District, subdivision 1
