Sample Ballots
View Sample Ballots
To find and view a sample ballot, please have the applicable municipality, ward, and district information available. That information can be found on the voter registration card or
the PA Voter Services tool
.
If there are issues in accessing the sample ballots in PDF format, full candidate lists for both parties are available on the
Candidate and Municipality Resources webpage
along with the ballot questions. For further assistance in accessing a sample ballot, please contact the Elections Division at
412-350-4500
(option 8).
