A write-in candidate is a person whose name does not appear on the ballot, but who wishes to be elected or nominated to an office. The
Certificate of Write-In Candidacy form
must be timely filed (see form for guidance) for a write-in candidate’s name to be counted.
Candidates who lost in the primary election cannot have write-in votes counted in the general election for the same office.
