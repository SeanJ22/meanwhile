Key Things to Know for Election Day 
 
➢ Belle Meade residents will be voting on Federal, State and local, municipal offices on 
the November 3rd Ballot.  Additionally, there are three proposed amendments to the 
state constitution that have been placed on the ballot.   
 
➢ U.S. Congressional Districts have changed.  Since 2024, all Belle Meade residents have 
been in U.S. Congressional District 5, although some voters with a 37205-zip code were 
in District 7.  As a result of redistricting approved in May 2026, all Belle Meade residents 
are voting on 2026 U.S. Congressional District 7 in the upcoming election.  The 
candidates for U.S. House District 7 are: Matt Van Epps (R), Darden Copeland (D), 
Andrew Koontz (Independent), and Lowell Reynolds (Independent). 
You may look up your revised district by address at: 
https://sos.tn.gov/announcements/2026-congressional-redistricting 
 
➢ City of Belle Meade will be holding a municipal election for three (3) seats on the five-
member Board of Commissioners.  There are four candidates running for three seats.  
Voters may select up to three individuals from the list of candidates and those three (3) 
receiving the highest number of votes will serve new four-year terms on the city’s 
governing body.  The Commissioner candidates include three incumbents: Louise Bryan, 
Neal Clayton, and Rusty Moore; as well as one former Commissioner, Gray Oliver 
Thornburg. 
 
➢ You may find a sample ballot at this webpage on the Davidson County Election 
Commission’s website to look ahead at all the options before you head to the polls:    
Step-by-step Instructions:  
1) Go to https://Nashville.gov/departments/elections 
2) Under “Services”, select “Find 2026 Election information” 
3) Then, under “November 3, 2026: State and Federal General, Belle Meade, 
Forest Hills, Goodlettsville Municipal Elections” choose the link for “Sample 
Ballot, November 3, 2026 Elections” 
➢ Early Voting opens at all locations on Wednesday, October 14, 2026 – Thursday, 
October 29, 2026.  Election Day is Tuesday, November 3, 2026, and all Belle Meade 
residents vote at Belle Meade City Hall.  For early voting locations and their hours, 
please visit the Davidson Election Commission or City of Belle Meade websites.
