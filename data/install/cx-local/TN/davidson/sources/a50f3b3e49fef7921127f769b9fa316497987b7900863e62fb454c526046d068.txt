A NEW MULTI-WAY STOP TO BE ADDED TO BELLE MEADE BLVD:
Following much study of traffic patterns, volumes, turning movements, and vehicle crash data from the intersection, city staff and consulting traffic engineers have recommended, and City Commissioners approved, the installation of an all-way stop at Belle Meade Boulevard and Tyne Boulevard.  The public safety benefits are that the new traffic control measures will reduce speeding, improve wait times for turning from Tyne Blvd and West Tyne Drive, and generally enhance pedestrian and drivers’ safe use of the city roadway.  Photos from video provided by the city’s traffic engineer demonstrate some of the challenges of the intersection.  Please click this link to view those photos:
Belle Meade Blvd and Tyne Blvd Intersection Photos
Warning! Permit Phishing Scams by Fake Officials:
The FBI has issued a warning about a phishing scam in which criminals pose as city officials to target people applying for planning and zoning permits.  The attackers use publicly available information to make their emails appear legitimate, referencing actual permit details and officials’ names. Victims are asked to pay fees via wire transfer, peer-to-peer payment apps, or cryptocurrency.  The FBI and City of Belle Meade advise you to verify any fee requests directly with city staff at Belle Meade City Hall. The City of Belle Meade will NEVER ask for payment by wire transfer or cryptocurrency.
Traffic Signal Upgrades at Harding Place & Belle Meade Boulevard Intersection
Belle Meade and Metro Nashville’s Department of Transportation and Multimodal Infrastructure will soon begin construction of new poles, signal heads, controllers, and pedestrian signals for the intersection of Harding Place and Belle Meade Boulevard. Final construction has been delayed while waiting for the signal support poles.
The revised completion date is December 31, 2026.
