Board members are charged with overseeing the governance of the school system which includes creating and updating district policies, developing and approving the annual budget for Lakeland School System, setting district goals and priorities, and hiring and evaluating the superintendent. They also serve as advocates for students and for public education in general. Email the LSS Board of Education Laura Harrison Chair Laura, and her husband, Scott, have been married since 2002. They have four children. Laura holds a Bachelor of Science in Mechanical Engineering from the University of Memphis and a Master of Business Administration from Bethel University. She is a Strategic Planning Manager for a Fortune 500 company where she’s had multiple responsibilities including project initiation, developing project budgets, data analysis and modeling, and postmortem review. She is also a two-time winner of the FedEx Five Star Award, the company’s highest honor, for her successful project work. She is also heavily involved in the Lakeland community serving or having served on various boards including Lakeland Elementary PTA, Lakeland Prep PTO, Lakeland Cheer Booster, Lakeland Prep Lacrosse Booster, and Lakeland Band Booster. Jeremy Burnett Vice-Chair Jeremy Burnett has been a resident of Lakeland since early 2012. He currently serves as the director of Lone Oaks Farm for the University of Tennessee Knoxville Institute of Agriculture. He has 23 years of community college leadership experience, having served as a dean and other positions at Southwest Tennessee Community College. He is an active member of Faith Baptist Church where he volunteers in the media ministry and is active in his Sunday school class. Mr. Burnett holds a Bachelor’s degree in Hotel and Restaurant Administration and a Master of Science in Recreation, Tourism and Hospitality, both from the University of Tennessee at Knoxville. He has work experience in the hotel and casino business with both the Kemmons Wilson companies – Holiday Inn and the MGM Grand Hotel and Casino in Las Vegas. Mr. Burnett has had the privilege to work with his now adult son, other boys and young men in the Boy Scouts as a father, cubmaster, merit badge counselor, Eagle Mentor and Troop committee chair. In that work he has helped more than 5 young men to earn the rank of Eagle. In his spare time, he enjoys woodworking and camping with his wife, Rachel, and two daughters. Keith Acton Keith, and his wife Stephanie, have been married since 1998. They have 4 children, with 2 currently in the Lakeland School System. Keith holds a Bachelor of Business Administration with a concentration in Finance and a Master of Science with a concentration in Real Estate Development, both earned at the University of Memphis. Keith has served in various roles in multifamily property management since 1996. He currently leads a local property management firm overseeing communities throughout the country. Keith has previously served on the city’s Industrial Development Board (IDB) and Economic Development Commission (EDC). He enjoys coaching his kids’ soccer and basketball teams. Michelle Childs Michelle earned her bachelor’s and master’s degrees in social work from The University of Alabama and is a Licensed Clinical Social Worker in the state of TN. She’s been a resident of Lakeland since 2008 except when her family was on an expat assignment to Poland. Michelle is the Executive Director of CASA, Court Appointed Special Advocates, through Juvenile Court of Memphis and Shelby County. Michelle and her staff and volunteers advocate for abused and neglected children in Juvenile Court and also in their schools. Michelle has worked with students for over 25 years, including 16 years as a social worker with Youth Villages. She served foster children, including close involvement with their educational needs. She’s also served students in crisis through Crisis Text Line. She continues to serve youth and women’s ministry through her church, Grace Community Church in Cordova. Michelle has two children who have/are attending Lakeland Schools. Michelle and her husband Craig have been married since 1998. Deborah Thomas Deborah grew up in Knoxville and attended the University of Tennessee, where she double majored in English and Latin and minored in secondary education. She then completed a year-long teaching internship and earned her master’s degree in Educational Curriculum and Instruction. Deborah taught Latin and English for thirteen years and has worked in educational assessment since 2008. She and her husband Chuck made Lakeland their home in 2009, and their son Bryce is a proud graduate of Lakeland Preparatory School. As a former teacher, Deborah is always mindful of the importance of students' connections to their teachers and their school. When she is not working, she can often be seen (and heard) at events and performances that feature Lakeland's kids and their incredible directors, coaches, and sponsors.
