CITY OF PRINCETON, TEXAS

ORDINANCE NO. 2026-08-10-26

AN ORDINANCE OF THE CITY COUNCIL OF THE CITY OF PRINCETON,
TEXAS, ORDERING AND CALLING FOR A GENERAL ELECTION TO BE
HELD ON NOVEMBER 3, 2026, FOR THE PURPOSE OF TO BE HELD TO
ELECT TWO COUNCILMEMBERS, PLACES 6 AND 7 ON THE CITY
COUNCIL, IN THE CITY OF PRINCETON; DESIGNATING THE POLLING
LOCATION(S) FOR SAID ELECTION; DESIGNATING THE ELECTION JUDGE
AND PROVIDING FOR CLERICAL PERSONNEL FOR SAID ELECTION;
DESIGNATING THE TIME AND PLACE OF SAID ELECTION; PROVIDING
FOR A RUN-OFF ELECTION; AND PROVIDING FOR THE FILING OF
APPLICATIONS FOR OFFICIAL BALLOTS FOR SAID ELECTION; AND
PROVIDING FOR AN EFFECTIVE DATE; AND CONTAINING OTHER
MATTERS RELATED THERETO.

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF THE CITY OF
PRINCETON, TEXAS:

SECTION 1. Findings. The above findings are hereby found to be true and correct and adopted
as findings of fact, incorporated herein, and operative provisions hereof.

SECTION 2. Election of Councilmembers. That a general election be held on the first Tuesday in
November 2026, being November 3, 2026, under and by virtue of the provisions of the Charter of the City
of Princeton, Texas, and the laws of the State of Texas, for the purpose of electing Councilmembers for

Places Six and Seven with each serving a term of four (4) years with their terms expiring in November
2030.

SECTION 3. Filing of Applications. Any person desiring his/her name to appear on the official
ballot for the general election of said City Councilmembers, or desiring to file as a write-in candidate,
shall file his/her sworn application with the City Secretary at the City Office, beginning July 18, 2026 and
ending on August 17, 2026 at 5:00 p.m.; write-in candidate filing period ends August 21, 2026.

SECTION 4. Election Notice. Notice of the Election shall be given by posting and publication,
as required by Chapter 4 of the Code and applicable law. The City Secretary is hereby directed to cause
notice of the Election to be (i) published at least once, not earlier than the thirtieth (30th) day or later than
the tenth (10th) day before Election Day, as provided in Section 4.003(a)(1) of the Code; and (ii) posted
on the bulletin board for posting notice of the City Council meetings not later than the twenty-first (21st)
day before Election Day, as provided in Section 4.003(a)(2) of the Code. The notice shall include all items
required under Section 4.004 of the Code. A copy of the published notice that contains the name of the
newspaper and the date of publication shall be retained as a record of such notice, and the person posting
the notice shall make a record at the time of posting stating the date and place of posting in accordance
with Section 4.005 of the Code. The City Secretary is also directed to post the election notice on the City’s
internet website.

SECTION 5. Early Voting. Early voting shall be administered by the Elections Administrator in
accordance with applicable law. Early voting by personal appearance shall begin on Monday, October 19,
2026, and conclude on Friday, October 30, 2026, and will be conducted at the early voting locations on
the dates and at the times specified in Exhibit A attached hereto. The early voting polling places shall


remain open during such hours for early voting by personal appearance for any registered voter of the City
at such early voting polling place. Exhibit A shall be updated and modified to include additional or
different early voting polling places required to conform to the Election Contract and the Code, which
updates and modifications shall be attached to this Ordinance and incorporated by reference for all intents
and purposes.

SECTION 6. Election Officials. The appointment of the Presiding Election Judges, Alternate
Judges, Early Voting Clerks, Presiding Judge of the early ballot board and other election officials for the
Election shall be made by the Elections Administrator in accordance with the Election Contract and the
Code. The Elections Administrator may employ other personnel necessary for the proper administration
of the Election, including, without limitation, such part-time help as is necessary to prepare for the
Election, to ensure the timely delivery of supplies during early voting and on Election Day, and for the
efficient tabulation of ballots at the central counting station.

SECTION 7. Voting by Mail. Applications for voting by mail for all residents of the City shall
submitted by personal delivery or shall be mailed to the address below prior to the deadline prescribed by
law:

SECTION 8. Early Voting Ballots. An Early Voting Ballot Board shall be created to process
early voting results of the Election, and the Presiding Judge of the Early Voting Ballot Board shall be
designated by the Elections Administrator. The Presiding Judge of the Early Voting Ballot Board shall
appoint two (2) or more additional members to constitute the Early Voting Ballot Board members and, if
needed, the Signature Verification Committee members required to efficiently process the early voting
ballots.

SECTION 9. Qualified Voters. All qualified electors of and residing in the City shall be entitled
to vote at the Election.

SECTION 10. Compliance with Law. In all respects, the Election shall be conducted in
accordance with the Code. Pursuant to the federal Help America Vote Act ("HAVA") and the Code, at
each polling place there shall be at least one (1) voting system that is equipped for disabled individuals,
and each such voting system shall be a system that has been certified by the Texas Secretary of State as
compliant with HAVA and the Code. The City hereby finds that the voting system to be used by the
Elections Administrator in administering the Election is such a system, and, in accordance with Section
123.001 of the Code, the City Council orders that such voting equipment or other equipment certified by
the Texas Secretary of State shall be used by the City in the Election.

SECTION 11. Election Administration and Procedures. The Council hereby authorizes the
Mayor, Mayor Pro Tempore, City Secretary, and/or City Manager of the City to execute or attest on behalf
of the City the Election Contract with the County.

SECTION 12. Bilingual Election. The election shall be conducted with bilingual (English and
Spanish) election materials and methods, in accordance with the Federal Voting Rights Act.


SECTION 13. Runoff Election. Should a runoff election be required following a canvass of the
Election, the Council hereby orders that a runoff election be held within the time period required by
applicable law.

SECTION 14. Severability, Savings, and Repealing Clauses. Should any word, sentence,
paragraph, subdivision, clause, term, provision, phrase, or section of this Ordinance be adjudged or held
to be invalid, void, or unconstitutional, the same shall be deemed to be severable and, accordingly, shall
not affect the validity of the remaining portions of this Ordinance, which shall remain in full force and
effect. All provisions of any ordinance in conflict with this Ordinance are hereby repealed to the extent
they are in conflict. Any remaining portions of said ordinances shall remain in full force and effect.

SECTION 15. Prerequisites Satisfied. All prerequisites for the adoption of this Ordinance have
been duly satisfied, conducted, and completed, including, without limitation, all public notices,
procedures, and publications as required by applicable law, including, without limitation, the Open
Meetings Act, Chapter 551 of the Texas Government Code.

SECTION 16. Effective Date. This Ordinance shall be in full force and effect from and after its
passage, and it is so ordained (the "Effective Date").

DULY RESOLVED AND ADOPTED by the City Council of the City of Princeton, Texas, this
the 10" day of August, 2026.
