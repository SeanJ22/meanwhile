Proposition A
THIS IS A TAX INCREASE; Authorizing the City’s
venue project, specifically being a new multipurpose
arena for professional or amateur sports,
entertainment, and civic events in The Shops at
Willow Bend shopping mall area in the City, and
authorizing imposition of one or more of the following
for the purpose of financing the venue project: (1) a
short-term motor vehicle rental tax in the City at a
rate not to exceed 5%; (2) an event parking tax at the
venue project not to exceed $3.00 per vehicle; (3) an
event admissions tax at the venue project at a rate
not to exceed 10% of each ticket price; (4) a venue
facility use tax in an amount not to exceed $5,000 per
game on each member of a major league team that
plays a professional sports game at the venue
project; (5) a hotel occupancy tax in the City at a rate
not to exceed 2% of the room price. If approved, the
maximum hotel occupancy tax rate imposed from all
sources in the City of Plano would be 15% of the
price paid for a room in a hotel.
(Proposición A)
(ESTE ES UN INCREMENTO DEL IMPUESTO;
Autorizar el proyecto del recinto de la Ciudad,
específicamente un nuevo estadio de usos múltiples
para eventos deportivos profesionales o amateurs,
de entretenimiento y cívicos en The Shops en el área
del centro comercial Willow Bend en la Ciudad y
autorizar la imposición de uno o más de los
siguientes con el fin de financiar el proyecto del
recinto: (1) un impuesto a corto plazo sobre el
alquiler de vehículos dentro de la Ciudad a una tasa
que no exceda del 5%; (2) un impuesto sobre el
