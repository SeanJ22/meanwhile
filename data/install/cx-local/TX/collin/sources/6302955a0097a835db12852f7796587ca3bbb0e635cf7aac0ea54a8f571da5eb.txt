School Board
The Wylie ISD Board of Trustees consists of seven members who act as our district’s policy-making body. Board members are elected by the Wylie ISD community to govern and set policy for the district.
Board members are elected at large to four-year terms. The Board of Trustees has set goals for the district and works with the Superintendent and staff to ensure that the community’s vision is carried out.
Together, the Wylie ISD Board of Trustees and Superintendent function as a “TEAM OF EIGHT” while maintaining open communication with district stakeholders.
