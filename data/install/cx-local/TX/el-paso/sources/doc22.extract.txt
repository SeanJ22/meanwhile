RECITALS

the Town of Clint, Texas (the “Town”) is a Type A general law municipality
operating pursuant to the laws of the State of Texas;

under Section 2.053(a) of the Texas Election Code, a political subdivision may
cancel an election if the election is one in which a declaration of write-in candidacy
is required, no opposed at-large race is on the ballot within that election, and each
candidate whose name is to appear on the ballot is unopposed;

the Town scheduled a General Election (the “General Election”) for Tuesday,
November 3, 2026 (“Election Day”), for the purpose of electing the Mayor and two
(2) Alderpersons for two (2)-year terms;

the Town Clerk, in accordance with Sections 2.051-2.053 of the Texas Election
Code, has certified in writing to the City Council that all offices are unopposed for
election; and

the Town wishes to cancel the General Election under the authority of Section
2.053(a) of the Texas Election Code.

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF THE TOWN OF
CLINT, TEXAS, THAT:

SECTION 2.

and Mayor is hereby canceled.
