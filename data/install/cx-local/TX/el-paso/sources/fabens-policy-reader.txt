**Board Policies**

Use the link below to access the Fabens ISD Online Board Policy Manual.

Please note that online versions of the FISD Board Policy Manual are provided as a courtesy and for your convenience.

*** While every effort has been made to try to ensure that the online version is consistent with the official copy, please keep in mind that online documents should be considered “unofficial” copies.  
  
 Official copies of the FISD Board Policy Manual may be found at the District's Central Office located at 821 NE "G" Avenue, Fabens, TX 79838.
