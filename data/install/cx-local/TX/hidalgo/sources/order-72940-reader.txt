## RESOLUTION OF THE EDINBURG CONSOLIDATED INDEPENDENT SCHOOL DISTRICT RELATIVE TO SCHOOL BOARD ELECTION TO BE HELD ON NOVEMBER 3, 2026, AND ORDER CALLING FOR ELECTION

WHEREAS, on the 23rd day of June 2026, the Board of Trustees of the Edinburg Consolidated Independent School District convened in a regular meeting, open to the public, and determined that the terms of office of the members of the Board of Trustees serving in Places 1, 2, 3, and 5 expire in November 2026 and that an election is required to fill those positions�

## WHEREAS, pursuant to Sections 3.004 and 3.005(c) of the Texas Election Code and Sections 11.058, 11.059, and 11.0581 of the Texas Education Code, the Edinburg Consolidated Independent School District will hold elections for the purpose of electing four (4) School Trustees.

## WHEREAS, it is necessary for this Board to pass an Order establishing the procedure for conducting said Trustee Election:

## NOW THEREFORE, BE IT ORDERED BY THE BOARD OF TRUSTEES OF THE EDINBURG CONSOLIDATED INDEPENDENT SCHOOL DISTRICT;

## SECTION ONE: That a School Election be held on November 3, 2026, for the purpose of electing to the Board of Trustees of the Edinburg Consolidated Independent School District, one trustee each for positions designated as positions I, 2, 3 and 5.

## SECTION TWO: That all requests by candidates to have their names placed upon the ballot for the above-mentioned election shall be in writing and signed by the candidate and filed with Dr. Anthony Garza, Secretary, Ex-Officio of the Board of Trustees, at the Edinburg Consolidated Independent School District Administration Building, 411 North 8th Avenue, Edinburg, Texas 78539, no later than 5:00 p.m. on Monday, August 17, 2026. All candidates for school office may file for a place on the ballot beginning Saturday, July 18, 2026. Applications may be filed in person during regular business hours or by mail, telephonic facsimile machine, or electronic mail in accordance with

Sections 1.007 and 144.003 of the Texas Election Code. The electronic mail address designated for filing is [ay.garza@ecisd.us.](mailto:ay.garza@ecisd.us)

SECTION THREE: That the said Election shall be held jointly with the general election for state and county officers conducted on November 3, 2026, as required by Section I 1.0581 of the Texas Education Code and Chapter 271 of the Texas Election Code. The Election shall be conducted at the countywide polling places designated by the Hidalgo County Elections Administrator, and voters may vote at any countywide polling place on Election Day. The countywide polling places currently designated, which are subject to change by Hidalgo County, include the following:

## POLLING PLACES ARE SUBJECT TO CHANGE

Said places of early voting shall remain open for twelve (l 2) hours on each day Monday through Saturday during the early voting period which begins **Monday, October 19, 2026 and continues through Friday, October 30, 2026. Early voting will also be available on Sunday, October 25, 2026 from 10:00 a.m. 4:00 p.m.**The main early voting polling place is designated as the Hidalgo County Elections Annex Building, 317

N. Closner Blvd. (Rear), Edinburg, Texas, or such other location designated by the Hidalgo County Elections Administrator for the joint election, in accordance with Section

85.004 of the Texas El ection Code.

Hilda A. Salinas, Hidalgo County Elections Administrator, will be appointed as Early voting clerk in compliance with Section 271.006 of the Texas Election Code.

Hilda A. Salinas, Hidalgo County Elections Administrator, will be the Early Voting clerk and appoint other early voting clerks as needed to process early voting mail and to conduct early voting at the main locations. The Early Voting Clerk's mailing address to which ballot applications and ballots voting by mail shall be sent to is P.0. Box 659, Edinburg, Texas 78540. Applications for ballots by mail must be received no later than the close ofregular business or 12:00 noon, whichever is later, on Friday, October 23, 2026. Voting by disabled voters on the day of election shall be at the designated polling locations. Immediately after the votes have been counted, the officers holding said elections shall make and deliver the returns of said election and perfonn all other duties as provided by the Texas Election Code.

SECTION SIX: That a Notice of Election shall be given by the Edinburg Consolidated Independent School District in the time and manner required by Chapter 4 of the Texas Election Code. Notice of this election shall also be published at least one time, in English and Spanish, in a newspaper of general circulation in Hidalgo County, Texas, the county in which this political subdivision is located, and said publication shall be not earlier than the 30th day or later than the 10th day before the date of this election, in accordance with Section 4.003(a)( I) of the Texas Election Code. Notice of this Election shall also be delivered to the County Clerk and Voter Registrar of Hidalgo County not later than the 60th day before the date of this election, as required by Section 4.008 of the Texas Election Code, and the District shall post the election information required by Section

4.009 of the Texas Election Code on the District's Internet website not later than the 21st day before the date of this election.

SECTION SEVEN: An Early Voting Ballot Board is hereby created pursuant to Section

87.001 of the Texas Election Code. The Early Voting Ballot Board shall be made up of members appointed by the County.

SECTION EIGHT: That immediately after the votes have been counted, the officers holding said election shall make and deliver the returns of said election and perfonn all other duties as provided by the Texas Election Code.

**SECTION NINE:**In accordance with Section 11.057 of the Texas Education Code there will not be a runoff Election if a candidate fails to receive a majority of the votes cast for such office. The candidate with the highest number of votes for each position will he elected to that office.

SECTION TEN: That Dr. Anthony GarLa, Executive Director for Support Services is appointed Secretary, Ex-Officio, to the Board of Trustees and is hereby directed to post proper notices of said Elections; including polling locations, in confonnity with the provisions of the Texas Election Code, at such time as the polling places are established.

SECTION ELEVEN: That this Election shall be conducted jointly with Hidalgo County under a joint election agreement and an election services contract with the Hidalgo County Elections Administrator, pursuant to Chapter 271 and Section 31.092 of the Texas Election Code, and the Board of Trustees hereby authorizes the Board President or the Superintendent of Schools to execute the joint election agreement, the election services contract, and any related documents on behalf of the District.

SECTION TWELVE: It is further ORDERED that this Resolution serve as the order calling said election for the Edinburg Consolidated Independent School District.
