## MONTE ALTO INDEPENDENT SCHOOL DISTRICT

November 3, 2026 General Election

THE STATE OF TEXAS COUNTY OF HIDALGO

MONTE ALTO INDEPENDENT SCHOOL DISTRICT

WHEREAS, the terms of office for Trustees representing Place 4, Place 5, Place 6, and Place 7 of the Monte Alto Independent School District Board of Trustees expire in November 2026; and

WHEREAS, pursuant to the Texas Election Code, an election shall be held on the uniform election date to elect members to the Board of Trustees;

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDERED by the Board of Trustees of the Monte Alto Independent School District that:

Section 1. Election Date

An election shall be held on **Tuesday, November 3, 2026,**for the purpose of electing one (1) Trustee each for the following positions:

*   Board Trustee, Place 4

*   Board Trustee, Place 5

*   Board Trustee, Place 6

*   Board Trustee, Place 7

Each trustee shall serve a four-year term.

Section 2. Poll i ng Places

The election shall be conducted at the polling location(s) established by Hidalgo County for voters residing within the Monte Alto Independent School District, pursuant to the

Joint Election Agreement and Election Services Contract with Hidalgo County, if applicable.

Section 3. Early Voting

Early voting by personal appearance shall be conducted at the locations, dates, and times established by Hidalgo County pursuant to the Texas Election Code, if the election is conducted jointly with the county.

Section 4. Election Officials

The Board authorizes the Super i ntendent to execute any necessary Joint Election Agreement and Election Services Contract with Hidalgo County and to appoint election officials as required by law.

Section 5. Voting Equipment

The election shall be conducted using voting equipment approved by the Texas Secretary of State and furnished by Hidalgo County, if applicable.

Section 6. Applications for a Place on the Ballot

Applications for a place on the ballot shall be filed with the Superintendent or the Superintendent's designee during the filing period prescribed by the Texas Election Code.

Section 7. Notice of Election

Notice of this election shall be published and posted in accordance with the Texas Election Code.

Section 8. Effective Date

This Order shall become effective immediately upon its adoption.
