ORDER CANCELLING ELECTION

AN ORDER OF THE AGUA SPECIAL UTILITY DISTRICT, PALMVIEW, TEXAS, DECLARING UNOPPOSED CANDIDATES FOR THE OFFICES OF DIRECTORS OF DISTRICT 4, DISTRICT 5, DISTRICT 6, AND DISTRICT 7; PROVIDING THAT THE NOVEMBER 3, 2026, GENERAL ELECTION FOR DIRECTORS OF DISTRICT 4, DISTRICT 5, DISTRICT 6, AND DISTRICT 7 SHALL NOT BE HELD; REPEALING ALL ORDERS OR PARTS OF ORDERS IN CONFLICT THEREWITH; PROVIDING A SEVERABILITY CLAUSE; FINDING AND DETERMINING THAT THE MEETING AT WHICH THE ORDER WAS PASSED WAS OPEN TO THE PUBLIC AS REQUIRED BY LAW; AND PROVIDING FOR AN EFFECTIVE DATE.

Whereas, the Board of Directors (the Board) of the Agua Special Utility District ("District"), Palmview, Texas, on July 20, 2026, adopted an order calling a general election ("election") to be held on November 3, 2026, for the purpose of electing Directors to the Board of Directors of the District for Districts 4, 5, 6, and 7, and

Whereas, pursuant to Sections 144.005 and 146.054, Texas Election Code, the deadline for filing applications for a place on the ballot and declaration of write-in candidacy for the District's election has expired; and

Whereas, Cynthia Villarreal, Custodian of Records and Agent to the Board Secretary as established under the Order Calling Election ("Agent"), in accordance with Section 2.052, Texas Election Code, has certified in writing to the Board that Jose Luis Ochoa, Jr. for District 4, Gerardo Alaniz for District 5, Ricardo Perez for District 6, and Adriana Flores-Villarreal for District 7 are unopposed for election to the office of Director for District 4, District 5 District 6, and District 7, respectively, and

Whereas, the Board of Directors hereby finds and determines that the candidates whose names are to appear on the ballot in said election for Directors of District 4, District 5 District 6, and District 7 are unopposed, there are no declared write-in candidates, and no propositions are to appear on the ballot for said election; and

Whereas, the Texas Election Code, Chapter 2, Subchapter C, requires the Board, upon receipt of certification that candidates for an election are unopposed, to declare the unopposed candidates to be elected and, as a result, to cancel the election.

NOW, THEREFORE, IT IS ACCORDINGLY FOUND, DECLARED, AND ORDERED BY THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF THE AGUA SPECIAL UTILITY DISTRICT THAT:

SECTION 1. The facts and matters set forth in the preamble of this Order are hereby found to be true and correct.

SECTION 2. In accordance with Section 2.053(a), Texas Election Code, the following unopposed candidates are hereby declared duly elected to the respective offices shown and shall

be issued a certificate of election following the date on which the election for Directors was to have been held:

*   Director for District 4, Jose Luis Ochoa, Jr.

*   Director for District 5, Gerardo Alaniz

*   Director for District 6, Ricardo Perez

*   Director for District 7, Adriana Flores-Villarreal

#### SECTION 3. Pursuant to Section 2.053(b), Texas Election Code, the election for Directors of District 4, District 5, District 6, and District 7, to be held on November 3, 2026, heretofore called and ordered by an Order Calling Election, adopted on July 20, 2026, shall not be held and is hereby canceled.

#### SECTION 4. Pursuant to Section 2.053(b), Texas Election Code, the District's Agent is hereby directed to cause a copy of this Order in the English and Spanish languages to be posted on election day, same being November 3, 2026, at all polling places that would have been used in such election. The Board authorizes and directs the District's Agent to notify the County Election Administrator of the cancellation of the election for District 4, District 5, District 6, and District 7 and that the District will no longer be participating in the election for District 4, District 5, District 6, and District 7. The District's Agent is hereby authorized to take any further actions authorized by or necessary under the Texas Election Code or other law to cancel the November 3, 2026, election for Directors for District 4, District 5, District 6, and District 7.

#### SECTION 5. All orders or parts of orders in conflict with the provisions of this Order are hereby repealed to the extent of such conflict.

#### SECTION 6. Should any section, paragraph, sentence, clause, phrase, or word of this Order be declared unconstitutional or invalid for any purpose by a court of competent jurisdiction, the remainder of this Order shall not be affected thereby, and to this end the provisions of this Order are declared to be severable.

SECTION 7. It is hereby found and determined that the meeting at which this Order was passed was open to the public required by Section 551.001 et seq., Texas Government Code, and that advance public notice of the time, place, and purpose of said meeting was given.

#### SECTION 8. This Order shall take effect immediately upon its passage.
