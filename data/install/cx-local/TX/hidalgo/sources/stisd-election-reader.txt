# Board Elections

## Elections 2026

The STISD Board of Directors is comprised of 24 members from Cameron, Hidalgo and Willacy Counties, making it the largest school board in the nation.  
  
 Each county has four elected positions representing each County Commissioner’s Precinct. In addition, each county judge appoints an individual for an at-large position for every 100,000 people. Elections for the board are held the first Tuesday in November on a biennial basis.  
  
 If you have any trouble accessing the information on this webpage or have any questions, please contact us via phone at 956.514.4216, via e-mail at [carmen.noriega@stisd.net,](mailto:info@stisd.net?subject=Information%20regarding%20STISD%20school%20board%20elections) or via mail at 7001 E. Expressway 83, Mercedes, TX 78570.  
  
**2026 Election Information**  
 South Texas Independent School District will conduct school board member elections on **Tuesday, Nov. 3, 2026**.  
  
**Seats up for election (by County and County Commissioner's Precinct):**  
 - Cameron County: County Commissioner Precincts 1, 2 & 3  
 - Hidalgo County: County Commissioner Precincts 2& 4  
 - Willacy County: County Commissioner Precincts 1 & 4

The deadline to file an application for a place on the ballot is Monday, August 17, 2026.

**Key dates in election cycle:**  
 - First day to file for a place on the ballot: Saturday, July 18, 2026  
 - Last day to file for a place on the ballot: Monday, Aug. 17, 2026, at 5:00 p.m.  
 - Deadline for declaration of write-in candidacy: Friday, Aug. 21, 2026  
 - Early voting by personal appearance: Monday, Oct. 19, 2026 - Friday, Oct. 30, 2026  
 - General elections for school board members: Tuesday, Nov. 3, 2026  
  
_Polling places and hours to be available at a later date._
