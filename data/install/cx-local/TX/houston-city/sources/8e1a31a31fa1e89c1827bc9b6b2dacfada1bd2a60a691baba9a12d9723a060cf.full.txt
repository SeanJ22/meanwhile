ELECTION OF HISD BOARD OF EDUCATION TRUSTEES



An election will be held in the Houston Independent School District on November 2, 2027, to elect a trustee from each of the following four districts:




	
Geographic District II held by Savant Moore

	
Geographic District III held by Dani Hernandez

	
Geographic District IV held by Dr. Patricia Allen

	
Geographic District VIII held by Plácido Gómez





Candidates and voters may consult the resources linked on the right side of this page for election information and related materials.



For additional assistance please contact the Board Services Department at 
BoardServices@HoustonISD.org

2025 Trustee Election

	

	

		

	


						


				


				

			
An election was held in the Houston Independent School District on November 4, 2025, to elect trustees from Geographic Districts V, VI, and VII. Candidates for Geographic Districts I and IX were unopposed so their names did not appear on the ballot.



The following are the newly elected or re-elected trustees:




	
Geographic District I - Felicity Pereyra (new)

	
Geographic District V - Maria Benzon (new)

	
Geographic District VI - Michael McDonough (new)

	
Geographic District VII - Bridget Wade (incumbent)

	
Geographic District IX - Myrna Guidry (incumbent)

Proposition A - Voter Information Document

	

	

		

	


				

			
The issuance of $3,960,000,000 schoolhouse bonds by Houston Independent School District for new school buildings and renovation and expansion of existing school buildings (including safety and security infrastructure for such school buildings), the purchase of necessary sites for school buildings and the imposition of taxes sufficient to pay the principal of and interest on the bonds and the costs of any credit agreements executed in connection with the bonds. This is a property tax increase.
