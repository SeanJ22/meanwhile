# ACC Board of Trustees Elections

Under Texas law, Austin Community College is a separate political subdivision. It is responsible for conducting elections to elect members of the ACC Board of Trustees, to levy taxes, and for annexation issues.

Members of the Board of Trustees represent residents of the ACC taxing district. Trustees are elected at large for six-year terms to provide appropriate high-level guidance and leadership to ensure the College meets the needs of the communities served. The next Board of Trustees election is Tuesday, November 3, 2026. Places 1, 2, and 3 will be up for election.

The window to apply to run for the Board of Trustees in the November 2026 election is closed. The next election will be in November 2028; Places 4, 5, and 6 will be up for election. Check this page in the summer of 2028 for information for how to apply to run.

Click **[HERE](https://offices.austincc.edu/community-and-government-affairs/acc-board-of-trustees-elections/candidates-for-the-acc-board-of-trustees-2026-election/)** to view the list of those who have applied to be on the ballot for the November 2026 election.

Click **[HERE](https://austincc.edu/accvotes)** for information on how to register to vote in the November 2026 election.

## Campaign Financial Reports

All approved candidates and members of the Board of Trustees must complete financial reporting required by Title 15 of the Texas Election Code regulating political funds and campaigns.
