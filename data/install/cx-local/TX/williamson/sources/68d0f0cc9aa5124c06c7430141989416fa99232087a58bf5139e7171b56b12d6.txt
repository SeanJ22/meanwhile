ORDER CALLING TRUSTEE GENERAL ELECTION FOR NOVEMBER 3, 2026;

MAKING PROVISIONS FOR CONDUCTING THE ELECTION; AND ORDERING
OTHER MATTERS INCIDENT AND RELATED TO SUCH ELECTION

WHEREAS, the Board of Trustees (the “Board”) of the Austin Community College
District (the “District” or “ACC”) has the authority to call a General Election (“General Election”)
for trustees to serve six-year terms for places one (1), two (2), and three (3); and

WHEREAS, other political subdivisions in all or part of the territory of ACC are holding
elections on the same date; and

WHEREAS, the Board also has the authority pursuant to Chapter 271, Texas Election
Code and Chapter 791 of the Texas Government Code, to enter into joint election agreements with
other entities also holding elections on the same date; and

WHEREAS, the Board also has the authority pursuant to Chapter 31, Texas Election Code,
to enter into a contract for election services with Bastrop, Caldwell, Hays, Lee, Travis, and
Williamson Counties; and

IT IS, THEREFORE, ORDERED BY THE BOARD OF TRUSTEES OF THE
AUSTIN COMMUNITY COLLEGE DISTRICT THAT:

Section 1, Call of Election; Date; Eligible Electors; and Hours, The General Election
(the “Election”), shall be held on Tuesday, November 3, 2026, which is seventy-eight (78) or more

days from the date of the adoption of this Order (the “Order’) within the entire territory of ACC
for trustees to serve six-year terms for places one (1), two (2), and three (3) at which all resident,
qualified electors of ACC’s taxing district shall be entitled to vote for candidates for the college
board trustee positions at issue. The Board hereby finds that holding the Election on such date,
which is a uniform election date, is in the public interest. The hours during which the polling places
are to be open on election day shall be from 7:00 A.M. to 7:00 P.M.

Section 2. Conduct of Election. The Elections shall be conducted by election officers, in
accordance with the Texas Education Code, Texas Election Code, the Constitution, laws of the
State of Texas and of the United States of America. As defined and described in Title 8 of the
Texas Election Code, electronic voting systems shall be utilized for the Elections. The following
counties will utilize the following systems:

County System

Bastrop ES&S ExpressVote Voting System; ES&S 850 Optical Scanner

ES&S ExpressVote Voting System; DS200 Digital Precinct Scanners;

Caldwell DS450 Central County Scanners
