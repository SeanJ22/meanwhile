NOTICE OF ELECTION

TO THE DULY QUALIFIED RESIDENT VOTERS OF THEON RANCHES MUNICIPAL
UTILITY DISTRICT NO. 1:

Notice is hereby given that a Confirmation and Directors Election, Bond Election, and
Maintenance Tax Election will be held for Theon Ranches Municipal Utility District No. 1 (the
“District”) on Tuesday, November 3, 2026, between 7:00 a.m. and 7:00 p.m. at the polling
location(s) on the attached Exhibit “A.” Early voting by personal appearance and by mail shall be
conducted at the locations on Exhibit "A." Bridgette Escobedo is hereby appointed as the Early
Voting Clerk. The Early Voting Clerk shall keep said place open for early voting as listed on the
attached Exhibit "A" beginning on October 19, 2026, through October 30, 2026.

Spanish assistance to voters will be available from the Early Voting Clerk or from
Williamson County upon request by the voter. The Board of Directors of the District has appointed
Coats Rose, P.C. as the District's agent for the election. The offices of the District's agent are
located at 16000 North Dallas Parkway, Suite 350, Dallas, Texas 75248, and are open from 8:30

a.m. to 5:00 p.m. each weekday.
