AGENDA ITEM: 2026-91"

August 3, 2026

SUBJECT:
Matters related to calling the Austin Community College District Board of Trustees General

Election on November 3, 2026.

PROPOSED ACTION
That the Board of Trustees calls a General Election on November 3, 2026. The General Election

is for the purpose of filling the six-year terms for places one (1), two (2), and three (3), which
expire this year.

The candidate filing period for the General Election places one (1), two (2), and three (3)
began July 18, 2026, and ends August 17, 2026.

RELATED BOARD POLICY OR PLANNING DIRECTIVE
This item is consistent with Board Policy BBB (Legal) Sections | & II.

RATIONALE
This item is required by law for the purpose of calling and providing for the conduct of the

elections.

BUDGETARY CONSIDERATION
Draft contracts with the six counties in which the election will be held have been received and
are currently under legal review. They will be presented to the Board at a future meeting prior to

the general election.
