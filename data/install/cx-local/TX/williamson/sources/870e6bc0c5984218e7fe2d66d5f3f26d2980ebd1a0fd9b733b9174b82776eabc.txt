SECTION 10. This Ordinance shall be construed and enforced in accordance with the

SECTION 11. Any provision of this Ordinance or the application thereof to any person

of Alderman hereby declares that this Ordinance would have been enacted without such invalid
provision.

SECTION 12. It is officially found, determined, and declared that the meeting at which
this Ordinance is adopted was open to the public notice of the time, place, and subject matter of
the public business to be considered at such meeting, including this Ordinance, was given, all
as required by Chapter 551, as amended, Texas Government Code.

SECTION 13. This Ordinance shall be in force and effect from and after the date of its
adoption, and it is so ordained.
