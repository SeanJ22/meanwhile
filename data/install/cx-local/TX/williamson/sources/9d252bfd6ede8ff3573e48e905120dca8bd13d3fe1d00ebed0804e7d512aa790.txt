ABOUT TISH
Meet Tish Flores
A Williamson County local for two decades, Tish Flores offers years of experience in regional administration and service. Now a write-in candidate for Commissioner of Precinct 4, she aims to give neighbors a renewed voice in their government. Tish lives in our community with her family and remains active in local civic life.



 



As a Precinct 4 candidate, Tish is focused on understanding the responsibilities of the office and the needs of the community she hopes to represent.

What Does a County Commissioner Do?
County commissioners are key members of the Commissioners Court, serving as both legislative and executive officers. They provide essential oversight for county government operations, ensuring that vital local services are delivered efficiently and responsibly to all residents.
County Budget & Taxation
Setting the county tax rate and adopting the annual budget to ensure fiscal responsibility and essential service funding.
Parks & Public Spaces
Managing county parks, community centers, and recreational facilities for the benefit of the entire community.
THE ROLE
Public Health & Welfare
Supporting health services and welfare programs that protect the safety and well-being of all county residents.
Contracts & Bonds
Reviewing and approving major county contracts, financial agreements, and bond issuances for public improvements.
Law Enforcement
Providing the necessary resources and oversight for county law enforcement, jail operations, and the judicial system.
County Facilities
Overseeing the construction and maintenance of county buildings and vital supporting infrastructure.
CANDIDATE PLATFORM
Protecting Williamson County
While the Commissioner’s Court oversees basic county functions, real leadership requires standing up for our community. Tish Flores is running with one clear goal: to protect our county from unchecked growth, wasted tax dollars, and industrial overreach.
01
No Blank Checks for Data Centers
Protecting our local resources and neighborhood integrity by opposing taxpayer subsidies and halting unchecked, high-impact industrial data center projects in Precinct 4. We must prioritize residents over corporate expansion.
02
Protect Our Water & Power
We must safeguard our essential utilities and natural resources from heavy industrial demands to ensure reliable, affordable water and electricity for all residents. We will protect reservoirs and maintain local power infrastructure.
03
Stop Unnecessary Tax Break Subsidies
Budget balance sheet, fiscal report, or tax document Striving to end corporate handouts and wasteful incentives. Tish guarantees that every tax dollar is managed with open transparency and rigorous fiscal oversight.
04
 Enforce Strict Local Controls
Empower Precinct 4 families by reclaiming local development control through dedicated oversight, intelligent zoning, and robust community standards that prioritize our residents over outside commercial interests.
05
 Demand Public Transparency
Restoring trust in the Commissioners Court by ensuring all county decisions remain open to public oversight and keeping every resident updated on the implications of each individual vote cast by leadership.
06
Empowered Education & Youth Development
Drawing from her extensive background in bilingual education, Tish advocates for inclusive community growth, robust family support systems, and superior youth programming to benefit every neighborhood throughout Williamson County.

Write-in voting allows voters to enter the name of a candidate who is running as a write-in candidate. It is essential for voters to always follow official election guidance to ensure their vote is counted properly.
