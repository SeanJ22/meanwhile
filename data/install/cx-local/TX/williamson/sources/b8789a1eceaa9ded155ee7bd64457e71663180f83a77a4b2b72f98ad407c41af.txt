Public Notice
Notice is hereby given that effective January 1, 2026, in accordance with Texas Election Code Chapter 42, the Williamson County Elections Department has implemented changes to Williamson County’s existing Voting Precincts for compliance with the Texas Congressional District boundaries as passed in the 2025 Legislature (PlanC2333). These changes were adopted by Commissioners Court on October 28, 2025.
