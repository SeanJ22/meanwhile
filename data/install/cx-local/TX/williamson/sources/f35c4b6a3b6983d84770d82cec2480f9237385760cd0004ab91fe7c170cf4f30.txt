NOTICE OF ELECTION 
 
TO WILLIAMSON AND TRAVIS COUNTIES AND THE DULY QUALIFIED RESIDENT 
VOTERS OF WILLIAMSON-TRAVIS COUNTIES MUNICIPAL UTILITY DISTRICT NO. 1: 
 
 
Notice is hereby given that a Directors Election will be held for Williamson-Travis Counties 
Municipal Utility District No. 1 of Williamson and Travis Counties on Tuesday, 
November 3, 2026, between 7:00 a.m. and 7:00 p.m. at the polling places designated by 
Williamson and Travis Counties, attached hereto as Exhibit A.  Early voting by personal 
appearance shall be conducted from Monday, October 19, 2026 through Friday, October 30, 2026 
between the hours of 7:00 a.m. and 7:00 p.m. at the polling places designated by Williamson and 
Travis Counties, attached hereto as Exhibit B. 
 
 
The county election clerks’ websites are: https://www.wilcotx.gov/elections and 
https://votetravis.gov/. The website for Williamson-Travis Counties Municipal Utility District 
No. 1 is  https://wtcmud1.org/. 
 
 
The voter may vote for up to any three (3) persons for director by placing an "X" in the 
square beside the person's name or by writing the name or names of a person or persons. 
 
 
THIS NOTICE MUST REMAIN CONTINUOUSLY POSTED UNTIL 7:00 P.M. ON NOVEMBER 3, 2026
