Alta Justice Court
Office Hours: Weekdays 10:00 AM – 4:00 PM
Location: in Town Office at 10201 East Hwy 210 in Alta
Phone Number: (801) 742-2407
Email:
mollya@utcourts.gov
To pay fines online go to Utah State Courts website.
You will need to enter either your citation # or case #
(only enter one)
.
The Alta Justice Court sessions are held one Thursday a month at 5:30 PM with Judge Paul Farr presiding.
Please call to schedule an appearance at the Alta Justice Court.
Currently, due to the pandemic court is being held virtually via WebEx. Instructions will be sent when you schedule an appearance.
Courtroom location: 10351 East Hwy 210, Alta, UT 84092
Our courtroom is a multi-use room on the second floor of the Alta Post Office building.
Access the courtroom through the post office door, and up the stairs to the left of the door.
Officers are equipped for extreme conditions and varied terrain.
To access Courtroom, enter Community Center/ Post Office through door on lookers right side of building and walk up stairs.
