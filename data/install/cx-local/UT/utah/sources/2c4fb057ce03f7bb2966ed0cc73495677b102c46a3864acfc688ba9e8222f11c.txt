Proposition #8 (Salem Residents)
Shall Salem, Utah (the “City”) be authorized to impose a sales and use tax of one-tenth of one percent (0.1%)to
provide revenues for purposes related to Recreational (Including Parks), and Cultural Facilities and
Organizations (commonly known as the “RAP TAX”)* within the City for a period of ten (10) years?
Note:   The tax rate of one-tenth of one percent (0.1%) is equal to one cent ($0.01) for every ten dollars
($10.00) spent on sales tax-eligible purchases within Salem City.
Funds collected from this tax may be used for RAP capital improvements of publicaly owened or operated
amenities such as, but not limited to:
Long-term planning for recreation and parks; including playing fields and courts
Upgrades for senior activities and facilities
Arts and cultural programs & organizations
Trails and pathways
Improvements to parks, recreational spaces and programs
