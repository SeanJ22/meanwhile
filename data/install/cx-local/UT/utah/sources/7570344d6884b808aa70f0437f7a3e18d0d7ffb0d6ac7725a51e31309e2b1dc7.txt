OFFICE OF THE LIEUTENANT GOVERNOR 

***AMENDED*** 2026 GENERAL ELECTION CERTIFICATION 

I, Deidre M. Henderson, Lieutenant Governor of the State of Utah, do hereby certify that: 

1. Pursuant to Utah Code Annotated§ 20A-5a-209(2)(a), candidates whose names appear on the attached list are qualified for placement and shaU appear on the 2026 General Election ballot; and, 2. Pursuant to Utah Code Annotated§ 20A-5a-209(2)(b), candidates' names shall appear on the 2026 General Election ballot as written and in the order listed on the attached list; and, 3. Pursuant to Utah Code Annotated§ 20A-12-201(3)(a), the names of the following judicial offices subject to retention election shall appear on the 2026 General Election ballot; and, 4. Pursuant to Utah Code Annotated§ 20A-5a-209(2)(c), no statewide ballot propositions have qualified for placement on the 2026 Utah General Election ballot; and, 5. Pursuant to Utah Code Annotated§ 20A-7-103(4), the following proposed constitutional amendments as passed by the Utah State Legislature shall appear on the 2026 General Election ballot; and, 6. Pursuant to Utah Code Annotated§ 20A-8-106(2)(b), the following official political parties are currently registered in Utah; and, 7. Pursuant to Utah Code Annotated§ 20A-5a-210, a county clerk shall publish the required notice for the county, as a class A notice, for 30 calendar days, no earlier than Monday, August 31, 2026, and no later than Wednesday, September 2, 2026. TN WITNESS WHEREOF, I have hereunto set my hand and affixed the Great Seal of the State of Utah at Salt Lake City, this 21st day of September, 2026.
