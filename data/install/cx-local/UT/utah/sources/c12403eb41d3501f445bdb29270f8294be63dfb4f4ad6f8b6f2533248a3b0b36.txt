Expungements

Expunging a criminal record will remove a conviction from your record and seals the history of the case. This process can be started on the Utah State Courts website. Follow the links below to expunge a case from your record.

[Expunge Adult Records (opens in new tab)Adult Records](https://www.utcourts.gov/en/self-help/case-categories/criminal-justice/expunge.html)[Expunge Juvenile Records (opens in new tab)Juvenile Records](https://www.utcourts.gov/en/self-help/case-categories/criminal-justice/juvenile.html)

MyCase

MyCase is an online system available from the Utah State Courts. With an account you can view your case history, all filed documents, and view all scheduled hearings. For certain cases, you can also file documents.

[Go to MyCase](https://www.utcourts.gov/en/self-help/services/mycase.html)
