ELECTION SCHEDULE General Elections : Annually on the Tuesday following the first Monday in November. Federal offices: even-numbered years. President-Vice President, Senate, House of Representatives. State and County offices: odd-numbered years in four-year cycles. For Example: Governor, Virginia Senate, Virginia House of Delegates, Fairfax County School Board, Board of Supervisors, sheriff. Primary elections , if called by a political party: Third Tuesday of June. Presidential primary elections , if called by a political party: First Tuesday of March in the year of a presidential election. Municipal elections (City and town councils): In conjunction with annual general elections. Town of Herndon: Even numbered years Town of Vienna : Odd-numbered years Town of Clifton: Odd-numbered years
