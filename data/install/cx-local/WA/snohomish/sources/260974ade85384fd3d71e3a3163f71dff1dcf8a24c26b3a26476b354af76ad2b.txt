Administrative Order 25 - 09
Recission of Temporary Adjustment of Filing Boundaries
The June 19, 2009 notice providing temporary adjustments to the filing boundaries for infraction and criminal cases between Everett Division and Cascade Division is rescinded.
Effective immediately all filing boundaries for infractions and criminal matters will track with electoral districts for District Court and comply with SCILIRLJ Infraction Rules 2.3 – Filings, and SCLCrRLJ Criminal Rules, 5.1 – Commencement of Actions, (b) – Under Other Laws.
Dated this 5th day of December, 2025.
