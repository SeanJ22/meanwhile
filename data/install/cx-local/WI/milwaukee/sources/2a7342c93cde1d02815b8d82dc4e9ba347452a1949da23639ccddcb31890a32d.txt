Election Day Polling Locations
Election Day polling locations are open from 7:00 AM until 8:00 PM on Election Day. A photo ID is required to vote. All polling places are accessible to elderly voters and voters with disabilities

You may Return your Absentee Ballot by 
any
 of the following ways-
Mail your ballot. Allow at least one week for mail.* 
Personally deliver your ballot to the City Clerk’s office, Room 102.* 
Please note that the clerk shall secure the ballot and cause the ballot to be delivered to the polling place serving the elector’s residence before 8 PM on Election Day. Please also note that, if returning your ballot to the City Clerk’s office 
on
 Election Day, the ballot must be delivered by 7 PM in order to ensure its timely delivery to the polling place by the Clerk’s Office by 8 PM.  Any ballot not mailed or delivered as provided in this subsection may not be counted, Wis. Stat. 6.87 (6).
Personally deliver your ballot to your polling location on Election Day, November 3, 2026, between the hours of 7 AM and 8 PM.*
The ballot shall be returned so it is delivered to the polling place no later than 8 PM on Election Day, Wis. Stat. 6.87(6).
Personally deliver your ballot to the drop box, located in the wall on the south side of the Greenfield City Hall building, 7325 W. Forest Home Ave.* 
The drop box closes at 7 PM on Election Day, November 3, 2026. After 7 PM, you may personally deliver your ballot to your polling location no later than 8 PM, or personally deliver your ballot to the City Clerk’s office, Room 102, by 7 PM so your ballot may be delivered to the polling place serving the elector’s residence before 8 PM, Wis. Stat. 6.87(6).
*Voters with disabilities have the right to assistance in returning an absentee ballot. The voter’s assistant can be anyone who is not the voter’s employer, an agent of the employer, a representative of their labor union, or a candidate on the ballot. See 52 U.S.C. § 10508
