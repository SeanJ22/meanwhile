Registration



					

				







	


		

			
Voter Registration
New voters, voters who have moved or had a name change are required to register. Registration can be completed in one of the following ways:
Online at: 
https://myvote.wi.gov/en-us/
Complete a 
Wisconsin Voter Registration Application form  (EL-131)
.
Voters must be 18 years or older, a US Citizen and reside at their current residence for 28 consecutive days immediately proceeding the Election to be eligible elector.
Proof of residence is required for all registrations. 
Wisconsin Election Commission Photo ID & Voting Guide
Applications can be accepted by online or mail through the 20th day prior to an Election
Within 20 days of an Election applications are required to be submitted in person at the Municipal Clerk's Office through the Friday prior to the Election.
There is no registering on the Monday before any Election.
 Election Day Registration 
Voters who need to register on Election Day should go to their polling place (
find your polling location
).
Voters must be 18 years or older, a US Citizen and reside at their current residence for 28 consecutive days immediately proceeding the Election to be eligible elector.
Proof of residence is required for all registrations. 
Wisconsin Election Commission Photo ID & Voting Guide
Registration will be completed on Badger Books therefore no paper form is required.
Voting Questions
For voting questions or to request an absentee ballot, send an email to 
elections@whitefishbaywi.gov
, contact the Village Clerk’s office at 414-962-6690, or visit the 
Wisconsin Elections Commission website
.

		

	








				

					

						
	
Mailed Absentee Ballots



					

				







	


		

			
Absentee Ballot Requests
Voting by mail is easy and secure. Absentee ballots can be requested in one of the following ways:
Online
 at: 
https://myvote.wi.gov/en-us/
 (preferred method)
Send a request via 
email
 to elections@whitefishbaywi.gov (please include a copy of WI photo ID)
Submit an Absentee Ballot Application by 
mail
. Application is available online at: 
Absentee Ballot Request (EL-121)
Voters who have not previously submitted a copy of their photo ID to vote by mail will need to include a copy of their ID with their request. If you are not sure whether you need to do so, please include a copy to avoid any delay in processing.
Track Absentee Ballot online:
 Click 
HERE
 to see instructions on how to track your absentee ballot.

 Returning An Absentee Ballot
Absentee Ballots may be returned to the Village Clerk's in one of the following ways:
1. 
Mail 
Absentee Ballot with USPS (postage is provided)
2. 
Drop off in person
 at the Administration Counter at Village Hall
3. 
Use the drop box
 located between the front doors at Village Hall labeled for Ballots
Per WI Court Ruling each voter is required to return their own ballot unless the voter self-identifies as being disabled and appoints someone to submit the ballot for them.
Track Your Ballot Online: 
Voter may track their ballot on 
MyVote.wi.gov
 
Click 
HERE
 for instruction on how to Track Your Ballot.

In-Person Absentee Voting



					

				







	


		

			
In- Person Absentee Voting for all wards occurs at Village Hall (5300 N Marlborough Drive) starting the Tuesday 2 weeks prior to an Election through the Friday immediately preceding the Election, Monday through Friday 8:00 am to 4:30 pm (open until 5:00 pm on the Thursday and Friday immediately preceding an Election).
