Voting & Elections - All Voting Takes Place at Village Hall






Upcoming Elections: November 3, 2026


 


Register to Vote or Request an Absentee Ballot:  
myvote.wi.gov


Register to Vote at the Clerk's Office: 5635 S New Berlin Road, Hales Corners, WI 53130 between the hours of 7:30 am and 4:00 PM with proper proof of residency. You must have established residency in Hales Corners at least the last 28 consecutive days prior to any Election Day.  


We strongly encourage registration 
prior to election day
. 
You may register on election day with proper proof of residence of at least 28 days in the Village of Hales Corners containing your current first and last name and residential address. 
Election day registration will be done electronically and may result in long and slow lines. You may use a current and valid WI Driver License or ID Card, any official identification card or license issued by a WI governmental body, employee ID card with photo, current property tax bill, residential lease, photo ID from a university along with a fee receipt, utility bill not earlier than 90 days before registration or a bank statement or paycheck.
 
Have your document ready before you get into line!
