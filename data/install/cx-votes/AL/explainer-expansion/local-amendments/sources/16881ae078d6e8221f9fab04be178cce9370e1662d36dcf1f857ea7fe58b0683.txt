The elected or appointed members of the governing body
of the City of Montgomery may participate in the Employees'
Retirement System of Alabama upon the same terms and
conditions as may be specified by law for any other employee
in the same retirement system and shall be treated as
employees of the city. A member holding office at the time of
the ratification of this amendment may purchase service credit
in the Employees' Retirement System of Alabama for the time
the official has served as a member. No individual may
participate in both a supernumerary program and the Employees'
Retirement System of Alabama.
