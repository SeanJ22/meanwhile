In St. Clair County, the mayor of a municipality that
participates in the Employees' Retirement System of Alabama
may participate in the retirement system upon the same terms
and conditions as may be specified by law for any other
employee in the same retirement system. A mayor holding office
at the time of ratification of this amendment shall be
eligible to purchase service credit in the Employees'
Retirement System of Alabama for the time the official has
served in that office. No person may participate in both a
supernumerary program and the Employees' Retirement System of
Alabama based on the same service.
