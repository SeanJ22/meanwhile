county school system by merging two or

Education to conduct an impact study on
the potential effects of the consolidation; to
require publication of the impact study; to
provide a procedure for protesting a
proposed consolidation of two or more
county school systems; and to require the
State Board of Education to adopt rules.
(Proposed by Act 2026- 378)
YES
NO
