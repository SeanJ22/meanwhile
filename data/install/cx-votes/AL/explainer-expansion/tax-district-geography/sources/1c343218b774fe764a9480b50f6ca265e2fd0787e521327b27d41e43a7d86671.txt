4 MILL PHENIX CITY SCHOOL TAX
PHENIX CITY SCHOOL DISTRICT
SPECIAL ELECTION FOR RENEWAL
AND
CONTINUATION OF THE LEVY OF
A 4 MILL SCHOOL TAX
(Section 269.02, Constitution of Alabama
of 2022)
Shall the governing body of Russell County,
Alabama, be authorized to levy annually, for
a period of thirty (30) consecutive years
commencing with the tax year that will
begin on October 1, 2026 (for which tax
year the tax will become due and payable
on October 1, 2027), a special district tax of
forty cents on each one hundred dollars
(equal to 4 mills on each dollar) of the
assessed value of the taxable property
located within the Phenix City School
District in said county for public school
purposes, which special district tax shall be
in renewal and continuation of the special
district tax at the same rate and for the
same purpose that is now being levied in
the said district?
