RENEWAL AND CONTINUATION OF
THE LEVY OF 9 MILLS OF AD
VALOREM TAX
SPECIAL ELECTION FOR RENEWAL
AND CONTINUATION OF THE LEVY OF
9 MILLS OF AD VALOREM TAX FOR
PUBLIC SCHOOL AND GENERAL
COUNTY PURPOSES
(Section 66-10.00 and Section 217(f),
Constitution of Alabama of 2022)
Shall the governing body of Wilcox
County, Alabama, in order to renew,
continue and preserve the current rate of
ad valorem taxation in the County, be
authorized to levy and collect, at the rate
of 19 mills per dollar ($1.90 per $100) of
the assessed value of taxable property in
the County, the special ad valorem tax
provided for under Section 66-10.00 of
the Constitution of Alabama of 2022,
which authorization, if approved, will
effect an increase in the rate of said tax of
9 mills; said increase to be allocated and
apportioned (i) 7 mills to the Wilcox
County Board of Education for public
school purposes and (ii) 2 mills to Wilcox
County for deposit into the general fund
of the County; said increase to be
effective for each tax year of the County
commencing with the tax year that will
begin on October 1, 2027 (for which taxes
will become due and payable on October
1, 2028); and said increase to become
effective simultaneous with expiration of a
9-mill special ad valorem tax levied in the
County for the benefit of the said Board of
Education and the County, all to the end
that there be no net increase in the
present rate of taxation in the County?
