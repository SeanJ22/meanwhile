RUSSELLVILLE 4.5 MILL SCHOOL TAX
RUSSELLVILLE CITY SCHOOL DISTRICT
SPECIAL ELECTION FOR RENEWAL
AND CONTINUATION OF THE LEVY OF A
4.5 MILL SCHOOL TAX
(Section 269.02, Constitution of Alabama of
2022)
Shall the governing body of Franklin
County, Alabama, be authorized to levy
annually, for a period of thirty (30)
consecutive years commencing with the tax
year that will begin on October 1, 2027 (for
which tax year the tax will become due and
payable on October 1, 2028), a special
district tax of forty-five cents on each one
hundred dollars (equal to 4.5 mills on each
dollar) of the assessed value of the taxable
property located within the Russellville City
School District in said county for public
school purposes, which special district tax
shall be in renewal and continuation of the
special district tax at the same rate and for
the same purpose that is now being levied
in the said district?
FOR proposed taxation
AGAINST proposed taxation
