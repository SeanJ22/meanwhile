Bonds of the City of Brookland in one or more series from time to time in the
maximum aggregate principal amount of $725,000 for the purpose of financing all
or a portion of the costs of the acquisition, construction, and equipping of storm
and emergency shelters, emergency alert sirens, and related facilities and apparatus,
including particularly, without limitation, any signage, equipment and land
acquisition, street lighting, and utility improvements or adjustments related thereto
or in support thereof and, in order to pay the bonds, the pledge of the existing 2%
local sales and use tax levied within the City by Ordinance No 2015-25, adopted
August 31, 2015, as amended (the "2015 Tax"). The 2015 Tax expires on the first
date on which the 2015 Tax can be administratively removed when (a) there are
sufficient funds to pay in full any outstanding bonds secured by a pledge of
collections of the 2015 Tax and (b) the City Council determines that the purposes
of the 2015 Tax cannot be or cannot continue to be fulfilled because no bonds
secured by a pledge of collections of the 2015 Tax will be issued in the future.
