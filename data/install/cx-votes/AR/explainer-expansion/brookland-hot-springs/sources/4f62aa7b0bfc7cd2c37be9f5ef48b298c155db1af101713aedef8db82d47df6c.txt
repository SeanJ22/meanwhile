Bonds of the City of Brookland in one or more series from time to time in the
maximum aggregate principal amount of $3,385,000 for the purpose of financing
all or a portion of the costs of acquiring, constructing, and equipping extensions,
betterments and improvements to the City’s sewer facilities and, in order to pay the
bonds, the pledge of the existing 2% local sales and use tax levied within the City
by Ordinance No. 2015-25, adopted August 31, 2015, as amended (the "2015 Tax").
The 2015 Tax expires on the first date on which the 2015 Tax can be
administratively removed when (a) there are sufficient funds to pay in full any
outstanding bonds secured by a pledge of collections of the 2015 Tax and (b) the
City Council determines that the purposes of the 2015 Tax cannot be or cannot
continue to be fulfilled because no bonds secured by a pledge of collections of the
2015 Tax will be issued in the future.
