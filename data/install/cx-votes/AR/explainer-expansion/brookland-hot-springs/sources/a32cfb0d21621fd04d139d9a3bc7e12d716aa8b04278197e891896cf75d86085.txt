26-07. COUNTY-WIDE 0.625% SALES AND USE TAX
The continuation of the levy of 0.625% local sales and use Tax within the County to finance all or a
portion of the costs of improvements to existing, roads, streets, and related structures, including
particularly, without limitation, overpasses, underpasses, bridges and sidewalks, and any necessary
land, easements, rights of way and related intersection improvements, drainage and traffic control
devices and improvements therefor (collectively, the "Improvements").
If the question is approved, there will be a continuation of the existing levy within the County of
0.625% sales and use tax, the net collections of which after the State of Arkansas deducts its
administrative charges and required rebates will be divided per capita among the County and the
Municipalities within in the County pursuant to state law. The term of the sales and use tax levy shall
be July 1, 2027, through June 30, 2032.
