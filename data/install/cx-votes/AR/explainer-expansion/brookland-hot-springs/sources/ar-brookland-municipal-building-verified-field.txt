Bonds of the City of Brookland in one or more series from time to time in the
maximum aggregate principal amount of $3,445,000 (the "2027 Municipal
Building Improvement Bonds") for the purpose of financing a portion of the costs
of the acquisition and construction of a municipal building that will contain city
offices and a police station, any land acquisition therefor, and any furnishings,
equipment, utility adjustments and lighting, street, parking, landscaping, signage
and drainage improvements related thereto or in support thereof and, in order to
pay the bonds, the pledge of the existing 2% local sales and use tax levied within
the City by Ordinance No. 2015-25, adopted August 31, 2015, as amended (the
"2015 Tax"). The 2015 Tax expires on the first date on which the 2015 Tax can be
administratively removed when (a) there are sufficient funds to pay in full any
outstanding bonds secured by a pledge of collections of the 2015 Tax and (b) the
City Council determines that the purposes of the 2015 Tax cannot be or cannot
continue to be fulfilled because no bonds secured by a pledge of collections of the
2015 Tax will be issued in the future. A portion of the costs of acquiring and
constructing the municipal building was also financed with proceeds of the City’s
Sales and Use Tax Refunding and Improvement Bonds, Series 2021, and the 2027
Municipal Building Improvement Bonds are proposed to finance the remainder of
the costs necessary to complete such facilities.
