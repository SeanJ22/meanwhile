Bonds of the City of Brookland in the maximum aggregate principal amount of
$4,640,000 for the purpose of financing all or a portion of the costs of refunding
the City's Sales and Use Tax Refunding and Improvement Bonds, Series 2021 and,
in order to pay the bonds, the pledge of the existing 2% local sales and use tax
levied within the City by Ordinance No. 2015-25, adopted August 31, 2015, as
amended (the "2015 Tax"). The 2015 Tax expires on the first date on which the
2015 Tax can be administratively removed when (a) there are sufficient funds to
pay in full any outstanding bonds secured by a pledge of collections of the 2015
Tax and (b) the City Council determines that the purposes of the 2015 Tax cannot
be or cannot continue to be fulfilled because no bonds secured by a pledge of
collections of the 2015 Tax will be issued in the future.
