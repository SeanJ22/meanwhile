Bonds of the City of Eureka Springs in one or more series from time to time in the maximum aggregate principal amount of $10,000,000 to finance all or a portion of the costs of acquiring, constructing, improving, and equipping extensions, betterments, and improvements to the City’s sewer facilities, including particularly, without limitation, sewer treatment plant improvements and sewer line improvements, and, in order to pay the bonds, the pledge of the new 1% local sales and use tax levied within the City by Ordinance No. 2384, adopted May 11, 2026, if such tax is separately approved by the voters.
