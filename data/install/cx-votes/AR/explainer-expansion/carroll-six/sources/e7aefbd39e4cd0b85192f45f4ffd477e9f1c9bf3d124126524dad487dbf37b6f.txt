FOR the rejection of Ordinance 2357, an Ordinance to Authorize the Creation of a Permanent Entertainment District at Pine Mountain Village, Eureka Springs, Arkansas.
AGAINST the rejection of Ordinance 2357, an Ordinance to Authorize the Creation of a Permanent Entertainment District at Pine Mountain Village, Eureka Springs, Arkansas.
That Ordinance No. 2357 of the City Council of Eureka Springs, Arkansas authorizes the Creation of a Permanent Entertainment District at Pine Mountain Village be referred to the people of said Municipality, to the end that the same may be approved or rejected by a vote of the voters.
