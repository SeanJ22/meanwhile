POPULAR NAME

Backyard Poultry Regulation and Animal Ordinance
Modernization Act

BALLOT TITLE

An ordinance amending Sections 6.04.02 and 6.12.01 of
the Horseshoe Bend Municipal Code to allow the
regulated keeping of domesticated poultry, including
chickens and ducks, within city limits; establishing limits
on number of birds, sanitation requirements, enclosure
standards, setback requirements, and prohibitions on
roosters and commercial breeding; and clarifying the list
of animals permitted within the city.

INITIATED ORDINANCE

City of Horseshoe Bend, Arkansas

AN ORDINANCE

Amending Sections 6.04.02 and 6.12.01 of the Horseshoe
Bend Municipal Code to allow regulated backyard
domesticated fowl and to clarify the list of animals permitted
within the city.

WHEREAS The City of Horseshoe Bend currently regulates
animals through Sections 6.04.02 and 6.12.01 of the
municipal code; and

WHEREAS the existing ordinance structure prohibits
certain animals including chickens while simultaneously
maintaining a “positive list” of allowed animals; and

WHEREAS domesticated poultry ownership under
reasonable regulations is permitted in many Arkansas
communities; and

WHEREAS modern municipal animal ordinances
commonly allow limited backyard poultry ownership while
maintaining sanitation and nuisance protections; and

SECTION 1

Amendment to Section 6.04.02 — Restrictions on
Keeping Animals

Section 6.04.02 of the Horseshoe Bend Municipal Code is
hereby amended to read as follows:

No livestock animals such as horses, cows, sheep, or goats
shall be kept within the boundaries of the City of Horseshoe
Bend except within areas designated by zoning ordinance for
pasture or stables.

Domesticated poultry may be kept within city limits under
the regulations established in this chapter.

SECTION 2

Backyard Poultry Regulations

Domesticated poultry including chickens and ducks may be
kept within the city subject to the following conditions:

1. No more than ten (10) domesticated fowl] per household
shall be permitted.

2. Roosters are prohibited within city limits.
3. Geese, guinea fowl, turkeys, and peafowl are prohibited.

4. Birds must be kept in clean, enclosed coops or runs
designed to protect the birds and prevent escape.

5. Birds shall not be allowed to roam freely outside of
enclosed areas except within fully fenced yards where
permitted.

6. Poultry coops or enclosures must be located at least
twenty-five (25) feet from neighboring residences.

7. Commercial breeding, slaughter, or sale of poultry is
prohibited within city limits.

8. Poultry enclosures must be maintained in a sanitary
condition to prevent odor, pests, or nuisance conditions.

9. The Animal Control authority of the City of Horseshoe
Bend retains full authority to enforce nuisance,

sanitation, cruelty, and animal control provisions.

SECTION 3

Amendment to Section 6.12.01 — Allowed Animals

Section 6.12.01 of the Horseshoe Bend Municipal Code is
hereby amended to include the following permitted species:

M. Domesticated poultry including chickens and ducks,
subject to the regulations established in Section 6.04.02.

This addition clarifies that domesticated poultry are
permitted animals within the city under regulated conditions.

SECTION 4

Enforcement

Violations of the regulations established in this ordinance
shall be subject to enforcement under the existing animal
control provisions of the Horseshoe Bend Municipal Code.

Animal Control officers may investigate complaints related
to nuisance conditions, sanitation, or violations of the
provisions contained in this ordinance.

SECTION 5

Severability

If any provision of this ordinance is declared invalid or
unconstitutional by a court of competent jurisdiction, the
remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect.

SECTION 6
Effective Date

This ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval
by the voters of the City of Horseshoe Bend.

SECTION 7
Repealer

All ordinances or parts of ordinances in conflict with this
measure are hereby repealed to the extent of the conflict.
