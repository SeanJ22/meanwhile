ORDINANCE NO. 633 OF THE CITY,

DESIGNATED USES OF THE
HOTEL/MOTEL GROSS RECEIPTS
TAX (THE "LODGING TAX") AND THE
RESTAURANT GROSS RECEIPTS

TAXES") LEVIED BY THE CITY; TO
AUTHORIZE COLLECTIONS OF THE
A&P TAXES TO BE PLEDGED TO
BONDS APPROVED BY THE
VOTERS AND ISSUED FROM TIME

CAPITAL IMPROVEMENTS
AUTHORIZED TO BE FINANCED

SUBCHAPTER 6 OF THE ARKANSAS
CODE OF 1987 ANNOTATED, AS

COLLECTIONS OF THE A&P TAXES
NOT NEEDED TO PAY DEBT

PROMOTION COMMISSION FOR ALL
PURPOSES AS ARE PERMITTED BY
THE AUTHORIZING LEGISLATION;
TO EXPAND THE CATEGORIES OF
BUSINESSES SUBJECT TO THE A&P
TAXES TO ALL CATEGORIES SET
FORTH IN THE AUTHORIZING
LEGISLATION; TO INCREASE THE
