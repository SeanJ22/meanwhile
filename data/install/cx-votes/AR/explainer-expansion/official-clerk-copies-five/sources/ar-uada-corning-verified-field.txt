FOR changing the terms of aldermen in the City of Corning from two-year terms to staggered four-year terms.
The questions of changing the terms of aldermen who make up the City Council of City of Corning from two-year to staggered four-year terms. Currently aldermen for each ward in the City of Corning are elected to two-year terms. If this question is approved, aldermen for South Ward Position 1 and North Ward Position 1 would be elected to a four-year term at the 2028 general election and those aldermen for South Ward Position 2 and North Ward Position 2 would be elected to a two-year term in the City of Corning general election, but would be elected to a four year term starting in the 2030 general election. If this question is approved, half of the City Council positions would be open for election for every general election after 2030. If this question is not approved, all of the aldermen positions for the City of Corning will be open for election every two years at the general election.
