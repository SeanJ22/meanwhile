HOSPITAL IMPROVEMENT BONDS

Bonds of Hempstead County in the maximum aggregate principal amount of
$13,500,000 to finance all or a portion of the costs of acquiring, constructing,
furnishing and equipping improvements to the hospital facility owned by the
County and, in order to pay the bonds, the pledge of the net collections of the
0.75% local sales and use tax levied within the County pursuant to Ordinance No.
2026-3, adopted March 26, 2026 (the "Sales and Use Tax"). Any collections of
the Sales and Use Tax not used for payment of the bonds will be used by the
County for other lawful purposes.
