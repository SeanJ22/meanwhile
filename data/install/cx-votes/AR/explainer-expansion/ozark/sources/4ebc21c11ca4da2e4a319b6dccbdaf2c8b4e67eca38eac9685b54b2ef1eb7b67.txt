REFUNDING BONDS AND REPLACEMENT TAX

Vote FOR or AGAINST an issue of bonds of the City of Ozark in the maximum
principal amount of $2,825,000 for the purpose of refunding the City’s
outstanding Sales and Use Tax Bonds, Series 2016, and, in order to pay and
secure the bonds, the levy and pledge of a new 1.0% Replacement Tax within
the City.

SYSTEM IMPROVEMENT BONDS AND REPLACEMENT TAX

Vote FOR or AGAINST an issue of bonds of the City of Ozark in the maximum
principal amount of $18,000,000 for the purpose of financing betterments,
improvements, upgrades, and expansion of the City’s water and sewer system
facilities and, in order to pay and secure the bonds, the levy and pledge of a
new 1.0% Replacement Tax within the City.
The bonds described below that are approved may be combined into a single
issue or may be issued in series from time to time. The City currently has in
place a one percent (1.0%) city-wide sales and use tax levied under Ordinance
No. 2015-05 adopted by the City Council of the City on June 8, 2015, which
was approved by special election of the electors of the City held on August 11,
2015 (the “2015 Sales and Use Tax”). No bonds will be issued for any purpose
unless each of the Refunding Bonds and the System Improvement Bonds are
both approved. If both the Refunding Bonds and the System Improvement
Bonds are approved, then there will be levied a replacement 1.0% sales and
use tax within the City (the “Replacement Tax”), the net collections of which
remaining after the State of Arkansas deducts its administrative charges and
required rebates, will be used and allocated (a) 7/8 solely to retire the bonds
and obligations of the City with respect thereto and shall expire on the last day
of the last month of the calendar quarter in which such bonds or any bonds
issued in refunding thereof have been retired and (b) 1/8 to the operation and
maintenance of the System Improvements and other City facilities and to pay
the principal of and interest on such bonds, which portion of the tax will not
expire. The Replacement Tax will replace the 2015 Sales and Use Tax. If the
Refunding Bonds and the System Improvement Bonds are not both approved,
then the 2015 Sales and Use Tax will remain in place along with the City’s
currently existing bonding authority with respect to the 2015 Sales and Use
Tax. The effective date of the Replacement Sales and Use Tax, if approved, will
be the day following the date the existing 2015 Sales and Use Tax expires.
