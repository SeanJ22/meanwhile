NOTICE OF ELECTION
GENERAL ELECTION

TUESDAY, NOVEMBER 3, 2026
REVISED 9-28-26

The Board of Election Commissioners of Sebastian County, Arkansas, pursuant to Arkansas Codes § 7-5-202 and 7-
11-205 does hereby proclaim that a General and Special Election will be held in Sebastian County, Arkansas, on
Tuesday, November 3, 2026.

VOTER REGISTRATION AND SAMPLE BALLOTS

To check your voter registration, go to: www.voterview.org. or go scan the QR code. You can also
view your sample ballot. If your voter registration has changed and/or if you have questions
concerning your eligibility to vote, please call the County Clerk's office at 479-782-5065.

Deadline to register to vote is Monday, October 5, 2026.

ABSENTEE VOTING

To request an absentee ballot, or to see if you are qualified to vote absentee, contact the County Clerk's office
beginning September 4, 2026, between 8 a.m. to 5 p.m., Monday - Friday, using the information below:

e Fort Smith: 479-782-5065
e Greenwood: 479-996-4195
