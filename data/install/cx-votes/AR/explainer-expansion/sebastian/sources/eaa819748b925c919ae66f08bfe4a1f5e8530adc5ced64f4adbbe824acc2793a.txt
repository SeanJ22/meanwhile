TOWN OF CENTRAL CITY SPECIAL ELECTION
AN ORDINANCE PROVIDING FOR THE LEVY OF ONE PERCENT (1%) LOCAL SALES AND USE TAX WITHIN THE TOWN OF
CENTRAL CITY, ARKANSAS
FOR or AGAINST adoption of a 1% local sales and use tax within the Town of Central City, Arkansas, with half to be used for
fire protection and half to be used for police protection.

CITY OF FORT SMITH SPECIAL ELECTION
A PROPOSITION TO ORGANIZE THE CITY OF FORT SMITH, ARKANSAS UNDER
THE MAYOR-COUNCIL FORM OF MUNICIPAL GOVERNMENT
FOR or AGAINST the proposition to organize the City of Fort Smith, Arkansas under the mayor-council form of government.

CITY OF LAVACA SPECIAL ELECTION
AN ORDINANCE PROVIDING FOR THE LEVY OF TWO PERCENT (2%) SALES AND USE TAX WITHIN THE CITY OF LAVACA,
ARKANSAS; TO SET JUNE 30, 2037 AS THE EXPIRATION DATE OF THE TAX; AND PRESCRIBING OTHER MATTERS
PERTAINING THERETO.
FOR or AGAINST adoption of a two percent (2%) local sales and use tax within the City of Lavaca, Arkansas.
