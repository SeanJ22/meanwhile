RESOLUTION AND CALL FOR ELECTION FOR A TRANSACTION PRIVILEGE TAX
FOR JAIL FACILITIES
A Resolution of the Maricopa County Board of Supervisors to place the following measure on the
November 3, 2026, General Election ballot, as authorized by the Legislature of the State of Arizona,
Arizona Revised Statutes (A.R.S.) § 42-6109.02, to establish a jail facilities excise tax of one-ﬁfth of one
cent ($0.002) on transactions, the same rate as the jail facilities excise tax that has been in place since
1998 and is set to expire in March 2027.
WHEREAS, the Jail Facilities Excise Tax has been a critical revenue source since 1998 for public safety
functions, including staﬃng, maintenance and construction of county detention facilities, adult
probation, juvenile probation, correctional health, and more.
WHEREAS, public safety is the largest expenditure in the County's budget with 49.5% of the Fiscal Year
2026 budget supporting essential services such as law enforcement, jails, correctional health,
probation, courts, and criminal prosecution, and defense.
WHEREAS, voters overwhelmingly approved the initial Jail Facilities Excise Tax at a rate of one-ﬁfth of
one cent in 1998 and again in 2002.
WHEREAS, the current transaction privilege tax is set to expire in March 2027.
WHEREAS, the Maricopa County Board of Supervisors formed the Citizen's Public Safety Funding
Committee, tasked with reviewing current uses of the Detention Fund and making recommendations for
how to sustain public safety programs and services.
WHEREAS, the Public Safety Funding Committee held 13 public meetings and gained consultant
expertise on the topic of public safety funding.
WHEREAS, the Committee also hosted public outreach webinars to allow for input prior to ﬁnalizing
their recommendations.
WHEREAS, the Board of Supervisors adopted the Committee's recommendation that the County pursue
legislation to allow a measure to be placed on the November 3, 2026 ballot for a transaction privilege
tax set at the same rate as the expiring tax, set at the rate allowed under A.R.S. § 42-6109.02, section A,
or one-ﬁfth of one cent, for a term of twenty years.
WHEREAS, the Legislature has authorized use of sales taxes for jail programs and facilities statewide
through dedicated sales taxes for jail districts in other counties, and by legislation authorizing the
citizens of Maricopa County to vote on dedicated sales tax for this purpose.
WHEREAS, the Legislature and Governor, in Laws 2025, Chapter 155, added A.R.S. §42-6109.02, which
granted Maricopa County voters the authority to consider a measure for a jail facilities excise tax levy
for facilities and programs to replace the current jail facilities excise tax levy that is expiring.
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the Board of Supervisors of Maricopa County, State of Arizona,
as follows:

SECTION 1. Purpose
Monies derived from the tax levied pursuant to this Resolution shall be used as set forth in this
Resolution, as speciﬁed in Laws 2025, Chapter 155, to:
• Finance construction or renovation of adult and juvenile jail facilities.
• Maintain and operate adult and juvenile jail facilities.
• Finance other programs to reduce the expense of adult and juvenile jail facilities.
For the purpose of this Resolution "Jail Facility" means a jail or other place of detention of persons
charged with or convicted of a crime or of persons who are subject to the jurisdiction of the Juvenile
Court, including related support facilities.
The County shall maintain its support for maintenance and operation of adult and juvenile facilities as
required by law. For the purpose of this subsection, "maintenance and operation" includes detention
personnel compensation, administrative support costs, and costs of maintaining and repairing the
facility and grounds.
SECTION 2. Tax for county public safety and jail facilities.
A. Under the authority of Arizona Revised Statutes (A.R.S) § 42-6109.02, the new levy of the
county's jail facilities excise tax will be at the rate of one-ﬁfth of one cent, the same rate as the current
levy that is set to expire, applying to each person engaging or continuing in the county in a business
taxed under A.R.S. Title 42, Chapter 5, Article 1 and limited to four percent of the statutory rate of the
state transaction privilege tax in that section, which equates to one-ﬁfth of one cent ($0.002).
B. The taxes under this section shall be levied beginning in the month following the expiration of
the tax under A.R.S. § 42-6109.01 and may continue for not more than twenty years after the date the
tax collection begins. The Board of Supervisors may, by resolution, decrease the rate of tax at any time
during the term of the tax.
SECTION 3: Election
A. The Board of Supervisors calls for the placement of the measure described in this Resolution on
the ballot for the November 3, 2026 General Election for consideration by the voters.
B. The County Elections Director shall prepare the ﬁnal ballot text in accordance with A.R.S. § 16-
502.M and publicity pamphlet for the following measure:
OFFICIAL TITLE: COUNTY JAILS: TRANSACTION PRIVILEGE TAX OF ONE-FIFTH OF ONE CENT, the
same rate as the existing jail facilities excise tax expiring in March 2027.
DESCRIPTIVE TITLE: Pursuant to authority granted by the State of Arizona, the County seeks voter
approval of a transaction privilege tax set at the rate allowed under A.R.S. § 42-6109.02, section A, one-
ﬁfth of one cent to be used for county jail facilities and programs.
A "yes" vote shall have the effect of approving a sales tax of one-ﬁfth of one cent ($0.002) for county
jail facilities and programs, the same rate as the current tax that is expiring.
A "no" vote shall have the effect of rejecting a sales tax of one-ﬁfth of one cent ($0.002) for jail facilities
and programs.

AN ACT
AMENDING SECTION 16-193, ARIZONA REVISED STATUTES; AMENDING TITLE 42, CHAPTER 6,
ARTICLE 3, ARIZONA REVISED STATUTES, BY ADDING SECTION 42-6109.02; RELATING TO COUNTY
EXCISE TAXES.
Be it enacted by the Legislature of the State of Arizona:
Section 1. Section 16-193, Arizona Revised Statutes, is amended to read:
16-193. Active registered voters; applicability
The terms "registered voters", "persons who are registered to vote", "registered electors" and
"voters registered" as used in the following sections include only active registered voters for purposes
of the following:
1. Calculating petition signature requirements pursuant to sections
11-133, 16-322, 16-341, 45-415, 45-433, 48-4433 and 48-4832.
2. Mailing and distributing election-related notices, pamphlets or ballots pursuant to sections
11-137, 15-905.01, 16-245, 16-412, 16-461, 16-510, 19-123, 19-141, 35-454, 41-563.02, 42-6109.01,
42-6109.02, 42-17057, 42-17107, 48-620, 48-4021, 48-4236, 48-5314, 48-6432 and 48-6433.
3. Furnishing ballots pursuant to sections 16-508 and 48-685.
4. Determining qualiﬁcation for political parties' continued representation on the ballot pursuant
to sections 16-244 and 16-804.
5. Choosing political party oﬃcers pursuant to sections 16-821 and 16-823.
Sec. 2. Title 42, chapter 6, article 3, Arizona Revised Statutes, is amended by adding section
42-6109.02, to read:
42-6109.02. Jail facilities excise tax; maintenance of effort; deﬁnitions
A. THE BOARD OF SUPERVISORS OF ANY COUNTY THAT LEVIES A JAIL FACILITIES EXCISE TAX
UNDER SECTION 42-6109.01, BY RESOLUTION ADOPTED BEFORE THE EXPIRATION OF THAT TAX,
MAY CALL FOR A COUNTYWIDE GENERAL ELECTION TO AUTHORIZE THE LEVY OF A TRANSACTION
PRIVILEGE TAX UNDER THIS SECTION IN ADDITION TO ALL OTHER TAXES. IF APPROVED BY THE
QUALIFIED ELECTORS VOTING AT THE COUNTYWIDE GENERAL ELECTION, THE COUNTY SHALL LEVY
AND THE DEPARTMENT SHALL COLLECT THE TAX AT RATES OF NOT MORE THAN 4.0 PERCENT OF
THE TRANSACTION PRIVILEGE TAX RATE PRESCRIBED BY SECTION 42-5010, SUBSECTION A
APPLYING TO EACH PERSON ENGAGING OR CONTINUING IN THE COUNTY IN A BUSINESS TAXED
UNDER CHAPTER 5, ARTICLE 1 OF THIS TITLE. BY RESOLUTION, THE BOARD OF SUPERVISORS MAY
DECREASE THE RATES OF THE TAX DURING THE TERM OF THE TAX.
B. THE RESOLUTION SHALL:
1. INCLUDE A STATEMENT OF THE TAXES COLLECTED PURSUANT TO SECTION
42-6109.01 AND AN ACCOUNTING OF THE BUDGETING OF THOSE REVENUES BY THE COUNTY.
2. INCLUDE A PROJECTION OF THE AMOUNT OF TAXES ANTICIPATED TO BE COLLECTED
ANNUALLY IF THE TAX LEVY UNDER THIS SECTION IS APPROVED IN THE COUNTYWIDE GENERAL
ELECTION.
10
3. IDENTIFY THE PROJECTS, PROGRAMS AND CATEGORIES FOR WHICH EXPENDITURES ARE
CONTEMPLATED AND THE ESTIMATED AMOUNT OF TAXES TO BE EXPENDED FOR EACH.
4. BE PRINTED IN THE BALLOT PROPOSITION PUBLICITY PAMPHLET THAT WILL BE MAILED
TO EVERY HOUSEHOLD WITH A REGISTERED VOTER.
C. TAXES UNDER THIS SECTION SHALL BE LEVIED BEGINNING IN THE MONTH FOLLOWING
THE EXPIRATION OF THE TAX UNDER SECTION 42-6109.01 AND MAY NOT CONTINUE FOR MORE
THAN TWENTY YEARS AFTER THE DATE THE TAX COLLECTION BEGINS.
D. AT THE END OF EACH MONTH, THE STATE TREASURER SHALL TRANSMIT THE NET
REVENUES COLLECTED PURSUANT TO THIS SECTION TO THE TREASURER OF THE COUNTY LEVYING
THE TAX. THE COUNTY TREASURER MAY DISBURSE REVENUES FROM THE TAX ONLY TO:
1. FINANCE CONSTRUCTION OR RENOVATION OF ADULT AND JUVENILE JAIL FACILITIES.
2. MAINTAIN AND OPERATE ADULT AND JUVENILE JAIL FACILITIES.
3. FUND AND IMPLEMENT OTHER PROGRAMS DESIGNED TO REDUCE THE EXPENSE OF ADULT
AND JUVENILE FACILITIES.
E. THE COUNTY SHALL MAINTAIN ITS SUPPORT OF ADULT AND JUVENILE JAIL FACILITIES. IN
THE FIRST FISCAL YEAR THE TAX IS LEVIED UNDER THIS SECTION, THE COUNTY SHALL PAY AN
AMOUNT EQUAL TO THE AMOUNT PAID UNDER SECTION 42-6109.01 IN THE PRECEDING FISCAL
YEAR, ADJUSTED BY THE PERCENTAGE CHANGE IN THE GDP PRICE DEFLATOR FROM THE
PRECEDING FISCAL YEAR. IN EACH FISCAL YEAR THE TAX IS IMPOSED, THE COUNTY SHALL PAY AN
AMOUNT EQUAL TO THE AMOUNT PAID UNDER THIS SUBSECTION IN THE PRECEDING FISCAL YEAR,
ADJUSTED BY THE PERCENTAGE CHANGE IN THE GDP PRICE DEFLATOR FROM THE PRECEDING
FISCAL YEAR. THE COUNTY TREASURER SHALL TRANSFER THAT AMOUNT, WITHOUT FURTHER
AUTHORIZATION, IN TWELVE EQUAL MONTHLY INSTALLMENTS FROM THE COUNTY GENERAL
FUND FOR THE USES PRESCRIBED IN SUBSECTION D OF THIS SECTION.
F. FOR THE PURPOSES OF THIS SECTION:
1. "GDP PRICE DEFLATOR" HAS THE SAME MEANING PRESCRIBED IN SECTION 41-563.
2. "JAIL FACILITY" MEANS A JAIL OR OTHER PLACE OF DETENTION OF PERSONS WHO ARE
CHARGED WITH OR CONVICTED OF A CRIME OR OF PERSONS WHO ARE SUBJECT TO THE
JURISDICTION OF THE JUVENILE COURT, INCLUDING RELATED SUPPORT FACILITIES.
Sec. 3. Conditional repeal
Section 42-6109.02, Arizona Revised Statutes, as added by this act, is repealed from and after
December 31, 2027 if, as of that date, the tax has not been approved by the qualiﬁed voters as provided
by section 42-6109.02, subsection A, Arizona Revised Statutes, as added by this act.
