Bond and Override Elections

This page is here to provide you with information about school district bond and override elections.

2026 Bond and Override Elections:

The Pima County School Superintendent's Office works directly with school districts on their bond and override elections. We help produce the voter information pamphlet, coordinate the flow of information and documents with the Pima County Elections Dept., and are the filing office for pro/con statements.

The following districts have provided notice to seek approval by their voters on the following questions:

  • Prop 423 - Vail Unified   |   12.5% Maintenance and Operations Budget Override Continuation
    Request for Arguments   |   Voter Information Pamphlet
  • Prop 426 - Amphitheater Unified – Sale, Lease, or Exchange of Real Property
    Request for Arguments   |   Voter Information Pamphlet
  • Prop 427 - Pima JTED   |   $250,000,000 Bond Election
    Request for Arguments   |   Voter Information Pamphlet
  • Prop 428 - Sahuarita Unified   |   15% Maintenance and Operations Budget Override Continuation
    Request for Arguments   |   Voter Information Pamphlet
  • Prop 429 - Tanque Verde Unified - 15% Maintenance and Operations Budget Override Continuation
    Request for Arguments   |   Voter Information Pamphlet

Important Dates

- May 7: School district notifies Pima County School Superintendent's Office (PCSSO) of intent to seek special election (bond, override, DAA, site sale) approval in the November election.
- July 6 : School district delivers the signed resolution calling for the election to PCSSO.
- July 15: School district submits Voter Informational Pamphlet content to PCSSO.
- August 5: Deadline for school district to post notices of election and provide affidavit of posting to PCSSO.
- August 7: Deadline for area residents and district governing boards to submit signed PRO/CON arguments to PCSSO.
- October 5: Last Day to register to vote.
- November 3: Election Day

Arguments For/Against School District Special Election(s)

The deadline to submit a statement of argument for/against a special election is Friday, August 7, 2026 at 5:00 p.m. Arguments cannot exceed two hundred words.

A change in Arizona statute § 15-481, now requires the following:

  • Statements submitted by an organization must have the signature of two executive officers.
  • Statements submitted by a political committee must have the signature of either the chairman or treasurer of the committee.
  • Statements submitted by an individual must have the signature of the individual and must be notarized.

Forms for each entity will be made available for download here:

  • Individual Pro/Con Form
  • Organization Pro/Con Form
  • Political Committee Pro/Con Form

The Pima County School Superintendent is required to review all arguments for factual accuracy and make any corrections to the statement.  The Superintendent will not review and correct portions of the arguments that are identified as the author’s opinion. 

To submit a physical paper statement, please download the correct PDF fillable form, print it and sign it and hand deliver or mail it to the following address. Individual statements MUST be notarized or they will not be accepted.
