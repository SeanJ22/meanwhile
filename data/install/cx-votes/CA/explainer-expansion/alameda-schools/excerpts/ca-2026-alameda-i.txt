ANALYSIS BY THE COUNTY COUNSEL FOR THE
COUNTY OF ALAMEDA OF A DUBLIN UNIFIED
SCHOOL DISTRICT SPECIAL PARCEL TAX
MEASURE

Measure I (“Measure”), a Dublin Unified School
District (“District”) special parcel tax measure, seeks
voter approval to authorize the District to levy a nine-
year special parcel tax, beginning on July 1, 2027, at the
initial uniform rate of $144 per parcel per year in the first
applicable tax year, then adjusted each tax year thereafter
by the percentage change in the consumer price index to
account for inflation on the cost of delivering programs and
services supported by the parcel tax.

The District estimates the parcel tax, if passed, will
provide approximately $2,900,000 annually. The District
will use the revenue only for the purposes listed in the
Measure, which include, but are not limited to: attracting
and retaining qualified teachers and staff; maintaining small
class sizes; providing enhanced science, technology, visual
and performing arts, reading, writing, and math programs;
supporting student wellness and academic intervention; and
maintaining instructional materials, classroom technology,
and school library collections.

If passed, as of July 1, 2027, the parcel tax will
supersede and replace the District’s Measure E, an existing
parcel tax passed in 2019. If the measure fails, Measure E
would continue until June 30, 2028, when it will cease to
be in effect.

In addition to properties otherwise exempt from
property taxes, the following people may apply to the
District for exemptions on residential parcels owned and
occupied as a principal residence: persons 65 years or
older; persons receiving Supplemental Security Income
for a disability; and persons receiving Social Security
Disability Insurance Benefits who meet specified income
requirements.

The Measure contains certain accountability provisions,
including requiring the District to deposit parcel tax
proceeds in a separate account, produce an annual report to

DMI-1

the District’s governing board from the District’s chief fiscal
officer detailing the amount of funds collected and expended
and the status of any projects funded by the proceeds, and
conduct an annual financial audit by the District’s governing
board to ensure parcel tax proceeds are spent for educational
purposes. The District’s governing board will also appoint
a citizen oversight committee to review the District’s use
of parcel tax proceeds to ensure that they are expended for
the purposes listed in the Measure.

A school district has the authority to levy special taxes
upon approval by two-thirds of the votes cast on the special
tax proposal. (Cal. Const. art. XIII A, § 4; art. XIII C, §
2; Cal. Gov’t Code §§ 50075-50077, 50079, & 53722.) If
two-thirds of the qualified electors voting on this Measure
do not vote for approval, it will fail, and the District will
not be authorized to levy the special parcel tax.

This Measure is placed on the ballot by the governing
board of the District.

INTRODUCTION

The Dublin Unified School District (“District”) provides
its students with a high-quality and challenging education,
ranging from preschool through adult education across 14
different school sites. Our excellent teachers and outstanding
staff prepare students to excel in college, their careers and
the competitive economy.

Since 2008 our community has supported a local parcel tax
that provides locally-controlled funding for our schools.
This funding, which has not been increased in 18 years,
has helped to maintain the high level of reading, writing,
math and science instruction in our schools and helps retain
high-quality teachers in our classrooms. In addition, this
funding maintains small class sizes that allow teachers to
work more closely with students. All funds received stay
in the District and none of the funds received are used for
District office administrators’ salaries.

The local parcel tax funding is set to expire soon. Without
this local funding, the District would need to make
significant cuts to teachers, academic programs, and student
services, impacting the level of education the District is able
to provide. The District has already made budget reductions
in recent years due to substantial increases in operating
costs. Without a renewal and increase of the District’s
expiring parcel tax, the District will be forced to make more
significant cuts, including teacher layoffs, increases in class
sizes and deep cuts to academic programs.

This proposed Measure provides needed funding to
maintain programs, keep class sizes small, and avoid
diminishing the quality of critical educational services
provided by the District.

The proposed school parcel tax is temporary, will automatically
expire in nine years, and cannot be renewed without voter
approval. The proposed parcel tax Measure includes an
exemption for seniors and low-income people with disabilities,
the only exemptions allowed by law, and are designed to ensure
that the cost is not a burden to those on fixed incomes.

The proposed parcel tax Measure requires fiscal
accountability protections including an independent
Citizens’ Oversight Committee, annual audits and
mandatory public disclosure to provide transparency and
ensure funds are spent as promised.

RATE AND PROCEDURE

Amount of Parcel Tax. Subject to two-thirds approval of
the voters, this local funding measure and its parcel tax
will be in effect for nine (9) years, commencing July 1,
2027. The parcel tax will be levied on each parcel of taxable
real property (except exempt parcels, as described below),
at the initial rate (base rate) of $144 per parcel per year in
the first applicable tax year, and adjusted each tax year
thereafter to account in part for the impact of inflation on
the cost of delivering the educational programs and services
supported by the parcel tax. The inflation adjustment for
each subsequent tax year shall be calculated by multiplying
the prior tax year’s parcel tax rate with the annualized
increase in the San Francisco-Oakland-Hayward Consumer
Price Index released by the Bureau of Labor Statistics for a
12-month period preceding the beginning of the applicable
tax year. In the event such index is no longer published, the
Board shall adopt a comparable index of general price levels
as it shall reasonably determine.

Method of Collection. The parcel tax will be collected
by the Alameda County Tax Collector (the “County Tax
Collector”) at the same time as and along with, and will be
subject to the same penalties as, general ad valorem taxes
collected by said County Tax Collector. The tax and penalty
will bear interest at the same rate as the rate for unpaid
ad valorem property taxes until paid. Any tax levied will
become a lien upon the properties against which taxes are
assessed and collectible as herein provided.

The Board may adopt such additional or supplemental
procedures as it deems necessary or convenient for the
administration of the parcel tax.

Definition of Parcel. A “parcel of taxable real property”
is defined as any unit of real property within the District’s
boundaries that receives a separate tax bill for ad valorem

EFFECT ON EXISTING MEASURE E PARCEL TAX

If approved by the voters, the parcel tax described in this
Measure shall entirely supersede and replace the parcel
taxes authorized by the District’s voters with the approval
of Measure E in 2019, and such Measure E parcel tax shall
cease to be in effect as of July 1, 2027. If this Measure is not
approved by two-thirds of the voters casting ballots hereon,
then the parcel tax authorized by Measure E will continue
in effect only until June 30, 2028, at which time that parcel
tax and Measure E will cease to be in effect, in accordance
with the terms of Measure E.

APPROPRIATIONS LIMIT

To the extent required by the California Constitution and
applicable laws, the appropriations limit for the District will
be adjusted periodically by the aggregate sum collected by
levy of this special tax.

ACCOUNTABILITY AND COMPLIANCE
MEASURES

In accordance with California Government Code sections
50075.1 and 50075.3, and as prescribed by the Board of Trustees
of the District, the following accountability measures shall apply
to the parcel tax levied in accordance with this Measure:

(a) The proceeds of the education parcel tax shall be applied
only to the specific purposes identified under the heading
“PURPOSE OF THE MEASURE,” above.

(b) The proceeds of the special tax shall be deposited into
a fund, which shall be kept separate and apart from other
funds of the District.

(c) No later than January | of each year while the parcel tax
is in effect, the chief fiscal officer of the District shall prepare
and file with the Board of Trustees of the District a report
detailing the amount of funds collected and expended, and the
status of any project, programs, or purposes identified under
the heading “PURPOSE OF THE MEASURE,” above.

(d) The Board will conduct an annual financial audit of
the proceeds of the special tax ensuring funds are spent for
educational purposes.

(ec) The Board shall appoint a Citizens’ Oversight
Committee for the purpose of reviewing the District’s
expenditures of proceeds of the education parcel tax to
ensure they are expended for the purposes set forth under
the heading “PURPOSE OF THE MEASURE,” above, and
to report annually to the Board and the public regarding the
expenditure of such proceeds.

PROTECTION OF FUNDING

Current law forbids any decrease in State or federal funding
to the District resulting from the adoption of a parcel tax.
However, if any such funding is reduced or affected because
of the adoption of this local funding measure, then the
Board may reduce the amount of the special taxes levied as
necessary in order to restore such State or federal funding
and/or maximize the District’s fiscal position for the benefit
of the District’s educational programs. As a result, whether
directly or indirectly, no funding from this Measure may be
taken away by the State or federal governments.
