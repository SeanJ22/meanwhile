ORDINANCE NO. 2026-02
AN ORDINANCE OF THE PEOPLE OF THE CITY OF ALBANY ADDING
SECTION 20.100.070.D.5 TO CHAPTER 20 OF THE ALBANY MUNICIPAL CODE
AUTHORIZING THE CITY COUNCIL TO ADOPT ZONING CHANGES IN THE
SINGLE FAMILY RESIDENTIAL (R-1) ZONED AREAS WITHOUT MAJORITY
NEIGHBORHOOD VOTER APPROVAL ESTABLISHED BY MEASURE D,
ADOPTED IN 1978, AFTER FOLLOWING THE NOTICED PUBLIC HEARING
AND ZONING ORDINANCE AMENDMENT PROCEDURES PRESCRIBED BY
STATE LAW AND THE ALBANY MUNICIPAL CODE
WHEREAS, at the November 7, 1978 General Election, the Albany voters approved
an initiative measure, which is commonly referred to as “Measure D”; and
WHEREAS, among the zoning provisions passed by Measure D, as originally codified
in Section 404.9 of the Albany Municipal Code, is the requirement that any zoning changes in
the single family residential (R-1) zoned areas only be permitted if at least fifty percent (50%)
of the resident voters within three hundred (300) feet of the proposed change indicated their
approval by signing a verified petition to that effect; and
WHEREAS, Program 1.B of the Albany 2023-2031 Housing Element, “Measure D
Repeal,” adopted by City Council in February 2023, provides for repealing provisions of
Measure D requiring majority voter approval through a neighborhood petition prior to zoning
changes to zoning in the R-1 District; and
WHEREAS, Goal 2 of the City Council’s April 2025-March 2027 Strategic Plan is to
“Promote Housing Availability & Quality” and the third objective under this goal is to
“Develop [a] ballot measure to update the City Council’s authority to consider changes to
single family zoning standards” in reference to Measure D; and

SECTION 2. RECITALS. The People of the City of Albany find that all Recitals contained

SECTION 3: CEQA FINDINGS. The adoption of this ordinance is exempt from the
California Environmental Quality Act (“CEQA”) because it can be seen with certainty that
there is no possibility that the adoption of the proposed ordinance will have a significant effect
on the environment. (CEQA Guidelines Section 15061(b)(3).) If approved by the voters, the
proposed ordinance would authorize the City Council to make future zoning changes to the R-
1 zoned areas without compliance with the neighborhood approval by petition requirements
established by 1978 Measure D. However, any such future zoning changes would be preceded
by compliance with CEQA as appropriate. The proposed ordinance, by itself, would not alter
the zoning of any land or effect any changes in applicable zoning/land use regulations.

OFFICIAL NEUTRAL ANALYSIS
CITY OF ALBANY
CITY ATTORNEY’S IMPARTIAL ANALYSIS OF MEASURE “O”
AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE CITY COUNCIL TO ADOPT ZONING
CHANGES IN THE SINGLE FAMILY RESIDENTIAL (R-1) ZONED AREAS
WITHOUT MAJORITY NEIGHBORHOOD VOTER APPROVAL
ESTABLISHED BY 1978 MEASURE D, AFTER FOLLOWING THE NOTICED
PUBLIC HEARING AND ZONING ORDINANCE AMENDMENT
PROCEDURES PRESCRIBED BY STATE LAW AND THE ALBANY
MUNICIPAL CODE
In 1978, Albany voters approved Measure “D”, a City initiative measure which, among
other things, currently provides that any zoning changes to land in the City’s single
family residential (R-1) zoned areas only be permitted if at least fifty percent (50%) of
the resident voters within three hundred (300) feet of the proposed change indicate their
approval by signing a verified petition to that effect.
On June 1, 2026, the City Council voted to place Measure “O” on the November 3,
2026 General Municipal Election ballot. If approved by a majority of Albany voters, this
Measure would amend the Albany Municipal Code to authorize the City to make zoning
changes to land within the R-1 zoned areas from time to time, without
majority neighborhood voter approval, after following noticed public hearing
and zoning ordinance amendment procedures prescribed by State law and the
Albany Municipal Code.
The stated purpose of this Measure is to allow greater flexibility for the City Council to
respond to changing conditions and promote community goals to encourage additional
housing availability and quality, as expressed in the adopted 2023-2031 General Plan
Housing Element, the City Council’s April 2025-2027 Strategic Plan and associated
planning documents.
By placing Measure “O” on the ballot, the City complies with California Elections
Code section 9217 which requires a majority of the Albany voters to approve the repeal
or amendment of a previously voter-approved measure.
A “yes” vote on Measure “O” will authorize the City Council to make zoning changes
within the R-1 zoned areas without majority neighborhood voter approval,
A “no” vote on Measure “O” will not authorize the City Council to make zoning
changes within the R-1 zoned areas without majority neighborhood voter approval.
The above statement is an impartial analysis of Measure “O”. If you desire a copy
of the Measure, please call the City Clerk’s Office at (510) 528-5710 and a copy will
be mailed at no cost to you.
