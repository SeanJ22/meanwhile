FULL TEXT OF MEASURE _____
ORDINANCE NO. 2026-05
AN ORDINANCE AMENDING SUBSECTION 4-5.2 OF SECTION 4-5
OF CHAPTER 4 OF THE CITY OF ALBANY MUNICIPAL CODE TO
ADJUST THE RATE OF THE GENERAL REAL PROPERTY TRANSFER TAX
FROM 1.5% TO BETWEEN 1.0% AND 3.0% OF THE REAL ESTATE VALUE
TRANSFERRED, DEPENDING UPON THE REAL ESTATE PURCHASE
PRICE/VALUE TRANSFERRED
NOW THEREFORE, THE PEOPLE OF THE CITY OF ALBANY DO ORDAIN AS
FOLLOWS:
SECTION 1: AUTHORITY.
This Ordinance is enacted in accordance with the authority granted to cities by Article XI,
Section 7, and Article XIIIC, Section 2 of the California Constitution and the California
Elections Code.
SECTION 2: AMENDMENT TO SUBSECTION 4-5.2 OF THE MUNICIPAL CODE.
Chapter 4, Section 4-5, Subsection 4-5.2 of the City of Albany Municipal Code is
hereby amended to read:
“4-5.2 Imposition of Tax -- Except as Otherwise Exempted.
a. There is hereby imposed a real property transfer tax levied at the following rates of one
hundred fifty hundredths (150/100) of one (1%) percent of the value of consideration, effective
January 1, 20271 on the following:

1. For properties where the value of consideration is in the first tier, the rate shall
be one percent (1.0%) of the value of consideration. For purposes of this Section, the first tier
shall be where the value of consideration for the transaction is below the 25th
percentile of
transactions during the 12 months preceding September 1 of the preceding year, as recorded by
the Alameda County Assessor, rounded to the nearest $100,000 increment.
2. For properties where the value of consideration is in the second tier, the rate
shall be one and one-half percent (1.5%) of the value of consideration. For purposes of this
Section, the second tier shall be where the value of consideration for the transaction is at the
25th
percentile but below the 50th
percentile of transactions during the 12 months preceding
September 1 of the preceding year, as recorded by the Alameda County Assessor, rounded to
the nearest $100,000 increment.
3. For properties where the value of consideration is in the third tier, the rate shall
be two percent (2.0%) of the value of consideration. For purposes of this Section, the third tier
shall be where the value of consideration for the transaction is at the 50th
percentile but below
the 75th
percentile of transactions during the 12 months preceding September 1 of the preceding
year, as recorded by the Alameda County Assessor, rounded to the nearest $100,000 increment.
4. For properties where the value of consideration is in the fourth tier, the rate shall
be two and one-half percent (2.5%) of the value of consideration. For purposes of this Section,
the fourth tier shall be where the value of consideration for the transaction is at the 75th
percentile but below the 90th
percentile of transactions during the 12 months preceding
September 1 of the preceding year, as recorded by the Alameda County Assessor, rounded to
the nearest $100,000 increment.

5. For properties where the value of consideration is in the fifth tier, the rate shall
be three percent (3.0%) of the value of consideration. For purposes of this Section, the fifth
tier shall be where the value of consideration for the transaction is at or above the 90th
percentile
of transactions during the 12 months preceding September 1 of the preceding year, as recorded
by the Alameda County Assessor, rounded to the nearest $100,000 increment.
b. The real property transfer tax shall be levied on the following:
a. 1. All transfers by deeds, instruments, writings, or any other document by which
any lands, tenements, or other interest in real property are sold, assigned, conveyed, transferred,
exchanged or title otherwise changed within the City of Albany; or
b. 2. Any transaction or change in title whereby the form of title to real property is
granted, assigned, changed, transferred or otherwise conveyed to or vested in a person(s),
purchaser(s), or transferee(s) in a different percentage of ownership on title that had been set
forth on the prior deed or document evidencing title.
c. 3. Any transaction or transfer of greater than five (5%) percent interest,
ownership, or control of stock or shares in a corporation, interest in partnerships, or interest in
other legal entities that is transferred or conveyed through the purchase or transfer of corporate
stock, partnerships interest, shares or interest in limited liability companies, or ownership
interest in other legal entities. Such purchase or transfer of such stock, shares, or other interest
shall be considered and shall constitute, for the purposes of imposing a tax, a change of
ownership of property owned by the corporation, limited liability company, partnership or
other legal entity.”

SECTION 3: INCREASE TO APPROPRIATIONS LIMIT.
Pursuant to California Constitution Article XIIIB, the appropriations limit for the City of
Albany is hereby increased by the aggregate sum authorized to be levied by this general tax
for fiscal year 2026/2027 and each year thereafter.
SECTION 4: CALIFORNIA ENVIRONMENTAL QUALITY ACT.
The findings for this Ordinance in compliance with the California Environmental Quality Act
(“CEQA”) are the same as those set forth in City Council Resolution No. 2026-64 calling for
an election on this Ordinance. The CEQA findings set forth in Resolution No. 2026-64 are
incorporated herein by reference.
SECTION 5: SEVERABILITY.
If any provision of this Ordinance or the application thereof to any person or circumstance is
held invalid, the remainder of the Ordinance and the application of such provision to other
persons or circumstances shall not be affected thereby.
SECTION 6: EFFECTIVE DATE.
Pursuant to California Constitution Article XIIIC §(2)(b) and California Elections Code §9217,
this Ordinance shall take effect only if approved by a majority of the eligible voters of the City
of Albany voting at the General Municipal Election to be held on November 3, 2026 and shall
become effective 10 days after the City Council has declared the results of the Election.
