Ballot Measures for General Municipal Election 2026

The City Council approved placement of six measures on the ballot for the November 3, 2026 General Municipal Election.

  • Update to Measure D (1978) to amend the requirement for zoning changes to the single family residential (R-1) zoned areas
  • Special Parcel Tax to update the Street Paving and Storm Drain Special Tax (approved by voters in 2006) 
  • Special Parcel Tax to update the Emergency Medical Services, Advanced Life Support, and Fire Protection Special Tax (approved by voters in 2022)
  • Business License Tax to support City of Albany general services, levying the tax upon business gross receipts instead of number of employees, and updating local tax collection procedures
  • Real Property Transfer Tax to support City of Albany general services, adjusting the rate from 1.5% to between 1.0% and 3.0% of the real estate value transferred, depending upon the real estate purchase price/value transferred
  • Special Parcel Tax to fund the improvement and maintenance of City street trees and streetlighting to enhance local safety and sustainability

The following provides information on the ballot measures including ballot measure question, full text of the measure/ordinance, City Attorney impartial analysis (when available), and instructions on filing arguments. Arguments received either in favor or against the ballot measure, if any, will be uploaded after submission deadline.

Deadline for filing Primary Arguments: Friday, August 14, 2026, 12:00 p.m.
Deadline for filing Rebuttals: Friday, August 21, 2026, 12:00 p.m.

*** For persons wishing to submit primary argument and/or rebuttal argument in favor of or against ballot measures on the November 3, 2026 General Municipal Election, please complete the "Form and Instructions for Ballot Measure Arguments(PDF, 210KB)"

MEASURE O - AMENDING REQUIREMENTS FOR ZONING CHANGES TO R-1 ZONED AREAS

Ballot Question: “Shall the measure authorizing the Albany City Council to amend the residential voter requirements for zoning changes to the R-1 zoned areas of the City established by Measure D (enacted by the Albany voters in 1978), without majority neighborhood voter approval, after following noticed public hearing and zoning ordinance amendment procedures prescribed by State Law and the Albany Municipal Code, be adopted?”
