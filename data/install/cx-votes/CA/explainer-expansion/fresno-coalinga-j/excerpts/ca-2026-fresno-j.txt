EXHIBIT A TO RESOLUTION 4337

ORDINANCE NO.
AN ORDINANCE AMENDING SECTION 102 TO TITLE 3A, CHAPTER 6 TO THE
COALINGA MUNICIPAL CODE TO EXTEND THE SUNSET CLAUSE OF THE ONE-
PERCENT TRANSACTIONS AND USE TAX FOR GENERAL PURPOSES.

The City Council of the City of Coalinga does ordain as follows:

SECTION I. AMENDMENT OF CODE.
Coalinga Municipal Code Title 3, Chapter 6, is hereby amended as follows:

Sec. 3-6A.102 Operative Date.
"Operative date" means the first day of the first calendar quarter commencing more than one

hundred ten (110) days after the adoption of the ordinance from which this chapter is derived, April 1,
2019. The ordinance from which this chapter is derived shall expire April 1, 2629 2039.

Sec. 3-6A.113 — Oversight.
The following measures shall be initiated by the City as mechanisms for citizen oversight of the

Junds collected and expended from revenues generated by this Chapter:

(a) Annual Citizens Advisory Committee: Beginning in the first quarter of each budget year, the
City shall advertise for applicants to a Citizens Advisory Committee to review community
priorities and provide recommendations to the City Council regarding the proposed use of

Measure J revenues during the upcoming fiscal year.
(b) Quarterly Revenue Reports: The City will publish quarterly reports detailing Measure J

revenue collections.
(c) Annual Expenditure Report; An annual report will summarize how Measure J

revenues were allocated and distributed during the previous fiscal year.

SECTION II: STATUTORY AUTHORITY FOR TAX.
This ordinance is adopted pursuant to Revenue and Taxation Code section 7285.9.

SECTION III: ELECTION REQUIRED.
This ordinance shall not become operative unless and until at least a majority of the electors

voting on this measure vote to approve the imposition of the tax at the election to be held on November
3, 2026.

SECTION IV: ANNUAL AUDIT.
By no later than December 31* of each year, the City’s independent auditors shall complete a

financial audit report to include the revenue raised and expended by this tax.

SECTION V: EFFECTIVE DATE.
This ordinance shall take thirty (30) days after certification by the County Elections Office, if

it receives approval by a majority of voters at the general election to be held on November 3, 2026.

RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COUNCIL OF THE CITY OF COALINGA
CALLING FOR A MUNICIPAL ELECTION TO SUBMIT
TO THE VOTERS A LOCAL BALLOT MEASURE EXTENDING THE SUNSET CLAUSE
FOR THE ONE-PERCENT TRANSACTIONS AND USE (SALES) TAX
TO FUND COALINGA CITY SERVICES
INCLUDING POLICE AND FIRE PROTECTION; AND OTHER
GENERAL SERVICES AND REQUESTING THE BOARD OF SUPERVISORS OF FRESNO COUNTY
TO CONSOLIDATE A MUNICIPAL ELECTION ON A LOCAL MEASURE
WITH OTHER ELECTIONS TO BE HELD ON NOVEMBER 3, 2026

BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF THE CITY OF COALINGA AS FOLLOWS:

WHEREAS, the City Council deemed it advisable to submit to the voters a ballot measure to approve
a one-percent (1%) transactions and use (sales) tax to fund city services, including police and fire protection,
and other general services during the 2018 Regular Election; and

WHEREAS, the voters elected to approve the ballot measure and a one-percent (1%) transactions
and use (sales) tax was adopted by the City with an expiration date of April 1, 2029; and

WHEREAS, the City Council has deemed it advisable to submit the renewal of the one-percent (1%)
transactions and use (sales) tax to the voters in order to extend the expiration date to April 1, 2039; and

WHEREAS, the City Council is authorized to request an order that the ballot measure election be
consolidated with other elections to be held on the same day and in the same territory (California Elections

Code 10400 et seq.); and

WHEREAS, it is desirable that said ballot measure election be consolidated with the statewide

general election to be held November 3, 2026; that within the City, the precincts, polling places, and election
officers for the two elections be the same; that the Board of Supervisors canvass the returns of the City ballot
measure election; and said City ballot measure election be held in all respects as if there were only one

election;

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF THE CITY OF COALINGA:

1. Call for Election. The City Council hereby calls a municipal election on November 3, 2026

for the purpose of submitting to the voters a ballot measure to approve extending the sunset clause on the
existing one-percent (1%) transactions and use (sales) tax to fund Coalinga city services, including police,
fire and emergency services; and other general services. A majority vote of the votes cast is required for the

passage of the ballot measure.

2: Request for Measure Designation, The City Council requests that the County designate this
measure as Measure J.

6, Full Text of the Measure. The full text of the "Ordinance of the City of Coalinga Removing

the Expiration of the Transactions and Use Tax to be Administered by the State Board of Equalization,"
removing the sunset clause for the one-percent Transactions and Use (Sales) Tax to fund Coalinga city

website: www.coalinga.com. A statement about how to obtain the full text of the Ordinance will be included

in the Voter Information Pamphlet below the Impartial Analysis.

ré Synopsis of the Measure.
Notice is hereby given that the following measure is to be voted on at the special municipal election

to be held in the City of Coalinga, on Tuesday, the 3rd day of November, 2026.

The proposed tax measure renews the previously voter-approved 1% sales tax (one cent for every
dollar spent) within the City of Coalinga which generates $2,000,000 annually. Adoption of this renewal does
not increase the sale tax above current levels. Funds generated by the revenue of the sales tax will continue
to be allocated to the General Fund and may be used for any general fund purpose. All tax revenue would
continue to be kept by the City and not be able to be spent by the State or County. The renewal will extend
the expiration of the sales tax to April 1, 2039. The tax measure was placed on the ballot by the Coalinga

City Council.

8, Consolidation of Election. The City Council requests that the Board of Supervisors of the
County of Fresno, State of California order the consolidation of the election in the City of Coalinga to be held
on November 3, 2026, with any other election(s) to be held on the same date, under California Election Code

Sections 10400 et seq.
