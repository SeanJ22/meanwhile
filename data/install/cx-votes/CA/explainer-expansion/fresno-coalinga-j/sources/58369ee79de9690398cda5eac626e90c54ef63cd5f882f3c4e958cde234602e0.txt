Re: City of Coalinga November 3, 2026 Election EPUTY

City of Coalinga Sales Tax Measure Impartial Analysis

Dear County Clerk:

Below is the impartial analysis from our office as City Attorney for the City of Coalinga
in reference to the City of Coalinga’s Tax Measure referenced above. Please let us know if you

have any questions.

The proposed tax measure extends the length of the current sales tax within
the City of Coalinga of 1% (one cent for every dollar spent) to 2039. Funds
generated by the revenue will continue to be allocated to the General Fund, where
the residents voice their preference of spending to the City Council. Funds may
continue to be used for any general fund purpose. The renewal will extend the
expiration of the sales tax to April 1, 2039. The tax measure was placed on the ballot

by the Coalinga City Council.

If you have any questions, please do not hesitate to contact us.
