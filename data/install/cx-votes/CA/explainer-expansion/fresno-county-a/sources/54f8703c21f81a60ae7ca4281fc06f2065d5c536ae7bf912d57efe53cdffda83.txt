FISCAL IMPACT STATEMENT JUL 24 2026
TRANSIENT OCCUPANCY TAX

By K
- « vutPUTY

This ballot measure asks the electorate to impose a transient occupancy tax on occupants of

hotels, motels, short-term rentals, and other lodging within the Fresno County unincorporated

areas at a rate of twelve percent (12%). If this measure passes, the County of Fresno estimates

the annual revenue from this tax to be $4,500,000. The revenue from this tax will go toward

Fresno County’s general fund and may be used for any general governmental purpose. These

include public safety, wildfire/disaster response, 911, road maintenance, behavioral and public

health, water infrastructure, park, and other public services.

The one-time cost of implementing this tax, including establishing a collection system and
registering operators, is estimated to be $50,000. Ongoing administrative costs, including staff
time for collection and enforcement, are estimated to be $130,000 annually. Ongoing annual
costs could decrease in subsequent years once the program is fully implemented, including
establishment of the collection system, operator registration processes, and related notices.

If this measure passes, the transient occupancy room tax will be effective January 1, 2027 and
would continue in effect until changed or repealed.

If this measure does not pass, there will be no fiscal impact as the County of Fresno does not
currently have a transient occupancy tax.
