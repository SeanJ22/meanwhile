NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the City Council of the City of Fowler
that:

Section 1. Findings.
The foregoing recitals are true and correct and are incorporated herein by this reference.

Section 2. Approval and Submission of Ordinance.

Pursuant to Revenue and Taxation Code Section 7285.9, the City Council approves the attached
"City of Fowler Public Safety, Street and Infrastructure Maintenance, and Essential City Services
Transactions and Use Tax Ordinance", which is attached as Exhibit A and orders that it be

submitted to the qualified voters of the City.

Section 3. Election.

A General Municipal Election shall be held on Tuesday, November 3, 2026, for the purpose of
submitting the Ordinance to the qualified electors of the City.

Section 4. Ballot Measure.

The City Clerk is directed to submit the Ordinance and ballot question (“Measure”) in
substantially the following form:

"To fund general City services, such as local police, fire protection, emergency response,
repair streets and roads, improve aging infrastructure, maintain parks and public facilities, and
other general City services, shall the measure, establishing a one-half cent (‘2¢) transactions and
use tax providing approximately $300,000.00 annually until ended by voters and public reporting

consistent with State law as required, be adopted?"

YES
NO

The complete text of the Measure shall be printed in the Fresno County Voter Information Guide
and be available for public inspection in the City Clerk’s office.

Section 5. Voter Approval Requirement.

The vote requirement for the Measure to pass is a majority (50% + 1) of the votes cast.

Section 6. Consolidation.

The City Council requests that the Fresno County Board of Supervisors consolidate the General
Municipal Election with the statewide election to be conducted on November 3, 2026. The
election will be held and conducted in accordance with the provisions of law regulating the
elections, including, but not limited to, Elections Code Section 10418. The City of Fowler
recognizes that additional costs will be incurred by the County of Fresno by reason of this
consolidation and agrees to reimburse the County of Fresno for any costs. The City Clerk shall
file a certified copy of this Resolution with the Fresno County Board of Supervisors and San

Fresno County Elections Department.
