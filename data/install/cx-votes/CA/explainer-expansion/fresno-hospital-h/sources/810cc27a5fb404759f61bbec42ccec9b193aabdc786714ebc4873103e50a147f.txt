SIERRA KINGS HEALTH CARE DISTRICT
NOVEMBER 2026 MEASURE

(IMPARTIAL ANALYSIS BY COUNTY COUNSEL PURSUANT TO
SECTION 9313 OF THE ELECTIONS CODE)

This measure seeks voter approval allowing the board of directors (“Board”) of the Sierra
Kings Health Care District (“District”) to enter into a new lease and option to purchase
agreement with Reedley Community Hospital (“RCH”), an affiliate of Adventist Health
System, for the lease and possible sale of the hospital facility and parcel located at 372
West Cypress Avenue, Reedley, California and an adjacent parcel (“New Lease’).

In 2011, the District's voters approved the original lease and option to purchase
agreement with RCH (“Original Lease”). The New Lease will replace the Original Lease
and will be effective the first day of the first calendar month following the date on which
the New Lease is approved by the District's voters. The New Lease will include a 20-year
initial term and two five-year renewal terms for the continued operation and maintenance

of the hospital.

The District is a local public health care district, organized under California’s Local Health
Care District Law. California law states that a health care district may “transfer, at fair
market value, any part of its assets to one or more corporations to operate and maintain
the assets.” A transfer is deemed to be at fair market value if a qualified independent
consultant/appraiser determines that the consideration to be paid is “fair and reasonable.”
Before transferring a substantial portion of its assets to a private party, a health care
district's board of directors must submit to the voters of the district a measure approving
the transfer. This measure implements the requirement to seek voter approval.

The measure was placed on the ballot by the District's Board. A “yes” vote supports the
passage of the measure; a “no” vote opposes passage of the measure. A majority vote
(more than 50% of votes cast) is required to pass the measure. The measure makes no

change to existing law.
