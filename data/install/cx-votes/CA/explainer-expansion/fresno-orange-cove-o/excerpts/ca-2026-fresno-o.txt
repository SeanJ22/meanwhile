CITY OF ORANGE COVE.

WHEREAS, Resolution No. 2026-28 amends Resolution 2026-12, the Orange Cove Police
Department provides essential public safety services to the citizens of Orange Cove; and

WHEREAS, the City General Fund lacks funds to hire the police officers needed to maintain
the necessary level for police protection in the City; and

WHEREAS, State law (including Government Code Section 53978 et seq.) authorizes a
special tax for the purpose of providing police services; and

WHEREAS, the voters of the City of Orange Cove have previously approved a special parcel
tax to help fund police and fire services codified by City Ordinance No. 396; and

WHEREAS, the ballot measure relating to Ordinance No. 396 to provide for the continued
funding of police and fire services was rejected by voters in March 2024; and

WHEREAS, the special parcel tax relating to Ordinance No. 396 was sunset in November
2024;and

WHEREAS, the City provides recreation services and performs park maintenance for the
benefit of the citizens of Orange Cove; and

WHEREAS, the City General Fund lacks funds to offer Recreation and Park Maintenance
services at a level of service the City and its citizens require; and

WHEREAS, the City Council held a Council Meeting on April 22, 2026, at which the City
Council considered a staff report, presentation, public testimony, and deliberated on the matter
regarding submitting a ballot measure to the voters concerning the imposition of a special
parcel tax for Police, Recreation, and Park Maintenance services; and

WHEREAS, the City Council has determined that a special parcel tax is necessary to generate
the financial resources necessary for maintaining Police, Recreation, and Park Maintenance

services as set forth above; and

Measure _ Impartial Analysis

Measure _ proposes to raise revenue for police, park maintenance, and recreation services
through a parcel tax. Measure _ has been placed on the ballot by the majority vote of the City of Orange
Cove’s governing body. Measure _ proposes to continue the police, park maintenance, and recreation
services parcel tax without expiration, until repealed by the voters. Measure _ is a proposed parcel tax
on each parcel of real property in the City. The tax would be paid in addition to current State and local
property taxes and would be collected at the same time and in the same manner as the existing real
property taxes. Measure _ proposes a parcel tax of $125 a unit on each single-family residential parcel
within the City; $140 on each multifamily unit within the City; $800 per acre for each nonresidential
parcel; and $400 per acre for each undeveloped parcel. Measure _ will generate approximately
$460,907 annually to fund police, park maintenance, and recreation services. Measure _ proposes an

annual 3% increase in the parcel tax to account for inflation.

Measure _ proposes that the parcel tax be used to fund police, park maintenance, and
recreation services. Seventy percent of the proposed Measure _ will be used to fund police services.

ORDINANCE NO. 403 
AN ORDINANCE OF THE CITY OF ORANGE COVE, CALIFORNIA AMENDING THE 
ORANGE COVE MUNICIPAL CODE BY AMENDING CHAPTER 3.34 OF TITLE 3 BY 
EXPANDING THE SPECIAL PARCEL TAX FOR POLICE, PARK, AND RECREATION 
SERVICES AND ADJUSTING THE SPECIAL TAX RATES THERETO 
 
The people of the City of Orange Cove do ordain as follows: 
 
SECTION 1: The title of Chapter 3.34 of the Orange Cove Municipal Code is hereby 
amended as follows: 
CHAPTER 3.34 – POLICE, PARK, AND RECREATION SERVICES SPECIAL 
PARCEL TAX 
SECTION 2: Section 3.34.010 of the Orange Cove Municipal Code is hereby amended 
to read as follows: 
3.34.010 – Imposition of police, park and recreation services special parcel tax.  
 
A special tax to fund police and recreation services and park maintenance is hereby 
imposed on every parcel of land in the City of Orange Cove. The tax shall be imposed each fiscal 
year in the amounts permitted by this chapter. The tax imposed by this chapter shall be a tax 
upon each parcel of property within the city and the tax shall not be measured by the value of the 
property.  
SECTION 3: Section 3.34.020 of the Orange Cove Municipal Code is hereby amended 
to read as follows: 
3.34.020 – Maximum tax amounts.  
 
The maximum annual amount of the tax imposed on each parcel pursuant to this chapter 
shall be determined as follows: 
1. A tax of one hundred twenty-five dollars ($125.00) shall be imposed on each single-
family residential parcel within the city. A “single-family residential parcel” shall mean a 
parcel zoned to accommodate single-family residences. 
2. A tax of one hundred forty dollars ($140.00) shall be imposed on each multi-family 
residential unit within the city. A “multi-family residential unit” shall mean a building or 
portion of a building designed for or occupied by one family which is part of a multi-
family residential unit parcel. A “multi-family residential unit parcel” shall mean a parcel 
zoned for a building or those portions thereof that accommodate or is intended to 
accommodate two or more residential units. 
3. A tax of eight hundred dollars ($800.00) per acre shall be imposed on each non-
residential parcel within the city. A “non-residential parcel” shall mean any parcel that is 


ORIGINAL PDF PAGE 47 — NATIVE PDF TEXT
 
2
not zoned for single-family or multi-family residences, but rather is zoned for 
commercial, light manufacturing, heavy manufacturing, or agricultural purposes. 
4. A tax of four hundred dollars ($400.00) per acre shall be imposed on each undeveloped 
parcel within the city. An “undeveloped parcel” shall mean any parcel that lacks man-
made structures, significant physical alterations, or functional utility connections and is 
vacant. 
5. All taxes imposed on each parcel pursuant to this chapter shall be subject to an automatic 
annual inflation of three percent (3.00%).  
SECTION 4: Section 3.34.030 of the Orange Cove Municipal Code is hereby amended to 
read as follows: 
3.34.030 – Exemptions.  
1. Parcels owned by the city, federal or state government or any other public agency and 
parcels owned by any public or private entity that are specifically exempted from a parcel 
tax enacted as a special tax under applicable state or federal statute or regulation shall be 
exempt from the imposition of the special parcel tax set forth in this chapter. 
2. Property owners claiming an exemption under subsection (A) above shall file proof of 
exemption on a form prescribed by the city manager prior to March 31 prior to the first 
fiscal year for which the exemption is sought. 
SECTION 5: Section 3.34.040 of the Orange Cove Municipal Code is hereby amended to 
read as follows: 
3.34.040 – Administrative determinations; appeal procedures.  
1. The records of the Fresno County Assessor as of March 1 of each year shall be used to 
determine each parcel for the calculation of the tax applicable to that parcel in the 
following fiscal year.  
2. The city manager, or his or her authorized designee, shall administer the special parcel 
tax under this chapter. 
3. Appeals of any determination of the city manager shall be submitted in writing to the city 
clerk within thirty (30) days of the date of the notice of the determination. The city 
council shall consider the appeal and issue a decision to the appellant in writing no later 
than June 30 of the fiscal year prior to the imposition of the tax. 
SECTION 6: Section 3.34.050 of the Orange Cove Municipal Code is hereby amended to 
read as follows: 
3.34.050 – Accountability measures.  
1. Independent Oversight Committee. The city council shall establish an independent 
oversight committee to review the annual report, as described below, on issues relating to 
the use of the special tax proceeds set forth in this chapter. The oversight committee will

independently review the use and expenditure of the tax proceeds and shall present an 
annual report to the city council. 
a. The oversight committee shall consist of five members, each shall be a resident of 
the City of Orange Cove, who shall serve without compensation. The members 
shall be nominated by the city manager following a review of their qualifications 
and appointed by city council approval. 
b. Members shall serve two-year terms, and each member shall serve until a 
successor is duly appointed and confirmed. Members are limited to a maximum of 
two consecutive terms. The expiration date of all terms shall be May 1. Any 
vacancy shall be filled for the remainder of the unexpired term. The oversight 
committee shall select a chair from among its members annually. The chair will 
serve a one-year term with the option of reappointment for an additional one-year 
term.  
c. The oversight committee shall meet at least twice annually with additional 
meetings convened as necessary and as determined by the chair, and shall set an 
attendance policy for its members. 
2. Annual Review Report. An annual review report shall be prepared by the oversight 
committee with support from the city manager, or his designee, setting forth the amount 
of revenues received under the special tax and the amount and purpose of disbursements. 
The purpose is to assure accountability and the proper disbursement of the proceeds of 
the special tax. The annual review report shall be presented to the city council not later 
than January 31 following each fiscal year in which the tax is collected. The annual 
review report shall be filed with and maintained in the city clerk's office. 
SECTION 7: Section 3.34.060 of the Orange Cove Municipal Code is hereby amended to 
read as follows: 
3.34.060 – Collection with property taxes.  
The special tax imposed by this chapter shall be due in the same manner, on the same 
dates, and subject to the same penalties and interest as established by law for other charges and 
taxes fixed and collected by the County of Fresno on behalf of the City of Orange Cove. The 
special tax imposed by this chapter, together with all penalties and interest thereon, shall 
constitute a lien upon the parcel upon which it is levied until it has been paid, and shall constitute 
a personal obligation of the owners of the parcel on the date the tax is due.   
SECTION 8: Section 3.34.070 of the Orange Cove Municipal Code is hereby amended to 
read as follows: 
3.34.070 – Term.  
The special tax adopted under this chapter shall continue indefinitely, until otherwise 
appealed by the electorate of the City of Orange Cove.
