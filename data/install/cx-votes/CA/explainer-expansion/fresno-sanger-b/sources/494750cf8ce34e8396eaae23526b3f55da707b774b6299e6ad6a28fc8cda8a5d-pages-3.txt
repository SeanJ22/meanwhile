SECTION 5. Passage of Measure.

The measure shall be deemed approved if it receives the affirmative vote of a majority of the voters
voting on the measure. If approved by the voters, the ordinance shall be enacted in accordance
with applicable law and shall take effect as provided therein.

SECTION 6. General Tax.

The measure is submitted as a general tax measure. Revenues from the tax shall be deposited in
the City's General Fund and may be used for any lawful municipal purpose. The measure does not

legally restrict the use of revenues to any specific purpose.
SECTION 7. Consolidation with Statewide General Election.

Pursuant to Elections Code sections 10400, 10403, and 10418, the City Council hereby requests
that the Fresno County Board of Supervisors consolidate the General Municipal Election called by
this Resolution with the statewide general election to be held on Tuesday, November 3, 2026. The
City Council further requests that the election on the measure be held in all respects as if there
were only one election, and that the Fresno County Clerk/Registrar of Voters canvass the returns
and certify the results to the City Council. The City Council hereby specifically acknowledges
that the election will be held and conducted per Elections Code 10418.

SECTION 8. Services of County Elections Official.

The City Council hereby requests that the Fresno County Clerk/Registrar of Voters provide all
services necessary to conduct the election on the measure, including but not limited to publication

of notices to the extent required or customary, preparation and mailing of voter information
materials, printing of ballots, furnishing vote centers and election officers, tabulation of ballots,

canvass of returns, and all other election services necessary or appropriate for the conduct of the
consolidated election.
