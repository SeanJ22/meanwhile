District Service Areas and Boundaries
The Grossmont-Cuyamaca Community College District, which includes Grossmont College
and Cuyamaca colleges, proudly serves students from all over San Diego County, with
primary service area boundaries in San Diego's East County.
The primary boundary includes the cities of El Cajon, La Mesa, Lemon Grove and Santee,
and the following unincorporated communities:
Cuyamaca
Dehesa
Descanso
Dulzura
Eucalyptus Hills

Jacumba

Lakeside
