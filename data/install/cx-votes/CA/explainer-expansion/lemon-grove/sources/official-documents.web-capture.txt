L17@P0: C. The City further finds that complaints from Lemon Grove residents and animal control

L19@P0: commercially bred birds.
L20@P0: D. This chapter does not regulate poultry or birds sold for agricultural or backyard flock
L21@P0: purposes.
L22@P0: E. The City further finds that adopting and extending the California rescue-only model to birds
L23@P0: will reduce abandonment, protect consumers, and advance the humane treatment of companion
L24@P0: birds within the City of Lemon Grove.

L55@P1: A. Consistent with the rescue-only sourcing requirements established under California law for
