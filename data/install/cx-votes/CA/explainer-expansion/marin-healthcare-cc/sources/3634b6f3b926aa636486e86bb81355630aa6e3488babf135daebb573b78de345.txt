OFFICIAL IMPARTIAL ANALYSIS
Impartial analysis by County Counsel of Measure CC
Marin Healthcare District
Measure CC
This Measure was placed on the ballot following submission of a voter initiative petition containing the number of signatures required by law.
Because Measure CC was placed on the ballot through the initiative process, it will be adopted if approved by a majority of the votes cast in this election. If it is adopted, the Marin Healthcare District will levy a parcel tax beginning the first full fiscal year after the effective date. The Measure will remain in effect for thirty years from the beginning of the parcel tax. The rate of the parcel tax shall be $0.14 per square foot of improved building area, which is defined as the area on a parcel of taxable real property on which there exists any building. The Marin Healthcare District estimates revenue from this Measure will average approximately $12,400,000 annually.
The revenue raised by this parcel tax must be placed in a special account and can only be used for the purposes stated in this Measure. The stated purposes for the funds raised by this special tax are the expansion and modernization of intensive care, trauma, triage, and hospital facilities, as well as additional capacity for the emergency room and patient rooms. The funds may also be spent to hire and retain doctors and nurses, with no funds for administrator salaries.
