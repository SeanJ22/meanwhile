Maps and geographic information systems (GIS)
Maps and geographic information systems (GIS)
Explore maps and location-based data about zoning, land records, supervisor districts, and more.
The Marin County Geographic Information Systems Open Data program provides location-based local government data.
Here you’ll find:
Schools and libraries
Environment and natural hazards
Public safety
Transportation and trails
Boundaries and districts
Parcels and addresses
Aerial and elevation data
Vegetation and land cover data
Maps are for informational purposes only and are not survey accurate. You'll need to disable popup blockers to use these applications.

MarinMap
MarinMap is a consortium of local agencies across the county. They build and use a shared mapping system for their day-to-day operations.
