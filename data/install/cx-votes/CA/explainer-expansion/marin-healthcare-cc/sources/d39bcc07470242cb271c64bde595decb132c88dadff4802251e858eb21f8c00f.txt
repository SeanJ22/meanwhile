Marin LAFCo GIS Map Viewer
Click here for Marin LAFCo Map Viewer
Click here for Marin LAFCo GEO Hub
In partnership with the firm of Cinquini & Passarino, Inc., Marin LAFCo now offers members of the public access to this GIS mapping tool that offers data specific to Marin LAFCo, including (but not limited to) jurisdictional boundaries and spheres of influence of local public agencies under Marin LAFCo purview (as well as some public agencies that are outside of Marin LAFCo purview). In addition, all of the mapping layers are publicly available for download on the Marin LAFCo Geo Hub which can be accessed above. Any questions on the use of the map viewer or the GEO Hub can be submitted to staff@marinlafco.org.
