OFFICIAL IMPARTIAL ANALYSIS
Impartial analysis by County Counsel of Measure EE
Stinson Beach County Water District
Measure EE
This Measure was placed on the ballot by the Board of Directors of the Stinson Beach County Water District.
Article XIIIB of the California Constitution restricts governmental spending of tax proceeds by setting an annual spending limit, also known as an appropriations limit. The Constitution authorizes voters to change this spending limit for a maximum period of four years at a time. The District Board of Directors has placed Measure EE on the ballot to submit to the voters the question of whether the District’s current appropriations limit should continue for another four years.
In general, if a jurisdiction receives more funds than allowed under its appropriations limit, it cannot spend the excess amount. If approved, this measure would continue the District’s authority to spend tax revenue above its base appropriations limit through fiscal year 2029-30 and would allow the District to continue meeting its current operating costs, which are more than the limits otherwise allowed under the constitutional limit. If this measure fails, the District’s current spending limits will be reduced, which would reduce the funds available to continue the District’s current levels of service. Measure EE does not authorize the District to impose a new assessment or increase any property tax rate.
