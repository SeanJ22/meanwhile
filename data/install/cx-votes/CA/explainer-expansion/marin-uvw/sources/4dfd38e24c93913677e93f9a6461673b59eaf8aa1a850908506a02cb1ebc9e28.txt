OFFICIAL IMPARTIAL ANALYSIS
Impartial analysis by Town Attorney of Measure U
The Town Council of the Town of Ross adopted Resolution No. 2637 on July 9, 2026 placing Measure U on the November 3, 2026 ballot. Measure U would adopt Ordinance No. 736, amending Chapter 3.30 of the Ross Municipal Code to continue an existing special tax that funds paramedic and pre-hospital emergency medical services provided by the Ross Valley Paramedic Authority. The ordinance authorizes the paramedic services tax to remain in effect for four more years, expiring on June 30, 2031. As a special tax, two-thirds of the voters voting on the tax must vote in its favor for the paramedic services tax to be continued and increased.
The Town’s existing paramedic services tax is $103.50 and expires June 30, 2027. Measure U would continue the tax for four additional fiscal years. For fiscal year 2027- 2028, the measure sets the rate at $107.50 per dwelling unit on a residential parcel, and $107.50 per 1,000 square feet of structure on a parcel in non-residential use. Under the existing tax, the non-residential rate is applied per 1,500 square feet, this measure would change that basis to 1,000 square feet.
The measure authorizes the Town Council to approve an annual increase of not more than $4.00, up to a maximum of $118.00 in fiscal year 2030-2031. Under the existing tax the maximum annual increase is $3.00. Any increase is not automatic, the Council must approve the rate each year by resolution.
Proceeds of the tax may be used only for paramedic and pre-hospital emergency medical care services. The measure also increases the Town’s appropriations limit under Article XIII B, Section 6 of the California Constitution by the amount of the tax proceeds.
A “YES” vote is a vote to continue the paramedic services special tax for four additional years on the terms stated above.
A “NO” vote is a vote not to continue the tax, which would expire on June 30, 2027.
