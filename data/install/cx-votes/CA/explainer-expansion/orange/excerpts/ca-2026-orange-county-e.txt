Shall an ordinance to amend the Laguna Beach Municipal Code by enacting a limit on consecutive years of service by
members of the City Council to a combined total of two four-year consecutive terms, with the ability to again serve as a
Councilmember after a lapse of service of at least two years, be adopted?

Full Text of Measure E
                                                                  City of Laguna Beach
        AN ORDINANCE OF THE CITY OF LAGUNA BEACH, CALIFORNIA, ADDING A NEW SECTION 2.04.080 TO CHAPTER 2.04 TO
        TITLE 2 OF THE LAGUNA BEACH MUNICIPAL CODE RELATING TO TERM LIMITS, SUBJECT TO APPROVAL OF A MAJORITY OF THE
        ELECTORS VOTING ON THE MEASURE.
THE PEOPLE OF THE CITY OF LAGUNA BEACH DO ORDAIN AS FOLLOWS:
        Section 1. Amendment of the Laguna Beach Municipal Code.
Pursuant to California Government Code section 36502(b) and subject to the approval of a majority of the voters of the City of Laguna Beach, the
Laguna Beach Municipal Code is hereby amended to add a new Section 2.04.080, Term Limits, to Chapter 2.04, City Council, to Title 2, Administrative
and Personnel, to read as follows:
        “§ 2.04.080. Term Limits.
        (A) No person shall be eligible to serve as a member of the City Council for more than two consecutive four-year terms following the adoption
        of this ordinance commencing with the, and including those persons elected to serve as a member of the City Council in the November 3, 2026
        election. Notwithstanding the foregoing, any person who is appointed by the City Council or elected to fill a vacant office of a member of the
        City Council for the balance of a four-year term, but serves less than two years in that office, shall be eligible for nomination and election for
        two consecutive four-year terms thereafter.
        (B) Any person who shall be ineligible for election, appointment, or further services as a member of the City Council due to the limitation set
        forth in subsection A of this section, shall again be eligible for election or appointment as a member of the City Council, and a new period of
        consecutive years of service shall commence, provided that at least two years has or will have elapsed between the most recent date of such
        person’s prior service as a member of the City Council and the effective date of such person’s most recent election or appointment to such
        office.
        (C) The phrase “two years” as used in subsection B of this section shall mean the period between the date of the declaration of the results
        of any general municipal election held in November of an even numbered year and the date of the declaration of the results of any general
        municipal election held in November of any immediately preceding or immediately succeeding even-numbered year, even though such period
        may be more or less than an actual period of two years.”
        Section 2. Voter Approval, Prospective Application, and Effective Date
Except as provided in section 3 below, this ordinance shall take effect if approved by a majority of the eligible voters of the City of Laguna Beach voting
on the measure in the general municipal election held on Tuesday, November 3, 2026, and shall only apply prospectively beginning with the terms of
members of the City Council that start after the November 3, 2026 election. If so approved, this ordinance becomes effective 10 days after the City
Council declares and certifies the results of the election by resolution.
        Section 3. Severability
If any provision of this ordinance or the application thereof to any person or circumstance is held invalid, such invalidity shall not affect other provisions
or applications of the ordinance which can be given effect without the invalid provision or application, and to this end the provisions of this ordinance
are severable. The People hereby declare that it would have adopted this ordinance irrespective of the invalidity of any particular portion thereof.
        Section 4. Publication
The City Clerk is hereby directed to publish this ordinance at least once, within 15 days after the election results are certified, in a newspaper of general
circulation published and circulated in the City of Laguna Beach.
        Section 5. Attestation of Ordinance
The Mayor is hereby authorized to attest to the adoption of this Ordinance by the People voting thereon on November 3, 2026, by signing where
indicated below.

                                                                   Impartial Analysis
                                                                  City of Laguna Beach
                                                                        Measure E
         The City of Laguna Beach is governed by a five-member City Council whose members are elected by Laguna Beach voters to four-year terms
of office. Currently, the City of Laguna Beach does not have term limits on elected City Council office.
     Measure E was placed on the ballot by a citizens’ initiative petition. On December 9, 2025, the City Council voted to place Measure E on the
November 3, 2026 General Municipal Election, in compliance with the California Elections Code.
        If approved by a majority of voters (50% +1), Measure E would add Section 2.04.080 to the Laguna Beach Municipal Code to establish term
limits on City Council service. Measure E would provide that no person may serve more than two consecutive four-year terms as a Council Member. In
the event a person is appointed or elected to fill a vacant City Council office for less than two years of a four-year term, that individual will be eligible
to serve two consecutive four-year terms thereafter.
        Any person whose Council Member service has reached the term limits described above is not eligible to serve again as a Council Member,
whether by appointment or election, for two years following the conclusion of that person’s last term of office as a Council Member. The term “two
years” is defined, for purposes of re-eligibility for office, to mean the period between the date of the declaration of the results of any general municipal
election held in November of an even-numbered year and the date of declaration of the results of any general municipal election in November of any
immediately preceding or immediately succeeding even-numbered year. After this two-year period, a person may serve another two consecutive four-
year City Council terms, as described above.
        Measure E will only apply to City Council terms starting as of the November 3, 2026 General Municipal Election. State law does not allow local
term limits to apply retroactively to prior terms. Therefore, any four-year term for any City Council office elected at the November 5, 2024 General
Municipal Election or at any prior election, or any prior-appointed or prior-elected partial City Council terms, will not count toward these term limits.
        A “yes” vote on Measure E will approve the establishment of City Council term limits as described above.
       A “no” vote on Measure E will not approve the establishment of City Council term limits as described above and the City of Laguna Beach will
continue not to have City Council term limits.
