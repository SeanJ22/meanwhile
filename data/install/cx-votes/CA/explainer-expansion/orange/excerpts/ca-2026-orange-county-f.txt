Shall the measure limiting city council members to two consecutive terms in office with a mandatory four-year out of
office period before being eligible to serve for another two consecutive terms in office, be adopted?

Full Text of Measure F
                                                                  City of Lake Forest
                                                                  ORDINANCE NO. __
               AN ORDINANCE OF PEOPLE OF THE CITY OF LAKE FOREST, CALIFORNIA, AMENDING SECTION 2.04.080 OF THE
               LAKE FOREST MUNICIPAL CODE TO CHANGE THE TERMS OF OFFICE ALLOWED FOR CITY COUNCILMEMBERS
               FROM TWO TERMS MAXIMUM TO TWO CONSECUTIVE TERMS WITH ADDITIONAL TERMS ELIGIBLE FOLLOWING
               A FOUR YEAR BREAK IN SERVICE
THE PEOPLE OF THE CITY OF LAKE FOREST DO ORDAIN AS FOLLOWS:
SECTION 1. Municipal Code Amendment. Section 2.04.080 of the Lake Forest Municipal Code is hereby amended to read as follows:
       Sec. 2.04.080 – Term Limits
       (a) No person shall be eligible to serve in the office of City Council member or Mayor for more than two consecutive four-year terms. Any
           person may hold office as a member of the City Council or Mayor for an additional two consecutive terms if he or she does not hold office
           as a member of the City Council or Mayor for four or more consecutive years immediately following the conclusion of each two consecutive
           terms of office as a member of the City Council or Mayor. There is no limit to the number of times a person may serve two consecutive terms
           with the requisite four year break in service.
       (b) If a person serves for at least two years of a term, that person for purposes of subsection (a) of this section shall be considered to have
           served a full four-year term.
SECTION 2. Severability. If any section, subsection, subdivision, paragraph, sentence, clause, or phrase added by this Ordinance, or any part thereof, is
for any reason held to be unconstitutional or invalid or ineffective by any court of competent jurisdiction, such decision shall not affect the validity of
effectiveness of the remaining portions of this Ordinance or any part thereof.
SECTION 3. Effective Date. This Ordinance shall become effective only upon adoption by a majority of the people voting on the measure to adopt this
Ordinance at the general municipal election on November 3, 2026. This Ordinance shall be considered as adopted upon the date that the vote on this
Ordinance is declared by the City Council for the City of Lake Forest, and shall go into effect 10 days after that date.
SECTION 4. Execution. The Mayor is hereby authorized to attest to the adoption of this Ordinance by the voters of the City of Lake Forest by signing
where indicated below.



                                                                     Impartial Analysis
                                                                     City of Lake Forest
                                                                         Measure F
Lake Forest Municipal Code Section 2.04.080 currently prohibits any person from serving as a Lake Forest City Council Member or Mayor for more than
two four-year terms. The City’s current term limits have been in place since the passage of Measure S in 2018.
Measure F amends Section 2.04.080 to prohibit any person from serving more than two four-year terms on the Lake Forest City Council unless he or
she does not hold office for four or more consecutive years immediately following the conclusion of each consecutive two terms of office. After the
required four-year break, a person may again serve up to two four-year terms. Each full City Council term is four years. Measure F was placed on the
ballot by the Lake Forest City Council.
If Measure F is approved, the new four-year break between consecutive four-year terms would apply to all new or re-elected City Council Members
beginning with the 2026 election. Adoption of Measure F would not increase the number of consecutive terms available to current City Council
Members, as the current two term limit established by Section 2.04.080 of the Lake Forest Municipal Code will continue to apply. Once a City Council
Member has served two full four-year terms on the City Council, the two-term limit will apply and he or she would no longer be eligible to serve on the
City Council without a four or more consecutive years break in service.
A “Yes” vote on Measure F will approve the Measure and prohibit City Council Members or the Mayor from serving more than two four-year terms
without a four more consecutive years break in service.
A “No” vote on Measure F will not approve the Measure and will prohibit the City Council members or Mayor from serving more than two four-year
terms.
Measure F requires simple majority approval of the voters to pass.
