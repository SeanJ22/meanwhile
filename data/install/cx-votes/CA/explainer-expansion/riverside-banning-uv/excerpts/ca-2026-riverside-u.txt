SECTION 3. FINDINGS FOR APPROVAL OF ZC 20-3502. 
BMC Section 17.116.040 provides that upon receipt of the Planning Commission's 
recommendation, the City Council may approve, approve with modifications, or 
disapprove the proposed amendment based upon its findings. Amendments to the Zoning 
Ordinance shall be adopted by ordinance. Further, BMC Section 17.116.050 provides 
that an amendment to the Zoning Ordinance may be adopted only if the following findings 
are made. The City Council hereby finds and determines as follows: 
A. FINDING No. 1: The proposed Amendment is consistent with the goals and 
policies of the General Plan. 
Findings of Fact: The proposed ZC 20-3502 is consistent with the goals and 
policies of the General Plan. The proposed amendment will establish a land 
use pattern consistent with the corresponding general plan amendment 20-
2501, allowing for General Commercial and Industrial districts while 
preserving natural on-site drainages and open spaces with Open Space 
designations to maintain an appropriate balance of land uses in the City and 
through adoption of a Specific Plan will regulate such uses to allow for the 
orderly development of the Property. The Project will assist with the 
development of a circulation system that serves the needs of the City 
(Circulation Element Policy 6) through dedication of land and widening of 
key roadways, provision of other infrastructure and payment of fair share of 
fees such that City streets are able to maintain peak hour LOS D or better 
with implementation of the Project and City and regional capital 
improvements.

B. FINDING No. 2: The proposed Amendment is internally consistent with the Zoning 
Ordinance. 
Findings of Fact: The proposed zone change is internally consistent with the 
Zoning Ordinance because the zone change will implement zoning map 
changes authorized by Banning Municipal Code Chapter 17.96, authorizing 
adoption of Specific Plans by the City Council including adoption of the Sunset 
Crossroads Specific Plan adopted concurrently with adoption of this Ordinance 
pursuant to Resolution 2026-29. The proposed Project shall require an 
amendment to Banning Municipal Code Section 17 .96.080 - Specific plan 
zoning districts and zoning map designations. Inclusion of the Sunset 
Crossroads Specific Plan in the BMC will immediately make it consistent with 
the zoning ordinance. As required by state law and provided that the MSJC Site 
Entitlements are adopted by the City Council, the proposed Project involves a 
transfer of residential density from Sunset Crossroads development project site 
to the MSJC Site to accommodate the commercial/industrial development 
project. The transfer of residential development capacity is consistent with state 
housing law in that it ensures no net loss in the residential development 
capacity identified in the City's General Plan. The proposed density transfer 
can be accommodated on an approximately 41-acre site because the proposed 
density range is from 24 to 30 dwelling units per acre, consistent with the 
Zoning Ordinance and development standards for the Very High Density 
Residential zone and General Plan Housing Element goals and Programs 4 
which require the City to create very high density zoning with a range of 24-30 
du/acre.

IMPARTIAL ANALYSIS OF MEASURE “U”
Overview of Measure U. Measure U is a referendum regarding City of 
Banning Ordinance No. 1618 which was adopted by the Banning City Council on 
April 14, 2026, (the “Ordinance”). The Ordinance amends the City’s official zoning 
map and approves a zoning change for property located south of Interstate 10 
and the Union Pacific Railroad, and west of Sunset Avenue. The Measure was 
placed on the ballot by a referendum petition signed by the requisite number 
of voters. If Measure U is approved, the Ordinance will become effective. If the 
Measure U is not approved, the Ordinance will not become effective.
Ordinance and Zoning Change. The Ordinance amends the City’s official 
zoning map and approves zone change 20-3502 and more specifically applies 
to Assessor Parcel Numbers (APNs) 537-110-003 through -005 and 537-110-011 
through -014 as well as pre-zoning of property located in the City of Banning’s 
Sphere of Influence located south of the northern boundary of Western Avenue 
and west of Sunset Avene (APNs 537-120-013, 537-120-025, and 537-120-028 
through -036), which is subject to annexation into the City of Banning subject to 
the approval of the Local Area Formation Commission (“LAFCO”).
The Ordinance amends the zoning for the property from High Density 
Residential, Medium Density Residential, Low Density Residential, General 
Commercial and Open Space - Resource and Open Space — Parks, where 
applicable, to “Specific Plan - Sunset Crossroads” to General Commercial (GC), 
Industrial (I), Open Space — Parks (OS-P), and Open Space — Resources (OS-
R) land uses pursuant to the Sunset Crossroads Specific Plan adopted pursuant 
to Resolution 2026-29.
If the Ordinance is approved by the voters the owner of the property, NP 
Banning Industrial, LLC will be able to build a project that includes General 
Commercial (GC), Industrial (I), Open Space - Parks (OS-P), and Open Space - 
Resources (OS-R) land uses pursuant to the Sunset Crossroads Specific Plan 
adopted pursuant to Resolution 2026-29. If the Ordinance is not approved by the 
voters, the Ordinance will not become effective and the property will remain zoned 
High Density Residential, Medium Density Residential, Low Density Residential, 
General Commercial and Open Space - Resource and Open Space — Parks.
Summary:
A “Yes” vote by a majority of those voting on Measure U means Ordinance 
No. 1618 will become effective.
A “No” vote by a majority of those voting on Measure U means Ordinance 
No. 1618 will not become effective.
The above statement is the impartial analysis of Measure “U.” If you 
desire a copy of the Ordinance or Measure, please call the City Election 
Official’s Office at (951) 922-3102 and a copy will be mailed to you at no cost.

PUBLIC BENEFITS, PUBLIC IMPROVEMENTS, AND DEVELOPMENT 
IMPACT FEES. 
5.1 
Intent.  The Parties intend to provide consideration to the public to balance the 
private benefits conferred on Owner under this Agreement by requiring that Owner provide the 
Public Benefits described in this Section 5. The City has either previously approved or is in the 
process of considering various public improvements that may occur on or adjacent to the Property 
including the Sun Lakes Boulevard Extension, the Substation, a reverse osmosis facility and a 
potable water reservoir. Each of these public improvements has either undergone prior 
environmental (CEQA) review or is under consideration by the City or would, prior to 
construction, undergo applicable environmental (CEQA) review by the City and may proceed with 
or without development of the Project. Nonetheless, the Public Benefits include land conveyance 
and payments to be made to the City by Owner which land and payments may be utilized by the 
City for these and for other purposes to the extent described in this Agreement, in the event the 
City does not proceed with construction of such public improvements.   
5.2 
Public Benefit Fee.  Owner shall pay the City a Public Benefit Fee (“Public Benefit 
Fee”) equal to the lesser of either Eight Million Dollars ($8,000,000) or One Dollar and Forty Four 
Cents ($1.44) per square foot of entitled warehouse/logistics/industrial building square footage. 
Furthermore, the Public Benefit Fee shall be paid in installments subject to the following terms 
and conditions: 
5.2.1 First Installment. In consideration for the City entering into this Agreement, 
Owner shall pay the first installment of the nonrefundable Public Benefit Fee of Three Million 
Dollars ($3,000,000) (First Installment”) by wire transfer in immediately available funds within 
sixty (60) days of the Effective Date without extension for any reason such as denial of the 
Annexation by LAFCO, a Development Limitation, force majeure, lawsuit challenging this 
Agreement or the Development Approvals or Tolling Period. In the event that  such payment is 
not made within such sixty (60) days, this Agreement and the Development Approvals shall, 
without the necessity of notice or cure, terminate. However, the provisions providing for the City 
to retain the First Installment shall survive such termination. Notwithstanding anything to the 
contrary in this Agreement and the Development Approvals, they shall not be deemed to be vested 
rights of Owner until the First Installment is made. If Owner elects to terminate this Agreement 
and the Development Approvals as provided for in this Agreement, the rights under this Agreement 
and the Development Approvals shall be deemed not to be vested.. Upon Owner’s payment of the 
First Installment, the Existing Land Use Regulations shall revert to the Land Use Regulations in 
effect on the Effective Date. 
5.2.2 Subsequent Installments. Following the issuance of occupancy permits for 
the first 694,444 square feet of warehouse/logistics/industrial buildings, Owner shall pay 
subsequent installments of the Public Benefit Fee upon issuance of occupancy permits for each 
warehouse/logistics/industrial building on a square-foot-by-square foot basis at the rate of One 
Dollar and Forty Four Cents ($1.44) per square foot (“Subsequent Installments”). 
5.2.3 Sales Tax Credits. Owner may claim Sales Tax Revenues generated by the 
Industrial Property (as defined below) as a credit on a dollar-for-dollar basis toward future 
Subsequent Installments up to a maximum cumulative credit of Two Million Dollars ($2,000,000).
