Local Ballot Measures

Election Date: Tuesday, November 3, 2026

The City of Banning will have the following local measures placed on the ballot for voters consideration at the November 3, 2026 General Municipal Election. The information provided on this webpage is intended to provide voters with access to official election-related material. The City of Banning does not advocate for or against any measure.

MEASURE U
Shall Ordinance No.1618, amending the land uses for property located south of l-10 and the Union Pacific Railroad and West of Sunset Avenue allowing General Commercial, Industrial, Open Space - Parks and Resources land uses on the property and pre-zoning territory within the City's Sphere of Influence located South of the North Boundary of Western Avenue and West of Sunset Avenue for General Commercial, Industrial, Open Space - Parks and Resources land uses, be adopted?

MEASURE V
Shall Ordinance No. 1619, which amends the land uses for property south of West Westward Avenue and east of Sunset Avenue to allow Very High-Density Residential use on 41 acres of the 49.2-acre site, be adopted?

MEASURE W
Shall Ordinance No. 1620, approving a development agreement with NP Banning Industrial to develop a master planned project including commercial and industrial uses, various public facilities with on and off-site related improvements subject to the terms of the agreement which includes dedicating over 68 acres of land to the City for open space, a fire station, parks, and public utilities and over $40,000,000 in public benefits to the City, be adopted?

 
The last day to file arguments with the City Clerk at cityclerks@banningca.gov  regarding any measure is August 17, 2026 at noon. Arguments shall not exceed 300 words and must be accompanied by a Statement of Authors form. No more than five authors may sign the statement
