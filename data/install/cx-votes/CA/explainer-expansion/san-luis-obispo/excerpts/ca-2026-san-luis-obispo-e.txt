                A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COUNCIL OF THE CITY OF PISMO BEACH,

                                          THEREOF


            WHEREAS, while the other members of the City Council serve four-year terms, the Mayor
            of Pismo Beach currently serves a two-year term; and

            WHEREAS, the City Council believes that it would be preferable for the Mayor to serve a
            four-year term; and

            WHEREAS, Government Code Section 34900 and Elections Code 9222 authorize the
            City Council to submit to the voters a measure to change the term of office for its elected
            Mayor; and

            WHEREAS, the City Council of the City of Pismo Beach has by Resolution R-2026-035
            called for a general municipal election to be held on November 3, 2026; and

            WHEREAS, the City Council desires to submit to the voters at the Election a measure
            that will convert the Office of the Mayor from a two-year term to a four-year term, effective
            with the term elected at the 2028 General Municipal Election.

            NOW, THEREFORE, THE CITY COUNCIL OF THE CITY OF PISMO BEACH DOES
            RESOLVE, DECLARE, DETERMINE AND ORDER AS FOLLOWS:

            SECTION 1. Pursuant to Election Code Section 9222 and Government Code Section
            34900, the proposed ballot proposition attached hereto in draft ordinance form as Exhibit
            A, and titled “FOUR-YEAR MAYORAL TERMS MEASURE”, is hereby approved for
            placement on the ballot and submission to voters at the City’s regular election scheduled
            for November 3, 2026. The question shall be posed as follows:

                                                ORDINANCE NO. O-2026-XXX

             AN ORDINANCE OF THE PEOPLE OF THE CITY OF PISMO BEACH, CALIFORNIA
                ADDING SECTION 2.04.005 OF THE PISMO BEACH MUNICIPAL CODE TO
              CHANGE THE TERM OF THE ELECTIVE OFFICE OF MAYOR TO FOUR YEARS


            WHEREAS, the City Council of the City of Pismo Beach is currently composed of a
            directly-elected Mayor who serves a two-year term and four Council Members who serve
            four-year terms; and

            WHEREAS, under current law, in each even-numbered year the Mayor and two Council
            Members come up for election; and

            WHEREAS, the People of the City of Pismo Beach desire that beginning with the Mayor
            elected at the 2028 general municipal election, the term of office of the Mayor be four
            years.

            NOW, THEREFORE, the People of the City of Pismo Beach, California do ordain as
            follows:

            SECTION 1.

            Section 2.04.005 of the Pismo Beach Municipal Code is hereby added to read as follows:

                     2.04.005 Membership.

                     The City Council shall be composed of a directly-elected Mayor and four at-large
                     Council Members.

                     The term of office for Council Members shall be four years. Commencing with the
                     term of office elected at the November 7, 2028 election, the term of office for the
                     Mayor shall be four years.

            SECTION 2.

            The intent of this ordinance is to convert the elective office of Mayor to a four-year term,
            beginning with the term of office elected at the November 7, 2028 election. Under existing
            law, the four other members of the City Council are elected to staggered four-year terms,
            so that in each even-numbered year, two of the seats come up for election and in the next
            even-numbered year the other two seats come up for election. Nothing in this ordinance
            is intended to in any way change the terms of office of the four Council Members or to
            change the election schedules for Council Member seats, and all language in this
            ordinance regarding the office of Council Member is declaratory of existing law. It is the
