Election Information

  • Key dates:
    • October 26 - Atascadero satellite Elections Office opens (M-F 8:30 to 4:00 and 7am to 8pm on Election Day)
    • October 31 - SLO Elections Office open (9am to 3pm)
    • November 3 - Election Day (polls open 7am to 8pm)
    • November 18 - Publicly observable post-election 1% Manual Tally (hand count of at least 1% of precinct ballots and mail ballots received before Election Day) will begin by 10am in the SLO Elections Center
  • If you are an active registered voter and did not receive your Vote-by-Mail (VBM) ballot:
    • A replacement VBM ballot can be requested at our SLO office during business hours (Mon-Fri, 8am-5pm) or at our Atascadero Office once it opens on October 26 (Mon-Fri, 8:30am-4:00pm). If the request is made in person, you will receive your replacement ballot at the time you submit your application. The deadline to have a replacement ballot mailed is 7 days prior to the election. After that date, you must come in person to one of our offices.
    • You can download a copy of the application in English or Spanish to print and complete before coming into the office, or you may print and mail it to the Clerk-Recorder's Office in SLO, fax it to us, or email it to [email protected]. You may also complete a separate form to authorize someone else to obtain the replacement ballot on your behalf.

    • H-26 Resolution

At a Special Meeting held on August 5, 2026, the San Luis Obispo County Board of Supervisors took action to remove Measure H-26 from the November 3, 2026, Consolidated General Election ballot.

As a result of the Board's action, the original Measure H-26 will not appear on the November 3, 2026, ballot and will not be submitted to the voters for consideration at this election. Instead, at the request of Allan Hancock Joint Community College District, the measure formerly titled Measure J-26 will be listed on the ballot as Measure H-26, as it is in Santa Barbara and Ventura counties.
