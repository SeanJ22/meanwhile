                                RESOLUTION NO. 24-56

       A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COUNCIL OF THE CITY OF GROVER
       BEACH, CALIFORNIA, SUBMITTING TO THE VOTERS AT THE
       GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION ON NOVEMBER 3, 2026, AN
       INITIATIVE MEASURE REQUIRING THAT THE OFFICE OF CITY CLERK
       BE AN ELECTED POSITION

       WHEREAS , the City Council of the City of Grover Beach will call a General
Mun icipal Election to be held in the City of Grover Beach on Tuesday, November 3, 2026 ;

       WHEREAS , an initiative petition requiring the Office of the City Clerk be an elected
position (hereinafter referred to as "Initiative Measure" or "Initiative Petition") has been
submitted to the City in accordance with the requirements of the California Elections
Code ;

       WHEREAS , on October 15, 2024, the City Council accepted the Certificate of
Sufficiency of the Initiative Petition issued by the City Clerk of San Luis Obispo ;

       WHEREAS , on October 15, 2024, the City Council voted to submit to the electors
of the City of Grover Beach the Initiative Measure at the general municipal election ,
scheduled on November 3, 2026.

     NOW, THEREFORE, THE CITY COUNCIL OF THE CITY OF GROVER
BEACH, CALIFORNIA, DOES DECLARE, DETERMINE, AND ORDER AS
FOLLOWS:

SECTION 1. Initiative Measure Submitted to Voters at General Municipal Election . A
regularly scheduled general municipal election shall be called for the City of Grover Beach
to be held on Tuesday, November 3, 2026 . The following question is submitted to the
voters at the election:


                         CITY OF GROVER BEACH
                        INITIATIVE MEASURE A-26                            YES
           Shall the current appointive Office of the City
           Clerk be required to be an elected position?


                                                                            NO
SECTION 2. Adoption of Measure. The full text of the measure to be submitted to the
voters is as follows :

       INITIATIVE MEASURE TO BE SUBMITTED DIRECTLY TO THE VOTERS
       TO REQUIRE THE OFFICE OF THE CITY CLERK TO BE AN ELECTED
       POSITION

       THE PEOPLE OF THE CITY OF GROVER BEACH DO ORDAIN AS
       FOLLOWS:

       SECTION 1. The Office of the City Clerk shall be an elected position . This
       measure supersedes and repeals any prior question approved by voters
       designating the office as appointive.

      SECTION 2. The City Clerk shall be the city elections official having
      jurisdiction over elections and related matters within the city.

       SECTION 3. Within ninety days of the adoption of this Measure, the City
       Council shall call a special election to be held on the next lawfully available
       established election date pursuant to Chapter 4 of Division 1 of California
       Elections Code, which may be consolidated with any other election as
       provided by law, to fill the office of City Clerk until the next succeeding
       General Municipal Election .

       SECTION 4. At the next succeeding General Municipal Election referenced
       in Section 3 of this Measure, the election for City Clerk shall be for a full
       four-year term of office. The election of the City Clerk shall be held every
       four years thereafter as provided by law.

       SECTION 5.       This measure may not be amended, except upon voter
       approval.

       SECTION 6. Each prov1s1on of this ordinance is severable , and the
       invalidity of any section or part thereof shall not affect the validity of its other
       provisions.

If a majority of qualified electors voting on such measure shall vote in favor of City of
Grover Beach Initiative Measure "A-26", it shall be deemed ratified and shall read as
provided above .

SECTION 3. Notice of Election. Notice of the time and place of holding the election is
hereby given , and the City Clerk is authorized , instructed and directed to give further or
additional notice of the election in time, form, and manner as required by law.

SECTION 4. Impartial Analysis . The City Council hereby directs the City Clerk to transmit
a copy of the City Attorney's prepared impartial analysis of the measure, showing the
effect of the measure on the existing law and the operation of the measure, and to provide
such as required to the San Luis Obispo County Registrar of Voters .

SECTION 5. Ballot Arguments . Arguments in favor of or against the measure shall be
submitted within the timelines proscribed within California Elections Code section 9286.

SECTION 6. Rebuttal Arguments . Rebuttal arguments shall be controlled by the
provisions of Elections Code section 9285 .

SECTION 7. Duties of City Clerk. The Grover Beach City Clerk shall do all things
requ ired by law to effectuate the November 3, 2026 , general municipal election , including
but not limited to causing the posting , publication and printing of all notices or other
election materials under the requirements of the California Elections and Government
Codes .

SECTION 8. Non-substantive Changes. The City Attorney is authorized to make any
non-substantive changes and/or to correct minor administrative, procedural and clerica l
errors necessary to ensure the initiative measure is placed on the ballot for the November
3, 2026 , general municipal election .

SECTION 9. Transmittal of Resolution. The City Clerk is hereby directed to submit a
certified copy of this resolution to the Board of Supervisors and Registrar of Voters of the
County of San Luis Obispo .
