                                            RESOLUTION        N0.    26-20

        A RESOLUTION    OF THE CITY COUNCIL    OF THE CITY OF GROVER     BEACH,
        CALIFORNIA,   SUBMITTING    TO THE VOTERS       AT THE NEXT    GENERAL
        MUNICIPAL   ELECTION   ON NOVEMBER     3, 2026, AN INITIATIVE  MEASURE
        TO ADOPT AN ORDINANCE      TO LIMIT BUILDING    HEIGHTS AND EST ABLISH
        A MINIMUM THIRTY-THREE    PERCENT   (33%) COMMERCIAL      COMPONENT    IN
        MIXED-USE   DEVELOPMENT

       WHEREAS,      on February 10, 2026, the proponents         of an initiative measure submitted   a
Notice of Intention to Circulate     Petition for a proposed  ballot measure to limit building heights
and establish    a minimum     thirty-three   percent   (33%) commercial       component  in mixed-use
development    ("Measure");  and

        WHEREAS,          on February    10, 2026,     the City Clerk     transmitted     the Measure       to the City
Attorney; and

      WHEREAS,    on February           24, 2026,     the City Attorney      prepared    a ballot title and impartia[
summary of the Measure; and

        WHEREAS,     the Measure        proponents      published    the Measure        in New Times       on March      5,
2026,   and the City Clerk certified    publication     on March     5, 2026; and

       WHEREAS,     on April 20, 2026, the Measure     proponents   submitted  one thousand  two
hundred forty-nine (1,249) petition signatures to the City Clerk, which signatures the City Clerk
accepted on March 14, 2024; and

        WHEREAS,     on April 30, 2026, the City Clerk certified               that the initiative   petition   qualified
with the requisite number of sufficient valid signatures; and

        WHEREAS,          on March   30, 2026,   the City Council     directed    City staff to provide     a report on
the potential   effects   of the Measure;    and

         WHEREAS,      on May 11, 2026, the City Council             accepted     the Certificate of Sufficiency         of
the Initiative Petition issued by the City Clerk; and

        WHEREAS,          on June 8, 2026,   the City Council       accepted     the report.

      NOW,       THEREFORE,   THE CITY COUNCIL  OF THE CITY OF GROVER                                           BEACH,
CALIFORNIA,       DOES DECLARE,  DETERMINE, AND ORDER AS FOLLOWS:

          SECTION  1. Initiative Measure    Submitted  to Voters at General Municipal   Election. A
regularly scheduled   general municipal   election has been called for the City of Grover Beach to
be held on Tuesday, November      3, 2026. The following question is submitted to the voters at the
election:
                                      CITY OF GROVER  BEACH
                                     INITIATIVE MEASURE  F-26
                                                                                                                    YES

                   Grover Beach Citizen Initiative      Ballot Measure.     Shall
                   the City of Grover Beach implement a building height limit
                   and establish    a minimum     thirty-three    percent  (33%)
                   commercial   component   in mixed-use      development?
                                                                                                                     NO




            SECTION      2. Adoption             of Measure.            The full text of the measure                to be submitted              to the
voters      is as follows:

            INITIATIVE          MEASURE     TO              BE SUBMITTED      DIRECTLY  TO THE VOTERS
            ADOPTING            AN ORDINANCE                 TO LIMIT BUILDING   HEIGHTS AND ESTABLISH
            A MINIMUM           THIRTY-THREE                PERCENT   (33%) COMMERCIAL    COMPONENT    IN
            MIXED-USE            DEVELOPMENT

            THE PEOPLE            OF THE CiTY OF GROVER                          BEACH        DO ORDAIN            AS FOLLOWS:

            SECTION        1.    Building      and Structure            Maximum        Height     - Commercial          Zoning         Districts     -
Building       and structure         heights       in all commercial             zoning      districts   shall     not exceed            three     (3)
stories      and forty (40) feet in height.                Industrial     Zoning      Districts    - buildings        and structures             in all
industrial zoning districts           shall not exceed            thirty-three       (33) feet in height.          Building     and Structure
Heights       in all zoning      districts     shall be measured              from the average           natural     grade      to the highest
point of the building           or structure       including      any appurtenances.               Appurtenances              include,     but are
not      limited    to, architectural          features,      roof      mounted       equipment,         elevators,        stairwell      towers,
chimneys,          parapets,     or any other rooftop            additions,        structures,     or furnishings.


            SECTION        2.    Mixed       Use - A minimum               of thirty-three        percent    (33%)        of a mixed-use
building      shall be dedicated             to commercial        or retail use.        The front of buildings             and structures
shall be oriented          to the primary        street     of the project        and contain       only commercial             uses on the
ground       floor fronting      the primary        street,     except     that accessory          ground      floor access         to upper
floors     shall    be allowed       in addition        to the minimum             thirty-three      percent       (33%)       ground      floor
commercial          use.   Minimum           ground    floor ceiling       heights     shall be reduced            to ten (10) feet.




           SECTION         3. These      regulations          shall be added         to the City's Objective           Design      Standards.
The City shall update            and amend            all applicable       sections       of the City of Grover           Beach        Municipa[




                                                                          2
Code Article      IX - Planning      & Zoning      Regulations        and any related    regulations    to reflect these
height limits, definition     of appurtenances,            and percentages.


           SECTION     4.    This Initiative      shall    be binding    and effective    as of the earliest        date
allowed     by law (the "Effective      Date").   The Community          Development     Department      shall update
all City of Grover      Beach zoning        information        and zoning     maps to reflect the above zoning
requirements      within    ninety (90) days of the Effective            Date.   All new decisions       or changes,
regardless     of the original date of approval,            shall comply with this measure.


           SECTION    5. If any provision         of this Initiative    Ordinance   conflicts   with other provisions
contained     in the Grover Beach Municipal               Code, the provisions   of this ordinance     shall supersede
any conflicting    provision.


           SECTION     6.    Nothing     in this ordinance          shall be amended     or repealed      except    by a
majority    vote of the people of Grover Beach.               The purpose of this measure        is to ask registered
voters     in the City of Grover       Beach if they wish to adopt an ordinance             to limit building      height
within     the commercial       and industrial      zones,      and to establish    a minimum          percentage     for
designated      commercial      use within mixed-use           developments.

        SECTION       7. If a majority of qualified electors voting on such measure shall vote in favor
of City of Grover     Beach Initiative Measure "F-26", it shall be deemed ratified and shall read as
provided above.

        SECTION 8. Notice of Election.       Notice of the time and place of holding the election is
hereby given,    and the City Clerk is authorized,      instructed, and directed to give further or
additional notice of the election in time, form, and manner as required by law.

        SECTION 9. Impartial Analysis. The City Council hereby directs the City Clerk to transmit
a copy of the City Attorney's prepared impartial analysis of the measure, showing the effect of
the measure on the existing law and the operation of the measure, and to provide such as
required to the San Luis Obispo County Registrar of Voters.

         SECTION "10. Ballot Arquments.    Arguments in favor of or against the measure shall be
submitted to the City Clerk on or before June 22, 2026, at 5:00 p.m. under Elections Code section
9286 et seq. If the City Clerk receives more than one argument for and/or against, the priorities
established by Elections Code section 9287 shall control.

          SECTION    "11. Rebuttal Arguments.                   Rebuttal arguments     shall be controlled by the
provisions   of Elections Code section 9285.                  The deadline for filing rebuttal arguments shall be
July 2, 2026, at 5:00 p.m.


        SECTION 12. Duties of City Clerk.                      The Grover Beach City Clerk shall do all things
required by law to effectuate the November                    3, 2026, general municipal election, including, but
