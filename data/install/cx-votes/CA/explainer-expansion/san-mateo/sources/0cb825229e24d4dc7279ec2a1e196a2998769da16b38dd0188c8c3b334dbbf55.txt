IMPARTIAL ANALYSIS OF MEASURE [TBD]

Article XI, Section 3 of the California Constitution, Government Code Sections 23720
and 23722, and Article I, Section 102, of the San Mateo County Charter (“Charter”) authorize
the Board of Supervisors (“Board”) to propose amendments to the Charter for approval by the
County’s voters at a special election. [41]

Approval of Measure [TBD] (“Measure”) would amend Section 415 of Article IV of the
Charter to extend, from 30 days to 60 days, the period in which the Board must either make an
appointment or call a special election to fill a vacancy in an elective office other than the Board,
and to require a public meeting before any appointment is made. This Measure was placed on the
ballot by ordinance and resolution passed by a majority vote of the Board. [80]

Section 415 currently requires the Board, within 30 days of a vacancy occurring in an
elective office other than a Member of the Board, to either make an appointment or order a
special election to fill the vacancy. If the Board does not act within 30 days, the county officer
responsible for conducting elections must immediately order a special election. Section 415 does
not currently require a public meeting before the Board makes an appointment. [75]

This Measure would extend the time period within which the Board must either make an
appointment or order a special election to fill a vacancy from 30 days to 60 days. It-would also
require that, before making an appointment, the Board hold at least one noticed public meeting to
receive public comment regarding the appointment and the qualifications of potential candidates.
If the Board does not make an appointment or call a special election within 60 days, the county
officer responsible for conducting elections would still be required to immediately order a
special election, as under current law. [99]

The Measure would not change or otherwise affect the other provisions of Section 415 of
the Charter, including the timing and conduct of any special election called to fill a vacancy, and
the procedures governing an elected officer’s resignation. [39]

Voters may call the County’s Registration and Elections Division at (650) 312-5222 to
request that a copy of the proposed Charter amendment be mailed to them at no cost. [25]

Measure requires approval by a majority of the qualified voters voting in the election.
