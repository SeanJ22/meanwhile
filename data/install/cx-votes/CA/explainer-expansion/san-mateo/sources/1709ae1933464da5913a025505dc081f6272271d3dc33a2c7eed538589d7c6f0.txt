                                    MEASURE X
                        CITY ATTORNEY’S IMPARTIAL ANALYSIS

The Brisbane City Council placed this Measure on the ballot. If approved by the voters, Measure
X would amend the Business License Tax codified at Title 5, Division I of the Brisbane Municipal
Code by replacing the current tiered rate structure with a single gross receipts formula, deleting
some specialized categories of tax rates and exemptions while retaining others, and updating
relevant definitions.

The Business License Tax is a general tax, meaning revenues are deposited into the General Fund
and used for general City services. Passage requires approval by a simple majority of voters voting
on it.

Under the current tax structure, most businesses pay multi-tiered rates ranging from $50 for
businesses with gross receipts of $50,000 or less, to $1,300 plus $0.04 per $1,000 of the business’s
gross receipts for businesses with gross receipts of $10,000,000 or more. Specified businesses pay
the greater of that amount or: (a) a rate based on employee count (for manufacturers, warehouse
operators, and businesses headquartered in the City); or (b) a rate based on square footage (for
storage facilities). Nineteen other business categories have individualized tax rates.

Measure X would replace the above formulas with a single tax formula for most businesses. A
business with gross receipts of $25,000 or less would pay a tax of $50. A business with more than
$25,000 in gross receipts would pay $50 plus $1.00 for every $1,000 in gross receipts above
$25,000.

Measure X does not alter separate business license taxes that apply to cannabis businesses, hotels,
liquid storage facilities, capital improvements and residential construction, outdoor advertising,
and recycling business.

Measure X deletes existing tax exemptions for newspapers, charitable or nonprofit organizations,
café musicians, real estate auctioneers, veterans, and residential rentals of less than five units.

Measure X clarifies definitions for: (a) “business” to include those without a fixed place of
business in the City and those compensated in goods or labor; and (b) “gross receipts” to be the
greater of all compensation received or receivable by the business, with specified exclusions, or
the business’s cost of operations.

The City estimates Measure X would generate approximately $1,000,000 in additional annual
revenue.

A “Yes” vote is a vote in favor of the Measure.

A “No” vote is a vote against the Measure. If the Measure is not approved, the current tax structure
will remain in place.
