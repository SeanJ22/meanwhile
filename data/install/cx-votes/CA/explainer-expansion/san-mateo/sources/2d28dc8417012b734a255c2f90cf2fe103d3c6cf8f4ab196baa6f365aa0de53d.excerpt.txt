RESOLUTION NO. 2026 - 075

A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COUNCIL OF THE CITY OF SAN BRUNO SUBMITTING TO
THE VOTERS AT THE NOVEMBER 3, 2026 GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION A MEASURE
THAT PROPOSES ADOPTION OF AN ORDINANCE REPEALING INITIATIVE ORDINANCE
NO. 1284 RELATING TO VOTER APPROVAL TO PERMIT BUILDINGS EXCEEDING THREE
STORIES OR FIFTY FEET IN HEIGHT, ANY INCREASE IN RESIDENTIAL UNITS OR
OCCUPANCY IN RESIDENTIAL ZONING DISTRICTS IN EXCESS OF LIMITS PERMITTED ON
OCTOBER 10, 1974, MULTISTORY PARKING STRUCTURES, AND DEVELOPMENT THAT_
ENCROACHES UPON, MODIFIES, WIDENS OR REALIGNS CRYSTAL SPRINGS ROAD
BETWEEN OAK AVENUE AND JUNIPERO SERRA FREEWAY, AND SNEATH LANE FROM
EL CAMINO REAL TO THE WESTERLY CITY LIMITS; REQUESTING THAT SUCH ELECTION
BE CONSOLIDATED WITH THE STATEWIDE GENERAL ELECTION HELD ON THAT DATE;
TAKING CERTAIN OTHER ACTIONS; AND FINDING SUCH ACTION EXEMPT FROM CEQA

WHEREAS, by prior resolution, the City Council called a general municipal election (the
“Election”) for November 3, 2026, to be consolidated with the statewide general election to be
held on that same date;

WHEREAS, the City Council desires to have the matter of an ordinance that would
repeal Initiative Ordinance No. 1284, an initiative measure to require an affirmative vote of the
voters of the City of San Bruno prior to any City approvals that allow: (i) buildings exceeding
three stories or fifty feet in height, (ii) any increase in residential units or occupancy in residential
zoning districts in excess of limits permitted on October 10, 1974, (iii) multistory parking
structures, and (iv) development that encroaches upon, modifies, widens or realigns Crystal
Springs Road between Oak Avenue and Junipero Serra Freeway, and Sneath lane from El
Camino Real to the westerly City limits; be presented to the voters on the November 3, 2026
ballot; and

WHEREAS, since the adoption of Ordinance No. 1284, the California Legislature has
enacted a broad array of State housing laws that override local zoning regulations, including
voter-approved height and density limits, reducing the City's local control over land use
decisions and such that many of the development scenarios the Ordinance was designed to
subject to voter approval may now proceed under State law regardless of local voter sentiment;
and

WHEREAS, State law, including but not limited to the State Density Bonus Law, SB 9
(2021), and SB 79 (2025), has significantly pre-empted the housing-related provisions of
Ordinance No. 1284, further eroding local control over residential development while the
Ordinance continues to impose meaningful constraints on commercial and economic
development; and

WHEREAS, the practical effect of Ordinance No. 1284 on San Bruno's economic
development goals has been documented, including the loss of the Crossings Hotel
development opportunity in 1999 and the decision by a major employer to redirect campus
expansion plans to other jurisdictions due in part to the constraints imposed by the Ordinance,
representing significant foregone property tax, business license tax, and transient occupancy
tax revenue; and

WHEREAS, the City Council has established strengthening the City's fiscal base and
attracting private investment as core economic development goals, including through the

EXHIBIT A
Proposed Ordinance

AN ORDINANCE OF THE PEOPLE OF THE CITY OF SAN BRUNO, CALIFORNIA TO
REPEAL INITIATIVE ORDINANCE NO. 1284 CODIFIED IN CHAPTER 12.26 GF THE
SAN BRUNO MUNICIPAL CODE

THE PEOPLE OF CITY OF SAN BRUNO ORDAIN AS FOLLOWS:

SECTION 1. The purpose of this Ordinance is to repeal Initiative Ordinance No. 1284, an
initiative measure to require an affirmative vote of the voters of the City of San Bruno prior to
City approvals that allow buildings exceeding three stories or fifty feet in height, any increase in
residential units or occupancy in residential zoning districts in excess of limits permitted on
October 10, 1974, multi-story parking structures, and development that encroaches upon,
modifies, widens or realigns Crystal Springs Road between Oak Avenue and Junipero Serra
Freeway, and Sneath Lane from El Camino Real to the westerly City limits.

SECTION 2. Chapter 12.26 of the San Bruno Municipal Code is hereby repealed in its entirety.

SECTION 3. If any provision, clause, sentence or paragraph of this Ordinance or the
application thereof to any person or circumstance shall be held invalid, such invalidity shall not
affect the other provisions of this Ordinance which can be given effect without the invalid
provisions or application, and to this end the provision of this Ordinance are declared to be
severable.

SECTION 4. This ordinance Shall take effect 10 days after the City Council declares the results
of the vote at the November 3, 2026, election.

Approved by the People of the City of San Bruno at the General Municipal Election Held
on November 3, 2026. =

CITY ATTORNEY IMPARTIAL ANALYSIS OF MEASURE S

Measure S asks the voters of the City of San Bruno whether they want to
repeal Ordinance No. 1284, a zoning ordinance that requires voter approval for
certain development projects in the City of San Bruno.

In 1977, the City of San Bruno adopted Ordinance No. 1284, which is codified
in Chapter 12.26 of the San Bruno Municipal Code. Chapter 12.26 requires
approval by a majority of the voters of the City of San Bruno before the City
can issue building permits, grading permits or other approvals for the
construction of buildings, other structures, land development projects or land
uses that include:

•      Buildings or other structures exceeding fifty feet in height;
•      Buildings or other structures exceeding three stories in height;
•      Buildings or other structures, modifications or redevelopment thereof in
       residential districts which increase the number of dwelling units per
       acre or occupancy, within each acre or portion thereof, in excess of
       limits permitted on October 10, 1974, under the then existing zoning
       chapter of the city of San Bruno;
•      Multistory parking structures or buildings; and
•      Buildings or other structures, modifications or redevelopment thereof
       which encroach upon, modify, widen or realign the following streets
       designated as scenic corridors: Crystal Springs Road between Oak
       Avenue and Junipero Serra Freeway; and Sneath Lane from El Camino
       Real to existing westerly City limits.

Measure S was placed on the ballot by the San Bruno City Council. This
measure, if approved by the voters, repeals Chapter 12.26 of the San Bruno
Municipal Code, in its entirety, which would remove the voter approval required
for development projects in the City of San Bruno meeting one or more of the
criteria listed above. As a result, the process for considering a development
project that meets one or more of the criteria listed above would be completed
by City staff, the City Planning Commission, the City Council, or a combination
thereof, according to the state and City laws governing the process for such
development project.

Measure S will become effective only if approved by a majority of the voters
voting on the measure. A “yes” vote on Measure S is a vote to remove the
voter approval requirements in Chapter 12.26 of the San Bruno Municipal
Code. A “no” vote on Measure S is a vote to continue the voter approval
requirements, as provided by the current Chapter 12.26 of the San Bruno
Municipal Code.
