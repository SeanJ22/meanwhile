        CITY ATTORNEY IMPARTIAL ANALYSIS OF MEASURE DD

        Measure DD asks the voters of the City of San Bruno whether they want to
        repeal the City of San Bruno Safe and Sane Fireworks Ordinance to prohibit
        the sale, use, and discharge of safe and sane fireworks in the City of San
        Bruno.

        Measure DD was placed on the ballot by the San Bruno City Council. This
        measure, if approved by the voters, adopts an ordinance to amend Chapter
        6.50 of the San Bruno Municipal Code to prohibit the sale, use, and discharge
        of safe and sane fireworks in the City of San Bruno.

        In 2005, the voters of the City of San Bruno approved an initiative measure
        adopting an ordinance to add Chapter 6.50 to the San Bruno Municipal Code.
        Chapter 6.50 permits the limited sale, use, and discharge of “safe and sane
        fireworks,” defined by the California Health and Safety Code to mean “any
        fireworks that have been approved by the United States Consumer Product
        Safety Commission and that have been carefully examined and tested by the
        State Fire Marshal and determined by the State Fire Marshal that the fireworks
        meet and are in compliance with the general and specific standards for design,
        construction, performance, and labeling for safe and sane fireworks, as set
        forth in Chapter 6 (commencing with Section 979) of Division 1 of Title 19 of
        the California Code of Regulations, as it reads on July 1, 2023.”

        Measure DD will become effective only if approved by a majority of the voters
        voting on the measure. A “yes” vote on Measure DD is a vote to prohibit the
        sale, use and discharge of safe and sane fireworks in the City of San Bruno. A
        “no” vote on Measure DD is a vote to continue the limited sale, use and
        discharge of safe and sane fireworks in the City of San Bruno, as provided by
        the current Chapter 6.50 of the San Bruno Municipal Code.
