IMPARTIAL ANALYSIS OF MEASURE [TBD)

Article XI, Section 3 of the California Constitution, Government Code Sections 23720
and 23722, and Article I, Section 102, of the San Mateo County Charter (“Charter”) authorize
the Board of Supervisors (“Board”) to propose amendments to the Charter for approval by the
County’s voters at a special election. [41]

Approval of Measure [TBD] (“Measure”) would amend the Charter to add a new
provision to the Charter’s Preamble affirming the County’s commitment to the dignity, civil
rights, and equal protection of all County residents. This Measure was placed on the ballot by
ordinance and resolution passed by a majority vote of the Board of Supervisors (“Board”). [53]

This Measure would add a new paragraph to the Charter’s Preamble stating that the
County affirms its commitment to the dignity, civil rights, and equal protection of all County
residents in a manner that promotes equity, fairness, inclusion, belonging, accessibility, and
opportunity, a subject not currently addressed by the Preamble. The Measure would not change
or otherwise affect the other provisions of the Charter. [64]

Voters may call the County’s Registration and Elections Division at (650) 312-5222 to
request that a copy of the proposed Charter amendment be mailed to them at no cost. [25]

This Measure requires approval by a majority of the qualified voters voting in the
election. [15]
