WHEREAS, by prior resolution, the City Council called a general municipal election (the
“Election”) for November 3, 2026, to be consolidated with the statewide general election to be

WHEREAS, in 2005, the voters approved a “Safe and Sane Fireworks Ordinance,”
codified as Chapter 6.50 of the San Bruno Municipal Code, to allow the continued sale, use, and
discharge of safe aind sane fireworks;

WHEREAS, the State Fireworks Law, being California Health and Safety Code sections
12500 through 12728, permits each city to regulate or prohibit the sale, use or discharge of
“safe and sane fireworks” within its jurisdiction;

WHEREAS, San Bruno and Pacifica are the only cities in San Mateo County that permit
the sale of "Safe and Sane" fireworks,

WHEREAS, the State Fire Marshal released updated Fire Hazard Severity Zone maps in
2025, and much of San Bruno is now designated as a high fire hazard severity zone,
heightening the risk associated with the discharge of consumer fireworks;

WHEREAS, the City Council has determined that the sale, use and discharge of safe

and sane fireworks presents a risk of fires and the generation of smoke and air emissions,
noise, and litter, which may adversely affect the environment;

WHEREAS, the City Council has determined that the sale, use, and discharge of safe
and sane fireworks presents a risk of harm to persons and property; and

WHEREAS, in order to more fully protect the health and safety of persons and property
within the City of San Bruno, the City Council desires to have the matter of an ordinance that
would prohibit the sale, use, and discharge of safe and sane fireworks be presented to the
voters on the November 3, 2026 ballot.

NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED, BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF THE CITY OF SAN
BRUNO, CALIFORNIA, AS FOLLOWS:

C AN ORDINANCE OF THE PEOPLE OF THE CITY OF SAN BRUNO, CALIFORNIA TO
REPEAL THE SAFE AND SANE FIREWORKS ORDINANCE CODIFIED IN CHAPTER 6.50

THE PEOPLE OF CITY OF SAN BRUNO ORDAIN AS FOLLOWS:

SECTION 1. The purpose of this Ordinance is to prohibit the sale, use or discharge of safe and
sane fireworks in the City of San Bruno by repealing the Safe and Sane Fireworks Ordinance,
an initiative measure adopted by the voters in 2005 to allow the continued sale, use, or
discharge of safe and sane fireworks in the City of San Bruno, and replacing such ordinance
with a ban on the sale, use, or discharge of safe and sane fireworks in the City of San Bruno.

SECTION 2. Chapter 6.50 of the San Bruno Municipal Code is hereby amended and restated
in its entirety, to read as follows:

“Section 6.50.010. Sale, use, and discharge of safe and sane fireworks prohibited. The

sale, use, or discharge of safe and sane fireworks is prohibited. The term “safe and

sane fireworks” shall have the meaning ascribed to it in Section 12529 of the California

Health and Safety Code, or such other law as amended from time to time.” ty

SECTION 3. If any provision, clause, sentence or paragraph of this Ordinance or the
application thereof to any person or circumstance shall be held invalid, such invalidity shall not
affect the other provisions of this Ordinance which can be given effect without the invalid
provisions or application, and to this end the provision of this Ordinance are declared to be
severable.

SECTION 4. This ordinance shall take effect 10 days after the City Council declares the results
of the vote at the November 3, 2026, election.

Approved by the People of the City of San Bruno at the General Municipal Election Held
on November 3, 2026.

        CITY ATTORNEY IMPARTIAL ANALYSIS OF MEASURE DD

        Measure DD asks the voters of the City of San Bruno whether they want to
        repeal the City of San Bruno Safe and Sane Fireworks Ordinance to prohibit
        the sale, use, and discharge of safe and sane fireworks in the City of San
        Bruno.

        Measure DD was placed on the ballot by the San Bruno City Council. This
        measure, if approved by the voters, adopts an ordinance to amend Chapter
        6.50 of the San Bruno Municipal Code to prohibit the sale, use, and discharge
        of safe and sane fireworks in the City of San Bruno.

        In 2005, the voters of the City of San Bruno approved an initiative measure
        adopting an ordinance to add Chapter 6.50 to the San Bruno Municipal Code.
        Chapter 6.50 permits the limited sale, use, and discharge of “safe and sane
        fireworks,” defined by the California Health and Safety Code to mean “any
        fireworks that have been approved by the United States Consumer Product
        Safety Commission and that have been carefully examined and tested by the
        State Fire Marshal and determined by the State Fire Marshal that the fireworks
        meet and are in compliance with the general and specific standards for design,
        construction, performance, and labeling for safe and sane fireworks, as set
        forth in Chapter 6 (commencing with Section 979) of Division 1 of Title 19 of
        the California Code of Regulations, as it reads on July 1, 2023.”

        Measure DD will become effective only if approved by a majority of the voters
        voting on the measure. A “yes” vote on Measure DD is a vote to prohibit the
        sale, use and discharge of safe and sane fireworks in the City of San Bruno. A
        “no” vote on Measure DD is a vote to continue the limited sale, use and
        discharge of safe and sane fireworks in the City of San Bruno, as provided by
        the current Chapter 6.50 of the San Bruno Municipal Code.
