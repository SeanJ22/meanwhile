IMPARTIAL ANALYSIS OF MEASURE [TBD

Article XI, Section 3 of the California Constitution, Government Code Sections 23720
and 23722, and Article I, Section 102, of the San Mateo County Charter (“Charter”) authorize
the Board of Supervisors (“Board”) to propose amendments to the Charter for approval by the
County’s voters at a special election. [41]

Approval of Measure [TBD] (“Measure”) would amend the Charter to add a new
provision to the Charter’s Preamble designating long-term resilience to extreme weather events
as a County priority. This Measure was placed on the ballot by ordinance and resolution passed
by amajority vote of the Board of Supervisors (“Board”). [49]

This Measure would add a new paragraph to the Charter’s Preamble stating that it is the
policy of the County that long-term resilience against extreme weather events, including fire,
flood, wind, rain, heat, drought, and sea level rise, is a County priority, to be undertaken
collaboratively with cities, districts, and other public entities, a subject not currently addressed
by the Preamble. The Measure would not change or otherwise affect the other provisions of the
Charter. [75]

Voters may call the County’s Registration and Elections Division at (650) 312-5222 to
request that a copy of the proposed Charter amendment be mailed to them at no cost. [25]

This Measure requires approval by a majority of the qualified voters voting in the
election. [15]
