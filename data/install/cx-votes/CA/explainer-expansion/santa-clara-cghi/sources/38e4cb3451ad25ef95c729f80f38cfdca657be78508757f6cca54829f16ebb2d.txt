Proposed Charter Amendment 
(City Manager Settlement Authority) 
(additions shown in underline, deletions in strikeout) 
 
The citizens of the City of Sunnyvale do hereby enact the following amendment to the City 
Charter: 
 
Section 1. Section 1314 of the City Charter (Claims Against City) is hereby amended to read as 
follows:  
 
 
§ 1314. 
Claims Against City. 
 
(1) Except as otherwise required by the provisions of State law applicable to chartered cities, 
claims and demands against the City shall be presented and audited as prescribed by ordinance. 
 
(2) 
The City Council shall prescribe by ordinance the monetary limits for the City Manager 
or Manager’s designee to settle any claim or action against the City, or any City officer, 
employee, board or department thereof in an official capacity. No claim or action against the 
City shall be settled for more than the amount set forth in the ordinance without the prior 
approval of the City Council.
