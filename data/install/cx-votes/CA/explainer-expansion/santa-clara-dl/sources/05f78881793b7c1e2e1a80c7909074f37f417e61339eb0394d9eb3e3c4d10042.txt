Measure L was placed on the ballot by the City Council of the City of Cupertino. The
measure would adopt an ordinance amending the Cupertino General Plan and adding Chapter 4
21.04 to the Cupertino Municipal Code, titled the “Cupertino Parks and Open Space Protection
Initiative.”

The measure applies to “Protected Parks and Open Space Land,” defined as the parcels and bo
portions of parcels identified in the ordinance exhibits and consisting of parcels designated as
“Parks and Open Space” on the Land Use Map of the Cupertino General Plan as of the effective

date of the ordinance.

City of Cupertino voting on the question at the City’s next general municipal election before the 93
