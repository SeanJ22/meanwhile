Parks and Open Space Protection Measure

On June 21, 2026, Cupertino City Council voted to place the following local measure on the November 2026 ballot for voters to consider.

Parks and Open Space Protection Measure

The Parks and Open Space Protection Measure, if approved, would require two-thirds voter approval before certain changes could be made to designated parks, parkland, and open space. These changes would impact:

  • Any attempt to rezone parks, parkland, or open space in the City for residential, commercial, or industrial uses; and
  • Any proposal for residential, commercial, or industrial development in parks, parkland, or open space.

Measure L requires majority (50 + 1) voter approval to be enacted.

On July 21, 2026, the City Council approved this measure to be placed on the November 3 General Election ballot.
The staff report and adopted resolution are available here:

  • Staff Report
  • Resolution

Learn More

More information about Measure L, or any other items included the November 3 General Election, are available through the Santa Clara County Registrar of Voters.

Questions may be submitted to communication@cupertino.gov.
