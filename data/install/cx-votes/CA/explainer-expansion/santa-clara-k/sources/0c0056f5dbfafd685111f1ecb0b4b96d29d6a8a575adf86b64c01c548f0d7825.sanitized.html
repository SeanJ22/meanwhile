Election Information

The City of Milpitas will conduct a General Municipal Election on November 3, 2026, to elect two City Council members and one Mayor.

Candidate Nomination Period

The nomination period for the November 3, 2026, General Municipal Election closed on Friday, August 7, 2026, and the extension nomination period closed on Wednesday, August 12, 2026. 

Obtaining and Filing Nomination Papers

Appointments are required to obtain and file nomination papers. To schedule an appointment with the City Clerk's Office, call (408) 586-3005 or email cityclerk@milpitas.gov.

The City Clerk's Office is open for appointments Monday through Friday, 9:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m. Appointments are available throughout the nomination period; however, all nomination documents must be received by the City Clerk's Office no later than 5:00 p.m. on the applicable filing deadline. Nomination documents received after the filing deadline cannot be accepted.

Measure K - City of Milpitas Business Tax Modernization Measure

Ballot Arguments

Registered voters may submit a written argument in favor of or against the ballot measure for inclusion in the Voter Information Guide. Arguments are limited to 300 words and must be filed with the City Clerk no later than Tuesday, August 11, 2026, at 5:00 p.m. Rebuttal arguments, limited to 250 words, may be filed by Tuesday, August 18, 2026, at 5:00 p.m. All arguments and rebuttals must be submitted in person by appointment with the City Clerk and may not be submitted by email, mail, overnight delivery, or scanned copy.

Please visit the Santa Clara County Registrar of Voters for forms required to be submitted in addition to written arguments.

Filing obligations

Candidates intending to file nomination papers during the Nomination Period may be subject to filing obligations prior to the Nomination Period, as stipulated by the Political Reform Act and the regulations of the Fair Political Practices Commission (FPPC). See FPPC Campaign Disclosure Manual 2 (PDF) for additional information regarding filing obligations.  

  • Form 501 (PDF) - Candidate Intention Statement: Must be filed before soliciting or receiving any contributions, or before making expenditures on behalf of a candidacy. This Form must be filed in hardcopy with the Office of the City Clerk. Note: Municipal elections are non-partisan.
  • Form 410 (PDF) - Statement of Organization of Recipient Committee: Must be filed within 10 days of receiving $2,000 in contributions.
  • Campaign Committee Training for Candidates and Treasurers - Fair Political Practices Commission
