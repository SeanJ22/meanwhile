REGISTER TO VOTE

​Last day to register to vote: October 19, 2026

  • If you have changed your name or political affiliation, you must re-register to vote.
  • Complete, sign, and mail to: Registrar of Voters, Voter Registration Division, PO Box 611300, San Jose, CA 95161-1300. Faxed registration forms will not be accepted.
  • More information about registering to vote

If you missed the voter registration deadline, you will have the option to register through a Conditional Voter Registration ​at the Registrar of Voters' Office or at any vote center.

VOTE BY MAIL

Every registered voter will receive a Vote by Mail Ballot starting 29 days before Election Day. Complete the Vote by Mail request form available below if you need a replacement ballot.  

Last day to request a Vote by Mail ballot replacement by mail: October 27, 2026

Vote by Mail ballot application forms information 

Mail or fax to: Registrar of Voters, Vote by Mail Division, PO BOX 611750, San Jose, CA 95161-1750. Fax: (408) 293-6002. 

  • Learn more about Voting by Mail​​

Vote by Mail Ballots for the November 3, 2026 General Election will be mailed starting the week of October 5, 2026.

  • Early Voting at the Registrar of Voters Office​​​​​: 1555 Berger Drive, Building 2, San Jose, CA 95112
    • Dates & Hours
      • Monday - Friday, October 5, 2026 – November 2, 2026, 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m.
      • ​Saturday​ and Sunday, October 24, 2026 – October 25, 2026, October 31, 2026 - November 1, 2026, 9:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m.
      • ​Election Day, November 3, 2026, 7:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m.
