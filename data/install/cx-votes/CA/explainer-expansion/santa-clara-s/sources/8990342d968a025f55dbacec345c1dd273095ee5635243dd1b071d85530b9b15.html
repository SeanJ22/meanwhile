ECHD Boundary Map

El Camino Healthcare District Boundaries Map data provided by the Department of Planning and Development, County of Santa Clara.

Steps to Look Up an Address

Step 1

Expand the map above by selecting the view larger map icon in the upper right corner.

Step 2

Select the magnifying glass located on the left-hand-side of the page.

Step 3

Enter the address and select "show all on map" link.
