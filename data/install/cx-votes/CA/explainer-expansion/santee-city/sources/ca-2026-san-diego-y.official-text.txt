A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COUNCIL OF THE CITY OF SANTEE, CALIFORNIA
CALLING FOR THE PLACEMENT OF A GENERAL TAX MEASURE ON THE BALLOT
FOR THE NOVEMBER 3, 2026 GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION FOR THE
SUBMISSION TO THE QUALIFIED VOTERS OF AN ORDINANCE TO ENACT A
GENERAL TRANSACTIONS AND USE TAX (SALES TAX) AT THE RATE OF ONE
PERCENT (1%) FOR TEN (10) YEARS

WHEREAS, the City of Santee (“City”) is authorized to levy a Transactions and Use
Tax (“TUT”) for general purposes pursuant to California Revenue and Taxation Code
section 7285.9, subject to approval by a majority vote of the electorate pursuant to Article
XIIIC, section 2 of the California Constitution (“Proposition 218”); and

WHEREAS, pursuant to California Elections Code section 9222, the City Council
has authority to place local measures on the ballot to be considered at a Municipal
Election; and

WHEREAS, the City Council would like to submit to the voters at the November 3,
2026 General Municipal Election a measure establishing a general TUT of one percent
(1%) on the sale and/or use of all tangible personal property sold at retail in the City for a
period of ten (10) years, as more specifically set forth in the attached proposed ordinance
adding Chapter 3.36 to Title 3 of the City’s Municipal Code; and

WHEREAS, the one percent (1%) TUT is a general tax, the revenue of which will
be placed in the City’s general fund and will be used to pay for general City services; and

WHEREAS, on November 6, 1996, the voters of the State of California approved
Proposition 218, an amendment to the State Constitution which requires that all general
taxes which are imposed, extended or increased must be submitted to the electorate and
approved by a majority vote of the qualified electors voting in the election; and

WHEREAS, pursuant to Proposition 218 (California Constitution, Article XIIIC,
section 2(b)), the general rule is that any local election for the approval of an increase to a
general tax must be consolidated with a regularly scheduled general election for members
of the governing body of the local government; and

WHEREAS, the next regularly scheduled general election at which City Council
members will be elected is November 3, 2026; and

WHEREAS, pursuant to Revenue and Taxation Code section 7285.9, a two-thirds

3.36.010 — Title.

This ordinance shall be known as the “City of Santee General Transactions and Use
Tax Ordinance”. The City of Santee hereinafter shall be called “City.” This ordinance
shall be applicable in the incorporated territory of the City.

3.36.020 — Purpose.

This ordinance is adopted to achieve the following, among other purposes, and directs
that the provisions hereof be interpreted in order to accomplish those purposes:

A.

To impose a retail transactions and use tax in accordance with the provisions of Part
1.6 (commencing with Section 7251) of Division 2 of the Revenue and Taxation
Code and Section 7285.9 of Part 1.7 of Division 2 which authorizes the City to adopt
this tax ordinance which shall be operative if a majority of the electors voting on the
measure vote to approve the imposition of the tax at an election called for that
purpose.

To adopt a retail transactions and use tax ordinance that incorporates provisions
identical to those of the Sales and Use Tax Law of the State of California insofar as
those provisions are not inconsistent with the requirements and limitations contained
in Part 1.6 of Division 2 of the Revenue and Taxation Code.

. To adopt a retail transactions and use tax ordinance that imposes a tax and provides

a measure therefore that can be administered and collected by the California
Department of Tax and Fee Administration in a manner that adapts itself as fully as
practicable to, ‘and requires the least possible deviation from, the existing statutory
and administrative procedures followed by the California Department of Tax and Fee


Administration in administering and collecting the California State Sales and Use
Taxes.

D. To adopt a retail transactions and use tax ordinance that can be administered in a
manner that will be, to the greatest degree possible, consistent with the provisions of
Part 1.6 of Division 2 of the Revenue and Taxation Code, minimize the cost of
collecting the transactions and use taxes, and at the same time, minimize the burden
of record-keeping upon persons subject to taxation under the provisions of this
ordinance.

3.36.030 - Contract With State.

Prior to the operative date, the City shall contract with the California Department of Tax
and Fee Administration to perform all functions incident to the administration and
operation of this transactions and use tax ordinance; provided, that if the City shall not
have contracted with the California Department of Tax and Fee Administration prior to
the operative date, it shall nevertheless so contract and in such a case the operative
date shall be the first day of the first calendar quarter following the execution of such a
contract.

3.36.040 - Transactions Tax Rate.

For the privilege of selling tangible personal property at retail, a tax is hereby imposed
upon all retailers in the incorporated territory of the City at the rate of one percent (1%)
of the gross receipts of any retailer from the sale of all tangible personal property sold at
retail in said territory on and after the operative date of this ordinance.

3.36.050 - Place of Sale.

For the purposes of this ordinance, all retail sales are consummated at the place of
business of the retailer unless the tangible personal property sold is delivered by the
retailer or his agent to an out-of-state destination or to a common carrier for delivery to
an out-of-state destination. The gross receipts from such sales shall include delivery
charges, when such charges are subject to the state sales and use tax, regardless of
the place to which delivery is made. In the event a retailer has no permanent place of
business in the State or has more than one place of business, the place or places at
which the retail sales are consummated shall be determined under rules and regulations
to be prescribed and adopted by the California Department of Tax and Fee
Administration.

3.36.060 - Use Tax Rate.

An excise tax is hereby imposed on the storage, use or other consumption in the City of
tangible personal property purchased from any retailer on and after the operative date
of this ordinance for storage, use or other consumption in said territory at the rate of one
percent (1%) of the sales price of the property. The sales price shall include delivery
charges when such charges are subject to state sales or use tax regardless of the place
to which delivery is made.

out-of-City and declaration under penalty of perjury, signed by the buyer,
that the vehicle will be operated from that address.

The sale of tangible personal property if the seller is obligated to furnish the

3.36.140 - Citizens’ Oversight Committee.

A. Establishment and Purpose. There is established a Citizens’ Oversight Committee
(the “Committee”) to review expenditures of revenue generated by this Ordinance
and to report publicly to the City Council. The Committee is advisory. It may not
direct expenditures, manage staff, or exercise budgeting authority reserved to the


City Council. Its purpose is to provide independent public review of how revenue
under this Ordinance is spent and to inform the public and the City Council through
an annual report.

. Composition.

1. The Committee shall consist of five members who are residents of, or own real
property or operate a business within, the City of Santee.

2. In making appointments, the City Council shall endeavor to seek members with
background in one or more of the following: municipal or public finance,
accounting or auditing, public works or construction, or project management. To
the extent qualified resident applicants are available, the Council should seek a
range of these backgrounds.

3. No member of the Committee may be a current City of Santee officer or
employee.

. Appointment. Members shall be appointed by the City Council through the City’s
standard process for appointive boards and commissions, including public
solicitation of applications. Vacancies shall be filled by the City Council for the
remainder of the unexpired term through the same process used for appointment.

1. Members appointed to the Committee shall complete Fiscal and Financial
Training in a manner similar to other City officials under California Government
Code Section 53238.

. Term Limits.

1. Members shall serve four-year terms, except that of the initial members, the City
Council shall designate two members to serve an initial two-year term so that
terms are staggered and no more than a majority of seats regularly turn over in
any two-year cycle.

2. A member shall serve no more than two full consecutive terms, after which he or
she shall cease serving on the Committee for at least two years. A partial term of
less than two years does not count toward this limit.

. Meetings. The Committee shall comply with the Ralph M. Brown Act. Meetings
shall be open to the public, agendized in advance according to the Brown Act, and
held at least annually. City staff shall provide reasonable administrative support to
the Committee.

. Responsibilities. The Committee shall:
1. Review a proposed annual spending plan (“Plan”), which shall be prepared by

City Staff and presented to the Committee for its review. The Plan shall identify
City of Santee improvements, projects, programs, services proposed to be


funded in the next Fiscal Year’s budget, in whole or in part, with revenue
generated from this Ordinance. The Plan shall be presented to the Committee
prior to the City Council’s annual budget process. After Committee review, it may
make recommendations about the Plan to the City Council. The Plan, along with
any recommendations of the Committee, shall be incorporated into the City
Manager's proposed Fiscal Year budget, which shall be subject to approval each
year by the City Council.

. Review the City’s annual independent financial audit and any staff reports
identifying revenue received from this tax and expenditures made from this tax
during the prior fiscal year.

. Review expenditures for consistency with terms of this Ordinance.

. Review the City Councils Maintenance of Effort regarding revenues and
expenditures that exist in the 3-years prior to this Ordinance.

. Prepare an annual report presenting its review and any findings or
recommendations. The report shall be presented to the City Council at an
agendized regular or special meeting in open session and shall be made
available to the public.

. If the Committee identifies a concern regarding the use of revenue under this
Ordinance, it shall state the concern in its annual report or in a separate written
report to the City Council. The City Council shall place the matter on an
agendized regular or special meeting and respond on the record within sixty days
of receiving the report.


CITY OF SANTEE
CITY ATTORNEY’S IMPARTIAL ANALYSIS OF MEASURE “Y”
1% GENERAL TRANSACTIONS AND USE (SALES) TAX
FOR TEN (10) YEARS

On June 24, 2026, the City Council voted to place Measure “Y” on the November 3,
2026 General Municipal Election ballot.

If approved by a majority of Santee voters, this Measure would authorize a one percent
(1%) retail transactions and use (sales) tax within the City of Santee. It is estimated that
Measure “Y” will provide an additional $14,000,000 in annual local funding for ten (10)
years that could be used for City of Santee services and infrastructure, including repairing
streets and potholes; cleaning and maintaining streets, sidewalks, parks and public
facilities, repairing and replacing stormdrains, local traffic management, fire protection,
paramedic, crime prevention, and 911 emergency response. This tax would be a “general
tax,” meaning that revenues raised from the tax would go into the City’s general fund to
pay for any lawful City program, improvement, or service.

The California Constitution (Proposition 218) and Revenue and Taxation Code section
7285.9 authorize the City to levy a general transactions and use/sales tax at a rate of one
percent (1%) so long as the tax is approved by a majority of the voters voting in an
election on that issue. If approved, the tax would become operative on April 1, 2027 and

Measure “Y” establishes a five (5) member Citizens’ Oversight Committee consisting of
Santee residents, property owners and business owners appointed by the City Council.
The Committee shall hold meetings open to the public pursuant to the California Brown
Act. Each year, City staff shall present a proposed annual spending plan to the
Committee, identifying City improvements, projects, programs, services proposed to be
funded with Measure “Y” revenues. The Committee may make recommendations about
the spending plan, which shall be incorporated into the City Manager’s proposed annual
budget and subject to City Council approval.

The Committee shall also be authorized to independently review the City’s annual
financial audit identifying Measure “Y” revenues collected and the expenditures made
from such revenues in the prior year. The Committee shall report their findings publicly,
including any concerns over use of Measure “Y” revenues, through an annual report
presented to the City Council at a public meeting.

The tax would be paid in addition to current sales taxes and would be collected at the
same time and in the same manner as existing sales taxes. All revenues raised by the tax
would remain in the City and would not be shared with the State, County or any other
agency.

A “yes” vote on Measure “Y” will authorize the 1% transactions and use (sales) tax for
10 years.

A “no” vote on Measure “Y” will not authorize the 1% transactions and use (sales) tax
