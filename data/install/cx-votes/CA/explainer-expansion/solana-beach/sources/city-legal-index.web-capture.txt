L33: # Election Information
L34: The 【28†City Council】 is made up of an elected Mayor and four district Councilmembers. On November 3, 2020, the first District elections took place for a separately elected 【28†Mayor and Councilmembers】 for 【29†District 1】 and 【30†District 3】. Councilmembers for 【31†District 2】 and 【32†District 4】 were selected by voters in those districts in the 2022 General Election.
L35: Pursuant to 【33†Resolution 2023-057†www.cityofsolanabeach.ca.gov】, a Deputy Mayor was appointed on an annual rotation. There are no term limits for City Councilmembers.
L36: Solana Beach's General Municipal Election is held in even numbered years on the first Tuesday after the First Monday in November. The next General Municipal Election will be held on November 3, 2026. The City contracts with the San Diego County Registrar of Voters to provide certain election services.

L129: ### Local Committee Contribution Limits
L130: 
L131: Local contribution limits are updated annually by applying the San Diego County Consumer Price Index on January 1st (per 【63†SBMC 2.24.040 A†www.codepublishing.com】). The contribution limits below were updated January 2026.
L132:   * Candidate and Controlled Committees are limited to receiving contributions of:
L133:     * $290 per individual
L134:     * $8,050 for aggregate contributions (from a combination of businesses, organizations, committees, etc.)
L135:   * Ballot Measure Committees are not subject to contribution limits.
L136: ### Local Campaign Committee Filing Requirements
L137: 
L138: Activities considered campaign related for local elections of candidates and/or measures may require 【64†campaign statement filings†www.fppc.ca.gov】 per state and local codes and regulations.
L139: 【65†The Fair Political Practices Commission (FPPC)†www.fppc.ca.gov】 requires the 【64†Form 501†www.fppc.ca.gov】 (Candidate Intention Statement) to be filed with the City Clerk prior to any expenditures or soliciting/receiving of contributions, including a Candidate's expenditure of personal funds (the Candidate Statement fee).
L140: City Committees (including Candidate Committees) are triggered for local filings when contributions or expenditures supporting/opposing a candidate or measure reach $200. Campaign Committee 【66†Form 410†www.fppc.ca.gov】. This dollar amount is lower than the FPPC trigger of $2,000. Both filings are required, when each are triggered, in addition to the subsequent pre-election filings.
