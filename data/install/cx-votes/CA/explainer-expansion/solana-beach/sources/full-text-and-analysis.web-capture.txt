L0@P0: City Attorney’s Impartial Analysis
L1@P0: Measure C
L2@P0: The Solana Beach City Council placed Measure C on the ballot. If approved, Measure C would
L3@P0: increase the City of Solana Beach’s (“City”) existing transient occupancy tax (“TOT”) and would
L4@P0: modernize certain TOT provisions. The TOT is only paid by persons staying at a hotel or other
L5@P0: lodging who are defined as “Transients” by existing law. ”Operator” collects the TOT from
L6@P0: transients on the City’s behalf. TOT revenue collected by the operator is then remitted to the City.
L7@P0: Measure C also addresses recent market changes.
L8@P0: Currently, the City charges a 13% TOT. If approved, this Measure would increase the TOT rate
L9@P0: by 1% for a total of 14% and would generate an estimated $250,000 per year in additional
L10@P0: revenue. The Measure’s 1% increase is a “general tax.” Thus, revenue generated by the Measure
L11@P0: is unrestricted and may be used for any general governmental purpose of the City.
L12@P0: Under the Measure, “Hotel” is broadly defined by the TOT ordinance to include any structure
L13@P0: occupied or intended or designed for occupancy by transients (30 days or less) like hotels, inns,
L14@P0: tourist homes, and short-term rentals.
L15@P0: The TOT paid is calculated based upon “rent” that is charged by operators. This Measure defines
L16@P0: “Rent” to include cleaning fees, resort fees, parking fees, pet fees, extra guest fees, cancellation
L17@P0: charges, WIFI/Internet charges, and any other fees, rates and charges as part of the total “rent”
L18@P0: charged, but excludes fees, rates and charges not collected directly.
L19@P0: This Measure also modernizes the TOT ordinance so that, if a web- or application-based hosting
L20@P0: platform, online travel company, or third-party booking vendor that facilitates rentals and
L21@P0: payments for rentals is used, the hosting platform, online travel company, or third-party booking
L22@P0: vendor is considered an agent of the operator for the purposes of collecting and remitting the TOT
L23@P0: to the City.
L24@P0: This Measure also makes provisions of state law applicable within the City’s jurisdictional
L25@P0: boundaries. Government Code Section 50990 et seq. allows the City to access certain records of
L26@P0: short-term rental facilitators and to audit the receipt of transient occupancy tax due and payable
L27@P0: to the City in certain circumstances.
L28@P0: A “yes” vote favors the Measure. A “no” vote opposes the Measure. This Measure requires a
L29@P0: simple majority of votes in favor to pass (50% + 1).

L31@P0: please call the City of Solana Beach’s City Clerk’s Office at (858) 720-2420 and a copy will be
L32@P0: mailed at no cost to you.
