                                    CITY OF FAIRFIELD

                                ORDINANCE NO. 2026-05

  ADDING A NEW ARTICLE IX (CITY CLERK) TO CHAPTER 2 (ADMINISTRATION) OF THE
     FAIRFIELD MUNICIPAL CODE TO CHANGE THE OFFICE OF CITY CLERK TO AN
                              APPOINTIVE OFFICE

WHEREAS, pursuant to Government Code Section 36502, the Office of the City Clerk of
the City of Fairfield is currently an elective office; and

WHEREAS, Government Code Section 36508 and Elections Code Section 9222 authorize
the City Council to submit to the electors the question of whether the elective city clerk
shall be an appointive office and, if a majority of voters voting on the measure approve it,
the office shall become appointive; and

WHEREAS, changing the Office of City Clerk to an appointive office would allow the City
to appoint an individual, including an appointment through employment, that has the
professional education, training, experience and knowledge in addition to the technical
skills necessary to understand and administer the wide range of laws and legal
responsibilities of the city clerk; and

WHEREAS, this measure would provide the City Council with the authority to delegate
the appointment authority for the city clerk position to the city manager to ensure the
selection of a professional with the skills and expertise needed for the position of city
clerk; and

WHEREAS, all other legal prerequisites to the adoption of this ordinance have occurred.

NOW, THEREFORE, THE PEOPLE OF THE CITY OF FAIRFIELD DO ORDAIN AS FOLLOWS:

SECTION 1. Chapter 2 of the Fairfield Municipal Code is hereby amended to add a new
Article IX to read as follows:

"ARTICLE IX — CITY CLERK

2.43 — Appointment.

        Pursuant to Government Code Sections 36501 and 36508 through 36510, the city
clerk shall be appointed and serve at the pleasure of the City Council. Notwithstanding
the foregoing, pursuant to Government Code Section 36510, the City Council may by
ordinance, vest in the city manager its authority to appoint the city clerk. The appointed
person shall not be required to be a City resident or elector.

2.44 — Compensation.

The city clerk shall receive such compensation as shall be fixed by resolution or ordinance
of the City Council.

2.45 — Powers and duties.

A.        The city clerk shall have the powers, duties and responsibilities granted to and
          imposed upon the office of the city clerk pursuant to the following:

     1.      The provisions of Chapter 2 of Part 3 of Division 3 of Title 4 of the Government
             Code (commencing with Section 40801), with the exception of the financial
             and accounting duties set forth in Government Code Sections 40802 through
             40805;

     2.      Other state laws, with the exception of those financial powers and duties
             imposed upon the city clerk by Title 4, Division 3, Part 2, Chapter 4 of the
             Government Code (commencing with Section 37201); and

     3.      The provisions of the Fairfield Municipal Code.

B.        The city clerk shall also perform such other duties as may be required of the city
          clerk by the city manager or the City Council.”

SECTION 2. If a majority of the voters voting on this measure at the November 3, 2026,
General Municipal Election approve this Ordinance, it shall take effect 10 days after the
City Council declares and certifies the results of the election, as required by applicable
law. The position of City Clerk shall be filled by appointment thereafter upon the earlier
of either a vacancy in the office or at the expiration of the current term of office of the
incumbent in accordance with Government Code Section 36510.

SECTION 3. Notwithstanding Elections Code Section 9217, the City Council may
subsequently amend this Ordinance without voter approval, except that voter approval
shall be required to re-instate the position of an elected city clerk.

SECTION 4. To the extent the terms and provisions of this Ordinance may be
inconsistent or in conflict with the terms or provisions of any prior City ordinance, motion,
resolution, rule or regulation governing the same subject, the terms of this Ordinance
shall prevail.

SECTION 5. In interpreting this Ordinance or resolving any ambiguity, this Ordinance
shall be interpreted in a manner that effectively accomplishes its stated purpose.

SECTION 6. If any section, subsection, subdivision, sentence, clause, phrase, or portion
of this Ordinance for any reason is held to be invalid or unconstitutional by the decision
of any court of competent jurisdiction, such decision shall not affect the validity of the
remaining portions of this Ordinance. The people of the City of Fairfield hereby declare
that they would have adopted this Ordinance, and each section, subsection, subdivision,
sentence, clause, phrase, or portion thereof, without regard to whether any one or more
sections, subsections, subdivisions, sentences, clauses, phrases, or portions thereof be
declared invalid or unconstitutional.

SECTION 7. The city clerk is hereby directed to publish this ordinance at least once,
within 15 days after the election results are certified, in a newspaper of general circulation
published and circulated in the City of Fairfield.

SECTION 8. The mayor is hereby directed to attest to the adoption of this Ordinance
by signing where indicated below upon certification by the city clerk that a majority of
those electors voting on this Ordinance have voted in the affirmative.

PASSED, APPROVED AND ADOPTED BY THE VOTERS OF THE CITY OF FAIRFIELD, STATE
OF CALIFORNIA, AT THE GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION HELD ON NOVEMBER 3, 2026.
