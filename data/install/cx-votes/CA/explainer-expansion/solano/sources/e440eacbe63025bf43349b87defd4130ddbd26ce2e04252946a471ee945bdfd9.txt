                        IMPARTIAL ANALYSIS OF MEASURE U
                          DIXON UNIFIED SCHOOL DISTRICT
                      GENERAL OBLIGATION BOND MEASURE

Measure U was placed on the ballot by the Governing Board of the Dixon Unified
School District. If approved, Measure U would authorize the District to issue and sell
general obligation bonds in an aggregate principal amount not to exceed $49,000,000,
at interest rates within legal limits.

Bond proceeds would be used for school facility projects identified in the measure,
including constructing and renovating classrooms and other school facilities; replacing
deteriorating portable classrooms; repairing or replacing roofs, plumbing, sewer and gas
lines, heating, ventilation, air-conditioning and electrical systems; making health, safety
and seismic improvements; improving technology infrastructure; and constructing or
renovating libraries, multipurpose rooms, cafeterias, athletic facilities, playgrounds and
other school facilities. The District would determine the priority and timing of projects,
and approval of the measure does not guarantee that all listed projects will be
completed.

The measure includes accountability requirements governing the use of bond proceeds.
Bond proceeds may be used only for the purposes specified in the measure and may
not be used for teacher or administrator salaries or other school operating expenses.
The measure requires an independent citizens’ oversight committee, annual
independent performance and financial audits, and annual reports regarding the receipt
and expenditure of bond proceeds and the status of funded projects.

Principal and interest on the bonds would be paid by property taxes levied on taxable
property within the District. According to the District’s Tax Rate Statement, the estimated
average annual tax rate is $29 per $100,000 of assessed valuation, and the estimated
highest tax rate is $30 per $100,000 of assessed valuation. The District estimates that
taxes will be collected through fiscal year 2061-62 and that total debt service, including
principal and interest, will be approximately $105,000,000. These amounts are
estimates and may vary.

A “yes” vote on Measure U means general obligation bonds totaling up to $49,000,000
may be issued to finance the school facility projects specified in the measure.

A “no” vote on Measure U means the bonds will not be authorized.

Measure U requires approval by at least 55 percent of the votes cast on the measure to
pass.
