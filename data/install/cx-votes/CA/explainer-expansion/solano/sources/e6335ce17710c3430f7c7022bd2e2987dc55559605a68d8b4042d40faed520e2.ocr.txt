WINTERS JOINT UNIFIED SCHOOL DISTRICT SCHOOL BONDS — MEASURE B
IMPARTIAL ANALYSIS BY YOLO COUNTY COUNSEL

This measure was placed on the ballot by the Board of Trustees of the Winters Joint Unified School
District, which lies within Yolo and Solano Counties. If approved, it would authorize the District to issue
and sell general obligation bonds in an amount not to exceed $29,900,000.

Under the California Constitution (Article XII A and Article XVI) and Education Code section 15266, a
school district may issue general obligation bonds for the construction, reconstruction, rehabilitation, or
replacement of school facilities, including furnishing and equipping them and acquiring or leasing real
property, upon approval of 55% of the voters voting on the measure, The bonds would be repaid, with
interest, from an ad valorem tax levied on taxable property within the District, in proportion to each
property's assessed value.

Proceeds could be used only for the school facilities projects identified in the measure's Bond Project List.
These include repairing or replacing leaky roofs, deteriorating plumbing, sewer systems, and outdated
heating and air conditioning; modernizing or renovating classrooms, restrooms, and school facilities;
making health, safety, accessibility (including ADA), and security improvements; upgrading technology,
electrical wiring, athletic fields, parking, and grounds; improving energy efficiency; abating hazardous
materials; and acquiring real property for school facilities. By law, bond proceeds may not be used for
teacher or administrator salaries, pensions, or other school operating expenses.

The measure includes accountability provisions required by law: proceeds must be deposited in a separate
account; an independent citizens' oversight committee must be appointed; and the District must conduct
annual independent performance and financial audits until the funds are spent, along with an annual report
to the Board of Trustees.

The District's Tax Rate Statement estimates the average annual tax rate required to fund the bonds at
$46.00 per $100,000 of assessed valuation, and the highest tax rate at $49.00 per $100,000 of assessed
valuation, first occurring in fiscal year 2027-28, The final fiscal year the tax is anticipated to be collected
is 2061-62, The best estimate of the total debt service (principal and interest) if all bonds are issued and
sold is $58,600,000. These estimates are based on the District's projections and are not binding; actual
rates and amounts may vary. The tax is based on a property's assessed value, not its market value.

This measure requires approval by 55% of the voters voting on it.

A"YES" vote is a vote to authorize the District to issue up to $29,900,000 in general obligation bonds
and to levy the property tax necessary to repay them.

A"NO" vote is a vote against authorizing the bonds and the associated property tax.

Dated: July 13, 2026
