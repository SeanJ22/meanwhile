​An accessible version of Official Ballot Drop Box and Vote Center Locations may be found in My Voter Portal.​​

Electioneering and Election Interference

Electioneering is when someone advocates for or against any candidate or contest on the ballot within 100 feet of a Vote Center, Official Ballot Drop Box, or our office. This is illegal pursuant to Elections Code 319.5.​ If you witness any electioneering, please report it.

If you are in immediate danger, please get to safety, and call 9-1-1.

If you are safe and calling to report an incident, please call (916) 875-6100 or email precinctoperations@saccounty.gov​.

For additional information, visit our webpage on electioneering.
