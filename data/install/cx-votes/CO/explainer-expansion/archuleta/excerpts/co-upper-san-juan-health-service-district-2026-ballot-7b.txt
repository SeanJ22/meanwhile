Upper San Juan Health Service District Ballot
Issue 7B
WITHOUT RAISING TAXES, SHALL THE UPPER SAN
JUAN HEALTH SERVICE DISTRICT (WHICH
OPERATES PAGOSA SPRINGS MEDICAL CENTER
AND PROVIDES EMS/AMBULANCE SERVICES,
HOSPITAL AND OUTPATIENT MEDICAL CARE TO THE
COMMUNITY) BE AUTHORIZED TO CONTINUE TO
COLLECT, RETAIN, AND SPEND ALL REVENUES OF
THE DISTRICT IN EACH FISCAL YEAR HEREAFTER
WITHOUT REGARD TO ANY REVENUE OR SPENDING
LIMITATION CONTAINED IN ARTICLE X, SECTION 20
OF THE COLORADO CONSTITUTION OR SECTION
29-1-301, C.R.S.?
