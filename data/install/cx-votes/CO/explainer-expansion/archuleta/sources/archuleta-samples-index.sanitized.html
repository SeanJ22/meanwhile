Voting Location Updates

Voting Location Updates

The drop box at Town Hall has been closed due to flooding. 

We will have a mobile drop box at

10 Solomon Drive (Parking lot of Mountain Spirits Liquor and Feather Your Nest) during the following hours
Monday 8:00 a.m. – 4:00 p.m. and
Election Day, November 3, 2026, 7:00 a.m. – 7:00 p.m. 

Please look for the Archuleta County vehicle that will have a bipartisan team of judges and a sealed box collecting ballots.

The drop box at the TARA Community Center located at 333 Milton Lane in Arboles is still available until Election Day at 7:00 p.m.  

The VSPC at the Ross Aragon Community Center will now be located at the Archuleta County Court House located at 109 Harman Park Drive, Pagosa Springs, CO.  They will be open 7:00 a.m. – 7:00 p.m. on Election Day ONLY.

The VSPC at the Archuleta County Extension Building located at 344 Hwy 84 will be open as planned at 7:00 a.m. – 7:00 p.m. on Election Day ONLY.

The main VSPC at the Archuleta County Election Office located at 305 Village Drive will be open Monday, November 2nd, 2026, 8:00 a.m. – 4:00 p.m. 

and on Election Day, November 3rd, 2026 7:00 a.m. – 7:00 p.m.  You can drop off your mail ballot or vote in person.  

We have a Joint La Plata/Archuleta County VSPC located at Pine River Library located at 395 Bayfield Center Drive, Bayfield, CO for Archuleta County residents to vote in person ONLY on Election Day.

Thank you for your understanding and patience as we navigate these unprecedented conditions.  Stay safe Archuleta County.
