New Wave of Scam Texts Impersonating Colorado DMV The Colorado DMV has identified a new surge of scam texts that are impersonating the department.  These messages are NOT from the Colorado DMV, even if they use the DMV logo. The DMV does not initiate contact via unsolicited text messages or emails to demand payment for tickets, or to threaten prosecution or suspension of privileges in this manner.

  • Do NOT click on any links.
  • Do NOT share any personal or financial information.
  • Do NOT reply to the message.

If your are concerned about the status of your driver's license, vehicle registration, or any potential tickets, you should contact the Colorado DMV directly through the DMV’s official website or by phone at (303) 205-5600. You should not use any contact information provided in a suspicious text and/or email.
