Boulder County Ballot Question 201

If the membership of the Boulder County Board of Commissioners is increased from a three member board to a five member board, by what method shall the Boulder County Board of Commissioners be elected? (Voters can choose (1) or (2)):

Five commissioners resident in districts to be elected only by voters resident in the district; or

Three commissioners resident in districts to be elected only by voters resident in the district; plus two commissioners resident in any district to be elected by voters of the whole county.

If the membership of the Boulder County Board of Commissioners is increased from a three member board to a five member board, by what method shall the Boulder County Board of Commissioners be elected? (Voters can choose (1) or (2)):

Five commissioners resident in districts to be elected only by voters resident in the district; or

Three commissioners resident in districts to be elected only by voters resident in the district; plus two commissioners resident in any district to be elected by voters of the whole county.

Commonly Answered Questions Regarding Ballot Questions 200 and 201

How did this get on the ballot?

A citizen petition, complying with Colorado Revised Statutes (C.R.S. 30‑10‑306.5(3)), secured enough signatures (at least 8% of Boulder County’s qualified electors) to get this on the ballot.

As required by C.R.S. 30-10-306.5(3), the petition specified two alternative methods for electing five commissioners that would be included on the ballot for voters to decide.

On August 25, 2026, the Board of County Commissioners (BOCC) approved Resolution 2026-056 certifying the ballot language exactly as submitted. The certified ballot language was then submitted to the Clerk & Recorder for inclusion on the November 2026 ballot.

Did the county modify the petition language?

No, the county did not modify the petition language. The Clerk & Recorder verified that the petition conformed to Colorado election laws and was sufficient for placement on the ballot. The BOCC certified the language exactly as the petitioners drafted it, without alterations.

What happens after the vote?

If approved by Boulder County voters, the BOCC will pass a resolution to expand its membership from three to five. Commissioners in the newly expanded BOCC will be elected in accordance with the method voters selected.

If rejected, the BOCC will remain at three commissioners with no changes.

If passed by Boulder County voters, when will this take effect?

If voters approve Question 1 in November 2026:

The Board of County Commissioners will officially expand from three to five members, with five commissioners in office, starting January 2029.

In 2027, the BOCC must convene a redistricting commission to approve one or more plans to divide the relevant county into as many districts as there are county commissioners elected by voters of their district pursuant to C.R.S. 30-10-306.1, -306.4.

The increased number of commissioner candidate races will be on the ballot at the next general election, scheduled for November 7, 2028.

After the expansion, commissioners will be elected according to the method selected by voters in Question 2 and pursuant to C.R.S. 30-10-306.5(5) and (6).
