The following summaries were prepared from comments filed by persons FOR Ballot Issue
SC:
• A YES vote on Issue SC will help keep experienced teachers in Weld Re-8
classrooms, expand career training for our students, and prevent a midyear budget
cut this January.
• Weld Re-8 is losing teachers faster than any nearby district. Our starting teacher
salary is $48,000, while neighboring districts start between $S3,000 and $6S,000.
That pay gap contributes to a 23% annual turnover rate, well above the 17%
statewide average.
• Nearly a quarter of our teachers leave every year, which drives up class sizes,
disrupts learning, and forces the District to recruit and train replacements year
after year.
• At the same time, our students are seeking career training opportunities the
District cannot currently provide. If approved, Ballot Issue SC funding would be
used to:
• Raise teacher and staff pay to help close the gap with neighboring districts
• Strengthen existing Career and Technical Education (CTE) pathways in
Agriculture, Business, Construction, Culinary Arts, Early Childhood
Education, and Information Technology/Cybersecurity
• Add instructors so more students can earn industry certifications and college
credit
• Open the door to new career pathways, including Healthcare
• Continue supporting teachers, staff, and instructional materials currently
funded through the existing levy
• Much of Ballot Issue SC is a renewal, not a new tax. The existing mill levy override
that helps pay teacher and staff salaries expires in December. Without renewal, the District will have to make staffing and program cuts starting in January, when
classes are already in session.
• Earlier this year, the District gathered community feedback on two funding
proposals, an operating MLO and a facilities bond. Residents said teacher pay and
career pathways should come first and that the District should not pursue both
measures at once. The District listened and removed the bond proposal from
consideration.
• Approval of Ballot Issue SC would provide many benefits, including:
• Attracting and retaining quality teachers and staff
• Reducing turnover that disrupts classrooms and drives up class sizes
• Preparing students for in-demand jobs
• Reducing the need for students to seek career training opportunities outside
the District
• Preventing midyear cuts to staffing and programs
• Ensuring tax dollars stay local and support Weld Re-8 schools
• Protecting property values
• Based on current assessed valuation, the oil and gas industry would pay more than
half of the taxes associated with the MLO. For a $400,000 home, the estimated
net impact of Issue SC is about $9/month. The tax impact would be even lower
for homeowners who qualify for and receive state property tax exemptions.
• Ballot Issue SC is a focused, fiscally responsible proposal that would help Weld Re8 retain quality teachers, expand career training opportunities for our students,
and keep every dollar local.
