City and County of Broomfield Ballot Question 1B

Shall the City and County of Broomfield approve the terms of a twenty-year franchise
contract, starting January 1, 2028, with Public Service Company of Colorado (Xcel Energy)
to continue Xcel’s program for undergrounding overhead electrical lines, maintaining
infrastructure safety and partnering with the City and County for emergency management
coordination, and to require that Xcel Energy continue to pay to the City and County for
reasonable use of public rights-of-way and other City and County property necessary to
provide safe and reliable utility services for the benefit of the City and County, its
residents and its businesses as set forth in Ordinance No. 2307?

Ballot Question 1B proposes a new 20-year franchise agreement with Public Service
Company of Colorado (“Xcel Energy”), commencing on January 1, 2028. Xcel has a
current franchise agreement with CCOB, approved by the voters in 2007, that will
expire December 31, 2027.
The franchise agreement, attached to Ordinance 2307, sets forth the specific terms,
fees, compensation, and other conditions of a gas and electric franchise agreement
with Xcel Energy. It grants the non-exclusive right to use public rights-of-way and
property to install, maintain, and repair infrastructure necessary to provide gas and
electric service. Under the proposed agreement, the franchise fee (3%) and
undergrounding fund contributions (1%) paid by Xcel Energy to CCOB remain identical
to those in the current agreement.
The franchise agreement does not set utility rates, and it does not govern Xcel
Energy’s plans, designs, or builds of utility infrastructure.
