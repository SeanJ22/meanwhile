City and County of Broomfield Ballot Question 1C

Shall Section 4.3 of the Broomfield Home Rule Charter be amended so future mayors
will serve a 4-year term and be limited to two consecutive terms starting at the
general municipal election to be held in 2027?

Ballot Question 1C proposes amending Section 4.3 of the Broomfield Home Rule
Charter to change the mayoral term of office from a 2-year term to a 4-year term,
while also changing the term limits from three consecutive terms to two consecutive
terms. If approved, this change would go into effect with the 2027 mayoral election.
It would not impact the current mayor’s term of office or term limits.
