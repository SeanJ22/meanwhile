## Election Judge Interest Form

Interested in being an election judge? Please fill out this [application](https://chaffeeclerk.colorado.gov/sites/g/files/lrnvjt2971/files/Election%20Judge%20Interest%20Form%20(1).pdf) and return it to [elections@chaffeecounty.org](mailto:elections@chaffeecounty.org) or drop it off at 104 Crestone Ave, Salida, CO.

### View Past Election Information Including Ballot Images Here

View results and ballot images from past elections through [Ballot Verifier](https://chaffeepastelections.colorado.gov/).
