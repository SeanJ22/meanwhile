COSTILLA COUNTY AMBULANCE DISTRICT BALLOT
ISSUE 6A
SHALL COSTILLA COUNTY AMBULANCE DISTRICT
TAXES BE INCREASED $550,057 (FIRST FULL FISCAL
YEAR DOLLAR INCREASE) ANNUALLY, BEGINNING IN
LEVY YEAR 2026 (FOR COLLECTION IN CALENDAR YEAR
2027) BY INCREASING THE DISTRICT'S EXISTING
PROPERTY TAX BY 3.0 MILLS, FOR THE FOLLOWING
PURPOSES:
MEETING THE NEEDS OF THE COMMUNITY BY
HIRING ADDITIONAL EMERGENCY MEDICAL
PROVIDERS AND OTHER STAFF NEEDED TO FULLY
STAFF THE DISTRICT'S OPERATIONS, INCLUDING
THE DISTRICT'S FACILITY IN NORTH COSTILLA
COUNTY;
PROVIDING NECESSARY TRAINING AND EDUCATION
TO THE DISTRICT'S EMERGENCY MEDICAL
PROVIDERS AND STAFF TO PROVIDE EMERGENCY
MEDICAL SERVICES AND TO SERVE THE NEEDS OF
THE COMMUNITY;
REPAIRING AND REPLACING AGING LIFE-SAVING
EQUIPMENT, VEHICLES, AND APPARATUS;
ATTRACTING AND RETAINING ADEQUATE NUMBERS
OF EMERGENCY MEDICAL PROVIDERS AND OTHER
STAFF FOR VITAL EMERGENCY SERVICES;
ACCOUNTING FOR THE INCREASING COST OF
MEDICAL SUPPLIES AND EQUIPMENT; AND
OFFSETTING OTHER ANTICIPATED LOST SOURCES
OF FUNDING/REVENUE FOR THE DISTRICT'S
LIFE-SAVING OPERATIONS,
AND OTHER OPERATIONAL AND CAPITAL NEEDS, AND
SHALL ALL REVENUE AND ANY EARNINGS ON THIS TAX
CONSTITUTE A PERMANENT VOTER-APPROVED
REVENUE CHANGE WITHIN THE MEANING OF ARTICLE
X, SECTION 20 OF THE COLORADO CONSTITUTION AND
AN EXCEPTION TO THE LIMITATIONS SET FORTH IN
SECTION 29-1-301 OF THE COLORADO REVISED
STATUTES, AND ANY OTHER LAW WITH THE
REQUIREMENT THAT THE FUNDS WILL BE SUBJECT TO
AN ANNUAL INDEPENDENT AUDIT PUBLISHED BY THE
DISTRICT?
