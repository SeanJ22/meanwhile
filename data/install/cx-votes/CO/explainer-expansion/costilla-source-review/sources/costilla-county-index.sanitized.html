                                                                Clerk & Recorder

                                                                Welcome to the official homepage of the Costilla County Clerk & Recorder.
                                                                Our office is committed to providing accurate records, fair elections, and professional service to the residents of Costilla County. 

                                                                Election Day: Tuesday, November 3, 2026

                                                                 

                                                                Voter Service and Polling Center (VSPC)

                                                                October 19–23: 8:00 AM – 4:30 PM
                                                                Saturday, October 24: 10:00 AM – 2:00 PM
                                                                October 26–30: 8:00 AM – 4:30 PM
                                                                Saturday, October 31: 10:00 AM – 2:00 PM

                                                                24-hour ballot drop boxes:
                                                                Clerk & Recorder's Office – North Entrance, 400 Gasper Street, San Luis

                                                                Blanca Town Hall - 601 Main Street, Blanca
                                                                Both close at 7:00 PM on November 3.
                                                                All ballots must be received by 7:00 PM on Election Day
