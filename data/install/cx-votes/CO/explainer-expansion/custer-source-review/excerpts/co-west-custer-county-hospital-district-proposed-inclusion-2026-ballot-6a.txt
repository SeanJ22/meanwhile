West Custer County Hospital District Ballot
Issue 6A
Inclusion of East Custer County/Wetmore Area
Shall the following described area become a part of the West
Custer County Hospital District upon the following conditions,
if any?
Description of Area: All real property located in eastern
Custer County and not presently located within the service
territory of either the West Custer County Hospital District or
the Rye Fire Protection District.
Summary of Conditions: None.
