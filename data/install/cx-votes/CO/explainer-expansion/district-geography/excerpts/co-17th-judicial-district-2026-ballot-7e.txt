Broomfield Ballot Question 7E - 17th Judicial District
DA Term Limits
Ballot question language
“Shall the term limits for District Attorney of the 17th Judicial District be limited to
no more than three consecutive terms of office, consistent with other county offices
in Adams County and the City and County of Broomfield?”

Summary
The 17th Judicial District includes Adams County and Broomfield. It is served by one
District Attorney, who is voted in by the people of Adams County and Broomfield.
Currently, the District Attorney is term-limited to two consecutive terms. This
measure would increase the term limits for the elected District Attorney from two
terms to three. State law permits the residents who live in the judicial district to
modify those term limits. Article XVIII, Sec. 11, Colo. Const.; C.R.S. 1-4-1401, et seq.
The ballot question must be the same for both Broomfield and Adams County voters.
