City of Castle Pines Ballot Question 300

Should the City of Castle Pines Home Rule Charter be amended to include the following provision: Property in
excess of 20 acres owned or controlled by a single owner or ownership group or that are part of a common plan
of development or related transactions may be annexed into the City only upon approval by the voters at an
election of the registered electors of the City of Castle Pines, Colorado, and for purposes of this provision, any
single, successive, cumulative, serial or simultaneous annexations of multiple parcels shall be aggregated and
treated as a single annexation as provided in C.R.S. Section 31-12-104(1)(a)?
