City of Castle Pines Ballot Question 301

Should the City of Castle Pines Home Rule Charter be amended to include the following provision: Any parcel
over 20 acres owned or controlled by a single owner or ownership group annexed within 180 days of the
effective date of this section shall be referred for ratification at an election of the registered electors of the City
of Castle Pines, Colorado, and if no such annexation occurs within such 180-day period, this section may be
repealed by Council ordinance without a vote of the registered electors of the City of Castle Pines, Colorado?
