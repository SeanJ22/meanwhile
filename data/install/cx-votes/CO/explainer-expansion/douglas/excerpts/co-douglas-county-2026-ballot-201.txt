Douglas County Ballot Question 201

If the membership of the Douglas County Board of Commissioners is increased from a three- member board to a
five-member board, by what method shall the Douglas County Board of Commissioners be elected? (Vote for
One)
Five District Commissioners who reside in districts to be elected by the voters only by the voters resident in the
district; or
Three District Commissioners who reside in districts to be elected by the voters only by the voters resident in
the district, and two At-Large Commissioners to be elected by the voters of the whole county.
