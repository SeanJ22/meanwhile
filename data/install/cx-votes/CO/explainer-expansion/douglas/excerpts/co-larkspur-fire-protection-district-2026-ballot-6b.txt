Larkspur Fire Protection District Ballot Issue 6B

SHALL LARKSPUR FIRE PROTECTION DISTRICT (LFPD) DEBT BE INCREASED $4.5 MILLION, WITH A REPAYMENT
COST OF UP TO $6.4 MILLION, AND SHALL DISTRICT TAXES BE INCREASED UP TO $427,333 ANNUALLY TO PAY
SUCH DEBT, SUCH DEBT TO BE USED FOR CAPITAL IMPROVEMENTS OF THE DISTRICT, INCLUDING:
• PURCHASE OF FIRE/EMS EQUIPMENT AND/OR APPARATUS TO REPLACE AGING AND OUTDATED
RESPONSE VEHICLES;
• DESIGN, ACQUISITION, AND INFRASTRUCTURE IMPROVEMENTS FOR FUTURE STATION IN THE
SOUTHEASTERN AREA OF THE DISTRICT RESPONSE JURISDICTION;
• DESIGN, INSTALLATION, AND CONSTRUCTION OF TRAINING GROUNDS INFRASTRUCTURE;
• PURCHASE OF TRAINING GROUNDS SIMULATORS AND EQUIPMENT TO TRAIN FIREFIGHTERS IN
MULTIPLE CERTIFICATION LEVELS;
• ACQUIRING CAPITAL ASSETS BY EXERCISING THE DISTRICT'S BEST OPTIONS TO PREPAY EXISTING LEASE
OBLIGATIONS
SUCH DEBT TO BE EVIDENCED BY THE ISSUANCE OF GENERAL OBLIGATION BONDS OR ANY OTHER MULTIPLE
YEAR FINANCIAL OBLIGATION OF THE DISTRICT; SUCH BONDS TO BE SOLD IN ONE SERIES OR MORE, FOR A
PRICE ABOVE OR BELOW THE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT OF SUCH SERIES, ON TERMS AND CONDITIONS, AND WITH
SUCH MATURITIES AS PERMITTED BY LAW AND AS THE DISTRICT MAY DETERMINE, INCLUDING PROVISIONS FOR
REDEMPTION OF THE BONDS PRIOR TO MATURITY WITH OR WITHOUT PAYMENT OF THE PREMIUM OF NOT TO
EXCEED THREE PERCENT; AND SHALL SUCH TAX REVENUES AND THE EARNINGS FROM THE INVESTMENT OF
SUCH BOND PROCEEDS AND TAX REVENUES BE COLLECTED, RETAINED AND SPENT AS A VOTER APPROVED
REVENUE CHANGE UNDER ARTICLE X, SECTION 20 OF THE COLORADO CONSTITUTION OR ANY OTHER LAW?
