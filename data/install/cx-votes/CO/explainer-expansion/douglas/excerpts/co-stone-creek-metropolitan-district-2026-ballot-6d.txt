Stone Creek Metropolitan District Ballot Question 6D

Shall Ballot Issue 5C, unanimously approved by three voters at a special election held on November 4, 2014, be
forever repealed and rescinded effective immediately, which ballot issue authorizes Stone Creek Metropolitan
District to enter into one or more multiple fiscal year financial obligations evidenced by intergovernmental
agreements or contracts and to increase ad valorem taxes up to $1 million annually to fund such agreements?
