Town of Basalt Ballot Issue 3A:
Increase Sales Tax for Public Services Facilities
SHALL THE TOWN OF BASALT’S SALES TAXES BE INCREASED
BY NOT MORE THAN $1,700,000 IN THE FIRST FULL FISCAL
YEAR (2027) AND BY SUCH ADDITIONAL AMOUNTS AS ARE
GENERATED ANNUALLY THEREAFTER BY INCREASING, FOR
A LIMITED PERIOD NOT TO EXCEED TWENTY YEARS, THE
TOWN’S SALES TAX BY 0.5% WHICH SHALL TAKE EFFECT
JANUARY 1, 2027, AND WHICH REVENUES THEREFROM SHALL
BE USED FOR THE ACQUISITION, PLANNING, CONSTRUCTION,
OPERATION, MAINTENANCE, REPAIR, AND REPLACEMENT
OF PUBLIC SERVICES FACILITIES AND RELATED
INFRASTRUCTURE AND EQUIPMENT; AND SHALL THE TOWN
BE AUTHORIZED TO COLLECT, RETAIN, AND SPEND SUCH
REVENUES, AND ANY INVESTMENT INCOME EARNED FROM
SUCH REVENUES, NOTWITHSTANDING ANY APPLICABLE
REVENUE OR EXPENDITURE LIMITATION IMPOSED BY
ARTICLE X, SECTION 20 OF THE COLORADO CONSTITUTION
OR ANY OTHER LAW?
