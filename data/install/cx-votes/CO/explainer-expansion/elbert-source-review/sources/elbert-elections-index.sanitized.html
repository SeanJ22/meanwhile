Elections

Elbert County Elections Mission Statement

In Elbert County Elections Department, our mission is to always seek the truth. We believe in transparency, asking questions, answering questions, and sharing information. We make an honest effort to maintain public confidence by registering eligible voters and conducting honest and impartial elections in a fair, efficient, and accurate manner
