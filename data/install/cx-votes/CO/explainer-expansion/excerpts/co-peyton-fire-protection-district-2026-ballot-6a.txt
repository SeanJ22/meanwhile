Peyton Fire Ballot Issue 6A

SHALL PEYTON FIRE PROTECTION DISTRICT TAXES BE INCREASED $327,903 ANNUALLY COMMENCING JANUARY 1,
2027, OR BY SUCH AMOUNT AS MAY BE RAISED, BY INCREASING THE DISTRICT’S PROPERTY TAX RATE BY AN
ADDITIONAL 4.85 MILLS, FOR THE PURPOSES OF PROVIDING FIRE PROTECTION, AMBULANCE, EMERGENCY
MEDICAL AND PARAMEDIC RESPONSE, RESCUE, SAFETY, AND SUPPORT SERVICES FOR THE RESIDENTS, PROPERTY
OWNERS, AND BUSINESSES SERVED BY THE DISTRICT, INCLUDING:

        • HIRE FULL-TIME FIREFIGHTER, EMERGENCY MEDICAL TECHNICIANS AND PARAMEDIC STAFF TO
          EFFECTIVELY AND EFFICIENTLY RESPOND TO SERVICE REQUESTS AND EMERGENCIES.

        • PROVIDE AND MAINTAIN EMERGENCY VEHICLES AND EQUIPMENT AND MODERNIZE FACILITIES TO
          BETTER PROTECT THE PUBLIC AND FIRST RESPONDERS.

AND SHALL ALL REVENUE AND ANY EARNINGS ON THIS TAX CONSTITUTE A PERMANENT VOTER-APPROVED
REVENUE CHANGE WITHIN THE MEANING OF ARTICLE X, SECTION 20 OF THE COLORADO CONSTITUTION AND AN
EXCEPTION TO THE LIMITATIONS SET FORTH IN SECTION 29-1-301 OF THE COLORADO REVISED STATUTES, AND
ANY OTHER LAW?
