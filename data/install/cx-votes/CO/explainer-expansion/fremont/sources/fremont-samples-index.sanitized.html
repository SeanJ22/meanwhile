Let's Talk!
Justin D Grantham, County Clerk

The Fremont County Election Division is dedicated to serving our community through secure, fair, and transparent elections. Operating under the Fremont County Clerk's Office, we coordinate and manage all local, state, and federal elections within our county. Our team works tirelessly to maintain accurate voter rolls, oversee voter registration, and conduct various types of elections throughout the year. We serve approximately 35,000+ registered voters, ensuring every eligible citizen has the opportunity to participate in our democratic process. Led by County Clerk Justin D Grantham, we strive to provide exceptional customer service and maintain the highest standards of election integrity. From ballot preparation to collaboration with special districts and state departments, we're committed to upholding the cornerstone of local democracy - your right to vote.

PLEASE CONFIRM!

You are attempting to contact the Fremont County Election Division. Please make sure your request is related to the following:

  • Voter registration and voter records
  • Ballots, drop boxes, and voting locations
  • Election results and canvass records
  • Candidate, petition, and TABOR filings
  • Election judges and poll worker service

We do not handle motor vehicle titles or registrations, recording, marriage licenses, court documents, driver's licenses, law enforcement/arrest records, or county finance and administration records. Please contact those offices with questions and/or requests.
