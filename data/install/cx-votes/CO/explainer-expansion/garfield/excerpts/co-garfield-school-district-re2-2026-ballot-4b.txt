Garfield School District RE-2 Ballot Issue 4B: Bond for School Building and Safety Improvements
WITHOUT IMPOSING A NEW TAX, SHALL GARFIELD SCHOOL DISTRICT NO. RE-2 DEBT BE INCREASED UP TO $118 MILLION
WITH A REPAYMENT COST OF UP TO $225 MILLION, AND SHALL ANNUAL BOND REDEMPTION TAXES APPROVED BY DISTRICT
VOTERS IN 2006 AND CURRENTLY RECEIVED BY THE DISTRICT BE EXTENDED TO PAY THE DEBT TO BE INCURRED FOR THE
PURPOSE OF ACQUIRING, CONSTRUCTING, REPAIRING, EQUIPPING AND IMPROVING DISTRICT CAPITAL ASSETS, TO THE
EXTENT OF AVAILABLE FUNDS, INCLUDING:
CONSTRUCTING A NEW ACCESS ROAD AT RIFLE HIGH SCHOOL TO IMPROVE SAFETY AND TRAFFIC CIRCULATION FOR
STUDENTS, STAFF, AND COMMUNITY MEMBERS;
RENOVATING SCIENCE CLASSROOMS AND AGING LAB SYSTEMS, AND CONVERTING OUTDATED COMPUTER LABS INTO
DEDICATED CAREER-READY AND ENGINEERING LABS AT RIFLE HIGH SCHOOL;
CONSTRUCTING AN ADDITION AND RELATED RENOVATIONS AT COAL RIDGE HIGH SCHOOL TO EXPAND TRADES,
AGRICULTURAL, AND CAREER-READY TRAINING, ATHLETIC FACILITIES, AND PERFORMING ARTS SPACES;
BUILDING A NEW EARLY LEARNING CENTER TO PROVIDE ESSENTIAL EARLY CHILDHOOD EDUCATION AND CARE SERVICES;
ACQUIRING AND CONSTRUCTING AFFORDABLE RENTAL HOUSING IN RIFLE, SILT, AND NEW CASTLE TO ATTRACT AND
RETAIN DISTRICT TEACHERS AND STAFF (SUCH AS BUS DRIVERS, PARAPROFESSIONALS, AND KITCHEN STAFF); AND
UPGRADING DISTRICT-WIDE TECHNOLOGY INFRASTRUCTURE AND ACQUIRING RELATED CAPITAL ASSETS, CRITICAL FOR
TEACHING AND LEARNING AT ALL SCHOOLS;
THROUGH ISSUANCE OF GENERAL OBLIGATION BONDS WHICH SHALL BEAR INTEREST, MATURE, BE SUBJECT TO
REDEMPTION, WITH OR WITHOUT PREMIUM, AND BE ISSUED AT SUCH TIME, AT SUCH PRICE (AT, ABOVE OR BELOW PAR) AND
IN SUCH MANNER AND CONTAINING SUCH TERMS, NOT INCONSISTENT WITH THIS BALLOT ISSUE, AS THE BOARD OF
EDUCATION MAY DETERMINE; AND SHALL AD VALOREM PROPERTY TAXES BE LEVIED WITHOUT LIMIT AS TO THE MILL RATE
BUT BY NOT MORE THAN $9.2 MILLION ANNUALLY, TO GENERATE AMOUNTS SUFFICIENT IN EACH YEAR TO PAY THE
PRINCIPAL OF AND INTEREST ON THE BONDS AND TO FUND ANY RESERVES FOR THE PAYMENT THEREOF?
