  1. Purpose

Purpose

The purpose of the Elections Office is to provide registration and voting services to those who are eligible to vote in Gunnison County so they can successfully cast their ballot in a timely and accurate manner.

Frequently Asked Questions

For some more in-depth information you can head over to our Elections FAQ for a deeper dive. Currently it covers Registrations & Ballots, Types of Elections, TABOR Information, and how to become an Election Judge. If your question isn't answered here feel free to contact us at ksimillion@gunnisoncounty.org (please allow a couple days for a reply) or call us at (970) 641-7927.

Register to Vote / Update Voter Information

You have options to register to vote or to update your voter information. If you follow this linkhave questions please call the Gunnison County Elections Office at (970) 641-7927.

  • Online: You can register or update your information online by visiting GoVoteColorado.gov. You can also review your current registration status, your mail ballot status, change your name, affiliation, or withdraw your registration if you have moved out of Gunnison County or the State of Colorado.
  • In Person / Over the Phone: You can visit the Clerk & Recorders office located in the Blackstock Government Center at 221 N. Wisconsin St. in Gunnison, call us at (970) 641-7927, or visit any of our open VSPC's up to and including Election Day to register or update your information and receive a ballot.
  • Pre-fill & Drop Off: If you would like to fill out a Voter Registration Form to mail in or drop off at the Clerk's Office, you can download those forms here.
    • Voter Registration Form - English (PDF)
    • Voter Registration Form - Spanish (PDF)

Ballot Tracking

Colorado offers the BallotTrax service to track your ballot. You will be notified when your ballot has been mailed, when it has been received by our office, and when it has been processed. If you are not already signed up for Ballot Trax and would like to, click this link to open the BallotTrax website and follow the prompts.

Deceased Voter

If a family member has passed, you can print the Notification of Deceased Voter (PDF) form and return it to us by mail, fax, or drop it off at the office.

Mail Ballot Election

Colorado law now allows for same day voter registration as long as the voter meets the 22 day residency requirement and can provide acceptable identification (click here to see acceptable forms) to vote their ballot. For those voters registering for the first time or changing precincts, applications can be made in the County Clerk's Office during regular business hours of Monday - Friday 8:00 AM until 5:00 PM. Replacement ballots can be obtained during those hours as well during an active election.

Military Absentee Vote by Mail

Uniformed Overseas Citizens Absentee Voting Act (UOCAVA)

Any registered voter of Gunnison County residing overseas and serving in the United States Military have a couple ways to apply for UOCAVA status.

  1. Download an online Application for the Federal Postcard application and mail to the following address:
    Gunnison County Clerk & Recorder
    Elections Division
    221 N. Wisconsin St,
    Gunnison, CO 81230
  2. Or visit https://www.GoVoteColorado.gov to locate and edit your voter registration information

Additional Information & Resources

  • Ballot Information Booklet (Blue Book): The Colorado Legislative Council has developed a ballot information booklet to provide voters with the text, title, and a fair and impartial analysis of each initiated or referred constitutional amendment, law, or question on the ballot. The analysis must include a summary of the measure, the major arguments both for and against the measure, and a brief fiscal assessment of the measure. The analysis may also include any other information that will help voters understand the purpose and effect of a measure. The website contains an English version, Spanish version, and an Audio Book version.
  • Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ): For answers to the most commonly asked questions, please visit the Colorado Secretary of State Elections Center.
  • Acceptable Forms of Identification: To see what forms of Identification is needed to register to vote in Colorado, visit the Colorado Secretary of State's website.

Ballot Dropbox

What is a Ballot Dropbox? A Ballot Dropbox is a secure box that allows for 24/7 drop off of ballots during the election period. These boxes are under 24/7 recorded surveillance and placed in accessible locations for easy drop off.
