TOWN OF LAKE CITY, COLORADO
RESOLUTION NO. 2026-09
A RESOLUTION SUBMITTING TO THE REGISTERED ELECTORS OF THE TOWN
OF LAKE CITY A BALLOT QUESTION AT THE NOVEMBER 2026 GENERAL
STATEWIDE ELECTION CONCERNING WHETHER THE NUMBER OF TRUSTEES
ON THE TOWN OF LAKE CITY BOARD OF TRUSTEES SHOULD BE REDUCED
FROM SIX TO FOUR

WHEREAS, if a majority of the registered electors voting on the question approve the
reduction in the number of trustees, the Board of Trustees shall consist of one mayor and four
trustees, three trustees shall constitute a quorum for the transaction of business, and the reduction
shall be implemented in a manner that does not shorten the term for which any member of the
Board of Trustees was previously elected; and

Section 2. Submission of Ballot Question.
Pursuant to applicable provisions of Colorado law and the ordinances of the Town of
Lake City, the Board of Trustees hereby submits to the registered electors of the Town at the
November 2026 General Statewide Election, coordinated by Hinsdale County, the following
ballot question, in substantially the same form, to appear on the mail ballot:
SHALL THE NUMBER OF TRUSTEES ON THE TOWN OF LAKE CITY BOARD OF
TRUSTEES BE REDUCED FROM SIX TO FOUR, TO TAKE EFFECT BEGINNING
WITH THE TOWN’S 2028 MUNICIPAL ELECTION, IN ORDER TO PROMOTE
MORE EFFICIENT TOWN GOVERNANCE AND REDUCE THE DIFFICULTY OF
FILLING ELECTED POSITIONS?

Section 5. Effect of Voter Approval.
If a majority of the registered electors voting on the ballot question vote in favor of the
question, the question shall be deemed to have passed. The number of trustees on the Board of
Trustees shall be reduced from six to four, and the legislative and corporate authority of the
Town shall thereafter be vested in the Board of Trustees consisting of one mayor and four
trustees.
Approval of the ballot question shall not have the effect of reducing or shortening the
term for which any member of the Board of Trustees was previously elected. The reduction shall
be implemented in accordance with applicable law and in a manner that preserves the full elected
terms of current trustees.
