TOWN OF LAKE CITY, COLORADO
RESOLUTION NO. 2026-08
A RESOLUTION SUBMITTING TO THE REGISTERED ELECTORS OF THE TOWN
OF LAKE CITY A BALLOT QUESTION TO CONSIDER CHANGING THE TOWN OF
LAKE CITY REGULAR MUNICIPAL ELECTION FROM APRIL TO NOVEMBER IN
EVEN-NUMBERED YEARS, BEGINNING IN NOVEMBER OF 2028, AT THE
NOVEMBER 2026 GENERAL STATEWIDE ELECTION COORDINATED BY
HINSDALE COUNTY

WHEREAS, consistent with C.R.S. § 31-10-109, if a majority of the registered electors
voting on the question support a change in the election date, the Board of Trustees anticipates: (i)
entering into an intergovernmental agreement with Hinsdale County before each municipal
election, or participating in whatever other process is required by Hinsdale County, to allow
Hinsdale County to conduct the Town’s municipal elections during coordinated elections; and (ii)
adopting an ordinance (a) changing the election date for regular municipal elections from the
first Tuesday in April of even-numbered years to the Tuesday following the first Monday in
November of even-numbered years, beginning in November of 2028, and (b) extending the terms
of office of the Trustees of the Town Board elected during the April 2, 2024 election from April
2028 to November of 2028 and those elected during the April 7, 2026 election from April of
2030 to November of 2030 to ensure that their terms are not shortened; and

Section 2. Submission of Ballot Question.
Pursuant to applicable provisions of Colorado law and the ordinances of the Town of
Lake City, the Board of Trustees hereby refers to the registered electors of the Town at the
November 2026 General Statewide Election to be held on November 3rd
, 2026, coordinated by
Hinsdale County, the following ballot question, in substantially the same form, to appear on the
mail ballot of the 2026 General Statewide Election:
SHALL THE TOWN OF LAKE CITY’S REGULAR MUNICIPAL ELECTION BE
MOVED FROM THE FIRST TUESDAY IN APRIL OF EVEN-NUMBERED YEARS TO
THE TUESDAY IN NOVEMBER OF EVEN-NUMBERED YEARS WHEN GENERAL
STATEWIDE ELECTIONS ARE HELD TO PROMOTE INCREASED VOTER
PARTICIPATION, REDUCE TOWN ELECTION EXPENSES, AND ALLOW THE
TOWN TO PARTICIPATE IN ELECTIONS COORDINATED BY HINSDALE
COUNTY?

Section 5. Effect of Voter Approval
If a majority of the registered electors voting on the ballot question vote in favor of the
question, the question shall be deemed to have passed and shall be effective upon adoption of an
ordinance changing the regular municipal election date, beginning with the Town’s regular
municipal election in November of 2028.
