Golden Gate Fire Protection District Ballot Issue 6B

WITHOUT INCREASING THE DISTRICT'S EXISTING OPERATIONS MILL LEVY, SHALL THE GOLDEN GATE FIRE PROTECTION DISTRICT'S TAXES BE INCREASED BY AN ADDITIONAL 4.5 MILLS ANNUALLY, COMMENCING WITH TAXES COLLECTED IN 2027, TO GENERATE APPROXIMATELY $137,376 IN THE FIRST YEAR (2027), AND BY WHATEVER ADDITIONAL AMOUNTS ARE GENERATED ANNUALLY THEREAFTER FROM CHANGES IN THE DISTRICT'S ASSESSED VALUATION, FOR THE SOLE PURPOSE OF FUNDING THE ACQUISITION, REPLACEMENT, REVITALIZATION, OR REHABILITATION OF FIRE APPARATUS AND FLEET VEHICLES, INCLUDING PERMANENTLY INSTALLED EQUIPMENT INTEGRAL TO THE OPERATION OF SUCH APPARATUS AND VEHICLES; WITH ALL REVENUES DEPOSITED INTO A DEDICATED "FLEET REVITALIZATION FUND" TO BE USED EXCLUSIVELY FOR SUCH PURPOSES; AND SHALL ALL REVENUES FROM SUCH MILL LEVY, TOGETHER WITH ANY EARNINGS THEREON, CONSTITUTE A VOTER APPROVED REVENUE CHANGE AND BE COLLECTED, RETAINED, AND SPENT BY THE DISTRICT AS AUTHORIZED BY THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS, WITHOUT REGARD TO ANY REVENUE OR EXPENDITURE LIMITATION CONTAINED IN ARTICLE X, SECTION 20 OF THE COLORADO CONSTITUTION OR ANY OTHER LAW?
