PROACTIVE STREET AND INFRASTRUCTURE MAINTENANCE; 

BICYCLE AND PEDESTRIAN SAFETY IMPROVEMENTS; 

PRESERVATION OF COMMUNITY SPACES AT WILMORE DAVIS AND KULLERSTRAND ELEMENTARY SCHOOLS; 

REPLACEMENT OF ANDERSON PARK OUTDOOR POOL; AND 

MODERN POLICE AND CITY SERVICES FACILITIES, 

AND FOR THE OPERATIONS AND MAINTENANCE OF THE SAME; AND SHALL THE CITY BE AUTHORIZED TO COLLECT, RETAIN, AND SPEND ALL REVENUES FROM SUCH TAX AS A VOTER-APPROVED REVENUE CHANGE AND EXCEPTION TO ANY SPENDING OR REVENUE RESTRICTIONS OR OTHER LIMITS UNDER SECTION 20 OF ARTICLE X OF THE COLORADO CONSTITUTION OR ANY OTHER LAW?
