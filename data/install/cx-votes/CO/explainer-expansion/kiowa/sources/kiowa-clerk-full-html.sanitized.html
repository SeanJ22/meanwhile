COLORADO OPEN RECORD REQUESTS (CORA) - THIS FORM IS FOR INFORMATION HOUSED BY THE CLERK & RECORDER'S OFFICE ONLY! WE DO NOT HOUSE ANYTHING RELATING TO CITATIONS, COURT CASES, FINANCE, OR COUNTY ADMINISTRATION. WE HOUSE PUBLICLY RECORDED DOCUMENTS ONLY!

Pursuant to C.R.S. 30-1-103 and 24-72-205, the Clerk's office may charge for preparation and copies of requested documents. 

Acceptance Method: CORA requests are accepted by the Clerk's office via the webform, email, fax, or hard copy ONLY. 

Delivery Method: Documents are provided in paper or pdf format only. Documents provided under CORA are NOT provided in an editable format. If a requestor is not present within the county and inspection of the paper documents in person is burdensome, the Clerk may mail, fax, ftp, or email the documents. Our office is open Monday - Thursday 8:00 am - 4:30 pm so any requests received after 4:30 pm on Thursday will be treated as received on Monday morning. 

Fee: If the request requires over 1 hour of time to prepare, the requestor will be charged $40.00 per hour thereafter. The hourly fee of $40.00 for the second hour and beyond will be charged in addition to the appropriate per copy or electronic image cost. 

Deposit May Be Required: When the Clerk's office receives a CORA request, they may estimate the hours to review and assemble the documents and require a deposit.

Click on the link below to be redirected to CORA Request webform to submit your Public Records Request to the Clerk and Recorder's office. 

Submit your CORA Request here.

If you are requesting recorded documents including but not limited to: recorded liens, plats, deeds, or any other document recorded by this office, you will need to include either the reception number or book and page number or your request will be denied until that information is obtained. Our office cannot research records. The websites below allow you to research on your own or you can contact the Kiowa County Assessor’s office at 719-438-5521 who can help you obtain the reception number or book and page.
