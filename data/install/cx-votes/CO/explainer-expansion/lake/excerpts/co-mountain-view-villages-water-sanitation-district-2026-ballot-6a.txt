MOUNTAIN VIEW VILLAGES WATER AND SANITATION DISTRICT
BALLOT ISSUE 6A: STATE AGENCY LOAN FOR WASTEWATER
SHALL MOUNTAIN VIEW VILLAGES WATER AND SANITATION DISTRICT DEBT BE INCREASED BY $1,800,000, WITH A MAXIMUM REPAYMENT COST OF UP TO $4,140,000, AND SHALL DISTRICT TAXES BE INCREASED UP TO $20,000 ANNUALLY, IF NECESSARY, FOR THE PURPOSE OF FINANCING NECESSARY AND REQUIRED CAPITAL IMPROVEMENTS FOR THE CONTINUED OPERATION OF THE DISTRICT'S WASTEWATER SYSTEM BY THE EXECUTION OF A LOAN AGREEMENT AND ISSUANCE OF DEBT OR OTHER MULTIPLE FISCAL YEAR OBLIGATION PAYABLE TO A STATE AUTHORITY, WITH SUCH DEBT OBLIGATION TO BEAR INTEREST AT A MAXIMUM NET EFFECTIVE INTEREST RATE NOT TO EXCEED SIX AND A HALF-PERCENT (6.5%) PER ANNUM, TO BE EXECUTED AND ISSUED AND IN SUCH MANNER AND CONTAINING SUCH TERMS, CONSISTENT HEREWITH, AS THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS MAY DETERMINE; AND TO THE EXTENT OTHER DISTRICT LEGALLY AVAILABLE REVENUES ARE NOT SUFFICIENT FOR THE REPAYMENT OF SUCH DEBT, SHALL AD VALOREM PROPERTY TAXES BE INCREASED IN ANY YEAR WITHOUT LIMITATION AS TO RATE AND IN AN AMOUNT SUFFICIENT TO PAY THE PRINCIPAL OF, AND INTEREST ON SUCH DEBT AS THE SAME BECOME DUE; AND SHALL ANY EARNINGS FROM THE INVESTMENT OF SUCH REVENUES AND TAXES AVAILABLE FOR REPAYMENT OF SUCH DEBT CONSTITUTE A VOTER APPROVED REVENUE CHANGE?
