Notice of General Mail Ballot Election


LAKE COUNTY, COLORADO
2026 General Election November 3, 2026.

Tracey Lauritzen
Lake County Clerk and Recorder
NOTICE OF GENERAL MAIL BALLOT ELECTION
November 3, 2026

The ballot issued to you will contain only those contests on which you are eligible to vote based on your affiliation on file with the Lake County Clerk and Recorder’s office.  The ballots shown includes all races, but voters may not have the same combination on the ballot they will soon receive.

  • Election Date: Tuesday, November 3, 2026. 
  • Election Type: Mail Ballot 
  • Election Hours: 7:00 a.m. to 7:00 p.m.

All active registered voters will be mailed ballots prior to October 9th. Requests for mail-in ballots to alternate addresses or replacement ballots must be made in writing and must include full name, date of birth, physical address, address to mail the ballot, and signature of the applicant. The last day to request a ballot to be mailed is October 26th, 2026. 

  • Mail requests to: Lake County Clerk and Recorder, PO Box 917, Leadville, CO 80461
  • Fax requests to: (719) 486-8061
  • Email requests to: tlauritzen@lakecountyco.gov 

VOTER SERVICE AND POLLING CENTER, LAKE COUNTY COURTHOUSE, DATES AND HOURS:

Weekdays; October19th - November 2nd8:30 a.m.  –  4:30 p.m.
October 31st (Saturday)8:00 a.m.  –  12:00 p.m.
Tuesday November 3rd, Election Day7:00 a.m.  –  7:00 p.m.


SERVICES AVAILABLE: 

  • Voter registration up to and including November 3rd, 2026. 
  • Disabled access voting 
  • In-person voting 
  • Ballot drop off boxes including 24-hour drop box 
  • Replacement ballots

Clerk and Recorder’s Office 
PO Box 917 
505 Harrison Ave 
Leadville, CO 80461

***PLEASE BE AWARE THAT BALLOTS CANNOT BE FORWARDED BY LAW***
