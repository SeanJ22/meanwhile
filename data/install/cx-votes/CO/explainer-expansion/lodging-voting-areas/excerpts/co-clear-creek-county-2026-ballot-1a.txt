A. Ballot Title
For purposes of C.R.S. § 1-11-203.5 and § 31-11-111, the ballot title for the ballot issue
is hereby determined to be: “Clear Creek County Ballot Issue 1 A -- Lodging Tax
Revenue – Additional Allowable Uses and Allocations”
4
B. Ballot Issue
SHALL CLEAR CREEK COUNTY LODGING TAXES BE INCREASED BY
$800,000.00 ANNUALLY COMMENCING ON JANUARY 1, 2027, AND BY
WHATEVER AMOUNTS ARE RAISED ANNUALLY THEREAFTER, BY
INCREASING THE LODGING TAX RATE FROM TWO PERCENT (2%) TO SIX
PERCENT (6%) ON THE RENTAL FEE, PRICE, OR OTHER CONSIDERATION
PAID OR CHARGED FOR THE LEASING, RENTAL, SALE OR FURNISHING OF
A ROOM OR ACCOMMODATION FOR A SHORT-TERM PERIOD OF LESS
THAN THIRTY (30) CONSECUTIVE DAYS IN CLEAR CREEK COUNTY,
EXCLUDING THE MUNICIPALITY OF THE TOWN OF GEORGETOWN THAT
CURRENTLY HAS ITS OWN ADOPTED LODGING TAX, AND SHALL THE
ALLOCATION OF COUNTY LODGING TAX REVENUE BE MODIFIED SO
THAT THE REVENUE COLLECTED FROM THE SIX PERCENT (6%) COUNTY
LODGING TAX SHALL BE USED AS FOLLOWS:
• UP TO 60% OF THE REVENUE FOR PUBLIC INFRASTRUCTURE
MAINTENANCE OR IMPROVEMENTS; AND ENHANCING PUBLIC
SAFETY MEASURES;
• AT LEAST TWENTY PERCENT (20%) OF THE REVENUE TO PROVIDE
CHILDCARE FOR THE TOURISM-RELATED WORKFORCE,
INCLUDING SEASONAL WORKERS, AND FOR OTHER WORKERS IN
THE COMMUNITY;
• AT LEAST TEN PERCENT (10%) OF THE REVENUE TO PROVIDE
HOUSING FOR THE TOURISM-RELATED WORKFORCE, INCLUDING
SEASONAL WORKERS, AND FOR OTHER WORKERS IN THE
COMMUNITY;
• AT LEAST TEN PERCENT (10%) OF THE REVENUE FOR
ADVERTISING AND MARKETING OF LOCAL TOURISM, AS
REQUIRED BY STATE LAW; AND
WITH ALL SUCH EXPENDITURES SUBJECT TO AN ANNUAL INDEPENDENT
AUDIT AND SPENT IN COMPLIANCE WITH LOCAL GOVERNMENT BUDGET
LAWS, AND SHALL THE FIRST-YEAR REVENUE, AND WHATEVER
ADDITIONAL AMOUNTS ARE RAISED ANNUALLY THEREAFTER, BE
COLLECTED AND SPENT AS A VOTER APPROVED REVENUE CHANGE
NOTWITHSTANDING ANY LIMITATION OR CONDITION UNDER ARTICLE
X, SECTION 20 OF THE COLORADO CONSTITUTION OR ANY OTHER
PROVISION OF LAW?
