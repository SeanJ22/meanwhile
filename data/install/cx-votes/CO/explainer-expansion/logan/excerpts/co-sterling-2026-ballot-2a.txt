City of Sterling
Referred Ballot Issue 2A
AUTHORIZATION TO INCREASE CITY SALES AND
USE TAX FOR STREET AND ROAD
IMPROVEMENTS
SHALL THE CITY OF STERLING TAXES BE
INCREASED $3,600,000 ANNUALLY, BEGINNING
JANUARY 1, 2027, AND BY SUCH AMOUNTS AS
ARE COLLECTED ANNUALLY THEREAFTER FOR A
PERIOD OF FIVE (5) YEARS, BY THE IMPOSITION
OF AN ADDITIONAL TEMPORARY 1.0% CITY
SALES AND USE TAX, IN ORDER TO INCREASE OR
IMPROVE PUBLIC SERVICES INCLUDING STREET
AND ROAD IMPROVEMENTS AND RELATED
MAINTENANCE NEEDS; AND SHALL THE
REVENUES FROM SUCH TAX:
BE DEPOSITED INTO A DEDICATED FUND
SEPARATE FROM THE CITY'S GENERAL FUND
AND USED SOLELY FOR THE PURPOSE OF
REPAVING, REPAIRING, MAINTAINING, AND
IMPROVING CITY STREETS AND ROADS,
INCLUDING RELATED IMPROVEMENTS SUCH AS
DRAINAGE, CURBS AND GUTTERS, AND
PEDESTRIAN SAFETY IMPROVEMENTS; AND
SHALL THE CITY BE AUTHORIZED TO COLLECT,
RETAIN, AND SPEND ALL REVENUES GENERATED
BY SUCH TAX, TOGETHER WITH ANY EARNINGS
THEREON, WITHOUT LIMITATION OR CONDITION,
AS A VOTER-APPROVED REVENUE CHANGE
PURSUANT TO ARTICLE X, SECTION 20 OF THE
COLORADO CONSTITUTION, OR ANY OTHER LAW?
