TOWN OF PALISADE BALLOT ISSUE 2A
SHALL TOWN OF PALISADE TAXES BE INCREASED BY AN ESTIMATED $600,000 ANNUALLY
FOR THE FIRST FULL FISCAL YEAR (FIRST FULL FISCAL YEAR INCREASE) AND BY WHATEVER
ADDITIONAL AMOUNTS ARE RAISED ANNUALLY THEREAFTER, THROUGH AN INCREASE IN
THE TOWN’S SALES TAX RATE FROM2.0% TO 3.0%, WHICH INCREASE WILL TERMINATE ON
DECEMBER 31, 2034, SUCH REVENUES TO BE COLLECTED, RETAINED AND SPENT BY THE
TOWN AS A VOTER APPROVED REVENUE CHANGE, NOTWITHSTANDING ANY REVENUE OR
EXPENDITURE LIMIT SET FORTH IN ARTICLE X, SECTION 20 OF THE COLORADO
CONSTITUTION OR ANY OTHER LAW;
AND SHALL ALL REVENUES DERIVED FROM SUCH INCREASED 1.0% SALES TAX BE
COLLECTED AND
0.75% OF SAID INCREASE SPENT TO FUND:
• STREET REPAIRS FOR 4TH, 5TH, 6TH AND 7TH STREETS,
• IMPROVEMENTS TO PEACH BOWL PARK,
• SWIMMING POOL IMPROVEMENTS,
• IMPROVEMENTS TO RIVERBEND PARK, AND
0.25% OF SAID INCREASE SPENT TO FUND EMERGENCY SERVICES,
AS A VOTER APPROVED REVENUE CHANGE, AND IN CONNECTION THEREWITH SHALL THE
TOWN BE AUTHORIZED TO COLLECT AND RETAIN OR EXPEND THE PROCEEDS OF SUCH
TAX AND INVESTMENT EARNINGS THEREON FOR SUCH PURPOSES, NOTWITHSTANDING
ANY APPLICABLE LIMITATION ON REVENUES AND EXPENDITURES, INCLUDING THE
LIMITATIONS SET FORTH IN ARTICLE X SECTION 20 OF THE COLORADO CONSTITUTION?
