PALSADE RURAL FIRE PROTECTION DISTRICT BALLOT ISSUE 6A
SHALL PALISADE RURAL FIRE PROTECTION DISTRICT TAXES BE INCREASED $185,000
ANNUALLY BEGINNING JANUARY 1, 2027 OR BY SUCH AMOUNTS AS ARE RAISED BY
INCREASING THE DISTRICT’S MILL LEVY FOR GENERALOPERATING PURPOSES FROM
5.242 MILLS TO 11.5 MILLS, FOR A TOTAL PROPERTY TAX RATE OF 12.5 MILLS (11.5 MILLS
FOR GENERALOPERATING PURPOSES PLUS 0.5 MILLS FOR CAPITAL EXPENDITURES AND
0.5 MILLS FOR PENSION FUND PAYMENTS) RESULTING IN AN EXPECTED 2027 ANNUAL TAX
INCREASE OF $42.55 FOR EACH $100,000 OF RESIDENTIAL VALUE FOR THE PURPOSES OF
PROVIDING EMERGENCY RESPONSE SERVICES INCLUDING FIRE RESPONSE, FIRE
PREVENTION, SAFETY INSPECTIONS, EMERGENCY AND MEDICAL RESPONSE, THE
CONSTRUCTION, OPERATION AND MAINTENANCE OF FIRE AND EMERGENCY BUILDINGS
AND STRUCTURES, AND THE ACQUISITION BY PURCHASE, LEASE OR OTHERWISE, OF
SUCH EQUIPMENT OR PROPERTIES AS REQUIRED IN PROVIDING EMERGENCY RESPONSE
SERVICES TO THE RESIDENTS, PROPERTY OWNERS AND BUSINESSES SERVED BY THE
DISTRICT AND SHALL ALL DISTRICT REVENUE GENERATED FROMTHE 12.5 MILL LEVY AND
ANY OTHER SOURCE BE COLLECTED, RETAINED AND SPENT AS A PERMANENT VOTER-
APPROVED REVENUE CHANGE AND AS AN EXEMPTION TO THE PROVISIONS AND
RESTRICTIONS SET FORTH IN ARTICLE X, SECTION 20 OF THE COLORADO CONSTITUTION
("TABOR") AND SECTIONS 29-1-301 AND 29-1-1702, C.R.S. AND ANY OTHER LAW THAT
LIMITS THE DISTRICT’S REVENUES OR EXPENDITURES AS IT CURRENTLY EXISTS OR AS IT
MAY BE AMENDED IN THE FUTURE, ALL WITHOUT LIMITING IN ANY YEAR THE AMOUNT OF
OTHER REVENUES THAT MAY BE COLLECTED AND SPENT BY THE DISTRICT?
