Ballot Title and Text — Town of Mancos Ballot Issue 2B:
SHALL TOWN OF MANCOS TAXES BE INCREASED $325,000 IN 2027 (FIRST
FULL FISCAL YEAR) BY SUCH AMOUNTS AS MAY BE GENERATED
THROUGH THE IMPOSITION OF AN ADDITIONAL TOWN-WIDE SALES
TAX OF .40% (FOR A TOTAL SALES TAX OF 4.4%) TO BE UTILIZED
EXCLUSIVELY FOR STREET IMPROVEMENTS, BROADBAND CAPITAL
IMPROVEMENTS, EQUIPMENT AND OPERATION AND MAINTENANCE
RELATING TO THE SAME; SUCH TAX INCREASES TO COMMENCE
JANUARY 1, 2027 AND CONTINUE ANNUALLY THEREAFTER; AND SHALL
SUCH TAX REVENUES AND INVESTMENT EARNINGS THEREON BE
COLLECTED, RETAINED AND SPENT AS A VOTER APPROVED REVENUE
CHANGE AND AN EXCEPTION TO THE LIMITS WHICH WOULD
OTHERWISE APPLY UNDER ARTICLE X, SECTION 20 OF THE COLORADO
CONSTITUTION OR ANY OTHER LAW?
