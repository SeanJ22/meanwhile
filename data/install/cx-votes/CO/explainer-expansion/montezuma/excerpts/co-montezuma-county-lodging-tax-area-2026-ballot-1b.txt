BALLOT TITLE AND TEXT — BALLOT ISSUE NO. 1B:
SHALL MONTEZUMA COUNTY LODGING TAXES BE INCREASED BY
$404,598 IN TAX COLLECTION YEAR 2027 AND BY WHATEVER AMOUNTS
ARE GENERATED ANNUALLY THEREAFTER BY INCREASING THE
COUNTY LODGING TAX FROM 2% TO 5% EFFECTIVE JANUARY 1, 2027,
EXCLUDING THE MUNICIPALITY OF CORTEZ WHICH HAS ITS OWN
LODGING TAX, AND SHALL THE LODGING TAX REVENUES RECEIVED
BY THE COUNTY BE USED FOR THE FOLLOWING PURPOSES:
• TOURISM PROMOTION AND MARKETING;
• RECREATION, OUTDOOR RECREATION, AND VISITOR-RELATED
INFRASTRUCTURE IMPROVEMENTS;
• DESTINATION DEVELOPMENT PROJECTS AND VISITOR EXPERIENCE
ENHANCEMENTS;
• TOURISM RESILIENCY, EMERGENCY RESPONSE, AND RECOVERY
EFFORTS RELATED TO EVENTS AFFECTING VISITATION;
• ADMINISTRATION, OVERSIGHT, AND IMPLEMENTATION OF
AUTHORIZED LODGING TAX PROGRAMS;
AND SHALL THE COUNTY BE AUTHORIZED TO COLLECT, RETAIN, AND
SPEND ALL REVENUES GENERATED BY SUCH TAX AS A VOTER-
APPROVED REVENUE CHANGE UNDER ARTICLE X, SECTION 20 OF THE
COLORADO CONSTITUTION, OR ANY OTHER LAW?
