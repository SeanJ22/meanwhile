NOVEMBER 3, 2026  GENERAL ELECTION DATES

  • September 19: Military and Overseas ballots are mailed
  • September 23: Public welcome for Logic and Accuracy Testing (LAT) of voting system at 11:00 a.m.
  • Week of October 5:  Ballots are mailed to ALL active, registered voters
  • October 19:  Montrose County Elections Office Voter Service Polling Center opens
  • October 26:  Last day to request a replacement mail ballot/register to vote and receive ballot by mail
  • October 29:  Naturita Community Library Voter Service Polling Center opens
  • November 3:  ELECTION DAY – Voter Service Polling Centers open from 7:00 a.m. to 7:00 p.m. Ballots must be received no later than 7:00 p.m.  Postmarks do not count
  • November 12:  Last day to cure a signature issue on a mailed ballot 
    Military and Overseas (UOCAVA) ballots must be received by this date
