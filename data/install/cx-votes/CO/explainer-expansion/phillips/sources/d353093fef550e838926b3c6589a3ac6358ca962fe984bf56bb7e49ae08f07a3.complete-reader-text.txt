Summary of Written Comments FOR the Ballot Question proposed: 

A YES vote on 2B would continue a 2.5% lodging tax to support a well-established year-round recreation program in Holyoke. A YES vote on 2B does not increase any local sales or property tax. This tax will be paid by hotel patrons. Recreation is a need in our community. Recreation is defined as any activity done for enjoyment when not at work. The recreation director has created a very successful program that will continue to: 

*Plan events for residents of all ages, not just youth, *Cover a wide variety of interests, not just sports, and *Have activities throughout the year, not just in the summer. 

Recreation can be a powerful economic driver. Prior to a full-time recreation director, every weekend families would leave Holyoke for youth recreation taking their hard-earned money out of town. Now the fully developed recreation program provides more activities here in Holyoke and has had great success hosting events such as: 

*3 on 3 Basketball Tournaments *Baseball/Softball College Camp *Soccer League *Flag Football Tournaments *Showdown on the Plains *Youth Track *Easter Egg Hunt/Youth Fishing Day *Regional Babe Ruth Baseball/Softball Tournaments *and many more 

These events have had a positive impact on our community, including increasing activity at hotels, restaurants, and stores. 

In 2025 the City of Holyoke Recreation Department hosted over 20 different events throughout the year. A total of 1,437 youths were reached through the events. Events such as Showdown on the Plains and the Youth Baseball/Soccer Tournament brought in teams from the region. Since the department was established the number of community members and out-of-town numbers have increased each year. 

Voting YES on 2B will continue to support a well-established city recreation program that brings residents together and is part of our thriving community. 2B is the result of thoughtful planning and community support for a full-time recreation program which will continue to benefit citizens of all ages. There is no cost to local residents as this lodging tax will be paid by people staying in local hotels. 2B is good for Holyoke. Vote YES on 2B.
