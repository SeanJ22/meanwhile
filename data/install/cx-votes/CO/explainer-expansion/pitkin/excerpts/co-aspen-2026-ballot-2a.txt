CITY OF ASPEN BALLOT ISSUE 2A:
DEBT FOR WATER TREATMENT FACILITY PROJECT
WITHOUT INCREASING ANY TAXES, SHALL THE CITY OF ASPEN DEBT BE INCREASED BY
AN AMOUNT NOT TO EXCEED $100,000,000, WITH A TOTAL REPAYMENT COST NOT TO
EXCEED $255,000,000, FOR THE PURPOSE OF FINANCING THE EXPANSION, RENOVATION,
IMPROVEMENT, REMODELING AND EQUIPPING OF ITS WATER TREATMENT FACILITY; SUCH
DEBT TO BE ISSUED IN THE FORM OF REVENUE BONDS OR OTHER MULTIPLE FISCAL YEAR
FINANCIAL OBLIGATION, WITH SUCH TERMS, AND BEARING INTEREST AT SUCH RATES AS MAY
BE APPROVED BY CITY COUNCIL, PAYABLE SOLELY FROM THE WATER ACTIVITY ENTERPRISE
REVENUE, AND TO COLLECT, RETAIN AND SPEND ALL REVENUE AND INTEREST FROM THESE
FINANCIAL OBLIGATIONS AS A VOTER-APPROVED REVENUE CHANGE TO LIMITS UNDER
COLORADO LAW, OR ANY OTHER LAW?
