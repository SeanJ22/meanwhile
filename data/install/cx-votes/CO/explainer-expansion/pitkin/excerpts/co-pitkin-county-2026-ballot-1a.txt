PITKIN COUNTY BALLOT ISSUE 1A:
HEALTHY COMMUNITY FUND MILL LEVY
SHALL PITKIN COUNTY TAXES BE INCREASED BY $5,266,081.00 ANNUALLY, BEGINNING
IN THE FIRST FULL FISCAL YEAR OF COLLECTION IN 2028, TO CONTINUE FUNDING THE
HEALTHY COMMUNITY FUND, WHICH HAS PROVIDED FUNDING FOR HEALTH AND HUMAN
SERVICES AND COMMUNITY NON-PROFITS FOR THE PAST TWENTY-FOUR YEARS, AFTER THE
CURRENT PROPERTY TAX EXPIRES IN 2027, AND BY SUCH ADDITIONAL AMOUNTS AS ARE
GENERATED ANNUALLY THEREAFTER FOR A PERIOD NOT TO EXCEED TWENTY FIVE YEARS,
BY THE IMPOSITION OF A NEW 0.853 MILL LEVY, FOR THE SOLE PURPOSE OF PROVIDING A
STABLE FUNDING SOURCE FOR HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES AND COMMUNITY NON-PROFITS
INCLUDING:
• PROTECTIVE AND SUPPORTIVE SERVICES, INCLUDING FOOD ACCESS, FOR CHILDREN AND
ADULTS
• SERVICES AND ACTIVITIES SUPPORTING HEALTHY AGING FOR OLDER ADULTS
• SUPPORTING SURVIVORS OF DOMESTIC VIOLENCE AND SEXUAL ASSAULT
• BEHAVIORAL HEALTH AND SUBSTANCE USE PREVENTION, TREATMENT, AND AFTERCARE 		
SERVICES
• CARE FOR THE TERMINALLY ILL AND THEIR FAMILIES
• PROTECTING AND SUPPORTING NATURAL RESOURCES
• PREVENTIVE AND INTEGRATED HEALTH CARE SERVICES FOR LOWER-INCOME INDIVIDUALS
• SERVICES SUPPORTING INDEPENDENCE FOR INDIVIDUALS WITH DEVELOPMENTAL 		
DISABILITIES AND THEIR FAMILIES
AND SHALL THE COUNTY BE AUTHORIZED TO COLLECT, RETAIN, AND SPEND ALL REVENUES
FROM SUCH TAX AND THE INTEREST EARNINGS THEREON AS A VOTER-APPROVED REVENUE
CHANGE ABOVE THE ANNUAL LIMITS OF ARTICLE X, SECTION 20 OF THE COLORADO
CONSTITUTION AND ABOVE ANNUAL LIMITS WHICH MIGHT OTHERWISE APPLY OR BE
PROPOSED, INCLUDING AS AN APPROVED EXCEPTION TO THE LIMITATIONS SET FORTH IN
SECTION 29-1-301 OF THE COLORADO REVISED STATUTES OR ARTICLE 9 OF THE COUNTY HOME
RULE CHARTER?
