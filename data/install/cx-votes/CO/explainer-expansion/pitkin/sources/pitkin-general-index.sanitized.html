    2026 SAMPLE BALLOT

    ​

    Update your Voter Registration

    ​

    In Colorado, ballots are mailed to all active, registered voters.

    ​

    We recommend that you check your voter registration at this link prior to every election and update it to ensure we have the correct mailing address for you.

    ​​

    You can also add a specific 'ballot mailing address' to your voter registration if you will be away from home when ballots are mailed. Please note that ballots cannot be forwarded by the U.S. Postal Service.

    ​

    ​

    Mail Ballot Election

    ​

    ​Receiving your mail ballot:

    ​

    • Ballots are mailed on October 5th to all active registered voters.

    • Didn't receive your mail ballot? Please check your registration to ensure we have the correct mailing address.

    • Need a replacement mail ballot? You can call our office at 970-429-2732.

    • Please note, after October 26th we can no longer mail a ballot to you. Instead, you can simply visit one of our Voter Service and Polling Centers to vote or collect a replacement mail ballot in person.

    ​

    Returning your mail ballot:

    ​

    • You can mail your ballot back to us, or drop it off at one of our locations.

    • Our office must receive your ballot by 7pm on election day for your vote to count. Postmarks do not count! 

    • After October 26th, we don't recommend that you mail your ballot. Instead, utilize any ballot drop box listed below to ensure that it arrives on time.

    • You can track your mail ballot using BallotTrax. This service will notify you when your ballot is received by our office and when it is accepted for counting.

    *Website Accessibility*

    ​If you use assistive technology (such as a Braille reader, a screen reader, or TTY) and the format of any material on this website interferes with your ability to access information, please contact us at (970) 920-5200 or via email at accessibility@pitkincounty.com. To enable us to respond in a manner most helpful to you, please indicate the nature of your accessibility problem, the preferred format in which to receive the material, the web address of the requested material, and your contact information. Users who need accessibility assistance can also contact us by phone through the Federal Information Relay Service at 1-800-877-8339 for TTY/Voice communication.

    ​Accessibility Design Guidelines

    • Our website has been designed with the following accessibility guidelines in mind:

      • We work to comply with Section 508 as well as 2.0 A and AA referring to website accessibility standards.

      • If you have difficulty accessing the site or have any comments or feedback, please do not hesitate to contact us at (970) 920-5200 or via email at accessibility@pitkincounty.com.
