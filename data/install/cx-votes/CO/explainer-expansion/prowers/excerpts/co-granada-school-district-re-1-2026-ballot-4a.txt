GRANADA SCHOOL DISTRICT RE-1 BALLOT
ISSUE 4A :
SHALL GRANADA SCHOOL DISTRICT RE-1 DEBT BE
INCREASED $1.35 MILLION, WITH A TOTAL REPAYMENT
COST OF UP TO $2.36 MILLION, AND SHALL DISTRICT
TAXES BE INCREASED BY UP TO $120,000 ANNUALLY,
WITH THE INCREASE IN DEBT TO OCCUR ONLY IF THE
DISTRICT RECEIVES A “BEST” GRANT WHICH EXCEEDS $23
MILLION IN AWARDED “BEST” GRANT FUNDS (WHICH ARE
NOT REQUIRED TO BE REPAID), FOR THE PURPOSE OF
IMPROVING AND EQUIPPING DISTRICT FACILITIES,
INCLUDING:
EXPANDING THE MAIN BUILDING TO IMPROVE
·
SCHOOL SAFETY AND SECURITY BY LOCATING ALL
CLASSROOMS WITHIN THE BUILDING;
REPLACING OUTDATED SEWER INFRASTRUCTURE
·
TO PROVIDE HEALTHIER FACILITIES FOR
STUDENTS, STAFF, AND COMMUNITY VISITORS;
BUILDING AN AUXILIARY GYM AND NEW VO-AG
·
SHOP; AND
UPGRADING OTHER DISTRICT AMENITIES,
·
INCLUDING THE MAIN GYMNASIUM, KITCHEN,
LOCKER ROOMS, AND SCIENCE CLASSROOM;
BY THE ISSUANCE AND PAYMENT OF GENERAL
OBLIGATION BONDS WHICH SHALL BEAR INTEREST,
MATURE, BE SUBJECT TO REDEMPTION, WITH OR
WITHOUT PREMIUM, AND BE ISSUED AT SUCH TIME, AT
SUCH PRICE (AT, ABOVE OR BELOW PAR) AND IN SUCH
MANNER AND CONTAINING SUCH TERMS CONSISTENT
WITH THIS BALLOT ISSUE, AS THE BOARD OF EDUCATION
MAY DETERMINE; AND SHALL AD VALOREM PROPERTY
TAXES BE LEVIED WITHOUT LIMIT AS TO THE MILL RATE
TO GENERATE AMOUNTS, NOT TO EXCEED THE AMOUNTS
SET FORTH ABOVE, SUFFICIENT IN EACH YEAR TO PAY
THE PRINCIPAL OF, PREMIUM IF ANY, AND INTEREST ON
THE BONDS?
