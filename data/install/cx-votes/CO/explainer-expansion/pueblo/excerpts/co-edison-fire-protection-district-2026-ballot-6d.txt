Edison Fire Protection District Ballot Question
6D
Shall the following described area become a part of the
Edison Fire Protection District?
Property that is located within the following described
areas that is not already located within the boundaries of
the Edison Fire Protection District or any other fire
protection district:
Portion of Township 18 South, Range 60 West of the 6th P.M.;
All of Township 19 South, Range 60 West of the 6th P.M.;
All of Township 19 South, Range 61 West of the 6th P.M.;
All of Township 20 South, Range 60 West of the 6th P.M.;
All of Township 20 South, Range 61 West of the 6th P.M.;
Portion of Township 20 South, Range 62 West of the 6th P.M.;
Portion of Township 20 South, Range 63 West of the 6th P.M.;
Portion of Township 21 South, Range 60 West of the 6th P.M.;
Portion of Township 21 South, Range 61 West of the 6th P.M;
Portion of Township 21 South, Range 62 West of the 6th P.M; and
Portion of Township 21 South, Range 63 West of the 6th P.M;
County of Pueblo, State of Colorado.
The area proposed for inclusion is also generally
described as follows:
Located in the northeast area of Pueblo County, State of
Colorado:
Beginning at the intersection of El Paso, Pueblo, Lincoln
and Crowley Counties, and continuing west along the
Pueblo County line to the northeast corner of the Edison
Fire Protection District boundary; following the eastern
and southern boundary of the Edison Fire Protection
District to its southwest corner, then along the line of Dot
Road south to the intersection of IL Ranch Rd; continuing
south along IL Ranch Road, and following the eastern and
southern boundary of the Pueblo Chemical Depot to its
intersection with the Pueblo Rural Fire Protection District's
boundary; following and continuing along the northern
boundary of the Pueblo Rural Fire Protection District's
boundary to the intersection with the Fowler Rural Fire
Protection District's boundaries; continuing east along
Fowler Rural Fire Protection District's northern boundary
to the Pueblo County line; and following the Pueblo
County line north to the El Paso County line (point of
beginning);
EXCEPTING all property located within the Town of
Boone, State of Colorado (Pueblo County Tax District
70G).
