BALLOT ISSUE 6A
SHALL THE PUEBLO CITY-COUNTY LIBRARY DISTRICT
WAIVE THE 5.25% PROPERTY TAX LIMIT FOR ALL
FUTURE PROPERTY TAX YEARS, WITHOUT

DISTRICT TO REALIZE THE FULL BENEFIT OF ITS
CURRENT VOTER AUTHORIZED PROPERTY TAX RATE
AND SHALL ALL DISTRICT REVENUES BE COLLECTED,
RETAINED, AND SPENT NOTWITHSTANDING ANY
REVENUE LIMITS PROVIDED BY LAW?
