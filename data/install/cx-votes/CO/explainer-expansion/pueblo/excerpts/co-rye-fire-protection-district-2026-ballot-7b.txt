RYE FIRE PROTECTION DISTRICT BALLOT
ISSUE 7B
MILL LEVY INCREASE FOR EMERGENCY SERVICES
SHALL RYE FIRE PROTECTION DISTRICT TAXES BE
INCREASED BY $1,098,568.97 IN THE FIRST FULL FISCAL
YEAR, AND BY SUCH AMOUNTS AS MAY BE GENERATED
ANNUALLY THEREAFTER, FOR A PERIOD OF TEN (10)
YEARS, BY AN INCREASE OF 11.652 MILLS IN THE
DISTRICT'S PROPERTY TAX MILL LEVY, WITH THE
REVENUE FROM SUCH INCREASE USED FOR GENERAL
DISTRICT PURPOSES, INCLUDING:
INCREASING AND MAINTAINING EMERGENCY
·
RESPONSE STAFFING;
MAINTAINING FIRE PROTECTION, EMERGENCY
·
MEDICAL, AMBULANCE, RESCUE, AND WILDLAND
FIRE SERVICES;
PURCHASING, REPLACING, AND MAINTAINING FIRE
·
APPARATUS, AMBULANCES, EMERGENCY
EQUIPMENT, AND FIREFIGHTER PROTECTIVE
EQUIPMENT;
REPAIRING, MAINTAINING, AND IMPROVING
·
DISTRICT FIRE STATIONS AND FACILITIES;
FUNDING CAPITAL IMPROVEMENTS AND PLANNED
·
APPARATUS AND EQUIPMENT REPLACEMENT; AND
MAINTAINING FINANCIAL RESERVES FOR DISTRICT
·
OPERATING AND CAPITAL NEEDS;
PROVIDED THAT THE AUTHORITY TO LEVY THE
ADDITIONAL 11.652 MILLS SHALL CONTINUE FOR TEN (10)
TAX YEARS, BEGINNING WITH THE 2026 PROPERTY TAX
YEAR FOR COLLECTION IN 2027 AND ENDING WITH THE
2035 PROPERTY TAX YEAR FOR COLLECTION IN 2036,
AFTER WHICH THE AUTHORITY TO LEVY THE ADDITIONAL
11.652 MILLS SHALL EXPIRE UNLESS OTHERWISE
AUTHORIZED BY THE REGISTERED ELECTORS OF THE
DISTRICT;
AND SHALL THE DISTRICT BE AUTHORIZED DURING
SUCH TEN-YEAR PERIOD TO COLLECT, RETAIN, AND
SPEND ALL REVENUES GENERATED BY SUCH TAX
INCREASE, TOGETHER WITH ANY INVESTMENT
EARNINGS THEREON, AS A VOTER-APPROVED REVENUE
CHANGE UNDER ARTICLE X, SECTION 20 OF THE
COLORADO CONSTITUTION, NOTWITHSTANDING ANY
REVENUE OR SPENDING LIMITATION CONTAINED IN
STATE LAW?
