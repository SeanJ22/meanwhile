Morrison Creek Metropolitan Water & Sanitation District Ballot Issue 6B
SHALL THE MORRISON CREEK METROPOLITAN WATER AND SANITATION DISTRICT (THE "DISTRICT") WAIVE THE 5.25% PROPERTY TAX LIMIT FOR ALL FUTURE PROPERTY TAX YEARS, AS SUCH PROPERTY TAX LIMIT IS SET FORTH IN SECTION 29-1-1702, C.R.S., PROVIDED HOWEVER THAT THE ADJUSTED PROPERTY TAX MILL LEVY OF THE DISTRICT FOR EACH FISCAL YEAR OF 2027 THROUGH 2031 SHALL BE EQUAL TO THE ADJUSTED PROPERTY TAX MILL LEVY IMPOSED BY THE DISTRICT IN 2026 (THE FOREGOING WAIVER BEING PROVIDED FOR AND PERMITTED BY SECTION 29-1-1704, C.R.S.), AND SHALL THE DISTRICT BE AUTHORIZED TO COLLECT, RETAIN, AND SPEND THE FULL AMOUNT OF ALL PROPERTY TAX MILL LEVY REVENUES IMPOSED, GENERATED, COLLECTED OR RECEIVED BY THE DISTRICT DURING 2026 AND EACH FISCAL YEAR THEREAFTER, SUCH AMOUNTS AND REVENUES TO CONSTITUTE A VOTER APPROVED REVENUE CHANGE, AND SHALL THE DISTRICT BE AUTHORIZED TO INCREASE THE TOTAL NUMBER OF MILLS LEVIED BY THE DISTRICT IN EACH FUTURE PROPERTY TAX YEAR TO A MAXIMUM OF 20 MILLS AS PERMITTED BY SECTION 29-1-1705(2)(a), C.R.S., AND SHALL THE PROPERTY TAX MILL LEVY REVENUES AS MAY BE IMPOSED, GENERATED, COLLECTED, RETAINED AND SPENT BY THE DISTRICT IN ALL FUTURE PROPERTY TAX YEARS BE WITHOUT REGARD TO ANY SPENDING, REVENUE RAISING, OR OTHER LIMITATION CONTAINED IN ARTICLE X, SECTION 20, OF THE COLORADO CONSTITUTION, AND WITHOUT REGARD TO ANY LIMITS IMPOSED ON INCREASES IN PROPERTY TAXATION OR REVENUES BY SECTION 29-1-301, C.R.S., AND BY SECTION 29-1-1702, C.R.S., IN ANY SUBSEQUENT PROPERTY TAX YEAR, AND WITHOUT REGARD TO ANY OTHER LAW WHICH PURPORTS TO LIMIT THE DISTRICT'S PROPERTY TAX REVENUES OR EXPENDITURES OR NUMBER OF MILLS LEVIED AS CURRENTLY EXISTS OR AS MAY BE AMENDED OR ADDED IN THE FUTURE?
