Clerk & Recorder

San Miguel County Clerk & Recorder's Office Services

All Clerk and Recorder’s departments located at the San Miguel County Courthouse in Telluride and the Motor Vehicle department at the Norwood location (Glockson Building)  are available for in-person service. 

While all services are available in person, we encourage all customers to process their transactions by mail, dropbox, or online whenever possible in order to help limit the in-person traffic.

Please see the links on the left menu bar for specific instructions for your transactions.

General Services

The Clerk’s Office provides various services, including document recording, motor vehicle licensing, elections, marriage licenses, and liquor licenses. The Clerk’s Office no longer acts as the Local Registrar to provide Birth and Death Certificate services. Please visit the Public Health page for more information.

The County Clerk is an Elected Official. Michael Wyszynski was elected in office in 2024.

The County Clerk is not the Clerk of the Court.

The Telluride Town Clerk’s office keeps the Town of Telluride records and licenses. Visit the Telluride Town Clerk’s web page for more information.

Elections

Please visit our Elections webpage for information on special district, local, and national elections.

Courts

Many courts have proceedings at the County Courthouse but are not associated with the San Miguel County Clerk.

  • Jury duty and District Attorney’s Office information may be found on the  San Miguel County Combined Courts web page  .
  • Parking tickets and other Telluride violation and citation information may be found on the Telluride Municipal Court web page .
