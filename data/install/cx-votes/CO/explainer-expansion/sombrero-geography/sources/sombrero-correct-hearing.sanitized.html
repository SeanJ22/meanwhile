County Attorney’s Office: Petition for the Organization of the Sombrero Ranch Public Improvement District of Boulder County

Public hearing pursuant to C.R.S. 30-20-508 to consider the petition to organize the Sombrero Ranch Public Improvement District of Boulder County for the purpose of taxing properties in the proposed district to resurface district roads.

  • Staff Contact: Robert Jacobs
  • Location – Hybrid: Hearing Room and Zoom Webinar
  • Action Requested: Decision

There will be opportunities for live virtual and in-person public comment, and written comments can be emailed to rjacobs@bouldercounty.gov.

Attend the Meeting

Virtual Attendee Link for Commissioners’ July 9 Morning Session
Call-in information: 1-833-568-8864, Webinar ID: 165 447 6429
In-Person Comment Sign-up for Commissioners’ July 9 Morning Session

A recording of the public hearing will be available through the county’s Meeting Portal.

More Information

Hybrid format

Board of County Commissioners public hearings and meetings are convened in a hybrid format where attendees can Zoom (FAQs on how to use Zoom) or attend in-person at the Boulder County Courthouse, 3rd Floor, 1325 Pearl St., Boulder. For questions regarding in-person hearings or technical assistance, please email hearings@bouldercounty.gov or call 303-441-3500.

Accessibility

Boulder County wants to ensure that everyone has equal access to our programs, activities, and services. To request an Americans with Disability Act (ADA) accommodation, please email ADA@bouldercounty.gov, or call 303-441-1386. Submit your request as early as possible, and no later than two business days before the event.

Agenda and format subject to change

Please note that the County Commissioners’ agenda is subject to change. To receive updates to the agenda, please subscribe to the Boulder County Commissioners Agenda list.

The commissioners retain the right to switch all hearings and meetings to in-person only instead of hybrid if technical difficulties arise.
