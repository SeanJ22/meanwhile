Summit School District RE-1 Ballot Issue 4A
SHALL SUMMIT SCHOOL DISTRICT RE-1 TAXES BE INCREASED $5.9 MILLION FOR TAX COLLECTION YEAR 2027 AND IN SUCH AMOUNTS AS MAY BE COLLECTED ANNUALLY THEREAFTER; WITH FUNDS TO BE USED FOR PURPOSES SUCH AS:
1. ATTRACTING AND RETAINING HIGH-QUALITY TEACHERS AND STAFF THROUGH COMPETITIVE COMPENSATION AND BENEFITS;
2. STRENGTHENING PK-12 PROGRAMS AND INCREASING STUDENTS’ OPPORTUNITIES FOR COLLEGE CREDITS, CAREER AND TECHNICAL INDUSTRY CERTIFICATIONS, AND HANDS-ON LEARNING EXPERIENCES ALIGNED TO WORKFORCE AND POSTSECONDARY PATHWAYS;
3. STRENGTHENING ACADEMIC OUTCOMES AND REDUCING ACHIEVEMENT GAPS;
FROM THE IMPOSITION OF A MILL LEVY IMPOSED AT A RATE, WHEN COMBINED WITH PRIOR VOTER APPROVED MILL LEVY OVERRIDE QUESTIONS, WILL GENERATE REVENUES UP TO THE LIMIT ESTABLISHED FOR THE DISTRICT BY SECTION 22-54-108(3)(B)(VI), C.R.S.; SUCH ADDITIONAL REVENUES TO BE DEPOSITED IN THE GENERAL FUND AND USED FOR EDUCATIONAL PURPOSES AS PROVIDED HEREIN AND AS PREVIOUSLY APPROVED BY THE DISTRICT VOTERS PURSUANT TO SECTION 22-54-108(V), C.R.S.; AND SHALL THE REVENUES FROM SUCH TAXES AND ANY EARNINGS FROM THE INVESTMENT OF SUCH REVENUES BE COLLECTED AND SPENT AS A VOTER APPROVED REVENUE CHANGE UNDER ARTICLE X, SECTION 20 OF THE COLORADO CONSTITUTION AND ANY OTHER LAW?
