Register Online

You can register to vote online if you have a valid Colorado Driver's License or ID card. Register to Vote Online

Register by Form

Voter Registration Forms are available on this webpage and at either of the offices of the County Clerk and Recorder - 101 West Bennett Avenue in Cripple Creek or at 800 Research Drive, Suite 200 - Tamarac Business Center in Woodland Park.

Download Voter Registration Form (PDF)

Voter Registration forms can be submitted by:

Mail - Teller County Clerk and Recorder
P.O. Box 1010
Cripple Creek, CO 80813

Fax - 719-686-8030

Email - Elections Department

Register at Driver's License Office

You may also register to vote at any Colorado Driver License Office. For more information regarding voter registration, email the Election Department with your questions.

Voter Lists

Voter registration lists may be purchased through this office. A request detailing format (such as party affiliation, voter status, mailing address, etc.) must be submitted in writing prior to issuance.  Costs for the voter registration lists required to be obtained under CRS 1-13.5-203(4) shall be no less than twenty-five dollars for the entire list or no more than once cent for each name contained on the registration list, whichever is greater.  Email requests to the Elections Department.
