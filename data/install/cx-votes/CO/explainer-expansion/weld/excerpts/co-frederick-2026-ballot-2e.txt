Municipal Ballot Measures
Town of Frederick Ballot Issue 2E
Debt Question
SUBJECT TO THE APPROVAL OF A ONE
PERCENT INCREASE IN SALES AND USE
TAX, SHALL THE TOWN OF FREDERICK
DEBT BE INCREASED UP TO $26 MILLION,
WITH A MAXIMUM REPAYMENT COST OF UP
TO $57 MILLION, FOR THE PURPOSE OF
PROVIDING FINANCING OF STREET AND
TRANSPORTATION CAPITAL PROJECTS BY
THE ISSUANCE AND PAYMENT OF
GENERAL OBLIGATION BONDS, WHICH
SHALL MATURE, BE SUBJECT TO
REDEMPTION, WITH OR WITHOUT
PREMIUM, AND BE ISSUED, DATED AND
SOLD AT SUCH TIME OR TIMES, AT SUCH
PRICES (AT, ABOVE OR BELOW PAR) AND
IN SUCH MANNER AND CONTAINING SUCH
TERMS, NOT INCONSISTENT HEREWITH, AS
THE TOWN BOARD MAY DETERMINE?
