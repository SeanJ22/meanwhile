BURLINGTON SCHOOL DISTRICT RE-6J
BALLOT ISSUE 5D
WITHOUT CREATING ANY NEW TAX OR
INCREASING ANY CURRENT TAX RATE, SHALL
BURLINGTON SCHOOL DISTRICT RE-6J TAXES BE
INCREASED BY $0 ANNUALLY BY AUTHORIZING
THE DISTRICT TO CONTINUE THE SCHOOL
DISTRICT'S MILL LEVY OVERRIDE TAX OF 3.515
MILLS ANNUALLY, WHICH WAS APPROVED BY
THE VOTERS ON NOVEMBER 5, 2019, SO THAT
SAID EXISTING TAX SHALL NOT TERMINATE IN
THE 2026 TAX COLLECTION YEAR AS SCHEDULED
BUT WILL SUNSET AS OF THE END OF TAX
COLLECTION YEAR 2035, TO PROVIDE
CONTINUING PROPERTY TAX REVENUES TO BE
USED FOR THE FOLLOWING EDUCATIONAL
PURPOSES: -COSTS ASSOCIATED WITH THE
SCHOOL DISTRICT'S HEATING, VENTILATION, AND
AIR CONDITIONING (HVAC) SYSTEM, AND,
-COSTS ASSOCIATED WITH REPAIR AND UPKEEP
TO EXTEND THE USEFUL LIFE OF FACILITIES,
BY CONTINUING TO IMPOSE A PROPERTY TAX
LEVY AT THE RATE SPECIFIED ABOVE ANNUALLY
THROUGH 2035, THE PROCEEDS FROM WHICH
SHALL CONTINUE TO BE DEPOSITED INTO AND
EXPENDED FROM GENERAL FUND OF THE
SCHOOL DISTRICT, AND SHALL CONTINUE TO BE
IN ADDITION TO THE PROPERTY TAXES THAT
OTHERWISE WOULD BE LEVIED FOR THE
GENERAL FUND, SHALL CONTINUE TO BE
INDEPENDENT OF ANY OTHER TAX LEVY
WHETHER OR NOT APPROVED BY THE VOTERS,
AND SHALL CONTINUE TO CONSTITUTE A VOTER
APPROVED REVENUE CHANGE THAT MAY BE
COLLECTED AND SPENT WITHOUT FURTHER
VOTER APPROVAL NOTWITHSTANDING THE
LIMITATION OF ARTICLE X, SECTION 20 OF THE
COLORADO CONSTITUTION OR ANY OTHER LAW?
