9. RESOLUTION & REPORT - Charter Revision Commission Final Report & Charter Revision Ballot
   Information
          A motion was made by Council member Gartner, seconded by Council member LaPine, to
          receive the communication and adopt the final report, and resolution as presented. The city
          council further resolves that the Charter amendments shall be submitted to the voters at the
          regular election to be held on November 3rd, 2026. Motion superseded by the approval of
          Amendment 2.

   Main Motion:
   A motion was made by Council member Gartner, seconded by Council member LaPine, to receive the
   communication and adopt the final report, and resolution as amended. The city council further
   resolves that the Charter amendments shall be submitted to the voters at the regular election to be
   held on November 3rd, 2026. Yes – 14, No – 6 (Coelho, Fay, Henry, Rotello, Chianese, Duane Perkins).
   Motion Carried.
   Council member Henry asked to send the item to an Ad Hoc. Council Member Buzaid asked legal
   counsel if that is appropriate; Mr. Casagrande cited Ordinance 2-32 and noted that the “appropriate
   committee” refers to the Committee of the Whole that already reviewed the item.

   A motion was made by Council member Henry, seconded by Council member Fay, to recommit the
   item. Motion deemed invalid due to state statute restrictions.

   Council member Buzaid referred to state statute language in Sec. 7-191 (d) that provides a timeline and
   specific actions that the Council can take at this point; Mr. Casagrande agreed that the next action in the
   state statute is to approve or reject the report as the appointing body. Council member Rotello stated that
                                               Page 3 of 6
any motion to recommit the report should have been made at the Special Meeting when the Council
recommendations were approved. Council member Buzaid reiterated that the motion is inappropriate.

Amendment 1:

       A motion was made by Council member Fay, seconded by Council member Coelho, to amend
       the report to amend all references of 4 year terms to 2 year terms. No – 15, Yes – 5 (Coelho,
       Fay, Henry, Rotello, Duane Perkins). Motion Failed.

       Council member Fay discussed concerns with the Charter Revision process and the functionality
       of the ballot questions in the resolution. Council member Coelho explained the benefits of
       electing officials every two years. Council member Rotello asked for procedural clarification and
       how the amendment would affect the Main Motion and the language in the resolution.

       Council member Fay asked for a Roll Call vote.

Amendment 2:

       A motion was made by Council member Rotello, seconded by Council member Coelho, to
       amend Question 1 to include a roll back of the Commission’s suggest to eliminate staggered
       terms of the Board of Education. Motion withdrawn by Council members Rotello and Coelho.

       Council members Buzaid and Rotello discussed the intent of the motion with Mr. Casagrande.
       Council member Gartner noted that the terms will remain staggered until the 2031 election and
       the Council can address the issue again if needed within the next five years. Council member
       Britton discussed the intent of un-staggering the terms. Additional discussion was had by
       Council members Rotello, Chianese and Fay regarding the addition of language with Mr.
       Casagrande. Council member Salvatore asked for clarification of the amendment language;
       Council member Robinson explained that the way the questions are currently written in the
       resolution, they are contingent on each other; therefore, she suggested amending the language in
       question 1 of the resolution.

A motion was made by Council member Rotello, seconded by Council member Chianese, to amend
Question 1 by adding: and un-stagger the Board of Education terms pursuant proposed section 2-2
A.c.1 and authorizes Corporation Counsel to ensure that the question is correct as to form). Motion
Carried.

Council member Rotello explained that the intent of the new motion is to allow the voters to decide if
they want to have 4 year terms and if they want to have staggered terms of the Board of Education.

Council member Henry asked to send the resolution and ballot questions to an ad hoc; Mayor Alves
clarified that this is one item. Council member Coelho asked what the deadline for approval of the
Report; Mr. Casagrande read the state statute language explaining that the appointing body has 15 days
from submission of the report; the ballot questions has to be submitted to the Secretary of State by
September 3rd. Council member supports the motion; Council member LaPine asked that the motion be
restated. Discussion was had regarding the language that should be used in the motion and if the
questions should be separated.
