Shall the Charter of the City of Danbury be revised, as recommended by the Charter Revision Commission and approved by the City Council, to provide that the terms of office for all municipal elected offices, including Mayor, Town Clerk, Treasurer, City Council, Zoning Commission, Zoning Commission Alternates, and Constable, upon the municipal election to be held November 2027, be four (4) years, and to provide that the terms of office for all Board of Education members be revised according to Section 2.2.A(c)(1) of the proposed revised Charter of the City of Danbury?

c. (1) A Board of Education 
         .At the  
          general City election in      
         November of 2027 there shall be elected six (6) members,  

         each of whom shall hold office for a term of four (4) years. At  
         the general election in November 2029, there shall be  
         elected five (5) members, each of whom shall hold office for  
         a term of two (2) years. At the general City election to be  
         held in November 2031 and quadrennially thereafter there
