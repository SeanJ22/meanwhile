c. (1) A Board of Education 
         .At the  
          general City election in      
         November of 2027 there shall be elected six (6) members,  

         each of whom shall hold office for a term of four (4) years. At  
         the general election in November 2029, there shall be  
         elected five (5) members, each of whom shall hold office for  
         a term of two (2) years. At the general City election to be  
         held in November 2031 and quadrennially thereafter there  
         shall be elected eleven (11) members, all of whom shall hold  
         office for a term of four (4) years.
