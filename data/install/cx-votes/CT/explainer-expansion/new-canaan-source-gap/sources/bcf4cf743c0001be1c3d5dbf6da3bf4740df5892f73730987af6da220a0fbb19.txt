  FINAL BALLOT QUESTIONS AS APPROVED BY THE TOWN
                 COUNCIL ON 8/12/26


Ballot Question 1:

Shall the current Charter be changed to require all elected and appointed Town
officers with managerial authority to comply with applicable Town policies, ethics
standards, and lawful administrative directives, and to be accountable to the First
Selectman for administrative and operational matters that would not otherwise
conflict with their statutory authority?

Ballot Question 2:

Shall the current Charter be changed to require Town Council confirmation of
appointments to the Planning and Zoning Commission?

Ballot Question 3:

Shall the current Charter be changed to require Town Council approval for
changes to Town-owned real property?

Ballot Question 4:

Shall the current Charter be changed to no longer require Board of Finance
members to be real property taxpayers?

Ballot Question 5:

Shall the current Charter be changed to add 3 alternate members to the Ethics
Board?

Ballot Question 6:

Shall the current Charter be changed to clarify definitions, delete outdated
references, and update provisions to current practice?
