The Town Charter of New Canaan outlines the governance structure and procedures for New Canaan's boards, commissions and elected officers: specifically, how responsibilities are distributed, decisions are made, and accountability is enforced. This year, voters will have a chance to decide which of the six proposed changes to the Town Charter should or should not be made. The proposed amendments to the Town Charter were developed by the Charter Revision Commission (CRC) after a comprehensive review process that culminated with a Final Report to the Town Council. The Town Council considered the CRC recommendations and voted to approve six questions to put before the voters. All New Canaan voters are invited to learn more about the CRC’s Final Report and the upcoming ballot, so they have knowledge about the Yes / No Charter questions by Election Day. UPDATE: The six proposed Charter amendments recommended by the Charter Revision Commission and approved by the Town Council will be placed on the November ballot with these questions for voters: Ballot Question 1 : Shall the current Charter be changed to require all elected and appointed Town officers with managerial authority to comply with applicable Town policies, ethics standards, and lawful administrative directives, and to be accountable to the First Selectman for administrative and operational matters that would not otherwise conflict with their statutory authority? Ballot Question 2 : Shall the current Charter be changed to require Town Council confirmation of appointments to the Planning and Zoning Commission? Ballot Question 3 : Shall the current Charter be changed to require Town Council approval for changes to Town-owned real property? Ballot Question 4 : Shall the current Charter be changed to no longer require Board of Finance members to be real property taxpayers? Ballot Question 5 : Shall the current Charter be changed to add 3 alternate members to the Ethics Board? Ballot Question 6 : Shall the current Charter be changed to clarify definitions, delete outdated references, and update provisions to current practice? Town of New Canaan CT!
