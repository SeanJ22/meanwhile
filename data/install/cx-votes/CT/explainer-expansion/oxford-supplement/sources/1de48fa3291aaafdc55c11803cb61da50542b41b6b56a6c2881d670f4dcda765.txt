Shall the Town of Oxford adopt the proposed 2026 Charter Revision as approved in accordance with Title 7, Chapter 99 of the Connecticut General Statutes?

Section 3-4: Capital Plan Public Hearing

Each year a Capital Plan Public Hearing shall be held by the Board of Selectmen and Board of Finance no later than the first week in February. The Capital Plan shall be a 5-year plan. A representative of the Board of Education shall be available to present a report on the present status of buildings and grounds and explain any present or future requirements for expansion or major improvements. Town Representatives from departments shall be present to explain any major improvements for the next 5 years. No funding for any items included in the Capital Plan shall be appropriated or approved at the meeting.

Section 6-5(B) (4) will become 6-5(B) (3): Recommendations of the Board of Selectmen:

The Board of Selectmen shall present a proposal previously considered at the Capital Plan Public Hearing, recommending those municipal capital improvement projects to be undertaken during the ensuing fiscal year with suggestions for financing.

Article 6
Section 6-2 (D)

(D) Holding Other Office: No member of the Board of Finance shall hold any other elective or appointive office in the Town government, including moderating Budget Town Meetings and Budget Referendums; nor shall they serve on any appointed board or commission of the Town. They may serve on Town advisory boards or as representatives or advisors to local, regional, and state agencies.

Section 10-5: Effective Date

(A) The effective date of this Charter shall be at noon on January 1, 1991. The effective date of any subsequent revisions shall be at noon on the January first immediately following their adoption.

(B) Copies of all previous Charters are on file in the Office of the Oxford Town Clerk and in the Office of the Secretary of the State of Connecticut.
