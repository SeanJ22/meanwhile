                                 EXPLANATORY TEXT

 PROPOSED REVISIONS OF THE SIMSBURY TOWN CHARTER TO BE VOTED ON
             AT THE NOVEMBER 3, 2026 STATE ELECTION

Pursuant to §9-369(b) of the Connecticut General Statutes (C.G.S.), as amended, the
following explanatory text of the proposed revisions to the Simsbury Town Charter was
prepared by the Simsbury Town Clerk and reviewed by the Town Attorney. It is not
intended to advocate for approval or disapproval at a referendum vote. The proposed
Charter revisions were recommended by the Simsbury Charter Revision Commission
and approved by the Simsbury Board of Selectmen for submission to the electorate at
the November 3, 2026 State Election, in accordance with C.G.S. §7-188. If a majority of
the electors approve the Charter revision question, the revisions shall be incorporated
into the Simsbury Town Charter effective December 7, 2026. The full text of the Revised
Charter is available on the Town's website.

   proposed by the Simsbury Charter Revision Commission to take effect December 7,

CHAPTER I: Incorporation and General Powers - clerical revisions and edits only

CHAPTER II: Elections - clerical revisions and edits only

CHAPTER III: Elected Boards and Commissions

Changes title to Elected Boards and Commissions

Section 301(a) requires that alternates seated on a Board in the absence of a regular
member must be from the same party when available

Section 302(a)(1) refers to First Selectman as the “First”

Section 302(a)(2) refers to Board of Selectmen as “Selectmen”

Section 304 titled Appointed Boards and Commissions is removed

CHAPTER IV: Board of Selectmen and the First
Changes title to Board of Selectmen and the First
Section 401 clarifies that the Selectmen serve as the Town’s legislative body
Section 402(a) removes the welfare subcommittee

Section 403(c) clarifies that the Selectmen may delegate authority to the Town Manager
to apply for grants and that the Selectmen shall conduct an annual performance review
of the Town Manager to be administered by the First

Section 403(d) provides that the Selectmen shall review the Code of Ordinances no less
than every four (4) years

Section 403(e) clarifies the role of the Selectmen with respect to Town employees

Section 404 defines the role and responsibilities of the First

Section 405(a) adds language about where public hearing notices must be published

Section 407 cross references Annual Budget definition with Section 802

Section 410(a)(3) adds language regarding the lease, license or development of Town-
owned property

CHAPTER V: Town Manager and Administration of Town

Section 502(a)(11) adds language regarding collaboration with residents and taxpayers
Section 502(a)(13) provides resources to support elected boards and commissions

Section 502(a)(15) requires the Town Manager to meet with the Boards of Selectmen
and Finance prior to commencing negotiations

Section 502(a)(16) requires the Town Manager to consult with Board Chairs prior to
annual performance review of Administrative Official assigned to that Board
Section 502 (c) provides that the Town Manager is responsible for developing position
descriptions for all administrative officials, subject to Selectmen approval; outlines
process for appointments, hiring, and removal of administrative officials

Section 502(d) clarifies the Town Manager’s role in executing contracts on behalf of the
Town

Section 503(c) clarifies who may fill the Town Manager position in case of inability,
temporary absence or vacancy

CHAPTER VI: Appointed Boards and Commissions

Reorganizes chapter by placing statutorily required boards and commissions first

Section 601 provides process for removal of board and commission members who are
absent for 3 consecutive meetings
Section 601(b) and (c) adds clarifying language
Section 601(d) requires public audience at all regular meetings

Section 614 reorganizes and redefines Police Commission powers and duties for
consistency with current operations, to include: reviewing policy and procedures for
handling citizen complaints; meeting with Boards of Selectmen and Finance prior to
entering into collective bargaining agreements; and submitting proposed budget to Town
Manager with the opportunity to address the Board of Selectmen on said budget

CHAPTER VII: Administrative Officials and Departments
Changes title to Administrative Officials and Departments

Section 702 establishes appointment and removal authority for Administrative Officials

Section 705 defines powers and duties of Administrative Officials
Section 705 removes Welfare Officer language and the distinction between Town Officer
and Administrative Officer

CHAPTER VIII: Finance and Taxation

Changes title of Section 801 to Duties of Board of Finance

Section 801(a) provides that the Board of Finance shall be the budget-making authority
for the Town

Section 801(b) defines Board of Finance authority regarding supplemental
appropriations and moves Section 809 from the current Charter to Section 801(b)

Section 801(c) authorizes the Board of Finance to approve transfers in accordance with
Section 812(f)

Section 801(d) replaces Section 813 of the current Charter, and clarifies requirements of
short and long-term borrowing and the issuance of bonds and notes

Section 801(e) requires that the Board of Finance select the independent public
accountant or auditor

Section 801(f) moves language on retirement funds from the current Charter Section
808 to Section 801(f)

Section 801(g) requires Boards of Selectmen and Education to consult with Board of
Finance prior to collective bargaining negotiations or contractual changes to retirement
plans
Section 801(h) creates a section regarding information, analyses and policies
Subsection 801(h)(1) establishes requirements for providing financial reports requested
by the Board of Finance
Subsection 801(h)(2) establishes requirements for publication and location of Annual
Budgets, audit reports and regular financial reports

Section 802 clarifies the dates for the fiscal year and the chronology regarding the Town
and Annual Budgets

Section 803 requires the Town Manager to present a Capital Improvement Plan for the
ensuing fiscal year and four (4) fiscal years thereafter

Section 807(a) and (b) changes the word “Budget” to “Town Budget” for clarification

Section 808 changes title to Duties of Board of Finance on the Adoption of the Annual
Budget for the purpose of clarification between the Town Budget and Annual Budget

Subsection 808(a) adds General Statutes reference regarding public hearing on the
proposed budget

Subsection 808(b) adds language regarding methods of public notice regarding a
referendum and amount of budget detail

Subsection 808(d) defines requirement of capital appropriations in the “then-current
fiscal year” being subject to an automatic referendum

CHAPTER IX: Town Employees - clerical revisions and edits only

CHAPTER X: Transition and Miscellaneous Provisions - clerical revisions and edits only
