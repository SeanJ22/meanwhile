Ansonia — Question 1

Shall Section 43 of the City of Ansonia Charter be amended to require that the Mayor submit a proposed fiscal-year budget to the Board of Apportionment and Taxation no later than the second Monday in March each year and if a public referendum is necessary shall the timeline for the public referendum be changed to no more than fourteen calendar days and no less than ten calendar days after approval by the Board of Aldermen?
