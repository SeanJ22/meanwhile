Enfield — Question 1

Shall the Town of Enfield appropriate $511,000,000 for the new construction of Hazardville Memorial Elementary School, Prudence Crandall Elementary School, Parkman Elementary School and Barnard Elementary School and for the conversion of Eli Whitney Elementary School into the Eagle Academy Therapeutic Day School, and authorize the issuance of bonds or notes of the Town in an amount not to exceed $89,000,000 with the balance funded by grants and other available funds?
