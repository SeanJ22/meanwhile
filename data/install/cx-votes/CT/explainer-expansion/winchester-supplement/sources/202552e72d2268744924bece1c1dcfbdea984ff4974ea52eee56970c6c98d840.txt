 before voting. It is intended for voter education and does not advocate for a YES or NO vote.

How to read this handout: For each proposal, “Current” describes the existing Charter and “Proposed” describes
what would change if the amendment is approved.

5. Uniform Definitions for Eligibility
The current Charter uses different definitions in different sections for people eligible to serve on Town boards. The
amendment would use uniform definitions for “elector” and “property owner.”
   Elector: A person eligible to vote in Winchester in Presidential elections.
   Property Owner: This term would replace “taxpayer” in the affected Charter provisions.
   Budget voting eligibility: The proposal states that people who are currently eligible to vote on Town budget and
   budget-related issues would remain eligible; there is no change in that regard.
   Affected Charter sections: 206, 305, 306, 309, 315, 609, 912, 1101, 1102, 1107 and 1708.
