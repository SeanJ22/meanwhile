To help residents make an informed decision, the Town has prepared a Charter Revision Voter Information Handout explaining the proposed changes in plain language. The handout outlines what the Charter currently provides and what would change if the proposed revisions are approved. The proposals address the Town Clerk position, Registrar of Voters terms, service on multiple Town boards, the annual budget process, and terminology used to define electors and property owners. Early Voting Residents do not have to wait until Election Day to cast their ballot. Early Voting will be available October 19 through November 1, 2026. In Winchester, all Early Voting takes place at Winchester Town Hall, 2nd Floor . Early Voting Hours: October 19 – November 1: 10:00 AM – 6:00 PM daily Tuesday, October 27: 8:00 AM – 8:00 PM Thursday, October 29: 8:00 AM – 8:00 PM Election Day – Tuesday, November 3 Polls will be open from 6:00 AM to 8:00 PM . Winchester's Election Day polling location is Isabelle M. Pearson School, 2 Wetmore Avenue, Winsted . Learn About the Charter Questions Before You Vote We encourage residents to review the Town's Charter Revision Voter Information Handout before casting their ballot. The purpose of the handout is not to advocate for or against the proposed changes, but to provide residents with clear information about what the Charter says today and what would change if the amendments were approved .
