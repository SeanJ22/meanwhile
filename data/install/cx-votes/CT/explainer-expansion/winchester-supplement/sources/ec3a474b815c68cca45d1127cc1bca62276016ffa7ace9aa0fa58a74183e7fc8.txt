The vested powers of the Board of Selectmen derive from the Town Charter. They meet twice monthly, on the first and third Monday evenings at 7:00 PM in Winchester Town Hall. Citizens wishing to address the Board may do so during either of the two Citizens’ Comments portions of each meeting.
