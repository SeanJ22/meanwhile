Shall the city charter be amended to provide that vacancies in the offices of mayor or city commission be filled by appointment by the remaining city commissioners until the next regular municipal election if the vacancy occurs with sufficient time before the candidate qualifying period for such election, and otherwise by appointment for the remainder of the unexpired term, as proposed by Ordinance No. 2026-165?

Ordinance No. 2026-165 would submit a ballot referendum to the electors of the City of Gainesville to amend the City Charter provision regarding the filling of vacancies in office for the Mayor and City Commission. The proposed amendment would eliminate the filling of vacancies of six months or more by special election and provide for vacancies in office to be filled through appointment by the remaining Commission members until the next practicable biennial regular election.
