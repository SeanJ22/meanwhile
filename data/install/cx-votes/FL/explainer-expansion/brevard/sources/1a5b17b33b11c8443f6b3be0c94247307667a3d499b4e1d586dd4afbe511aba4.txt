Charter Amendment Related to the Process
for Filling Vacancies on the City Council
Shall section 3.062 of the Palm Bay City
Charter be amended to provide that in the
event of a vacancy on the City Council, if
there are 12 months or less remaining in
the unexpired term the vacancy may be
filled by appointment by the remaining
members of the City Council, and if there
are more than 12 months remaining in the
unexpired term the vacancy shall be filled
by election?
Enmienda del Acta Constitutiva en relación

Concejo Municipal
¿Se debe modificar la Sección 3.062 del
Acta Constitutiva de Palm Bay para
establecer que, en caso de vacante en el

podrá ser cubierta mediante designación
por los miembros restantes del Concejo
Municipal, y si restan más de 12 meses de
mandato, la vacante se cubrirá mediante
elección?
