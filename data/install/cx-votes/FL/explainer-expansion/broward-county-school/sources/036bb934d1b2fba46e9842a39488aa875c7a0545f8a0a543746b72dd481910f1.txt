                                   RESOLUTION NO. 2026-114

 1   A RESOLUTION OF THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS OF BROWARD

 2   COUNTY, FLORIDA, PROVIDING FOR A REFERENDUM ELECTION TO BE HELD ON

 3   NOVEMBER 3, 2026, TO DETERMINE WHETHER TO APPROVE A 0.25% SALES

 4   SURTAX FOR A PERIOD OF FIVE YEARS TO FUND THE BROWARD COUNTY

 5   HEALTH CARE PLAN; AND PROVIDING FOR SEVERABILITY AND AN EFFECTIVE

 6   DATE.

 7                             (Sponsored by Mayor Mark D. Bogen)

 8

 9           WHEREAS, heart disease is the leading cause of death in the United States,

10   according to the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (“CDC”);

11           WHEREAS, a 2023 report from the American Heart Association found that 52.4%

12   of men and 44.8% of women have cardiovascular disease, which includes coronary heart

13   disease, heart failure, stroke, and hypertension;

14           WHEREAS, the CDC calculates that one person dies every 34 seconds in the

15   United States from cardiovascular disease and that almost 700,000 people in the United

16   States die from heart disease every year;

17           WHEREAS, the Florida Department of Health indicates that in 2024, the most

18   recent year for which statistics are available, heart disease was the leading cause of death

19   in Florida and in Broward County, accounting for 3,532 deaths in Broward County;

20           WHEREAS, a recent study published in the American Journal of Managed Care,

21   “Heart Health Outcomes Linked to Low Socioeconomic Status,” indicates that the rate of
22   heart disease among persons living in areas of low socioeconomic status is more than

23   twice the rate for persons living in areas of high socioeconomic status;

24         WHEREAS, heart disease and cardiovascular disease are treatable diseases,

25   particularly when detected early;

26         WHEREAS, the Broward County preventive heart test pilot project provided free

27   screenings to more than 7,000 residents; more than 4,000 participants had abnormal

28   results indicating a need for medical care, such as prescription medication, cardiac

29   catheterization, open heart surgery, and in some cases urgent non-cardiac care;

30         WHEREAS, the Florida Department of Health indicates that in 2024, cancer was

31   the second leading cause of death in Florida, accounting for 47,990 deaths in Florida and

32   for 3,427 deaths in Broward County;

33         WHEREAS, according to the Florida Cancer Data System, the top five most

34   frequently diagnosed cancers—lung, breast, prostate, colorectal, and melanoma—

35   account for approximately half of the cancer diagnoses in Florida;

36         WHEREAS, Broward County already funds certain primary health care services

37   provided by the North and South Broward Hospital Districts, and also funds a number of

38   other significant programs such as mental health services, programs to reduce infant

39   mortality in high-risk areas, reimbursement for emergency hospital services provided to

40   indigent residents, and HIV/AIDS medical and support services;

41         WHEREAS, it is in the public interest of Broward County to provide programs for

42   qualified Broward County residents, including residents who are indigent or medically

43   poor, to receive primary care, preventive care, and hospital care services to prevent and

44   treat these leading causes of death;




                                                 2
45         WHEREAS, public and private insurance plans are required to cover certain

46   preventive cancer screenings, such as mammograms, pap smears, and colonoscopies,

47   without imposing cost-sharing on the patient; but those same insurance plans typically do

48   not cover preventive cardiac screening tests (e.g., calcium scoring (“CAC”) or a coronary

49   CT angiography (“CCTA”)) at no additional cost to the patient;

50         WHEREAS, as a result, many Broward County residents with public or private

51   insurance have medical needs that are not covered by their insurance programs, or have

52   insufficient third-party insurance coverage to address these serious health risks;

53         WHEREAS, Broward County retained medical experts to develop a Broward

54   County Health Care Plan (“Health Care Plan”) for qualified Broward County residents,

55   including those who qualify as indigent or medically poor, to receive primary care,

56   preventive care, and hospital care services, with an emphasis on preventing and treating

57   heart disease and cancer, the two leading causes of death in Broward County;

58         WHEREAS, Section 212.055(4), Florida Statutes, provides for the levy of an

59   Indigent Care and Trauma Center Surtax (“Health Care Surtax”) by enactment of an

60   ordinance by a majority of the members of the Broward County Board of County

61   Commissioners (“Board”), subject to approval by a majority of the electors of Broward

62   County voting in a referendum election on the Health Care Surtax;

63         WHEREAS, if the levy of the Health Care Surtax is approved by referendum at the

64   General Election on November 3, 2026, the Health Care Surtax in the amount of 0.25%

65   will be levied for a period of five (5) years, and the proceeds of the Health Care Surtax

66   will be expended as authorized in Section 212.055(4), Florida Statutes, and in accordance




                                                 3
67   with the Health Care Plan, as approved by the Board and as amended from time to time;

68   and

69          WHEREAS, the public interest will be served by having the Health Care Plan,

70   including the allocation of funds, reevaluated and adjusted from time to time to ensure the

71   Health Care Plan is meeting its objectives and to ensure the best and most efficient use

72   of the Health Care Surtax proceeds, NOW, THEREFORE,

73          BE IT RESOLVED BY THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS OF

74   BROWARD COUNTY, FLORIDA:

75          Section 1.    Conditioned upon the Board’s approval of an Ordinance levying a

76   one-quarter percent (0.25%) Health Care Surtax for a period of five (5) years, a

77   referendum election is called for the General Election on November 3, 2026, and at such

78   other times as permitted for voting under Chapter 101, Florida Statutes.

79          Section 2.    In accordance with Section 100.342, Florida Statutes, at least thirty

80   (30) days’ notice of the referendum election shall be published by the County

81   Administrator in a newspaper of general circulation in Broward County or, if applicable, in

82   accordance with Section 50.0311, Florida Statutes. The publication shall be made at least

83   twice, including once in the fifth week and once in the third week prior to the week in which

84   the referendum is to be held. The notice is hereby attached as Exhibit A and made a part

85   hereof.

86          Section 3.    Said referendum election shall be held between the hours of

87   7:00 a.m. and 7:00 p.m. on Tuesday, November 3, 2026, and at such other days and

88   times as provided under applicable law, including early voting as provided by

89   Section 101.657, Florida Statutes, at polling places established in Broward County. All



                                                  4
 90   registered and qualified electors in Broward County shall be eligible to vote in the

 91   referendum election. Vote-by-mail ballots and early voting access for the referendum shall

 92   be made available as provided by law.

 93          Section 4.    The election boards consisting of the clerks and inspectors

 94   appointed to serve for the November 3, 2026, General Election are hereby appointed to

 95   serve in the referendum election in the manner provided by law.

 96          Section 5.    The Broward County Canvassing Board shall canvass the returns in

 97   said election and shall certify the results of the referendum election as provided by law.

 98          Section 6.    The County Administrator shall furnish a certified copy of this

 99   adopted Resolution to the Supervisor of Elections for Broward County promptly upon

100   adoption of this Resolution and enactment of the referenced Ordinance by the Board.

101          Section 7.    The title and summary of the referendum to appear on the General

102   Election ballot on November 3, 2026, as embodied in this enabling Resolution, shall be

103   as follows:

104    HEALTH CARE SERVICES, SUCH AS HEART AND CANCER SCREENINGS, FOR

105                        COUNTY RESIDENTS, FUNDED BY SURTAX

106   Shall Broward County fund (1) preventive heart and cancer tests, to the extent not

107   covered by insurance, for qualified Broward County residents, (2) primary and preventive

108   health care for indigent or medically poor residents, and (3) other health services, as

109   described in a County health care plan, as periodically amended, by levying a one quarter

110   of one percent sales surtax for five years subject to automatic repeal if a 1% infrastructure

111   surtax is levied?

NOTICE OF REFERENDUM ELECTION TO BE HELD ON TUESDAY, NOVEMBER 3,

2026, AND AT SUCH OTHER TIMES AS PROVIDED FOR EARLY VOTING BY

SECTION 101.657, FLORIDA STATUTES, TO DETERMINE WHETHER TO APPROVE

A 0.25% SALES SURTAX FOR A PERIOD OF FIVE YEARS TO FUND THE BROWARD

COUNTY HEALTH CARE PLAN.

       A countywide referendum election will be held on Tuesday, November 3, 2026,

and at such other times as provided for by Chapter 101, Florida Statutes, for the purpose

of determining whether to approve a 0.25% sales surtax pursuant to Section 212.055(4),

Florida Statutes, for a period of five (5) years.

       As set forth in enabling Resolution No. 2026-___, adopted on ___, by the Board of

County Commissioners of Broward County, Florida, the title and summary to appear on

the referendum ballot shall be as follows:

 HEALTH CARE SERVICES, SUCH AS HEART AND CANCER SCREENINGS, FOR

                     COUNTY RESIDENTS, FUNDED BY SURTAX

Shall Broward County fund (1) preventive heart and cancer tests, to the extent not

covered by insurance, for qualified Broward County residents, (2) primary and preventive

health care for indigent or medically poor residents, and (3) other health services, as

described in a County health care plan, as periodically amended, by levying a one quarter

of one percent sales surtax for five years subject to automatic repeal if a 1% infrastructure

surtax is levied?

       The referendum shall be held on November 3, 2026, between the hours of
