 9              WHEREAS, the Broward County Charter (the "Charter") currently provides that an

10   independent board nominates one individual for the position of County Auditor, subject to

11   ratification by a majority of the Broward County Board of County Commissioners (the

12   "Commission"); and

13              WHEREAS, this Charter amendment, if approved by the electorate, would provide that

14   the independent board nominate three candidates and that the Commission appoint the

15   County Auditor, by a majority vote , from among those three candidates , NOW, THEREFORE ,

16              BE IT RESOLVED BY THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS OF BROWARD

17   COUNTY, FLORIDA:

18              Section 1.     Subject to an affirmative vote of the general electorate, Section 4.01 of

19   the Charter of Broward County, Florida , entitled "County Auditor," is hereby amended to read

20   as follows :

               NOTICE OF REFERENDUM ELECTION TO BE HELD ON
               TUESDAY, NOVEMBER 3, 2026 , THE DATE OF THE

       A countywide referendum election will be held on Tuesday, November 3, 2026,

and at such other times as provided for by Chapter 101 , Florida Statutes, for the purpose

of determining whether to amend the Broward County Charter to allow for the selection

of the County Auditor from three candidates .

       As set forth in enabling Resolution No. insert , adopted on insert , by the Board

of County Commissioners of Broward County, Florida , the title and summary to appear

on the referendum ballot shall be as follows:

                    Revised Process for Selecting the County Auditor

Currently under the Broward County Charter, an independent board nominates one individual

for County Auditor, and the County Commission ratifies that selection upon determining the

nominee is qualified . Should the Charter be amended to provide that the independent board

nominates three individuals , thereby enabling the County Commission to appoint as County

Auditor the qualified nominee it determines is best able to serve in that position?

       YES

       NO

       A     complete    copy    of    the     proposed    amendment       is   available   at

https://www.broward .erg/charter.

7:00 a.m. and 7:00 p.m., and at such other days and times as provided under applicable
