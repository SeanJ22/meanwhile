              NOTICE OF REFERENDUM ELECTION TO BE HELD ON
              TUESDAY, NOVEMBER 3, 2026, THE DATE OF THE
              GENERAL ELECTION, TO DETERMINE WHETHER TO
              AMEND THE BROWARD COUNTY CHARTER TO ALLOW
              FOR ALTERNATIVE METHODS OF APPROVAL OF PLATS.

      A countywide referendum election will be held on Tuesday, November 3, 2026,

and at such other times as provided for by Chapter 101, Florida Statutes, for the purpose

of determining whether to amend the Broward County Charter to allow for altemative

methods of approval of plats.

      As set forth in enabling Resolution No. [insert], adopted on [insert], by the Board

of County Commissioners of Broward County, Florida, the title and summary to appear

on the referendum ballot shall be as follows:

                    Altemative Process for County Plat Approval

Currently under the Broward County Charter, all plats in Broward County must be

approved by the County Commission prior to recording; this proposal amends the Charter

to allow the County Commission to provide, by ordinance, as permitted under state law,

an altemative process for approving plats, such as administrative approval, which might

enable more expeditious plat approval including by allowing approval when the County

Commission is in recess.
