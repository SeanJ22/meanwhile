Shall the Charter of the City of Cooper City be amended to provide that the annual salary and benefits of the Mayor and City Commissioners shall be established by ordinance, but in no event shall such salary and benefits exceed the salary and benefits provided to the lowest paid, full-time position in the City?

ORDINANCE NO. 26-12



                           EXHIBIT “A”

                     CHARTER AMENDMENT


Section 3.06. - Compensation; expenses.

(1) The city commission may determine any change in the monthly salary of the mayor and
commissioners by ordinance, but no ordinance increasing such salary shall become effective
until the date of commencement of the terms of commissioners elected at the next regular
election. The annual salary and benefits of the Mayor and City Commissioners shall be
established by ordinance; provided, however, in no event shall said salary and benefits exceed
the salary and benefits provided to the lowest paid, full-time City employee.

(2) Any benefit plans to be provided to the mayor and commissioners shall first be established
by ordinance, said ordinance to be effective immediately upon its passage and final adoption.

(3) The mayor and commissioners shall receive their actual and necessary expenses incurred in
the performance of their duties of office.

Commissioner Shrouder asked whether the wording of the question is sufficient with respect to the term
"employee". The City Attorney can revise the language from "employee" to "position". This change shifts
the focus from an individual to a position with a range.

Tena Granit, a member of the Charter Review Board, said the word “position” is sufficient.

MOTION: Commissioner Shrouder moved to approve Ordinance 24-12, amending the language to state
the lowest full-time position in the City. Commissioner Smith seconded the motion, which prevailed by
a unanimous roll-call vote. (5-0)
