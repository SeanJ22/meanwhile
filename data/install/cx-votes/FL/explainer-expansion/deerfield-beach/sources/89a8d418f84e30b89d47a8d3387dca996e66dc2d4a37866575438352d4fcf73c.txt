Shall the Charter be amended to require that any future action by the City Commission to transition, transfer, contract out, consolidate, or otherwise assign, police or fire services from the existing provider to another governmental entity or agency, special district, or sheriff’s office, be approved by an affirmative vote of at least four-fifths (4/5) of the City Commission?

- FOUR-FIFTHS VOTE FOR TRANSITION OF POLICE OR FIRE SERVICES. No future action to transition, transfer, contract out, consolidate, or otherwise assign, the provision of police services or fire services from the existing provider to another governmental entity or agency, special district, or sheriff’s office, shall be effective unless approved by an affirmative vote of at least four-fifths (4/5) of the City Commission.

Effectiveness. A. Each of the Charter Amendments provided for in Sections 2 and 4 above shall become effective only if the majority of the qualified electors voting on the specific Charter Amendment vote for its adoption, and it shall be considered adopted and effective upon certification of the election results. B. That the City Attorney is authorized to revise the Charter to the extent necessary to assure that any amendments adopted conform to one another and are properly included in the publication of the revised City Charter. Further, that in the event that some of the Charter Amendments are approved by the electors, conforming amendments shall be deemed to be adopted and the City Attorney is authorized to reflect and implement such revisions of the Charter to the extent necessary to assure that each amendment adopted conforms to all remaining Charter provisions. C. That following the adoption of the Charter Amendments, the City Clerk shall file the adopted Charter Amendments with the Florida Department of State. Application of Amendments. That each of the Charter Amendments adopted by the electors shall be applied prospectively only.
