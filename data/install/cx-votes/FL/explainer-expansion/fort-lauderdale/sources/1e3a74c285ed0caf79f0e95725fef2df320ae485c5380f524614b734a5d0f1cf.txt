Should the City of Fort Lauderdale Charter be amended to update the qualifications for the offices of mayor and city commissioner, and provide that elected officials assume office at the first regular City Commission meeting following certification of their election, and that a vice mayor be designated at the City Commission’s first meeting after all elected officials assume office following a regular municipal election and annually thereafter, instead of at the first meeting in April?
