SECTION 2. That this Ordinance shall not take effect unless a corresponding ballot measure
is approved by a majority of the electors of the City of Fort Lauderdale voting at the election to be

held on November 3, 2026. The ballot measure to be submitted shall be in substantially the
following form:

CHARTER AMENDMENT NO. 4
SPECIAL MEETINGS OF THE CITY COMMISSION

Should the City of Fort Lauderdale Charter be amended so that special
meetings of the City Commission may be called upon at least seventy-two hours’
notice, except that in the event of an emergency, where there is the potential for
imminent harm or the ability to lessen or avert a catastrophe, a special meeting of

the City Commission may be called upon such notice as is practicable under the
circumstances?

SECTION 4. That all ordinances or parts of ordinances in conflict herewith, be and the same
