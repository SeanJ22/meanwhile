Should the City of Fort Lauderdale Charter be amended to establish the Charter position of Police Chief, who shall report directly to the City Commission rather than to the City Manager, and be employed and compensated under such terms and conditions as the City Commission deems appropriate?

(a) There is hereby established the position of Police Chief. The Police Chief shall be appointed by the City Commission and employed under such terms and conditions as the City Commission may deem appropriate. The Police Chief shall receive such compensation as the City Commission may by resolution fix and designate.

(b) The individual serving as Police Chief on the effective date of this section shall automatically become the first appointed Police Chief.

(c) The Police Chief shall reside in the City of Fort Lauderdale during his or her employment with the city, but he or she need not be a resident of the City of Fort Lauderdale or the State of Florida at the time of his or her appointment. Notwithstanding the foregoing sentence, the first appointed Police Chief need not reside in the City of Fort Lauderdale. Nor shall a Police Chief who is promoted to the position of Police Chief from within the Police Department need to reside in the City of Fort Lauderdale.
