PoliceAndFire_Zones – Map service
For Internal Use Only

This map service contains GIS data for Emergency Management in Okaloosa County, FL and is being used in the Emergency Responder Lookup (ERL) Application. These layers included are: Sheriff Response Zones, Sheriff Districts, Fire Districts, EMS Zones, Emergency Response Zones (Run Zones), Emergency Service Number, and Auto Launch Zones for Helo. This service was published with ArcGIS Pro. 

Access & Permissions
Although this data is not secured, it is intended solely for use by authorized public safety agencies within Okaloosa County, FL.
