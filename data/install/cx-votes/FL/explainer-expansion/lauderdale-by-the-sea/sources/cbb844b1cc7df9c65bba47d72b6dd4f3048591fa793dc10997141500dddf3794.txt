The Town Charter establishes three two-year terms for the Mayor-Commissioner. This amendment aligns the Mayor-Commissioner and Commissioner term limits by changing the term length to four years and the number of terms to two terms, revising the maximum term limit accordingly, and clarifying application to the Mayor-Commissioner term ending in November 2028. Shall the Town Charter be amended to change the Mayor-Commissioner’s term to a maximum of two four-year terms, effective November 2028?

Sec. 6.1. Mayor-Commissioner and Commissioners; term of office; election; transition
(1) Beginning with the regular election to be held on the second Tuesday in the month of March, 2008, and every two (2) years thereafter, a Mayor-Commissioner shall be elected for a term of two (2) years until his successor is elected and qualifies. Beginning with the regular election to be held in November 2028, and in every regular election thereafter, a Mayor-Commissioner shall be elected for a term of four (4) years and shall serve until a successor is elected and qualifies. For the purpose of calculating eligibility under this section, any person who has served as Mayor-Commissioner immediately prior to November 2028 shall be deemed to have served one term under this section.
Sec. 6.3. - Qualifications of members of Town Commission
(3) No person may be a candidate for Mayor-Commissioner, nor may be appointed to fill a vacancy as the Mayor-Commissioner, if that person has served during each of the two preceding consecutive Mayor-Commissioner terms as a Mayor-Commissioner, without a two year break in service.
(6) No person may be a candidate for Mayor-Commissioner or Town Commissioner, nor may be appointed to fill a vacancy as the Mayor-Commissioner or Town Commissioner, if that person has consecutively served sixteen (16) years in any combination of offices without a two year break in service.

What would change?
The amendment would change the Mayor-Commissioner's term from two years to four years and establish a maximum of two four-year terms.
The change would take effect beginning with the regular election in November 2028. The amendment also establishes how the term ending in November 2028 will be treated for purposes of calculating eligibility under the new term limits.
