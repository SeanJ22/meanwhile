      22
      23            WHEREAS, the Board of County Commissioners desires to enact an ordinance amending
      24    Article I, Section 1.6. of the Home Rule Charter of Leon County, Florida, relating to Relation to
      25    Municipal Ordinances., by adding Article I, Section 1.6.(3) of the Home Rule Charter of Leon County
      26    Florida, relating to Uniform Countywide Street Naming, Renaming, and Property Numbering
      27    Standards; and
      28
      29        BE IT ORDAINED BY THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS OF LEON
      30    COUNTY, FLORIDA, that:
      31
      32    Section 1. Amendment to Charter.
      33
      34           Article I, Section 1.6. of the Home Rule Charter of Leon County, Florida, is hereby amended
      35    by adding a subsection to be numbered Section 1.6.(3) to read as follows:
      36
      37    Sec. 1.6. Relation to municipal ordinances.
      38
      39              (3) Uniform Countywide Street Naming, Renaming, and Property Numbering Standards.
      40                  County ordinances shall establish uniform countywide procedures and requirements to
Docusign Envelope ID: A6329EC0-04E7-8EF6-83B6-6D2EB84D10EE




       1                  ensure the consistent and coordinated implementation of street naming, renaming, and
       2                  property numbering within the unincorporated and incorporated areas of the County. Such
       3                  standards shall include, but shall not be limited to, the addressing of properties, street
       4                  naming, renaming, street signage, public notification, as well as the review and approval
       5                  procedures as the Board of County Commissioners determines necessary to support public
       6                  safety and emergency response needs throughout Leon County. The County’s ordinances
       7                  establishing uniform countywide standards for street naming, renaming, and property
       8                  numbering shall prevail in the event of a conflict with municipal ordinances, to the extent
       9                  otherwise permitted by law.
      10
      11    Section 2. Ballot Question to be Presented to Electorate.
      12
      13            The proposed amendment to the Home Rule Charter of Leon County, Florida, shall be presented
      14    to the qualified Leon County electorate by placing the question of whether to adopt the same on the
      15    ballot at the general election to be held on November 3, 2026.
      16
      17    Section 3. Ballot Question Form.
      18
      19             The question on the ballot shall be substantially in the following form:
      20

      10    Section 6. Effective Date.
