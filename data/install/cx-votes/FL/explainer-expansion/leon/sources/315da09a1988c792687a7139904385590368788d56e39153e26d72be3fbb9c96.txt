       4                     COMMISSIONERS OF LEON COUNTY, FLORIDA, PLACING
       5                     A NONBINDING, COUNTYWIDE ADVISORY REFERENDUM
       6                     ON POTENTIAL CITY–COUNTY CONSOLIDATION ON THE
       7                     NOVEMBER 3, 2026 GENERAL ELECTION BALLOT;
       8                     PROVIDING BALLOT TITLE AND BALLOT SUMMARY

      10                     SUPERVISOR OF ELECTIONS; AND PROVIDING AN
      11                     EFFECTIVE DATE.

      13                                                     RECITALS
      14
      15           WHEREAS, Leon County is a charter county and political subdivision of the State of Florida,
      16    operating under Article VIII of the Florida Constitution and the Leon County Home Rule Charter; and
      17
      18           WHEREAS, Section 125.01(1), Florida Statutes, provides that the Board of County
      19    Commissioners “shall have the power to carry on county government,” subject only to limitations in
      20    general or special law; and
      21
      22            WHEREAS, Section 125.01(1)(y), Florida Statutes, authorizes the Board to “place questions
      23    or propositions on the ballot at any primary election, general election, or otherwise called special
      24    election” to obtain an expression of voter sentiment on matters of substantial concern within the county,
      25    and further provides that no special election may be called for the purpose of conducting a straw ballot;
      26    and
      27
      28           WHEREAS, potential consolidation of the governments of Leon County and the City of
      29    Tallahassee is a matter of substantial concern to the citizens of Leon County within the meaning of
      30    Section 125.01(1)(y), Florida Statutes; and
      31
      32           WHEREAS, at its November 18, 2025 meeting, the Board directed staff to provide an analysis
      33    of county-city consolidation in Florida, including the legal process and additional information for the
      34    Board’s consideration regarding potential consolidation of Leon County and the City of Tallahassee;
      35    and
      36
      37            WHEREAS, at its December 9, 2025 meeting, the Board received a comprehensive status
      38    report on county-city consolidation that reviewed the national and Florida experience, recounted the
      39    local history of four prior consolidation referenda (1971, 1973, 1976, and 1992), and outlined the legal
      40    and procedural framework for consolidation under Article VIII, Section 3 of the Florida Constitution;
      41    and

       1           WHEREAS, on December 9, 2025, the Board directed staff to prepare ballot language for
       2    Board consideration to proceed with a non-binding referendum on the November 3, 2026 General
       3    Election Ballot, to gauge public interest in the possibility of consolidation; and
       4
       5            WHEREAS, Section 106.113, Florida Statutes, imposes specific limits on how local
       6    governments may use public funds to communicate about ballot issues, while expressly preserving the
       7    ability of local governments to report on official actions, post factual information on government
       8    websites and in printed materials, host and provide information at public forums, provide factual
       9    information in response to inquiries, and provide information as otherwise authorized or required by
      10    law; and
      11
      12           WHEREAS, the proposed referendum would be advisory and nonbinding under Section
      13    125.01(1)(y), Florida Statutes, and would not, by itself, create a consolidated government, amend the
      14    Leon County Home Rule Charter, or change any existing powers, responsibilities, or revenue structures
      15    of Leon County or the City of Tallahassee; and
      16
      17            WHEREAS, Article VIII, Section 3 of the Florida Constitution requires that any consolidation
      18    of a county and one or more municipalities into a single government be set forth in a special act of the
      19    Legislature and approved by a vote of the voters; and
      20
      21           WHEREAS, the Board desires to place a nonbinding, countywide advisory question on the
      22    November 3, 2026 General Election ballot to gauge voter sentiment on whether the County should
      23    pursue development of a proposed plan for potential consolidation of Leon County and City of
      24    Tallahassee governments, and to direct that the ballot title and ballot summary set forth herein be
      25    transmitted to the Leon County Supervisor of Elections.
      26
      27        NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY                               THE    BOARD       OF    COUNTY
      28    COMMISSIONERS OF LEON COUNTY, FLORIDA, that:
      29
      30    Section 1. Recitals Adopted.
      31
      32    The foregoing recitals are hereby adopted and incorporated herein by reference as if fully set forth in
      33    this Resolution.
      34
      35    Section 2. Nonbinding Advisory Referendum Authorized.
      36
      37    Pursuant to Section 125.01(1)(y), Florida Statutes, the Board hereby authorizes the placement of a
      38    nonbinding, countywide advisory question on the November 3, 2026 General Election Ballot to obtain
      39    an expression of voter sentiment regarding potential consolidation of the governments of Leon County
      40    and the City of Tallahassee.

       2    Section 3. Ballot Title and Ballot Summary.
       3
       4    The following ballot title and ballot summary are hereby approved for placement on the November 3,
       5    2026, General Election ballot, in compliance with the requirements of Section 101.161, Florida
       6    Statutes:
       7
       8             Ballot Title:
       9             Advisory Referendum to Pursue County-City Consolidation Plan Under Leon County
      10             Government Structure and Leadership
      11
      12             Ballot Summary:
      13             Shall Leon County pursue development of a plan to consolidate the governments of Leon
      14             County and the City of Tallahassee into a single consolidated government, under the Leon
      15             County government structure and leadership, through the constitutional process requiring a
      16             special act of the Florida Legislature and voter approval? This nonbinding referendum only
      17             gauges voter sentiment and does not create a consolidated government.

       1    Section 4. Advisory Nature.
       2
       3    The referendum authorized by this Resolution is nonbinding and advisory only under Section
       4    125.01(1)(y), Florida Statutes. The results of the referendum shall not, by themselves, create a
       5    consolidated government, amend the Leon County Home Rule Charter, or change any existing powers,
       6    responsibilities, or revenue structures of Leon County or the City of Tallahassee. The Board retains
       7    full discretion to determine whether and how to proceed following the referendum, including whether
       8    to pursue special legislation under Article VIII, Section 3 of the Florida Constitution.
       9
      10    Section 5. Transmittal to Supervisor of Elections.
      11
      12    The County Administrator, or designee, is hereby directed to transmit a certified copy of this
      13    Resolution, including the ballot title and ballot summary set forth in Section 3, which shall also be
      14    translated into Spanish, to the Leon County Supervisor of Elections within the deadlines established by
      15    the Supervisor of Elections and the Florida Election Code for ballot layout, coding, and logic and
      16    accuracy testing, for placement of the advisory question on the November 3, 2026, General Election
      17    ballot.
      18
      19    Section 6. Notice.
      20
      21    The County Administrator, or designee, in coordination with the Supervisor of Elections, shall ensure
      22    that notice of the referendum is provided in accordance with Section 100.342, Florida Statutes,
      23    including publication at least twice, once in the fifth week and once in the third week before the week
      24    of the election.
      25
      26    Section 7. Public Information.
      27
      28    The County Administrator, or designee, is authorized to develop and implement a neutral public
      29    information effort regarding the advisory referendum, consistent with the requirements and limitations
      30    of Section 106.113, Florida Statutes, and in consultation with the County Attorney’s Office.
      31
      32    Section 8. Severability.
      33
      34    If any provision of this Resolution or the application thereof to any person or circumstance is held
      35    invalid, the invalidity shall not affect other provisions or applications of this Resolution which can be
      36    given effect without the invalid provision or application, and to this end the provisions of this
      37    Resolution are declared severable.
      38
      39    Section 9. Effective Date.
      40
      41    This resolution shall become effective immediately upon its adoption.
