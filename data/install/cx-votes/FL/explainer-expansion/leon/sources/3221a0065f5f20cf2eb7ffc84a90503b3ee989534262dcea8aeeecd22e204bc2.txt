Advisory Referendum to Pursue County-City
Consolidation Plan Under Leon County
Government Structure and Leadership
Shall Leon County pursue development of a plan to
consolidate the governments of Leon County and the
City of Tallahassee into a single consolidated govern­
ment, under the Leon County government structure
and leadership, through the constitutional process re­
quiring a special act of the Florida Legislature and voter
approval? This nonbinding referendum only gauges
voter sentiment and does not create a consolidated
government.
    Yes = For the advisory proposal
   No = Against the advisory proposal
