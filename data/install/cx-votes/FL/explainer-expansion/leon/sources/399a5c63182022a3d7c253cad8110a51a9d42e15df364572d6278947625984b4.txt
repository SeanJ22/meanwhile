      18
      19            WHEREAS, the Board of County Commissioners desires to enact an ordinance amending
      20    Article I, Section 1.6. of the Home Rule Charter of Leon County, Florida, relating to Relation to
      21    Municipal Ordinances.
      22
      23        BE IT ORDAINED BY THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS OF LEON
      24    COUNTY, FLORIDA, that:
      25
      26    Section 1. Amendment to Charter.
      27
      28            Article I, Section 1.6. of the Home Rule Charter of Leon County, Florida, is hereby amended
      29    to read as follows:
      30
      31    Sec. 1.6. Relation to municipal ordinances.
      32
      33             (1)   Except as otherwise provided by law or this Charter, County ordinances adopted after
      34                   January 1, 2027, shall prevail over municipal ordinances shall prevail over County
      35                   ordinances to the extentin the event of any conflict within the boundaries of the
      36                   municipality. To the extent that a county ordinance and a municipal ordinance shall cover
      37                   the same subject without conflict, then both the municipal ordinance and the county
      38                   ordinance shall be effective, each being deemed supplemental to the other.
      39

       1             (2)   Minimum Environmental Regulations. County ordinances shall establish minimum
       2                   standards, procedures, requirements and regulations for the protection of the environment
       3                   and shall be effective within the unincorporated and incorporated areas of the County.
       4                   Such standards, procedures, requirements and regulations shall include, but shall not be
       5                   limited to, tree protection, landscaping, aquifer protection, stormwater, protection of
       6                   conservation and preservation features, and such other environmental standards as the
       7                   Board of County Commissioners determines to be necessary for the protection of the
       8                   public health, safety, and welfare of the citizens throughout Leon County. Standards shall
       9                   be designed to place emphasis on supporting healthy natural systems occurring in the
      10                   environment. However, nothing contained herein shall prohibit a municipality from
      11                   adopting ordinances, standards, procedures, requirements or regulations establishing a
      12                   more stringent level of environmental protection within the incorporated area of the
      13                   County.
      14
      15    Section 2. Ballot Question to be Presented to Electorate.
      16
      17            The proposed amendment to the Home Rule Charter of Leon County, Florida, shall be presented
      18    to the qualified Leon County electorate by placing the question of whether to adopt the same on the
      19    ballot at the general election to be held on November 3, 2026.
      20
      21    Section 3. Ballot Question Form.
      22
      23             The question on the ballot shall be substantially in the following form:
      24
      25                        COUNTY ORDINANCES TO PREVAIL OVER MUNICIPAL
      26                       ORDINANCES AS PROPOSED BY THE BOARD OF COUNTY
      27                                       COMMISSIONERS
      28
      29                                                      Question
      30
      31                     Shall the Leon County Charter be amended to provide that County
      32                     ordinances adopted after January 1, 2027, shall prevail over conflicting
      33                     municipal ordinances in the event of conflict, to the extent permitted by
      34                     law, so that the County may establish regulations and standards on
      35                     matters affecting residents in both incorporated and unincorporated
      36                     areas?

       1    Section 4. Further Authorization.
       2
       3           The Board of County Commissioners of Leon County, Florida, is authorized to adopt all
       4    ordinances and take all actions necessary in order for this Charter Amendment referendum to be
       5    properly placed on the ballot for the general election of November 3, 2026. Said referendum shall be
       6    conducted according to the requirements of law governing referendum elections in the State of Florida.
       7
       8    Section 5. Severability.
       9
      10           If any word, phrase, clause, section, or portion of this Ordinance is declared by any court of
      11    competent jurisdiction to be void, unconstitutional, or unenforceable, then all remaining provisions and
      12    portions of this Ordinance shall remain in full force and effect.
      13
      14    Section 6. Effective Date.
      15
      16           This Ordinance shall take effect upon being filed with the Florida Department of State, but shall
      17    be of no further force or effect if the proposed Charter Amendment is not duly approved at the
      18    November 3, 2026 General Election. The amendment to the Home Rule Charter of Leon County,
      19    Florida, as proposed by this Ordinance, shall become effective January 1, 2027, if the Charter
      20    Amendment is approved by a “yes” vote by a majority of those duly qualified electors voting on the
      21    question posed at the November 3, 2026, referendum.
