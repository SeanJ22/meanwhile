County Ordinances to Prevail Over Municipal
Ordinances as Proposed by the Board of County
Commissioners
Shall the Leon County Charter be amended to provide
that County ordinances adopted after January 1, 2027,
shall prevail over conflicting municipal ordinances in the
event of conflict, to the extent permitted by law, so that
the County may establish regulations and standards
on matters affecting residents in both incorporated and
unincorporated areas?

   No for Rejection
