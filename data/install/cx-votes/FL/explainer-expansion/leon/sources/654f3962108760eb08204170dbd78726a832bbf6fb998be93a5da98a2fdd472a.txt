School Board Ad Valorem Levy to Improve
Teacher Compensation, School Safety and School
Enrichment Programs
To increase compensation  for teachers and  staff,
enhance school security operations, and fund essential
school programs including arts education and athlet­
ics, shall the Leon County School Board authorize
an annual ad valorem operating millage of one mill
annually from July 1, 2027 through June 30, 2031, with
revenues collected to be shared with eligible charter
schools pursuant to Florida law, and with expenditures
reviewed by a citizen's advisory committee?
    Yes = For the 1 mill levy
   No = Against the 1 mill levy
