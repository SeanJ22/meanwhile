  Voter Registration
  If you were registered to vote in Florida by the October 5 voter registration deadline, you are
  eligible to vote in the General Election. All Leon County voters can vote in the General Election.
  Your party affiliation does not affect your ballot in the General Election — you can vote for any
  candidate on your ballot regardless of party.
  You can verify your voter information using any of these options:
   • Visit LeonVotes.gov/YourVoterInfo and enter your information. Here you can view your
     voter info, see your specific sample ballot, locate your Election Day polling place, check
     Early Voting schedules and locations, and even request a Vote-by-Mail ballot!
   • Check your Voter Information Card. Your polling place address and party affiliation are
     listed on the card, along with other important information.
   • Call our office at (850) 606-8683 and we will be happy to confirm your information.

  Votaciones en Español
  Si necesita ayuda en español, habrán recursos disponibles en su centro de votación y en nuestro
  sitio web: LeonVotes.gov. En nuestro sitio web encontrarás información sobre el proceso electoral
  en el condado León, instrucciones sobre como votar-por-correo, la boleta oficial en español
  y como registrarse para votar o actualizar su información. Si necesita ayuda en español por
  teléfono, llame a nuestra oficina para hablar con un miembro del personal que puede ayudarle.

  Accessible Voting
  We are committed to aiding voters with disabilities and ensuring that everyone can exercise their
  right to vote. If you need assistance when voting, please inform the clerk at the voting site and
  they will be happy to help. All Early Voting sites and Election Day polling places are accessible
  and supplied with equipment that helps voters with disabilities cast their ballots. We also have
  an accessible Vote-by-Mail option for voters with disabilities. Contact us to learn more.

RETURNING YOUR BALLOT
Follow the instructions enclosed with your ballot. We must receive your voted mail ballot by 7 p.m. on Election Day,

ballot by mail or Secure Ballot Intake Station. Note that you CANNOT return Vote-by-Mail ballots to Election Day polling
places. You can instead vote in-person or drop your voted ballot off at our office by 7 p.m. on Election Day.

                   Election Day
Tuesday, November 3 is Election Day – your last chance to vote!
If you vote on Election Day, you must cast your ballot at your assigned
polling place. Your polling place is based on your precinct (where you
live). The name and address of your Election Day polling place are
printed on the cover of this election guide. You can also find your
polling place on your Voter Information Card, or online at LeonVotes.gov.
For assistance, please call us at (850) 606-8683. You may have a new
polling place since the last time you voted, so please check before you
head to the polls!
Your polling place is open 7 a.m. to 7 p.m. on November 3, Election Day.
All voters waiting in line at 7 p.m. will have the opportunity to cast a ballot.

eligibility cannot be verified at the polling site. It allows us time to research the voter’s eligibility and count the
ballot if the voter is eligible. Some Provisional Ballot voters may need to submit additional information for their
ballot to be counted. The deadline to submit this is 5 p.m. on Thursday, November 5.
Voters may track their Provisional Ballot status at LeonVotes.gov or by contacting our office at (850) 606-8683.

                          A General Election Ballot Full of Possibilities
                          We have a very lengthy General Election ballot this year. You will be picking a new governor and
                          cabinet, as well as making decisions on federal, state, and many local races. In addition, there are
                          11 ballot questions that will chart the course for our state and local community over the coming
                          years. I strongly recommend that you carefully read your sample ballot today and start thinking
                          about the choices you want to make on your actual ballot. Do not wait until you are standing in
                          a voting booth to consider your options! By planning now, you can ensure that your votes will go
                          to the candidates and causes that reflect your values and intentions.
                           Many voters have shared with me their concerns about the security and integrity of our elections,
       especially this year. As your Supervisor of Elections, I will do everything in my power to ensure that our elections
       will be free of disruption. Leon County will continue to be at the forefront of accessible, transparent, and accurate
       elections, always adding to our legacy of outstanding voter services and championing the will of the voter.
       To that end, I encourage you to use the power of your vote. Our system of government relies on having informed
       and engaged voters. If you do not use your vote, then you are silencing yourself from the great debate that is our
       democracy. Make your voice heard by voting.
       To best prepare yourself for the 2026 General Election, I encourage you to thoroughly read this election guide and
       plan on how you are going to cast your ballot. This guide includes Early Voting locations and times, information on
       how you can request a Vote-by-Mail ballot, and where you can vote on Election Day. It also includes a generic sample
       ballot, which you can use to start your research on the candidates and issues that you will see on your actual ballot.
       By doing this, you will be Election Ready for the General Election.
       Your country depends on your vote. Please speak up, vote, and be heard!
