      20          WHEREAS, the Board of County Commissioners desires to enact an ordinance amending the

      26    Section 1. Amendment to Charter.
      27
      28            The Preamble of the Home Rule Charter of Leon County, Florida, is hereby amended to read
      29    as follows:
      30
      31    SECTION 1. HOME RULE CHARTER
      32
      33             The proposed Home Rule Charter of Leon County, Florida, shall read as follows: PREAMBLE
      34
      35            We, the citizens of Leon County, Florida, united in the belief that governmental decisions
      36    affecting local interests should be made locally, rather than by the State, and that County government
      37    should be reflective of the people of the County and should serve them in achieving a more responsive
      38    and efficient form of government, hereby affirm the esteemed humanity of all residents of Leon County.
      39    We recognize in every person a shared dignity, not granted by government, but inherent and deserving
      40    of consideration in our public affairs. and iIn order to empower the people of this County to make

       2    Florida.

       4    Section 2. Ballot Question to be Presented to Electorate.
       5
       6            The proposed amendment to the Home Rule Charter of Leon County, Florida, shall be presented
       7    to the qualified Leon County electorate by placing the question of whether to adopt the same on the
       8    ballot at the general election to be held on November 3, 2026.
       9
      10    Section 3. Ballot Question Form.

      29    Section 4. Further Authorization.

      31           The Board of County Commissioners of Leon County, Florida, is authorized to adopt all
      32    ordinances and take all actions necessary in order for this Charter Amendment referendum to be
      33    properly placed on the ballot for the general election of November 3, 2026. Said referendum shall be

      36    Section 5. Severability.
