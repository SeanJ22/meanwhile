Shall the Leon County Charter be amended to provide for uniform countywide street naming, renaming, and property numbering standards to be established by county ordinance, which would prevail in the event of a conflict with municipal ordinances, as permitted by law, to support emergency response needs throughout the County?

(3) Uniform Countywide Street Naming, Renaming, and Property Numbering Standards. County ordinances shall establish uniform countywide procedures and requirements to ensure the consistent and coordinated implementation of street naming, renaming, and property numbering within the unincorporated and incorporated areas of the County. Such standards shall include, but shall not be limited to, the addressing of properties, street naming, renaming, street signage, public notification, as well as the review and approval procedures as the Board of County Commissioners determines necessary to support public safety and emergency response needs throughout Leon County. The County’s ordinances establishing uniform countywide standards for street naming, renaming, and property numbering shall prevail in the event of a conflict with municipal ordinances, to the extent otherwise permitted by law. 

The amendment to the Home Rule Charter of Leon County, Florida, as proposed by this Ordinance, shall become effective January 1, 2027, if the Charter Amendment is approved by a “yes” vote by a majority of those duly qualified electors voting on the question posed at the November 3, 2026, referendum.
