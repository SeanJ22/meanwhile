School District Referendum Continuing Sales
Surtax to Fund School Safety, Technology,
Transportation and Facilities Improvements
To provide resources to improve safety and security
and maintain  high-quality education by expanding,
constructing, and improving school  facilities,  tech­
nology and  transportation, with collected revenues
shared with eligible charter schools based on their
proportionate share of total school district enrollment,
shall the school district of Leon County continue to
levy a one-half cent sales surtax for fifteen years to
fund such activities for such schools, with review by
a School Board appointed citizen advisory committee?
    Yes = For the 1/2% (0.5 cents) tax
   No = Against the 1/2% (0.5 cents) tax
