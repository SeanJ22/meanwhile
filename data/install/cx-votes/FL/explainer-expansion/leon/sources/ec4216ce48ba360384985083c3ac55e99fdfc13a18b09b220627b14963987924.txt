WHEREAS, the proposed referendum would be advisory and nonbinding under Section 125.01(1)(y), Florida Statutes, and would not, by itself, create a consolidated government, amend the Leon County Home Rule Charter, or change any existing powers, responsibilities, or revenue structures of Leon County or the City of Tallahassee; and WHEREAS, Article VIII, Section 3 of the Florida Constitution requires that any consolidation of a county and one or more municipalities into a single government be set forth in a special act of the Legislature and approved by a vote of the voters; and 

Section 4. Advisory Nature. The referendum authorized by this Resolution is nonbinding and advisory only under Section 125.01(1)(y), Florida Statutes. The results of the referendum shall not, by themselves, create a consolidated government, amend the Leon County Home Rule Charter, or change any existing powers, responsibilities, or revenue structures of Leon County or the City of Tallahassee. The Board retains full discretion to determine whether and how to proceed following the referendum, including whether to pursue special legislation under Article VIII, Section 3 of the Florida Constitution.
