      20            WHEREAS, the Board of County Commissioners desires to enact an ordinance amending
      21     Article V, Subsection 5.2.(2) of the Home Rule Charter of Leon County, Florida, relating to
      22     Amendments and Revisions by Citizen Charter Review Committee; and
      23
      24         BE IT ORDAINED BY THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS OF LEON
      25     COUNTY, FLORIDA, that:
      26
      27     Section 1. Amendment to Charter.
      28
      29           Article V, Subsection 5.2.(2) of the Home Rule Charter of Leon County, Florida, is hereby
      30     amended to read as follows:
      31
      32     (2)     Amendments and Revisions by Citizen Charter Review Committee.
      33
      34             (A) A Citizen Charter Review Committee shall be appointed by the Board of County
      35                 Commissioners at least twelve (12) months before the general election occurring every
      36                 eight (8) years thereafter, to be composed and organized in a manner to be determined by
      37                 the Board of County Commissioners, to review the Home Rule Charter and propose any
      38                 amendments or revisions which may be advisable for placement on the general election
      39                 ballot. Three Ppublic hearings shall be conducted by a Citizen Charter Review Committee
      40                 prior to delivery of the final report and recommended amendments to the Home Rule
      41                 Charter to the Board of County Commissioners. as provided by F.S. § 125.63.

       1             (B) No later than one hundred fifty (150)ninety (90) days prior to the general election, the
       2                 Citizen Charter Review Committee shall deliver to the Board of County Commissioners
       3                 the proposed amendments or revisions, if any, to the Home Rule Charter, and the Board
       4                 of County Commissioners shall consider such amendments or revisions to be placed on
       5                 the general election ballot, in accordance with F.S. § 125.64. Notice of the referendum
       6                 on the proposed charter amendments shall be published in a newspaper of general
       7                 circulation in the County not less than thirty (30) days nor more than forty-five (45) days
       8                 before the election.
       9
      10             (C) If the Citizen Charter Review Committee does not submit any proposed Charter
      11                 amendments or revisions to the Board of County Commissioners at least one hundred fifty
      12                 (150)ninety (90) days prior to the general election, the Citizen Charter Review Committee
      13                 shall be automatically dissolved.
      14
      15     Section 2. Ballot Question to be Presented to Electorate.
      16
      17             The proposed amendment to the Home Rule Charter of Leon County, Florida, shall be presented
      18     to the qualified Leon County electorate by placing the question of whether to adopt the same on the
      19     ballot at the general election to be held on November 3, 2026.
      20
      21     Section 3. Ballot Question Form.
      22
      23             The question on the ballot shall be substantially in the following form:

      28

      32                      references related to the establishment of a Charter, specify public
      33                      hearing and notice requirements to consider amending the Charter, and
      34                      require the Citizen Charter Review Committee to submit its

       1     Section 4. Further Authorization.
       2
       3            The Board of County Commissioners of Leon County, Florida, is authorized to adopt all
       4     ordinances and take all actions necessary in order for this Charter Amendment referendum to be
       5     properly placed on the ballot for the general election of November 3, 2026. Said referendum shall be
       6     conducted according to the requirements of law governing referendum elections in the State of Florida.
       7
       8     Section 5. Severability.
       9
      10            If any word, phrase, clause, section, or portion of this Ordinance is declared by any court of
      11     competent jurisdiction to be void, unconstitutional, or unenforceable, then all remaining provisions and
      12     portions of this Ordinance shall remain in full force and effect.
      13
      14     Section 6. Effective Date.
      15
      16            This Ordinance shall take effect upon being filed with the Florida Department of State, but shall
      17     be of no further force or effect if the proposed Charter Amendment is not duly approved at the
      18     November 3, 2026 General Election. The amendment to the Home Rule Charter of Leon County,
      19     Florida, as proposed by this Ordinance, shall become effective January 1, 2027, if the Charter
      20     Amendment is approved by a “yes” vote by a majority of those duly qualified electors voting on the
      21     question posed at the November 3, 2026 referendum.
