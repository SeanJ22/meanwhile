                                          Requiring A Supermajority Vote
                                            Relating to Village-Owned Property

                                                   This Charter amendment would require a
                                                    supermajority Council vote for the Village to
                                             convey or purchase fee simple title to real
                                                      property. The amendment also requires a
                                                    supermajority vote to amend the Future Land
                                        Use Map designation or zoning of certain
                                                 Village-owned property, or the use of
                                                 Village-owned property used as a park to
                                             uses not permitted in the Recreation orAMConservation districts.
