Charter Amendments

Three-Year Staggered Terms for
Councilmembers With Limit of Three
Terms


The Village Charter provides for two-year
terms for Council, with an eight-year term
limit.

It is proposed to amend the Village Charter to
establish three-year staggered Council terms
and a three-term limit, with an initial transition
in November 2026 where the three
candidates receiving the most votes or
running unopposed receive three-year terms
and the remaining candidates receive
two-year terms.

amendment be adopted?
