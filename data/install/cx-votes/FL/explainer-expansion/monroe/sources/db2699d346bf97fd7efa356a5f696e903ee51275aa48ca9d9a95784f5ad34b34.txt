                                       Procedure for Filling Councilmember
                                        Vacancies Exceeding Half a Term

                                          The Village Charter provides that if a
                                              councilmember vacancy occurs and 50
                                                  percent or more of the term remains the
                                                 remaining councilmembers must appoint a
                                                replacement by majority vote.

                                                         In this scenario, the proposed amendment
                                                     requires the remaining Councilmembers to
                                                   appoint an interim councilmember within 30
                                             days and call a special election within 6
                                            months to fill the vacancy for the remainder of
                                                   the unexpired term.

                                         amendment be adopted?
