The City Charter currently provides for the potential sanction of forfeiture of City elective office for the unexcused failure to attend four (4) consecutive regular Commission meetings. It is proposed to amend the Charter to apply the same attendance standards and sanctions for required attendance of City elected officials at Workshop Commission meetings. Shall the above-described Charter amendment be adopted?

 A City Commissioner Member shall forfeit his or her office if he or she:

(1) lacks at any time during his or her term of office any of the qualifications for the office
prescribed by this Charter or by law.,

2) fails to attend four (4) consecutive regular or workshop meetings of the eCity eCommission
without being excused by the City Commission by formal action entered upon the

minutes., or

3) is convicted in a court of competent jurisdiction of a felony or other crime involving moral
turpitude.

(c) Standards. The City Commission shall by ordinance provide standards and procedures for
determining whether absences from Commission meetings shall be excused by the City
Commission. The same standards and penalties shall be applied to absences from regular
and workshop meetings of the City Commission.



That the Charter Amendments provided for in Section 2 above shall become effective if the majority of the qualified electors voting on the specific Charter Amendment vote for its adoption, and it shall be considered adopted and effective upon certification of the election results.
