Emergencies. To meet a public emergency affecting life, health, property or the public

peace, the Getneit Commission may adopt one or more emergency ordinances, but
such ordinances may not levy taxes, grant, renew or extend a franchise, regulate the

rate charged by any utility for its services, or authorize the borrowing of money.

Form. An emergency ordinance shall be introduced in the form prescribed for
ordinances generally, except that it need not be pre-published and that it shall be
plainly designated as an emergency ordinance and shall contain, after the enacting
clause, a declaration stating that an emergency exists and describing it in clear and

specific terms.

Process. An emergency ordinance may be adopted with or without amendment or
rejected at the meeting at which it is introduced; but the affirmative vote of at least
four (4) members of the Geureit Commission shall be required for adoption. After its
adoption, the ordinance shall be published and printed as prescribed for other

adopted ordinances.

Effective Date. Emergency ordinances shall become effective upon the adoption or at
such later date as may be specified in the ordinance.
