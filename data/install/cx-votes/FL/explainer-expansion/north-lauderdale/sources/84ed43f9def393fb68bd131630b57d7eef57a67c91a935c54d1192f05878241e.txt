AN ORDINANCE OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF NORTH LAUDERDALE, FLORIDA, 
PROVIDING FOR THE SUBMISSION TO THE ELECTORS OF THE CITY FOR APPROVAL OR REJECTION 
A SERIES OF PROPOSED AMENDMENTS TO THE CITY CHARTER WHICH RESULTED FROM THE CITY 
COMMISSION’S REVIEW OF THE RECOMMENDATIONS OF THE CHARTER REVIEW COMMISSION, 
IN ACCORDANCE WITH SECTION 9.9 OF THE CITY CHARTER; PROVIDING REQUISITE LANGUAGE 
FOR THE SUBMISSION OF FIVE (5) BALLOT MEASURES TO THE ELECTORATE; CALLING A SPECIAL 
ELECTION ON THE PROPOSED AMENDMENTS TO THE CITY CHARTER TO BE HELD ON TUESDAY, 
THE 3RD DAY OF NOVEMBER, 2026; PROVIDING NOTICE OF ELECTION; PROVIDING FOR 
INCLUSION IN THE CHARTER; PROVIDING FOR SEVERABILITY; AND PROVIDING FOR AN 
EFFECTIVE DATE. 
 
Commissioner Dr. Martin moved to approve the second and final reading of the proposed Charter
