City of Palm Coast Charter Amendment 3
AMENDING THE CITY CHARTER TO INCREASE CITY COUNCIL’S UNFUNDED
CONTRACTING AUTHORITY TO $30,000,000.00.
Shall Art. VI (3) (e) of the City Charter be amended to increase the Council’s unfunded contracting authority from
$15,000,000.00 to $30,000,000.00 and extend the term from 36 months to 30 years? Unfunded multi-year or lease-
purchase contracts exceeding these limits shall require voter approval via referendum. The $30,000,000.00 threshold
shall be adjusted annually for inflation (CPI), rounded to the nearest $1,000.00.
o Yes
o No
