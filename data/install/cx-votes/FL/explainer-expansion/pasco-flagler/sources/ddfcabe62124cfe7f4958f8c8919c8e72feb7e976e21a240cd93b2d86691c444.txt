City of Palm Coast Charter Amendment 1
AMENDING ARTICLE IV, SECTION (7) OF CITY CHARTER REGARDING REMOVAL OF
COUNCIL MEMBERS OR MAYOR.
Shall Article IV, Section (7) of the City Charter be amended to expand and clarify standards for forfeitures and
suspension of the Mayor and City Council members by defining ethics violations under Florida law, establishing
additional meeting attendance requirements, and authorizing the City Council, after three formal censures, to petition
the Governor for suspension or removal of a City Council Member for neglect of duty or malfeasance.
