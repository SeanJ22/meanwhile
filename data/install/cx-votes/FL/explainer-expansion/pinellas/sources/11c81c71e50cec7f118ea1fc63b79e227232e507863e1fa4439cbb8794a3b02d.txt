Section 6. This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon its passage and adoption, provided, however, it shall not be effective until such is approved by the electorate at a referendum election thereon to ratify the terms and conditions of this Ordinance. In the event that any of the referendum questions shall be defeated, then this Ordinance shall be of no further force and effect.
