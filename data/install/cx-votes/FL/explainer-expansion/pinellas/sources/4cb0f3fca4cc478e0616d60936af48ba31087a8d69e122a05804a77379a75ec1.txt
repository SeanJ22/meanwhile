May City Council approve amendment of the lease of City-owned submerged lands at Harborage Marina subject to conditions in ordinance 640-H? Such amendment would extend the term of the lease to 30 years and require the tenant to construct new improvements at no cost to the City, including 200 linear feet of docks, boat lifts, and expanded restrooms. Those improvements would be owned by the City at the end of the lease.

SECTION 3—AUTHORIZATION FOR AMENDMENT: If the 2026 Referendum is ap- proved by a majority vote, the City is authorized to amend the Current Lease subject to the following conditions (each, an “Amendment”): (A) Authority to Amend. Authorization to amend the Current Lease in accordance with this section is not limited to a single Amendment, and multiple Amendments may be approved pursuant to this section as long as no aspect of an Amendment is inconsistent with the 2004 Referendum, the 2018 Referendum, or the 2026 Referendum (with any conflict among those referenda resolved in favor of the later referendum). But if the first Amendment ap- proved in accordance with this section (the “Initial Amendment”) is not fully executed on or before December 31, 2027, the authority provided by the 2026 Referendum will expire at the end of that date. (B) Approval Process. A City Council resolution receiving at least six affirmative votes is required to approve each Amendment, (C) Premises. The area subject to the Current Lease must not be expanded beyond what was authorized under the 2018 Referendum. (D) Duration. The maximum duration of the Current Lease must be no more than 30 years from the effective date of the Initial Amendment.

The Current Lease must require the tenant to complete construction of the following improvements at no cost to the City: (1) 200 linear feet of new docks at the location shown on the Improvement Diagram, with fire suppression, water, and electric service. (2) New boat lifts at the location shown in the Improvement Diagram. (3) Expanded restrooms. (F) Ownership. Pursuant to the terms of the Current Lease, each improvement described in subsection (E) becomes property of the City upon expiration or earlier termination of the Current Lease. (G) Nature of Conditions. These conditions are intended to establish certain minimum stand- ards for each Amendment, and they do not prevent an Amendment or the Current Lease from including provisions that provide more detail than these conditions or that concern matters not mentioned in these conditions.
