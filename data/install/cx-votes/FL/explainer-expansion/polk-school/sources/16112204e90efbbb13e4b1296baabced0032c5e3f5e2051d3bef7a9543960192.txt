Notice is hereby given that a referendum election will be held throughout Polk County on November 3, 2026, at the polling place designated within each voting precinct of the County for the purpose of determining the following question: County School Board Referendum / Referéndum Junta Escolar del Condado Millage for School Safety, Teachers, and Career Education Shall the Polk County School District levy an additional 1.0 mill in ad valorem taxes annually to fund school operations for teacher recruitment and retention and education support staff, for career and technical education, school safety, arts, and early learning--with proceeds shared with charter schools as required by law and monitored by an independent citizens' oversight committee? Tasa Impositiva para la Seguridad Escolar, los Maestros y la Educación Profesional ¿Debería el Distrito Escolar del Condado de Polk imponer anualmente una tasa adicional de 1.0 milésima en impuestos ad valorem para financiar las operaciones escolares destinadas a la contratación y retención de maestros y personal de apoyo educativo para la educación profesional y técnica, la seguridad escolar, las artes y la educación temprana, distribuyendo los fondos recaudados con las escuelas chárter según lo exige la ley y bajo la supervisión de un comité ciudadano independiente? _______ Yes / Sí _______ No / No The polls will be open at the voting places on the date of such election from 7:00 a.m. to 7:00 p.m. All qualified voters residing within Polk County may vote in said election. Early voting and absentee voting will be permitted upon compliance with the applicable provisions of general law. By: Justin Sharpless, Board Chair Attest: Frederick R.
