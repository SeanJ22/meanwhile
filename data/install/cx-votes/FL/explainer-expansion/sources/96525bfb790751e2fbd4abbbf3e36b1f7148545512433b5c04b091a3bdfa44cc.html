Municipal Elections

There will be three (3) Town of Fort Myers Beach Town Council seats for the Tuesday, November 3, 2026, General Election.

The Lee County Supervisor of Elections office will conduct the non-partisan election. 

For more information on Candidate Qualifying, call us at (239) LEE-VOTE (533-8683) or email elections@lee.vote

Per Sec. 4.01(c) of the Town Charter:

Each member of the council shall be a resident of the town and a registered voter and shall have resided within the corporate limits of the municipality for a minimum of 1 year prior to qualifying.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

How and when can I announce my candidacy?
  • You may announce at any time HOWEVER, prior to accepting any contributions or spending any funds on the campaign, Forms DS-DE 9 and DS-DE 84 must be completed and properly filed.   The most current version of these forms may be found here: https://dos.fl.gov/elections/forms-publications/forms/
Will I have to file a Form 1?
  • Yes, the Statement of Financial Interests for 2025 (Form 1) is required during qualification.  Form 1 must be filed electronically with the Florida Commission on Ethics.  The Electronic Financial Disclosure Management System may be found here: https://disclosure.floridaethics.gov/Account/Login
