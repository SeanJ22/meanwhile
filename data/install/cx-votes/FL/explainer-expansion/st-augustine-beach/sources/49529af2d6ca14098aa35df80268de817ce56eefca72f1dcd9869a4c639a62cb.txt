Ordinance 2026-05, Final Reading, to Amend the City Charter to Adopt Language Regarding Term Limits for City Commissioners (Presenter: John Steinmetz, City Attorney) City Attorney John Steinmetz presented Ordinance 2026-05 on final reading, which proposes ballot language for an amendment to the City Charter to establish term limits for City Commissioners. He advised that the ordinance reflects the work of the Charter Review Committee, whose final report was submitted in 2024, and that all necessary minor revisions had been completed since the prior reading. He reiterated that adoption of the ordinance would authorize placement of the proposed charter amendment on the ballot for voter consideration, rather than directly impose term limits.

Motion: To approve Ordinance 2026-05. Moved by Commissioner Samora, Seconded by Vice Mayor Morgan. Motion passed unanimously.
