2026 Municipal Election 



/DocumentCenter/View/10251/Campaign-Treasurer-Report-10-17-26-to-10-29-26-G3---D-Arminio_RedactedThe 2026 Municipal Election will be held on Tuesday, November 3, 2026. 

The purpose of the election is to elect a Mayor to a two-year term of office and two (2) Commissioners to a four-year term of office. Candidates for Mayor/Commission shall be domiciled in the City for six (6) months prior to qualifying for office and also be a registered voter.

Commission Seats Up for Election:

Mayor: Scott NewtonOrdinance 2026-010 Referendum Charter Amendment Vacancies, Forfeiture of Office, Filling of Vacancies

Commissioners: Don D'Arminio and Paul Rolli

Candidate Qualifying Period: Noon, Monday, June 8 to Noon, Friday, June 12, 2026.

To access information about the upcoming election, voting precincts, or how to register to vote, please contact the Broward County Supervisor of Elections Office, phone no. 954-357-7050, https://browardvotes.gov

The ballot will also have one (1) referendum with proposed changes to the City Charter. The referendum is as follows: 

FILLING EXTRAORDINARY VACANCIES OF ALL COMMISSIONERS AND THE MAYOR

The Charter currently authorizes the Governor to fill vacancies if all commissioners, including the mayor, are removed from office. Shall the Charter be amended to provide for the City Manager, or Assistant City Manager, to appoint a mayor and two commissioners, for those appointees to appoint two additional members of the commission, and for the interim commission to call a Special Election if there is more than 180 calendar days remaining in the unexpired term(s)?
