No. 1 Hillsborough County Referendum
N.º 1 Referéndum del Condado de Hillsborough
Providing for an Elected, Rather than an Appointed,
Superintendent of Schools
Shall Chapter 2026-196, Laws of Florida, which provides that the
Superintendent of Schools for Hillsborough County Public Schools
shall no longer be appointed by the School Board, but shall be elected
in a partisan election for a term of 4 years, beginning with the 2028
general election, become effective?
