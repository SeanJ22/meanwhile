                                    A BILL TO BE ENTITLED
                                              AN ACT


 1   To amend an Act to provide a homestead exemption from certain Bartow County ad valorem
 2   taxes for county purposes in the amount of $5,000.00 of the assessed value of the homestead
 3   for certain residents of that county, approved March 24, 1994 (Ga. L. 1994, p. 4058), as
 4   amended, particularly by an Act approved May 1, 2023 (Ga. L. 2023, p. 3891), so as to
 5   increase the exemption to $30,000.00; to provide for compliance with constitutional
 6   requirements; to provide for a referendum, effective dates, automatic repeal, mandatory
 7   execution of election, and judicial remedies regarding failure to comply; to provide for
 8   related matters; to repeal conflicting laws; and for other purposes.


 9             BE IT ENACTED BY THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF GEORGIA:


10                                          SECTION 1.
11   An Act to provide a homestead exemption from certain Bartow County ad valorem taxes for
12   county purposes in the amount of $5,000.00 of the assessed value of the homestead for
13   certain residents of that county, approved March 24, 1994 (Ga. L. 1994, p. 4058), as
14   amended, particularly by an Act approved May 1, 2023 (Ga. L. 2023, p. 3891), is amended
15   by revising Section 2 as follows:

16                                           "SECTION 2.
17    Each resident of Bartow County is granted an exemption on that person's homestead from
18    Bartow County ad valorem taxes for county purposes in the amount of $30,000.00 of the
19    assessed value of that homestead. The value of that property in excess of such exempted
20    amount shall remain subject to taxation."


21                                           SECTION 2.
22   In accordance with the requirements of Article VII, Section II of the Constitution of the State
23   of Georgia, this Act shall not become law unless it receives the requisite two-thirds' majority
24   vote in both the Senate and the House of Representatives.


25                                           SECTION 3.
26   The election superintendent of Bartow County shall call and conduct an election as provided
27   in this section for the purpose of submitting this Act to the electors of the Bartow County for
28   approval or rejection. The election superintendent shall conduct that election on the Tuesday
29   following the first Monday in November of 2026 and shall issue the call and conduct that
30   election as provided by general law. The election superintendent shall cause the date and
31   purpose of the election to be published once a week for two weeks immediately preceding
32   the date thereof in the official organ of Bartow County. The ballot shall have written or
33   printed thereon the words:

34    "( ) YES Shall the Act be approved that provides for an amendment to the $15,000.00
35                 homestead exemption from Bartow County ad valorem taxes for county
36        ( ) NO   purposes so as to increase the exemption to $30,000.00?"
37   All persons desiring to vote for approval of the Act shall vote "Yes," and all persons desiring
38   to vote for rejection of the Act shall vote "No." If more than one-half of the votes cast on
39   such question are for approval of the Act, Section 1 of this Act shall become of full force and
40   effect on January 1, 2027. If the Act is not so approved or if the election is not conducted

41   as provided in this section, Section 1 of this Act shall not become effective, and this Act shall
42   be automatically repealed on the 365th calendar day following the election date provided for
43   in this section. The expense of such election shall be borne by Bartow County. It shall be
44   the election superintendent's duty to certify the result thereof to the Secretary of State. The
45   provisions of this section shall be mandatory upon the election superintendent and are not
46   intended as directory. If the election superintendent fails or refuses to comply with this
47   section, any elector of the Bartow County may apply for a writ of mandamus to compel the
48   election superintendent to perform his or her duties under this section. If the court finds that
49   the election superintendent has not complied with this section, the court shall fashion
50   appropriate relief requiring the election superintendent to call and conduct such election on
51   the date required by this section or on the next date authorized for special elections provided
52   for in Code Section 21-2-540 of the O.C.G.A.


53                                            SECTION 4.
54   Except as otherwise provided in Section 3 of this Act, this Act shall become effective upon
55   its approval by the Governor or upon its becoming law without such approval.


56                                            SECTION 5.
57   All laws and parts of laws in conflict with this Act are repealed.
