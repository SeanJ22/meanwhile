                                     A BILL TO BE ENTITLED
                                                AN ACT


 1   To provide a homestead exemption from Bibb County school district ad valorem taxes for
 2   educational purposes in the amount of $17,000.00 of the assessed value of the homestead for
 3   residents of that school district who are between the ages of 65 and 74; to provide a
 4   homestead exemption from Bibb County school district ad valorem taxes for educational
 5   purposes in the amount of $32,000.00 of the assessed value of the homestead for residents
 6   of that school district who are between the ages of 75 and 84; to provide a homestead
 7   exemption from Bibb County school district ad valorem taxes for educational purposes in the
 8   amount of the full assessed value of the homestead for residents of that school district who
 9   are 85 years of age and older; to provide for definitions; to specify the terms and conditions
10   of the exemption and the procedures relating thereto; to provide for applicability; to provide
11   for compliance with constitutional requirements; to provide for a referendum, effective dates,
12   automatic repeal, mandatory execution of election, and judicial remedies regarding failure
13   to comply; to provide for related matters; to repeal conflicting laws; and for other purposes.


14             BE IT ENACTED BY THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF GEORGIA:


15                                           SECTION 1.
16   (a) As used in this Act, the term:

17        (1) "Ad valorem taxes for educational purposes" means all ad valorem taxes for
18        educational purposes levied by, for, or on behalf of the Bibb County school district,
19        including, but not limited to, any ad valorem taxes to pay interest on and to retire county
20        school district bonded indebtedness.
21        (2) "Homestead" means homestead as defined and qualified in Code Section 48-5-40 of
22        the O.C.G.A., as amended.
23    (b)(1) Each resident of the Bibb County school district who is between the ages of 65 and
24    74 is granted an exemption on such person's homestead from Bibb County school district
25    ad valorem taxes for educational purposes in the amount of $17,000.00 of the assessed
26    value of such homestead. The value of that property in excess of such exempted amount
27    shall remain subject to taxation.
28    (2) Each resident of the Bibb County school district who is between the ages of 75 and
29    84 is granted an exemption on such person's homestead from Bibb County school district
30    ad valorem taxes for educational purposes in the amount of $32,000.00 of the assessed
31    value of such homestead. The value of that property in excess of such exempted amount
32    shall remain subject to taxation.
33    (3) Each resident of the Bibb County school district who is 85 years of age or older is
34    granted an exemption on such person's homestead from Bibb County school district ad
35    valorem taxes for educational purposes in the amount of the full assessed value of such
36    homestead.
37   (c) A person shall not receive the homestead exemption granted by subsection (b) of this
38   section unless such person or person's agent files an application with the tax commissioner
39   of Bibb County, giving such person's age and such additional information relative to
40   receiving such exemption as will enable the tax commissioner of Bibb County to make a
41   determination regarding the initial and continuing eligibility of such person for such
42   exemption. The tax commissioner of Bibb County shall provide application forms for this
43   purpose.

44   (d) The exemption shall be claimed and returned as provided in Code Section 48-5-50.1 of
45   the O.C.G.A., as amended. The exemption shall be automatically renewed from year to year
46   so long as the person granted the homestead exemption under subsection (b) of this section
47   occupies the residence as a homestead. After such person has filed the proper application as
48   provided in subsection (c) of this section, it shall not be necessary to make application
49   thereafter for any year, and such exemption shall continue to be allowed to such person. It
50   shall be the duty of any such person granted the homestead exemption under subsection (b)
51   of this section to notify the tax commissioner of Bibb County in the event that such person
52   for any reason becomes ineligible for such exemption.
53   (e) The exemption granted by subsection (b) of this section shall not apply to or affect any
54   state ad valorem taxes, county ad valorem taxes for county purposes, municipal ad valorem
55   taxes for municipal purposes, or independent school district ad valorem taxes for educational
56   purposes. The homestead exemption granted by subsection (b) of this section shall be in lieu
57   of and not in addition to any other homestead exemption applicable to Bibb County school
58   district ad valorem taxes for educational purposes.
59   (f) The exemption granted by subsection (b) of this section shall apply to all taxable years
60   beginning on or after January 1, 2027.


61                                            SECTION 2.
62   In accordance with the requirements of Article VII, Section II of the Constitution of the State
63   of Georgia, this Act shall not become law unless it receives the requisite two-thirds' majority
64   vote in both the Senate and the House of Representatives.


65                                            SECTION 3.
66   The election superintendent of Bibb County shall call and conduct an election as provided
67   in this section for the purpose of submitting this Act to the electors of the Bibb County
68   school district for approval or rejection. The election superintendent shall conduct that

69   election on the Tuesday after the first Monday in November, 2026, and shall issue the call
70   and conduct that election as provided by general law. The election superintendent shall
71   cause the date and purpose of the election to be published once a week for two weeks
72   immediately preceding the date thereof in the official organ of Bibb County. The ballot shall

74    "( ) YES Shall the Act be approved which provides a homestead exemption from
75                 Bibb County school district ad valorem taxes for educational purposes:

80                   (3) In the amount of the full assessed value of the homestead for residents
81                   who are 85 years of age and older?"
82   All persons desiring to vote for approval of the Act shall vote "Yes," and all persons desiring
83   to vote for rejection of the Act shall vote "No." If more than one-half of the votes cast on
84   such question are for approval of the Act, Section 1 of this Act shall become of full force and
85   effect on January 1, 2027. If the Act is not so approved or if the election is not conducted
86   as provided in this section, Section 1 of this Act shall not become effective, and this Act shall
87   be automatically repealed on the 365th calendar day following the election date provided for
88   in this section. The expense of such election shall be borne by Bibb County. It shall be the
89   election superintendent's duty to certify the result thereof to the Secretary of State. The
90   provisions of this section shall be mandatory upon the election superintendent and are not
91   intended as directory. If the election superintendent fails or refuses to comply with this
92   section, any elector of the Bibb County school district may apply for a writ of mandamus to
93   compel the election superintendent to perform his or her duties under this section. If the court
94   finds that the election superintendent has not complied with this section, the court shall
95   fashion appropriate relief requiring the election superintendent to call and conduct such
