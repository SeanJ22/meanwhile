                                     A BILL TO BE ENTITLED
                                               AN ACT


 1   To provide a homestead exemption from City of Bremen independent school district ad
 2   valorem taxes for educational purposes in the amount of $90,000.00 of the assessed value of
 3   the homestead for residents of that school district who are 65 years of age or older and who
 4   have been residents and property taxpayers of the school district for at least five consecutive
 5   years; to provide for definitions; to specify the terms and conditions of the exemption and
 6   the procedures relating thereto; to provide for applicability; to repeal the exemption granted
 7   in an Act approved May 13, 2008 (Ga. L. 2008, p. 4065); to provide for compliance with
 8   constitutional requirements; to provide for a referendum, effective dates, automatic repeal,
 9   mandatory execution of election, and judicial remedies regarding failure to comply; to
10   provide for related matters; to repeal conflicting laws; and for other purposes.


11              BE IT ENACTED BY THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF GEORGIA:


12                                           SECTION 1.
13   (a) As used in this Act, the term:
14        (1) "Ad valorem taxes for educational purposes" means all ad valorem taxes for
15        educational purposes levied by, for, or on behalf of the City of Bremen independent

16        school district, including, but not limited to, any ad valorem taxes to pay interest on and
17        to retire independent school district bonded indebtedness.
18        (2) "Homestead" means homestead as defined and qualified in Code Section 48-5-40 of
19        the O.C.G.A., as amended.
20        (3) "Senior citizen" means a person who is 65 years of age or older on or before January
21        1 of the year in which application for the exemption under subsection (b) of this section
22        is made and who has been a resident and property taxpayer of the City of Bremen
23        independent school district for at least five consecutive years immediately prior to
24        applying for the exemption provided for in this Act.
25   (b) Each resident of the City of Bremen independent school district who is a senior citizen
26   is granted an exemption on such person's homestead from City of Bremen independent
27   school district ad valorem taxes for educational purposes in the amount of $90,000.00 of the
28   assessed value of such homestead. The value of that property in excess of such exempted
29   amount shall remain subject to taxation.
30   (c) A person shall not receive the homestead exemption granted by subsection (b) of this
31   section unless such person or person's agent files an application with the governing authority
32   of the City of Bremen, or the designee thereof, giving such person's age and such additional
33   information relative to receiving such exemption as will enable the governing authority of
34   the City of Bremen, or the designee thereof, to make a determination regarding the initial and
35   continuing eligibility of such person for such exemption; provided, however, that any person
36   claiming the $10,000.00 homestead exemption granted in an Act to provide a homestead
37   exemption from City of Bremen independent school district ad valorem taxes for educational
38   purposes for residents of that school district who are 62 years of age or older and whose
39   income including the income of such person's spouse, excluding certain retirement income,
40   does not exceed $10,000.00, approved May 13, 2008 (Ga. L. 2008, p. 4065), shall
41   automatically receive the exemption provided for in this Act without having to apply for such

42   exemption. The governing authority of the City of Bremen, or the designee thereof, shall
43   provide application forms for this purpose.
44   (d) The exemption shall be claimed and returned as provided in Code Section 48-5-50.1 of
45   the O.C.G.A., as amended. The exemption shall be automatically renewed from year to year
46   so long as the person granted the homestead exemption under subsection (b) of this section
47   occupies the residence as a homestead. After such person has filed the proper application as
48   provided in subsection (c) of this section, it shall not be necessary to make application
49   thereafter for any year, and such exemption shall continue to be allowed to such person. It
50   shall be the duty of any such person granted the homestead exemption under subsection (b)
51   of this section to notify the governing authority of the City of Bremen, or the designee
52   thereof, in the event that such person for any reason becomes ineligible for such exemption.
53   (e) The exemption granted by subsection (b) of this section shall not apply to or affect any
54   state ad valorem taxes, county ad valorem taxes for county purposes, municipal ad valorem
55   taxes for municipal purposes, or county school district ad valorem taxes for educational
56   purposes. The homestead exemption granted by subsection (b) of this section shall be in lieu
57   of and not in addition to any other homestead exemption applicable to City of Bremen
58   independent school district ad valorem taxes for educational purposes granted by local Act,
59   but shall be in addition to and not in lieu of any other homestead exemption applicable to
60   City of Bremen independent school district ad valorem taxes for educational purposes
61   granted by general state law.
62   (f) The exemption granted by subsection (b) of this section shall apply to all taxable years
63   beginning on or after January 1, 2027.

71                                           SECTION 3.
72   In accordance with the requirements of Article VII, Section II of the Constitution of the State
73   of Georgia, this Act shall not become law unless it receives the requisite two-thirds' majority

75                                           SECTION 4.
76   The municipal election superintendent of the City of Bremen shall call and conduct an
77   election as provided in this section for the purpose of submitting this Act to the electors of
78   the City of Bremen independent school district for approval or rejection. The municipal
79   election superintendent shall conduct that election on the Tuesday following the first Monday
80   in November, 2026 and shall issue the call and conduct that election as provided by general
81   law. The municipal election superintendent shall cause the date and purpose of the election
82   to be published once a week for two weeks immediately preceding the date thereof in the
83   official organ of Haralson County. The ballot shall have written or printed thereon the
84   words:

85    "( ) YES Shall the Act be approved which replaces the current homestead exemption
86                 from City of Bremen independent school district ad valorem taxes for
87        ( ) NO   educational purposes in the amount of $10,000 for certain senior citizens
88                 with a new homestead exemption of $90,000.00 of the assessed value of the
89                 homestead for residents of that school district who are 65 years of age or
90                 older and who have resided in and paid property taxes of the school district
91                 for at least five consecutive years, provided that any person receiving the
92                 current exemption shall automatically receive the new exemption?"

 93   All persons desiring to vote for approval of the Act shall vote "Yes," and all persons desiring
 94   to vote for rejection of the Act shall vote "No." If more than one-half of the votes cast on
 95   such question are for approval of the Act, Sections 1 and 2 of this Act shall become of full
 96   force and effect on January 1, 2027. If the Act is not so approved or if the election is not
 97   conducted as provided in this section, Sections 1 and 2 of this Act shall not become effective,
 98   and this Act shall be automatically repealed on the 365th calendar day following the election
 99   date provided for in this section. The expense of such election shall be borne by the City of
100   Bremen. It shall be the municipal election superintendent's duty to certify the result thereof
101   to the Secretary of State. The provisions of this section shall be mandatory upon the
102   municipal election superintendent and are not intended as directory. If the municipal election
103   superintendent fails or refuses to comply with this section, any elector of the City of Bremen
104   independent school district may apply for a writ of mandamus to compel the municipal
105   election superintendent to perform his or her duties under this section. If the court finds that
106   the municipal election superintendent has not complied with this section, the court shall
107   fashion appropriate relief requiring the municipal election superintendent to call and conduct
108   such election on the date required by this section or on the next date authorized for special
109   elections provided for in Code Section 21-2-540 of the O.C.G.A.


110                                           SECTION 5.
111   Except as otherwise provided in Section 4 of this Act, this Act shall become effective upon
112   its approval by the Governor or upon its becoming law without such approval.


113                                           SECTION 6.
