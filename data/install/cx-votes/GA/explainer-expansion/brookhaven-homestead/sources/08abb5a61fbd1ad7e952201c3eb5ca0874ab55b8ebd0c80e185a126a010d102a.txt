                                     A BILL TO BE ENTITLED
                                                AN ACT


 1   To amend an Act to incorporate the City of Brookhaven in DeKalb County, approved April
 2   16, 2012 (Ga. L. 2012, p. 5527), as amended, so as to increase the existing homestead
 3   exemption for senior citizens and disabled persons to $200,000.00; to specify the terms and
 4   conditions of the exemption and the procedures relating thereto; to provide for applicability;
 5   to provide for compliance with constitutional requirements; to provide for a referendum,
 6   effective dates, automatic repeal, mandatory execution of election, and judicial remedies
 7   regarding failure to comply; to provide for related matters; to repeal conflicting laws; and for
 8   other purposes.


 9              BE IT ENACTED BY THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF GEORGIA:


10                                           SECTION 1.
11   An Act to incorporate the City of Brookhaven in DeKalb County, approved April 16, 2012
12   (Ga. L. 2012, p. 5527), as amended, is amended by revising paragraph (1) of subsection (b)
13   of Section 5.09 as follows:
14        "(1) Each resident of the City of Brookhaven who is disabled or is an eligible senior
15        citizen is granted an exemption on that person's homestead from City of Brookhaven ad

18                                             SECTION 2.
19   In accordance with the requirements of Article VII, Section II of the Constitution of the State
20   of Georgia, this Act shall not become law unless it receives the requisite two-thirds' majority
21   vote in both the Senate and the House of Representatives.


22                                             SECTION 3.
23   The municipal election superintendent of the City of Brookhaven shall call and conduct an
24   election as provided in this section for the purpose of submitting this Act to the electors of
25   the City of Brookhaven for approval or rejection. The municipal election superintendent
26   shall conduct that election on the Tuesday following the first Monday in November, 2026,
27   and shall issue the call and conduct that election as provided by general law. The municipal
28   election superintendent shall cause the date and purpose of the election to be published once
29   a week for two weeks immediately preceding the date thereof in the official organ of DeKalb
30   County. The ballot shall have written or printed thereon the words:

31    "( ) YES Shall the Act to incorporate the City of Brookhaven be amended so as to
32                  increase the current homestead exemption for senior citizens and disabled
33        ( ) NO    persons from $160,000.00 to $200,000.00?"
34   All persons desiring to vote for approval of the Act shall vote "Yes," and all persons desiring
35   to vote for rejection of the Act shall vote "No." If more than one-half of the votes cast on
36   such question are for approval of the Act, Section 1 of this Act shall become of full force and
37   effect on January 1, 2027. If the Act is not so approved or if the election is not conducted
38   as provided in this section, Section 1 of this Act shall not become effective, and this Act shall
39   be automatically repealed on the 365th calendar day following the election date provided for
40   in this section. The expense of such election shall be borne by the City of Brookhaven. It
