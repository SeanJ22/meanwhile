The millage rate in 2025 was 2.74 mills, the same rate that has been in effect since 2015. To fund the 2026 General Fund Budget, the proposed 3.85 millage rate results in an increase in property tax by 1.233 mills over the Rollback Millage rate. Without this tentative millage rate increase, the millage rate will be no more than 2.617 mills. The proposed increase for a homesteaded property with a fair market value of $800,000 would be approximately $394.56, and the proposed increase for a non-homestead property with a fair market value of $1.575 million is about $764.46. Fiscal Year 2026 City of Brookhaven Mill Rates City of Brookhaven Maintenance and Operations: 3.85 mills General obligation bond mill rate: 0.046 mills Special tax district mill rate: 6.45 mills Special service district mill rate: 5.000 mills Fiscal Year 2026 City of Brookhaven Exemptions Homestead exemption: $40,000 Senior/Disabled tax exemption (aged 65 or older, $15,000 maximum income): $160,000 Residents are encouraged to attend upcoming public hearings for the 2026 millage rate. The meetings will be held Tuesday, June 9, 2026 at 6:30 p.m. and Tuesday, June 23, 2026 at 10:30 a.m. and 6:30 p.m. at City Centre at 4001 Peachtree Road, Brookhaven.
