                                     A BILL TO BE ENTITLED
                                                 AN ACT


 1   To amend an Act providing a $40,000.00 homestead exemption from certain ad valorem
 2   taxes levied by, for, or on behalf of the Chattooga County School District for residents of that
 3   district who are 70 years of age or older and whose gross income does not exceed
 4   $20,000.00, approved April 23, 1998 (Ga. L. 1998, p. 4660), so as to increase the income cap
 5   on such exemption; to increase the value of the exemption in steps over a two-year period;
 6   to revise a definition; to provide for compliance with constitutional requirements; to provide
 7   for a referendum, effective dates, automatic repeal, mandatory execution of election, and
 8   judicial remedies regarding failure to comply; to provide for related matters; to repeal
 9   conflicting laws; and for other purposes.


10              BE IT ENACTED BY THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF GEORGIA:


11                                           SECTION 1.
12   An Act providing a $40,000.00 homestead exemption from certain ad valorem taxes levied
13   by, for, or on behalf of the Chattooga County School District for residents of that district who
14   are 70 years of age or older and whose gross income does not exceed $20,000.00, approved
15   April 23, 1998 (Ga. L. 1998, p. 4660), is amended in Section 1 by revising paragraph (1) as
16   follows:

17        "(1) 'Income' means federal adjusted gross income determined pursuant to the Internal
18        Revenue Code of 1986, as amended, for federal income tax purposes of the resident of
19        the homestead and his or her spouse."


20                                           SECTION 2.
21   Said Act is further amended by revising Section 2 as follows:


22                                           "SECTION 2.
23    Each resident of the Chattooga County School District who is a senior citizen and whose
24    income for the immediately preceding taxable year does not exceed $38,000.00 is granted
25    an exemption on that person's homestead from Chattooga County School District ad
26    valorem taxes for educational purposes in the amount of:
27        (1) $41,150.00 of the assessed value of that homestead for tax year 2027;
28        (2) $55,000.00 of the assessed value of that homestead for tax year 2028 and subsequent
29        tax years.
30    The value of a homestead in excess of the amount exempted by this section shall remain
31    subject to taxation."


32                                           SECTION 3.
33   In accordance with the requirements of Article VII, Section II of the Constitution of the State
34   of Georgia, this Act shall not become law unless it receives the requisite two-thirds' majority
35   vote in both the Senate and the House of Representatives.


36                                           SECTION 4.
37   The election superintendent of Chattooga County shall call and conduct an election as
38   provided in this section for the purpose of submitting this Act to the electors of the Chattooga
39   County School District for approval or rejection. The election superintendent shall conduct

40   that election on the Tuesday following the first Monday in November of 2026 and shall issue
41   the call and conduct that election as provided by general law. The election superintendent
42   shall cause the date and purpose of the election to be published once a week for two weeks
43   immediately preceding the date thereof in the official organ of Chattooga County. The ballot
44   shall have written or printed thereon the words:

45    "( ) YES Shall the Act be approved which increases the income cap on the current
46                 homestead exemption for senior citizens 70 years of age or older from
47        ( ) NO   Chattooga County School District ad valorem taxes for educational
48                 purposes from $20,000.00 to $38,000.00, and for the exemption to increase
49                 to $41,150.00 for tax year 2027 and to $55,000.00 for tax year 2028 and all
50                 subsequent tax years?"
51   All persons desiring to vote for approval of the Act shall vote "Yes," and all persons desiring
52   to vote for rejection of the Act shall vote "No." If more than one-half of the votes cast on
53   such question are for approval of the Act, Sections 1 and 2 of this Act shall become of full
54   force and effect on January 1, 2027. If the Act is not so approved or if the election is not
55   conducted as provided in this section, Sections 1 and 2 of this Act shall not become effective,
56   and this Act shall be automatically repealed on the 365th calendar day following the election
57   date provided for in this section. The expense of such election shall be borne by Chattooga
58   County. It shall be the election superintendent's duty to certify the result thereof to the
59   Secretary of State. The provisions of this section shall be mandatory upon the election
60   superintendent and are not intended as directory. If the election superintendent fails or
61   refuses to comply with this section, any elector of the Chattooga County School District may
62   apply for a writ of mandamus to compel the election superintendent to perform his or her
63   duties under this section. If the court finds that the election superintendent has not complied
64   with this section, the court shall fashion appropriate relief requiring the election
65   superintendent to call and conduct such election on the date required by this section or on the
