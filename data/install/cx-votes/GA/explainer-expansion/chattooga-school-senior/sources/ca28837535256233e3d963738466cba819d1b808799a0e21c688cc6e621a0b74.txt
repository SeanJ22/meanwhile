Shall the Act be approved which increases the income cap on the current homestead exemption for senior citizens 70 years of age or older from Chattooga County School District ad valorem taxes for educational purposes from $20,000.00 to $38,000.00, and for the exemption to increase to $41,150.00 for tax year 2027 and to $55,000.00 for tax year 2028 and all subsequent tax years?
