     House Bill 1575 (AS PASSED HOUSE AND SENATE)
     By: Representatives Sainz of the 180th and Corbett of the 174th




                                     A BILL TO BE ENTITLED
                                                AN ACT


 1   To amend an Act to provide a homestead exemption from all City of Kingsland ad valorem
 2   taxes for any city purposes, approved April 7, 1992 (Ga. L. 1992, p. 5686), so as to apply the
 3   homestead exemption to residents who are 62 years of age or older; to provide for an
 4   effective date and applicability; to repeal conflicting laws; and for other purposes.


 5             BE IT ENACTED BY THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF GEORGIA:


 6                                             SECTION 1.
 7   An Act to provide a homestead exemption from all City of Kingsland ad valorem taxes for
 8   any city purposes, approved April 7, 1992 (Ga. L. 1992, p. 5686), is amended by revising
 9   subsection (a) of Section 1 as follows:
10    "(a) Each resident of the City of Kingsland who is 62 years of age or older shall be granted
11    a homestead exemption from City of Kingsland ad valorem taxes for municipal purposes
12    in the amount of $25,000.00 of the assessed value of the resident's homestead. This
13    exemption shall apply to all such taxes, including those levied to pay bonded indebtedness,
14    on property that qualifies as a homestead under Code Section 48-5-40 of the O.C.G.A."

15                                            SECTION 2.
16   In accordance with the requirements of Article VII, Section II of the Constitution of the State
17   of Georgia, this Act shall not become law unless it receives the requisite two-thirds' majority
18   vote in both the Senate and the House of Representatives.


19                                            SECTION 3.
20   The municipal election superintendent of the City of Kingsland shall call and conduct an
21   election as provided in this section for the purpose of submitting this Act to the electors of
22   the City of Kingsland for approval or rejection. The election superintendent shall conduct
23   that election on the Tuesday following the first Monday in November, 2026, and shall issue
24   the call and conduct that election as provided by general law. The election superintendent
25   shall cause the date and purpose of the election to be published once a week for two weeks
26   immediately preceding the date thereof in the official organ of Camden County. The ballot
27   shall have written or printed thereon the words:

28    "( ) YES Shall the Act be approved which provides a homestead exemption of
29                 $25,000.00 from City of Kingsland ad valorem taxes for municipal purposes
30        ( ) NO   for residents 62 years of age or older, which represents a three-year decrease
31                 in age to qualify for such exemption?"
32   All persons desiring to vote for approval of the Act shall vote "Yes," and all persons desiring
33   to vote for rejection of the Act shall vote "No." If more than one-half of the votes cast on
34   such question are for approval of the Act, Section 1 of this Act shall become of full force and
35   effect on January 1, 2027. If the Act is not so approved or if the election is not conducted
36   as provided in this section, Section 1 of this Act shall not become effective, and this Act shall
37   be automatically repealed on the 365th calendar day following the election date provided for
38   in this section. The expense of such election shall be borne by the City of Kingsland. It shall
39   be the election superintendent's duty to certify the result thereof to the Secretary of State.
40   The provisions of this section shall be mandatory upon the election superintendent and are
