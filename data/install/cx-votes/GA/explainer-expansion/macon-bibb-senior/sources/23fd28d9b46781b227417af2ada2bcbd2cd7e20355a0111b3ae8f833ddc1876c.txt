                                     A BILL TO BE ENTITLED
                                                AN ACT


 1   To provide a homestead exemption from Macon-Bibb County ad valorem taxes for county
 2   purposes in the amount of $17,000.00 of the assessed value of the homestead for residents
 3   of that county who are between the ages of 65 and 74; to provide a homestead exemption
 4   from Macon-Bibb County ad valorem taxes for county purposes in the amount of $32,000.00
 5   of the assessed value of the homestead for residents of that county who are between the ages
 6   of 75 and 84; to provide a homestead exemption from Macon-Bibb County ad valorem taxes
 7   for county purposes in the amount of the full assessed value of the homestead for residents
 8   of that county who are 85 years of age or older; to provide for definitions; to specify the
 9   terms and conditions of the exemption and the procedures relating thereto; to provide for
10   applicability; to provide for compliance with constitutional requirements; to provide for a
11   referendum, effective dates, automatic repeal, mandatory execution of election, and judicial
12   remedies regarding failure to comply; to provide for related matters; to repeal conflicting
13   laws; and for other purposes.


14             BE IT ENACTED BY THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF GEORGIA:

15                                          SECTION 1.
16   (a) As used in this Act, the term:
17        (1) "Ad valorem taxes for county purposes" means all ad valorem taxes for county
18        purposes levied by, for, or on behalf of Macon-Bibb County, including, but not limited
19        to, any ad valorem taxes to pay interest on and to retire county bonded indebtedness.
20        (2) "Homestead" means homestead as defined and qualified in Code Section 48-5-40 of
21        the O.C.G.A., as amended.
22        (b)(1) Each resident of Macon-Bibb County who is between the ages of 65 and 74 is
23        granted an exemption on such person's homestead from Macon-Bibb County ad valorem
24        taxes for county purposes in the amount of $17,000.00 of the assessed value of such
25        homestead. The value of that property in excess of such exempted amount shall remain
26        subject to taxation.
27        (2) Each resident of Macon-Bibb County who is between the ages of 75 and 84 is
28        granted an exemption on such person's homestead from Macon-Bibb County ad valorem
29        taxes for county purposes in the amount of $32,000.00 of the assessed value of such
30        homestead. The value of that property in excess of such exempted amount shall remain
31        subject to taxation.
32        (3) Each resident of Macon-Bibb County who is 85 years of age or older is granted an
33        exemption on such person's homestead from Macon-Bibb County ad valorem taxes for
34        county purposes in the amount of the full assessed value of such homestead.
35   (c) A person shall not receive the homestead exemption granted by subsection (b) of this
36   section unless such person or person's agent files an application with the tax commissioner
37   of Macon-Bibb County, giving such person's age and such additional information relative to
38   receiving such exemption as will enable the tax commissioner of Macon-Bibb County to
39   make a determination regarding the initial and continuing eligibility of such person for such
40   exemption. The tax commissioner of Macon-Bibb County shall provide application forms
41   for this purpose.

42   (d) The exemption shall be claimed and returned as provided in Code Section 48-5-50.1 of
43   the O.C.G.A., as amended. The exemption shall be automatically renewed from year to year
44   so long as the person granted the homestead exemption under subsection (b) of this section
45   occupies the residence as a homestead. After such person has filed the proper application as
46   provided in subsection (c) of this section, it shall not be necessary to make application
47   thereafter for any year, and such exemption shall continue to be allowed to such person. It
48   shall be the duty of any such person granted the homestead exemption under subsection (b)
49   of this section to notify the tax commissioner of Macon-Bibb County in the event that such
50   person for any reason becomes ineligible for such exemption.
51   (e) The exemption granted by subsection (b) of this section shall not apply to or affect any
52   state ad valorem taxes, county or independent school district ad valorem taxes for educational
53   purposes, or municipal ad valorem taxes for municipal purposes. The homestead exemption
54   granted by subsection (b) of this section shall be in lieu of and not in addition to any other
55   homestead exemption applicable to Macon-Bibb County ad valorem taxes for county
56   purposes.
57   (f) The exemption granted by subsection (b) of this section shall apply to all taxable years
58   beginning on or after January 1, 2027.


59                                            SECTION 2.
60   In accordance with the requirements of Article VII, Section II of the Constitution of the State
61   of Georgia, this Act shall not become law unless it receives the requisite two-thirds' majority
62   vote in both the Senate and the House of Representatives.

68   that election as provided by general law. The election superintendent shall cause the date and

83   effect on January 1, 2027. If the Act is not so approved or if the election is not conducted

85   be automatically repealed on the 365th calendar day following the election date provided for
86   in this section. The expense of such election shall be borne by Macon-Bibb County. It shall
87   be the election superintendent's duty to certify the result thereof to the Secretary of State.
88   The provisions of this section shall be mandatory upon the election superintendent and are
89   not intended as directory. If the election superintendent fails or refuses to comply with this
90   section, any elector of Macon-Bibb County may apply for a writ of mandamus to compel the
91   election superintendent to perform his or her duties under this section. If the court finds that
92   the election superintendent has not complied with this section, the court shall fashion
