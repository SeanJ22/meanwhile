                                    A BILL TO BE ENTITLED
                                               AN ACT


 1   To amend an Act providing a homestead exemption from all Pickens County School District
 2   ad valorem taxes for educational purposes for the full value of the homestead of certain
 3   residents of that school district, approved April 21, 1997 (Ga. L. 1997, p. 4283), so as to
 4   increase the income cap on such exemption; to provide for compliance with constitutional
 5   requirements; to provide for a referendum, effective dates, automatic repeal, mandatory
 6   execution of election, and judicial remedies regarding failure to comply; to provide for
 7   related matters; to repeal conflicting laws; and for other purposes.


 8             BE IT ENACTED BY THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF GEORGIA:


 9                                          SECTION 1.
10   An Act providing a homestead exemption from all Pickens County School District ad
11   valorem taxes for educational purposes for the full value of the homestead of certain
12   residents of that school district, approved April 21, 1997 (Ga. L. 1997, p. 4283), is amended
13   in Section 2 by revising subsection (a) as follows:
14    "(a) Each resident of the Pickens County School District who is a senior citizen or who is
15    disabled is granted an exemption on that person's homestead from all Pickens County
16    School District ad valorem taxes for educational purposes for the full value of that

17    homestead if that person's income, together with the income of all members of the family
18    residing within the homestead, does not exceed $40,000.00 for the immediately preceding
19    taxable year."


20                                            SECTION 2.
21   In accordance with the requirements of Article VII, Section II of the Constitution of the State
22   of Georgia, this Act shall not become law unless it receives the requisite two-thirds' majority
23   vote in both the Senate and the House of Representatives.


24                                            SECTION 3.
25   The election superintendent of Pickens County shall call and conduct an election as provided
26   in this section for the purpose of submitting this Act to the electors of the Pickens County
27   school district for approval or rejection. The election superintendent shall conduct that
28   election on the Tuesday following the first Monday in November of 2026 and shall issue the
29   call and conduct that election as provided by general law. The election superintendent shall
30   cause the date and purpose of the election to be published once a week for two weeks
31   immediately preceding the date thereof in the official organ of Pickens County. The ballot
32   shall have written or printed thereon the words:

33    "( ) YES Shall the Act be approved which increases the income cap on the current
34                 full homestead exemption that certain senior citizens and disabled persons
35        ( ) NO   receive from Pickens County School District ad valorem taxes for education
36                 purposes from $25,000.00 to $40,000.00?"
37   All persons desiring to vote for approval of the Act shall vote "Yes," and all persons desiring
38   to vote for rejection of the Act shall vote "No." If more than one-half of the votes cast on
39   such question are for approval of the Act, Section 1 of this Act shall become of full force and
40   effect on January 1, 2027. If the Act is not so approved or if the election is not conducted
41   as provided in this section, Section 1 of this Act shall not become effective, and this Act shall

42   be automatically repealed on the 365th calendar day following the election date provided for
43   in this section. The expense of such election shall be borne by Pickens County. It shall be
44   the election superintendent's duty to certify the result thereof to the Secretary of State. The
45   provisions of this section shall be mandatory upon the election superintendent and are not
46   intended as directory. If the election superintendent fails or refuses to comply with this
47   section, any elector of the Pickens County school district may apply for a writ of mandamus
48   to compel the election superintendent to perform his or her duties under this section. If the
49   court finds that the election superintendent has not complied with this section, the court shall
50   fashion appropriate relief requiring the election superintendent to call and conduct such
51   election on the date required by this section or on the next date authorized for special
52   elections provided for in Code Section 21-2-540 of the O.C.G.A.


53                                           SECTION 4.
54   Except as otherwise provided in Section 3 of this Act, this Act shall become effective upon
55   its approval by the Governor or upon its becoming law without such approval.


56                                           SECTION 5.
57   All laws and parts of laws in conflict with this Act are repealed.
