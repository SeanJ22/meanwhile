                                     A BILL TO BE ENTITLED
                                               AN ACT


 1   To amend an Act providing a homestead exemption from all Pike County ad valorem taxes
 2   for county purposes for certain residents of that county who are totally disabled, approved
 3   May 13, 2002 (Ga. L. 2002, p. 5732), so as to increase the amount of the homestead
 4   exemption for residents of Pike County who are totally disabled; to provide for increased
 5   amounts of homestead exemptions for certain residents who have resided in the county for
 6   a certain amount of time; to provide for compliance with constitutional requirements; to
 7   provide for a referendum, effective dates, automatic repeal, mandatory execution of election,
 8   and judicial remedies regarding failure to comply; to provide for applicability; to provide for
 9   related matters; to repeal conflicting laws; and for other purposes.


10             BE IT ENACTED BY THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF GEORGIA:


11                                           SECTION 1.
12   An Act providing a homestead exemption from all Pike County ad valorem taxes for county
13   purposes for certain residents of that county who are totally disabled, approved May 13, 2002
14   (Ga. L. 2002, p. 5732), is amended by revising subsection (b) of Section 1 as follows:

15        "(b)(1) Each resident of Pike County who is totally disabled is granted an exemption on
16        that person's homestead from all Pike County ad valorem taxes for county purposes as
17        follows:
18         (A) For all residents of Pike County who are totally disabled, in the amount of
19         $15,000.00 of the assessed value of that homestead;
20         (B) Beginning with the 2028 taxable year, for residents of Pike County who are totally
21         disabled and age 70 or older who have resided in the county for five to nine years,
22         $17,500.00 of the assessed value of that homestead;
23         (C) Beginning with the 2031 taxable year, for residents of Pike County who are totally
24         disabled and age 75 or older who have resided in the county for ten to 19 years,
25         $22,500.00 of the assessed value of that homestead; and
26         (D) Beginning with the 2035 taxable year, for residents of Pike County who are totally
27         disabled and age 80 or older who have resided in the county for 20 years or more,
28         $30,000.00 of the assessed value of that homestead.
29        (2) For the purpose of this section, the amount of time residing in the county is the total
30        amount of time a totally disabled person has lived in the county and need not be
31        consecutive."


32                                            SECTION 2.
33   In accordance with the requirements of Article VII, Section II of the Constitution of the State
34   of Georgia, this Act shall not become law unless it receives the requisite two-thirds' majority
35   vote in both the Senate and the House of Representatives.


36                                            SECTION 3.
37   The election superintendent of Pike County shall call and conduct an election as provided in
38   this section for the purpose of submitting this Act to the electors of Pike County for approval
39   or rejection. The election superintendent shall conduct the election on the Tuesday following

40   the first Monday in November, 2026, and shall issue the call and conduct that election as
41   provided by general law. The election superintendent shall cause the date and purpose of the
42   election to be published once a week for two weeks immediately preceding the date thereof
43   in the official organ of Pike County. The ballot shall have written or printed thereon the
44   words:

45    "( ) YES Shall the Act be approved which increases the homestead exemption for
46                 totally disabled residents of Pike County to $15,000.00 for all totally
47        ( ) NO   disabled residents, $17,500.00 for all totally disabled residents age 70 or
48                 older who have resided in the county for five to nine years, $22,500.00 for
49                 all totally disabled residents age 75 or older who have resided in the county
50                 for ten to 19 years, and $30,000.00 for all totally disabled residents age 80
51                 or older who have resided in the county for 20 or more years?"
52   All persons desiring to vote for approval of the Act shall vote "Yes," and all persons desiring
53   to vote for rejection of the Act shall vote "No." If more than one-half of the votes cast on
54   such question are for approval of the Act, Section 1 of this Act shall become of full force and
55   effect on January 1, 2027. If the Act is not so approved or if the election is not conducted
56   as provided in this section, Section 1 of this Act shall not become effective, and this Act shall
57   be automatically repealed on the 365th calendar day following the election date provided for
58   in this section. The expense of such election shall be borne by Pike County. It shall be the
59   election superintendent's duty to certify the result thereof to the Secretary of State. The
60   provisions of this section shall be mandatory upon the election superintendent and are not
61   intended as directory. If the election superintendent fails or refuses to comply with this
62   section, any elector of Pike County may apply for a writ of mandamus to compel the election
63   superintendent to perform his or her duties under this section. If the court finds that the
64   election superintendent has not complied with this section, the court shall fashion appropriate
65   relief requiring the election superintendent to call and conduct such election on the date
