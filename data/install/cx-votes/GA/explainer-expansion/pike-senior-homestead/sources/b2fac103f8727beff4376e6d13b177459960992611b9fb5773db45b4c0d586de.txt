Shall the Act be approved which increases the homestead exemption for senior citizens to $15,000.00 for all senior citizens, $20,000.00 for senior citizens 70 years of age or older who have resided in the county for five to nine years, $30,000.00 for all senior citizens 75 years of age or older who have resided in the county for ten to 19 years, and $50,000.00 for all senior citizens 80 years of age or older who have resided in the county for 20 or more years?
