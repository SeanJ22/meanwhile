CALL FOR SPECIAL ELECTION
Notice is hereby given that, in accordance with O.C.G.A. §21-2-540, a special election shall be
held in the City of Statham on November 3, 2026 for the purpose of submitting to the voters the
following question for approval or rejection:
“Shall the Act be approved which provides a homestead exemption from City of Statham ad
valorem taxes for municipal purposes in the amount of $20,000.00 of the assessed value of the
homestead for residents of that city who are 62 years of age or older and an additional exemption
in the amount of $10,000.00 of the assessed value of the homestead for residents of that city who
are 67 years of age or older??”
All citizens and residents of the City of Statham, who desire to vote in the special election, who
are not already registered to vote, must do so before the close of business on October 5, 2026.
Advance Voting will be weekdays, October 13 – 30 from 8:00am to 5:00pm, and Saturday,
October 17 & 24 from 9:00am to 5:00pm at the Barrow County Elections Office located at 233 E.
Broad St. Winder.
Election Day voting will be held at 1025 Church located at 1914 Broad Street Statham, GA 30666,
polls are open 7:00 a.m. and 7:00 p.m.
