Shall the Act be approved which provides a homestead exemption from City of Statham ad valorem taxes for municipal purposes in the amount of $20,000.00 of the assessed value of the homestead for residents of that city who are 62 years of age or older and an additional exemption in the amount of $10,000.00 of the assessed value of the homestead for residents of that city who are 67 years of age or older?
