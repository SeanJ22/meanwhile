                                      A BILL TO BE ENTITLED
                                               AN ACT


 1   To provide for an advisory referendum for the purpose of ascertaining whether the electors
 2   of Union County desire the governing authority of that county to be changed from a sole
 3   commissioner to a five-member board of commissioners; to provide for mandatory execution
 4   of election and judicial remedies regarding failure to comply; to provide for related matters;
 5   to provide for legislative purposes and findings; to repeal conflicting laws; and for other
 6   purposes.


 7               BE IT ENACTED BY THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF GEORGIA:


 8                                           SECTION 1.
 9   (a) It is the purpose of this Act to provide for an advisory referendum for the purpose of
10   ascertaining whether the electors of Union County desire the governing authority of that
11   county to be changed from a sole commissioner to a five-member board of commissioners.
12   (b) It is found, determined, and declared that the holding of the advisory referendum
13   provided for in this Act is:
14        (1) In all respects for the benefit of the people of Union County;
15        (2) A public purpose; and
16        (3) An essential governmental function for which public funds may be expended.

17                                           SECTION 2.
18   The election superintendent of Union County shall call and conduct an advisory referendum
19   election as provided in this section for the purpose of submitting a question to the electors
20   of Union County to determine whether a majority of said electors desire the governing
21   authority of that county to be changed from a sole commissioner to a five-member board of
22   commissioners. The advisory referendum election shall be held on the Tuesday after the first
23   Monday in November, 2026, and the election superintendent shall issue the call and conduct
24   that election as provided by general law. The election superintendent shall cause the date and
25   purpose of the election to be published once a week for two weeks immediately preceding
26   the date thereof in the manner required by law. The ballot shall have written or printed
27   thereon the words:

28                                        "ADVISORY REFERENDUM
29     ( ) YES     Should the governing authority of Union County be changed from a sole
30        ( ) NO   commissioner to a five-member board of commissioners?"
31   All persons desiring to vote for approval of the question shall vote "Yes," and all persons
32   desiring to vote for rejection of the question shall vote "No." The expense of such election
33   shall be borne by Union County. It shall be the election superintendent's duty to certify the
34   result thereof to the Secretary of State. The provisions of this section shall be mandatory
35   upon the election superintendent and are not intended as directory.           If the election
36   superintendent fails or refuses to comply with this section, any elector of Union County may
37   apply for a writ of mandamus to compel the election superintendent to perform his or her
38   duties under this section. If the court finds that the election superintendent has not complied
39   with this section, the court shall fashion appropriate relief requiring the election
40   superintendent to call and conduct such election on the date required by this section or on the
41   next date authorized for special elections provided for in Code Section 21-2-540 of the
42   O.C.G.A.
