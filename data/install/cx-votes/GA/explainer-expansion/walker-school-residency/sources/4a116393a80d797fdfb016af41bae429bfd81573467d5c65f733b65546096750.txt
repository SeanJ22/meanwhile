                                     A BILL TO BE ENTITLED
                                                AN ACT


 1   To amend an Act to provide a homestead exemption from Walker County school district ad
 2   valorem taxes for educational purposes, approved May 13, 2025 (Ga. L. 2025, p. 3901), so
 3   as to remove the five year residency requirement for the homestead exemption for residents
 4   of such school district who are 75 years of age or older; to provide for definitions; to specify
 5   the terms and conditions of the exemption and the procedures relating thereto; to provide for
 6   applicability; to provide for compliance with constitutional requirements; to provide for a
 7   referendum, effective dates, automatic repeal, mandatory execution of election, and judicial
 8   remedies regarding failure to comply; to provide for related matters; to repeal conflicting
 9   laws; and for other purposes.


10             BE IT ENACTED BY THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF GEORGIA:


11                                           SECTION 1.
12   An Act to provide a homestead exemption from Walker County school district ad valorem
13   taxes for educational purposes, approved May 13, 2025 (Ga. L. 2025, p. 3901), is amended
14   by revising Section 1 as follows:

15                                            "SECTION 1.
16    (a) As used in this Act, the term:
17        (1) 'Ad valorem taxes for educational purposes' means all ad valorem taxes for
18        educational purposes levied by, for, or on behalf of the Walker County school district,
19        including, but not limited to, any ad valorem taxes to pay interest on and to retire county
20        school district bonded indebtedness.
21        (2) 'Homestead' means homestead as defined and qualified in Code Section 48-5-40 of
22        the O.C.G.A., as amended, with the additional qualification that it shall include not more
23        than five contiguous acres of homestead property.
24        (b)(1) Each resident of the Walker County school district who is at least 70 years of age
25        but less than 75 years of age on or before January 1 of the year in which the application
26        for exemption under this Act is made or any year subsequent to the making of such
27        application is granted an exemption on such person's homestead from Walker County
28        school district ad valorem taxes for educational purposes in the amount of $50,000.00 of
29        the assessed value of such homestead. The value of that property in excess of such
30        exempted amount shall remain subject to taxation. The unremarried surviving spouse of
31        the person who has been granted the exemption provided for in this paragraph shall
32        continue to receive the exemption provided under this Act, provided that the unremarried
33        surviving spouse is 67 years of age or older and continues to occupy the home as a
34        residence and homestead.
35        (2) Each resident of the Walker County school district who is 75 years of age or older
36        on or before January 1 of the year in which the application for exemption under this Act
37        is made or any year subsequent to the making of such application is granted an exemption
38        on such person's homestead from Walker County school district ad valorem taxes for
39        educational purposes in the amount of the full assessed value of that homestead. The
40        unremarried surviving spouse of the person who has been granted the exemption provided
41        for in this paragraph shall continue to receive the exemption provided under this Act,

44                                           SECTION 2.
45   Said Act is further amended by revising Section 3 as follows:


46                                           "SECTION 3.
47    The election superintendent of Walker County shall call and conduct an election as
48    provided in this section for the purpose of submitting this Act to the electors of the Walker
49    County school district for approval or rejection. The election superintendent shall conduct
50    that election on the Tuesday after the first Monday in November, 2026, and shall issue the
51    call and conduct that election as provided by general law. The election superintendent shall
52    cause the date and purpose of the election to be published once a week for two weeks
53    immediately preceding the date thereof in the official organ of Walker County. The ballot
54    shall have written or printed thereon the words:

55    "( ) YES Shall the Act be approved which provides a homestead exemption from
56                  Walker County school district ad valorem taxes for educational purposes in
57        ( ) NO    the amount of $50,000.00 of the assessed value of the homestead for
58                  residents of such school district who are 70 years of age or older and which
59                  provides a homestead exemption from Walker County school district ad
60                  valorem taxes for educational purposes in the amount of the full assessed
61                  value of the homestead for residents of such school district who are 75 years
62                  of age or older?"
63    All persons desiring to vote for approval of the Act shall vote "Yes," and all persons
64    desiring to vote for rejection of the Act shall vote "No." If more than one-half of the votes
65    cast on such question are for approval of the Act, Section 1 of this Act shall become of full
66    force and effect on January 1, 2027. If the Act is not so approved or if the election is not

68    this Act shall be automatically repealed on the 365th calendar day following the election
69    date provided for in this section. The expense of such election shall be borne by Walker

80                                           SECTION 3.

83   vote in both the Senate and the House of Representatives.


84                                           SECTION 4.
