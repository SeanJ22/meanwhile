2026 General Election

November 3, 2026

General Election
A General Election is the election in which federal and state officers, members of Congress and of the general assembly, county and township officers, and other officers appear on the ballot. General Elections are held the 1st Tuesday after the 1st Monday in November of each even-numbered year.
Results will be posted on election night.

The 2026 General Election will be held on Tuesday, November 3rd 2026. Polling places will be open on Election Day from 7:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m.

Public Notice

Voter Registration

The Pre-Registration deadline for voter registration closes at 5pm Monday, October 19, 2026. Persons wishing to participate in this election who register to vote after May 18th will be required to provide proof of identification (i.e. current and valid photo identification card with an expiration date), AND proof of residency in their voting precinct, when they go to the polls on election day, or when they request an absentee ballot in-person. Other provisions may also apply. Persons not registered to vote by October 19th, should expect a longer processing time at the polls on election day.

Absentee Voting

Absentee Ballots

Wednesday, October 14th absentee ballots will be mailed and in-person voting starts in the Auditor's Office.

Download an Absentee Ballot Request Form

Important Dates

  • Tuesday, August 25th is the first day to submit an absentee ballot request form to the Auditor's Office.
  • Wednesday, October 14th is the first day absentee ballots will be mailed.
  • Wednesday, October 14th is the first day to cast an absentee ballot in Auditor's Office.
  • Monday, October 19th at 5pm is the deadline to request an absentee ballot by mail for this election.
  • Monday, November 2nd at 5pm is the deadline to vote an absentee ballot in Auditor's Office.
  • Requests for absentee ballots must be in writing and should be mailed or delivered to:
    Sac County Auditor
    100 NW State St.
    Sac City, IA 50583
  • Find more information on Absentee Voting.

All absentee ballots must be received by 8:00pm on Election Day to be considered on time to be counted. Postmarks no longer are acceptable.
