General Election 2026

November 3, 2026

General Election
A General Election is the election in which federal and state officers, members of Congress and of the general assembly, county and township officers, and other officers appear on the ballot. General Elections are held the 1st Tuesday after the 1st Monday in November of each even-numbered year.

Important Dates

First day to submit requests for a mailed absentee ballot: August 25, 2026

Candidate Filing Deadline (Soil & Water, Ag Ext): August 26, 2026 @ 5:00 PM

First day of in-person absentee voting: October 14, 2026

Last day to request mailed ballot: October 19, 2026

Office open for voting on Saturday, October 31, 2026 from 8AM - 5PM

Last day to vote absentee in the Auditor’s Office = November 2, 2026

ELECTION DAY: November 3, 2026, polls are open from 7AM - 8PM
