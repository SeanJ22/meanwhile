Shall the following Public Measure be adopted?

A local option sales and services tax shall be imposed in the City of Scranton at the Rate of 1% to be effective July 1, 2027, with no sunset.

Revenues from the local option sales and services tax shall be allocated as follows: 20% of said local option sales and services tax shall be allocated for any costs associated with operating expenses of the Scranton Public Library. 30% of said local option sales and services tax shall be allocated for any costs associated with the general expenses of the City of Scranton. 30% of said local option sales and services tax shall be allocated for any costs associated with the public works department of the City of Scranton. 20% said local option sales and services tax shall be allocated for any costs associated with the water and sewer expenses of the City of Scranton.
