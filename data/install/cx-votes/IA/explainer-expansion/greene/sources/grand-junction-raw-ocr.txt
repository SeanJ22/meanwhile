City of Grand Junction
Public Measure Il

Shall the following Public Measure be
adopted?
A local option sales and services tax
shall be imposed in the City of Grand
Junction at the Rate of 1% to be
effective July 1, 2027, with no sunset.
Revenues from the local option sales
and services tax shall be allocated as
follows: 100% of said local option sales
and service tax to be allocated for any
costs associated with property tax relief.
