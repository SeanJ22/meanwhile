City of Rippey

Public Measure IL
Shall the following Public Measure be
adopted?
A local option sales and services tax
shall be imposed in the City of Rippey at
the Rate of 1% to be effective July 1,
2027, with no sunset.
Revenues from the local option sales
and services tax shall be allocated as
follows: 100% of said local options sales
services tax to be allocated for any
costs associated with property tax relief.
