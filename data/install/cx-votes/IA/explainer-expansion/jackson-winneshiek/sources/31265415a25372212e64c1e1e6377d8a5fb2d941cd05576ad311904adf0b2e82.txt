Summary: To authorize a change in the
use of the one percent (1%) local sales
and services tax in the City of Spillville
effective July 1, 2027.
The use of the one percent (1%) local
option sales and services tax shall be
changed in the City of Spillville, effective
July 1, 2027.
PROPOSED USES OF THE TAX:
Revenues from the local option sales
and services tax (LOSST) shall be
allocated as follows:
Zero percent (0%) for property tax relief,
One Hundred percent (100%) will be
allocated annually by the City Council,
for any lawful purpose, including but not
limited to the following purposes:
Capital equipment purchases and
repairs, street, water, sewer and other
capital improvement and maintenance
projects, and/or for assistance to the
Bily Clocks Museum and Antonin
Dvorak Exhibit.
The City of Spillville LOSST fund was
previously established in 1991.  At that
time, its use was limited to street
improvements. This amendment will
broaden the permissible uses of the
LOSST funds, allowing for flexible
planning for the use of LOSST funds,
with the annual LOSST funds to be as
allocated by the City Council, based on
its determination of the City’s changing
annual needs.
