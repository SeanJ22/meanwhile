November 3, 2026: General Election

General Information:

  • Polling places will be open from 7 a.m. to 8 p.m. on Election Day, Tuesday, November 3.
  • Voters will need to vote at their assigned polling place on Election Day.  Click here to view a list of Linn County Polling Places (PDF).
  • Voters can find their polling place and sample ballot by entering their address in the Lookup Tool.
  • Candidate List (PDF)
  • Public Measure Language - All (PDF)
  • The last day to file for a satellite location is 5 p.m. on Thursday, September 17, 2026.
  • Absentee ballots will be mailed on or after Wednesday, October 14, to those voters who requested a mailed ballot.
  • Early in-person absentee voting will begin on Wednesday, October 14.
  • The deadline for requesting an absentee ballot is 5 p.m. Monday, October 19. Absentee ballot request forms (PDF) must be in the office by that date. Postmarks do not count!
  • The voter pre-registration deadline closes at 5 p.m. Monday, October 19. Voter registration forms (PDF) must be in the office by that date.
  • Open Saturday, October 31 from 8 a.m. - 5 p.m.
  • Early in-person absentee voting will end at 4:30 p.m. on Monday, November 2.
  • Voted absentee ballots must be returned by 8 p.m. to Election Services on Election Day for counting.

Election Services is on the second floor of the Jean Oxley Public Service Center located at 935 2nd Street SW, Cedar Rapids.

  1. Voter Registration

Registering to Vote Before the Pre-Registration Deadline

The pre-registration deadline for the General Election closes at 5 p.m. on Monday, October 19.

You can register to vote online through the Iowa Department of Transportation website. You will need to have an Iowa Driver's License or Non-operator ID to complete the process.

Completed Iowa Voter Registration Forms (PDF) can be mailed or dropped off at Election Services.

Our address is: Linn County Election Services, 935 2nd Street SW, Cedar Rapids, IA 52404

Additional Information about Registering or Updating Voter Registration

Registering to Vote After the Pre-Registration Deadline

After the pre-registration deadline, you must prove who you are (ID) and where you live (residence). The best document to bring is your valid Iowa driver's license with your current address. Election Day Registration can be completed on Election Day, during early in-person voting at our office, or at a satellite location.

Additional Information about Election Day Registration

  1. Vote Before Election Day

Vote Early In-Person Absentee Voting

Linn County Public Service Center

Early in-person absentee voting will begin on or after Wednesday, October 14.

Early in-person absentee voting ends at 4:30 p.m. on Monday, November 2.

Election Services is on the second floor of the Jean Oxley Public Service Center located at 935 2nd Street SW, Cedar Rapids.

Online Election Calendar: Early Voting and Satellite Locations

Voter ID

Voters participating in early in-person absentee voting must provide an ID. These are the valid forms of ID:

  • Iowa Voter Identification Card (needs to be signed)
  • Iowa Driver's License
  • Iowa Non-Operator ID
  • U.S. Military ID or Veteran ID
  • U.S. Passport
  • Tribal ID Card/Document

The voter may also use Election Day Registration documents.

Vote by Mail

The first day our office can accept absentee ballot request forms (PDF) is Tuesday, August 25.

Absentee ballots will be mailed on or after Wednesday, October 14, to those voters who requested a mailed ballot.

The deadline for absentee ballot request forms (PDF) is 5 p.m. on Monday, October 19. Request forms must be physically in the office. Postmarks do not count!

The elections office must receive voted ballots by 8 p.m. on Election Day, Tuesday, November 3.

  1. Vote on Election Day

Voting at the Polls

  • Find your polling place and sample ballot using our Lookup Tool.
  • The polls will be open from 7 a.m. to 8 p.m. on Election Day.
  • Voters must vote at their assigned polling place.
  • Voters will need to provide identification at the polling place.
  • There is no voting at the Jean Oxley Public Service Center on Election Day.

Voter ID

Voters on Election Day will be asked to provide ID to vote. These are the valid forms of ID:

  • Iowa Voter Identification Card (needs to be signed)
  • Iowa Driver's License
  • Iowa Non-Operator ID
  • U.S. Military ID or Veteran ID
  • U.S. Passport
  • Tribal ID Card/Document

The voter may also use Election Day Registration documents.

What If I'm not registered before Election Day?

If you are new to Linn County or missed the pre-registration deadline, you can still register and vote on Election Day using Election Day Registration.


Use the Linn County Lookup Tool to find your correct polling place.


At the polling place, you must provide proof of ID and residency. The best document to bring is your Iowa driver's license with your current address.


Additional Information about Election Day Registration can be found on our website.
