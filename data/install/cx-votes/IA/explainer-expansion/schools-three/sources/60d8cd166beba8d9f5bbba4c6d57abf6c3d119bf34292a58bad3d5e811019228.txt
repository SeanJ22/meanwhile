Shall the Board of Directors of the
Pocahontas Area Community School
District in the Counties of Pocahontas,
Calhoun and Palo Alto, State of Iowa,
be authorized to contract indebtedness
and issue General Obligation Bonds in
an amount not to exceed $20,000,000
to provide funds to remodel, repair,
improve, and equip the middle/high
school and elementary school, including
career and technical education and
classroom spaces; to build, furnish, and
equip additions to the middle/high
school and elementary school, including
site improvements; for playground
improvements at the elementary school;
and to build, furnish, and equip a central
office and regional learning center
building and a transportation facility,
including site improvements?
