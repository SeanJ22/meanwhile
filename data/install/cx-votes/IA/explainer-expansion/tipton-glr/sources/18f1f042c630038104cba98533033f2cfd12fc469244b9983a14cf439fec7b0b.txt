Shall the Board of Directors of the
George-Little Rock Community School
District in the Counties of Lyon, Osceola
and Sioux, State of Iowa, be authorized
to contract indebtedness and issue
General Obligation Bonds in an amount
not to exceed $14,950,000 to provide
funds to build, furnish and equip
additions to its Elementary School and
Middle School/High School facilities,
including related remodeling and
improvements; to remodel, repair, and
improve the Elementary School and
Middle School/High School facilities,
including HVAC and bathroom
improvements, and site improvements,
exterior improvements to the Middle
School/High School; to build, furnish,
and equip a new bus barn; and to
remodel, repair, and improve the Middle
School/High School track?
