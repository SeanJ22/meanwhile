Shall the Board of Directors of the
Norwalk Community School District, in
Warren County, State of Iowa, be
authorized to contract indebtedness and
issue general obligation bonds in an
amount not exceeding $65,000,000 for
the purpose of acquiring, furnishing,
equipping, constructing, improving,
repairing, and renovating school
buildings and improving the sites of the
School District's school buildings and
athletic facilities located in Norwalk,
Iowa, including without limitation: (a) a
new elementary school building and
site/land improvements; (b) HVAC
system replacement and improvements,
kitchen ventilation system
improvements, and other mechanical or
electrical upgrades to Oviatt Elementary
School, Lakewood Elementary School,
and Secondary Campus; (c) building
improvements and upgrades to enhance
security at all existing school buildings,
including security systems and
enhanced building safety measures; (d)
a new transportation building, including
maintenance bays, a wash bay, a
custodial room, offices, common areas,
and site/land improvements; and (e)
improving athletic facilities and related
parking areas, including construction of
a new practice field and building
improvements and expansions for
additional practice areas, offices,
storage space, common areas, and
related site improvements?
