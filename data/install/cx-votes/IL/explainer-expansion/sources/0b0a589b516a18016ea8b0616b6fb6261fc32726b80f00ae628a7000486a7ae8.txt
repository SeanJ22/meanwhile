BOURBONNAIS PUBLIC LIBRARY
PROPOSITION TO INCREASE THE LIMITING
TAX RATE
Yes
No
"Shall the limiting rate under the Property Tax
Extension Limitation Law for the Bourbonnais Public
Library District, Kankakee County, Illinois, be
increased by an additional amount equal to 0.134%
above the limiting rate for the levy year 2025 and be
equal to 0.290% of the equalized assessed value of
the taxable property therein for levy year 2026?"
(1) The approximate amount of taxes extendable at the most recently
extended limiting rate is $1,196,169.87, and the approximate amount
of taxes extendable if the proposition is approved is $2,223,649.11.
(2) For the 2026 levy year, the approximate amount of the additional
tax extendable against property containing a single-family residence
and having a fair market value at the time of the referendum of
$100,000 is estimated to be $44.67.
(3) If the proposition is approved, the aggregate extension for 2026
will be determined by the limiting rate set forth in the proposition,
rather than the otherwise applicable limiting rate calculated under the
provisions of the Property Tax Extension Limitation Law (commonly
known as the Property Tax Cap Law).

RATE OF THE GREATER MOMENCE FIRE

Yes
No
Shall the limiting rate under the Property Tax
Extension Limitation Law for the Greater Momence
Fire Protection District, Kankakee County, Illinois,

ambulance purposes for levy year 2025 and be

tax extendable against property containing a single family residence
and having a fair market value at the time of referendum of $100,000

will be determined by the limiting rate set forth in the proposition,
rather than the otherwise applicable limiting rate calculated under the
provisions of the Property Tax Extension Limitation Law (commonly
