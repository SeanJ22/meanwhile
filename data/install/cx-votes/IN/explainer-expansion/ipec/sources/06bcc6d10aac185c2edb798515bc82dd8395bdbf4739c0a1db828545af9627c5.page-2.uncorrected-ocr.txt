NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED, by the Indianapolis Public Education Board as
follows and in effect upon passage of this Resolution:

Section 1. It is hereby determined that based on current revenue calculations for the years
2027 through and including 2030, taking into account the projected monthly distributions received
from the State of Indiana, the loss of property tax revenue because of the application of the credit
under Indiana Code chapter 6-1.1-20.6, as amended, and the loss of other revenues that have been
received by IPS in recent years from federal, state and local governments and other entities, IPEC
and IPS will not be able to carry out its public educational duty unless it annually imposes for the
years 2027 through and including 2030 an operating referendum tax levy, which is in addition to
all other property tax levies of IPS, of $95,000,000, which is in addition to all other property tax
rates of IPS and IPEC, not to exceed $0.372 on each one hundred dollars ($100) of the assessed
valuation, all in accordance with Indiana Code chapter 20-46-1, as amended.

Section 2. Pursuant to Indiana Code § 20-46-1-8(f), as amended, IPEC hereby projects
annual levy distributions in the amounts set forth in Exhibit B hereto, with such amounts subject
to change from time to time, to eligible charter schools and subject to revision based upon the
calculation of maximum annual levy.
