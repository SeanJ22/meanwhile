RESOLUTION 13 - 2026
INDIANAPOLIS PUBLIC EDUCATION CORPORATION BOARD

RESOLUTION APPROVING THE PRESENTATION OF A REFERENDUM LEVY
QUESTION TO VOTERS

WHEREAS, Indiana Code chapter 20-46-1 permits public school corporations to adopt
resolutions and certify ballot questions for voter approval that allows the imposition of a
referendum property tax levy;

WHEREAS, in calendar year 2026 it is expected that a record number of school
corporations throughout Indiana will seek referendum levies under Indiana Code chapter 20-46-1;

WHEREAS, through House Enrolled Act 1423 (2026), Public Law 101-2026, the Indiana
General Assembly created the Indianapolis Public Education Corporation (“IPEC”) for the public
purpose of establishing a unified student transportation, school property, and school performance
system within the geographic boundaries of Indianapolis Public Schools (“IPS”) to maximize
efficient use of taxpayer resources, respect the decision making of public schools within the
boundaries of IPS, and create the best conditions for student learning and success;

WHEREAS, pursuant to House Enrolled Act 1423, and as codified in Indiana Code § 20-
25.3-5-5(a), IPEC “shall assume the powers and duties of [IPS] under IC 20-46-1 in the territory
of [IPS]” with respect to referendum levies for taxpayers within IPS’s boundaries;

WHEREAS, Indiana Code chapter 20-46-1 allows IPEC to adopt a resolution to place a
referendum under Indiana Code chapter 20-46-1 for any of the following purposes: the IPEC Board
(“Board”) determines that (a) IPS cannot, in a calendar year, carry out its public educational duty
unless it imposes an operating referendum tax levy under Indiana Code chapter 20-46-1, as
amended; or (b) a referendum tax levy under Indiana Code 20-46-1, as amended, should be
imposed to replace property tax revenue that the school corporation will not receive because of the
application of the credit under Indiana Code 6-1.1-20.6;

WHEREAS, voters of IPS approved the first operating referendum levy in the amount of
$0.1960 on November 6, 2018, that took effect in 2019 (“2019 Operating Referendum Levy”);

WHEREAS, pursuant to Indiana Code chapter 20-46-1, the 2019 Operating Referendum
Levy expires on December 31, 2026;

WHEREAS, the Board, being the IPEC governing body, hereby determines that based on
current revenue calculations for the years 2027 through and including 2030, taking into account
the significant reductions in the monthly distributions received from the State of Indiana as well
as other revenues that have been received by IPS on an annual basis in prior years from federal,
state and local governments and other entities, [PS cannot carry out its public educational duty
unless it annually imposes a referendum tax levy of no more than $95,000,000 for each of the years
2027 through and including 2030, in accordance with Indiana Code chapter 20-46-1, as amended
(the “2027 Referendum Tax Levy Fund”);
