    FINDINGS AND FINAL DETERMINATION ON PROPOSED BALLOT QUESTION


1. The Town of Ellettsville and Richland Township, Monroe County, propose to reorganize.

2. Under Indiana law, voters in the political subdivisions proposing to reorganize will vote in a
   referendum to approve or deny the proposed reorganization.

3. Indiana law governs the format and wording of the ballot question for the referendum.

4. Under Indiana law, the public question shall be placed on the ballot in all of the precincts that are
   located in the reorganizing political subdivisions in substantially the following form:

        (Insert a brief description of the structure of the proposed reorganized political
        subdivision that will succeed the reorganizing political subdivisions.)
        “Shall _________ (insert name of political subdivision) and _________ (insert name
        of political subdivision) reorganize as a single political subdivision?”.

    Indiana Code § 36-1.5-4-28.

5. Thus, the ballot question must contain a brief description of the structure of the proposed
   reorganized political subdivision that will succeed the reorganizing political subdivisions and the
   names of the political subdivisions proposing to reorganize.

6. The law requires the Department of Local Government Finance (“Department”) to evaluate
   whether the description of the reorganized political subdivision that will succeed the reorganizing
   political subdivisions is accurate and is not biased against either a vote in favor of the
   reorganization or a vote against the reorganization. The Department may either approve the ballot
   language as submitted or modify the ballot language as necessary to ensure that the description of
   the reorganized political subdivision that will succeed the reorganizing political subdivisions is
   accurate and is not biased.

7. On July 24, 2026, the Department received from the Monroe County Clerk, on behalf of the
   Monroe County Election Board (“Board”), the following:
       a. A July 24, 2026 letter from the Monroe County Clerk and an auto-generated transcript of
       the Board’s July 10, 2026 special meeting, reflecting unanimous approval of the proposed
       ballot question.

       b. The Plan of Reorganization containing the proposed ballot question and certified June 22,
       2026 adoption resolutions of the Ellettsville Town Council and Richland Township Board.

8. The Board requested that the Department review this proposed question:

       “Richland Township of Monroe County, Indiana and the Town of Ellettsville of
       Monroe County, Indiana have each adopted a Plan of Reorganization that provides for
       the consolidation of Richland Township into the Town of Ellettsville as a single
       governmental unit that will be governed and administered as a town with a seven (7)
       member elected Town Council. Shall Richland Township and the Town of Ellettsville
       reorganize as a single political subdivision?”

                                         Accuracy and Bias

9. The Department must review the language of the public question to evaluate whether the
   description of the reorganized political subdivision that will succeed the reorganizing political
   subdivisions is accurate and is not biased against either a vote in favor of the reorganization or a
   vote against the reorganization. The Department concludes that the description of the reorganized
   political subdivision that will succeed the reorganizing political subdivisions is accurate and is
   not biased in favor of or against the reorganization.

                                        Final Determination

WHEREFORE, based on the above findings and applicable law, the Department finds that the ballot
language complies with IC 36-1.5-4-28 and approves the ballot language as submitted.
The Department certifies this approval to the Monroe County Election Board and certifies its final
approval of the ballot language to the Monroe County Recorder pursuant to IC 36-1.5-4-28(c).

This determination is limited to the Department’s review of the accuracy and neutrality of the ballot
language under IC 36-1.5-4-28(c) and does not determine compliance with other statutory
requirements governing adoption of the Plan of Reorganization or certification of the public question
for placement on the ballot.
