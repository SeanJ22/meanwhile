                               OF THE GOVERNING BODY OF
                           THE CITY OF THE HIGHLANDS, KANSAS
                                 HELD ON September 14, 2026


      The governing body met in regular session at 6:00 p.m. The Mayor presided and all council
members were present: Jackie Ashcraft, Joey Dean, Tom Dobson, Christie Gardner, Jeff Jackson

       Thereupon, and among other business, an Ordinance was presented entitled:


       AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING AND PROVIDING FOR THE CALLING OF A
       BOND ELECTION IN THE CITY OF THE HIGHLANDS, KANSAS FOR THE
       PURPOSE OF SUBMITTING TO THE QUALIFIED ELECTORS OF THE CITY
       THE QUESTION OF ISSUING GENERAL OBLIGATION BONDS; AND
       PROVIDING FOR THE GIVING OF NOTICE OF SAID BOND ELECTION.


        Thereupon, on motion of Councilmember Jeff Jackson, seconded by Councilmember Jackie
Ashcraft, the Ordinance was passed unanimously.

         Thereupon, a majority of the members of the governing body having voted in favor of the
Ordinance, it was declared duly passed and the Ordinance was then duly numbered Ordinance No. 2026-38;
and was approved and signed by the Mayor and attested by the Clerk. The Clerk was further directed to
take all action necessary to proceed with calling said bond election in the manner prescribed by law.

                                ORDINANCE NO. 2026-38
       AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING AND PROVIDING FOR THE
       CALLING OF A BOND ELECTION IN THE CITY OF THE HIGHLANDS,
       KANSAS FOR THE PURPOSE OF SUBMITTING TO THE QUALIFIED
       ELECTORS OF THE CITY THE QUESTION OF ISSUING GENERAL
       OBLIGATION BONDS; AND PROVIDING FOR THE GIVING OF
       NOTICE OF SAID BOND ELECTION.

        WHEREAS, K.S.A. 15-731 (the “Act”) authorizes any city of the third class to issue
general obligation bonds payable by the city at large to surface, resurface, pave, repave, curb,
recurb, gutter, regutter, or otherwise improve any street or streets, avenue or avenues or other
public highway or highways, other than an alley, together with necessary bringing to grade and
grading, and with such culverts, drainage facilities and other incidentals as may be necessary,
and to build bridges and approaches thereto, any or all of these, when authorized so to do by an
election called for any one or more of such purposes and when a majority of those voting on the
proposition vote in favor thereof; and

        WHEREAS, the governing body of the City of the Highlands, Kansas (the "City"), a city
of the third class, deems it advisable to authorize and provide for the calling of a bond election in
the City pursuant to the Act for the purpose of submitting to the qualified electors of the City the
question of issuing general obligation bonds of the City to pay the costs of certain street
improvements; and

        WHEREAS, in order to authorize such general obligation bonds, it is deemed advisable to
call a bond election in the City, to be held on November 3, 2026.

     NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE
CITY OF THE HIGHLANDS, KANSAS:

        SECTION 1. It is hereby deemed necessary and advisable to pave and improve East
Snokomo Road, West Snokomo Road, Crazy Horse Road and Northlinks Drive in the City, and all
other related improvements (the "Project"), at an estimated cost of $500,000.

       SECTION 2. In order to pay the estimated $500,000 costs of the Project, it is necessary to
issue general obligation bonds (the "Bonds") of the City, in an amount not to exceed $500,000;
which Bonds shall be issued if a majority of the qualified electors of the City voting on the
proposition shall vote in favor thereof at a bond election to be held in the City for that purpose.
       SECTION 3. It is hereby authorized, ordered and directed that a bond election shall be
and is hereby called to be held in the City on November 3, 2026, at which time there shall be
submitted to the qualified electors of the City the following proposition:

         Shall the following be adopted?

         Shall the City of the Highlands, Kansas, issue general obligation bonds in an
         amount not to exceed $500,000 to pay the costs to pave and improve East Snokomo
         Road, West Snokomo Road, Crazy Horse Road and Northlinks Drive in the City,
         and all other related improvements; all pursuant to the provisions of K.S.A. 10-101
         et seq. and K.S.A. 15-731?

       SECTION 4. The vote at said bond election shall be by ballot, and the proposition stated
above shall be printed on the ballot, together with voting instructions as provided by law.

        SECTION 5. The Clerk of Reno County, Kansas shall give notice of the bond election as
provided by law by publishing a Notice of Bond Election in substantially the form attached hereto
as Exhibit A on the County election office website and once each week for two (2) consecutive
weeks in a newspaper of general circulation in the City, with the first publication to be not less than
twenty-one (21) days prior to the date of the bond election, and the last publication being not more
than ninety (90) days prior to the date of the bond election.


        PASSED by the governing body and APPROVED AND SIGNED by the Mayor of the
City of the Highlands, Kansas, on September 14, 2026.
