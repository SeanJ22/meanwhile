NOTICE OF SPECIAL QUESTION ELECTION
KECHI TOWNSHIP, SEDGWICK COUNTY, KANSAS
Notice is hereby given to the qualified electors of the Kechi Township, Sedgwick County, Kansas (the “Township”) that a special question election has been called and will be held on November 3, 2026, for the purpose of submitting to the qualified electors of the Township the following proposition:
Shall the following be adopted?
“Shall the Kechi Township, Kansas, be authorized to construct, equip and furnish a new township building for administrative use, equipment storage and maintenance, at an estimated total cost not to exceed $400,000; provided that all required funds for said project shall be derived solely from existing cash reserves currently held by the township and the proceeds from the sale of the existing township building, with zero dollars ($0.00) raised through the issuance of new bonds or any increase in the township's property tax levy, pursuant to the provisions of K.S.A. 80-104 et seq., as amended??”
To vote IN FAVOR of the proposition, darken the oval next to the word "YES".
To vote AGAINST the proposition, darken the oval next to the word "NO".
