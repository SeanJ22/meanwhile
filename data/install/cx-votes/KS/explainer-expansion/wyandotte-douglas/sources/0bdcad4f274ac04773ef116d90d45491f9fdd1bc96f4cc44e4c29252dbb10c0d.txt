                     NOTICE OF SPECIAL QUESTION ELECTION
                          CITY OF LAWRENCE, KANSAS


TO ALL THE QUALIFIED ELECTORS OF
THE CITY OF LAWRENCE, KANSAS:

        Notice is hereby given to the qualified electors of the City of Lawrence, Kansas
(the “City”) that a special question election in the City of Lawrence, Kansas has been
called and will be held in conjunction with the general election on the 3rd day of
November 2026, for the purpose of submitting to the qualified electors of the City the
following proposition:

Question

       Shall the following be adopted?

        Shall the City of Lawrence, Kansas be authorized to repeal the 2019 Sales Tax in
the amount of two tenths of one percent (0.2%) in the City of Lawrence, Kansas, and
replace it with the levying of a new special purpose retailers’ sales tax in the amount of
three tenths of one percent (0.3%) for the purposes of operating a City Public Transit
System, including purchasing and maintaining buses and other transit vehicles,
constructing and maintaining transit facilities, and purchasing and maintaining equipment
and such other related Public Transit purposes as may be in the best interest of the City,
with such special purpose retailers’ sale tax to take effect on the 1st day of April, 2027, if
approved by a majority of the qualified electors voting thereon. The new special purpose
retailers’ sales tax referenced herein, if approved by a majority of the qualified electors
voting thereon, shall expire after ten (10) years from the date upon which such tax is first
collected.

[Note: In the event that the new special purpose retailers’ sales tax referenced herein is
not approved by a majority of the qualified electors voting thereon at the November 3,
2026, general election, then the 2019 Sales Tax shall not be repealed and shall remain
in full force and effect until it expires in accordance with the terms of Ordinance No.
9549.]

       To vote in favor of this question, darken the oval completely next to the word,
“Yes.” To vote against it, darken the oval completely next to the word, “No.”
