Fire Protection District No. 18 Proposition (Millage Renewal)
Shall Fire Protection District No. 18 of the Parish of Rapides, State of Louisiana (the "District"), continue to levy a special tax of 20 mills (the "Tax") (an estimated $436,000 reasonably expected at this time to be collected from the levy of the Tax for an entire year) on all the property subject to taxation in the District for a period of 10 years, beginning with the year 2028 and ending with the year 2037, for the purpose of acquiring, constructing, maintaining and/or operating fire protection and emergency medical service facilities, vehicles and equipment, including both movable and immovable property, and including paying the cost of obtaining water for fire protection purposes, provided that a portion of the Tax proceeds is required to be contributed to state and statewide retirement systems as provided in R.S. 11:82?
