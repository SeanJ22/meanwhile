Fire Protection District No. 9 Proposition
(Renewal)

Shall Fire Protection District No. 9 of the Parish of Livingston, State of Louisiana (the "District") renew the levy and collection of a special tax of ten and thirty-eight hundredths mills (10.38) (an estimated $228,900 reasonably expected at this time to be collected from the levy of the tax for an entire year) on all the property subject to taxation in the District for a period of ten (10) years, beginning with the year 2028 and ending with the year 2037, for the purpose of maintaining and operating the District's fire protection facilities and for purchasing fire fighting equipment necessary and incidental thereto?
