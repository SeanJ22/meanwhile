Parishwide Proposition (Sales & Use Tax Rededication)
Shall the Parish of Plaquemines, State of Louisiana ("Parish") be authorized (i) to rededicate 30% of net revenues collected in the future from the levy and collection of an existing 1% sales and use tax (the "Tax") authorized to be levied in the Parish perpetually pursuant to an election held on November 14, 2009 (an estimated $15,000,000 reasonably expected to be collected from the levy of the Tax for an entire year), to provide funding for Plaquemines Parish Emergency Medical Services, such funds to be used for the purposes of paying costs associated with the provision of emergency medical services in the Parish, including operations, salaries, training and the acquisition and/or construction of land, buildings, equipment, and machinery for such purposes, and (ii) to continue to use 70% net revenues of the Tax for paying salaries, benefits, training, insurance, administration, maintenance and other costs associated with establishing a paid fire department for the Parish, with said funds to be additionally dedicated for expenditure for the acquisition of firefighting equipment, lands, buildings and machinery as may be necessary to provide adequate fire protection?
