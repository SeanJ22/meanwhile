Parkwood Terrace Crime Prevention and Neighborhood Improvement District Proposition
Shall the Parkwood Terrace Crime Prevention and Neighborhood Improvement District ("District"), be authorized to renew, levy, and collect the annual parcel fee on each improved parcel within the District for a period of ten years, beginning in 2028 and ending in 2037, in the amount of $100.00 per year, and authorized to increase the fee annually after the first year, not to exceed 10% of the amount imposed during the previous calendar year, provided said fee does not exceed $150.00 per year as provided in La. R.S. 33:9097.29, with the proceeds of the parcel fee (after deducting administrative costs) to be used to aid in crime prevention by providing added security for the District residents and to provide funding for beautification and improvements for the overall betterment of the District (an estimated $23,500.00 is reasonably expected to be collected from the initial collection)?
