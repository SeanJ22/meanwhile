Waterworks District No. 5 Proposition (Millage Continuation)
Shall Waterworks District No. 5 of the Parish of Beauregard, State of Louisiana (the "District"), be authorized to continue to levy a 16.29 mills tax (an estimated $67,400 reasonably expected at this time to be collected from the levy of the tax for an entire year), on all property subject to taxation in said District, for a period of 10 years, beginning with the year 2028 and ending with the year 2037, for the purpose of paying the cost of improving, maintaining and operating waterworks facilities in the District, said millage to represent a .88 mills increase (due to reappraisal) over the 15.41 mills tax authorized to be levied through the year 2027 pursuant to an election held on October 14, 2017?
