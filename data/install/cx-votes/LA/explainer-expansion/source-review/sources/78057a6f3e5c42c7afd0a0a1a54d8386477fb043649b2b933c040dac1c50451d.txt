Road District Number 7 Proposition (Tax Renewal)
Shall Road District Number 7 of the Parish of Beauregard, State of Louisiana (the "District"), be authorized to continue to levy a special tax of 32.2 mills on all the property subject to taxation in the District (an estimated $357,000 reasonably expected at this time to be collected from the levy of the tax for the entire year), for a period of 10 years beginning with the year 2027 and ending with the year 2036, for the purpose of constructing, improving and maintaining Parish roads and bridges within the District?
