Gravity Drainage District No. 5 Proposition (Renewal)
Shall Gravity Drainage District No. 5 of Cameron Parish, Louisiana ("District"), be authorized to renew a levied tax of six and twenty-one hundredths (6.21) mills on the dollar of assessed valuation on all property subject to taxation in said District, for a period of ten (10) years, commencing with the year 2027 and ending with the year 2036, with collections from the levy of the tax estimated to be $154,070 for one entire year, for the purpose of constructing, operating and maintaining the district's drainage systems within and for said District?
