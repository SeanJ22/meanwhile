City of Gretna Proposition (Fire Millage)
Shall the City of Gretna, Parish of Jefferson, State of Louisiana (the "City") be authorized to levy and collect a special tax of eight point five (8.5) mills (an estimated $1,453,371.38 reasonably expected at this time to be collected from the levy of the special tax for an entire year) on all property subject to taxation in the City of Gretna; that being the same area contained within voting precincts G-001 through G-011, City of Gretna; excluding voting precincts G-012 and G-013 Parts, Annexed Timberlane Subdivision; for a period of ten (10) years, beginning with the year 2028, and ending with the year 2037, for the object and purpose of providing fire protection in the City of Gretna, a work of permanent public improvement, title to which shall be in the public?
