City of DeQuincy Proposition
(Sales Tax Renewal)

Shall the City of DeQuincy, Louisiana (the "City") per Louisiana law continue to levy and collect a sales and use tax of one-half of one percent (1/2%), for a period of 15 years beginning August 1, 2027, with collections estimated at $362,976.24 for one calendar year, upon the sale at retail, use, lease or rental, consumption and storage for use or consumption of tangible personal property and on sales of services within the City, as presently or hereafter defined in La. R.S. 47:301 to 317, inclusive (the "Tax"), with the avails of the Tax (after paying reasonable costs of collecting and administering the Tax) dedicated for constructing, acquiring, operating, maintaining, or improving public safety, fire protection, criminal justice facilities, operations, equipment, and other operating expenses of and works of public improvement within the City, title to which shall be in the public; and, authorization to fund avails of the Tax into bonds in various series at various times, to fund capital costs of the City, including the purchase of fire trucks or other public safety equipment, per Louisiana law?
