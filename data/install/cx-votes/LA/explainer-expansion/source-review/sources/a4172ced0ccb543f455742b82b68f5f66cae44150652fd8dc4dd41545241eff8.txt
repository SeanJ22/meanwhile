Kinder Drainage District No. 2 Proposition (Tax Renewal)
Shall Drainage District No. 2 of the Parish of Allen, State of Louisiana (Kinder Drainage District No. 2), continue to levy a special tax of 4 mills (the estimated amount reasonably expected to be collected from the levy of the tax for one entire year being $105,000) on all the property subject to taxation in said District, for a period of 10 years beginning with the year 2028 and ending with the year 2037, for the purpose of improving, maintaining and operating said District's drainage works?
