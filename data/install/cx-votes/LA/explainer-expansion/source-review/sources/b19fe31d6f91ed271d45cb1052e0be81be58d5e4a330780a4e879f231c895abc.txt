Ward Two Cemetery Tax Proposition (Millage)
Shall the Parish of Morehouse, State of Louisiana (the "Parish"), under the authority of La. R.S. 33:2740.45, and other constitutional and statutory authority, levy and collect a special ad valorem tax of two (2) mills, on all the property subject to taxation in Ward Two of the Parish (an estimated $35,000 reasonably expected at this time to be collected from the levy of the tax for one entire year) for a period of ten (10) years, beginning with the year 2027 and ending with the year 2036, inclusive, for the acquisition, improvement, maintenance and upkeep of public cemeteries in Ward Two of the Parish, with the initial levy of such tax for the year 2027 to be made in lieu of the final authorized levy of a similar tax that was approved at an election held on April 29, 2017?
