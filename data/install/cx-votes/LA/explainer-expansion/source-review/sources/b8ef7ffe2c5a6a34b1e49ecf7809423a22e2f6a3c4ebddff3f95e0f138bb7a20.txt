Sales Tax District No. 1 Proposition (Sales Tax)
Shall Sales Tax District No. 1 of the Parish of West Feliciana, State of Louisiana (the "District"), be authorized to levy and collect a tax of 1/2% (the "Tax"), in accordance with Louisiana law (an estimated $1,253,500 reasonably expected at this time to be collected from the levy of the Tax for an entire year), for a period of 7 years, commencing January 1, 2027, for the purposes of acquiring, constructing, improving, maintaining, supporting and/or operating recreational parks, facilities, equipment, programs and services, provided at the discretion of the District's governing authority, up to twenty-five percent (25%) of the annual proceeds of the Tax may be used for the purposes of maintaining and supporting social services and programs?
