Parishwide Hospital Service District Proposition (Millage Renewal)
Shall East Carroll Parish Hospital Service District, East Carroll Parish, Louisiana (the "District"), be authorized to continue to levy a special tax of 10.57 mills on all property subject to taxation within the District (an estimated $690,300 reasonably expected at this time to be collected from the levy of the tax for an entire year), for a period of 10 years, beginning with the year 2028 and ending with the year 2037, with the avails or proceeds of said tax to be used exclusively for the operation and maintenance of the East Carroll Parish Hospital?
