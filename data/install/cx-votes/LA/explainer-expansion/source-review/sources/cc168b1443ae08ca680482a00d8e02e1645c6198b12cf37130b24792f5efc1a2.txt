Fire Protection District No. 3 Proposition (Millage Continuation)
Shall Fire Protection District No. 3 of the Parish of Webster, State of Louisiana (the "District"), continue to levy a special tax of 15.59 mills (the "Tax") on all property subject to taxation in the District (an estimated $236,200 reasonably expected at this time to be collected from the levy of the Tax for an entire year), for a period of 10 years, beginning with the year 2028 and ending with the year 2037, for the purpose of acquiring, constructing, improving, maintaining and operating fire protection facilities and equipment in and for the District, including the cost of obtaining water for fire protection purposes, said millage to represent a .13 mills increase (due to reappraisal) over the 15.46 mills tax authorized to be levied through the year 2027 pursuant to an election held on December 10, 2016, provided that a portion of the Tax proceeds is required to be contributed to state and statewide retirement systems as provided in R.S. 11:82?
