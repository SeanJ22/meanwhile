City of Oakdale Proposition (Millage)
Shall the City of Oakdale, State of Louisiana (the "City"), be authorized to levy a 3.10 mills tax on all the property subject to taxation in the City (an estimated $74,800 reasonably expected at this time to be collected annually from the levy of this tax), for a period of 10 years, beginning with the year 2027 and ending with the year 2036, for the purpose of acquiring, constructing, improving, maintaining and/or operating recreational facilities for the City?
