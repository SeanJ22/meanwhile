Parishwide Proposition No. 1 of 2 (Sales Tax Rededication)
Shall the Parish of Assumption, State of Louisiana (the "Parish"), having been previously authorized at elections held on October 12, 1968, and January 17, 1998, to levy a 1% sales and use tax (the "Tax") (an estimated $3,700,000 reasonably expected at this time to be collected from the levy of the Tax for an entire year), after providing for the principal, interest and reserve requirements in connection with the issuance and payment of all bonds of the Parish heretofore or hereafter sold and issued by the Parish and payable from the proceeds of the Tax, then be authorized to appropriate, dedicate and use the remaining revenues heretofore or hereafter derived from the Tax for the purposes set forth in the prior propositions approving the levy of the Tax and for the additional purpose of providing funding for needs and expenditures of inmates at the Assumption Parish Detention Center, provided that no more than 1/8 of the revenues of the Tax received in any year may be used for inmate purposes?
