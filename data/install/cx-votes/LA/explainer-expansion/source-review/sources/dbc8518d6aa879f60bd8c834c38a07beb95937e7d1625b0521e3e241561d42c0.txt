Ambulance Service District No. 1 Proposition (Tax Renewal)
Shall Ambulance Service District No. 1 of the Parish of Allen, State of Louisiana (the "District"), continue to levy a 9.76 mills tax (the estimated amount reasonably expected to be collected from the levy of the tax for one entire year being $640,000) on all property subject to taxation in said District for a period of 10 years, beginning with the year 2029 and ending with the year 2038, for the purpose of acquiring, maintaining and operating equipment and facilities necessary to provide ambulance service and care for the residents of the District?
