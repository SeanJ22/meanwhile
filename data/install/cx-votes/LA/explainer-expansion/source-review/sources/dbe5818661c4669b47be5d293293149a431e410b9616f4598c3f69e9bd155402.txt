Sales Tax District No. 2 Proposition (Sales Tax)
Shall Sales Tax District No. 2 of the Parish of Claiborne, State of Louisiana (the "District"), be authorized to levy and collect a 0.625% sales and use tax (the "Tax") in accordance with Louisiana law (an estimated $419,000 reasonably expected at this time to be collected from the levy of the Tax for an entire year), for a period of 10 years from January 1, 2027, with the proceeds of the Tax (after paying the expenses of collecting and administering the Tax) to be dedicated and used for the purposes of paying the costs of (i) providing for solid waste collection and disposal in the District, including acquiring, improving, maintaining and/or operating the necessary land, facilities and equipment required in connection therewith, and (ii) materials required for surfacing and maintaining public roads within the boundaries of the District?
