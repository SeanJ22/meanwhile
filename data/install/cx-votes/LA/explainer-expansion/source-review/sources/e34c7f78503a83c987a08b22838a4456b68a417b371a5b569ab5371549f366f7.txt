Waterworks District No. Two Proposition (Maintenance Millage)
Shall Waterworks District No. Two of the Parish of Beauregard, State of Louisiana (the "District"), be authorized to levy a nineteen and seventeen hundredths (19.17) mills tax with the estimated amount reasonably expected to be collected from the levy of the tax for one entire year being $786,513.30 on all property subject to taxation in said District, for a period of ten (10) years, beginning with the year 2028 and ending with the year 2037 for the purpose of constructing, improving, maintaining and/or operating facilities and equipment of and for the District, said tax to represent a 1.89 mill increase over the 17.28 mills authorized to be levied through the year 2027 pursuant to an election held on October 14, 2017, such increase having been effective due to reappraisal?
