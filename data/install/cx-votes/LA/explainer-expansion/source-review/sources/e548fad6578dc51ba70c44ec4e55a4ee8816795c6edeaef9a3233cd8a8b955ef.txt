Road District Number 8 Proposition (Tax Continuation)
Shall Road District Number 8 of the Parish of Beauregard, State of Louisiana (the "District"), be authorized to continue to levy a special tax of 26.46 mills on all the property subject to taxation in the District (an estimated $212,500 reasonably expected at this time to be collected from the levy of the tax for the entire year), for a period of 10 years beginning with the year 2027 and ending with the year 2036, for the purpose of constructing, improving and maintaining Parish roads and bridges within the District, said millage to represent a .51 mills increase (due to reappraisal) over the 25.95 mills tax authorized to be levied through the year 2026 pursuant to an election held on April 29, 2017?
