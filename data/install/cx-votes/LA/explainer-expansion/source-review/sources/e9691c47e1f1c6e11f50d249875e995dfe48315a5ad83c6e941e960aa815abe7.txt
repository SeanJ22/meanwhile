Road Lighting District No. 2 Proposition (Tax Renewal)
Shall Road Lighting District No. 2 of the Parish of Terrebonne, State of Louisiana (the "District"), continue to levy a three and ninety-nine hundredths (3.99) mills tax on all the property subject to taxation in said District (an estimated $947,168 reasonably expected at this time to be collected from the levy of the tax for an entire year), for a period of ten (10) years, beginning with the year 2028 and ending with the year 2037, for the purpose of providing and maintaining electric lights on the streets, roads, highways, alleys and public places within and for said District?
