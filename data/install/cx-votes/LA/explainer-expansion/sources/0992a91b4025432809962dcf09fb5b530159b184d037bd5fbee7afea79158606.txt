Parishwide Proposition No. 1 of 3 (Millage Renewal)
Shall the Parish of West Feliciana, State of Louisiana (the "Parish"), continue to levy a 0.5 mill tax on all the property subject to taxation within the Parish (an estimated $255,000 reasonably expected at this time to be collected from the levy of the tax for an entire year), for a period of 10 years, beginning with the year 2027 and ending with the year 2036, for the purpose of maintaining, operating and/or improving the public hospital in the Parish?
