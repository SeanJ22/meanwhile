Parishwide Proposition (Millage)
Shall the Parish of Union, State of Louisiana (the "Parish"), levy a 12 mills tax on all property subject to taxation in the Parish (an estimated $2,173,000 reasonably expected to be collected from the levy of the tax for an entire year), for a period of 10 years, beginning with the year 2027 and ending with the year 2036, with the revenues therefrom to be used for the purpose of constructing, improving and maintaining public roads and bridges throughout Union Parish, Louisiana, said tax to be an extension and replacement of (i) a 5.13 mills tax and (ii) a 6.15 mills tax authorized to be levied through 2026 pursuant to an election held on December 10, 2016?
