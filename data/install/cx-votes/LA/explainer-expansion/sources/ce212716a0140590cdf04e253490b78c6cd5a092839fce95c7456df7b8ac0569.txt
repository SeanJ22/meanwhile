Parishwide Proposition (Millage Continuation)
Shall the Parish of Tensas, State of Louisiana (the "Parish"), be authorized to continue to levy a special tax of 14.32 mills on all property subject to taxation in the Parish (an estimated $754,000 reasonably expected at this time to be collected from the levy of the tax for an entire year), for a period of 10 years, beginning with the year 2028 and ending with the year 2037, for the purposes of providing court systems for civil, criminal and juvenile cases; expenditures for election expenses and other related costs; expenditures necessary to construct, repair and maintain governmental buildings and plants; general governmental insurance expenditures; expenditures for the temporary detention, custody and medical services of offenders; and expenses related to the operation of the office of the Coroner, said millage to represent a .61 mills increase (due to reappraisal) over the 13.71 mills tax authorized to be levied through the year 2027 pursuant to an election held on December 10, 2016?
