Town of Arcadia Proposition (Sales Tax)
Shall the Town of Arcadia, State of Louisiana (the "Town"), be authorized to levy and collect a 1% sales and use tax (the "Tax") in accordance with Louisiana law (an estimated $862,000 reasonably expected at this time to be collected from the levy of the Tax for an entire year), in perpetuity, beginning January 1, 2027, with the proceeds of the Tax (after paying reasonable and necessary expenses of collecting and administering the Tax) to be dedicated and used for at least 40% for constructing, acquiring, extending and/or improving sewer and sewerage disposal facilities of the Town and the waterworks system of the Town and the remainder of such proceeds for any lawful corporate purpose?
