Parishwide Proposition No. 2 of 2 (Sales Tax Renewal)
Shall the Parish of Pointe Coupee, State of Louisiana (the "Parish"), be authorized to continue to levy and collect a tax of 1/4% (the "Tax") in accordance with Louisiana law, for a period of 10 years from July 1, 2027 (an estimated $1,150,000 reasonably expected at this time to be collected from the levy of the Tax for an entire year), with the proceeds of the Tax (after paying the reasonable and necessary costs and expenses of collecting and administering the Tax), to be used solely for capital improvements, maintenance and operation of the Pointe Coupee Parish Health Services District Number One?
