Pollard Middle School Debt Exclusion Vote		
Background: Pollard Middle School Project 
Since 2019, the Town of Needham has been studying and planning for future school building needs. The Schools Master Plan, completed in 2020 and revised in 2022, studied all of Needham’s school buildings and identified three schools in need of renovation or replacement due to infrastructure and/or space issues: Pollard (grades 7-8), High Rock (grade 6), and Mitchell (grades K-5).  
Based on years of study, the Needham School Committee voted the preferred solution of rebuilding Pollard for grades 6-8, followed by rebuilding Mitchell, with High Rock serving as swing space for elementary school students.  
In 2024, the Town was accepted into the Massachusetts School Building Authority (MSBA) Core Program, which provides significant reimbursements to towns to help reduce school building costs. The Pollard School Building Committee was formed to guide the project through the MSBA process and make decisions to move the project forward, informed by input from the School Committee, Needham Public Schools educators and staff, and the community at large. 
Project Overview
Multiple options were studied and narrowed down to the current proposed project: a new grade 6-8 school built on the current Harris Avenue site to be built in two phases. The design of the school is based on an enrollment of 1,335 students and driven by the educational program, as voted by the School Committee. The total project cost is $325.4 million and will require a voter-approved debt exclusion to be funded. A portion of the total project cost will be reimbursed by the MSBA (to be determined by the end of August). If approved, construction would begin in Spring 2028 with Phase 1 (including Grades 7 and 8) completed in Summer 2030 and Phase 2 (including Grade 6) completed by the end of 2031. 
An overview of the project including the project timeline, an explanation of the MSBA process, and public meeting materials can be found on the Pollard Middle School Project website.
Ballot Question
On August 3, 2026, the Select Board voted to place the Pollard Middle School Debt Exclusion on the ballot for the November 3, 2026 election. The ballot question will be as follows: 
“Shall the Town of Needham be allowed to exempt from the provisions of proposition two and one-half, so-called, the amounts required to pay for the bonds issued in order to provide architectural design, engineering, construction, demolition, and site work, including incidental costs, for a new Pollard Middle School on Harris Avenue?  
YES or NO”

The Town has a property tax calculator available for residents and businesses to estimate how their property taxes would be affected if Needham voters approve the debt exclusion on November 3.
