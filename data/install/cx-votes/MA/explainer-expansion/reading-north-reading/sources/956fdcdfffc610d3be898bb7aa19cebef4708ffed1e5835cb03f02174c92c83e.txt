Shall the Town of North Reading be allowed to exempt from the provisions of proposition two and one-half, so-called, the amounts required to pay for the bond issued in order to remove and replace the modular classrooms at the J. Turner Hood and E. Ethel Little Schools?
At October 2026 Town Meeting, North Reading voters appropriated $3,500,000.00 to remove and replace modular classrooms at Hood and Little Schools, to be bonded contingent on approval of Proposition 2 1/2 Debt Exclusion.
Proposition 2 ½ is a state law that caps the overall property tax “levy limit” on existing property to 2 ½ percent increases per year, plus tax revenue from new growth that adds to the town’s tax base. Question 10 proposes to temporarily increase property taxes above the amounts otherwise allowed under Proposition 2 ½. The increase is for the amount of the annual debt service payments for the approved project, and it lasts only for the life of the related borrowing.
