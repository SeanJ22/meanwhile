Shall the Town approve the interlocal agreement entitled "Joint Public Works Department," entered into by and between the Town of Damariscotta and the Town of Newcastle, pursuant to the provisions of [30-A M.R.S.A. § 2201], a copy of which agreement is on file with the Town Clerk, and to authorize the Select Board to sign the agreement on behalf of the Town.

This article asks voters to approve an agreement between Damariscotta and Newcastle to establish a joint Public Works Department. The agreement creates a framework for shared management, staffing, equipment, budgeting, and long-term planning, with the goal of improving efficiency and service to both communities. Approval of the agreement does not itself appropriate funds for a new facility, land acquisition, or other capital expenditures; those decisions would require separate consideration and approval through the appropriate municipal budget and voting processes.
