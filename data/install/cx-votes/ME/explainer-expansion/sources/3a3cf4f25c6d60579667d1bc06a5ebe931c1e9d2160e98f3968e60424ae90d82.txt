Shall the Town authorize the Select Board to expend up to $75,000 from Undesignated Fund Balance for preliminary planning, engineering, site evaluation, and related professional services to explore potential locations and develop a preliminary feasibility assessment and estimated total project cost for a future joint public works facility, provided that the Interlocal Agreement between the Town of Damariscotta and the Town of Newcastle is approved pursuant to Article 3.

The Select Board is further authorized to negotiate and, if deemed appropriate, execute agreements and make reasonable deposits or other acquisition-related commitments necessary to evaluate or preserve an opportunity to acquire a potential site, provided that no such agreement or expenditure shall obligate the Town to complete the purchase of real property or proceed with construction of a public works facility.

Any future property acquisition or construction project shall require separate authorization and appropriation by the Town’s legislative body. If Article 3 is not approved, the authorization granted by this article shall be null and void.

This article asks voters to authorize up to $75,000 from Undesignated Fund Balance to explore potential locations for a future joint public works facility with Newcastle. The funds would support preliminary planning, engineering, site evaluation, and professional services to help determine whether a suitable site exists and what a future project might cost, including the potential to preserve an opportunity to acquire a site. This authorization would not commit the Town to purchasing property or constructing a facility; any future acquisition or construction would require separate approval and funding through the appropriate municipal process.
