## Register to Vote

General info: Voters can register at [Voter Registration](https://www.michigan.gov/sos/elections/voting/register-to-vote) or send an application to the County Clerk’s office or directly to their City or Township Clerk’s office by mail or scan to email. Voter can register in-person at their City or Township Clerk’s up to and including on Election Day. Learn more about how to register to vote, [here](https://mvic.sos.state.mi.us/).
