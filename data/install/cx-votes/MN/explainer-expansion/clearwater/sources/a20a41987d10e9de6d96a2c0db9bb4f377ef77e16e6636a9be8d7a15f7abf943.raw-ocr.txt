CLEARWATER TOWNSHIP
WRIGHT COUNTY
STATE OF MINNESOTA

RESOLUTION NO,___2026-1

RESOLUTION TO ADOPT OPTION B
APPOINTED TOWN CLERK

WHEREAS, Minn. Stat. §367.30 provides towns the option to change from an
elected Clerk to an appointed Clerk as Option B under such statute; and

WHEREAS, the resolution adopting an appointed Clerk may be proposed by
either the Town Board or by a petition of the electors equal in number to at least 15
percent of the electors voting at the last previous Town election; and

WHEREAS, the Town Board wishes to initiate the adoption of an appointed
Clerk under Option B via this resolution such that the question of adopting an appointed
Clerk can be put on the November 3, 2026 ballot; and

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED, that the Town Board of Clearwater
Township, Wright County, Minnesota does hereby designate that the Township Clerk
shall hereafter be appointed by the Town Board, provided that the electors vote to
authorize the adoption of said Option B at the November 3, 2026 Town election.

BE IT FINALLY RESOLVED, that the Town Clerk is hereby authorized and
directed to prepare the ballot question and take such other actions as may be needed to
submit the question of adopting said Option B to the electors for a vote at the next Town
election.

Adopted this 14 day of January, 2026.
