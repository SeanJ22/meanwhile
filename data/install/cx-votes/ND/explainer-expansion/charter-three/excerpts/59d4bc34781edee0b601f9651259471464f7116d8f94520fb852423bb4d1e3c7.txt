This initiated Home Rule Charter Amendment, published in The Forum on August 25, 2026, would add a new Article X to the Home Rule Charter of the City of Harwood, North Dakota. The initiated amendment would impose additional significant requirements on the City of Harwood for certain development or building projects within the City. These requirements would place new limitations and obligations on how the City evaluates, approves, and administers development or building projects. The full publication in The Forum on August 25, 2026, outlines the specifics of those requirements. The full financial and operational consequences of the initiated amendment cannot presently be determined. The estimated fiscal impact of this measure is unknown.
