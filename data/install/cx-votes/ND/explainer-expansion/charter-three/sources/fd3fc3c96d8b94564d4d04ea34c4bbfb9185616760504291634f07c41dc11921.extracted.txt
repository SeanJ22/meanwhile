Section 10.01. Purpose.
The purpose of this Article is to establish uniform requirements governing discretionary
municipal decisions concerning Qualified Developments.

Section 10.00. Legislative Findings and lntent.
The People of the City of Harwood flnd that certain developments, because of their scale,
infrastructure requirements, resource demands, operational characteristics, or potential impacts,
may substantially affect municipal infrastructure, public services, public safety, natural
resources, community welfare, and the long-term flscal interests of the City.
The People further find that discretionary municipal decisions concerning such developments
should be based upon complete and objective information, independent professional evaluation,
transparent governmental procedures, meaningful public participation, and written findings
supported by substantial evidence.
The People further find that informed municipal decision-making promotes responsible
economic development while protecting the public interest, preserving municipal resources, and
ensuring that significant public decisions are made only after the Governing Body has received
sufficient information to reasonably evaluate foreseeable impacts upon the City and its
residents.

This Article is intended to ensure that such decisions are based upon objective evidence,
independent professional evaluation, transparent govemmental procedures, meaningful public

Nothing in this Article is intended to prohibit responsible economic development or to regulate
matters reserved exclusively to the State of North Dakota or the United States. Rather, this
Article governs the procedures by which the City exercises its own discretionary authority when
considering Qualifi ed Developments.
This Article shall be applied uniformly to all Qualified Developments without regard to the
identity of the applicant, ownership structure, industry classification, or proposed business
activity.
The requirements established by this Article constitute minimum procedural protections
governing discretionary municipal decisions concerning Quallfied Developments and shall be
interpreted consistently with the City's Home Rule authority.

Section 10.03. Required Review Procedures.
10.03(A) General Requirement.
Before granting discretionary municipal approval for a Qualified Development, the Governing
Body shall ensure that the review procedures required by this Article have been completed and
that the information required for informed municipal decision-making has been made available
for public consideration.

No discretionary approval shall be granted until the Governing Body determines that the
procedural requirements of this Article have been substantially satisfied.
1 0.03(B) lndependent Professional Evaluation.
The Governing Body shall base its decision upon the administrative record developed pursuant
to this Article, including competent professional evidence, and shall not rely solely upon studies,
reports, analyses, projections, or representations prepared by or on behalf of an applicant or by
persons having a direct financial interest in the approval of the proposed development.
When technical information is necessary to evaluate reasonably foreseeable impacts of a
Qualified Development, the City shall obtain an independent professional review by individuals
or firms possessing appropriate education, training, experience, and professional qualifications.
lndependent review may include verification, evaluation, peer review, or supplemental analysis
of technical information submitted by the applicant.
The purpose of independent review is to provide the Governing Body with objective professional
evaluation sufficient to permit informed municipal decision-making.
I 0.03(C) Selection of lndependent Professionals.
lndependent professionals shall be selected by the City.
The City may consult with the applicant regarding qualifications or scope of work but shall retain
sole authority to select, direct, and supervise independent reviewers.
Nothing contained herein shall prohibit the City from utilizing qualified governmental agencies,
regional organizations, educational institutions, or independent consulting firms possessing
relevant expertise.
f 0.03(D) Applicant Responsibility for Review Gosts.
The City may require the applicant to reimburse the Cig for the reasonable and necessary costs
of independent professional review performed pursuant to this Aficle.
Such reimbursement shall be limited to costs reasonably necessary to evaluate the reasonably
foreseeable impacts of the proposed development and shall be applied uniformly to all Qualified
Developments.
Nothing contained herein authorizes the City to require unnecessary duplicative, or
unreasonable studies or expenses.

5. reasonable opportunity for questions directed through the Governing Body regarding
technical information relied upon in the decision-making process; and

Section '10.04. Required Findings Prior to Municipal Approval.
f 0.04(A) Written Findings Required.
Before granting any discretionary municipal approval for a Qualified Development, the
Governing Body shall adopt written findings of fact demonstrating compliance with this Article.
The written findings shall be based upon the administrative record and supported by substantial
evidence.
The Governing Body shall not grant discretionary approval until the findings required by this
Section have been adopted.
10.04(C) Gonsideration of Mitigation Measures.
When reasonably foreseeable municipal impacts have been identified, the Governing Body shall
consider mitigation measures proposed by the applicant, governmental agencies, independent
professionals, or members of the public.
12
10.04(B) Required Findings.
The Governing Body shall determine, based upon substantial evidence contained within the
administrative record, that:
'1. the review procedures required by this Article have been completed;
2. sufficient competent professional information has been obtained to reasonably evaluate
the foreseeable municipal impacts of the Qualified Development;
3. independent professional review has been completed where required under this Article;
4. reasonably foreseeable impacts upon municipal infrastructure, public services,
transportation systems, emergency response capabilities, public finances, and other
municipal interests have been evaluated;
5. the Governing Body has considered relevant public comments received during the
review process;
6. the Governing Body has considered reasonably foreseeable cumulative impacts
associated with the Qualified Development;
7. the Governing Body has considered competent professional information concerning
reasonably foreseeable facility closure, decommissioning, reclamation, longterm site
management, and any reasonably foreseeable municipal impacts associated with the
cessation of operations;
8. information relied upon in reaching the decision is supported by competent professional
evidence; and
9. the Governing Body possesses sufficient information to exercise its discretionary
authority in an informed manner.


1 0.04(D) lnsuffi cient lnformation.
lf the Governing Body determines that the administrative record does not contain sufficient
competent professional evidence to support one or more findings required by this Section, the
Governing Body shall not grant discretionary municipal approval until such deficiencies have
been adequately addressed.
The Governing Body may continue proceedings, request additional information, direct additional
independent review, or take such other lawful action as may be reasonably necessary to
complete the administrative record.
Nothing contained herein shall require denial of an application solely because additional
information is required before an informed decision can be made.
10.04(E) Basis for Decision.
The Governing Body's decision shall be based upon the administrative record developed
pursuant to this Article.
The Governing Body may assign such weight to the evidence as it deems appropriate, provided
its findings are supported by substantial evidence contained within the administrative record.
Nothing contained herein shall require unanimous professional opinions or eliminate the
Governing Body's authority to exercise reasonable legislative judgment based upon conflicting
competent evidence.
10.04(F) Written Decision.
The Governing Body's final decision approving, conditionally approving, or denying a Qualifled
Development shall be accompanied by written findings satisrying the requiremenls of this
Article.
The written findings shall identify the principal facts supporting the Governing Body's decision
and shall reference the evidence relied upon in reaching those findings.
