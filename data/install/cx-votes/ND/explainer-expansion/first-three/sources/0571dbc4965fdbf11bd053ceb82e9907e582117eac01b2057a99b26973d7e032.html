Ballot Resolution to Fund NDSU Extension

Resolution on Ballot to Fund NDSU Extension in Hettinger County
On July 8, 2026, the Hettinger County Commissioners approved a resolution to ask the voters of Hettinger County to increase the NDSU Extension mill levy from 2 mills to 4 mills. The resolution on the November 3rd General Election ballot states: “Shall the Board of County Commissioners of Hettinger County be authorized to assess up to four (4) mills for county agent extension services for a period not to exceed ten (10) years in accordance with Sections 57-15-06.7(2) and 11-38-01 of the North Dakota Century Code? A “YES” vote means you approve the measure above. A “NO” vote means you disapprove the measure above.”
In 2026, one mill in Hettinger County was worth $22,471 therefore levying the maximum of 2 mills will generate around $45,000 per year. After paying for half of the agent’s salary, there are insufficient funds to pay for both a workable operating budget and county-paid administrative support. If levying all 4 mills, it would comprise 81% of what is needed for the annual budget for the Extension agent. Comparatively to the other counties in North Dakota, Hettinger is one of the lowest for operating budget, if not the lowest.
NDSU Extension operates under a financial partnership with counties. NDSU Extension agents are NDSU employees. Under the base policy, counties agree to cover half of each agent’s salary, along with providing office space, the bulk of the operating budget, and county-paid administrative support. NDSU pays the other half of agent salaries and all fringe benefits. NDSU also provides sick and annual leave payout, most training programs, access to specialists, curricula, educational materials, email and website service, payroll processing, supervision (in collaboration with the county commission), hiring and coaching/ mentoring.
The county-based approach allows our agents to develop and deliver educational programs to farmers, ranchers, families, communities, and 4-H members that are tailored to the unique needs in each county.
ND Century Code allows counties to levy a base 2 mills to support the local NDSU Extension office. If voters approve, the county can levy an additional 2 mills above the 2-mill base levy for a maximum of 4 mills. Until recently, Hettinger County had the authority to levy up to 4 mills to support their Extension office. That authority expired at the end of 2024, so now the County can only levy a maximum of 2 mills to support their Extension office. Since the 4-mill levy expired in 2024, Hettinger County pulls these funds from its general fund. If the resolution passes, the county could cover the costs of a county-paid administrative assistant and have a workable operating budget for the Extension office if it had authority to levy up to 4 mills in support of Extension. If approved, the additional mills will continue until 2036.
