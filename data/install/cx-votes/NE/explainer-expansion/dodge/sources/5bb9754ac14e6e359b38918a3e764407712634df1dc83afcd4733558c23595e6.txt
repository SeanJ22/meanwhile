"Shall Washington County School District 0024
(Arlington Public Schools) in the State of Nebraska
issue its bonds in a total principal amount not to
exceed $10,500,000 to finance the costs of (a)
renovating the Career Technical Education space
within the District's existing school building and
constructing an addition thereto to expand skills and
trades instruction, (b) constructing an addition to the
District's existing school building to provide flexible
space for wrestling, recess, cheer, dance, choir,
learning and instruction, and other needs, (c)
constructing necessary building and site
improvements relating to such additions and
renovations, and (d) purchasing certain equipment,
furniture and apparatus for such additions and
renovations; said bonds to be issued from time to
time, to be sold at such prices, to bear interest at
such rates, to become due at such times, and to
have such other terms and provisions, all as may
be fixed and determined by the Board of Education
of the District; and

"Shall the District cause to be levied and collected
annually a special levy of taxes against all the
taxable property in the District sufficient in rate and
amount to pay the principal of, the premium, if any,
and the interest on said bonds as the same become
due?"
