Fire Station Project

Fire Station Project Overview

View the Fire Station Project Presentation (PDF)

The City of Fremont is planning improvements to its fire station facilities, including a proposed second station near East Military Avenue and Johnson Road and replacement of the aging main station at 415 East 16th Street that no longer adequately meets the department’s operational needs.

Fremont currently provides fire and emergency medical services from one station. City planning studies have identified needs related to response coverage and the capacity of the existing building and site.

The proposed satellite station would place emergency response resources closer to eastern and southeastern Fremont and reduce reliance on a single location for citywide service. Construction of a new facility at the main station location would address the aging facility’s limitations, provide space suited to       current and future department operations, and support firefighter recruitment and retention through improved working and living conditions.

This page provides project background, planning studies, a draft response coverage map, and comparisons with other Nebraska communities.

Station Locations and Response Coverage

Response Coverage

The map above shows areas firefighters could reach within an estimated four-minute drive from the existing main station at 415 East 16th Street, shown in blue, and the proposed satellite station near east Military Avenue and Johnson Road, shown in green.

Where the colors overlap, either station could reach the area within that estimated travel time. The proposed satellite station would extend coverage into eastern and southeastern Fremont.

Four minutes refers to driving time only. It does not include the time needed to process a 911 call or prepare crews to leave the station. Actual response times may vary depending on traffic, railroad crossings, and the availability of personnel and equipment.

Costs, Funding, and Project Status

The City is planning a satellite station on east 16th Street and replacement of the main station at its existing location. The Estimated project cost for both facilities is $30,000,000.

How would the fire station projects be funded?

Fremont voters will consider an additional ½% local sales and use tax on November 3, 2026. If approved, the City’s local rate would increase from 1.5% to 2%. The additional revenue would fund public infrastructure, with construction of a second fire station and replacement of the current, aging fire station identified as the first planned project.

Why isn’t the City planning to use existing public safety sales tax revenue to pay for the fire station bonds?

More of the City’s existing public safety sales tax revenue is needed to cover ongoing police and fire operating costs, leaving less available for equipment, vehicles, and building projects.

From 2024 to 2026, annual public safety personnel costs increased by approximately $2.5 million. Contributing factors include state-required retirement contributions, compensation adjustments under union agreements, and market adjustments to recruit and retain police officers and firefighters.

State law also limits annual growth in the City’s property tax request. This year, the statutory formula allowed approximately $433,818 in additional property tax revenue. That amount covers only part of the increase in public safety operating costs.

Existing public safety sales tax revenue also helps pay for ambulances, fire trucks, police vehicles, radios, protective gear, and other equipment. Based on current costs and revenue, the City does not anticipate sufficient funds from this source to also pay the proposed fire station bonds.

If approved, the additional ½% sales tax would provide funding for public infrastructure, with a second fire station identified as the first planned project. Existing public safety sales tax revenue would continue to support operations and equipment needs.

The additional tax would apply to taxable purchases in Fremont made by both residents and visitors.

Project Status

Construction plans are complete, and contractor bidding is underway. Project pricing is expected in early November for City review and consideration by the City Council.

Groundbreaking is tentatively planned for March 2027, depending on project funding, the results of the November 3 sales tax ballot question, and City Council approval.
