"Shall the governing body of the incorporated
municipality - the City of Fremont - increase the
local sales and use tax rate by an additional
one-half of one percent (1/2%) and impose a sales
and use tax at the increased rate upon the same
transactions within such municipality on which the
State of Nebraska is authorized to impose a tax for
20 years with all revenues generated by the
additional one-half of one percent (1/2%) to be used
for public infrastructure projects, which include, but
are not limited to, any of the following projects, or
any combination thereof: Fire Station and Satellite
construction, public highways and bridges and
municipal roads, streets, bridges, and sidewalks;
solid waste management facilities; wastewater,
storm water, and water treatment works and
systems, water distribution facilities, and water
resources projects, including, but not limited to,
pumping stations, transmission lines, and mains
and their appurtenances; hazardous waste disposal
systems; resource recovery systems; port facilities;
buildings and capital equipment used in the
operation of municipal government; mass transit
and other transportation systems, including parking
facilities; and equipment necessary for the provision
of municipal services.

A "YES" Vote: If a majority of the votes cast upon
such question shall be in favor of increasing such
local sales and use tax rate by an additional
one-half of one percent ( 1/2%), then the City of
Fremont shall be empowered as provided by
Section 77-27,142 of the Nebraska Statutes and
shall forthwith proceed to increase the local sales
and use tax rate from one and one-half percent (1
1/2%) to two percent (2%) and impose a sales and
use tax at the increased rate pursuant to the Local
Option Revenue Act, with all revenues generated
by the additional one-half of one percent (1/2%) to
be used for the public infrastructure projects.

A "NO" Vote: If a majority of those voting on the
question shall be opposed to such an increase of
the local sales and use tax rate by an additional
one-half of one percent (1/2%), then the governing
body of the incorporated municipality shall not
impose such a tax increase and the local sales and
use tax rate will remain unchanged at one and
one-half percent (1 1/2%).

Additional Information Regarding City of Fremont
Proposed Sales and Use Tax Rate Increase:

If the additional sales and use tax is approved, it will
not result in a reduction or elimination of any other
taxes or fees. All additional revenue will be used
solely for the construction of a Fire Station Satellite,
a new Fire Station Facility in the current station
location, together with equipment related thereto,
and public infrastructure improvements. The
revenue will be collected for 20 years beginning in
2027. The City of Fremont has entered into or
expects to enter into an interlocal agreement with
Dodge County for the long term development of
unified governance of the City of Fremont public
infrastructure projects specifically as is provided in
the interlocal agreement to provide public safety
services and improved streets to City and County
residents. Additional revenue collected from
increasing the sales and use tax rate by one half of
one percent (1/2%) will be used solely to fund the
public infrastructure projects of the City of Fremont
and none (0%) will be used for purposes of the
administration of the interlocal agreement. Revenue
will also be collected until the payment in full of any
bonds."
