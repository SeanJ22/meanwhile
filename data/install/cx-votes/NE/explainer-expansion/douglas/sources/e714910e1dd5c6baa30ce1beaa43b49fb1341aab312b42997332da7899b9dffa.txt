A vote “YES” in favor of the Proposition will
authorize the City of Valley to allocate up to 50%
of City sales tax revenue for the construction and
related costs of a new municipal swimming pool
and aquatic facility, while maintaining the City’s
current plan to replace all critical water mains in
four years.

A vote “NO” opposed to the Proposition will
require all of the local sales and use tax to
continue to be allocated to water improvement
and wastewater improvement projects.

Shall the City Council of the City of Valley,
Nebraska, be authorized to allocate up to fifty
percent (50%) of the revenue the City collects
from its one and one-half percent (1.5%) local
sales and use tax, on and after January 1,
2027, for the purpose of planning, designing,
constructing, equipping, financing, and
maintaining a new municipal swimming pool
and related aquatic facilities?
