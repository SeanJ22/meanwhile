Sample Ballots

Sample ballots are published on the Election Commission website approximately five weeks prior to statewide primary or general elections and approximately two weeks prior to special elections. Links to current and past countywide sample ballots are below. All races may not appear on your specific ballot.

View a sample of your specific ballot (when available) by searching for your address under "Find Your Voting Information" on the left side of the home page.
