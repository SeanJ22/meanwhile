                                           Ballot Question 1

N4VB, a Nebraska nonproﬁt corporation, and the City of Lincoln, Nebraska, propose to establish a
volleyball sports complex economic development project by appropriating annually from state
assistance received by the City pursuant to the Sports Arena Facility Financing Assistance Act for the
purpose of funding a volleyball sports complex to be owned by N4VB, and related infrastructure
improvements, which may include ﬁnancing of the privately owned sports complex through the
issuance of revenue bonds to be repaid from such state assistance.

Shall the City of Lincoln, Nebraska establish a volleyball sports complex economic development
project as described above by appropriating annually from state assistance received by the City
pursuant to the Sports Arena Facility Financing Assistance Act?


              I vote FOR authorizing the City to establish a volleyball sports complex economic
              development project and appropriate state assistance to such project.

              I vote AGAINST authorizing the City to establish a volleyball sports complex economic
              development project and appropriate state assistance to such project.


Electors voting in favor of the proposition shall blacken the oval opposite the words "FOR" following
such proposition, and electors voting against such proposition shall blacken the oval opposite the
words "AGAINST" following the proposition.

If a majority of the votes cast upon such question shall be in favor, then the governing body of the City
of Lincoln shall be empowered to establish and implement a sports complex economic development
project and appropriate the state assistance upon the terms and conditions contained in the Sports
Arena Facility Financing Assistance Act.
