                         NOTICE OF GENERAL ELECTION 2026
          Notice is hereby given, that on Tuesday, November 3, 2026 at the usual polling places in the precincts of Lancaster County,
Nebraska, an election will be held. The polls will be open at 8:00 a.m. and close at 8:00 p.m.
          Said election will be for the election of candidates to various offices and 10 issues: City of Lincoln Sports Arena Facility Financing
Assistance, five City of Lincoln Charter Amendments, Freeman Public Schools School Bond, Village of Denton Board of Trustees Member
Recall, Village of Raymond Sales and Use Tax, and Village of Sprague Sales and Use Tax. At such election the voters may accept or reject
three (3) Initiatives and one (1) Constitutional Amendment. The Secretary of State will publish a true copy of the title and text of the
Initiatives and Constitutional Amendment once each week for three consecutive weeks preceding the election.
          Unless specified otherwise, the term for each office is 4 years and the voter will vote for one (1) candidate per contest.

Village of Raymond Sales and Use Tax
VILLAGE OF RAYMOND, LANCASTER COUNTY, NEBRASKA
PROPOSAL TO IMPOSE LOCAL SALES AND USE TAX ALLOCATED
TO CAPITAL AND INFRASTRUCTURE IMPROVEMENTS INCLUDING
STREETS, UTILITIES, PARKS, LANDSCAPING, BEAUTIFICATION
AND TREE PLANTING

Shall the governing body of the Village of Raymond, Lancaster
County, Nebraska impose a sales and use tax upon the same
transactions within the municipality on which the State of Nebraska
is authorized to impose a tax, specifically, impose a tax of one and
one-half percent (1.5%) for village capital and infrastructure
improvements including streets, utilities, parks, landscaping,
beautification and tree planting.

FOR said imposition of the Sales and Use Tax, subject to the stated
terms.

AGAINST said imposition of the Sales and Use Tax, subject to the
stated terms.

A VOTE FOR: If a majority of votes are cast in favor of the question,
then the governing body of the Village shall be empowered to
proceed to impose a sales and use tax pursuant to the Local Option
Revenue Act.

A VOTE AGAINST: If a majority of those voting on the question shall
be opposed to such tax, then the governing body shall not impose
such sales and use tax.
