Bond Proposal Question 2
(Bond Proposal Question No.
2 will only go into effect if
Bond Proposal Question No.
1 is also approved by the
voters at this election.)
The Board of Education of the
West Essex Regional School Dis-
trict in the County of Essex, New
Jersey (the :Board") is authorized
to (a) undertake various improve-
ments,     renovations,    and
upgrades  at West Essex  High
School, including but not limited
to  HVAC   and   mechanical
upgrades, upgrades to the ath-
letic field, upgrades to the exist-
ing  football  field bleachers and
press box, and the replacement
of  select  interior  and  exterior
doors and door hardware, includ-
ing acquisition and installation of
fixtures, equipment, and any site
work;   (b)  undertake   various
improvements, renovations, and
upgrades at West Essex Middle
School, including but not limited
to renovations to various  office
spaces, and upgrades to the ath-
letic field, and the replacement of
select interior and exterior doors
and  door  hardware,  including
acquisition and installation of fix-
tures, equipment, and any  site
work; (c) appropriate $7,493,250
for such improvements; and (d)
issue bonds in an amount not to
exceed $7,493,250.
The  final  eligible costs  for the
projects approved by  the New
Jersey Commissioner of Educa-
tion are $2,993,250 (consisting
of $1,716,000  for West Essex
High School and $1,277,250 for
West Essex Middle School). The
projects   include  $4,500,000
(consisting  of  $4,500,000  for
West Essex High School and $0
for West Essex Middle School) for
school  facility construction ele-
ments in addition to the facilities
efficiency  standards  developed
by the Commissioner of Educa-
tion or not otherwise eligible for
State   support   pursuant    to
N.J.S.A. 18A:7G-5(g). The State
debt  service  aid percentage  is
40% of the annual debt service
due with respect to the final eligi-
ble  costs  of the  projects. The
Board of Education is authorized
to transfer funds among the proj-
ects approved at this election.
Do you approve this Bond
Proposal Question No. 2?
