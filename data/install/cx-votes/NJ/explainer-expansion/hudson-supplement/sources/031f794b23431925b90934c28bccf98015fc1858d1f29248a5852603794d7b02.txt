Shall a free public library be established in the Town of Guttenberg pursuant to N.J.S.A. 40:54-1 through 40:54-29?

A “Yes” vote authorizes the establishment of a free public library in the Town of Guttenberg pursuant to N.J.S.A. 40:54-1 et seq. If approved by the voters, the Town would be authorized to organize and operate a municipal free public library in accordance with New Jersey law. A “No” vote would reject the establishment of a municipal free public library.
