Shall the Township Committee of the Township of West Amwell amend Chapter 35, Section 35-3 of the Township Code, entitled “Funding,” to provide that the Municipal Open Space, Farmland and Recreation and Historic Preservation Trust Fund shall be funded by an annual tax levy in an amount up to, but not exceeding, $0.06 per $100.00 of assessed valuation, rather than requiring the annual levy to be set at $0.06 per $100.00 of assessed valuation?

A “Yes” vote authorizes the Township Committee to amend Section 35-3 of the Township Code so that the annual dedicated levy for the Municipal Open Space, Farmland and Recreation and Historic Preservation Trust Fund may be set each year in an amount up to, but not exceeding, $0.06 per $100.00 of assessed valuation. The current ordinance provides for an annual levy of exactly $0.06 per $100.00 of assessed valuation. Approval of this question would not increase the maximum authorized open space levy. Instead, it would allow the Township Committee to levy less than $0.06 per $100.00 of assessed valuation in a particular year if the Township Committee determines that a lower levy is appropriate. Funds raised by the levy would continue to be dedicated to the purposes authorized for the Municipal Open Space, Farmland and Recreation and Historic Preservation Trust Fund, including open space, recreation, farmland preservation, historic preservation, and related purposes authorized by law. A “No” vote means Section 35-3 would remain unchanged, and the ordinance would continue to provide for an annual levy of $0.06 per $100.00 of assessed valuation.
