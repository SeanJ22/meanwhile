Municipal Public Questions 2026
Colts Neck Township
Municipal Question
Should Colts Neck Township increase the annual levy for Open Space, Farmland and Historic
Preservation, within Colts Neck Township from the amount of $0.012 per $100 to the amount of
$0.027 per $100 of assessed valuation of real property?
Explanatory Statement
The proposed referendum seeks to increase the dedicated farmland, open space and historic
preservation tax from the current $0.012 to $0.027 per $100 of assessed valuation. The increase is
being proposed to provide additional funds to afford the Township the ability to acquire additional
land dedicated for farmland, open space and/or historic preservation, in furtherance of the
Township’s commitment to maintaining the rural character of the Township by preserving open
space.
For an average home assessed at $1,367,047, this would increase the tax payment from $164.05
per year to $369.10 per year. Revenues from this dedicated open space tax will be used for
farmland, open space and historic preservation, including debt service.Deal Borough
Municipal Question
Should the BOROUGH OF DEAL withdraw from the Monmouth County Library System?
Explanatory Statement
The voters of the Borough of Deal are asked whether to withdraw from the Monmouth County Library System
and authorize the withdrawal process directing Municipal Officials to take all actions necessary to effectuate the
withdrawal.
Deal Borough does not maintain a public library within its municipal boundaries and contributes approximately
Six Hundred Thousand Dollars ($600,000.00) annually toward the operation of the Monmouth County Library
System.
The Mayor and Commissioners have undertaken a review of the financial burden associated with continued
participation in the Monmouth County Library System and found that it is disproportionate to the services
utilized by Borough residents. The Board of Commissioners have concluded that in the efforts to reduce the tax
burden on its residents and promote fiscal responsibility, it is in the best interests of the Borough to withdraw
from the Monmouth County Library System at the earliest date permitted by law.
The Borough intends to continue providing free public library services to its residents in accordance with New
Jersey law and evaluating options including entering into agreements with neighboring public libraries and/or
other authorized library providers.
The Borough recognized that withdrawal from the Monmouth County Library System is governed by provisions
of New Jersey law (N.J.S.A. 40:33-13b and N.J.S.A. 40:54-7.1).
Interpretive Statement
Voting YES means you WANT the Borough to WITHDRAW from the Monmouth County Library System.
Voting NO means you DO NOT WANT the Borough to withdraw from the Monmouth County Library System.
