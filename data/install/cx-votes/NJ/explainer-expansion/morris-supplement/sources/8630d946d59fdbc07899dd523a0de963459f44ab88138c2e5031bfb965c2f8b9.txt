OPEN SPACE TRUST FUND REFERENDUM:

Shall the Pequannock Township Council adopt an Ordinance revising the permitted uses of the Open

Space, Recreation, Farmland and Historic Preservation Trust Fund, currently codified as Section 5-112

of the Pequannock Township Code, to allow the Open Space, Recreation, Farmland and Historic

Preservation Trust Fund to broaden the purposes for which it may be used to include all purposes

authorized by N.J.S.A. 40:12-15.7 specifically including the purpose of stewardship of permanently

preserved land acquired for recreation and conservation purposes that are designed to restore or

enhance its utilization for those purposes, and allow the use of previously collected funds for these

purposes.

INTERPRETIVE STATEMENT:

This proposal will give Pequannock Township greater flexibility in its use of the funds in its Open

Space, Recreation, Farmland and Historic Preservation Trust Fund. A “Yes” vote will mean the voters

want to maximize the purposes for which the fund can be used to include natural resource

management, habitat restoration and wildlife stewardship as eligible activities on permanently

preserved land acquired for recreation and conservation purposes in addition to other purposes within

the limits allowed by state law.

THIS IS NOT A REQUEST TO RAISE ADDITIONAL TAXES.
