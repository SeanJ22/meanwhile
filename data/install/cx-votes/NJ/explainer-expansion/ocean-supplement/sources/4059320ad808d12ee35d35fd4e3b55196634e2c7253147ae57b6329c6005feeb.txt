COUNTY QUESTION NO. 1

OCEAN COUNTY NATURAL LANDS TRUST FUND

Shall the Ocean County Natural Lands Trust Fund, which was approved and established by

referendum in 1997, and amended by referendum in 2019 to allow for the acquisition,

Pe |

development and maintenance of lands for recreational and historical preservation purposes,

increase its funded rate from 1.2 cents per $100 of total County equalized real property valuation,

to 2.2 cents per $100 of total County equalized real property valuation?

ese |

INTERPRETIVE STATEME!

T

This proposal shall increase the tax rate of the Ocean County Natural Lands Trust Fund by

1 cent in order to continue to appropriately support the costs of the Ocean County Natural Lands

Trust Fund Program. The Trust Fund prioritizes the preservation of properties in order to preserve

natural areas of open space, protect watersheds including groundwater recharge areas and

drinking water sources, and the acquisition, development, and maintenance for recreational and

Historical preservation purposes.

AYES" vote will permit the County to levy a maximum tax of 2.2 cents per $100 of total County

equalized real property valuation. This will be a 1 cent increase, the first and only increase to occur

since the inception of the program. As an example, at a rate of 2.2 cents, the owner of a property

assessed at $100,000 would contribute a maximum of $22 per year to the Trust Fund.
