Should the Township of Lacey be authorized to issue bonds in an amount of Forty-Five Million Dollars ($45,000,000) for the construction, furnishing and equipping of a new community recreation facility to be operated in partnership with the YMCA, providing recreational, aquatic, wellness, youth, senior and community programming to be paid through municipal debt service in the annual operating budget and the amount will be financed through local property taxes?

Currently the Township of Lacey is exploring a partnership with the YMCA of the Jersey Shore to construct an approximate 63,000 square foot recreation facility within the boundaries of Lacey Township. The project would include, but not be limited to: Indoor aquatic facilities, including competitive and recreational pools; Fitness and wellness facilities; Gymnasiums and indoor sports courts; Childcare and youth development space; Senior Citizen programming space; Multi-purpose community meeting rooms; Walking/running track and wellness areas; Administrative and support facilities; Parking, utilities, site improvements and related infrastructure. The Township’s share of the project would be financed through the issuance of general obligation bonds in an amount of $45,000,000, subject to all applicable laws and regulations. The Township and the YMCA will work cooperatively together to ascertain outside funding sources through discretionary funds, grants and benefactors to reduce the general obligation bonds. A "YES" vote means you favor the Township of Lacey proceeding to borrow $45 million through the issuance of general obligation bonds to construct a new community recreation facility in partnership with the YMCA. If approved and the Township Committee subsequently decides to proceed, the Township would own and participate in the development of the facility pursuant to a negotiated agreement with the YMCA, which would be responsible for operating the facility and providing recreational, aquatic, wellness, youth, senior and community programming. The ownership of the facility will be the Township of Lacey. The final operating responsibilities, membership structure, maintenance obligations and public access provisions would be established in a formal agreement approved by the Township Committee. The cost of the bonds would be repaid over time through municipal debt service in the annual operating budget and the amount will be financed through local property taxes. A "NO" vote means the voters are not in favor of the Township of Lacey issuing general obligations bonds in the amount of $45,000,000.
