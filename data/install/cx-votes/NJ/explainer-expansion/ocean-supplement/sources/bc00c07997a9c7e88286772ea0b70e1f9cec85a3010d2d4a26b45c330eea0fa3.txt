Should the Point Pleasant Beach Open Space Trust Fund, which was approved by voters in 2005 to have a collection rate of one cent per $100 in assessed valuation, have this authorization modified to a rate not to exceed one-half cent per $100 in assessed valuation, as may be determined by the governing body?

In 2005, Point Pleasant Beach voters approved the establishment of an Open Space Trust Fund, with a dedicated tax levy of one cent per $100 of assessed valuation. In 2025, a revaluation was conducted and implemented for the 2026 Tax Year, which resulted in the approximate doubling of this Open Space Trust Fund tax collection imposed upon residents. If approved, this referendum would reduce the authorized collection rate to one-half cent per $100 of assessed valuation, which will result in the Open Space Trust Fund tax collection remaining at approximately the same amount before and after the 2026 revaluation. The referendum would further establish the one-half cent per $100 of assessed valuation collection rate as a ceiling, such that the governing body may reduce the rate in the future, including to offset the impact of future revaluations.
