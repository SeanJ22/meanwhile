                                    RESOLUTION NO. 2026-151

     REQUESTING THE SOMERSET COUNTY CLERK TO PLACE A NON-BINDING
     REFERENDUM QUESTION ON THE NOVEMBER 3, 2026 GENERAL ELECTION
        BALLOT REGARDING PRESERVATION OF THE HOFHEIMER HOUSE

WHEREAS, in the early 1900’s the Hofheimer family acquired over 300 acres of land in Warren
Township to build their summer retreat which included the construction of the Hofheimer House
located at 46 Mountain Boulevard; and

WHEREAS, the Hofheimer House served as the former Municipal Building of the Township, but
has been vacant since 2017 when municipal operations were moved into the current Municipal
Building; and

WHEREAS, at the request of the Township Committee, the Warren Historic Sites Committee
spearheaded a study regarding the preservation and renovation of the Hofheimer House and the
potential future use thereof; and

WHEREAS, Concept Plans and Cost Estimates for preservation and renovation of the Hofheimer
House were prepared by Clark Caton Hintz and presented to the Township Committee at a public
meeting; and

WHEREAS, the Concept Plans and Cost Estimates are on file with the Township and available for
public inspection at the office of the Township Clerk and also available on the Township website at
https://www.warrennj.org/DocumentCenter/View/11645/Hofheimer-House-Plan-and-Cost-Estimate;
 and

WHEREAS, N.J.S.A. 19:37-1 authorizes a governing body of a municipality to ascertain the
sentiment of the legal voters of the municipality on any question or policy pertaining to the
government or internal affairs thereof, by adoption of an ordinance or resolution requesting the clerk
of the county to print upon the official ballots to be used at the next ensuing general election a certain
proposition formulated and expressed in the ordinance or resolution in concise form; and

WHEREAS, the Township Committee desires to obtain the sentiment of the legal voters of the
Township as to whether the Township should appropriate funds and incur debt service in the
estimated amount of $7,500,000.00 to preserve and renovate the Hofheimer House for community
meeting space in accordance with the Concept Plans and Cost Estimates prepared by Clark Caton
Hintz.

Interpretative Statement

In the early 1900’s the Hofheimer family acquired over 300 acres of land in Warren Township to
build their summer retreat which included the construction of the Hofheimer House located at 46
Mountain Boulevard. The Hofheimer House served as the former Municipal Building of the
Township, but has been vacant since 2017 when municipal operations were moved into the current
Municipal Building. At the request of the Township Committee, the Warren Historic Sites Committee
spearheaded a study regarding the preservation and renovation of the Hofheimer House and the
potential future use thereof. Concept Plans and Cost Estimates for preservation and renovation of the
Hofheimer House were prepared by Clark Caton Hintz and presented to the Township Committee at a
public meeting. The Concept Plans and Cost Estimates are on file with the Township and available
for public inspection at the office of the Township Clerk and also available on the Township website
at https://www.warrennj.org/DocumentCenter/View/11645/Hofheimer-House-Plan-and-Cost-
Estimate

The Township Committee desires to obtain the sentiment of the legal voters of the Township as
whether the Township should appropriate funds and incur debt service in the estimated amount of
$7,500,000.00 to preserve and renovate the Hofheimer House for community meeting space in
accordance with the Concept Plans and Cost Estimates prepared by Clark Caton Hintz. The estimated
cost of the preservation and renovation project would increase the municipal portion of the property
taxes paid by the average residential household with an assessed valuation of $1,021,852.00 by $81
per year assuming a 20-year bond issue.

This is a non-binding Referendum. A YES vote on this non-binding Referendum would indicate to
the Township Committee that you are IN favor of the Township appropriating funds and incurring
debt service for the preservation and renovation of the Hofheimer House. A NO vote on this non-
binding Referendum would indicate to the Township Committee that you are NOT IN favor of the
Township appropriating funds and incurring debt service for the preservation and renovation of the
Hofheimer House. Your vote will influence, but not bind the Township Committee to the majority
vote.
