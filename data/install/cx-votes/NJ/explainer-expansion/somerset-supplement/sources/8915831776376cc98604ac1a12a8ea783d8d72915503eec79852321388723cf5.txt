## County Clerk Steve Peter's Election News

##### Updated on October 31, 2026

**_S_****_ample Ballots have been mailed_**

You can view the[sample ballots pdfs](https://www.somersetcountynj.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/68125/639271590157170000)here.

##### V _oting By Mail_

[Vote By Mail Ballots](https://www.somersetcountynj.gov/government/elected-officials/county-clerk/election-division/vote-by-mail) can no longer be mailed but are available for in-person pick up at the County Clerk's Office during the following hours:

Monday, November 2 8:15 AM to 3 PM*

*No ballots may be issued after 3 PM on the day before the election, per NJSA 19:63-3.

[Drop boxes](https://www.somersetcountynj.gov/government/affiliated-agencies/election-board/drop-boxes)are open to return your voted ballot.

**If you want to vote in person**but are currently a mail-in voter, you can vote during early voting or on election day with a provisional ballot. You can cancel your mail-in request for future elections by using this [opt-out form](https://www.somersetcountynj.gov/government/elected-officials/county-clerk/election-division/vote-by-mail/opt-out-form) or[sending us an e-mail.](mailto:vbm@somersetcountynj.gov)

**_Election Results_**

Unofficial election results will be posted to the[interactive results site](https://www.somersetcountynj.gov/government/elected-officials/county-clerk/election-division/interactive-election-results)after the close of polls on Tuesday, November 3 at 8 PM and will be updated as more results are received. Final certification will take place on November 18.
