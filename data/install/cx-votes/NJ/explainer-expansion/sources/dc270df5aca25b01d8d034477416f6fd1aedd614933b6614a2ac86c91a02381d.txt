“Shall the Township of Wyckoff continue, maintain, and fund the existing Municipal Open Space Trust Fund through the collection of an annual tax levy in an amount not to exceed $0.01 (1 cent) per $100 of assessed valuation, to be used for: (1) Acquisition of lands for recreation and conservation purposes; (2) Development and maintenance of lands acquired for recreation and conservation purposes; (3) Acquisition of farmland for farmland preservation purposes; (4) Historic preservation of historic properties, structures, facilities, sites, areas, or objects, and the acquisition and maintenance of such properties, structures, facilities, sites, areas, or objects for historic preservation purposes; (5) Payment of debt service on indebtedness issued or incurred for such purposes; and (6) Blue Acres projects?”

This referendum would authorize the continuation of the Township of Wyckoff Municipal Open Space Trust Fund through the collection of an annual tax levy in an amount not to exceed $0.01 (1 cent) per $100 of assessed valuation. The proceeds of the Trust Fund may be used for open space acquisition and preservation, recreational and conservation purposes, farmland preservation, historic preservation, Blue Acres projects, and related debt service costs. The Trust Fund has been utilized by the Township to preserve and maintain open space, parks, athletic fields, recreational facilities, and historic properties, including the Zabriskie House and the Larkin House. The Township currently preserves and maintains approximately 152.04 acres of open space, including the approximately 21.04-acre former Maple Lake property acquired during the last five years. The authorization approved by this referendum shall remain in effect unless and until modified or repealed by future public referendum. Based upon the 2026 Tax List, the estimated annual revenue generated by the levy at the full 1 cent authorization would be approximately $486,930 annually.
