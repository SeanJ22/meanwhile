Shall the Borough of Hopatcong permit State-licensed cannabis cultivation, manufacturing, wholesale and distribution facilities to operate within designated manufacturing zoning districts, subject to the local zoning regulations, security requirements, and municipal licensing requirements?

This is a non-binding Referendum.

A YES vote on this non-binding Referendum would indicate to the Borough Council that you are IN favor of the Borough allowing cannabis cultivation, manufacturing, wholesale, and distribution facilities to operate within designated manufacturing zones. A NO vote on this non-binding Referendum would indicate to the Borough of Hopatcong Council that you are NOT IN Favor and continue the prohibition on such facilities within the Borough. Your vote will influence, but not bind, the Mayor and Council to the majority vote.
