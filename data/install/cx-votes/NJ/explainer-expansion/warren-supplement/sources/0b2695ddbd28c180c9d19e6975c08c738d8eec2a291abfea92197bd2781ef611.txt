WARREN COUNTY PUBLIC QUESTION: YES OR NO
Shall the County of Warren amend the Warren County Open Space, Farmland, Floodplain, and
Historic Preservation Trust Fund to include the purpose of stewardship of permanently
preserved lands acquired for recreation and conservation purposes that are designed to restore
or enhance its utilization for those purposes and allow the use of previously collected funds for
those purposes?
INTERPRETIVE STATEMENT:
A "YES" vote will permit the County of Warren to include natural resource management,
habitat restoration, and stewardship as eligible activities on permanently preserved land
acquired for recreation and conservation purposes. These include projects that are
designed to enhance or restore the land's utilization for those purposes as eligible activities
funded as part of the Warren County Open Space, Farmland, Floodplain Protection and
Historic Preservation Trust Fund. A "YES" vote will not increase taxes.
A “NO” vote is that you do not approve this amendment to the Open Space Trust Fund.
