ALLAMUCHY BOARD OF EDUCATION PUBLIC QUESTION: YES OR NO
BOND PROPOSAL QUESTION
The Board of Education of the Township of Allamuchy in the County of Warren, New Jersey (the “Board”) is
authorized to: (a) undertake various improvements, renovations, and upgrades at Allamuchy Township School,
including but not limited to the replacement of roofing, windows, and wood siding, masonry repairs, and
mechanical and HVAC system upgrades, including acquisition of any fixtures, equipment, and site work; (b)
undertake various improvements, renovations, and upgrades at Mountain Villa School, including but not limited
to repairs and upgrades to windows and lintels, masonry repairs, and upgrades to the insulation, including
acquisition of any fixtures, equipment and site work; (c) appropriate $16,421,600 for such improvements; and (d)
issue bonds in an amount not to exceed $16,421,600.
The final eligible costs for the projects approved by the New Jersey Commissioner of Education are $16,421,600
(consisting of $15,746,600 for Allamuchy Township School and $675,000 for Mountain Villa School). The projects
include $0 for school facility construction elements in addition to the facilities efficiency standards developed by
the Commissioner of Education or not otherwise eligible for State of New Jersey (the “State”) support pursuant
to N.J.S.A. 18A:7G-5(g). The State debt service aid percentage is equal to 40% of the annual debt service due
with respect to the final eligible costs of the projects. The Board of Education is authorized to transfer funds
among the projects approved at this election.
