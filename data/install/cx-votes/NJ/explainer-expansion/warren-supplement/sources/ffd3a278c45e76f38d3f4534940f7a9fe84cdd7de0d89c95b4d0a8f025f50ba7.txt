Should the prohibition of data centers anywhere within the Town of Belvidere be pursued as part of the re- examination of the Town’s Master Plan?

The development of a data center within the Town has the potential to generate significant tax revenue. At the same time, concern has been raised with respect to data centers utilizing excessive amounts of water and electricity as well as generating excessive noise from roof-mounted HVAC units. At present, data centers are not a permitted use in any zone within the Town. However, data centers are not an expressly prohibited use, and a data center developer could make application for variance relief. A YES vote would mean that you are in favor of expressly prohibiting data centers as an allowable use within the Town that would completely prohibit the ability of a data center to be built within the Town and eliminate any potential tax revenue from such a development. A NO vote would mean that you are not in favor of expressly prohibiting data centers and are in favor of retaining the ability of the Town’s Planning Board or Board of Adjustment and its professionals to consider a future application for a data center and potentially approve the application, with appropriate conditions.
