Notice of Election on Proposition
R.C. 3501.11(G)

Notice is hereby given that in pursuance of an Ordinance of the City Council of Columbus, Ohio, passed on the 31st day of August,
2026, there will be submitted to a vote of the people of City of Columbus, Ohio at the General Election to be held at the regular places
of voting on Tuesday, the 3rd day of November, 2026 the question of:

This proposed charter amendment would limit the ability to vote for candidates for Columbus City Council to only those electors
living within the districts that those candidates seek to represent.

The amendment specifies that ballots within council districts will include both citywide races as well as the district race.

The amendment allows for the initiation of a recall election of a council district representative if a petition is submitted bearing the
signatures of the electors of that district in an amount that is at least fifteen (15%) percent of the votes cast within the district in the
preceding mayoral election.

Shall the proposed charter amendment be approved?

The polls for the election will be open at 6:30 a.m. and remain open until 7:30 p.m. on election day.

By order of the Board of Elections,
of Franklin County, Ohio.
