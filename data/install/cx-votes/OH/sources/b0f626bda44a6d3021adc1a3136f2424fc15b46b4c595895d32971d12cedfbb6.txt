Issue 12
Proposed Charter Amendment
City of Akron
A majority affirmative vote is
necessary for passage.

Shall the Charter of the City of Akron
be amended to enact Section 100a
to add the City of Akron's existing
codified anti-discrimination
protections in the Charter; to, with
exceptions for sex offenders and
arson offenders, prohibit owners
and operators from declining to rent
to, or engage in real estate
transaction with, an individual on
the basis of that individual's criminal
history such as arrests, convictions,
incarceration; making individuals
with criminal histories a protected
class similar to race, gender, sexual
orientation, and other existing
protected classes as it relates to
renting, home sales, and other real
estate transactions; giving Akron
Civil Rights Commission jurisdiction
over all real estate discrimination
claims arising in Akron; and
requiring City Council to enact
implementing legislation within
ninety (90) days of the effective date
of this amendment?
YES
NO
