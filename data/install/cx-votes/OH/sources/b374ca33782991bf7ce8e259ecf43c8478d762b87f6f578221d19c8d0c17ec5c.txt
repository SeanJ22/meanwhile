Issue 11
Proposed Charter Amendment
City of Akron
A majority affirmative vote is
necessary for passage.

Shall the Charter of the City of Akron
be amended to enact Section 101b
so as to require property owners or
operators to permit security
deposits for residential rentals to be
paid over three or six month
periods; to require operators to
inform tenants or prospective
tenants of their right to make
security deposit payments over
three or six months; criminalizing
the failure to provide the
notification; banning fees, charges,
deposits or other payments prior to
executing rental agreements for
residential rental properties; and
criminalizing the collection of an
application fee for residential
rentals?
