Issue 13
Proposed Charter Amendment
City of Akron
A majority affirmative vote is
necessary for passage.

Shall Section 68a of the Charter of
the City of Akron be amended to
change the composition and board
member-appointment process of
the citizen's police oversight board
by reducing the number of
members appointed by City Council
and requiring the Board to select
one-third of its own members from
a preselected list provided by a
committee comprised of individuals
who are members of a "qualifying
civil rights organization"; to
establish procedures and timing
requirements for the Mayor, Chief
of Police, and City Council to
respond to recommendations made
by the Office of the Independent
Police Auditor; to explicitly
authorize the Office of the
Independent Police Auditor to issue
subpoenas compelling production
of records in the possession of
public or private entities or
individuals; and to authorize the
Office of Independent Police Auditor
to compel attendance and testimony
from private citizens and public
employees?
YES
NO
