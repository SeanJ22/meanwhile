26-265
Measure No:
Five-year Local Option Levy for Police, Fire & 911 Operations.
Caption:
Question:
Summary:
Shall Troutdale adopt a local option levy at $1.60 per $1,000 of assessed value for police, fire & 911
services? This measure may cause property taxes to increase more than three percent.
Troutdale provides Police, Fire, and 911 through contracts: Multnomah County Sheriff's Office, City of
Gresham Fire Department, and Bureau of Emergency Communications.
Costs of essential public safety services continually increase and are projected to rise faster than available
funding. Without a sustainable funding source, the gap between revenues and public safety costs will
become unsustainable, risking the long-term reliability of Police, Fire, and 911 services.
If this measure passes, the current Council will replace the $25 Public Safety Fee, with a voter-approved
local option levy of $1.60 per $1,000 of assessed property value.
The average Troutdale home assessed at approximately $267,800, would cost about $428.48 annually.
Because the Public Safety Utility Fee would be eliminated, the average homeowner would not pay the current
$300 annual fee, resulting in an estimated net annual cost increase of approximately $128.48 while
providing dedicated funding for Police, Fire, and 911 services.
The amount collected is estimated to be $3,556,392 in 2027-28, $3,663,084 in 2028-29, $3,772,977 in
2029-30, $3,886,166 in 2030-31, and $4,002,751 in 2031-32.
Public safety costs already exceed $10 million annually and are projected to surpass $14 million by 2030.
This levy ensures stable, reliable public safety funding for Troutdale’s future.
