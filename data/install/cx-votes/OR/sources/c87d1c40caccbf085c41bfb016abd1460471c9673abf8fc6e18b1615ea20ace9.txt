ADVISORY VOTE: Should the City proceed with planning and creation of a Troutdale Police Department?
This is a non-binding advisory vote and does not authorize or impose any tax, fee, or expenditure.
This is a non-binding advisory vote and does not authorize or impose any tax, fee, or expenditure.
In 2015, the City of Troutdale dissolved its Police Department and contracted with the Multnomah County
Sheriff's Office to provide police services. The decision was based, in part, on the expectation that
contracting would reduce long-term costs while maintaining public safety services.
Since then, the cost of contracted police services has increased, and the City has continued evaluating
service levels, operational needs, community expectations, and long-term financial sustainability. As part of
that review, the City has developed preliminary plans for the possible re-establishment of a Troutdale Police
Department.
This advisory vote asks whether the City should continue planning to create a Troutdale Police Department
while evaluating the costs, operational considerations, and benefits of both a municipal Police Department
and continued contracted police services.
The City Council will consider the results of this advisory vote when evaluating future police service options.
