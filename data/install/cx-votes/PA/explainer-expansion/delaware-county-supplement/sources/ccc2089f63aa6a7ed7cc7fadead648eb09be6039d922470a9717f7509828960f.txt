NOTICE TO VOTERS OF BETHEL TOWNSHIP IN DELAWARE COUNTY, PENNSYLVANIA
REFERENDUM QUESTION AT THE NOV. 3, 2026 GENERAL ELECTION
On the ballots at the General Election on Tuesday, November 3, 2026, the voters in all
precincts in Bethel Township, Delaware County, Pennsylvania will be presented with a
referendum question.
EXPLANTORY STATEMENT
In 2006 voters in Bethel Township instituted an annual tax on the assessed value of all
taxable real property situated in Bethel Township at the rate of .05 mills, which equates
to $5 per $100,000 of assessed value, to support Rachel Kohl Community Library (the
"Library Tax"). In 2021, when Delaware County reassessed the value of all taxable real
estate, state law required the Township to reduce the Library Tax millage to .027 mills.
At the current rate of .027 mills, the Library Tax costs $2.70 per $100,000 of assessed value
annually. The purpose of the foregoing question is to determine whether the Township
should increase the Library Tax to .037 mills. The proposed rate of .037 mills would
cost $3.70 per $100,000 of assessed value, an increase of $1.00 per $100,000 per year.
This would provide the Rachel Kohl Community Library (the "Library") with a total tax
revenue from Bethel Township of just over $5.00 per resident in Bethel Township, which is
the level required throughout Library's service area to qualify for Quality Libraries Aid, a
form of additional funding from the Commonwealth. The question shall appear on the
Bethel Township ballots as follows:
REFERENDUM: BETHEL TOWNSHIP
TO AID IN MAINTAINING THE
RACHEL KOHL LIBRARY
Do you favor increasing the annual
tax on the assessed value of all
taxable real property situated in
Bethel Township from .027 mills
to .037 mills to aid in maintaining
the Rachel Kohl Community
