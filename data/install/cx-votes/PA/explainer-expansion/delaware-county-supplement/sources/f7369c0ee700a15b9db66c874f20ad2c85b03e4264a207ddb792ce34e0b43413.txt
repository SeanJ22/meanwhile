NOTICE TO VOTERS OF DELAWARE COUNTY, PENNSYLVANIA
REFERENDUM QUESTION AT THE NOV. 3, 2026 GENERAL ELECTION
On the ballots at the General Election on Tuesday, November 3, 2026, the voters in all
precincts of Delaware County, Pennsylvania will be presented with a referendum question.
EXPLANATORY STATEMENT
This ballot question, if approved by the voters, would authorize the County to borrow up to
$120 million for projects that protect water quality, natural areas, and wildlife habitat within
the County. The projects could include purchasing land to preserve open space, creating
new parks, improving existing parks and outdoor recreation areas, building trails for
walking, hiking, and biking, and other projects related to environmental conservation and
protecting natural areas from development. If approved by the voters, the County could
choose to borrow all of the amount approved by the voters, or only some of the amount
approved, and can choose to fund some or all of the listed types of projects. If the voters
approve the question, the County commits to make full public disclosure of the projects
funded with the money it borrows and to provide annual audits by an independent auditor
of all spending. The question shall appear on the Delaware County ballots as follows:
