Wolf Township Board of Supervisors size

Should two additional supervisors be elected to serve in this township?

The purpose of the ballot question is to determine whether the electors of Wolf
Township favor increasing the size of the township’s Board of Supervisors to five
members from the current number of three members. Wolf Township would then
have five elected Supervisors instead of three elected Supervisors.

If the question is approved as a result of the number of “Yes” votes being greater
than the number of “No” votes, then one additional Supervisor would be elected
to a six-year term and one additional Supervisor would be elected to a four-year
term on the 2027 municipal ballot in order to effect the increase, in addition to the
normal number of Supervisors to be elected. Thereafter, Supervisors would be
elected corresponding to the number of Supervisors whose terms are scheduled to
expire on the first Monday of the following January.

If the question is not approved as a result of the number of “No” votes being
greater than or equal to the number of “Yes” votes, then the number of elected
Supervisors for Wolf Township would remain at three.
