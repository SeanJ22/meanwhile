10. CHARTER REVISIONS
Shall the Town Charter be revised in
the following Sections to remove
and/or insert words, terms, and/or
phrases to remove scrivener’s errors
and/or clarify, correct word usage,
terms, and phrases:
a. Preamble, be amended to remove
the words “and Providence
Plantations” from the name of the
State of Rhode Island, to conform
to the amendment of the Rhode
Island Constitution in November
2020?
b. Part 1, Establishment of Town;
Effect of Charter, Article V, Real
and Personal Property Held by
Town, § C-16 Granting Use of
Property; regulation for clarity to
who is authorized?
c. Part 3, Administrator, Article X,
Appointment and Qualifications,
§ C-41 Appointment; term,
Paragraph C., to insert the word
“of” to resolve a scrivener’s
drafting error?
d. Part 3, Administrator, Article XIV,
Acting Administrator, § C-46
Appointment for temporary
absences, to insert the word “to” to
resolve a scrivener’s drafting
error?
e. Part 5, (Reserved), Article XVIII,
Clerk and Deputy Clerk § C-51,
Paragraph A, to insert a comma “,”
after the word 'interviews' to
resolve a scrivener’s drafting
error?
f. Part 5, (Reserved), Article XIX,
Duties of Clerk, § C-53
Enumeration, Paragraph I, to insert
the word “of” to resolve a
scrivener’s drafting error?
g. Part 6 Finance, Article XXIII Tax
Assessment, § C-65, Duties of Tax
Assessor, to insert the word "in" to
resolve a scrivener's drafting
error?
h. Part 8, Inspectors, Article XXXII
Function of Building
Inspector/Zoning Official, § C-115,
Powers and Duties,
Sub-Paragraph F to delete a
comma "," between the words
'Housing' and 'Maintenance' to
resolve a scrivener's drafting
error?
i. Part 6 Finance, Article XXVII
Purchasing, § C-103, Policy,
Paragraph C, For construction
purchases, Sub-Paragraph (ii) to
clarify purchasing thresholds?
j. Part 6 Finance, Article XXVII
Purchasing, § C-103, Policy,
Paragraph D, For all other
purchases, Sub-Paragraph (ii) to
clarify purchasing thresholds?
k. Shall the Town Charter be
amended at Part 14, Commissions
and Boards, Article LIII, Planning
Commission, § C-174, Powers
and duties, at E. to remove the
requirement to provide a report
with the Rhode Island
Development Council as that
entity is no longer in existence?
