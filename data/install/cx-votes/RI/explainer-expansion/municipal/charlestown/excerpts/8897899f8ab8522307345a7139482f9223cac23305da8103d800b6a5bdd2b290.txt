8. ORDINANCES
Shall the Town Charter be amended
at Part 2, Town Council, Article IX,
Ordinances, § C-33, Introduction, to
provide, when allowable by law, a
summary of the proposed ordinance,
including a description of the matter
under consideration and the intent
and effect of the proposed ordinance
be published in a newspaper of
general circulation within the Town,
with the full text of the proposed
ordinance made available on the
Town’s official website? The
summary shall include directions on
where to locate the proposed
ordinance.
