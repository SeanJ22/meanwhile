7. PLANNING COMMISSION
Shall the Town Charter be amended
at Part 14, Commissions and Boards,
Article LIII, Planning Commission,
§ C-172 Establishment and
organization; compensation, to
provide for a Planning Commission
composed of five (5) members
elected at large on a non-partisan
basis for terms of four (4) years with
staggered terms, plus two (2)
alternate members elected on a
non-partisan basis for terms of two
(2) years, subject to ratification of
these amendments by special act of
the RI General Assembly?
