6. REAL ESTATE
CONVEYANCE TAX —
COVENTRY LAND TRUST
ALLOCATION
Amendment to Article VIII,
Section 8.16(d) of the Home
Rule Charter
(Vote of the Town Council on July
28, 2026)
Shall the Town of Coventry Home
Rule Charter be amended to reduce
the minimum percentage of Real
Estate Conveyance Tax receipts
required to be set aside annually for
the Coventry Land Trust for the
acquisition and preservation of open
space from no less than fifty percent
(50%) to no less than twenty-five
percent (25%), while retaining the
requirement that such funds be used
exclusively for capital expenditures
and not for administrative expenses,
and while permitting the Town
Council to appropriate a higher
percentage in its discretion?
Statement of Purpose:
This amendment would modify Article
VIII, Section 8.16(d) of the Home
Rule Charter, which governs the
allocation of Real Estate Conveyance
Tax receipts to the Coventry Land
Trust.
Under the current Charter, the Town
Council is required to set aside, as
part of each annual budget, an
amount equal to no less than fifty
percent (50%) of all monies remitted
to the Town by the Rhode Island Tax
Administrator under the Real Estate
Conveyance Tax, R.I. Gen. Laws
§ 44-25-1 et seq. Those funds must
be used exclusively by the Coventry
Land Trust for the acquisition and
preservation of open space, for
capital expenditures only, with no
portion available for administrative
expenses.
This amendment would reduce the
mandatory minimum allocation from
fifty percent (50%) to twenty-five
percent (25%). The Town Council
would retain the discretion to
appropriate more than twenty-five
percent (25%) in any given budget
year. All other conditions on the use
of the funds - exclusive use by the
Coventry Land Trust for open space
acquisition and preservation, capital
expenditures only, no administrative
use - would remain unchanged.
A vote to Approve would adopt this
Charter amendment, reducing the
minimum required allocation to
twenty-five percent (25%).
A vote to Reject would leave the
current fifty percent (50%) minimum
requirement unchanged.
