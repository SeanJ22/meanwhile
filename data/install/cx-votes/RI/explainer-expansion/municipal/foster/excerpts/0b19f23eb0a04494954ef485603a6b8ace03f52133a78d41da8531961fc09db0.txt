6. ARTICLE X, FINANCIAL
PROCEDURES
To move the Financial Town Meeting
from May to June and to remove
language regarding voting
procedures in conflict with State law.
Shall Article X, Sec. 10.02, entitled
"Financial Town Meeting", be
amended to read as follows?:
The electors of the Town shall
assemble each year in a Financial
Town Meeting on the first Tuesday in
June at 7:00 p.m. for the purpose of
ordering any tax which lawfully may
be ordered, making appropriations,
and transacting any other business
appertaining to the financial affairs of
the Town which legally may come
before such meeting.
