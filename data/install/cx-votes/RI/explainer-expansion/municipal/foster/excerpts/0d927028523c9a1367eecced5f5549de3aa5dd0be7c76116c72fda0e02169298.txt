7. ARTICLE X, FINANCIAL
PROCEDURES
To increase the appropriated amount
from two to four percent.
Shall Article X, Sec. 10.10, entitled
"Capital Reserve Fund", be amended
to read as follows?:
There shall be an annual addition to
the amount appropriated by the
Financial Town Meeting equivalent to
four percent (4%) of the total of all
other appropriations approved at the
meeting, as a capital reserve fund.
Such appropriation, and the addition
of the same to the amount to be
raised by taxation, shall be made by
operation of this section of the
Charter, without the necessity of any
vote or any other action by the Town.
Such amount and any accumulations
shall be held and invested by the
Finance Director as a separate fund,
and from time to time any amount
from such funds may be expended
upon authorization by the annual or a
special Financial Town Meeting for
the purchase of improvements set
forth in the capital program.
