8. ARTICLE X, FINANCIAL
PROCEDURES
To reduce the percentage of the
Land Use Conveyance Tax received
by the Foster Land Trust from 100%
to 50%.
Shall Article X, Sec. 10.17 of the
Foster Town Charter be amended to
read as follows?:
The Town Council shall set aide, as
part of each annual budget, no less
than 50% of all funds collected
pursuant to the Land Use
Conveyance Tax which are retained
by the Town of Foster as provided by
State law. Except as provided for in
Section 10.17(a), the funds shall be
used exclusively by the Foster Land
Trust for the acquisition and
preservation of open space pursuant
to the legislative Charter of said Land
Trust.
(a) The operating and
administrative budget of the
Foster Land Trust shall be
approved by the voters at the
annual Financial Town Meeting.
The approved amount shall be
reimbursed to the Town by the
Foster Land Trust from the funds
designated in section 10.17.
