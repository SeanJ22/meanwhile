8. BOARD OF ASSESSMENT
REVIEW MEMBERSHIP (Art. VII,
Sec. C7-9)
Shall Section C7-9 (Board of
Assessment Review) of the Home
Rule Charter be amended to increase
the membership of the Board of
Assessment Review from three
members to five members, each a
qualified elector of the Town
appointed by the Town Council, and
to change the term of office from six
years to three years, with the terms
so arranged that they expire on a
staggered basis?
