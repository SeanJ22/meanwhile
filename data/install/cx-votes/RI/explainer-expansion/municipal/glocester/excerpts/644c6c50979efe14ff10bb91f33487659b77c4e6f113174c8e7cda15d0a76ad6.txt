9. INCREASE CAPITAL
RESERVE FUND (Art. VIII, Sec.
C8-8)
Shall Section C8-8 (Capital Reserve
Fund) of the Home Rule Charter be
amended to increase the annual
capital reserve fund contribution from
2% to 4% of the operating budget,
phased in by one-quarter of one
percent (0.25%) per year over an
eight-year period beginning July 1,
2027?
