14. ARTICLE VII BOARDS AND
COMMISSIONS
Shall Article VII, Sections 703, 706,
707 and 710 be amended, and a new
Section 711 be added to change the
composition and qualifications for
members of the Conservation
Commission, change the name of the
Pension Committee to the Retiree
Benefits Committee and broaden the
scope of its duties and its
membership distribution, specifying
that appointees to the Harbor
Commission shall be qualified
electors and year-round residents of
Little Compton, specify the terms of
Housing Trust members, and add a
new section recognizing the Historic
Preservation Advisory Committee
and the purpose for which it was
created?
