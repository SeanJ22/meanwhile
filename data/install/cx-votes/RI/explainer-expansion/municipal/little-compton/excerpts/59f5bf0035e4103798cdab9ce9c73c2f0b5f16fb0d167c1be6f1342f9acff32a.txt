9. ARTICLE III FINANCIAL
TOWN MEETING
ORGANIZATION
Shall Article III, Sections 302, 304,
and 305 be amended to limit the time
to hold a recessed Financial Town
Meeting to no later than seven days,
require that a listing of the full text of
all warrant items be posted on the
Town’s website at least forty-eight
hours prior to the Financial Town
Meeting, set forth procedures for
notice, check-in and voting at the
Financial Town Meeting, timing for
recessed and special Financial Town
Meetings, and specify that the Town
Moderator shall coordinate with other
Town officials to ensure that there is
adequate space for full participation
by voters at the Financial Town
Meeting?
