16. ARTICLE X
APPOINTMENTS,
QUALIFICATIONS AND
VACANCIES
Shall Article X, Section 1006 be
amended to require that any member
of a board or commission who is
appointed to a Town office must
either relinquish their position on the
board or commission, or seek an
advisory opinion from the State
Ethics Commission, and further
define the criteria and reasons for
vacancies on boards or
commissions?
