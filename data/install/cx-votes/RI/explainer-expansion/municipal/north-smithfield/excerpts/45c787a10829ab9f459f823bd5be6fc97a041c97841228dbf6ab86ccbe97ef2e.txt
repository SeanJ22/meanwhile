7. PERSONNEL BOARD
DUTIES AND
RESPONSIBILITIES
(Article XIII, § 1)
Shall the Home Rule Charter of the
Town of North Smithfield be
amended by revising Article XIII,
Section 1 (Personnel Board) to
remove the existing prohibition that
members “shall not hold or be a
candidate for any other public office
or position” and related political
activity restrictions, and to instead
provide that Personnel Board
members shall not hold or be
candidates for elective office within
the Town of North Smithfield, with
resignation required upon candidacy,
while permitting such members to
hold or seek elective office in other
municipalities or at the state or
federal level as allowed by Rhode
Island law and also be amended to
reflect the accuracy of the current
duties of the Personnel Board which
have been modified since the last
update?
Explanation:
This amendment would update
Article XIII, Section 1 of the Charter
governing the Personnel Board by:
• Removing the current blanket
prohibition on holding or being a
candidate for any public office and
certain political activities;
• Replacing it with a narrower
restriction prohibiting Personnel
Board members from holding or
running for elective office within the
Town of North Smithfield;
• Requiring resignation from the
Board if a member becomes a
candidate for Town elective office;
• Allowing members to hold or seek
elective office in other
municipalities or at the state or
federal level, consistent with
Rhode Island law;
• The duties of the Personnel Board
have been updated to reflect the
actual responsibilities of the
Personnel Board, removing
responsibilities that are now
performed by different entities; and
• The amendment is intended to
prevent conflicts of interest within
Town government while aligning
the Charter with constitutional
requirements articulated in cases
such as Broadrick v. Oklahoma,
Magill v. Lynch, and Cranston
Teachers Alliance Local No. 1704
AFT v. Miele.
