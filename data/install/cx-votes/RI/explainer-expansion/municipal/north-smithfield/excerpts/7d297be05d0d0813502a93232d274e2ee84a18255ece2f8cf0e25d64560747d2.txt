13. PLANNING BOARD
POWERS AND
COMPREHENSIVE PLAN
(Article XII, § 2)
Shall Article XII, Section 2 of the
Home Rule Charter of the Town of
North Smithfield be amended to
provide that the Planning Board's
powers and duties include those set
forth in R.I. Gen. Laws § 45-22-7, and
to update public notice requirements
for Comprehensive Plan adoption or
amendment in accordance with R.I.
Gen. Laws § 45-22.2-8, including
expanded newspaper, municipal
posting, and website notice
requirements?
Explanation:
This amendment updates the
Planning Board's powers and duties
to include those specifically outlined
in state law, and revises public notice
requirements for adopting or
amending the town's Comprehensive
Plan. State law gives planning boards
extensive responsibilities including
preparing comprehensive plans,
overseeing land development
regulations, and conducting
community studies. The amendment
also updates notice requirements to
comply with state law, which requires
multiple forms of public notice
including newspaper publication for
three successive weeks, posting in
municipal buildings, and website
accessibility at least fourteen days
before hearings.
