10. TERMINOLOGY
CORRECTION
(Article III, § 8(c))
Shall Article III, Section 8(c) of the
Home Rule Charter of the Town of
North Smithfield be amended by
replacing the word "hiring" with the
word "borrowing" in reference to the
Town Council's authority to authorize
tax anticipation notes and related
municipal financing?
Explanation:
This is a simple word correction to fix
an error in the Charter. The current
Charter incorrectly uses the word
“hiring” when describing the Town
Council's authority to authorize tax
anticipation notes and municipal
financing. Since tax anticipation
notes are a form of municipal
borrowing, not hiring, the word should
be “borrowing”. This technical
correction aligns the Charter
language with the actual legal nature
of these financial instruments and
eliminates confusion.
