8. DEPARTMENT OF
FACILITIES AND CAPITAL
ASSETS
(Article XV, § 12)
Shall the North Smithfield Home Rule
Charter be amended to replace
Section 12 of Article XV with a new
Section, establishing a Department of
Facilities and Capital Assets, headed
by a Director of Facilities appointed
by the Town Administrator,
responsible for:
• Managing, maintaining, repairing,
and planning all municipal
buildings, grounds, and
infrastructure;
• Developing a Capital Improvement
Plan;
• Overseeing construction and
renovation projects;
• Managing utilities and energy
programs;
• Ensuring compliance with
environmental standards;
• Preparing and administering the
Department’s operating budget;
and to renumber the current
Section 12 as Section 13?
Explanation:
This amendment would replace
Section 12 of Article XV to establish a
Department of Facilities and Capital
Assets, headed by a Director of
Facilities. The Department would
provide professional oversight for:
• Maintenance, repair, and long-term
planning of municipal buildings,
grounds, and infrastructure;
• Capital projects, including
construction and renovation;
• Utilities management, energy
programs, and environmental
compliance;
• Preparation and administration of
the Department’s operating budget.
• The amendment also renumbers
the current Section 12 as Section
13 to maintain clear organization in
the Charter.
