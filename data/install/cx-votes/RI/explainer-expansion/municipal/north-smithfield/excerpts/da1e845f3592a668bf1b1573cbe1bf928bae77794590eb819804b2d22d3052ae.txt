15. ETHICS PROVISIONS
UPDATE
(Article XVI, § 8)
Shall Article XVI, Section 8 of the
Home Rule Charter of the Town of
North Smithfield be amended to
replace existing local ethics
provisions with a requirement that all
town officials and employees comply
with the Rhode Island Code of Ethics,
R.I. Gen. Laws § 36-14-1 et seq.?
Explanation:
This amendment would replace any
existing local ethics provisions in the
charter with a requirement that all
town officials and employees comply
with the Rhode lsland Code of Ethics.
The state Code of Ethics already
applies to all municipal officials and
employees, requiring adherence to
high ethical standards, avoiding
conflicts of interest, and not using
public positions for private gain. This
amendment would eliminate
potentially conflicting local ethics
rules and ensure the town operates
under the uniform statewide ethics
standards that apply to all Rhode
lsland municipalities.
