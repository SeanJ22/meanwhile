BONDS AND NOTES
(Local Acts 037/038 – Acts and

Shall an act passed at the January
2026 session of the General
Assembly entitled “An Act authorizing
the City of Pawtucket to provide for
the design, construction,
reconstruction, repair, and equipping
of streets, sidewalks, and other
transportation infrastructure
improvements including, but not
limited to, drainage and landscaping

improvement program for the two
fiscal years 2028 and 2029” be
approved?
