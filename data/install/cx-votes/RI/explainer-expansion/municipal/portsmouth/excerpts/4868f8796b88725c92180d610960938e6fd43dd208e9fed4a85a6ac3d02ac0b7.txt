7. NON-PARTISAN TOWN
COUNCIL AND SCHOOL
COMMITTEE ELECTIONS
(Resolution of the Town Council
adopted April 14, 2025)
Shall the offices of Town Council and
School Committee be non-partisan?
(Amends Art. II, Sec. 201 and Art. VII,
Sec 701)
Explanation:
If approved, Article II, Section 201,
and Article VII, Section 701, of the
Portsmouth Town Charter will be
amended to identify the offices of
Town Council and School Committee
as “non-partisan”, to remove party
designations from the ballot, and to
add text for a candidate placement
lottery for School Committee
candidates.
