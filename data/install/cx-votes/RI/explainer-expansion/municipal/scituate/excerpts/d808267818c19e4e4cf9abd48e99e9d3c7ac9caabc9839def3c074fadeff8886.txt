6. MOTIONS AT THE ANNUAL
FINANCIAL TOWN MEETING
(Chapters 315/316 – Public Laws
of 2026)
Shall an act, passed at the 2026
session of the General Assembly,
entitled "An Act regarding motions at
the Town of Scituate annual Financial
Town Meeting" be approved?
Explanation:
This act requires prior notice and
posting of motions to be made from
the floor at the annual Financial Town
Meeting of the Town of Scituate now
held on the first Tuesday in the
month of June. This requirement
does not apply to special meetings
held under R.I. Gen. Laws §45-3-6 or
§45-3-7.
