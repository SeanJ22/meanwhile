20. KEY PERFORMANCE
INDICATORS AND
INDEPENDENT ASSESSMENT
Shall the Town replace the current
five (5) year audit with a system
where each department tracks at
least five clear performance
measures and reports results every
year, with trends over time? This
system would also require an
independent review of selected
departments each year to check
accuracy and recommended
improvements. The independent firm
shall be selected by the Budget and
Financial Review Board. (Repeals
and replaces §C-5.20)
