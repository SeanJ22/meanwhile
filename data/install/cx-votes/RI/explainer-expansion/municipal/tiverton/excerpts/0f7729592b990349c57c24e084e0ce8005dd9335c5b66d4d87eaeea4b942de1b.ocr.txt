ARTICLE Ill ANNUAL BUDGET PROCESS/FINANCIAL TOWN REFERENDUM
Section 301(b)(6)

Ballot Question: Shall the Charter be amended to remove all references toa Budget Committee and its duties?

Reason for Change: In 2022, voters approved a change to the budget creation and adoption process and made the Budget
Committee an elected advisory committee to the Town Council. Since that approved change, persons have not sought to be
elected to the Committee, and it has failed to meet due to a lack of a quorum. Therefore, the Budget Committee's role has
become obsolete.

Effect of Change: The amendment will remove the Budget Committee as an elected body within the Town.
