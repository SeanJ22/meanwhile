QUESTION 12. ARTICLE X BOARDS AND COMMISSIONS
Section 1008 Tiverton Harbor and Coastal Waters Management Commission

Ballot Question: Shall the Charter be amended to reduce the number of members of the Tiverton Harbor and Coastal Waters
Management from nine (9) to seven (7) and eliminate the need from one of its members to also be a sitting member of the
Planning Board?

Reason for Change: The reduction in the number of members will allow the Commission to reduce its quorum to conduct
business. The position allocated for the Planning Board has not been filled for a number of years. Removing the requirement
will allow for the position to be filled by an active member.

Effect of Change: The Tiverton Harbor and Coastal Waters Management Commission will be reduced from nine (9) to seven
(7) members and no longer require that one of its members also be a member of the Planning Board.

Sec, 1008. Tiverton Harbor and Coastal Waters Management Commission.

The Tiverton Harbor and Coastal Waters Management Commission (THCWMC) shall consist of
nine-(9)seven (7) members appointed by the Town Council for two (2) year terms, staggered so
that no more than five-S}-four (4) terms expire in any one year. Fhe FHCWMS shall inelude-one
member-of the Planning Beard. Preference shall be given to representation by the following
special interests: Recreational boaters, recreational fishermen, commercial fishermen, riparian
property owners, and conservationists. In the event of a vacancy the Town Council shall appoint
a replacement from the same interest group for the duration of the unexpired term.

Effective Date: This Amendment will become effective upon approval of the voters on
November 3, 2026, and if necessary, subsequent ratification by the General Assembly.
