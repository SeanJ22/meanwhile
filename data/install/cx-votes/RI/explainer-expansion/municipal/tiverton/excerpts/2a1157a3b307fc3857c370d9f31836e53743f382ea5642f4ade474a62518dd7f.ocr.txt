QUESTION 7. ARTICLE VI FINANCIAL SERVICES
Sections 602 through 606

Ballot Question: Shall the Charter be amended to provide for the appointment of a Town Treasurer by the Town
Administrator, subject to Town Council approval, for a period not to exceed (3) three years, and update the position’s powers
and duties?

Reason for Change: Tiverton is only one of two Rhode Island communities that elects its Town Treasurer. As an elected
position, the only qualification necessary to seek the office is that the person be 18 years of age and a resident of the Town for
at least 30 days prior to the election. There is no guarantee that a person who may seek this elected office has the necessary
financial skills to manage the Town’s finances.

Effect of Change: The Town Treasurer will no longer be elected by the voters but will become an appointed position and serve
as a department head who reports to the Town Administrator.
