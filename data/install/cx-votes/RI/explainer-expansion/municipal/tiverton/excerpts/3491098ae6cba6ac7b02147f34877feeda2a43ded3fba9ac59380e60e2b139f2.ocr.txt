QUESTION 9. ARTICLE Vill TOWN OFFICIALS AND OFFICES
New Section 806

Ballot Question: Shall the Charter be amended to require that there be a Town Planner whom shall be appointed by the Town
Administrator, subject to Town Council approval, for an initial contract period not to exceed three (3) years?

Reason for Change: Given the pressures of development, environmental concerns, and economic growth, making the Town
Planner a charter required position will ensure that the Town has the necessary resources and oversight to help manage these
important issues.

Effect of Change: As a Charter mandated position, adoption of the amendment will require that a person be appointed to
ensure that the Town’s development is managed and overseen by a professional planner with the necessary qualifications.

2026 PROPOSED CHARTER AMENDMENTS
ARTICLE VIII TOWN OFFICIALS AND OFFICES
New Section 806: Town Planner

There shall be a town planner who shall be appointed by the Town Administrator, subject to the
approval of the Town Council, for an initial contract period not to exceed (3) three years.
Renewal contract shall not exceed (3) years. The qualifications for appointment for town
planner shall be determined by the Town Council pursuant to Section 407 (9) of this Charter.

(a) Duties and Responsibilities: The town planner shall work with and advise the Planning
Board regarding land use policies and procedures, the implementation of state law and local
regulations related to land development, economic growth and long-term planning related to land
use, sustainability, and environmental protection. The Town Planner shall balance the needs of
the community, environment, and local economy by reviewing proposals, analyzing data and
trends, and making recommendations to the Planning Board in order to ensure functional, safe,
and sustainable growth.

Effective Date: This section shall become effective upon approval of the voters on November 3,
2026, and, if necessary, subsequent ratification by the General Assembly.
