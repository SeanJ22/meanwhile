Section 204 Transfer of Land &
ARTICLE IV TOWN COUNCIL

Section 407 Powers and Duties

Ballot Question: Shall the Charter be amended to authorize the Town Council to sell, transfer, or lease land except land
owned by the Town for open space, recreation, or conservation purposes? Land owned by the Town for such purposes could
only be sold or leased with the approval of electors of the Town at a regular or special election.

Reason for Question: The current Charter language requires that any property rights or sale of land owned by the Town must
be approved by the voters at a Financial Town Referendum (FTR), which no longer exists. The Town often obtains interests in
land through tax sales, and those properties cannot be sold without FTR approval under the current Charter language.

Effect of Question:

The proposed amendments will clarify the authority of the Town Council to sell, transfer or lease land. Ina practical sense, the
amendments will provide the Town Council with the power to sell land acquired at tax sale or by other means without having to
go through the process of voter approval prior to its sale or lease. Land held and owned by the town for open space, recreation,
or conservation purposes could not be sold or leased without voter approval. This amendment is designed to allow the Town to
sell land it acquires from tax sales and other means and return it to the tax base and eliminate the need for voter approval for
such sales.
