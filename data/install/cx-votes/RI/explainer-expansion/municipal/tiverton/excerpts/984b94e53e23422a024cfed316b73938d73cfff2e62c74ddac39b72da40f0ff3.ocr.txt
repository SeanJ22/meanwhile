QUESTION 8. ARTICLE Vill TOWN OFFICIALS AND OFFICES
Section 801 Town Clerk

Ballot Question: Shall the Charter be amended to provide for the appointment of a Town Clerk by the Town Administrator,
subject to Town Council approval, for a period not to exceed three (3) years?

Reason for Change: The duties and responsibilities of a Town Clerk have become more specialized and detailed based on the
complexities of local governance. By making the position appointed, the appointing authority can ensure that the person has the
necessary qualifications, skills, experience, and temperament to perform the office’s important functions. As an elected position,
anyone who is at least 18 years of age and a resident of the Town for at least 30 days is now eligible to seek the office.

Effect of Change: The amendment would make the office of Town Clerk an appointed rather than an elected position within
Town government.
