QUESTION 17. ARTICLE XI SCHOOL DEPARTMENT
Section 1101 School Committee

Ballot Question: Shall the Charter be amended to clarify that the School Committee shall elect officers from its membership
(ie. chair and vice-chair) who shall serve at the pleasure of the School Committee?

Reason for Change: The language clarifies that the officers of the Committee must be from its elected members and that the
Chair and Vice-Chair serve at the pleasure of the majority of the Committee

Effect of Change: The updated language clarifies that the Chair and Vice Chair serve at the pleasure of the majority of the
membership and are chosen from the elected members of the School Committee.

2026 PROPOSED CHARTER AMENDMENTS

ARTICLE XI: SCHOOL DEPARTMENT

Sec. 1101. School Committee.

There shall be a non-partisan School Committee consisting of five (5) members, elected "at-large" for four (4)
year terms. Terms shall be staggered so that no more than three (3) expire every two (2) years. School Committee
members shall be elected at the General Election in even numbered years. School Committee members shall be
electors of the Town and shall not hold other elected town offices or be employees of the Town. Should a
Committee member fail to meet the above qualifications, or become disqualified from continuing in office under
the provisions of the State Constitution, State Law or this Charter, his/her office shall be deemed to have become
vacant. The vacancy shall be filled in the manner provided in the provisions of this Charter. The School Committee
shall anntatty elect from its members a Chairperson, Vice Chairperson, and such other officers as they deem
appropriate, each of whom shall serve at the pleasure of the committee: Copies of the minutes of School
Committee meetings shall be on file in the office of the Town Clerk.

Effective Date: This Charter Amendment shall become effective upon the approval of the voters on
November 3, 2026, and if necessary, subsequent ratification by the General Assembly.
