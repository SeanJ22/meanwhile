10. ARTICLE II ELECTIONS
Section 204 Transfer of Land &
ARTICLE IV TOWN COUNCIL
Section 407 Powers and Duties
Shall the Charter be amended to
authorize the Town Council to sell,
transfer, or lease land except land
owned by the Town for open space,
recreation, or conservation
purposes? Land owned by the Town
for such purposes could only be sold
or leased with the approval of
electors of the Town at a regular or
special election.
