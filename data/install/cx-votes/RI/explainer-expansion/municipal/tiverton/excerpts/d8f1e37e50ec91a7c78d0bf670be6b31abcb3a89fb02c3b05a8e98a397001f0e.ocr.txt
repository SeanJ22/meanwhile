QUESTION 6. ARTICLE IV TOWN COUNCIL
Section 401 Organization

Ballot Question: Shall the Charter be amended to increase the term of Town Council members from two (2) years to four (4)
years and further provide for staggered terms so that no more than four (4) members are eligible for re-election each election
cycle commencing November 7, 2028?

Reason for Change: In order to provide stability and continuity in the Town Council, a four-year council term is created along
with staggered terms. A longer term is sought to provide those elected a meaningful opportunity to learn about the operation
and structure of local government and provide continuity for better management and leadership.

Effect of Change: The proposed amendment would not take effect until the election of November 7, 2028. It would increase
the current term of the Town Council from two (2) years to four (4) years and provide for staggered terms.
