QUESTION 15. ARTICLE X BOARD AND COMMISSIONS
Section 1002 Zoning Board

Ballot Question: Shall the Charter provision related to the Zoning Board be amended to reflect changes in state law?

Reason for Change: Because of changes in state law related to the powers and duties of the Zoning Board and the transfer of
certain appeals from the Planning Board, the charter amendments are designed to conform the local charter language to those
laws as well as clarify some of the existing language.

Effect of Change: The changes will ensure that the Charter language is consistent with the requirements of state law.
