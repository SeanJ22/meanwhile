QUESTION 6. ARTICLE IV TOWN COUNCIL
Section 401 Organization

Ballot Question: Shall the Charter be amended to increase the term of Town Council members from two (2) years to four (4)
years and further provide for staggered terms so that no more than four (4) members are eligible for re-election each election
cycle commencing November 7, 2028?

Reason for Change: In order to provide stability and continuity in the Town Council, a four-year council term is created along
with staggered terms. A longer term is sought to provide those elected a meaningful opportunity to learn about the operation
and structure of local government and provide continuity for better management and leadership.

Effect of Change: The proposed amendment would not take effect until the election of November 7, 2028. It would increase
the current term of the Town Council from two (2) years to four (4) years and provide for staggered terms.

Ballot Question: Shall the Charter be amended to provide for the appointment of a Town Treasurer by the Town
Administrator, subject to Town Council approval, for a period not to exceed (3) three years, and update the position’s powers

Effect of Change: The Town Treasurer will no longer be elected by the voters but will become an appointed position and serve
as a department head who reports to the Town Administrator.

New Section 806

Ballot Question: Shall the Charter be amended to require that there be a Town Planner whom shall be appointed by the Town
Administrator, subject to Town Council approval, for an initial contract period not to exceed three (3) years?

Reason for Change: Given the pressures of development, environmental concerns, and economic growth, making the Town

important issues.

Section 204 Transfer of Land &
ARTICLE IV TOWN COUNCIL

Section 407 Powers and Duties

Ballot Question: Shall the Charter be amended to authorize the Town Council to sell, transfer, or lease land except land
owned by the Town for open space, recreation, or conservation purposes? Land owned by the Town for such purposes could
only be sold or leased with the approval of electors of the Town at a regular or special election.

Reason for Question: The current Charter language requires that any property rights or sale of land owned by the Town must
be approved by the voters at a Financial Town Referendum (FTR), which no longer exists. The Town often obtains interests in
land through tax sales, and those properties cannot be sold without FTR approval under the current Charter language.

Effect of Question:

The proposed amendments will clarify the authority of the Town Council to sell, transfer or lease land. Ina practical sense, the
amendments will provide the Town Council with the power to sell land acquired at tax sale or by other means without having to
go through the process of voter approval prior to its sale or lease. Land held and owned by the town for open space, recreation,
or conservation purposes could not be sold or leased without voter approval. This amendment is designed to allow the Town to
sell land it acquires from tax sales and other means and return it to the tax base and eliminate the need for voter approval for
such sales.
