9. GENERAL PROVISIONS -
ADOPTION OF ORDINANCES
(Art. XV, Ch. II, §2(b) “Procedure
for Adoption of Ordinances")
Shall Section 15-2-2(b) of the
Westerly Town Charter be amended
to provide newspaper publication of
proposed ordinances in a summary
format in a newspaper of general
circulation when newspaper
publication is required by state law
and further amended to provide for
electronic notice of a proposed
ordinance when newspaper
publication is not required by state
law?
If approved, Section 15-2-2(b) of
the Westerly Town Charter would
be amended as follows:
When newspaper publication is
required by state law, the Town Clerk
shall publish in a newspaper having a
general circulation in the Town, a
summary of the proposed ordinance
which summary shall identify the
section and any subsections to be
adopted, repealed or amended which
said publication shall contain a notice
setting out the time and place for a
public hearing thereon and for its
consideration by the Council. The
public hearing shall be at least seven
days after the publication and may be
held separately or in conjunction with
a regular or special Council meeting
and may be adjourned from time to
time.
When newspaper publication of a
proposed ordinance is not required
by state law, the Town Clerk shall
make electronic public notice of the
proposed ordinance in the same
manner as provided for above on the
Town's official website and such
other available public digital
platforms as may be necessary to
assure public access to said
electronic notice. When notice
required by this section is electronic
or by newspaper, said proposed
ordinance shall be published on the
Town's official website in the form
required for final adoption at least
seven days prior to the public hearing
provided for herein.
