8. SCHOOL COMMITTEE
VACANCIES
(Art. XI, Ch. I, §3 “Vacancies”)
Shall Section 11-1-3 of the Westerly
Town Charter be amended to provide
that when a vacancy occurs in the
membership of the School
Committee, the replacement shall
serve until the next scheduled
General Election for the remainder of
that term?
If approved, Section 11-1-3 of the
Westerly Town Charter would be
amended as follows:
Whenever a vacancy shall occur in
the membership of the School
Committee by death, removal, out of
town, resignation, or by neglect or
refusal to qualify, or for any other
cause, the Council shall offer the
right to assume the office
sequentially to the first, second, or
third runner up candidates from the
preceding election to serve until the
next scheduled General Election for
the remainder of that term. If none of
the three runner up candidates from
the preceding election assumes the
office, within 30 days of the date of
the vacancy, the Council shall by
majority vote appoint a qualified
person to the office to serve the
remainder of the vacant office's term.
