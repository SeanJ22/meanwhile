7. SCHOOL COMMITTEE
(Art. XI, Ch. I, §1 “School
Committee”)
Shall Section 11-1-1 of the Westerly
Town Charter be amended to
establish that no member of the
School Committee shall serve more
than two consecutive terms?
If approved, Section 11-1-1 of the
Westerly Town Charter would be
amended as follows:
The School Committee shall consist
of seven (7) members elected at
large to serve a term of four (4) years
and until each one's successor is
elected and qualified. Members shall
be elected in staggered fashion and
no member shall serve more than
two (2) consecutive terms.
