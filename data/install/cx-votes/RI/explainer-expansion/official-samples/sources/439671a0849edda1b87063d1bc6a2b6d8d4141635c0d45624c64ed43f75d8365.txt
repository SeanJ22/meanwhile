6. TOWN COUNCIL TERMS
(Article II, § 2 and Article IV, § 1)
Shall the North Smithfield Home Rule
Charter be amended to modify
Article II, Section 2 and Article IV,
Section 1 to provide that,
commencing December 1, 2028:
• The Town Council shall consist of
five (5) members elected at-large
at regular biennial elections in
even-numbered years. Except as
provided below for the 2028
regular election, each member
shall serve a four-year term and
until a successor is elected and
qualified, and at each regular
election only those seats whose
terms are expiring shall be filled;
• At the 2028 regular election only,
the three (3) candidates receiving
the highest number of votes shall
be elected to four-year terms
expiring December 1, 2032, and
the two (2) candidates receiving
the next highest number of votes
shall be elected to two-year terms
expiring December 1, 2030, so that
Town Council terms are staggered
thereafter;
• The existing Charter provision
limiting elected officials to eight (8)
consecutive years in the same
elected office shall remain in effect;
• A person who has served eight (8)
consecutive years in the same
elected office shall remain
ineligible to seek election to that
office until at least four (4) years
have elapsed following the
expiration of such service; and
• As a one-time transition to
staggered Town Council terms,
any Town Council member elected
in 2028 shall be permitted to serve
the full term to which elected
notwithstanding the
eight-consecutive-year limitation,
and any Town Council member
elected to a two-year term in 2028
shall be eligible to seek election to
a four-year Town Council term at
the 2030 regular election, with
such two-year term not counted
toward the Charter’s
eight-consecutive-year limitation?
Explanation:
This amendment would modify
Articles II and IV of the Home Rule
Charter.
The amendment would establish
staggered four-year terms for
members of the Town Council
beginning December 1, 2028. At the
2028 regular election, the three
candidates receiving the highest
number of votes would
serve four-year terms ending in 2032
and the two candidates receiving the
next highest number of votes would
serve two-year terms ending in 2030.
Thereafter, all Town Council seats
would be filled for staggered
four-year terms, with three seats
elected in 2032 and every four years
after that, and two seats elected in
2030 and every four years after that.
The amendment would retain the
existing Charter provision limiting
elected officials to eight consecutive
years in the same elected office and
would continue to require a four-year
break in service before a person who
has reached that limit may again
seek election to the same office.
The amendment would also establish
a one-time transition provision to
implement staggered Town Council
terms. Under this provision, any
Town Council member elected in
2028 would be permitted to complete
the term to which elected even if
such service would otherwise exceed
the Charter’s eight-consecutive-year
limitation. In addition, a Town Council
member elected to a two-year
transitional term in 2028 would be
eligible to seek election to a four-year
term in 2030, and the transitional
two-year term would not be counted
toward the Charter’s
eight-consecutive-year limitation.
Approve
Reject

7. PERSONNEL BOARD
DUTIES AND
RESPONSIBILITIES
(Article XIII, § 1)
Shall the Home Rule Charter of the
Town of North Smithfield be
amended by revising Article XIII,
Section 1 (Personnel Board) to
remove the existing prohibition that
members “shall not hold or be a
candidate for any other public office
or position” and related political
activity restrictions, and to instead
provide that Personnel Board
members shall not hold or be
candidates for elective office within
the Town of North Smithfield, with
resignation required upon candidacy,
while permitting such members to
hold or seek elective office in other
municipalities or at the state or
federal level as allowed by Rhode
Island law and also be amended to
reflect the accuracy of the current
duties of the Personnel Board which
have been modified since the last
update?
Explanation:
This amendment would update
Article XIII, Section 1 of the Charter
governing the Personnel Board by:
• Removing the current blanket
prohibition on holding or being a
candidate for any public office and
certain political activities;
• Replacing it with a narrower
restriction prohibiting Personnel
Board members from holding or
running for elective office within the
Town of North Smithfield;
• Requiring resignation from the
Board if a member becomes a
candidate for Town elective office;
• Allowing members to hold or seek
elective office in other
municipalities or at the state or
federal level, consistent with
Rhode Island law;
• The duties of the Personnel Board
have been updated to reflect the
actual responsibilities of the
Personnel Board, removing
responsibilities that are now
performed by different entities; and
• The amendment is intended to
prevent conflicts of interest within
Town government while aligning
the Charter with constitutional
requirements articulated in cases
such as Broadrick v. Oklahoma,
Magill v. Lynch, and Cranston
Teachers Alliance Local No. 1704
AFT v. Miele.
Approve
Reject

9. BOARD OF CANVASSERS
AND REGISTRATION
(Article II, § 11)
Shall Article II, Section 11 of the
Home Rule Charter of the Town of
North Smithfield be amended to
provide that members of the Board of
Canvassers and Registration are
appointed by the Town Council rather
than elected by the Town Council, in
accordance with R.I. Gen. Laws
§ 17-8-1 requiring a bipartisan
canvassing authority appointed by
the municipal legislative body?
Explanation:
Currently, North Smithfield's Town
Council “elects” members to the
Board of Canvassers and
Registration through an internal
council vote. This amendment would
change the process to “appointment”
by the Town Council, which aligns
with State law requirements. Under
Rhode lsland law, the Town Council
must appoint a three-member
bipartisan canvassing authority with
no more than two members from the
same political party (R.I. Gen. Law
§ 17-8-1). The Council President
nominates candidates from lists
submitted by political party
committees, and the Town Council
approves the appointments. This
ensures proper bipartisan
representation in election oversight
and brings the Charter into
compliance with state requirements.
Approve
Reject
10. TERMINOLOGY
CORRECTION
(Article III, § 8(c))
Shall Article III, Section 8(c) of the
Home Rule Charter of the Town of
North Smithfield be amended by
replacing the word "hiring" with the
word "borrowing" in reference to the
Town Council's authority to authorize
tax anticipation notes and related
municipal financing?
Explanation:
This is a simple word correction to fix
an error in the Charter. The current
Charter incorrectly uses the word
“hiring” when describing the Town
Council's authority to authorize tax
anticipation notes and municipal
financing. Since tax anticipation
notes are a form of municipal
borrowing, not hiring, the word should
be “borrowing”. This technical
correction aligns the Charter
language with the actual legal nature
of these financial instruments and
eliminates confusion.
Approve
Reject

(Article IV, § 9)
Shall Article IV, Section 9 of the
Home Rule Charter of the Town of
North Smithfield be amended to allow
public notice of proposed ordinances
and regulations to be published
electronically on the Secretary of
State's website in lieu of newspaper
publication, in accordance with R.I.
Gen. Laws § 45-6-1(c)?
Explanation:
Currently, when the town wants to
adopt or change ordinances and
regulations, it must publish advance
notice in local newspapers. This
amendment would allow the town to
publish these notices electronically
on the Secretary of State's official
website instead of in newspapers.
Rhode lsland law specifically permits
municipalities to use electronic notice
on the Secretary of State's website in
lieu of newspaper publication (R.I.
Gen. Laws § 45-6-1(c)). This
modernizes the notification process
while maintaining public access to
information about proposed local
laws, and may reduce costs
associated with newspaper
publication requirements.
Approve
Reject

§ 45-10-4, including requiring annual

accountants and providing that
selection of auditors is subject to
approval by the Rhode Island Auditor

This amendment updates the charter
to conform with current state audit
requirements. Rhode lsland law
requires each municipality to hire
independent certified public

annual audits of the town's financial
records (R.I. Gen. Laws § 45-10-4).
The law also requires that the
selection of auditors must receive
final approval from the Rhode lsland
Auditor General before the town can
enter into a contract for audit services
(R.I. Gen. Laws § 45-10-4). This
amendment ensures North
Smithfield's Charter reflects these
state-mandated audit procedures and
oversight requirements.

Home Rule Charter of the Town of
North Smithfield be amended to
clarify that the powers and duties of
the School Committee are in addition
to those set forth in R.I. Gen. Laws
§ 16-2-9?
Explanation:
This amendment clarifies that the
School Committee's powers and
duties include those provided under
state law. Rhode lsland law gives
school committees comprehensive
authority over public school interests,
including educational governance,
personnel decisions, and financial
management. However, there is an
important legal consideration: under
Rhode lsland Supreme Court
precedent, charter provisions
affecting school committees may
require express ratification by the
General Assembly to be fully
effective, since school committees
are considered agents of the state
rather than purely local bodies.

Reject

under the uniform statewide ethics
