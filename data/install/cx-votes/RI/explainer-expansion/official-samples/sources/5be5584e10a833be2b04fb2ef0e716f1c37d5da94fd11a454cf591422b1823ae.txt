OF ELEMENTARY SCHOOL ON
SINGLE CAMPUS

Resolves of 2026)

session of the General Assembly
entitled “An Act authorizing the
Chariho Regional School District to
finance the construction, furnishing,
and equipping of an elementary
school serving the towns of
Charlestown, Richmond, and
Hopkinton, consisting of one or more
buildings on a single campus, and
improvements at the Switch Road
Campus, including but not limited to
costs of land acquisition, demolition,
design, health and safety projects,
playgrounds, landscaping, paving,
and all expenses incidental thereto,
by the issuance of not more than
$116,000,000 bonds and/or notes

housing aid at a reimbursement rate

61% at the time of issuance, which
rate or ratio may increase to 65% at
project completion” be approved?

constructed so as to provide safe,
energy efficient, and cost-effective
schools in accordance with Northeast
Collaborative for High Performance
Schools standards to reduce
long-term operating costs and
support healthy learning
environments for students and staff.

Reject
