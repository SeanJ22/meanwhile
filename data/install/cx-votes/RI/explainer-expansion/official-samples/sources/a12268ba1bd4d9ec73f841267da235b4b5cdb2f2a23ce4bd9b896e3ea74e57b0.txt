Questions 6-8
Amendments to the Town of
Foster Home Rule Charter
Art. XIII, Sec. 8 of R.I.
Constitution
(Approved by the Foster Town
Council on July 16, 2026)
6. ARTICLE X, FINANCIAL
PROCEDURES
To move the Financial Town Meeting
from May to June and to remove
language regarding voting
procedures in conflict with State law.
Shall Article X, Sec. 10.02, entitled
"Financial Town Meeting", be
amended to read as follows?:
The electors of the Town shall
assemble each year in a Financial
Town Meeting on the first Tuesday in
June at 7:00 p.m. for the purpose of
ordering any tax which lawfully may
be ordered, making appropriations,
and transacting any other business
appertaining to the financial affairs of
the Town which legally may come
before such meeting.
Approve
Reject
7. ARTICLE X, FINANCIAL
PROCEDURES
To increase the appropriated amount
from two to four percent.
Shall Article X, Sec. 10.10, entitled
"Capital Reserve Fund", be amended
to read as follows?:
There shall be an annual addition to
the amount appropriated by the
Financial Town Meeting equivalent to
four percent (4%) of the total of all
other appropriations approved at the
meeting, as a capital reserve fund.
Such appropriation, and the addition
of the same to the amount to be
raised by taxation, shall be made by
operation of this section of the
Charter, without the necessity of any
vote or any other action by the Town.
Such amount and any accumulations
shall be held and invested by the
Finance Director as a separate fund,
and from time to time any amount
from such funds may be expended
upon authorization by the annual or a
special Financial Town Meeting for
the purchase of improvements set
forth in the capital program.
Approve
Reject
8. ARTICLE X, FINANCIAL
PROCEDURES
To reduce the percentage of the
Land Use Conveyance Tax received
by the Foster Land Trust from 100%
to 50%.
Shall Article X, Sec. 10.17 of the
Foster Town Charter be amended to
read as follows?:
The Town Council shall set aide, as
part of each annual budget, no less
than 50% of all funds collected
pursuant to the Land Use
Conveyance Tax which are retained
by the Town of Foster as provided by
State law. Except as provided for in
Section 10.17(a), the funds shall be
used exclusively by the Foster Land
Trust for the acquisition and
preservation of open space pursuant
to the legislative Charter of said Land
Trust.
(a) The operating and
administrative budget of the
Foster Land Trust shall be
approved by the voters at the
annual Financial Town Meeting.
The approved amount shall be
reimbursed to the Town by the
Foster Land Trust from the funds
designated in section 10.17.
Approve
Reject
