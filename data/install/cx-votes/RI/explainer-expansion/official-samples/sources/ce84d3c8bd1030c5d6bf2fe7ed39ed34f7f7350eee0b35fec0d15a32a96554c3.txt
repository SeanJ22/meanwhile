6. SCHOOL CONSTRUCTION
AND RENOVATION PROJECTS
$41,000,000
BONDS AND NOTES
(Local Acts 017/018 - Acts and
Resolves of 2026)
Shall an act, passed at the 2026
session of the General Assembly,
entitled “An Act authorizing the Town
of Portsmouth to issue not to exceed
$41,000,000 general obligation
bonds, notes, and other evidences of
indebtedness to finance the
construction, additions, renovation,
improvement, alteration, repair,
furnishing, and equipping of schools
and school facilities throughout the
Town" be approved?
Explanation:
If approved, the Town shall borrow an
amount not to exceed $41,000,000
for the above school projects. All
school projects included in the
Necessity of School Construction
Stage II application approved by the
Council on Elementary and
Secondary Education and funded by
the bond will be eligible for a
minimum of 35% Housing Aid
Reimbursement.
Approve
Reject

7. NON-PARTISAN TOWN
COUNCIL AND SCHOOL
COMMITTEE ELECTIONS
(Resolution of the Town Council
adopted April 14, 2025)
Shall the offices of Town Council and
School Committee be non-partisan?
(Amends Art. II, Sec. 201 and Art. VII,
Sec 701)
Explanation:
If approved, Article II, Section 201,
and Article VII, Section 701, of the
Portsmouth Town Charter will be
amended to identify the offices of
Town Council and School Committee
as “non-partisan”, to remove party
designations from the ballot, and to
add text for a candidate placement
lottery for School Committee
candidates.
