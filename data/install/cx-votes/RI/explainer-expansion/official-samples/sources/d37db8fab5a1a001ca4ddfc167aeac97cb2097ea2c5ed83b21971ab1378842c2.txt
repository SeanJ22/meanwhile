Questions 6-17
Amendments to the Little
Compton Home Rule Charter
(Resolution of the Little Compton
Town Council adopted on July 30,
2026)
6. ARTICLE I INCORPORATION
AND POWERS OF THE TOWN
Shall Article I, Sections 102 and 103
be amended to specify that the Town
shall comply with all ordinances,
regulations and state and federal law,
as well as include that the sale or
lease of real and intangible property
be approved by the Financial Town
Meeting and that Town-owned
property must be maintained in
compliance with current local and
state building codes?
Approve
Reject
7. ARTICLE II ELECTIONS
Shall Article II, Section 202 be
amended to include a six-year term
for alternate members to the Board of
Canvassers?
Approve
Reject
8. ARTICLE III FINANCIAL
PROCEDURES
Shall Article III, Sections 301 and 306
be amended to specify how monies
can be expended from the
unassigned portion of the general
fund, including revised oversight and
guidelines of the general fund as well
as setting an earlier date to submit
citizen strokes, set review
requirements of citizen stroke
requests and include language for
compliance with the state maximum
tax levy law as well as authorize the
Board of Canvassers to designate
the time for voters to be admitted to
the Financial Town Meeting?
Approve
Reject
9. ARTICLE III FINANCIAL
TOWN MEETING
ORGANIZATION
Shall Article III, Sections 302, 304,
and 305 be amended to limit the time
to hold a recessed Financial Town
Meeting to no later than seven days,
require that a listing of the full text of
all warrant items be posted on the
Town’s website at least forty-eight
hours prior to the Financial Town
Meeting, set forth procedures for
notice, check-in and voting at the
Financial Town Meeting, timing for
recessed and special Financial Town
Meetings, and specify that the Town
Moderator shall coordinate with other
Town officials to ensure that there is
adequate space for full participation
by voters at the Financial Town
Meeting?
Approve
Reject
10. ARTICLE IV TOWN
COUNCIL
Shall Article IV, Sections 405, 408,
and 413 be amended to make
grammatical corrections, include new
requirements for the calling of a
special Council meeting, add
electronic notifications of meetings to
Council members and set forth
additional duties for the Council
regarding codifying ordinances, the
conduct of audits and oversight of
Town officials, departments,
agencies and financial statements
review and reporting?
Approve
Reject
11. ARTICLE V FINANCIAL
PROCEDURES – BUDGET
COMMITTEE
RESPONSIBILITIES
Shall Article V, Sections 501, 502,
503, and 504 be amended to include
definitions for greater transparency
for financial procedures, including a
budget timeline, budget committee
procedures, reporting, financial
forecasting, public hearing notice by
mail and electronic means and
timeline?
Approve
Reject
12. ARTICLE V FINANCIAL
PROCEDURES – TOWN FUNDS
Shall Article V, Sections 505, 510,
511 and 512 be amended to require
the adoption of a five year capital
improvement plan, specify that the
Finance Director is responsible for
yearly reports on transfers of funds,
changing the name of the Council
Discretionary Fund to the Town
Council Incidental and Emergency
Budget and changing the name of the
Reserve Fund to the Town
Contingency Fund including
requirements for the operation of the
Contingency Fund?
Approve
Reject
