                 Ed. Capital Imp. & Use Tax Referendum 
             
             
                 
                     Election 
                     Statewide General Election 
                 
                 
                     Election Date 
                     
11/3/2026                     
                 
                 
                     Responsible County 
                     UNION 
                 
                 
                     District Type 
                     
                 
                 
                     District

                     Local Questions 
                 
                 
                     Referendum Text 
                     
                        Must a special one percent sales and use tax be imposed in Union County for not more than fifteen (15) years with the revenue of the tax used to pay, directly or indirectly, the cost of some or all of the following education capital improvement projects in Union County: 
Improving, renovating, replacing, converting, equipping, furnishing and updating (including land acquisition, demolition, and utility, parking and traffic infrastructure, if necessary) any of the following facilities:  
1.	Monarch Elementary School
2.	Foster Park Elementary School
3.	Buffalo Elementary School
4.	Jonesville Elementary/Middle School
5.	Sims Middle School
6.	Union County High School
7.	Union County Career Center
8.	Union County Achievement Academy?

If the voter wishes to vote in favor of the question, select “Yes, In Favor of the Question”; if the voter wishes to vote against the question, select “No, Opposed to the Question.”

                         Yes, In Favor of the Question
