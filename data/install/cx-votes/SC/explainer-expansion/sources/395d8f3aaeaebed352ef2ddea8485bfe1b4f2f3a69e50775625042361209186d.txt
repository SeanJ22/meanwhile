                 Town of Salem Municipal Tax Referendum 
             
             
                 
                     Election 
                     Statewide General Election 
                 
                 
                     Election Date 
                     
11/3/2026                     
                 
                 
                     Responsible County 
                     OCONEE 
                 
                 
                     District Type 
                     Municipality 
                 
                 
                     District 
                     Salem 
                 
                 
                     Referendum Type 
                     Local Questions 
                 
                 
                     Referendum Text 
                     
                        Must a one percent (1%) sales and use tax be levied in the Town of Salem for no more than eight (8) years for the purpose of allowing a credit against certain municipal ad valorem tax liability and for the purpose of raising the amounts specified for the following purposes?

1.	$230,000 - Main Street repair and revitalization — design and construction for curbing, sidewalks, drainage, and retaining wall (Rock Wall).

2.	$192,000 - Town Hall updates, modernization, repairs, and renovation — design, construction, and creation of additional work space for government services.

3.	$63,000 — Basketball and Tennis courts - structural upgrades, ADA compliance, and resurfacing.

4.	$62,000 — Municipal parking lot and Salem Town Park repaving.

5.	$23,000 — Design and construction of new paved parking area at the Salem Police Department.

Total: $570,000.

YES [ ] NO [ ]

INSTRUCTIONS TO VOTERS: All qualified electors of the Town of Salem desiring to vote in favor of imposing the tax for the stated purpose of allowing a credit against certain municipal ad valorem tax liability (municipal property taxes) and for the purpose of raising the amount specified above shall vote "YES," and all qualified electors opposed to levying the tax shall vote "NO."
