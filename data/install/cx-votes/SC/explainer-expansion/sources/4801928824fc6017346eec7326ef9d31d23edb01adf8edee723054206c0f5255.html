Capital Project Sales Tax Act

Election Statewide General Election
Election Date 11/3/2026
Responsible County SALUDA
District Type
District
Referendum Type Local Questions
Referendum Text
Must a special one percent (1%) sales and use tax be reimposed in Saluda County, South Carolina for seven (7) years, beginning May 1, 2027, to raise funds to be used to pay for the following capital improvement projects: Project Description Amount Construction of a Saluda County Recreation and Wellness Center $5,700,000 Construction of a new fire station in the Duncan area of Saluda County $800,000 Construction of a new fire substation on Fruit Hill Road in Saluda County $230,000 Improvements to and expansion of the current Saluda County Sheriff’s Office $3,500,000 Improvements to the Saluda County recreation area on Webb Road $500,000 Construction of a new Town of Saluda fire department/station $1,750,000 Construction of a Saluda County Emergency Medical System (EMS) substation in the Traffic Circle area of Saluda County $1,150,000 Construction of a Saluda County Emergency Medical System (EMS) substation in the Town of Ridge Spring $1,150,000 Construction of an extension of the Saluda County Water and Sewer Authority sewer line(s) to the Traffic Circle located in Saluda County $3,013,750 Total $17,793,750.00 and pending the receipt of such sales and use tax revenue, must Saluda County be authorized to issue and sell general obligation bonds of Saluda County in an aggregate principal amount not exceeding $18,000,000.00 to defray the costs of the capital projects described above plus issuance costs, and to pledge to the payment of the principal of and interest on such bonds the receipts of the sales and use tax authorized hereby, provided that in the event such sales and use tax is inadequate for the payment of such bonds, such bonds shall be payable from an ad valorem tax imposed on all taxable property in Saluda County? INSTRUCTIONS TO VOTERS: All qualified electors of the County desiring to vote in favor of reimposing the tax for the stated purposes outlined above shall vote “YES,” and all qualified electors opposed to levying the tax shall vote “NO.” Yes [ ] No [ ]
