                 McCormick School District Referendum 
             
             
                 
                     Election 
                     Statewide General Election 
                 
                 
                     Election Date 
                     
11/3/2026                     
                 
                 
                     Responsible County 
                     MCCORMICK 
                 
                 
                     District Type 
                     
                 
                 
                     District 
                     
                 
                 
                     Referendum Type 
                     Local Questions 
                 
                 
                     Referendum Text 
                     
                        Shall the Board of Trustees of McCormick County School District, South Carolina (the
“School District”) be empowered to issue, in one or more series, in one or more years,
general obligation bonds of the School District, in a principal amount of not exceeding
$18,500,000, the proceeds of which will be used to finance the costs (including
architectural, engineering, legal and related fees) of the following capital improvement
projects at the McCormick County Educational Complex: Constructing and equipping an
auditorium; constructing and equipping additional classroom space for use as a band room,
a culinary arts room and flexible space; renovating and equipping existing classrooms for
use as a weight room, a cyber security room, an art room and a welding room; and
constructing and equipping other improvements identified by the School District?

If the voter wishes to vote in favor of the question, select “Yes, In Favor of the Question”;
if the voter wishes to vote against the question, select “No, Opposed to the Question.”
                     
                 
                 
                     Responses 
                     
                         Yes, In Favor of the Question 
                         No, Opposed to the Question
