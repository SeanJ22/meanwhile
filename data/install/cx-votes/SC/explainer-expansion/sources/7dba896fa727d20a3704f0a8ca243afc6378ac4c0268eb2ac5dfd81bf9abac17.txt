                 Capital Proj. Sales & Use Tax Referendum 
             
             
                 
                     Election 
                     Statewide General Election 
                 
                 
                     Election Date 
                     
11/3/2026                     
                 
                 
                     Responsible County 
                     CHESTER 
                 
                 
                     District Type 
                     
                 
                 
                     District 
                     
                 
                 
                     Referendum Type 
                     Local Questions 
                 
                 
                     Referendum Text 
                     
                        Shall a special, one-percent capital project sales and use tax be imposed in Chester County beginning immediately at the expiration of Chester County’s existing capital project sales and use tax (which is anticipated to expire on or about April 30, 2028) to ensure no interruption in the imposition or collection of the sales and use tax and ending April 30, 2035, and shall the County be permitted to issue general obligation bonds, in one or more series, sufficient to fund project costs in an amount not to exceed the aggregate of $35,530,000, which the County intends to repay using the sales and use tax, and which is otherwise repayable from a millage levy on property taxes, the proceeds of which shall be distributed and expended for the acquisition, construction, equipping, or rehabilitation of each project as follows:
GRASP Food Pantry and Back Door Improvements	$20,000.00
Dawson Drive Baseball Complex Improvements	 $4,430,000.00
North Chester Fire Service Station Improvements	$500,000.00
Richburg Park (Bathrooms) Improvements	$150,000.00
Wylie Park Swimming Pool Improvements	$600,000.00
Lando-Manetta Museum Improvements	$180,000.00
Countywide Fire Service Air Packs Acquisition	 $3,290,000.00
Great Falls Park Improvements	$715,000.00
Sunset Park	
David Lex Minors Recreation Park	
Republic Park	
Dearborn Pocket Park	
Rossville Fire Service Station Improvements	 $3,500,000.00
SL Finley Historic School Shop Improvements	$805,000.00
Lowry's Community Center Renovation (Former Service Station) Improvements	$300,000.00
Edgemoor Community Action Center Improvements	$180,000.00
ADA Elementary School Playgrounds (Chester Park, Lewisville Elementary, Great Falls Elementary) Improvements	$960,000.00
Historic City of Chester City Hall Business Economic/Arts Entertainment Center Improvements	 $1,700,000.00
Countywide 911 Back-up Radio System Acquisition	$560,000.00
Fort Lawn Historic Cultural Arts Center Improvements	$900,000.00
County Body Cameras Acquisition	 $1,815,000.00
City of Chester Performing Arts and Entertainment Center (Amphitheatre) Improvements	$220,000.00
Great Falls Sewer System (Belt Press/Chlorine Contact) Improvements	 $2,475,000.00
Historic Post Office (Federal Building 158 Main Street, Chester) Improvements	 $5,560,000.00
City of Chester Phase 3 Streetscaping, Parking, and Dumpster Enclosure Improvements	$850,000.00
County Fire Service Richburg Ladder Truck Acquisition	 $2,490,000.00
Great Falls Rail Trail (to Fishing Creek) Improvements	 $1,330,000.00

ADDITIONAL CONDITIONS
The capital project sales and use tax shall be used for acquiring an interest in, paying debt service for previously issued bonds and/or reimbursing other prior expenditures from whatever source, related to, or procurement, design, architectural, engineering, surveying, soil testing, construction, improvement, or similar type uses related to the projects listed above, or any combination related to the foregoing that are related to the acquisition, construction, equipping, or rehabilitation of a project. Net proceeds of the sales and use tax must be expended for the purposes stated, in the priority listed above. However, the completion of each project funded by the sales and use tax is governed by the Capital Project Sales Tax Act (S.C. Code Ann. § 4-10-300, et seq.) and subject to acquisition of property and right-of-way, design and engineering considerations, funding of projects from other sources (as may be provided, for example, in the County’s Ordinance No. 2026-11, enacted June 15, 2026, as may be subsequently amended), bids in excess of project estimates, qualifications of bidders, cost overruns, financing costs, exhaustion of net sales and use tax revenues prior to the completion of each project in the order and priority stated above, other unforeseen circumstances and conditions, and other conditions as may be reasonably imposed by Chester County Council, which, if any of these circumstances occurs or is imposed, then the County may move to the next priority project and continue through the priority list until completed, then may, in the County’s sole discretion, return to any project(s) passed over, and may be otherwise provided for by enacted ordinance of County Council.
