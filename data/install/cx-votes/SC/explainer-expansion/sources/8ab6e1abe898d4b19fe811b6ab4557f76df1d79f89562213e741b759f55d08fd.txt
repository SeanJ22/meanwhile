                     Election 
                     Statewide General Election

                     District Type

                     Local Questions 
                 
                 
                     Referendum Text 
                     
                        Must a one percent sales and use tax be levied in the City of Simpsonville, South Carolina for no more than eight years for the purpose of allowing a credit against certain municipal ad valorem tax liability and for the purpose of raising the amounts specified for the following purposes?


(1)	$44,400,000 for citywide road infrastructure improvements, including repaving, drainage systems, curbs and sidewalks


INSTRUCTIONS TO VOTERS: All qualified electors of the City of Simpsonville desiring to vote in favor of imposing the tax for the stated purposes shall vote “YES,” and all qualified electors opposed to levying the tax shall vote “NO.”
