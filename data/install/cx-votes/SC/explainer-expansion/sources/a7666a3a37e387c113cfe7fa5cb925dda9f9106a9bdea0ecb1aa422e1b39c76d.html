City of Walhalla Mun. Tax Referendum

Election Statewide General Election
Election Date 11/3/2026
Responsible County OCONEE
District Type Municipality
District Walhalla
Referendum Type Local Questions
Referendum Text
Must a one percent sales and use tax be levied in the City of Walhalla, South Carolina for no more than eight years for the purpose of allowing a credit against certain municipal ad valorem tax liability and for the purpose of raising the amounts specified for the following purposes? Priority one projects: 1. $125,000.00 for Fire Station Generator and Installation (emergency response buildings) 2. $40,000.00 for Fire Station HVAC Replacement (emergency response buildings) 3. $25,000.00 for Fire Station Security Cameras & Locks (emergency response buildings) 4. $150,000.00 for Police Station Generator and Installation (police facilities) 5. $200,000.00 for Police Sally Port Construction (police facilities) 6. $220,000.00 for Depot Pond Drainage and Pipe Restoration (drainage systems) 7. $250,000.00 for Kaufman Square repairs and improvements (public assembly facilities) Priority two projects: 8. $365,000.00 for Sertoma Parking Restoration (public parking lots) 9. $350,000.00 for Public Facilities Sidewalk Repairs (streets and related facilities) 10. $210,000.00 for City Hall Parking & Driveway Repairs (public parking lots) 11. $200,000.00 for Community Center Generator and Installation (emergency response buildings) Priority three project: 12. $600,000.00 for City Hall Expansion (public assembly facilities) Yes [ ] No [ ]
