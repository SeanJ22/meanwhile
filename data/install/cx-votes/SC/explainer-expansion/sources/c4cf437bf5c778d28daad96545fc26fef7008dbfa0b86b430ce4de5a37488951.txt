                     CHESTERFIELD 
                 
                 
                     District Type 
                     
                 
                 
                     District

                     Local Questions 
                 
                 
                     Referendum Text 
                     
                        Must a special one percent sales and use tax be imposed in Chesterfield County, South
Carolina for not more than eight (8) years to raise the amounts specified for the design,
engineering, purchase (including, in certain instances, the purchase of real property),
construction, renovation, equipping, and furnishing of the following:

Construction of a New County Detention Center Cost $50,000,000 and must the
County Council of Chesterfield County be authorized to issue not exceeding $50,000,000
principal amount of general obligation bonds of Chesterfield County as a single issue or
several issues, provided that the proceeds of such bonds shall be applied to defray the
costs of the foregoing project, including costs overruns, and issuance costs, and provided
further that in the event the sales and use tax to be imposed as stated herein is inadequate
for the payment of such bonds, such bonds shall be payable from an ad valorem tax
imposed on all taxable property in Chesterfield County?

INSTRUCTIONS TO VOTERS: If you are in favor of the question, touch the screen
next to the word "Yes"; if you are opposed to the question, touch the screen next to the
word “No.
