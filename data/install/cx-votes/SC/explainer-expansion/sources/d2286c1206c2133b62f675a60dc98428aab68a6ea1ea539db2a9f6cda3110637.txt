                 Hampton School District Referendum 
             
             
                 
                     Election 
                     Statewide General Election 
                 
                 
                     Election Date 
                     
11/3/2026                     
                 
                 
                     Responsible County 
                     HAMPTON 
                 
                 
                     District Type 
                     
                 
                 
                     District 
                     
                 
                 
                     Referendum Type 
                     Local Questions 
                 
                 
                     Referendum Text 
                     
                        Shall the Board of Trustees of Hampton County School District, South Carolina (the "School District") be empowered to issue, at one time or from time to time, general obligation bonds of the School District, in a principal amount of not exceeding $29,000,000, the proceeds of which shall be used to finance the costs (including architectural, engineering, legal and related fees) of the following projects:
1. Complete the construction and equip the athletic complex at the Hampton County High School;
2. Construct and equip a greenhouse of the Career and Technical Education Agriculture program at the Hampton County High School; and 
3. Construct and equip a dedicated alternative educational placement learning center at the Hampton County High School?

If the voter wishes to vote in favor of the question, select "Yes, In Favor of the Question"; if the voter wishes to vote against the question, select "No, Opposed to the Question."
                     
                 
                 
                     Responses 
                     
                         Yes, In Favor of the Question 
                         No, Opposed to the Question
