EXPLANATION FOR VOTERS
QUESTION 3
Proposed Amendment
The proposed amendment enshrines in the Constitution the
fundamental right of all qualified persons in the
Commonwealth to vote. Two exceptions to this right are for
persons who are serving a sentence of incarceration for a felony
conviction and persons who are determined by a court to not
understand the act of voting. Such right cannot otherwise be
restricted by laws.
Under the proposed amendment, a person who is convicted of a
felony loses the right to vote during their period of
incarceration. Once released from incarceration, the person gets
back the right to vote without needing to apply for restoration
by the Governor.
The proposed amendment also requires a specific finding by a
court that a person does not understand the act of voting in
order for that person’s right to vote to be taken away.
With the right to vote, a person also has the right to be a juror,
the right to be elected to an office, and the right to be a notary
public.
