SPECIAL MEETING NOVEMBER 3, 2026
VOTING BY AUSTRALIAN BALLOT

The legal voters of the Central Vermont Career Center School District are hereby notified and
warned to meet at their respective polling places on Tuesday, November 3, 2026, to vote by
Australian ballot on the following articles of business:

Article 1:
The Central Vermont Career Center School Board proposes to incur bonded indebtedness to
expand access to Career and Technical Education in Central Vermont through a multi-campus
model by building on the U-32 campus, at an estimated total project cost of $54,900,000.
The Vermont State Legislature suspended the School Construction Aid Program in 2007.
Therefore, the school district must build 100% of the project at its own cost, without State
participation. State funds may not be available when this project would otherwise be
eligible to receive State school construction aid. The district is responsible for all costs

incurred in connection with any borrowing done in anticipation of State school

construction aid.

Shall bonds of the Central Vermont Career Center School District in an amount not to exceed
$54,900,000 be issued for the purpose of expanding technical education in Central Vermont
by building on the U-32 campus?

O Yes
O No

Article 2:
Shall the voters of the Central Vermont Career Center School District authorize the School
Board to enter into a 30-year ground lease with the Washington Central Unified Union
School District for property located at the U-32 Middle & High School campus, 930 Gallison
Hill Road, Montpelier, Vermont, for the purpose of constructing, operating, and maintaining
facilities for the Central Vermont Career Center, pursuant to the terms of a Ground Lease
Agreement negotiated by the School Board?

O Yes
O No
