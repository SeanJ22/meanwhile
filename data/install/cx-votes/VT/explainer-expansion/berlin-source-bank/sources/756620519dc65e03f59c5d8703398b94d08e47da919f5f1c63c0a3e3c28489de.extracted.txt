The legal voters of the Washington Central Unified Union School District are hereby notified and
warned to meet at their respective polling places on Tuesday, November 3, 2026, to vote by
Australian ballot on the following articles of business:

Article 1:
Shall the voters of the Washington Central Unified Union School District authorize the School Board
to enter into a 30-year ground lease with the Central Vermont Career Center School District for
property located at the U-32 Middle & High School campus, 930 Gallison Hill Road, Montpelier,
Vermont, for the purpose of constructing, operating, and maintaining facilities for the Central
Vermont Career Center, pursuant to the terms of a Ground Lease Agreement negotiated by the School
Board?
