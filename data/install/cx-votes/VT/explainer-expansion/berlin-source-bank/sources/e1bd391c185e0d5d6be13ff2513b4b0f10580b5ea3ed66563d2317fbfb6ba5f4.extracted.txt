OFFICIAL BALLOT
CENTRAL VERMONT CAREER CENTER SCHOOL DISTRICT
SPECIAL MEETING
TUESDAY, NOVEMBER 3, 2026
Page3 — Article1
The Central Vermont Career Center School Board proposes to incur bonded indebtedness to expand access to Career and Technical Education in Central Vermont through a multi-campus model by building on the U-32 campus, at an estimated total project cost of $54,900,000. The Vermont State Legislature suspended the School Construction Aid Program in 2007. Therefore, the school district must build 100% of the project at its own cost, without State participation. State funds may not be available when this project would otherwise be eligible to receive State school construction aid. The district is responsible for all costs incurred in connection with any borrowing done in anticipation of State school construction aid.
Shall bonds of the Central Vermont Career Center School District in an amount not to exceed $54,900,000 be issued for the purpose of expanding technical education in Central Vermont by building on the U-32 campus?
YES / NO

Article2
Shall the voters of the Central Vermont Career Center School District authorize the School Board to enter into a 30-year ground lease with the Washington Central Unified Union School District for property located at the U-32 Middle & High School campus, 930 Gallison Hill Road, Montpelier, Vermont, for the purpose of constructing, operating, and maintaining facilities for the Central Vermont Career Center, pursuant to the terms of a Ground Lease Agreement negotiated by the School Board?
