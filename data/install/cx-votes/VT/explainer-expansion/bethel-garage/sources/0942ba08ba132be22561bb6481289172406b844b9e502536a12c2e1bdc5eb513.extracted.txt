Public Comment

David Alexander asked why the flags have been removed from the veterans’ graves in the cemeteries as in the past they have remained all year round. Therese Kirby stated volunteers have been placing the flags in recent years. In consultation with Therese several different schedules have been tried and the most recent schedule was to place the flags by Memorial Day, remove them and replace them for Veterans Day as the flags are expensive and this schedule would preserve the flags for longer use.
David Alexander, Penny Griffin and Dale Hoff asked that the flags remain in place all year. Therese said she would consult with other cemeteries to learn other practices, discuss with the volunteers and report back to the Selectboard.

Barbara Nickerson indicated there would be a Two Rivers Ottauquechee Regional Committee (TRORC) meeting on Wednesday, September 30, at 6:30 pm and asked that all Selectboard members attend.

Town Garage Bond Documents:

Ben Roberts moved, Jordan Garrow seconded, to approve the necessity for a Capital Construction Project, to approve the declaration of Official Intent of the Town of Bethel, VT to reimburse certain expenditures from proceeds of indebtedness up to $500,000, and to approve the Official Warning for a Special Town Meeting

Therese Kirby reviewed the plans for constructing the new bldg. There will be Bond Informational meetings held on 10/12 and 10/26 at 6pm at the Town Hall. People will also be receiving a post card in the mail soon, regarding the vote. Therese also stated the bond vote for Church Street Bridge ends in 2028, The Town Hall Bond ends in 2030, a Fire Tanker bond ends in 2031, as does a water main replacement bond. That is one of the reasons we waited until now to bond. (note: all of those bond payments currently equal $101,002 annually).
Justin Merrill is concerned about allocating enough money for contaminated material under the current building. There will be soil testing. Still hoping for other savings to come, but time will tell. All figures now are preliminary costs. We need a motion now to prepare for the bond vote. Documents must be signed 30 days prior to the vote, per Chris Jarvis. Ben and Jordan would have liked the probable cost of construction in their packet. 
Motion passed with Jed, Dave & Denise voting yes and Ben and Jordan voting no, because the probable cost of construction was not in their packet.
