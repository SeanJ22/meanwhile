News and Announcements

Election Day Coming Soon 11/3/2026

Reminder - November 3, 2026 is election day. If you have not voted yet, you can use the ballot that was mailed to you - it is the same ballot that will be at the polls.

You can bring it with you and either complete it there and run through the tabulator, or drop it off with the poll workers, already completed, and we will run it through the tabulator for you.

The Bond Vote for the town garage is also that day. Ballots will be available at the polls.

If you have any questions, please contact the Town Clerk's Office at 802-234-9722 or email pbrown@townofbethelvt.com.

Town Garage Information

Tuesday, November 3, 2026 is Voting Day in Vermont. Polls in Bethel will be open from 8am to 7pm, at the Bethel School Campus. We will be holding a bond vote to build a new Town Garage.

Our current Town Garage was built in 1974, and has outlived its useful life. The budget for the new garage is $3.5 million. This will be a 30 year note at +/- 4.23% interest via the Vermont Bond Bank. This number may be reduced by use of capital funds & other savings.

How will this affect your tax bill?

A home valued at $350,000 will pay $218 per year, or 60₡ per day.  A home valued at $375,000 will pay $234 per year, or 64₡ per day.

Please come to the Bond Informational on October 26 at 6pm at the Town Hall as part of a Selectboard meeting, to understand the financial plans to deal with the bond payment added to our budget. We do have existing bonds being paid off in 2028, two in 2030 and another in 2031.
