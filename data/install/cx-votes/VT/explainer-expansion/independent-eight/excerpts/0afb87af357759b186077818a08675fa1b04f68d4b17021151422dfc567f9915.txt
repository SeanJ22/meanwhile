Shall general obligation bonds or notes of the City of Vergennes in an additional amount not to exceed Twenty-Two Million Dollars ($22,000,000) be issued for the purpose of financing the cost of improvements to the City’s wastewater collection system and treatment facility, the estimated cost of such improvements being Fifty-Four Million Dollars ($54,000,000)? Such borrowing is conditioned on the following: The aggregate principal amount of general obligation bonds or notes issued for these improvements pursuant to Article 2 approved on March 1, 2022 and pursuant to this Article shall not exceed Thirty-Nine Million Five Hundred Thousand Dollars ($39,500,000); and Grants, subsidies, principal forgiveness, ARPA funds, City funds, and other available resources shall be applied to eligible project costs and may reduce the amount of debt actually issued.
