Shall the Town of Hartland be authorized to issue general obligation bonds in an amount not to exceed Three Million Dollars ($3,000,000.00) for the purpose of making public repairs, upgrades and improvements to the Town’s highway known and identified as Quechee Road (TH-2), such repairs, upgrades and improvements currently estimated to cost Seven Million, Four Hundred Forty-Four Thousand, Seven Hundred Twenty-Five Dollars ($7,444,725.00).
