Amtrack Station Send us a Message Latest News view all news Informational Meeting Agenda 10-30-2026 Informational Meeting - Article I 10-19-2026 CITY OF VERGENNES: INFORMATIONAL MEETING The City of Vergennes will hold a public informational hearing on Monday, November 2, 2026, at 6:00PM at The Vergennes Opera House. The meeting is a public informational meeting regarding three important ballot questions that voters will consider on Tuesday, November 3, 2026. The informational hearing will provide residents with an opportunity to learn more about the following ballot articles and ask questions before voting. ARTICLE I Shall general obligation bonds or notes of the City of Vergennes in an additional amount not to exceed Twenty-Two Million Dollars ($22,000,000) be issued for the purpose of financing the cost of improvements to the City’s wastewater collection system and treatment facility, the estimated cost of such improvements being Fifty-Four Million Dollars ($54,000,000)? Such borrowing is conditioned on the following: The aggregate principal amount of general obligation bonds or notes issued for these improvements pursuant to Article 2 approved on March 1, 2022 and pursuant to this Article shall not exceed Thirty-Nine Million Five Hundred Thousand Dollars ($39,500,000); and Grants, subsidies, principal forgiveness, ARPA funds, City funds, and other available resources shall be applied to eligible project costs and may reduce the amount of debt actually issued. The legal voters of the City of Vergennes are further notified that voter qualification, registration and absentee voting relative to said meeting shall be as provided in Chapters 43, 51 and 55 of Title 17, Vermont Statutes Annotated. The legal voters of the City of Vergennes are further notified that an informational meeting will be held on Monday, November 2, 2026 at Vergennes Opera House in the City of Vergennes at six o’clock (6:00) in the evening (p.m.), for the purpose of explaining the proposed improvements and the financing thereof. Such meeting will be available by Zoom as well. Voting Information Voting will be on Tuesday, November 3, 2026, from 9:00 AM – 7:00 PM at St. Peter’s Parish Hall, 85 South Maple Street, Vergennes, VT 05491. Residents are encouraged to attend the informational hearing, ask questions, and learn more about each of these important ballot questions before casting their votes. The meeting is a public informational meeting regarding three important ballot questions that voters will consider on Tuesday, November 3, 2026. The informational hearing will provide residents with an opportunity to learn more about the following ballot articles and ask questions before voting. ARTICLE II Shall the City of Vergennes approve the conveyance of a leasehold interest to T-Mobile Northeast LLC in the existing leased premises at the City Water Tower, Main and North Streets, for an initial five-year extended term beginning July 23, 2026, at an initial monthly rent of approximately $4,900, increasing by 5% annually, and three automatic additional five-year renewal terms, subject to the Tenant’s right not to renew, for a maximum term ending July 22, 2046; and authorize the Mayor to execute the amendment under the City Seal and cause the conveyance to be recorded pursuant to Section 26(a) of the City Charter? ARTICLE III Shall the voters of the City of Vergennes advise the City Council that they support the City’s acquisition of the real property and improvements located at 20 Armory Lane, Vergennes, Vermont, for the purchase price of $1.00, plus customary closing and transaction costs, for municipal and community purposes, including recreation and youth programming? (This article is advisory and non-binding.) The legal voters of the City of Vergennes are further notified that voter qualification, registration and absentee voting relative to said meeting shall be as provided in Chapters 43, 51 and 55 of Title 17, Vermont Statutes Annotated. The legal voters of the City of Vergennes are further notified that a public informational meeting will be held on Monday, November 2, 2026, at Vergennes Opera House in the City of Vergennes at six o’clock (6:00) in the evening (p.m.), for the purpose of explaining and receiving public comment on the proposed T-Mobile leasehold conveyance (Article II) and the advisory question concerning the City’s acquisition of 20 Armory Lane (Article III). Such hearing will be available by Zoom as well. Voting Information Voting will be on Tuesday, November 3, 2026, from 9:00 AM – 7:00 PM at St. Peter’s Parish Hall, 85 South Maple Street, Vergennes, VT 05491. Residents are encouraged to attend the informational hearing, ask questions, and learn more about each of these important ballot questions before casting their votes.
