to vote by Australian ballot on the following articles. 
 
Article I: 
Shall the Lake Region Union High School District, which the State 
Board of Education has found necessary to include in the proposed unified union 
school district, join with the school district of the Lake Region Union Elementary-
Middle School District, which the State Board of Education has found necessary to 
include in the proposed unified union school district, for the purpose of forming a 
unified union school district to be named the Orleans Central Unified Union School 
District, as provided in Title 16, Vermont Statutes Annotated, upon the following 
conditions and agreements: 
 
 
(a) Grades. The unified union school district will offer pre-kindergarten through 
grade twelve education to all of the students in the unified union school district. 
 
(b) Operation of Schools. The unified union school district shall operate and 
manage one or more schools offering instruction in grades pre-kindergarten 
through grade twelve.  
 
(c) Board of School Directors. The unified union school district board of school 
directors shall consist of fourteen (14) members. Two seats shall be allocated 
to each of the following communities: Albany, Barton, Brownington, Glover, 
Irasburg, Westmore, and the Village of Orleans. The allocation of Board seats 
among the District’s communities is not based on, and is not intended to be 
closely proportional to, each community’s relative population. Accordingly, 


Board members shall be nominated and elected pursuant to the modified at-
large model authorized by Vermont law. Each candidate shall be a current voter 
and resident of the community to which the seat is allocated. Every qualified 
voter of the Orleans Central Unified Union School District shall be entitled to 
vote for candidates for every seat appearing on the District ballot, regardless of 
the community to which the seat is allocated. Each Board member shall exercise 
one vote. There shall be no weighted voting. 
 
(d) Assumption of debts and ownership of school property. The unified union 
school district shall assume the indebtedness of forming districts; assume all 
operating deficits and/or surpluses or reserve funds of the forming districts; 
acquire and pay for the school properties of member districts; all as specifically 
identified and provided for in the Final Report/Articles of Agreement. 
 
(e) Final Report/Article of Agreement. The provisions of the Final Report/Articles 
of Agreement approved by the State Board of Education on September 16, 
2026, which is on file in the Town Clerk’s office, shall govern the unified union 
school district. 
 
 
Article II: 
To elect two (2) school directors from each municipality from the 
nominees to serve on the school board of the proposed unified union school district 
board from the date of the organizational meeting for the following initial terms: 
 
Albany

Village of Orleans
