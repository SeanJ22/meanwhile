Proposition No. 2 Educational Technology and Capital Improvements Levy
The Board of Directors of Highline School District No. 401 adopted Resolution No. 12-26, concerning educational technology and capital improvements to maintain student learning. Official explanatory statement
Proposition 2 authorizes a capital levy for educational technology and capital improvements to maintain student learning and improve student and staff safety and security. Proposition 2 will address the existing condition of educational technology equipment, infrastructure and systems and aging and educationally outdated school facilities. The capital levy will fund: (1) cybersecurity protections and systems that protect student and staff data; (2) reliable internet, wireless networks and technology infrastructure that support teaching and learning; (3) student and staff devices, classroom technology and instructional software; (4) educator and staff training and support to implement technology improvements; and (5) other technology improvements and upgrades districtwide. The capital levy will also fund districtwide capital improvements to support the construction, modernization and remodeling of schools and facilities. More information is available at highlineschools.org/bondandlevy. The proposed four-year levy would authorize collection of $12,000,000 in each of the years 2027 through 2030. The levy rate per $1,000 of assessed value required to produce these amounts is estimated to be $0.36 in 2027, $0.35 in 2028, $0.34 in 2029, and $0.32 in 2030. Exact levy rates will be determined based on actual assessed value at the time of levy. Exemptions from taxes may be available to certain homeowners. For more information, contact the King County Department of Assessments, 206-296-3920.
