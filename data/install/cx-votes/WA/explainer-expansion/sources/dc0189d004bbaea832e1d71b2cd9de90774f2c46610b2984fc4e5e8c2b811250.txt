Highline School District No. 401
Proposition No. 1 Bonds to Replace and Rebuild Deteriorating Schools and Improve Safety
The Board of Directors of Highline School District No. 401 adopted Resolution No. 11-26, concerning neighborhood school improvements and safety upgrades. This proposition would authorize the District to: replace or rebuild four deteriorating schools (Cascade, Chinook, Sylvester, CHOICE Academy), including new career/technical learning spaces, secure entrances, security camera upgrades; make critical districtwide core building system upgrades, including heating, roofing, electrical, plumbing; implement districtwide health, security, fire system and earthquake safety upgrades; issue $595,400,000 of general obligation bonds maturing within 21 years; and levy annual excess property taxes to repay the bonds, all as provided in Resolution No. 11-26. Should this proposition be:
Approved Rejected

Official explanatory statement
Proposition 1 authorizes school construction bonds to replace and rebuild deteriorating schools and make districtwide safety and accessibility improvements and upgrades. Proposition 1 will improve student, staff and community safety, security and accessibility, enhance student learning environments, improve and expand educational opportunities, and provide necessary student educational facilities (including career/technical learning spaces). The bonds will allow Highline Public Schools to: (1) address critical needs districtwide, including core building system upgrades and site improvements (plumbing, hot water systems, HVAC, electrical, roofing, security, fire systems and earthquake safety upgrades); (2) construct a new school to replace Sylvester Middle School; (3) construct a new school to replace Cascade Middle School; (4) partially rebuild and modernize Chinook Middle School (replacing the oldest, most outdated buildings, facilities and infrastructure with new construction; renovating and updating existing usable buildings; and replacing portables with permanent classrooms); (5) rebuild and modernize the former Southern Heights Elementary School site as a new facility for CHOICE Academy; and (6) develop design plans for construction, redevelopment or replacement of Valley View Early Learning Center and Hilltop Elementary School. More information is available at highlineschools.org/bondandlevy. The $595,400,000 of general obligation bonds will be repaid from annual excess property taxes sufficient to pay bond debt service over a maximum term of 21 years. Exemptions from taxes may be available to certain homeowners. For more information, contact the King County Department of Assessments, 206-296-3920.
