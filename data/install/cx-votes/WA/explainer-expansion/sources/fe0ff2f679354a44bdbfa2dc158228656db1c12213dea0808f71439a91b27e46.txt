City of Bothell
Proposition No. 1 Annexation Into Shoreline Fire Department Regional Fire Authority
The Bothell City Council adopted Resolution No. 1784, approving annexation into the Shoreline Fire Department Regional Fire Authority and related plan amendment. To maintain current fire and emergency medical service levels and provide long-term financial and operational sustainability for such services within Bothell, should Bothell be annexed into and become a part of the Shoreline Fire Department Regional Fire Authority, effective March 1, 2027, pursuant to the plan amendment, as provided in Resolution No. 1784?
Yes No

Official explanatory statement
The Bothell City Council adopted Resolution 1784 requesting voter approval to annex into and become part of the Shoreline Fire Department Regional Fire Authority ("RFA"). As the region has grown and costs have increased, Bothell has considered governance/service models for fire and emergency medical services (“EMS”). As part of its study, Bothell contracted with the RFA for fire administration (Fire Chief) services. The City Council has determined that annexing into the RFA ensures long-term sustainability of funding to maintain current fire protection and EMS service levels. Annexation will not change the delivery or the level of services to Bothell residents. If annexation is approved, Bothell Fire Department would become part of the larger RFA, instead of operating as a standalone, smaller fire department. By combining resources and expertise and coordinating efforts, fire and EMS service can gain efficiencies, including response operations. Bothell voters will be entitled to vote on all ballot measures proposed by the RFA—including those pertaining to taxation and benefit charges. Bothell will have representation on the RFA Board. If annexation is rejected, Bothell would need to reseek voter approval, negotiate a new agreement with RFA or another agency, or reconstitute the Bothell Fire Department. https://bothellwa.gov/2209/Fire-Service-Study
