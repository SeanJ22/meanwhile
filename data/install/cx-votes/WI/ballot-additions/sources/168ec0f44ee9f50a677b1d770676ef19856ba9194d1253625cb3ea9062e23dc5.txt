Ballot Text
                     The question will appear on the ballot as follows:

                     “Shall an ordinance be enacted creating Chapter 2.53 of the Code of Ordinances of the City of Eau Claire creating the Citizens’ Advisory
                     Healthcare Action Committee?”


Explanation
                     A “Yes” vote would adopt an ordinance to create a new advisory committee to the city council regarding healthcare affordability and accessibility.
                     The committee would research healthcare related issues, hold at least two meetings each year, and produce a report to the Eau Claire City Council by
                     September 15, 2028.

                     A “No” vote would not create such a committee.

                     Done in the City of Eau Claire, Eau Claire and Chippewa Counties on November 2, 2026,
                      Nicholas L. Koerner
                      City Clerk for the City of Eau Claire
