Waukesha County Election Results
Please note that due to software reporting limitations some precincts may show that their results are 100% complete yet that may only be a partial result for that contest or reporting unit.
Many of our municipalities have multiple reporting units, multiple ballot tabulators, and/or utilize Central Count. Therefore, these municipalities’ voter totals from their polling sites or the totals from their central count locations will be reported separately and their results totaled at the end of the evening.
Do not consider any results as fully reported until the County’s election posting reads "Unofficial Results – Final".
If you have any questions or want to verify if results are complete, call or text the County Clerk's Office at (262) 666-3335.
