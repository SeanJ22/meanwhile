BE IT RESOLVED by the School Board of the School District of Lodi, Columbia and
Dane Counties, Wisconsin that the revenues included in the School District budget be authorized
to exceed the revenue limit specified in Section 121.91, Wisconsin Statutes, by $5,980,000 per
year for five years, beginning with the 2027-2028 school year and ending with the 2031-2032
school year, for non-recurring purposes consisting of operational expenses for instructional and
educational programming, technology updates and staff compensation.


Shall the School District of Lodi, Columbia and Dane Counties, Wisconsin be authorized to exceed the revenue limit specified in Section 121.91, Wisconsin Statutes, by $5,980,000 per year for five years, beginning with the 2027-2028 school year and ending with the 2031-2032 school year, for non-recurring purposes consisting of operational expenses for instructional and educational programming, technology updates and staff compensation?
