Shall the School District of Brown Deer, Milwaukee County, Wisconsin be authorized to issue pursuant to Chapter 67 of the Wisconsin Statutes, general obligation bonds in an amount not to exceed $35,000,000 for the public purpose of paying the cost of a school facility improvement project consisting of: district-wide capital maintenance, building infrastructure, energy efficiency and accessibility improvements, including elevator replacement and playground renovations at the Elementary School; renovations at the Middle/High School, including to classrooms, the auditorium, pool and locker rooms; site improvements, including replacing the track and adding turf to the field at the Middle/High School; and acquisition of furnishings, fixtures and equipment?
