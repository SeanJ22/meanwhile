Shall the foregoing resolution of the Board of Education of the School District of Greenfield be approved: BE IT RESOLVED by the Board of Education of the School District of Greenfield, Milwaukee County, Wisconsin shall be issued pursuant to Chapter 67 of the Wisconsin Statues, general obligation bonds in an amount not to exceed $35,000,000 for the public purpose of paying the cost of school facility improvement projects consisting of capital maintenance and improvement, safety, security, and other site improvements at schools of the School District; and acquisition of furnishings, fixtures, and equipment?
