Referendum Survey Information

This summer, the Cashton School District reached out to the community seeking feedback about considering an operational referendum this November to assist with additional funding for the schools beginning in the 2027-2028 school year.  


On August 10th, the Board heard a presentation from Daren Sievers from School Perceptions, the company the district worked with to gather feedback from the community before considering an operating referendum question on the November ballot.  The results of the survey are available and can be found below.  In addition, School Perceptions has provided a video presentation summarizing the results of the survey.


After careful reflection and discussion based on the valuable survey results and the projected financial position of the school district, the Board decided to reduce the size of the pending resolution to $450,000 annually for three years rather than $500,000 as listed in the survey.  We appreciate the information the community shared as this important topic is explored.  The Board will meet on Friday, August 14th at 7 pm to record a vote on the draft resolution language and to determine whether to proceed with placing a question on the November ballot.

Election Notice

NOTICE OF ELECTION
SCHOOL DISTRICT OF CASHTON
NOVEMBER 3, 2026


Referendum Details


At an election in the School District of Cashton on Tuesday, November 3, 2026, the following question will be submitted to a vote of the people:


"Shall the School District of Cashton, Monroe, Vernon and La Crosse Counties, Wisconsin be authorized to exceed the revenue limit specified in Section 121.91, Wisconsin Statutes, by $450,000 per year for three years, beginning with the 2027-2028 school year and ending with the 2029-2030 school year, for non-recurring purposes consisting of operational and maintenance expenses?"


A copy of the entire text of the resolution directing submission of the question set forth above to the electorate and information concerning District boundaries can be obtained from the District Clerk at the School District offices located at 540 Coe Street, Cashton, Wisconsin.


Additional Information


Acceptable photo ID will be required to vote at this election.  If you do not have a photo ID you may obtain a free ID for voting from the Division of Motor Vehicles.


Persons with questions regarding the referendum election should contact Ryan Alderson, District Administrator.


Done in the School District of Cashton
on October 1, 2026
Amanda Schultz
District Clerk
