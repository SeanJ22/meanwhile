Shall the Pulaski Community
School District, Brown, Oconto,
Shawano and Outagamie Counties,
Wisconsin be authorized to issue
pursuant to Chapter 67 of the
Wisconsin Statutes, general obligation
bonds in an amount not to exceed
$59,800,000 for the public purpose of
paying the cost of a school facility
improvement project consisting of:
construction of an addition and
renovations at Lannoye Elementary
School, including for classrooms,
special education, and support spaces;
renovations at Pulaski Community
Middle School in Career and
Technology Education (CTE), including
Technology and Engineering
Education, Agriscience, Family and
Consumer Science, and Business and
Information Technology classroom
spaces, and conversion of the existing
pool to a multi-purpose activity facility;
construction of additions and
renovations at Pulaski High School,
including for a community swimming
pool facility, a fitness/weight center,
wrestling space, additions and
renovations to Career and Technology
Education (CTE) classroom spaces in
select program areas, renovations to
classrooms and labs, and construction
of tennis courts and parking lot
expansion; district-wide capital
maintenance, building systems and
site improvements; and acquisition of
furnishings, fixtures and equipment?
