This question will appear on the November 3, 2026, ballot as follows:

Shall the School District of Reedsburg, Sauk and Juneau Counties, Wisconsin be authorized to issue, pursuant to Chapter 67 of the Wisconsin Statutes, general obligation bonds in an amount not to exceed $83,000,000 for the public purpose of paying the cost of a school building project consisting of: construction of a new middle school on district-owned land, site improvements, and acquisition of furnishings, fixtures and equipment?
