In Person Absentee Voting for the
November 3rd 2026 General Election

Available in the clerk's office at the Apple River Town Hall
606 US Highway 8, Amery, WI 54001

Thursday, October 22nd, 2026 3 pm to 6 pm

No appointment needed
Register to vote online at https://myvote.wi.gov/en-us/Register-To-Vote through October 14th, 2026 for the November 3rd 2026 General Election.  Valid State of Wisconsin Driver License or State of Wisconsin ID card issued by the Wisconsin Department of Motor Vehicles (DMV) required. 
After October 14th, 2026, register in the Clerk's office by appointment through Friday, October 30th, 2026 or register in-person on Election Day. 
The Clerk's office, the Election Day Polling Place and the Apple River Town Hall are all located at the same address: 606 US Highway 8 Amery WI 54001 . 
No in-person registration is permitted on the Saturday, Sunday and Monday prior to the election.   You must always provide a Proof of Residence document when registering to vote. Details are available at Voter Registration and Proof of Residence | Wisconsin Elections Commission
UNOFFICIAL Election Results are updated throughout election night. OFFICIAL Election Results of Federal, State & County contests are posted following the County Board of Cavass' certification of the results. Results are available on the Polk County Website ELECTIONS - Polk County.
