Shall the School District of Thorp, Clark and Taylor Counties, Wisconsin be authorized to issue pursuant to Chapter 67 of the Wisconsin Statutes, general obligation bonds in an amount not to exceed $8,500,000 for the public purpose of paying the cost of a school facility improvement project consisting of: district-wide capital maintenance and building infrastructure improvements, including plumbing, electrical, HVAC, roof, asphalt and fire safety repairs and replacements; and acquisition of furnishings, fixtures and equipment?
