Shall the School District of Sheboygan Falls, Sheboygan County, Wisconsin be authorized to issue pursuant to Chapter 67 of the Wisconsin Statutes, general obligation bonds in an amount not to exceed $65,700,000 for the public purpose of paying the cost of a school facility improvement project consisting of: - renovations and updates at the Elementary School, including to special education and classroom spaces, the media center and cafeteria/kitchen; - construction of additions and renovations at the High School for science classrooms and labs, career and technical education, a science, technology, engineering and math lab, the media center and weight room/fitness area; - renovations and updates at the High School, including to special education and classroom spaces, the locker rooms, existing gymnasium and auditorium; - district-wide accessibility, safety, security and site improvements; - other capital projects identified in the District's capital improvement plan; and - acquisition of furnishings, fixtures and equipment?
