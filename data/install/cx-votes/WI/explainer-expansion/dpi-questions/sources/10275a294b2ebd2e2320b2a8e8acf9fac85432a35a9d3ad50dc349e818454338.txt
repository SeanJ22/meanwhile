Shall the School District of Weyauwega-Fremont, Waupaca, Winnebago and Waushara Counties, Wisconsin be authorized to issue pursuant to Chapter 67 of the Wisconsin Statutes, general obligation bonds in an amount not to exceed $8,450,000 for the public purpose of paying the cost of a school facility improvement project consisting of: safety, security, building infrastructure and site improvements at Fremont Elementary School, Weyauwega Elementary School and Weyauwega Middle School; construction of a secure entrance at Weyauwega Elementary School; district-wide capital maintenance; and acquisition of furnishings, fixtures, and equipment?
