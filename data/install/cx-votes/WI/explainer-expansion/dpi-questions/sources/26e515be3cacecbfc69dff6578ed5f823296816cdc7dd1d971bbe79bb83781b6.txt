Shall the School District of Princeton, Green Lake and Marquette Counties,Wisconsin be authorized to issue pursuant to Chapter 67 of the Wisconsin Statutes, general obligation bonds in an amount not to exceed $14,000,000 for the public purpose of paying the cost of a school facility improvement project consisting of: district-wide safety and security improvements, including construction of secure entrances, building systems, infrastructure and capital maintenance improvements; renovations to instructional spaces; site and track improvements; and acquisition of furnishings, fixtures and equipment?
