Shall the School District of Mayville, Dodge County, Wisconsin
be authorized to exceed the revenue limit specified in Section
121.91, Wisconsin Statutes, by $950,000 per year for seven years,
beginning with the 2027-2028 school year and ending with the
2033-2034 school year, for non-recurring purposes consisting of
operational and maintenance expenses, including to maintain
district facilities, for school safety and security, student
transportation, and educational programming?
