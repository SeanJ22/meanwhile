QUESTION 1: Shall the City of Eagle River adopt a charter ordinance creating Section 2-105 of the Eagle River Municipal Code to establish term limits for the offices of Mayor and Alderperson by: 1. Limiting service as Mayor or Alderperson to four consecutive two-year terms; 2. Requiring a six-year period before a person who has served four consecutive terms may again serve in the same office; 3. Prohibiting a person who has served four consecutive terms as Mayor from immediately serving as an Alderperson, and prohibiting a person who has served four consecutive terms as an Alderperson from immediately serving as Mayor; and 4. Counting terms served both before and after enactment of the ordinance when applying these term limits.
