Under state law, the increase in the levy of the City of Muskego for the tax to be imposed for the next fiscal year, 2027, is limited to 1.593%, which results in a levy of $14,732,877.00. Shall the City of Muskego be allowed to exceed this limit and increase the levy for the next fiscal year, 2027, for the purpose of improving public safety services in the City of Muskego, which, are the City’s Police, Fire, EMS, Rescue, and Public Works services, including staffing and support for those services, by a total of 40.047%, which results in a levy of $20,632,877.00, and on an ongoing basis, include the increase of $5,900,000.00 for each fiscal year going forward?
