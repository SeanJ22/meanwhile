Under state law, the increase in the levy of the City of Hudson for the tax to be imposed for the next fiscal year, 2027, is limited to 0.872%, which results in a levy of $13,335,657. Shall the City of Hudson be allowed to exceed this limit and increase the levy for the next fiscal year, 2027, for the purpose of improving emergency services with 9 additional full-time fire staff and ongoing Hudson Fire Department expenses, by a total of 9.698%, which results in a levy of $14,628,943 and on ongoing basis, include the increase of $1,293,285.68 for each fiscal year going forward?
