Elections

The Elections page provides residents with essential election‑resources, all in one place. It includes downloadable forms required for registration, absentee voting, and other election-related processes; timely notifications such as polling locations, sample ballots, and notices of public hearings; and official results from recent and past elections. Whether you're preparing to vote, checking deadlines, or reviewing outcomes, this page ensures you’re informed and up‑to‑date.
