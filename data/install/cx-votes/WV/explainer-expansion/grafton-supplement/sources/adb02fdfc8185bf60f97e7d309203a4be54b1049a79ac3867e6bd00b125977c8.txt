               CLASSIFICATION OF PROPERTY


For ad valorem tax purposes, property is classified as follows:

  Class I     (NO LONGER TAXABLE)


    All tangible personal property employed exclusively in agriculture, including horticulture and
    grazing; all products of agriculture, including livestock, while owned by the producer; all
    notes, bonds, bills and accounts receivable, stocks and any other intangible personal property.

  Class II

    All property owned, used and occupied by the owner exclusively for residential purposes; all
    farms, including land used for horticulture and grazing, occupied and cultivated by their
    owners or bona fide tenants.


  Class III

    All real and personal property situated outside of municipalities, exclusive of Classes I and II.


  Class IV

    All real and personal property situated inside of municipalities, exclusive of Classes I and II.




Total levy rates by class vary between counties. The rate of Class II property is two times
that of Class I and the rates of Classes III and IV property are four times that of Class I.

         GUIDE TO COUNTY, SCHOOL, AND MUNICIPAL LEVY RATES
            (ALL RATES OF LEVY IN CENTS PER $100 VALUATION)

Rural Rates
  Rates levied by State, County, and Board of Education.

State Current
  Rates for State Current are .25 for Class 1; .50 for Class 2, and 1.00 for Class 3 and 4 set by West Virginia
  State Code.

County Current
  County Current - Maximum for Class 1 is 14.3, Class 2 is 28.6, Class 3 and 4 is 57.2.

County Bonds
  Rate is determined by amount of money that must be raised to pay the principal and interest on the bond.
  Total Bond Rate and County Current may not exceed the maximum rates of 14.3, 28.6, and 57.2.

County Excess Levy
  May be imposed for up to 5 years if such levy is approved by 60% of the voters in a levy election. Maximum
  rates are 7.15 for Class 1, 14.3 for Class 2 and 28.6 for Classes 3 and 4.

Public Improvement
  Total of County Current, Bond and Public Improvements may not exceed maximum levy rates stated in #2,
  above.

Maximum Rate for County
 Current, Bond, Excess Levy and Public Improvement rates may not exceed 21.45 for Class 1, 42.9 for Class
 2, and 85.8 for Classes 3 and 4.

School Current
  Maximum rates are 22.95 for Class 1, 45.9 for Class 2, and 91.8 for Classes 3 and 4.

School Bonds
  Rate is determined by amount of money that must be raised to pay the principal and interest on the bond.
  Rate is in addition to the School Current Rate.

School Excess Levy
  May be imposed for up to 5 years if such a levy is approved by a majority of the voters in a levy election.
  Maximum rates are 22.95 for Class I, 45.9 for Class 2, and 91.8 for Classes 3 and 4.

Permanent Improvements
  Total School Current and Permanent Improvement rates may not exceed maximum rates of 22.95, 45.9, and
  91.8.

Total Rural Rate (State, County, and School)
  Rate to be charged for taxes on property located in rural districts (outside a municipality).

         GUIDE TO COUNTY, SCHOOL, AND MUNICIPAL LEVY RATES
            (ALL RATES OF LEVY IN CENTS PER $100 VALUATION)

Municipal Current
 Maximum rates are 12.5 for Class 1, 25 for Class 2, and 50 for Class 4.

Municipal Bonds
 Rates determined by amount of money that must be raised to pay the principal and interest on the bond. Total
 Bond and Municipal Current may not exceed maximum rates of 12.5, 25, and 50.

Municipal Excess Levy
 May be imposed for up to 5 years if such a levy is approved by 60% of the voters in a levy election.
 Maximum rates are 6.25 for Class I, 12.5 for Class 2, and 25 for Class 4.

Maximum Rate for Municipality
 Current, Bond, and Excess Levy may not exceed 18.75 for Class 1, 37.5 for Class 2, and 75.0 for Class 4.

Total Rural and Municipal Rates
  Rate to be charged for taxes on property located inside a municipality.
