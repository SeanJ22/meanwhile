MINERAL COUNTY COMMISSION
SPECIAL ELECTION TO AUTHORIZE
ADDITIONAL LEVIES
FOR THE AMBULANCE AUTHORITY
(November 3, 2026)

for the fiscal years beginning July 1, 2027, July
