ADDITIONAL LEVY
Election to authorize a levy for the fiscal years
beginning July 1, 2027, 2028, 2029, and 2030
for the purpose of contributing funds from such
levy for emergency medical services for the
acquisition of equipment and other capital
improvements, payment of a portion of general
operating, maintenance, and other expenses in

the Levy. The need for such funding has been
determined by the Order of the County
Commission of Monongalia County, West
Virginia entered on the 19th day of August
2026.

dollars assessed valuation of property for each
of the fiscal years beginning July 1, 2027, July
1, 2028, July 1, 2029, and July 1, 2030, shall
be:
CLASS I properties 2.40 cents
CLASS II properties 4.80 cents
