As noted above, the amounts set forth above are the average annual amounts for each purpose. The actual amounts for each purpose likely will vary
from one fiscal year to the next. If any item in the above-stated purposes is declared unconstitutional or invalid due to judicial or legislative action,
then any affected funds will be distributed among the remaining constitutional and valid itemized purposes. The actual amounts budgeted, as well as
the amounts spent, for each purpose and in aggregate, likely will vary from one fiscal year to the next depending on the realized tax collections and
the needs of the Marshall County School District, as determined by the Board with respect to each fiscal year. If the amounts budgeted by the Board
for a specific purpose or purposes for a fiscal year are not able to be spent entirely in such fiscal year, then the unspent amount may be carried over
and spent on such specific purpose or purposes in subsequent fiscal years.
If additional State, Federal, private or other special funds become available to provide funding for any of the above-stated purposes, or if surplus
funds are generated due to increased assessed valuations, then in either case the Board shall be authorized and empowered to expend at the end of
each fiscal year, during the term of the levies, the surplus (if any) in excess of the amount needed for any of the above-stated purposes for the
enrichment, supplementation and advancement of all educational programs in Marshall County, and other purposes pertinent to the operation of
Marshall County Schools, in the discretion of the Board. Such additional or surplus funds may be used either in substitution for or in addition to the
funds obtained from the Excess Levy.
In the event that the actual amount collected is less than the approximate annual amounts allocated for the above-stated purposes for any one or
more fiscal years (including, without limitation, as a result of any amendments to the West Virginia Constitution or changes the Code of West Virginia
impacting property taxes, levy rates or similar matters), the Board is hereby authorized and empowered to determine in its discretion (i) which one or
more of the above-stated purposes shall be reduced for each such fiscal year, and (ii) the amount of each such reduction for such fiscal year;
provided, however, that the total of all such reductions shall not exceed the amount of such collection shortfall each year.
In the event that a specific library or school facility that is named in the above-stated purposes closes or otherwise ceases operation, the Board, in its
sole discretion, may reallocate funds that were originally allocated to those specific library or school facilities to any of the other above-stated
purposes. The Board will not issue bonds in connection with the approval of the proposed Excess Levy.
The Board reserves unto itself as the levying body the right and authority to reduce the rates of the excess levy, in its sole discretion, if increased
assessments would produce funds beyond projected needs in any fiscal year specified in the levy call.
The additional levies, in cents per $100 of assessed value on each class of property, shall be on Class I property 20.66 cents; on Class II property
41.32 cents; on Class III property 82.64 cents; and on Class IV property 82.64 cents.
That the proposed years to which the additional rate of levies shall apply are the fiscal years beginning July 1, 2028, July 1, 2029, July 1, 2030, July
1, 2031, and July 1, 2032.
