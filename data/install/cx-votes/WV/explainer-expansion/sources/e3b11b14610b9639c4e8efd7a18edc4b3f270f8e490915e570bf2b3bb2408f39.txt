OFFICIAL LEVY BALLOT
MONTGOMERY CITY SPECIAL LEVY
Special election to authorize continuation of
additional levies for the fiscal years beginning
July 1, 2027, July 1, 2028, July 1, 2029, and July
1, 2030, and for the purpose of (a.) repairs &
maintenance of facilities & equipment, (b.)
Repair & Maintenance of Streets, (c.) Purchase
of Equipment and (d.) Parks & Recreation,
according to the order of the Council entered the
7th day of July 2026.
The additional levy shall be on Class I property
6.25 cents, on Class II property 12.5, on Class
IV property 25 cents per $100 of assessed
valuation.
