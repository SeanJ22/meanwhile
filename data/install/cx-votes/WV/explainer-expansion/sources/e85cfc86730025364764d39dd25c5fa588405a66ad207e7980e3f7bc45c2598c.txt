OFFICIAL LEVY BALLOT
Election to authorize additional levies for the
fiscal years beginning July 1, 2027; July 1, 2028;
July 1, 2029; July 1, 2030; and July 1, 2031, for
the provision, continuation, maintenance,
improvement, and expansion of countywide
emergency medical services, including
operations, staffing, training and workforce
development, vehicles, equipment, supplies,
facilities, administration, community EMS
education and training, and lawful support for
qualifying community emergency medical
services providers and recognized EMS
response partners serving Hampshire County,
according to the Order of the County
Commission of Hampshire County entered on
the 11th day of August, 2026.
The approximate net annual amount for the
stated purpose is $2,173,478.
The additional levy shall be on Class I property
4.75 cents; on Class II property 9.50 cents; on
Class III property 19.00 cents; and on Class IV
property 19.00 cents per $100 of assessed
value. The County Commission may reduce, but
shall not increase, these rates in any year if
reduced rates will meet projected needs.
Instructions: those favoring the additional Levy
fill in the oval FOR THE LEVY; those against the
Levy fill in the oval AGAINST THE LEVY.
