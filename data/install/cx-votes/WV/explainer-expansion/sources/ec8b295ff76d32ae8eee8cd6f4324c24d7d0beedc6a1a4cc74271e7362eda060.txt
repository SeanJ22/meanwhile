OFFICIAL LEVY BALLOT
BOARD OF EDUCATION OF
THE COUNTY OF JACKSON
NOVEMBER 3, 2026
Special election to authorize additional levies for
the fiscal years beginning July 1, 2029; July 1,
2030; July 1, 2031; July 1, 2032; and July 1,
2033; for the purposes set forth as follows:
1. That the purposes for which additional
funds are needed and the annual approximate
amount for such purposes after due allowances
for exonerations, delinquencies, etc., are as
follows:
A. To provide funds to repair, improve, and
maintain the existing Board owned motor
vehicles and where necessary to purchase new
and additional motor vehicles in order to facilitate
a safe and efficient school system at the rate of
five percent (5%) of the total annual additional
levy proceeds, for an approximate annual cost of
$688,380
B. To provide funds to distribute free
textbooks and workbooks to all students in the
Jackson County School District, and to supply
and equip elementary and secondary schools at
the rate of ten percent (10%) of the total annual
additional levy proceeds, for an approximate
annual cost of $1,376,760
C. To provide funds to repair, improve, add
to, and maintain all school facilities, to defray
utility costs, and to provide funds for land and for
the construction of buildings thereon or upon any
land now owned by the Board of Education at
the rate of twenty-five percent (25%) of the total
annual additional levy proceeds, for an
approximate annual cost of $3,441,901
D. To provide funds for the employment of
additional school system personnel to meet the
needs of the school system and to maintain
and/or increase the present supplement to the
state basic salary scale and provide benefits for
all school personnel at the rate of sixty percent
(60%) of the total annual additional levy
proceeds, for an approximate annual cost of
$8,260,563
2. That the total approximate amounts
needed during the term of this five-year (5) levy
after due allowances for exonerations,
delinquencies, etc., for said purposes is
approximately $68,838,020
All according to the order of the Board of
Education of the County of Jackson entered on
the 23rd day of July, 2026.
The additional levy shall be on Class I
property 22.95 cents; on Class II property 45.90
cents; on Class III property 91.80 cents; and on
Class IV property 91.80 cents. Such additional
levies shall be based on each one hundred
dollars valuation of Classes I, II, III, and IV
property.
