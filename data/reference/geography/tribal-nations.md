The tribal nation rows preserve the existing Census 2020 AIANNH and ANSI identifiers and recorded source evidence. State membership is reference data. This inventory names nations and their governments; a geographic lookup does not establish a reader's tribal membership.

Source: https://www2.census.gov/geo/docs/reference/codes2020/national_aiannh2020.txt. The original rows record the check date and additional discovery evidence in `provenance`.
