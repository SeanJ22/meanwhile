# National county clocks

`national_county_time_zones.json` maps five-character county FIPS strings to IANA clocks for all 50 states, DC and the five inhabited territories, including the current Census county equivalents and legacy identifiers still held in Census of Governments records. `scripts/build-county-time-zone-reference.mts` reproduces it from committed bytes.

The source fixture is the National Weather Service's 3 March 2020 county/public-forecast-zone correlation file, served as CSV by [HealthRex/CDSS at fbbac63d90866ec66cf0b6db6385eb93bbef851c](https://github.com/HealthRex/CDSS/blob/fbbac63d90866ec66cf0b6db6385eb93bbef851c/medinfo/geography/bp03mr20.csv). NOAA's federal data is public domain. The extract retains the source's county assignments and maps its documented clock codes to IANA representatives with the same current daylight-saving rules. It does not promise a county's complete historical clock rules.

[NWS county documentation](https://www.weather.gov/gis/Counties) identifies split counties: Gulf FL, Idaho ID, McKenzie/Dunn/Sioux ND, Cherry NE, Malheur OR, Stanley SD and Culberson TX. Aleutians West also spans Anchorage/Adak clocks. Multiple clocks are retained; the resolver returns no single zone for them. Broad forecast-zone rows crossing Bay FL, Clearwater ID, Gila/Pinal AZ do not change those counties' own clocks. New Chugach/Copper River county equivalents retain Anchorage time; Connecticut planning regions retain Eastern time. Kalawao and American Samoa's uninhabited Rose Island use their state's clock.

Navajo lands in Apache/Coconino/Navajo AZ observe daylight saving, while surrounding Arizona does not, as described by the [Navajo Nation](https://opvp.navajo-nsn.gov/260307-daylight-savings-times/) and [Arizona State Library](https://azsos.libguides.com/azalmanac/timezone). Both clocks are retained. Elko NV includes [West Wendover's Mountain clock](https://www.westwendovercity.com/government/city-departments/municipal-court-justice-court/general-information). Chambers/Lee/Russell AL retain Eastern as well as Central because their eastern border governments use Eastern time, including [Phenix City](https://phenixcityal.gov/), Valley and Lanett. A source's explicit clock may disambiguate these counties; no county majority is guessed.

The current NOAA file [bp16ap26.dbx](https://www.weather.gov/source/gis/Shapefiles/County/bp16ap26.dbx) was readable through the connected web tool, but direct workspace fetching failed because its configured proxy was unreachable. This reference uses the held mirror, names its exact SHA-256, and joins current county identities from the committed Census registration references.
