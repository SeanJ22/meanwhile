## Geography repair and capacity

The published geography contained only `cd:119`, `county:2020`, `cbsa:2023`, `sldu:2024`, and `sldl:2024`. Missing `cd:120`, `sldu:2026`, or `sldl:2026` now makes the registered task due, guarded by its six-hour retry window even after a successful partial publication. The runner reads only the scope-version list, not the national payload. A refresh cannot publish a partial election-plan set. Ordering is unchanged.

Real source replay exposed three independent parser problems: historical GRFN VINTAGE 70 rows conflict with current VINTAGE 90 rows; South Dakota includes a former Nebraska tabulation block whose current state is South Dakota; territories can establish a congressional plan without a legislative-plan year. Current names/current state are now used, and missing legislative years remain an error for states. Adding a coexisting plan also exposed a lexical temporal-version comparison; geography versions now use the existing unordered-version policy while source/acquisition time checks remain in force.

National byte buffers are decoded in 64 KiB chunks. Candidate and durable promotion proofs use the existing streaming canonical hash and verified held envelope. Geography reads the prior proof without its national change set and identity lists. These changes preserve the canonical digests and tampered-pointer refusal.
