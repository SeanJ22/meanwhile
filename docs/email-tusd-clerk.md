# Draft — TUSD, asking for a machine-readable meeting feed

**Status: DRAFT. Not sent. Nothing here has been reviewed by counsel.**
Related: tracker **D13**, **D14**, **B09**.

Send only after the author has read it. This is the better letter of the two:
the district is the publisher of the record and the party whose agreement
governs the portal, and a "yes, here's our calendar feed" reply removes the
vendor question entirely for this body. It is also the route that generalizes —
most districts that publish through a vendor also publish somewhere simpler,
and asking is cheaper than discovering.

---

**To:** Tucson Unified School District — Governing Board / Clerk of the Board
**Subject:** Machine-readable feed for Governing Board meeting agendas

---

Hello,

I'm building a free civic tool that shows residents which public bodies govern
their area and when those bodies next meet, linking each meeting to the agenda
the district itself published. No advertising, nothing for residents to pay,
and no republishing of your documents — the link goes to your posting.

I would like to include the TUSD Governing Board, and I'd rather ask you how to
read your calendar than guess.

Do you publish Governing Board meetings in any machine-readable form? Any of
these would work, in roughly this order of preference:

- an iCal or RSS feed of the meeting calendar
- an open-data or JSON endpoint
- a plain agendas page on a district domain, separate from the board portal
- a notification list I can subscribe to

If the answer is that the Diligent Community portal is the only channel, that
is a useful answer too, and I'd ask two follow-ups: does the district object to
a modest, identified, rate-limited client reading the meeting pages that are
already public there, and is there someone on your side I should coordinate
with? I would identify the project and a contact address in every request,
space requests out, and never re-fetch an agenda that has not changed.

Two commitments, so you know what would appear:

- **Attribution names the district.** The agenda is TUSD's record; the software
  vendor is the filing cabinet. Every entry credits TUSD and links your page.
- **Nothing is inferred.** If the district has not posted an agenda, the tool
  says "no agenda posted yet" rather than guessing at one. If I cannot confirm
  a detail against your posting, it does not appear.

If there's a better contact for this — a records officer, or whoever maintains
the board portal — I'd appreciate a pointer.

Thank you,

[name]
[project] — [url]
[contact address]
