# EX-02: explainers reach every measure

Moves the §0 goal that a resident sees a plain-English explainer, with its source, for every measure on their ballot.

## What holds the standing job

`scripts/measure-translation-routine.mts` runs one clock per state. Only a failed save, or a state ballot that cannot be read at all, holds that state's clock. A measure with no saved official text stays an open blank: it is listed in the `MEASURE_TRANSLATION_RESULT` line and the job goes on to every other measure and state.

## Measures with no saved official text

`lib/translate/routine-sources.ts` saves every page the ballot cites. A measure is a blank when none of its cited pages names it (its number or title, the same names the writer step requires). For each blank, in order:

1. A page found earlier (`lib/translate/routine-finds.ts`, kept in the state's existing durable attempts) is read again.
2. The state or county election office's own pages are read: the registered door's election page and root, plus the official linking page that gives a non-`.gov` site its standing. Links on held pages that name the measure are followed, and up to six lists of measures per state are followed one step further (`lib/translate/routine-measure-pages.ts`).
3. If Admin has paid search on and a Brave or Exa key is present, up to two searches run through the existing search guard and cost ledger. Only results that are official (`.gov`, or linked from a saved official page) are opened. An empty search waits six hours before the next one.

A page is kept only if its saved text names the measure. It is saved like any cited source, joins the state's captures, and the explainer is written and checked from it by the existing writer and independent checker. `withMeasureTranslations` attaches the record to its exact contest and division when the page was cited or when its saved text names that measure, and the ballot card's Sources section lists the explainer's page.
