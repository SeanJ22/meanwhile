# LV-01 — voting locations panel

READY on `stream/lv-01`, cut from `origin/river` at `78f82a232dd1d0545a46835e7716032ff3807425`. Built and checked; River owns merging, combined gates, deployment and the requested post-shipping live check.

Residents can click a voting pin without dismissing the panel. The picked row highlights and scrolls onto the screen. When an answer has no sites, election-office contact information leads the panel: Google's local/state office name, address, phone, hours and polling-place finder wherever supplied. The finder takes precedence over the general elections page. Missing fields are filled individually from the same state's FEC election-office directory; supplied Google fields stay intact. When Google supplies no office or fails, the directory supplies the offices. Lake Street now shows Google's Secretary of State contact and both California phone numbers from the FEC directory. Multiple phone numbers have separate working telephone links. Sources credit every contributor.

These office answers remain request-only: uncached and never published or saved as civic records. Failed Google attempts log their HTTP status (null only when no response arrives) and up to the first 300 characters of the available body in server stderr only, with known keys and reader addresses redacted. This includes retried HTTP errors, incomplete bodies, and unreadable HTTP-200 responses. Only a safe failure reason reaches the answer. No response-body diagnostic was added to the repository, data store or source vault.

The route overlaps the held state read and, only when holdings are empty, the address-free public election catalog with Census. The live source deadline is 2.4 seconds, leaving time to display the fallback office. Voting camera movement lands immediately; unchanged jurisdiction records avoid rendering again when pins lift to the map, and offscreen rows defer layout. All sites remain available, including all 678 Phoenix records. The always-empty `pollingPlaceLinks` field, singular office-source representation and replaced code are removed.

Days remain as filed. Held schedules have labels and free-text days/hours without actual schedule date ranges; **VS-07** owns adding those dates and the unattended voting-sites refresh. Neither unattended refresh nor currently unpublished county office hours blocks LV-01. Google hours render whenever present, verified with the complete-office browser fixture. The separate county-office lane owns further county contact coverage.
