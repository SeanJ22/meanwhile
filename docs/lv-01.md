# LV-01 — voting locations panel

Built and pushed on `stream/lv-01`, cut from `origin/river` at `78f82a232dd1d0545a46835e7716032ff3807425`. **Not READY, merged or deployed.**

Residents can select a voting pin without dismissing their address form, and the selected row scrolls into view. An answer without sites leads with the local and state election offices Google supplies, including published address, phone, hours and polling-place finder. The finder URL takes precedence over the general elections page. If Google supplies no office, the existing FEC state-office directory supplies the contact instead. Missing fields are not invented. Office contacts exist only on the uncached answer; Google's voterinfo publication prohibition remains in place. The always-empty `pollingPlaceLinks` response field and the empty Google jurisdiction previously used only for office citations are removed.

Voting camera movement lands immediately, the held state read overlaps Census, and unchanged jurisdiction records avoid rendering again when pins lift to the map. These changes remove work before a resident can use the answer. They do not guarantee a response from an unavailable source.

Days remain exactly as filed. `VotingSite.schedules` has election labels and free-text days/hours, without actual schedule date ranges. Filtering past voting days waits for **VS-07** to provide those dates.

## Connections

**VS-01 → LV-01 → VS-03.** VS-01 supplies the official voting holdings and live Google fallback (`readVotingSitesProjection`, `answerVotingSites`, `lookupCivicAddress`). LV-01 carries those results through `/api/voting-sites` into `VotingSitesPanel`. VS-03's cross-state map consumes `onSites` and `onAddressPoint` in `MapEntry`, draws the actual `MapCanvas` markers and passes the selected id back to the displayed row. VS-07 is the additional upstream dependency for future day filtering.

This moves §0's correct, fast, usable way for residents to understand and act on their government. No data, spending settings, source schedules or production records were changed.
