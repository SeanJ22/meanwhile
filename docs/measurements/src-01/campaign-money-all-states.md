# Campaign money: every routine jurisdiction

The routine visits 56 jurisdictions across the states, the District of Columbia, and U.S. territories. Eight currently have person-bound acquisition profiles: CT, HI, ID, KY, NH, SC, VT, and WA. The other 48 explicitly lack a supported person/office/cycle binding. A correct missing-money reason for those jurisdictions proves reason delivery; it does not establish financial-source coverage.

All 56 visits completed. The 55 jurisdictions with production targets pass reason persistence and actual person-card checks; Guam has no published targets. On both initial and tomorrow runs, all 32,720 person/seat missing-reason targets retain their own expected reason, representing 31,751 unique people. All 31,363 eligible missing-money cards show that reason, representing 30,394 unique people, and 714 cards retain held money. The routine contains 33,215 total person/seat targets. Reusing the first aggregate reason would assign a different reason to 3,417 targets. No production write or SAM request occurred.

All 48 unsupported jurisdictions completed the two publication/API/card runs. Of those, 47 have published targets and Guam has none. Each day, 29,303 person/seat targets retain their own expected reason and 28,229 eligible missing-money person cards show it. Forty eligible cards retain existing money. The 42 production cycle facts held by Arizona and New Jersey preserve every numeric amount, person binding, and source identity/hash; their complete amount/binding fingerprints are identical tomorrow.

`campaign-money-all-states.json` reports each state's completed and failed checks separately. Counts distinguish person/seat targets, unique people, eligible state-money cards, existing cards with money, and federal targets whose `FundingBlock` uses FEC. Every persisted reason is compared with the expected reason for that individual source target. The retained local checks also show how many targets would have received a different reason from the first statewide aggregate entry.

The replay exports production through the existing `readonly` role, processes one state per worker, acquires real publisher response bytes, and writes only to local stores. It then calls the actual campaign-finance API handler and renders each eligible missing-money target through `StateCampaignSection` and `PersonCard`. Initial and tomorrow publication runs both execute; tomorrow uses retained source responses and a controlled clock. Washington additionally executes an actual next-hour publication/API/card run; other jurisdictions check hourly admission without claiming a third publication. This does not claim a future live request or a deployed page.

Washington exposed a real recurring failure: a due committee seat reconciled previously filed rows belonging to another, funded seat for the same person. Reconciliation now matches the person and seat together. Its corrected initial, hourly, and tomorrow runs each retain all 333 own reasons and show the correct reason on all 331 eligible person cards. Every person binding and source URL/filing URL/publisher remains unchanged across 434 held cycle facts, and the complete amount/binding fingerprint is stable hourly and tomorrow. Three contribution amounts increase in the newly captured official source; `campaign-money-wa-updates.json` retains the exact native rows and both prior source hashes. The original 130-mismatch result is preserved separately.
