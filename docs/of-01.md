## Implementation

- White House acquisition reads the official administration page and each office's own profile, verifies President and Vice President separately, and captures the official contact link. Both roles use the existing national people contract and reader value.
- National publication checks each office independently and retains last verified holders and captured evidence when a replacement fails. A verified replacement retires only that office's previous holder.
- Legislative authority filters legislative memberships only. Nonlegislative officials retain their offices, contacts and division links, including legacy rows with stale legislative links or chamber metadata. The composition also preserves the federal directory's identities and contact precision and carries already-held official executive citations over supplementary GitHub roster citations.
- A new contract file registers daily state-binding refreshes with hourly retries for all 50 states, DC and the five territories. Existing statewide people contracts already run daily. Neither requires an owner order. The normal runner admits the tasks through their registered source contracts.
- Each binding refresh verifies its official Census state identity and carries every previous GOOD member and original source date. An unchanged verified binding can be confirmed again. Initial publication still requires verified current source facts. No reader request gains an external source fetch.

The runner and runtime integration changes are the five-line, two-file patch Sean explicitly approved. No changes were made to `contracts.ts`, `automatic-refresh.ts`, other lanes' official readers, migrations, Admin controls, paid-work settings or spending limits.
