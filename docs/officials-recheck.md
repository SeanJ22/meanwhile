# OR-01: officials stay current

When an official leaves or a successor takes office, readers get the sourced current holder or an explicit vacancy. Admin's **Check officials now** brings every people feed covering a state, county, district or ZIP forward to the next source-runner pass. **What I heard** is a Scout lead, verified against captured official pages within the existing paid-work controls.

People feeds carry their country or state division. Place marking includes those covering the place, and their feed marks become ordinary due requests. The runner passes the mark's time into mirror revision reads, so a recent cached commit check cannot consume a deliberate new request. Shared state consumers reuse that provider observation; unchanged immutable releases keep their original acquisition dates.

The first Admin check or mission prepares its covering registered people feeds before general door derivation runs. Missing source rows are created; legacy rows receive their intrinsic division while keeping manual settings, pauses and existing clocks. Settling an older request preserves a newer check renewed concurrently.

`official-rosters.mts` reads the Senate, House Clerk and each legislature's own roster daily. Official responses use the shared known-release cache and conditional HTTP checks. Publishers without ETag or Last-Modified are recorded as such; their bytes must be compared after a read. Each successfully enumerated chamber can replace the mirror's seat claims independently of its sibling. An explicit single-seat vacancy or successor can replace its exact native office independently. A partial multi-seat district cannot remove unnamed co-holders.

The canonical candidate saves source responses, changed seats and losing mirror values for BL-01. Before publication, the source parser reconstructs the holder, office, native membership and represented division from those responses. Census labels join statutory district designations within one state and chamber. Optional facts retain their own sources. Failed source reads preserve earlier verified facts and leave the gaps visible.

Federal and state readers compose official rosters with mirror enrichment. The runner publishes the composition into the existing reader artifacts, including vacancies. A vacancy row displays **Vacant since <date>** and its source; when the publisher does not give the departure date, the row says **Vacant**. A vacancy opens its source rather than a person card.

Quiet publication passes compare accepted version identities and carry unchanged federal artifacts without reopening source payloads. Reader imports remain separate from source acquisition and publication, so the deployed route traces stay within the existing bundle budget. The new daily roster contracts extend the standing portfolio while preserving its existing source order.

A failed chamber preserves its prior records while the other chamber publishes.
