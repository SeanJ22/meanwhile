## Implementation

Portrait acquisition uses `storeVerifiedPortrait`. Open States and Congress feeds publish a matching native person image; official and campaign pages and freely licensed Commons images follow §3. Personal sources still need matching independent evidence. Candidate portrait acquisition no longer consumes campaign-site approvals; the approval burdens and the replaced portrait module are deleted.

Cards and ballots use one identity-based portrait selector. The reader state loads candidate photo holdings before building official cards, so an existing candidate photograph is available when the official record has none. An official photograph wins over a candidate photograph. Native aliases retain their source-bound photo without a separate re-binding step. Statewide source records reuse CL-01’s verified official-publisher and office relationship, with its pairwise native-identity conflict rule. An unresolved ballot or funding bridge supplies no extra portrait aliases and cannot suppress an independently verified official photo.

The standing portrait job includes state, congressional and live local roster records. National portraits have their own hourly contract. The duplicate portrait call inside the national executive crawl is removed; National cards display the same portrait data.
