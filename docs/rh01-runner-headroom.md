## Causes and implementation

The runner could start nested work without a separate reservation. Admission also used recent small runs to reduce a script's reservation, and some prefixed commands were classified by their wrapper instead of their executable. Every child now acquires shared admission before spawn, keeps its executable's declared floor, and raises that floor from observed high-water memory. Adaptive observations belong to the exact loaded release. Nested reservations own their process memory separately, so their parent does not reserve the same growth twice. Zombie processes no longer retain reservations.

Source jobs loaded national source records and national predecessor projections before selecting a state. The replacements stream acquisition to disk, publish state or native-subject partitions, and slice legacy publications inside PostgreSQL before transfer. Routine upkeep, audit history, mirror scheduling, task handouts and file-backed repositories use bounded pages or disk indexes. The shared copy host streams uploads and downloads instead of buffering complete copies in the supervisor.

Hunter budget memberships and the spend-window counter now use disk-backed snapshots, bounded pages and exact subject lookups. Research and freshness scheduling now use state or subject pages and disk indexes; complete parent measurements still prevent a final capacity claim. The authorized state FEC implementation preserves the production source identities and amounts as verified below. Authentic worker replay and the complete frozen before/after workload remain open.
