Google Civic is a reader-requested fallback for November 3, 2026 voting locations. `/api/voting-sites` first resolves the reader's place and loads the official voting-source projection. Only a separate explicit fallback request, and no loaded official sites, rules or election-publication statement for that place, permit an address query to Google. Results stay on the voting panel and carry the label "from Google's election data (Voting Information Project)" with their upstream sources in Sources.

The `google-civic-voterinfo` routine contract refreshes the elections catalog and per-state inventory summaries for Admin. It sends no addresses and runs no background voterinfo probes. Its election-window clock is daily, weekly afterward. Requests honor provider Retry-After; the API's missing publication timestamp remains unknown. Existing saved public-source observations remain attributed separately from official election holdings.

The explicit fallback reuses `lookupCivicAddress` and `civicLookupFromRaw`, selects the November 3 election ID from Google's catalog, and adapts the returned polling places, early-vote sites and ballot-return locations to the shared voting panel. The source parser removes normalizedInput. Reader addresses, key-bearing URLs and lookup responses are neither logged nor persisted. Ask and the ballot page do not retain or automatically reuse an address, and there is no separate endpoint that bypasses the official-source check.

Committed real Google API samples test the shared parser, explicit election selection, source attribution, missing native district IDs, transient fallback rendering, and the absence of background address requests. Live-site and production checks are performed after River releases VS-01, by Sean's oversight session, as specified in the READY commit.
