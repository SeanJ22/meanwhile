# SP-01

Reader answers stay saved for 15 minutes and can be served while they refresh. Source checks that find no change leave those copies alone. Published changes clear the affected states; national executive changes still clear place answers everywhere. Ballot copies vary by ZIP and expire at midnight in Phoenix. Representative office pins consume coordinates saved during publication.

## Connections

OF-01's people and national-executive publications and SH-01's federal-directory publications feed SP-01 through the canonical publication store. The existing scheduled source publishers call the point-saving code before candidate hashing. SP-01 feeds place-page delivery and BL-01's ballot page. River owns the combined release and the live speed/transfer check.

The reader tag mapping splits `+` scopes, recognizes `openfema:XX`, gives national people their own tag, and excludes the specified federal-feed and roll-call scopes. Shared curated publications retain state-wide invalidation. Both successful unchanged-confirmation paths retain their database receipts without purging reader copies.

`next.config.ts` opts `/ballot` into Netlify caching and varies it by `zip`, `state`, `lat`, `lng`, and Next's `_rsc` discriminator. The existing middleware resolves the ZIP's actual state, sets its tag, and shortens the lifetime to the next Phoenix midnight. Its Date header includes rendering time in the copy's age. Map-point selections and unknown or conflicting ZIP inputs use no-store. Netlify's adapter merges the configured variation with its own Next/RSC variations and preserves the explicit CDN policy even when Next's browser policy is private/no-store.

Points retain their exact public address and Census source separately from directory-supplied coordinates. A later publication reuses points for the same address. An address change starts a new lookup. Postal boxes and records without a street address do not start lookups. Addresses Census cannot locate keep their contact facts and retry during a later publication. Reader visits perform no representative-office geocoding. The replaced server lookup, eager worker lookup, representative-pin fallback, and point-priming payload/code were removed.

River also checks that reader reads in `database_transfer_daily` fall with comparable traffic. Those deployed outcomes remain the release owner's live check.
